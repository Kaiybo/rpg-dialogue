# RPG Dialogue: The Hinterlands (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-16)
- New: Revantusk Village, eight people: Primal Torntusk and his kidnapped mate, Huntsman Markhor, Mystic Yayo'jin, Katoom the Angler, Lard the ogre innkeeper, Smith Slagtree, Otho Moji'ko and Gorkas.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-15)
- Replies for the classes and races these people would really react to (warriors, hunters, rogues, mages, warlocks, druids, shamans, paladins; every race of the faction, and the Skyborne), each leading somewhere.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Every person here writes a first impression in your diary after you first talk (the journal's People page shows it too).

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.2.1 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: Aerie Peak now talks in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments. The Wildhammer keep their brogue, Falstad is as loud as ever and Talonaxe as gruff, in connected speech rather than scraps.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)
- Depth: Falstad (the trolls, the Alliance, Quel'Danil, a word for Ironforge, a Skyborne's glide), Talonaxe (Sharpbeak), Fraggar (a reply for Ironforge dwarf shamans) and Thulfram.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits bring personal lines (habits, worries, running jokes), and some NPCs get night-time lines. Fraggar, Thulfram.

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- Aerie Peak (Alliance): Falstad Wildhammer, Gryphon Master Talonaxe, Fraggar Thundermantle and Innkeeper Thulfram. The Wildhammer Sentries.
