# Hinterlands folk (keys: hl_truk, hl_killium, hl_drakk, hl_nioma, hl_rennaz)

Wiki pages read 2026-11-17 (warcraft.wiki.gg): Aerie Peak, Revantusk Village, Truk Wildbeard, Killium Bouldertoe, Drakk Stonehand, Nioma, Renn'az.

## Sources
- **Truk Wildbeard** (male Wildhammer, Bartender, Wildhammer Keep): food and drink vendor (cheese, meat, drink). Was part of the old Wildhammer Clan faction until 2.0.3, so present in Classic.
- **Killium Bouldertoe** (male Wildhammer, Stable Master, Wildhammer Keep).
- **Drakk Stonehand** (male Wildhammer, (Master) Leatherworking Trainer, Wildhammer Keep). Wiki: one of very few black-haired Wildhammer. Gossip: "I can make amazing things with the materials I find out here. By Muradin's beard, there are fantastic creatures in these hills! Go have a whack at them, <name>, and bring me back some tall tales." (paraphrased in his lines).
- **Nioma** (female Wildhammer, Leatherworking Supplies, Wildhammer Keep).
- **Renn'az** (male troll, Ammunition Vendor, Revantusk Village; a jungle troll model before 4.0.3a, treated simply as Revantusk here).

## Voices
- **Truk**: boisterous barman, every round with a story; pride in the sky over the mountain.
- **Killium**: slow, friendly, ashamed of being afraid of heights.
- **Drakk**: delighted, boastful craftsman.
- **Nioma**: brisk, keeps Drakk's accounts and sighs about them.
- **Renn'az**: Revantusk troll speech ("mon", "be"), proud, hates Vilebranch and Wildhammer alike.

## Authored, not canon
- Truk: "thunder ale" brewed in caves under the Keep; Falstad tried to fly without a gryphon and landed in a herring barrel; a rider called Durgan and the hole in a Vilebranch hut.
- Killium: afraid of heights; Falstad's offer to "cure" him; Witherbark sniffing round the stables at night.
- Drakk: black hair blamed on soot or an unmentioned great-grandfather.
- Nioma: Drakk borrows her stock and pays in boots and belts; dye from hill flowers; thread by gryphon from Ironforge, salt from Menethil.
- Renn'az: young Revantusk who want peace with the Wildhammer.
