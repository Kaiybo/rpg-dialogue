# Wildhammer Keep trades  (keys: truk, drakk, nioma, killium)

Sources for all four: https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Aerie_Peak and each NPC's page (read 2026-11-17). All four are Classic NPCs (Wildhammer Clan faction until 2.0.3). None gives a quest; each is built from role, place and neighbours (CONTENT_GUIDE §1).

# Truk Wildbeard  (key: truk)
## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Truk_Wildbeard (Wildhammer male, Bartender, food and drink vendor in Wildhammer Keep; NPC id 4782)
## Their quests
- none: bartender.
## Identity
Truk Wildbeard · Wildhammer Keep · Wildhammer · dwarf · bartender. Jovial, gossiping, teasing rival of Thulfram. Brogue.
## Authored, not canon
- Rivalry with Thulfram (beds vs barrels); buys Rhapsody's moonshine on the quiet; his father poured for Second War riders; Witherbark stealing gryphon eggs (gossip); Falstad drinks one mug a night to someone he never names (matches Falstad's 0.34.0 secret).
## What they notice
- Drunkenness; Ironforge dwarves.

# Drakk Stonehand  (key: drakk)
## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Drakk_Stonehand (Wildhammer male, Master Leatherworking Trainer in Classic; one of very few black-haired Wildhammer; gossip about fantastic creatures and tall tales, paraphrased, not quoted; beta name Wark Nightsky)
## Their quests
- none: trainer.
## Identity
Eager, proud craftsman who wants the story with the hide. Brogue.
## Authored, not canon
- Hides sold to Ironforge merchants instead of him; his parents' sixty-year argument about Bronzebeard blood; owlbeast pelts; won't skin razorbeaks near Talonaxe.
## What they notice
- Leatherworkers; hunters.

# Nioma  (key: nioma)
## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Nioma (Wildhammer female, Leatherworking Supplies, NPC id 8160)
## Their quests
- none: vendor.
## Identity
Brisk, dry supplier; Drakk's main customer and creditor.
## Authored, not canon
- Thread and dye come from the Wetlands by gryphon; salt from the coast, made dear by the Revantusk; Drakk pays late.
## What they notice
- Leatherworkers.

# Killium Bouldertoe  (key: killium)
## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Killium_Bouldertoe (Wildhammer male, Stable Master, NPC id 10061; may be related to Geofram and Golnir Bouldertoe: not used beyond "a few of us scattered about")
## Their quests
- none: stable master.
## Identity
Calm, gruff, talks to beasts more than owners.
## Authored, not canon
- Pets hide under the hay from gryphons; a young gryphon once went after a stabled beast; a hunter's biting turtle terrified the gryphons.
## What they notice
- Pets; hunters.
