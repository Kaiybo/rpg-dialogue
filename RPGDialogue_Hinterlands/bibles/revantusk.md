# Revantusk Village  (keys: torntusk, markhor, yayojin, katoom, lard, slagtree, otho, gorkas)

## Sources (warcraft.wiki.gg, Classic sections; village added in patch 1.5.0)
- Revantusk_Village: the Revantusk kept faith with the Horde after the Second War, were too few to free the interned orcs, fled to the Hinterlands coast; sent runners to Thrall after the Third War, pact of friendship and mutual assistance; other forest trolls call them weaklings, they call the others traitors; enmity with the Wildhammer. A Vilebranch midnight raid took Elder Torntusk and Guard Captain Malkhor to Jintha'Alor. Saltwater snapjaws steal bait. Flights: Undercity, Tarren Mill, Hammerfall.
- Primal_Torntusk (female, co-leader, Elder Torntusk's mate; spies at Jintha'Alor): Kidnapped Elder Torntusk!, Wanted: Vile Priestess Hexx and Her Minions, Job Opening: Guard Captain of Revantusk Village, Return to Primal Torntusk (from Elder Torntusk after Recover the Key!).
- Huntsman_Markhor (stable master, hunter; twin of Malkhor; hates arcane magic; Classic gossip: Zul'jin will return, keep the forest trolls alive): Stalking the Stalkers (Silvermane), Hunt the Savages (savage owlbeasts), Avenging the Fallen (Razorbeak Skylord plume; Wildhammer train gryphons to kill trolls), Separation Anxiety (Malkhor's remains fed to oozes and wolves at Jintha'Alor; Yayo'jin prepares the burial).
- Mystic_Yayo'jin (female reagent vendor on the north platform): Cannibalistic Cousins (Vilebranch soothsayers and scalpers near Shaol'watha and Agol'watha; "if Zul'jin were here").
- Katoom_the_Angler (fishing trainer and supplies; Nat Pagle is his mentor): Snapjaws, Mon!, Gammerita, Mon!
- Lard (ogre innkeeper; keeps the peace, sells fish): Lard Lost His Lunch (Vilebranch drove him off his picnic on the island to the north).
- Smith_Slagtree (orc smith; a gnome saved his life when he was young and gave him tools): Vilebranch Hooligans (tools at the northeastern temples or Skulk Rock).
- Otho_Moji'ko (cooking supplies): Message to the Wildhammer (Highvale high elves at Quel'Danil), Another Message to the Wildhammer (gryphon feathers), The Final Message to the Wildhammer (feathered spear planted by the well at Aerie Peak).
- Gorkas (orc wind rider master, Orgrimmar; fishes off the pier, wants a huge grouper). Speaks as an orc (plain, proud, no troll dialect); Slagtree likewise.

- Torntusk: Kidnapped Elder Torntusk!, Wanted: Vile Priestess Hexx and Her Minions, Job Opening: Guard Captain of Revantusk Village.
- Markhor: Stalking the Stalkers, Hunt the Savages.
- Yayo'jin: Cannibalistic Cousins.
- Katoom: Snapjaws, Mon!, Gammerita, Mon!
- Lard: Lard Lost His Lunch.
- Slagtree: Vilebranch Hooligans.
- Otho: Message to the Wildhammer, Another Message to the Wildhammer, The Final Message to the Wildhammer.
- Gorkas: none: wind rider master; fishes for a grouper.
