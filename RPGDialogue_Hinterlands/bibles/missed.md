# The Hinterlands, missed folk  (keys: hl_brannik_ironbelly, hl_grella_stonefist, hl_ruppo_zipcoil, hl_gigget_zipcoil, hl_jalinde_summerdrake, hl_oran_snakewrithe)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg via `tools/npc.py` (read 2026-11-18): each NPC's own page; quest pages Rin'ji's Secret, Oran's Gratitude.
- Classic placement: Brannik and Grella trade at Aerie Peak (Stormfeather Outpost is Cataclysm); Jalinde is at Quel'Danil Lodge; Oran Snakewrithe is in the Undercity's Magic Quarter (Revantusk Village is Cataclysm) and is filed here because her study and quests are the Hinterlands'.
- Existing pack: hinterlands.md (Falstad, Aerie Peak), wildhammer_keep.md, revantusk.md (the tribes, Quel'Danil as the dwarves' allies).

## Their quests
- Oran Snakewrithe: Rin'ji's Secret (turn-in), Oran's Gratitude. Her other wiki quests are later-era and not used.
- The rest have none. Jalinde is the Horde's target at the lodge; not spoken.

## Identity / voice
- Brannik Ironbelly · Wildhammer dwarf man · loud, hearty; armor light enough for a gryphon.
- Grella Stonefist · Wildhammer dwarf woman · brisk, motherly; packs your bag for you.
- Ruppo Zipcoil · gnome man · excitable inventor; percentages; builds while he talks.
- Gigget Zipcoil · gnome woman · organised, dry; counts Ruppo's prototypes and fires.
- Jalinde Summerdrake · high elf woman, ranger · proud, guarded; grieves Quel'Thalas; trusts the Wildhammer.
- Oran Snakewrithe · Forsaken woman · dry scholar; pays others to take the risks.

## Authored, not canon
- Brannik: a gryphon refusing a rider over a saddle strap. Grella: supplies come up by gryphon.
- The Zipcoils: how they are related is not on the wiki, so it is left unsaid; the testing range, the flag system with the riders, the lost tent, the tripwire alarm.
- Jalinde speaking for the lodge (the wiki only speculates that she leads it); Horde scouts in the trees.
- Oran writes the finder's name in the margin; she stays in the Undercity and rewards couriers.

## Never
- Blood elves, Silvermoon rebuilt, or anything of the north after the Sunwell's fall beyond "we hear little".
- Stormfeather Outpost, Hiri'watha Research Station, Revantusk Oran.
