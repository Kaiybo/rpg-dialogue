## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Atal%27ai_Exile (read): jungle troll priest, banished from the Atal'ai for questioning Jammal'an and the summoning of Hakkar; took refuge with the Witherbark at Shadra'Alor; wants his tribe freed but accepts he may no longer be part of it.
- Quest text read: The Atal'ai Exile (Fel'Zerul's; completion), Return to Fel'Zerul, Jammal'an the Prophet (Classic).

## Their quests
- The Atal'ai Exile (turn-in): "You are brave to come here. Brave or foolish..."; recognizes summoning artifacts; Jammal'an leads the Chamber of Blood; "Has Hakkar returned to the Temple I wonder?"
- Return to Fel'Zerul (gives): once thought Jammal'an worthy; his unyielding faith will destroy the Atal'ai; labelled an infidel; the artifacts are harmless; if Jammal'an has the fetish enchantment, the world is in peril.
- Jammal'an the Prophet (gives): the Witherbark don't welcome strangers; "I don't have a home anymore"; urged caution, "What if the prophecy was nothing more than manipulation?"; wants revenge, and his people freed; completion: "reckless trust of false visions led my people to their eternal doom."
