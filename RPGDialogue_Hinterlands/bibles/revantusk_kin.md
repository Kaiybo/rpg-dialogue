# Elder Torntusk  (key: elder_torntusk)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Elder_Torntusk (read 2026-11-17: co-leader of the Revantusk with his mate Primal Torntusk; fought for the Old Horde in the Second War; the Revantusk kept faith with the Horde, sent to Thrall after the Third War, pact of friendship and mutual assistance; taken by a Vilebranch midnight raid to Jintha'Alor for Vile Priestess Hexx's altar; freed by Horde adventurers with a key; added 1.5.0, NPC id 14757. Cataclysm role (leading at Revantusk, Skulk Rock) NOT used.)
- bibles/revantusk.md (Primal's quests and the raid).

## Their quests
- Kidnapped Elder Torntusk! (Primal's quest; he is the one found)
- Recover the Key! (gives): one of the Vilebranch keepers holds the cage key; the keeper is not named here.
- Return to Primal Torntusk (gives): tell his mate he's coming, slowly.

## Identity
Elder Torntusk · caged at the summit of Jintha'Alor · Horde (Revantusk) · forest troll · elder
Personality: proud, dry, defiant, fond of his mate
Speech: troll accent ("mon", "de", "dat", "be"), whole sentences, jokes under pressure
Knows and cares about: his mate, his tribe, the old Horde, Thrall's word
Default attitudes: orcs are old comrades; Darkspear are cousins in loyalty; Wildhammer are enemies who at least never sacrificed anyone
Never says: Hakkar by name, Zul'Gurub, any Cataclysm events

- He regrets the warlocks who led the old Horde but not standing with the orcs.
- Wildhammer throw stones at Revantusk boats.
