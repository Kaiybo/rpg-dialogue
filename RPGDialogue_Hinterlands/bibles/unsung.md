# Aerie Peak: the unsung (0.39.0; keys: guthrum, harggan)

## Sources
- Classic NPC roster of Aerie Peak as remembered (no quests, no gossip on record): Guthrum Thunderfist <Gryphon Master>, Harggan <Blacksmithing Supplies>. Wiki pages not re-read this pass.

## Their quests
- none: service NPCs; every conversation keeps BUSINESS.

## Identity
- Guthrum Thunderfist: Wildhammer dwarf, hearty; gryphons raised from the egg; riders lost over Jintha'Alor; fond but wary of Ironforge.
- Harggan: Wildhammer dwarf, gruff; hauls coal and iron up the mountain path; the owlbeast that sat on the coal.

## Authored, not canon
Two riders lost this year; Guthrum's father throwing him on a gryphon; Harggan's lost loads; the Peak being poor in ore.

## Never
Anything after Year 1; Wildhammer politics beyond the old Ironforge rift.

## What they notice
Guthrum: dwarves, riding up mounted. Harggan: smiths and miners, gnomes.
