# Aerie Peak folk  (keys: doran, kerr, claira, howin)

Sources: https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Aerie_Peak and each NPC's page (read 2026-11-17). All four are Classic NPCs at Aerie Peak (Doran id 7778, Kerr id 11813, Claira 11812, Howin 11810). Doran's and Kerr's wiki quests are Cataclysm (Stormfeather Outpost, Vilebranch/Skulk Rock chains) and are NOT used; in this pack they have no quests.

# Doran Steelwing  (key: doran)
## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Doran_Steelwing (Wildhammer male; before Cataclysm in Aerie Peak; later gossip "I'd rather be back home at Aerie Peak than starin' down these ugly" trolls: used only as a temperament hint)
## Their quests
- none in Classic (see above).
## Identity
Fierce, blunt rider who hates the forest trolls.
## Authored, not canon
- He flies patrols for the Peak; the Thane tried talking to the trolls and "buried the messenger" each time; relation to Khaelyn Steelwing not assumed.
## What they notice
- Warriors; trolls.

# Kerr Ironsight  (key: kerr)
## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kerr_Ironsight (Wildhammer female; before Cataclysm in Aerie Peak; her cousin fought in the Second War (Cataclysm quest text, not used))
## Their quests
- none in Classic.
## Identity
Watchful, dry lookout.
## Authored, not canon
- She watches the passes; the name came from her grandmother; Witherbark scouts below Shadra'Alor and Vilebranch fires over Jintha'Alor; the Thane wants proof before sending riders.
## What they notice
- Hunters; stealth.

# Claira Kindfeather  (key: claira)
## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Claira_Kindfeather (Wildhammer female in Aerie Peak; no other information)
## Their quests
- none.
## Identity
Warm, chatty elder; calls people "dear".
## Authored, not canon
- Married to Howin (the wiki only says he stands beside her); has lived at the Peak since before the First War; remembers Sharpbeak's mother as a chick.
## What they notice
- Low-level characters; pets.

# Howin Kindfeather  (key: howin)
## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Howin_Kindfeather (Wildhammer male, stands next to Claira and walks around town)
## Their quests
- none.
## Identity
Cheerful wanderer who greets everyone and knows every gryphon by name.
## Authored, not canon
- Claira's husband; walks laps of the keep to "keep his legs young".
## What they notice
- Ironforge dwarves.
