# The Hinterlands  (keys: falstad, talonaxe, fraggar, thulfram)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Aerie_Peak (Wildhammer capital in the northwestern Hinterlands; built into a mountainside; gryphon aviary; Wildhammer Keep; master gryphon riders; not part of the reformed Alliance after the Third War but friendly to those who prove themselves; Witherbark, Vilebranch and Revantusk trolls attack the aeries; allied with the high elves of Quel'Danil; Wildhammer Sentries; Classic NPCs Falstad, Fraggar)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Falstad_Wildhammer (High Thane of the Wildhammer and lord of Aerie Peak in Classic, dealing with encroaching forest trolls; boisterous, proud, combative, thick Scottish-flavored dialect; flew with gryphon riders in and after the Second War; To The Hinterlands, Gryphon Master Talonaxe)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gryphon_Master_Talonaxe (master of the aviary; gruff, wishes he were flying; can't always get what you want; Witherbark Cages, The Altar of Zul, Thadius Grimshade, Return to the Hinterlands, Saving Sharpbeak, Rhapsody Shindigger)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Saving_Sharpbeak (Sharpbeak: a gryphon, young of Swiftwind and Keenclaw, caged in Jintha'Alor in Vilebranch territory; final step after Witherbark Cages, The Altar of Zul, Thadius Grimshade, Nekrum's Medallion, The Divination, Return to the Hinterlands; remains in Classic and Forever)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhapsody_Shindigger (male Wildhammer dwarf with a moonshine camp east of Aerie Peak)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Fraggar_Thundermantle (Wildhammer shaman, partially sighted; defender of the land's spirits against dark magic, sacrifice and the Vilebranch; Troll Necklace Bounty, Skulk Rock Clean-up)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Innkeeper_Thulfram (Wildhammer innkeeper at Wildhammer Keep; hospitable and jovial)

## Their quests
- Falstad: To The Hinterlands, Gryphon Master Talonaxe.
- Talonaxe: Witherbark Cages, The Altar of Zul, Return to the Hinterlands, Saving Sharpbeak, Rhapsody Shindigger.
- Fraggar: Troll Necklace Bounty, Skulk Rock Clean-up. Thulfram: none.

## Never
- The Council of Three Hammers, Falstad in Ironforge, Talonaxe as acting leader, Twilight Highlands, any later war (Cataclysm and on).
- Falstad's views on the Bronzebeards beyond friendly rivalry (the wiki's material is later).
- Gossip verbatim (Talonaxe's "can't all get what we want", Thulfram's "make this inn your home").

## Voices
- Falstad: loud, proud, brogue; wants to fight trolls.
- Talonaxe: gruff, would rather be flying.
- Fraggar: solemn, partly blind shaman, hears the land.
- Thulfram: jovial innkeeper.

## Authored, not canon (prejudices, 0.80.0)

- Talonaxe: dislikes gnomes for their machines (`prejudice`, race Gnome); a gnome's flying contraption over the Peak scattered three gryphons last summer, and one came back with a cut wing.
