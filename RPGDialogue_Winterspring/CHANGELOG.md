# RPG Dialogue: Winterspring (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.8.0 (2026-11-16)
- New people: Rivern Frostwind, Salfa, Ranshalla, Haleh, Lorax and Himmik.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-16)
- Everlook: Legacki, Felnok Steelspring, Jessica Redpath, Malyfous Darkhammer, Storm Shadowhoof, Yugrek and Maethrya; Vizzie, Umi and Gregor have more to say.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-15)
- Replies for the classes and races these people would really react to (warriors, hunters, rogues, mages, warlocks, druids, shamans, paladins; every race of the faction, and the Skyborne), each leading somewhere.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Every person here writes a first impression in your diary after you first talk (the journal's People page shows it too).

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.2.2 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: Everlook, Donova and Mau'ari now talk in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments. Donova keeps her burr and Mau'ari her island speech and third person, as connected talk rather than scraps.

## 1.2.1 (unreleased)
- Everlook's "another bear" is said only to bears.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)
- Depth: Vizzie, Umi, Gregor (a reply for the Forsaken), Donova and Mau'ari.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits bring personal lines (habits, worries, running jokes), and some NPCs get night-time lines. Vizzie, Mau'ari.

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
