# Winterspring  (keys: vizzie, umi, gregor, donova, mauari)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Everlook (Steamwheedle Cartel trading post at Winterspring's crossroads; no formal leader; Argent Dawn sends Gregor Greystone; Everlook Bruiser guards; Classic NPCs Gregor Greystone, Jessica Redpath, Storm Shadowhoof)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Innkeeper_Vizzie (female goblin innkeeper in Everlook; afraid of insects; playful)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Umi_Rumplesnicker (female goblin engineering trainer in Everlook; playful, whimsical, scatterbrained, mischievous; Are We There, Yeti? (three parts): build a Mechanical Yeti from gathered parts and frighten goblin friends with it; A Yeti of Your Own for engineers)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gregor_Greystone (male human, Argent Dawn representative in Everlook, reports to Argent Officer Pureheart; both factions; The Everlook Report, Duke Nicholas Zverenhoff)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Argent_Dawn (neutral order against the Scourge and the Legion, not political; open to all races of both factions; Light's Hope Chapel; agents in Everlook; Argent Officer Pureheart, Duke Nicholas Zverenhoff; Tyrosus left the Silver Hand over what became the Scarlet Crusade)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Donova_Snowden (female dwarf by a hot spring near Frostfire Hot Springs; neutral; friendly, hints at Winterspring's secrets; Winterfall Firewater, Falling to Corruption, Mystery Goo, Toxic Horrors, High Chief Winterfall, Words of the High Chief, It's a Secret to Everybody and others)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Linken (Donova Snowden tempers Linken's sword)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Witch_Doctor_Mau'ari (female jungle troll witch doctor in Winterspring; neutral; turns E'ko from Winterspring's creatures into juju and buffs; Cache of Mau'ari gives the charm for collecting E'ko; A Little Luck, Luck Be With You; confident, playful, mystical, folksy dialect)

## Their quests
- Vizzie: none. Umi: Are We There, Yeti?, A Yeti of Your Own.
- Gregor: The Everlook Report, Duke Nicholas Zverenhoff.
- Donova: Winterfall Firewater, Falling to Corruption, Mystery Goo, Toxic Horrors, High Chief Winterfall, Words of the High Chief, It's a Secret to Everybody.
- Mau'ari: Cache of Mau'ari, A Little Luck, Luck Be With You.
