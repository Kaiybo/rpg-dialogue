## Their quests
- None of their own. Chal sends would-be riders to Rivern Frostwind.

## Identity / voice
- Blixxrak · goblin man · chatty pitchman; frostbite priced by the toe.
- Nixxrak · goblin man · gruffer, proud of tested steel; "boss", "pal".
- Xizzer Fizzbolt · goblin man · eager tinker, one eyebrow, explosions as research costs.
- Gogo · goblin woman · cheerful gossip, "sweetheart", "hon"; sells nothing.
- Jack Sterling · human man · jovial, wry southerner who never made his fortune.
- Lyranne Feathersong · night elf woman · gentle, unhurried; cool to the Horde but feeds them.
- Syurana · night elf woman · precise; patient disapproval of Everlook.
- Chal Fairwind · night elf woman · calm, watchful, speaks softly for the sabers' sake.
- Sar Browneye · tauren woman · calm hunter far from the grass; Earth Mother imagery.
- Rilan Howard · Forsaken man · dry clerk; the cold suits him.
- Vi'el · imp, male · shrill, greedy, cannot stop buying; afraid of his warlock.

## Authored, not canon
- Blixxrak, Nixxrak and Wixxrak as kin who share custom (suggested only by the names); the shared shop and split rent.
- Nixxrak leaves armour in the yard overnight to test it. Xizzer dried powder by the stove.
- Gogo lives on generous friends; Kezzig is shown still in Everlook and still courting Lunnix (his move to Silithus is later and not mentioned).
- Jack came north on a goblin's promise of easy money.
- Lyranne's supply route through Felwood; Syurana's view of the goblins.
- Chal watches the wild sabers on the east side and counts pelts.
- Sar and Rilan share a camp: she came to see the frostsabers, he was sent by the Undercity to see how the Alliance gets its mounts. The wiki gives neither a purpose.
- Vi'el: the gorge is called Darkwhisper Gorge here (its Classic name; "Frostwhisper" is the later rename). His unnamed warlock master.
