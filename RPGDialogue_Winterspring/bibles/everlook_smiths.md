# Everlook's weapon masters  (npcs/everlook_smiths.lua; keys kilram, seril, lilith)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kilram (jungle troll, "I am Kilram, master of the axe."), https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Snakestone_of_the_Shadow_Huntress (Vosh'gajin, the scar "love tap"; axesmith; Dawn's Edge recipe)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Seril_Scourgebane (human, "Back off or I'll cut you in half."), https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Corruption_(blacksmithing) (Black Guard Swordsmith, Stratholme; "a sword is always the best choice")
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lilith_the_Lithe (Forsaken; dancing and threatening; "Don't get too close..."), https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sweet_Serenity (Crimson Hammersmith's apron; choosing the hammer closes sword and axe)
- Their Cataclysm quests (Kilram's Boast, Seril's Boast, Hammer Time, deliveries) are not used.

## Their quests
- Kilram: Snakestone of the Shadow Huntress (revenge; trades his knowledge for it).
- Seril: Corruption (curt because the Black Guard stole the plans).
- Lilith: Sweet Serenity (wants the Crusade's best hammer in a Forsaken's hands).

## Identity
- Kilram: jungle troll dialect ("mon", "da"), proud and jovial; loves the rivalry.
- Seril: cold, blunt Lordaeron survivor.
- Lilith: dry, theatrical Forsaken; "darling"; measures how close you stand.

## Authored, not canon
- Seril earned "Scourgebane" cutting his way out of his risen village; once sold blades in Stratholme's market; won't name his dead.
- Lilith relearned to walk/dance after waking; never dances in Undercity; the Crusade burned people she knew.
- Kilram came north "for one winter"; the three once worked together on a Brotherhood order for Malyfous and didn't speak for a month.
