## Identity
Harlo Wigglesworth · Everlook, Winterspring · neutral (Steamwheedle town) · goblin, male · quest giver
Personality: chatty, cheerful, nosy, generous with news
Speech: quick, teasing, keeps the best part back.
Knows and cares about: everyone's business; Winna; Jaron's story.
Default attitudes: equal curiosity for Horde and Alliance.
Never says: anything he doesn't think is fun to pass on.
