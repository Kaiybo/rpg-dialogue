# Winterspring strangers  (npcs/winterspring_strangers.lua; keys umbranse, natheril)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Umbranse_the_Spiritspeaker (Classic: human medium northeast of Everlook, gave Studies in Spirit Speaking to Gregor Greystone of the Argent Dawn). His Cataclysm turn (Mazthoril, hoarding essences) is not used and not foreshadowed beyond his curiosity.
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Natheril_Raincaller (night elf, general goods, Starfall Village)

## Their quests
- Umbranse: none of his own in Classic; tied to Gregor's Duke Nicholas Zverenhoff chain through the book.
- Natheril: none (vendor).

## Identity
- Umbranse: soft-spoken, polite, very curious about the dead and about what the player has seen.
- Natheril: gentle, worried night elf shopkeeper; keeps a kettle on.

## Authored, not canon
- Umbranse's sources (Highborne in Kel'Theril and Mazthoril, unfinished sentinels, "something older" he won't call); his questions about the grey country.
- Natheril's supplies come through Timbermaw Hold; the debate about buying from Everlook.
