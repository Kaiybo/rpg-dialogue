# RPG Dialogue: Desolace (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.16.0 (2026-11-16)
- Horde priests are noticed: 5 people greet a Forsaken or troll priest as one and have something to ask and argue about. Some also gain lines for Horde mages, warlocks or rogues where they had none.

## 1.15.0 (2026-11-16)
- Rogues, mages and warlocks are noticed: named people here greet them as such, with a question to ask and something to argue about. No new files. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.43.0.

## 1.14.0 (2026-11-16)
- Horde shaman get their own conversation with 5 people, among them the Gizeltons and Rexxar.

## 1.13.0 (2026-11-16)
- Druids are noticed: nine people at Nijel's Point, Shadowprey and on the roads greet a druid as one and have something to ask and argue about.

## 1.10.0 (2026-11-15)
- Replies for the classes and races these people would really react to (warriors, hunters, rogues, mages, warlocks, druids, shamans, paladins; every race of the faction, and the Skyborne), each leading somewhere.

## 1.9.0 (2026-11-15)
- Every person here writes a first impression in your diary after you first talk (the journal's People page shows it too).

## 1.8.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-15)

- Takata on the Burning Blade, Maurin Bonesplitter, how demons get a foothold, why the Alliance's opinion matters, and the post he won't leave; Nataka on the Kodo Graveyard, his brother at Freewind, Bloodbelly fish and the centaur.
- Replies for who you are: warlocks, Forsaken, orc mages (Takata), tauren, fishers, players who've met Cliffwatcher Longhorn (Nataka).
- Fix: Takata's Alliance Relations line (Keldran's worry that the Alliance will think the Horde made pacts with the Legion, not bargaining with a centaur clan).
