## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg pages of the same names (via `tools/npc.py`), Classic ids 11438, 6019, 12277, 12031, 12045, 12028, 12027, 12033, 8153, 13699.
- Bibbly F'utzbuckle: goblin quest giver at Kormek's Hut; he dances. Hornizz Brimbuzzle: goblin (Ratchet) with a small camp at the southwest end of the Kodo Graveyard; wants ghost-o-plasm from the Valley of Bones to sell to the Magram; sister Melizza captured by the Maraudine. Melizza Brimbuzzle: goblin (Gadgetzan) in a cage in the northern Valley of Spears; escort.
- Shadowprey Village vendors: Hae'Wilani (Axecrafter), Lah'Mawhani (Trade Goods), Mai'Lahii (Fishing Supplies), Tukk (general goods: wood, flint), Wulan (Cooking Supplies).
- Narv Hidecrafter: tauren leatherworking trainer at Ghost Walker Post.
- Selendra: dryad of the Cenarion Circle on Sar'theris Strand south of Shadowprey, with several of Marandis' Sisters; came from Stonetalon with her brother Keeper Marandis to end Princess Theradras and recover Zaetar's body. The Shadowprey trolls tolerate her.
- Existing pack: Smeed Scrabblescrew and Willow (Kormek's Hut), Felgur, Maurin, Gurda Wildmane (Ghost Walker Post), Taiga Wisemane, Drulzegar Skraghook, Lui'Mala, Malux, Innkeeper Sikewa, Thalon (Shadowprey), Keeper Marandis (Nijel's Point), the Gizelton caravan.

## Identity / voice
- Bibbly F'utzbuckle · goblin man · jaunty, never still; prices everything; "sacred is stock that hasn't been priced yet".
- Hornizz Brimbuzzle · goblin man · harried schemer; fast talk that stops dead when his sister comes up.
- Melizza Brimbuzzle · goblin woman · sharp, furious rather than frightened; "sweetheart"; the one with the nerve.
- Mai'Lahii · troll woman · laughing, easy; feet over the dock.
- Hae'Wilani · troll woman · fierce, proud of her edges; headhunter's daughter.
- Lah'Mawhani · troll woman · sly; watches hands, not faces.
- Tukk · troll man · lazy on the surface, old raider underneath.
- Wulan · troll man · jolly cook; everything hot.
- Narv Hidecrafter · tauren man · grave, reverent; kodo hides as a duty to the dead.
- Selendra · dryad · earnest, quick and bright over an old grief; talks more than her brother would like.
- Trolls keep the light lilt (mon, de/dat/dis, "I be") on whole sentences.

## Authored, not canon
- Bibbly's buyers (carvers, alchemists, a button-maker in Ratchet) and his arithmetic.
- Hornizz's first crew died in the Valley of Bones (the quest's dead goblins); he sent their pay home. Melizza keeps books in Gadgetzan and went south to find buyers; she told the Maraudine she was poisonous.
- Mai'Lahii's mother fished the Echo Isles; her running quarrel with Wulan over clam meat.
- Hae'Wilani's headhunter father; fish oil against the salt. Lah'Mawhani reading hands. Tukk rowed with Sen'jin's boats. Wulan cooked for a war band.
- Narv learned in Mulgore; takes hides only from newly fallen kodo after rites; his account of how Ghost Walker Post got its name.
- Selendra's view that Theradras "loved him the way stone loves"; the naga wary of dryads.
