# Desolace: more folk (keys: pariah, willow, aboda, luimala, malux, muuran, maxton, shelgrayn)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg: Centaur Pariah (exiled for heresy; wanders east of Gelkis Village and south of Mannoroc Coven; gossip: "I need no home to know I still have a place in this world... They fight amongst one another, and for what? Scraps left over by vultures... lands infested by the Legion"; "The centaur should be proud of their heritage--it is just as noble as any Night elf's or orc's". Classic: The Pariah's Instructions: enter Maraudon, take the Amulet of Spirits from the Nameless Prophet, join it with the spirits of the five first Khans into the Amulet of Union; "only the most devout are ever allowed to enter"; "I hope when next we meet, it will be on much different terms").
- Willow (Forsaken at Kormek's Hut, NE of the Kodo Graveyard; claims to be a researcher for science, secretly Twilight's Hammer. Classic: Twisted Evils, theradric crystal carvings from the crystal caverns of Terramok in Maraudon, "relics of the old gods... These powers have been too long at rest"; progress "Don't ask questions!"; completion "Ha ha ha! I knew you'd be back... So easy to convince").
- Aboda <Stable Master> (tauren, Shadowprey Village), Lui'Mala <Fishing Trainer> and Malux <Skinning Trainer> (jungle trolls, Shadowprey), Muuran <Superior Macecrafter> (tauren, Ghost Walker Post), Maxton Strang <Mail Armor Merchant> (human of Stormwind, Nijel's Point), Shelgrayn <Stable Master> (night elf, Nijel's Point). Roster only.

## Their quests
- Pariah: The Pariah's Instructions (TODO_QUEST). Willow: Twisted Evils (TODO_QUEST). The rest: none; BUSINESS.

## Voices
- **Centaur Pariah.** Fierce, proud, bitter exile; heritage and unity; honest that his people raided the tauren.
- **Willow.** Smooth, sly, "darling"; a scholar's front with slips ("notes to burn... to write"); never names the Twilight's Hammer or the Old Gods outright.
- **Aboda.** Gentle; sings to the beasts; trust over fear.
- **Lui'Mala.** Jovial Darkspear, changes his mind about the Echo Isles daily; light troll lilt.
- **Malux.** Dry, blunt troll; scorpid hides; hands off the Kodo Graveyard.
- **Muuran.** Proud, gruff; maces don't care what they hit; melts Burning Blade weapons.
- **Maxton Strang.** Wry Stormwind armourer; sand in the rings; came for coin, stays for the soldiers.
- **Shelgrayn.** Calm, formal kaldorei; the Legion doesn't stop at Ashenvale's edge.

## Authored, not canon
Willow's falling-out with the apothecaries; Aboda's songs; Lui'Mala's daily change of heart; Malux's scorpid-tail knife; Muuran's leather-wrapped hafts; Maxton arriving with Pentigast's posting; Shelgrayn answering the call with Talendria.

## Never
Willow's true allegiance stated plainly; anything after Year 1 (no Cataclysm Desolace, no Khan Leh'Prah).

## Added later (keys: azore, hornizz, bibbly, narv, lahmawhani, mailahii, wulan)

### Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg (Classic parts only): Azore Aldamort <The Argent Dawn> (human mage, tower at Ethel Rethor; Sceptre of Light, Book of the Ancients). Hornizz Brimbuzzle (goblin, south-west of the Kodo Graveyard; Ghost-o-plasm Round Up; sister Melizza held by the Maraudine). Bibbly F'utzbuckle (goblin at Kormek's Hut; Bone Collector; he dances). Narv Hidecrafter <Leatherworking Trainer> (Ghost Walker Post). Lah'Mawhani <Trade Supplies>, Mai'Lahii <Fishing Supplies>, Wulan <Cooking Supplies> (Shadowprey Village).

### Their quests
- Azore: Sceptre of Light (from the Burning Blade seer at Thunder Axe Fortress), Book of the Ancients (Lord Kragaru, Ranazjar Isle). His Forever quests are not written here.
- Hornizz: Ghost-o-plasm Round Up (ghost magnets, Valley of Bones, Magram spectres); ends Get Me Out of Here!.
- Bibbly: Bone Collector (kodo bones from the graveyard; apparitions).
- Narv, Lah'Mawhani, Mai'Lahii, Wulan: none.

### Voices
- **Azore Aldamort.** Learned, guarded, dry; studies the hand as well as the knife.
- **Hornizz Brimbuzzle.** Goblin patter until his sister comes up, then only frightened.
- **Bibbly F'utzbuckle.** Jittery, gleeful, dances to drown out the kodo ghosts.
- **Narv Hidecrafter.** Deliberate, reverent; speaks to each hide; kodo only from Gurda's hand.
- **Lah'Mawhani.** Sharp, teasing Darkspear trader; bargaining is the fun of it.
- **Mai'Lahii.** Easy, superstitious; a charm on every line against the naga.
- **Wulan.** Motherly, firm tauren cook among trolls; rations the pepper.

### Authored, not canon
Azore's doubts about the Dawn's priorities; Hornizz's skinless kodo; the skull on Bibbly's doorstep; Narv's words over a hide; Mai'Lahii's charms; Wulan's hidden store under the inn and her friendship with Sikewa.

## Added later (keys: tukk, haewilani)

### Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg (Classic parts only): Tukk <General Goods> (jungle troll, male, Shadowprey Village; the page has a Classic section; there is no wiki page for "Tukaw", so the Classic name Tukk is used). Hae'Wilani <Axecrafter> (jungle troll, female, axe vendor at Shadowprey Village).

### Their quests
- none: vendors, built from role, place and neighbours.

### Voices
- **Tukk.** Easy, sly Darkspear; reads a traveller by what has run out; homesick for Sen'jin's smoked fish.
- **Hae'Wilani.** Exacting, fierce; axes and nothing else; complains about the forge and would not move it. Consistent with Lah'Mawhani's line that she buys flux and says it is not hot enough.

### Authored, not canon
Tukk's lost season against Lah'Mawhani, his torches cut open on the road, the waterskin he leaves for Drulzegar; Hae'Wilani's mother on the Echo Isles, the driftwood charcoal, and the axe she made for Drulzegar.
