## Their quests
- Drulzegar: Other Fish to Fry (start/finish). Wants revenge; fears his own guilt; never satisfied.

## Identity
- Drulzegar Skraghook · jungle troll male · fisherman (former) · Darkspear. Bitter, haunted. Speech: troll dialect ("mon", "de", "dem", "me" for my) in whole sentences.
- Innkeeper Sikewa · tauren female. Warm, wry, knows everyone.
- Thalon · tauren male · Wind Rider Master. Laconic, patient.
Never says: Jinar'Zillen's later quests; anything after Year 1.

## Agenda
Drulzegar: more dead naga. Sikewa: a home for travellers. Thalon: keep riders on the dock.

## Reliability
Drulzegar is honest about his guilt.

## What they notice   (2–3 things)
Drulzegar: trolls, shaman, naga kills. Sikewa: trolls, tauren, weary, drunk. Thalon: Skyborne, weary.
