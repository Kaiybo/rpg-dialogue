## Their quests

## Voices
- **Taiga Wisemane** (tauren elder): grave, patient; speaks of demons like weather coming.
- **Vark Battlescar** (tauren warrior): blunt, brooding, plain; hates the stairs.
- **Drulzegar Skraghook** (troll): bitter, haunted, troll speech; vengeance as the only quiet.
- **Jinar'Zillen** (troll): cheerful idler with a philosophy of doing nothing; troll speech.
- **Innkeeper Sikewa** (tauren woman): warm, motherly ("little one"), fish stew.

## Authored, not canon
- Taiga holds the Hand only until he can give it to the earth.
- Vark's big scar from a Grimtotem who objected to tauren fighting beside orcs; he spared him.
- Drulzegar's crew: five Darkspear from Sen'jin Village, his cousin Zin'ka among them; he talks to them from the cliff at night; Jinar'Zillen told him the bubble secret.
- Jinar'Zillen's hammock; Sikewa makes him carry the shellfish to her pot himself.
- Bloodbelly fish helps you breathe under water for a while (the fish's in-game use, phrased as his sales pitch).
