## Voices
- **Corporal Melkins** (human): earnest young soldier repeating his orders; homesick for Stormwind rain.
- **Talendria** (night elf): cool, watchful, quietly angry at Maraudon.
- **Baritanas Skyriver** (night elf): wry exile who misses the trees.
- **Gurda Wildmane** (tauren shaman): calm and cunning, gentle with kodo, ruthless with centaur.

## Authored, not canon

- Talendria: the Dream goes dark around Maraudon; some of her sisters would wall it off.
- Baritanas: hippogryphs hate the dust and complain every morning.
- Gurda: the kodo spirits complain the Gizelton goblins drive their kin too hard, and she has "had words" with the goblins.
