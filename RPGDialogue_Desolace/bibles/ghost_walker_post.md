## Identity
- Gurda Wildmane · tauren female · shaman, leader of Ghost Walker Post · Horde. Shrewd, solemn, cold strategist, gentle singer for dying kodo.
- Harnor · tauren male · Food & Drink. Cheerful, earnest, worries whether you've eaten.
- Kireena · tauren female · Trade Goods. Quiet, shrewd, unsentimental buyer of camp goods.
Relationships: Takata Steelblade, Felgur Twocuts, Nataka Longhorn, Maurin (post); Warug, Uthek (clans); Roon Wildmane (Gurda's clan; see shadowprey_elders.md).
Never says: Maraudon's fate before it's cleared; Cataclysm events (Karnum's Glade, Furien's Post, the Magram/Gelkis later politics).

## Agenda
Gurda: keep the clans divided, always lean on the losing one; spare foals. Harnor: keep everyone fed. Kireena: thicken thin trade.

## Reliability
Gurda is candid about her methods. Her account of centaur origins is hearsay from the druids (canon: Zaetar and Theradras).

## What they notice   (2–3 things)
Gurda: tauren, shaman, Magram/Gelkis standing. Harnor: Forsaken, orcs, worn gear. Kireena: skinners/leatherworkers, tailors, trolls.
