# Desolace, Forever-only quest givers  (keys: akarai, umarak)

NPCs added in World of Warcraft: Forever (patch 1.60.1). The wiki has no quest text for their quests; everything beyond the sources is **authored, not canon**.

## Their quests
- Aka'rai, Hate the Hatefury (gives/turn-in): from the title, cull the Hatefury satyrs of Sargeron. Reveals a Darkspear's hatred of bargains with dark powers.
- Umarak, Khan Hratha (gives/turn-in): kill the Maraudine khan. Reveals an old grudge and a clan pride forced to ask outsiders for help.
- Note: the Gelkis and Magram quests share the title "Khan Hratha" (Uthek, Warug). TODO_QUEST matches by title, so Umarak's quest lines may also fire for those; they are written so that they still read true (Hratha is dead either way).

## Identity and voices
- Aka'rai: fierce, "mon"; satyrs were elves who sold themselves; links the Hatefury to the Burning Blade at Thunder Axe Fortress; left Sen'jin because he couldn't bear to look at the Echo Isles. Notices warlocks, night elves, shamans.
- Umarak: proud, bitter, old; calls the player "outsider"; despises orcs and tauren for driving the Kolkar out, but Hratha is the closer enemy. Notices tauren, orcs, Maraudine kills.

## Invented details (authored, not canon)
- Aka'rai came to Desolace from Sen'jin; drank twice to the satyrs' quiet.
- Umarak's sons died in a Maraudine raid on Kolkar herds while Hratha laughed; the Kolkar leave the old behind ("the herd moves on"); a new, timid Maraudine khan after Hratha.
