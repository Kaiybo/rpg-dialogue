## Their quests
- Taiga: Hand of Iruxos (start/finish) — fears demons entering the world.
- Vark: Vyletongue Corruption (start/finish) — wants Maraudon's plants healed.
- Roon: Hunting in Stranglethorn (start) — wants to hunt with Hemet again; enmity forgotten in the wilds.

## Identity
- Taiga Wisemane · tauren male · elder. Grave, steady, hates demons; uneasy using the Hand.
- Vark Battlescar · tauren male · old warrior. Blunt, restless; wants to grow something before he dies.
- Roon Wildmane · tauren male · hunter. Genial, wistful storyteller.
Relationships: each other; Drulzegar, Sikewa, Thalon (Shadowprey); Gurda Wildmane (Roon's clan); Hemet (Roon).
Never says: Rokaro (TBC replacement for Rexxar); Hemet's race (the Classic quest calls him a dwarf, Hemet is human: Roon never names a race); anything after Year 1.

## Agenda
Taiga: seal the portals, then bury the Hand. Vark: heal the caverns. Roon: reach Hemet.

## Reliability
Taiga admits uncertainty about Mannoroc Coven; Roon embellishes.

## What they notice   (2–3 things)
Taiga: warlocks, orcs, holy casters. Vark: druids, tauren, Noxxious kills. Roon: hunters, pets, dwarves.

## Topics
background, advice, burning_blade (Taiga), _none.

## Authored, not canon
- Taiga: got the demon pick from a cultist the trolls caught on the coast road (sent to Orgrimmar at his request); keeps the Hand in a chest under his bed; Mannoroc Coven's name tied to Mannoroth by the Blade's belief, not his knowledge (Mannoroth died in Ashenvale).
- Vark: twenty years fighting centaur; named for a Kolkar spear scar; the vial's coating made by tauren shaman (Talendria credits the Circle's druids: they disagree, or both make it); went grey on hate; would work beside the night elves in Maraudon.
- Roon: the white kodo bull tracked nine days that Hemet chose not to shoot; Hemet taught him to read a trail backwards; Roon taught Hemet to thank the beast; Gurda is "Wildmane, like me"; troll sea stories (great turtle, sharks).
