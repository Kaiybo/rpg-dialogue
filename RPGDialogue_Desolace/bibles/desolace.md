## Their quests
- Pentigast: Strange Alliance, Brutal Politics. Marandis: Corruption of Earth and Seed.
- Kreldig: Reclaimers' Business in Desolace, The Karnitol Shipwreck, Reagents for Reclaimers Inc.
- Nataka: Family Tree, Catch of the Day.
- Anton: Brother Anton, Down the Scarlet Path. Takata: Befouled by Satyr, The Corrupter, Alliance Relations.

## Never
- Anything Cataclysm or later: Anton's corpse in the Valley of Bones, the Alliance campaign, Furien.
- Anton never learns or says what the Crusade has become; the player may hint at it, he deflects.
- Zaetar named by Marandis (he says "one dear to the Circle"); Theradras only at level 45 and up.
- Gossip verbatim (Pentigast's "many stakes... unstable", Marandis's "lesser being", Kreldig's "a lot going on").

## Voices
- Pentigast: calculating officer; divide and hold.
- Marandis: formal, proud keeper (authored from his gossip's tone).
- Kreldig: busy, friendly salvage dwarf.
- Anton: zealous, sincere, a little out of date.
- Lyshaerya: calm night elf innkeeper (authored).
- Takata: hard satyr-hunter.
- Nataka: easygoing, fond of a grey land; the less worried brother (authored contrast).
