## Depth rewrite: Ghost Walker Post (Horde towns pass, authored on sourced quests)
Sources read for this pass (warcraft.wiki.gg, Classic sections): Takata_Steelblade, Nataka_Longhorn, Ghost_Walker_Post, Alliance_Relations (2-4), Befouled_by_Satyr, The_Corrupter (4, 5), Family_Tree_(Thousand_Needles), Catch_of_the_Day, Kodo_Graveyard, Desolace_(Classic).
- Takata (Classic gossip): the Burning Blade are members of the Horde who gave their loyalty to the demons and care little for Thrall's vision of the Horde's future in Kalimdor. Alliance Relations: Keldran fears the Alliance will wrongly conclude the Horde made pacts with the Legion; Takata dislikes aiding the centaur indirectly and needing the warlocks, but does his duty; Maurin Bonesplitter (Forsaken) handles the Blade's warlock ways while Takata handles the other demonic threats; he hopes the Warchief will let them go home "before the seasons change". Befouled by Satyr: the Hatefury satyrs to the northeast; evil gains a foothold through a weakened spirit, a desire for power, a thirst for revenge, or the appeal of final justice. The Corrupter: an apparition of Lord Azrethoc outmatched Maurin; Takata would disgrace his clan by leaving his post for his own honor; Azrethoc and Jugkar Grim'rod at the Mannoroc Coven; "I sometimes wish it was only the centaur we had to deal with."
- Nataka: Cliffwatcher's report comes a year after his last letter, about unusual centaur activity; Catch of the Day: the smell of cooked fish missing at the post; Jinar'Zillen at Shadowprey Village; Bloodbelly fish, his favourite; "my hoof tingles". The Kodo Graveyard is the kodo's final resting place, and kodo apparitions rise among the bones. The centaur tribes (Kolkar, Gelkis, Magram, Maraudine) fight each other as much as anyone.
- Fixed in this pass: Takata's Alliance Relations line (Keldran's worry about the Alliance's view of the Horde, not bargaining with a centaur clan).
- Authored, not canon: Takata's view of an orc mage and of Maurin's other studies; Nataka's teasing of his brother; directions to Jinar'Zillen.
