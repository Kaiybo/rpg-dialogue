## Their quests
- Melkins: Centaur Bounty (Alliance, start/finish). Believes in it, dislikes it.
- Talendria: Vyletongue Corruption (Alliance, start/finish). Angered by a satyr in a holy place.

## Identity
- Corporal Melkins · human male · garrison corporal, keeps the ledgers · Stormwind. Earnest, tired, homesick.
- Talendria · night elf female · Darnassus. Intense, sorrowful, cold about satyrs.
- Baritanas Skyriver · night elf male · Hippogryph Master. Cheerful, a little vain; loves Desolace from above.
Relationships: Captain Pentigast, Brother Anton, Kreldig Ungor, Keeper Marandis, Innkeeper Lyshaerya.
Never says: Cataclysm-era Desolace (Thargad's Camp, Ethel Rethor, Rerouted!); Varian's return.

## Agenda
Melkins: hold the Point, honour the dead, go home. Talendria: heal Maraudon. Baritanas: keep birds out of the war.

## What they notice   (2–3 things)
Melkins: humans, priests, dwarves, Kolkar kills. Talendria: night elves, druids, warlocks. Baritanas: night elves, gnomes, Skyborne, weary.

## Authored, not canon
- Melkins: Elwynn-born near Goldshire, mother keeps sheep and writes about the dead ones; accepted a dwarf's bag of kodo tails once; two patrols killed in the pass last winter; eleven graves behind the barracks he carved himself; Pentigast writes to Stormwind weekly for men and is refused; distrusts Anton for "counting doubts".
- Talendria: came with the keeper's people; went into Maraudon once, sick for a month, dreams of the purple caves; vial coating from moonwell water and a Moonglade plant (Vark credits tauren shaman); her ambivalence about the centaur's guilt.
- Baritanas: named for "the river of stars"; flew the Darkshore coast before; refuses to send birds out with archers.
