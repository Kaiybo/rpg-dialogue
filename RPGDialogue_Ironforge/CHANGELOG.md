# RPG Dialogue: Ironforge (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.4.0 (unreleased)
- Depth rewrite for all sixteen NPCs: every conversation is now a hub of subjects to explore, two or three steps deep, with "what else?" lines when you come back to it, and each NPC answers Ask about their story and their advice.
- King Magni: the Dark Iron and Madoran's statue, the Reserve away at the front, his brothers (Muradin and the sword he forged for the Silver Hand in his grief, Brann missing for months), why Ironforge digs for its past, the gnomes and the tram. Priests and paladins can ask how the Light came to Ironforge; he reacts if you've met the Whitebeards or carried Senir's report, if you went under his hall for Farsen (Old Ironforge Incursion), or if you came out of Lordaeron.
- High Tinker Mekkatorque: the fall of Gnomeregan and the five years of secret war, the tram and its rats, Thermaplugg and the lenses, life in Tinker Town; priests can ask what the Light might do for the sick, gnomes whether they'll ever go home, engineers talk shop.
- Archesonus, Magellas, Stormpike and Senator Stonehallow: the War of the Three Hammers, the stonewrought method's Dark Iron inventor, Muradin's lost expedition, Brann's empty chair, Ironband's idols, the missing blastpowder and Saean, ciphers from Southshore, Baelog in Uldaman, what wakes the troggs, Farsen's camp above the quarry.
- Brandur, John Turner and Jordan Stilwell: Tiza and Muiredon, how dwarves came to the Light, the orphans and the linen, John's "monthly" visits, Verigan, Pyrewood and the hammer left at Silverlaine's keep, Daphne on the Westfall coast. Jordan's hammer lines now key on The Test of Righteousness (the real quest title).
- Dun Morogh: Eric's camping lesson, Sten's mail and the class runes (priests, and Forever's dwarf shamans), Grelin's apprentice and the King's letter, Senir's careful distance from Frostmane Hold and Senator Redstone, Ragnar's ribs, trapper and missing brother, Belm's Lazlo and the trainers under his roof. Belm's welcome for arrivals from Coldridge no longer uses a Cataclysm quest.
- Voice pass over every line in the pack: whole, spoken sentences instead of clipped fragments; the dwarves keep their burr and the gnomes their chatter.
