# The Great Forge's crafts (Ironforge pack, 0.39.0)

Wiki infoboxes and gossip read 2026-11-16 (warcraft.wiki.gg API). Later-expansion daily quests (cooking, Ahn'Qiraj) are not used.

# Bengus Deepforge  (key: if_bengus)
## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Bengus_Deepforge (read): male dwarf blacksmithing trainer at the center of the Great Forge; gossip about not coming just to stay warm (paraphrased in the greeting, not quoted).
## Their quests
- none: trainer.
## Identity
Gruff, proud, exacting. Authored, not canon: sends apprentices back four times; Deepforges beside the forge for generations.
## What they notice
Craft: blacksmiths.
- Night elves (0.94.0 work): Bronzebeard pride; a Darnassian envoy told him the kaldorei need no such fires while wearing his buckle. Grudgingly admits Mathiel's blade was good. A night elf can win him round by asking to be taught (`[Persuade]`, npcFlag `bengus_soft`).

# Nissa Firestone  (key: if_nissa)
## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Nissa_Firestone (read): female dwarf first aid trainer, Ironforge Physician; gossip points to Doctor Gustaf VanHowzen in Theramore. Classic: Expert First Aid book from Deneb Walker (Stromgarde).
## Their quests
- Alliance Trauma (gives): sends experts to Theramore. Not tied to a line yet.
## Identity
Brisk, compassionate. Authored, not canon: Second War field medic; set a Dark Iron prisoner's arm.
## What they notice
Weary: broken armor.

# Gimble Thistlefuzz  (key: if_gimble)
## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gimble_Thistlefuzz (read): male gnome enchanting trainer, upper floor of Thistlefuzz Arcanery; gossip is suspicious ("Who are you? What do you want from me?"). Tilli Thistlefuzz (supplies) is kin.
## Their quests
- none.
## Identity
Anxious, secretive, then talkative. Speech: gnomish, no burr; wards, calibrations, ciphers and exact figures. Authored, not canon: lost ten years of notes in Gnomeregan; keeps three copies.
## What they notice
Arcane: mages and warlocks.
