## Voices
**Foreman Stonebrow (dwarf).** Furious, red-faced, shouting at troggs from a safe distance; proud of Gol'Bolar granite.
**Felix Whindlebolt (gnome).** Nervous, apologetic, gentle; talks too much when frightened.

## Authored, not canon
- Stonebrow: two miners dead and one maimed in the trogg rising; Gol'Bolar granite in "the new stair in the Commons"; forty years of stone dust clouding his eyes and a healer's warning about his heart.
- Felix: the Gnomeregan Institute of Applied Gear-Work, Second Wing (invented name); fell asleep by the road and woke to the Frostmane carrying off his things; precision Institute bolts collected since apprenticeship; dwarves offering him ale for breakfast.
