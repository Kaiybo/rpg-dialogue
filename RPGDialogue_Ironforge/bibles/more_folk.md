# More Ironforge and Dun Morogh folk (npcs/more_folk.lua, npcs/more_folk2.lua)

## Sources
warcraft.wiki.gg pages via `tools/npc.py`, read 2026-11-18: each NPC's own page (infobox, quests, quotes where any). Quests are original-WoW listings, unconfirmed for Forever; all IDs are TODO.

## Their quests
- **Deliana** (High Seat): An Earnest Proposition, Just Compensation, In Search of Anthion, Anthion's Parting Words, Bodley's Unfortunate Fate, Saving the Best for Last (the 1.10 dungeon-set upgrade chain). Waiting for an audience with Magni.
- **Gerrig Bonegrip** (Bonegrip's Runes and Dooms, Forlorn Cavern): Knowledge in the Deeps (Lorgalis Manuscript, Blackfathom Deeps), To Ironforge for Yagyin's Digest, The Star, the Hand and the Heart, Forbidden Knowledge. Later-era Twilight's Hammer ties NOT used.
- **Laris Geardawdle** (Library): A Little Slime Goes a Long Way (Un'Goro slimes). Ooze-origin theorist.
- **Muren Stormpike** (Hall of Arms): Muren Stormpike (from Granis Swiftaxe), Vejrek, Tormus Deepforge. Warrior test chain.
- **Tormus Deepforge** (Great Forge): Tormus Deepforge, Ironband's Compound, Grey Iron Weapons. (Supplying the Front / Gearing Redridge are later; not used.)
- **Roetten Stonehammer** (Library, Reclaimers Inc.): Reclaimers' Business in Desolace, The Karnitol Shipwreck, Reagents for Reclaimers Inc.
- **Pilot Longbeard** (Hall of Arms): The Brassbolts Brothers (Shimmering Flats).
- **Artilleryman Sheldonore** (Commons): Arrows Are For Sissies.
- **Belia Thundergranite** (pet trainer): Training the Beast.
- **Beldin Steelgrill** (Steelgrill's Depot): Tools for Steelgrill (ends), A Visitor to Dun Morogh (Forever, to Earthseer Farsen's fire).
- **Tharek Blackstone** (Kharanos toolsmith): Tools for Steelgrill (starts).
- **Thran / Thorgas / Bromos / Solm** (Anvilmar trainers): Simple Rune, Etched Rune, Consecrated Rune, Encrypted Rune (from Sten Stoutarm).
- **Granis Swiftaxe** (Kharanos warrior trainer): sends warriors on to Muren Stormpike.
- No quests used: Alexander Calder, Valgar Highforge, Lieutenant Rotimer, Milli Featherwhistle.

## Voices (one note each)
- **Deliana**: composed, patient human; careful words; honest about coin; quietly braced for bad news about Anthion.
- **Gerrig**: wry, secretive dwarf shopkeeper; defends forbidden reading as "know the enemy"; never says what he wants it for.
- **Laris**: gnome who runs on in excitement; jars, samples, "not not saying it".
- **Muren**: stern Stormpike; clan pride, Alterac; tests warriors, respects the head-on answer.
- **Tormus**: gruff smith of an old forge family; suspicious of Dark Iron spies; "watch the colour, no' the clock".
- **Roetten**: restless salvage salesman; Desolace as gold mine; jokes about payroll.
- **Longbeard**: jovial old pilot; Second War scouting stories; fond of the Brassbolts and Stonegear.
- **Sheldonore**: loud, half-deaf artilleryman; rifles over bows; old wall-gunner from the Second War.
- **Calder**: cold, precise human warlock; speaks of control and power, never warm.
- **Valgar**: senior Silver Hand paladin in the city; gentle, humble, grieving Uther's order.
- **Belia**: warm, brisk pet trainer; greets the beast first; kindness over the firm hand.
- **Rotimer**: crier's voice; earnest Stormpike Guard recruiter; Vanndar and Dun Baldar.
- **Beldin**: proud siege-engine technician; wears out tools; watches the fire on the Tundrid Hills.
- **Tharek**: overworked, grumbling toolsmith; counts every spanner.
- **Milli**: cheerful gnome builder; sells striders only to gnomes or those Gnomeregan trusts (exalted).
- **Granis**: hearty warrior trainer in the distillery's back room; sends the ready ones to Muren.
- **Thran**: gruff, patient first teacher at Anvilmar; survival before glory.
- **Thorgas**: very thick burr ("Wot kin I do fer ye?"); learn in the snow.
- **Bromos**: confident, warm; dwarf-human brotherhood of the Silver Hand.
- **Solm**: sly, principled; honour among thieves; rogues watch the watchers.

## Authored, not canon
Belia and Regnus as cousins (wiki: relatives, unspecified); Tormus's wife; Longbeard's Burning Steppes scouting; Sheldonore's twenty years on the wall guns; Tharek's wife and apprentice; Milli's three years perfecting the stride.

# Missed folk, added later (npcs/more_folk2.lua)

## Sources

## Their quests
- **Lomac Gearstrip** (Tinker Town): An Old Colleague (ends; from Gaxim Rustfizzle in Stonetalon, see the Stonetalon pack), Ineptitude + Chemicals = Fun (4 Minor Mana Potions, 2 Elixirs of Minor Fortitude for a sample of NG-5). Suspicious, gleeful about explosions, respects alchemists.
- **Hands Springsprocket** (Coldridge Pass): Supplies to Tannok (starts). Can't get through the trogg-held pass without a mountaineer escort.
- **Tannok Frosthammer** (Thunderbrew Distillery, Kharanos): Supplies to Tannok (ends). Worried by the lack of word from Anvilmar.
- **Mountaineer Barleybrew** (South Gate Outpost): Stout to Kadrell (after Rejold's New Brew / Shimmer Stout). His brother Rejold brewed the stout.
- none: Bruuk Barleybeard (Bartender, Bruuk's Corner, Military Ward), Dolman Steelfury (Weapon Merchant, Steelfury's Weapon Emporium, Commons), Barim Jurgenstaad (Reagents, Commons), Myra Tyrngaarde (Bread Vendor, walks the Commons), Sraaz (Pie Vendor, walks the Great Forge), Pithwick (Bag Vendor, Fizzlespinner's General Goods), Fizzlebang Booms (Fireworks Vendor, Tinker Town): vendors, built from role, place and neighbours.

## Voices
- **Lomac**: gnome, quick and accusing, laughs at the thought of someone exploding; "Hmm? Hmm?".
- **Hands**: gnome, cheerful and cold, frets about the crate.
- **Tannok**: dwarven burr, hospitable, a worrier; fire and flagon first.
- **Barleybrew**: jolly dwarf sentry, family pride against the Thunderbrews.
- **Bruuk**: gruff ex-soldier barman who hears everything.
- **Dolman**: proud, direct; fits the weapon to the arm.
- **Barim**: dry, careful; asks no questions and loses sleep over it.
- **Myra**: brisk, motherly, "dear"; sells on the move.
- **Sraaz**: anxious, apologetic gnome; percentages about pie.
- **Pithwick**: precise, chatty gnome; capacity, seams, how people pack.
- **Fizzlebang**: exuberant gnome; colour, height, eyebrows.

## Authored, not canon
Lomac's "third workshop"; Hands's half-heated coat; Tannok as kin to Balir Frosthammer and the bar argument over clearing the pass; Barleybrew having trained with Kadrell and his mother's disapproval; Bruuk as a former wall soldier; Grenil Steelfury as Dolman's kinsman at the other counter and their disagreement; Barim's bad batch of powder; Myra's sister and the bread she sells to the Stonefire; Sraaz's circuit and the gryphon; Pithwick's arrows; Fizzlebang's indoor rocket and the display for the refugees.

## What they notice
Lomac: alchemists, gnomes. Hands: new recruits, gnomes. Tannok: new arrivals, worn gear. Barleybrew: drink, dwarves. Bruuk: drink, veterans. Dolman: worn weapons, casters. Barim: mages, warlocks, paladins. Myra: the worn-out, humans. Sraaz: gnomes, the worn-out. Pithwick: tailors, worn gear. Fizzlebang: engineers, dwarves.
