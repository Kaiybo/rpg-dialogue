# Tinker Town (Ironforge pack, 0.35.0 content push)

Four gnomes still working to get home. Wiki pages read 2026-11-15 (warcraft.wiki.gg, raw wikitext). Classic only: Overspark's later roles (Skyfire, Mechagon, Khaz Algar) are not used. WoW: Forever changes nothing here that the wiki records.

---

# Tinkmaster Overspark  (key: overspark)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tinkmaster_Overspark (read): gnomish engineering trainer in Tinker Town; Techbot malfunctioned and Brother Sarno in the Cathedral sent adventurers; receives engineers specializing in gnome engineering and has them sign a lifelong pledge of secrecy; Oglethorpe Obnoticus dislikes him because Overspark and the gnome council banished him for (they believed) releasing the Crowd Pummeler 9-60.
- Quest pages read: Tinkmaster Overspark (quest) ("Techbot has gone rogue! Please, can you help me?"), Save Techbot's Brain! (chain), Gnome Engineering (Ironforge) ("all I do is stand here while our beloved city lies in a cloud of irradiated death"; "forgive my melodrama"; the one true discipline; membership is permanent).

## Their quests
- Tinkmaster Overspark / Save Techbot's Brain!: grief for a helpful machine.
- Gnome Engineering / The Pledge of Secrecy: pride, secrecy, permanence.

## Identity
Proud, melodramatic, grieving, kind to engineers. Speech: gnomish, theatrical, apologizes for drama and continues.
Authored, not canon: reads Techbot's memory core in the evenings; "I built him to like people, and perhaps that was the flaw."

## What they notice
Craft: engineers of 200+; priests (thanks to Brother Sarno).

## Topics
knows, background, advice, gnomeregan
- Night elves (0.94.0 work): they look at gnomes like a toddler with a lit fuse; Darnassus answered his question about Teldrassil's roots with 'Through balance'. The player can point out his own Pledge of Secrecy. A night elf can soften him (`[Persuade]`, npcFlag `overspark_soft`).

# Master Mechanic Castpipe  (key: castpipe)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Master_Mechanic_Castpipe (read): male gnome in Tinker Town.
- Castpipe's Task (read): organizing bands to re-enter Gnomeregan on a data retrieval mission; "eighty-four percent probability" line from the Assembly.
- Data Rescue (read): the prismatic punch card from the Matrix Punchograph 3005-D; white, yellow, blue, red cards through terminals A-C; "We left all the punch cards in the city. It's a catastrophe!"; "vitally, critically, immeasurably important data".

## Their quests
- Castpipe's Task, Data Rescue: anxious about what's lost; proud of procedure.

## Identity
Anxious, list-keeping, earnest. Authored: the data is designs, records, census rolls; "a people is what it knows"; the B terminal sticks.

## What they notice
Craft: tinker-apprentice gnomes; engineers.

# Klockmort Spannerspan  (key: klockmort)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Klockmort_Spannerspan (read): male gnome in Tinker Town; gossip "I'm on quest. A quest! A quest for new alloys and harder metals for the greatest invention of all!!"; friends with Mathiel.
- Quest pages read: Klockmort Spannerspan (Furen Longbeard's notes; "they make my knees go all a-wobbly"; "Good day, or morning or evening. I can't tell from way down here"), Iron Coral (searing coral south of Menethil; "my toes twist"), Klockmort's Creation (Fire Hardened Gauntlets; siege engine line), Essential Artificials (nigh-universal component; nobody took any in the exodus; artificial extrapolators).

## Their quests
- The Rethban/Furen chain and Essential Artificials: delight in technique; hunger for materials.

## Identity
Eager, scatterbrained, delighted. Speech: stacked superlatives inside whole sentences.
Authored, not canon: he doesn't know yet what the greatest invention is ("the metal will tell me").

## What they notice
Kit (broken armor), craft (blacksmiths).

# Gnoarn  (key: gnoarn)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gnoarn (read): male gnome in Tinker Town; had the only "gnomefro", lost it to the Gnomeregan incident; gossip about troggs and leper gnomes, families lost, "We must make our stand! We must save Gnomeregan!"
- Quest pages read: The Day After (sends you to Ozzie Togglevolt and Razzle in Kharanos; "damn those damned dirty troggs"; Ozzie tells the hair story), Find Bingles (Bingles Blastenheimer, recon over Gnomeregan, last seen over the Loch; "I just know he's gone and blown himself up"), Speak with Shoni (Shoni the Shilent, commander of the underground assault crew, gyrodrillmatic excavationators, "central command").

## Their quests
- The Day After, Find Bingles, Speak with Shoni: he coordinates; furious at the troggs; hopeful.

## Identity
Fierce, comic, loyal, self-appointed central command. Speech: exclamations, military words.
Never says: Gnogaine works (it doesn't yet).

## What they notice
Kin: refugee gnomes; dwarves (whose digging woke the troggs).

## Topics
knows, background, advice, troggs, gnomeregan


## Humans (human patch)
- overspark: Bitter: humans pat gnomes on the head; Stormwind wanted Gnomeregan's engineers but not its refugees, and sent no soldiers. Softens if a human says they would have marched with them. Flag `overspark_human_soft`.
- klockmort: Wry, eager: humans work iron like they're angry at it and assume small means simple; a Goldshire journeyman took his ratios and never wrote. Softens if a human promises to stay and finish. Flag `klockmort_human_soft`.
