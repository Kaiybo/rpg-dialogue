## Their quests
- Kelv Sternhammer: The Islander (warriors, 30: Klannoc Macleod on Fray Island off Ratchet), A Troubled Spirit (warriors, 50: a fallen orc's ghost by the Swamp of Sorrows). Pointers only, level-gated.
- Krom Stoutarm: Tome of the Cabal (warlocks, 30; from Strahad in Ratchet). His share is in a strongbox at Ironband's dig in Loch Modan. From memory of the Classic chain; confirm in Forever.
- Lago Blackwrench: The Slaughtered Lamb (gnome warlocks, to Gakin the Darkbinder in Stormwind); Gakin's Summons not written.
- Lomac Gearstrip: An Old Colleague (from Gaxim Rustfizzle), Ineptitude + Chemicals = Fun (potions for the NG-5).
- Mountaineer Barleybrew: Shimmer Stout (ends; from Rejold), Stout to Kadrell (Mountaineer Kadrell, Thelsamar). "Reinforcements for Loch Modan" is Cataclysm and not used.
- Myolor Sunderfury: no quest of his own in Classic; he points blacksmiths to Ironus Coldsteel (weapons) and Grumnus Steelshaper (armor). "Imperial Plate Armor" on his wiki page is not used.
- Olmin Burningbeard: The Hunter's Path (to Grif Wildheart, Kharanos), The Hunter's Charm (50: Ogtinc in Azshara).
- Nittlebur Sparkfizzle: the Frazzle Frostfingers quests are Cataclysm; not used. He names Bink, Dink, Juli Stormkettle and Milstaff Stormeye.
- Forever (wiki "Forever" sections, content unconfirmed, kept vague on purpose): Jemma, Data Hoarders; Reyna, Camping 101: Herbalism; Uthrar, Camping 101: Tailoring. Each has a `questActive` greeting and line, written like the Camping 101 lines in the Teldrassil pack.
