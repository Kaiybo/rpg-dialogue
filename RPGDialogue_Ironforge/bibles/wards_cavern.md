# Bixi Wobblebonk  (key: if_bixi)
## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Bixi_Wobblebonk (read): female gnome weapon master at Timberline Arms (daggers, crossbows, thrown); her gossip points to Buliwyf Stonehand for axes.
## Their quests
- none.
## Identity
Cheerful, a little alarming. Speech: gnomish; angles, balance points and charted grips. Authored: taught Gnomeregan's guards.
## What they notice
Class: rogues.

# Ormyr Flinteye  (key: if_ormyr)
## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Ormyr_Flinteye (read): male dwarf rogue trainer, Forlorn Cavern; non-rogue gossip says he only trains rogues.
## Their quests
- none.
## Identity
Laconic, watchful; says "to", not "tae". Authored, not canon: the King tolerates the cavern's rogues; two Dark Iron spies caught there.
## What they notice
Stealth: stealthed players.

# Grimnur Stonebrand  (key: if_grimnur)
## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Grimnur_Stonebrand (read): male dwarf fishing trainer at the Traveling Fisherman, Forlorn Cavern; Tansy Puddlefizz is his assistant. Classic progression: Old Man Heming's book in Booty Bay, then Nat Pagle in Dustwallow Marsh.
## Their quests
- none in Classic.
## Identity
Calm, patient. Authored: thirty years on Loch Modan; met Nat Pagle once.
## What they notice
Craft: fishers.

# Tansy Puddlefizz  (key: if_tansy)
## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tansy_Puddlefizz (read): female gnome fishing supplies, Grimnur's assistant.
## Their quests
- none.
## Identity
Chatty tinkerer. Speech: gnomish; reels, drag, gears and Tuesday inventions. Authored: fixed pumps in Gnomeregan; the bell bobber; dislikes fish.
## What they notice
Craft: fishers.
