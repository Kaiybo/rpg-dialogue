# Dun Morogh  (keys: eric, sten, grelin, senir, ragnar, belm)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Great_Outdoors_(Forever) (Forever: Eric Brighthammer teaches camping in Dun Morogh at 46.6, 53.8; the lesson: a basic campfire, which cooks can learn, then fixtures around it; a leatherworker's tent; fishers; resting by the fire grants boons)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Adventurer (Forever: each starting zone has an Adventurer quest that leads to its camping teacher; Dun Morogh's giver isn't named there)

- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Coldridge_Valley (dwarf and gnome starting valley; Anvilmar, Whitebeard's Encampment, Frostmane Hovel; troggs raiding through Coldridge Pass; Coldridge Mountaineers)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sten_Stoutarm (Anvilmar's postman before the Cataclysm; gloves for wolf meat; mail through the hostile pass; friendly but gruff about troggs and "green apprentices"; Dwarven Outfitters, Coldridge Valley Mail Delivery)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Grelin_Whitebeard (Senate Special Envoy at Whitebeard's Encampment, sent to investigate the trolls; won't let his King down; trusts heroes over his bumbling Apprentice Soren; Grik'nir the Cold, "a big brute with markings", stole his journal; The Troll Cave, The Stolen Journal, Senir's Observations)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Senir_Whitebeard (Senate envoy in Kharanos, Grelin's brother; Senir's Observations, Frostmane Hold, The Reports)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Ragnar_Thunderbrew (part owner of the Thunderbrew Distillery; his meat supplier joined the King's Army, so he buys from hunters; Beer Basted Boar Ribs; Ragnar Thunderbrew and Hurley Blackbreath (high level))
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Innkeeper_Belm (Thunderbrew Distillery innkeeper; sells Rhapsody Malt; friend of the gnome Lazlo Grindwidget, toasted in his honor; Bound for Kharanos)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kharanos (Lazlo Grindwidget repelled a giant Frostmane attack; Frostmane on Shimmer Ridge; wendigo cavern)

## Their quests
- Eric: The Adventurer (turn-in), The Great Outdoors.
- Sten: Dwarven Outfitters, Coldridge Valley Mail Delivery. Grelin: The Troll Cave, The Stolen Journal, Senir's Observations.
- Senir: Senir's Observations, Frostmane Hold, The Reports. Ragnar: Beer Basted Boar Ribs, Ragnar Thunderbrew, Hurley Blackbreath. Belm: Bound for Kharanos.

## Never
- Anything Cataclysm or later: the Shattering, the cave-in of Coldridge Pass, Brewfest disputes with Maeve.
- Whether Grimbooze is Ragnar's father or brother (sources disagree): never mentioned.
- Eric's race or history (the pages don't say). His surname suggests a dwarf; his lines don't claim it.

## Voices
- Eric: hearty, warm, cold-weather humor (authored).
- Sten: gruff postman; troggs are all the same.
- Grelin: determined envoy; his apprentice.
- Senir: measured envoy; the drier brother (authored contrast).
- Ragnar: jovial brewer; ribs and the family recipe.
- Belm: sociable innkeeper; toasts Lazlo.
