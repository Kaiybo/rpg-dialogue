## Their quests
- Eric: The Adventurer (turn-in), The Great Outdoors (Forever).
- Sten: Dwarven Outfitters, Coldridge Valley Mail Delivery, the class runes (Hallowed Rune for priests; Forever Archaic Rune for shamans).
- Grelin: The Troll Cave, The Stolen Journal, Senir's Observations.
- Senir: Senir's Observations, Frostmane Hold, The Reports.
- Ragnar: Beer Basted Boar Ribs, Ragnar Thunderbrew, Hurley Blackbreath.
- Belm: none in Classic. ("Bound for Kharanos" is a Cataclysm quest, so Belm's line for arrivals from Coldridge now keys on Senir's Observations.)

## Never
- Anything Cataclysm or later: the Shattering, the cave-in of Coldridge Pass, Bound for Kharanos, Brewfest rivalries, Grimbooze as Ragnar's father (Classic calls him the brother; that's what Ragnar says).
- Eric's race or history (the pages don't say). His surname suggests a dwarf; his lines don't claim it.

## Voices (whole sentences; the burr stays)
- Eric: hearty, warm, cold-weather humour.
- Sten: gruff postman; troggs are all the same; gloves.
- Grelin: determined envoy; his apprentice; the King's name on the letter.
- Senir: measured, drier, a little lazy about the cold; his excellent directions.
- Ragnar: jovial brewer; ribs, malt and the family.
- Belm: sociable innkeeper; toasts Lazlo.

## Depth (Ironforge 1.4.0)
- Eric: why camp, how to make a camp (then resting), who stops by, is it safe (then Lazlo). Gated: leatherworkers.
- Sten: troggs (then the pass), the mail (then Talin), gloves. Gated: a priest's rune (levels 1-6), a dwarf shaman's rune.
- Grelin: Soren (his arm), the trolls (and if he's wrong), who sent him (the King's letter), Felix. Gated: gnomes.
- Senir: the hold (was he afraid?), his brother, the reports (Redstone), the cold (a drink). Gated: carrying Grelin's report.
- Ragnar: the ribs (the real secret), the supplier (who brings meat now), the family (Grimbooze). Gated: the stolen lager (45+), cooks, hunters.
- Belm (service): Lazlo (and the song), Rhapsody Malt, the trainers under his roof.
