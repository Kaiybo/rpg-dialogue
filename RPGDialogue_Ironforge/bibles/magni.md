# King Magni Bronzebeard  (key: magni)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Magni_Bronzebeard (read 2026-11-14). Year 1 facts used: King of Ironforge, head of the Bronzebeard clan; eldest of three brothers, doubted as a prince that he'd make a good king; pledged Ironforge to the Alliance after the Alliance helped liberate it in the Second War; asked Mekkatorque for a faster road to Stormwind (the Deeprun Tram); ordered the kingdom to shift from mining to archaeology after ruins turned up, and helped found the Explorers' League; Muradin believed dead in Northrend, Magni told Arthas was responsible; forged a sword from a crystal of pure Light for Alexandros Mograine at the Great Anvil, three days, grieving Muradin, and heard of Terenas's death while finishing it (Ashbringer comic); Brann missing for months and Magni losing hope (Game Manual); Moira taken by the Dark Iron; distrusts warlocks; authorized Grelin Whitebeard against the trolls; gave the gnomes a district
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Old_Ironforge_Incursion (Forever: Earthseer Farsen, Magni's old friend, sends the head of the Dark Iron commander Durgen Dirgehammer to Magni; Magni has the breaches sealed and the lower levels reinforced)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Reports and https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Senir%27s_Observations_(2) (the King's name on Grelin's letter ruffles the Senate; Senator Barin Redstone is furious)
