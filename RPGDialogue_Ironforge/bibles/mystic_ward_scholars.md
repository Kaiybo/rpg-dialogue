# Mystic Ward scholars (keys: talvash, tymor)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg: Talvash del Kissel (gnome, Mystic Ward; The Shattered Necklace (initials "TdK" with Ironforge's seal), Lore for a Price (five silver bars; "a jeweler of great renown... former renown anyway", a competitor's chicanery), Back to Uldaman (the competitor's rumour that the necklace cursed the paladin; Talvash's Phial of Scrying), Find the Gems (ruby, sapphire, topaz in Uldaman), Restoring the Necklace; Return of the Ring (the ring from the Sparklematic 5200) and Gnome Improvement.
- Tymor (dwarf, Mystic Ward, student of the arcane, interested in Azshara's history; Passing the Burden, Arcane Runes: rubbings of the Runes of Beth'Amara, Jin'yael, Markri and Sael'hai at Eldarath, then signal Pilot Xiggs Fuselighter from the island off Azshara's southern point; "the Explorer's League isn't the only one interested in Eldarath").

## Voices
**Talvash (gnome).** Vain, wounded, quick to blame his competitor; lonely shop, eager salesman. No brogue.
**Tymor (dwarf).** Earnest, scattered, delighted by glyphs; light brogue ("cannae", "doesnae").

## Authored, not canon
- Talvash: his unnamed competitor across the Ward makes rings "fat as sausages" for Stormwind nobles; the necklace's enchantment makes the stones sing in candlelight; he puts his mark on everything so it finds its way home.
- Tymor: rubbings papering his room; reading one rune as a rule about the cost of drawing power from a well (a nod to the Highborne, not a translation); "thirst" as a possible reading; others digging at Eldarath "who aren't so friendly" (unnamed).

## Never
No Highborne or Well of Eternity reveals beyond what Classic Azshara quests imply; no later Azshara (Cataclysm).
