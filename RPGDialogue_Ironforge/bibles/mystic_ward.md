# The Mystic Ward: the Hall of Mysteries (Ironforge pack, 0.35.0 content push)

Eight people in the Hall of Mysteries: the High Priest, three priest trainers, the paladin Tiza and her wounded husband, and two gnome mages. Wiki pages read 2026-11-15 (warcraft.wiki.gg, raw wikitext). Classic only: nothing from the World of Warcraft comic (the New Council of Tirisfal), *The Shattering* or later is used, except that Bink and Dink are siblings (a plain fact about them, not an event). WoW: Forever changes nothing here that the wiki records.

# High Priest Rohan  (key: rohan)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/High_Priest_Rohan (read): dwarf priest trainer in the Hall of Mysteries; highest-ranking member of the Church in the city; paces the Hall; sends priests to Laurena (Stormwind) and Alathea (Darnassus); owns an exorcism censer he used in several exorcisms and gives it to Shadowbreaker's paladins for a donation to bolster Ironforge's food reserves for the needy; sends priests to aid the Cenarion Circle in Azshara. Comic and novel sections (New Council, Anduin, Moira) are later: not used.
- Quest pages read: A Lack of Fear, Desperate Prayer (Ironforge), Arcane Feedback (Ironforge), Elune's Grace (Ironforge), Cenarion Aid, To Show Due Judgment.
- LORE_NOTES.md "Ironforge and Dun Morogh depth": the Light came to the dwarves from the humans after the Second War; the dwarven priests and paladins were the gnomes' only link to the Light.

## Their quests
- A Lack of Fear (finish, dwarf priests): he teaches dwarves their own ward against fear. Proud that some lessons belong to dwarves.
- Desperate Prayer / Arcane Feedback (give, human priests): sends them home to Laurena "with my regards". Respects the Cathedral; no rivalry.
- Elune's Grace (give, night elf priests): passes on Alathea's summons.
- Cenarion Aid (give, level 60): "the way of the divine is to help those in need; the why matters, not the how". Help across faiths.
- To Show Due Judgment (give, paladins): the censer for a donation that feeds the city's poor. Practical charity over relics.

## Identity
High Priest Rohan · Hall of Mysteries, Mystic Ward · Alliance · Dwarf · priest (role "priest", major)
Personality: warm, restless, plain-spoken, practical in his charity, quietly worried
Speech: dwarven burr ("ye", "an'", "tae", "cannae"); walks while talking; forge and mine metaphors
Knows and cares about: the young priests; the gnomes at the back of the Hall; the city's food stores; the Cathedral and the Temple of the Moon
Relationships: Laurena and Alathea (colleagues, by letter); Tiza and Brandur (the Silver Hand in his Hall); Mekkatorque (readings vs prayers); Shadowbreaker (sends paladins for the censer)
Default attitudes: welcomes every race; especially tender toward gnome converts; wary but not hostile to Shadowform
Never says: anything about later councils or events; names of the possessed he exorcised

## Agenda
Keep the Hall feeding people as well as praying.

## Secrets (authored, not canon)
- (Persuade) The food stores are lower than the Senate tells the city; he has been selling the Hall's old treasures, the censer last. Grows from the canon donation for "sagging reserves".

## What they notice
1. Priests (human first, then dwarf, gnome).
2. Shadowform.
