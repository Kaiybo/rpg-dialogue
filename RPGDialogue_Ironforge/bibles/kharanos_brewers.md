# Kharanos and Brewnall: brewers and tinkers (keys: dm_marleth, dm_jarven, dm_rejold, dm_ozzie, dm_shelby)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg: Marleth Barleybrew (Brewnall Village before Cataclysm; Bitter Rivals, Return to Marleth), Jarven Thunderbrew (cellar of the Thunderbrew Distillery; Distracting Jarven: "all this booze down here, but I have strict orders not to touch it"), Bitter_Rivals (swap a barrel of Thunder Ale for Barleybrew Scalder), Rejold Barleybrew (The Perfect Stout: seven shimmerweed from the Frostmane baskets on Shimmer Ridge; Evershine, A Favor for Evershine, Return to Bellowfiz; Rejold's New Brew, Shimmer Stout), Ozzie Togglevolt (Classic: The Day After from Gnoarn, Gnogaine, The Only Cure is More Green Glow; partner of Razzle), Shelby Stoneflint (Kharanos stable master, female).
- Consistent with Ragnar Thunderbrew and Belm (dunmorogh.lua), Razzle (razzle_sprysprocket.lua), Gnoarn (tinker_town.lua).

## Voices
Dwarves in the pack's light brogue ("ye", "an'", "o'", "wee"), whole sentences.
**Marleth.** Mischievous, proud Barleybrew; the prank is "a free sample".
**Jarven.** Grumpy guard who may not drink; counts barrels hourly.
**Rejold.** Dreamy brewer chasing the perfect stout; his late father's doubt.
**Ozzie.** Distracted, hopeful gnome inventor talking from inside a gearbox; loyal to Mekkatorque.
**Shelby.** Warm, brisk; bear cub on her boots.

## Authored, not canon
- Marleth: her grandfather's drunken duel with Ragnar's grandfather over a hop field; Rejold is her cousin.
- Jarven: tasted each barrel once and sang the "Ballad of Khaz Modan" to the rats; barrel nine "tastes off" since the swap; casks for the King's table.
- Rejold: his father said the perfect stout couldn't be done.
- Ozzie: "The Only Cure is More Green Glow" painted on his shed; Gnogaine turns the fallout purple; a Mystic Ward mage turned the fallout blue for an hour; a leper gnome neighbor owes him a spanner.
- Shelby: an orphaned bear cub from near the Grizzled Den; a nightsaber that ate a blanket.
