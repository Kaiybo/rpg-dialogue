# Sara Balloo  (key: sara_balloo)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sara_Balloo (read): female dwarf quest giver in the Military Ward; Sully Balloo's Letter (finish), Sara Balloo's Plea (start).
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sully_Balloo (read): soldier "with much faith in his missions", stationed in Dun Modr, sent to watch the Thandol Span; died in the Dark Iron assault, his body in the water beneath the Span with his letter.
- Quest pages read: Sully Balloo's Letter ("My dear Sara..."; "These times we live in are trying on the soul. What news do you bring?"; she breaks down), Sara Balloo's Plea (she cannot go before the King "in my grief-ridden state"), A King's Tribute (1-3: Magni sighs, says the travesties of war must be endured for the greater good, commissions a memorial from Marblesten in Alterac granite from Darrow Hill).

## Their quests
- Sully Balloo's Letter (finish): grief; she had been waiting for news.
- Sara Balloo's Plea (give): asks the player to carry her note to Magni.
- A King's Tribute (about her): the memorial. She is moved and unconsoled at once.

## Identity
Sara Balloo · Military Ward · Dwarf · citizen (quest)
Personality: gentle, loving, private, brave in grief. Speech: soft burr, plain words.
Never says: the contents of Sully's letter beyond "My dear Sara" (it's hers). No invented family or history beyond the quests.
Authored, not canon: she asks every traveler about Dun Modr; sits in the back of the Hall of Mysteries with Toldren; where the memorial stands is left vague.

## What they notice
Soldier: warriors and paladins (have you passed Dun Modr?); priests after the letter (she doesn't want to be prayed at today).

## Topics
knows, background, advice, thandol_span, dark_iron
