# Jordan Stilwell  (key: jordan)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Jordan_Stilwell (outside the Gates of Ironforge in Dun Morogh; was to become a paladin but followed his father into smithing; one of the Church's most faithful; Daphne's husband; trained under the dwarf Bailor Stonehand; once helped Thundris Windweaver; works outside "for the cool mountain air", as Duthorian Rall says)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Jordan%27s_Weapon_Notes (Classic item: Daphne's life saved; Bailor Stonehand in Thelsamar, his old teacher; before the plague the Stilwells lived in Pyrewood and Jordan learned his craft at the keep now called Shadowfang; they fled, leaving his father's hammer in the stables; his father, killed by the Scourge, always said the hammer was enchanted)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Test_of_Righteousness (and parts 2 and 3) (the Classic paladin quests that end in Verigan's Fist; he is a little envious of the paladin's exploits; the hammer is named for Bosch Verigan, a dwarven paladin friend)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Tome_of_Valor_(3), https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Daphne_Stilwell (Daphne alone on the Westfall Longshore while Jordan works with the dwarves; she fights the Defias herself)
