## Their quests
- Mekkatorque: The Grand Betrayal.
- Archesonus: The Smoldering Ruins of Thaurissan.
- Magellas: The Platinum Discs; Prehistoric Prism (Forever).
- Stormpike: Report to Ironforge, Powder to Ironband, Letter to Stormpike, Stormpike's Deciphering, The Lost Dwarves, The Hidden Chamber (lines stay general about contents).
- Stonehallow: The Public Servant; nearby, A Visitor to Dun Morogh and Farsen's Watch (Forever).
- Firebrew: Assisting Arch Druid Staghelm (passes on word from Darnassus, level 47+).

## Never
- Anything Wrath or later: Operation: Gnomeregan, King of Gnomes, New Tinkertown, the Senate's later politics, Magellas's later quests, Thermaplugg's faked death.
- What the Platinum Discs say. Ragnaros by name (Archesonus only records that "something old and hot" is rumoured).
- Brann's whereabouts. He is "long in the field".
- That the dam was built by Franclorn: Archesonus only says the stonewrought method was his and lets the player draw the conclusion.

## Voices (whole sentences; personality in word choice, not chopped lines)
- Mekkatorque: quick, warm, self-deprecating engineer who runs his thoughts together; carries the guilt of approving the plan and says so plainly.
- Archesonus: learns visitors by voice; long-winded, exact; "I record that it's said."
- Magellas: eager, serious about discovery, frets over his Prospectors; less brogue than most dwarves, but still a light burr (ye, o') and stone, beard and pick imagery.
- Stormpike: curt and hot-tempered, but in whole sentences; wants to be left alone with his reading.
- Stonehallow: brisk public servant with dirty boots; mild jokes about long speeches.
- Firebrew: jolly host; the Stonefire's rule is story for story.

## Depth (Ironforge 1.4.0)
- Mekkatorque (major): the fall (sorry / whose plan / why secret, then where the troggs came from), building (the tram and its rats, the way home), Thermaplugg (the lenses, where he is now), the exiles in Ironforge. Gated: the Light (priests), going home (gnomes), shop talk (engineers).
- Archesonus: Thaurissan (the blast, Blackrock, the rumoured old evil), the Dark Iron now (the dam and Franclorn's method; key line `d_default`, echoed by Hinderweir), her books, the War of the Three Hammers. Gated: a Lordaeron refugee's account, a priest's question about the plague.
- Magellas: the League (who founded it, Muradin's expedition), what he hopes to find, Brann (key line `h_brann`, echoed by Magni), where the League digs (Ironband's idols; Darkshore for night elves). Gated: League apprentices, Prehistoric Prism.
- Stormpike: the blastpowder (Saean after Protecting the Shipment), ciphers (Dibbs; rogues), Uldaman and Baelog. Gated: the Mountaineer at Algaz.
- Stonehallow: why a senator in a quarry, where troggs come from (his own view: dwarven digging), the Senate (soot on beards), the quarry. Gated: miner's children, Farsen's camp.
- Firebrew (service): the story rule, the regulars (John Turner's collecting).
