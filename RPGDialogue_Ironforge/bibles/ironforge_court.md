# Ironforge's great and learned  (keys: mekkatorque, archesonus, magellas, stormpike_p, stonehallow, firebrew)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gelbin_Mekkatorque (High Tinker, elected leader of the gnomes; leads the Gnomeregan Exiles from Tinker Town, a district Magni gave them; troggs dug up into Gnomeregan and were fought in secret for years; Thermaplugg proposed a toxic plan, Mekkatorque approved it, it failed and killed about 80% of the gnomes; Thermaplugg stayed behind and rules the leper gnomes; sentenced for treason; Gnomeregan radioactive and overrun; Mekkatorque designed the Deeprun Tram; practical, inventive, curious, compassionate, quick-witted, puts leadership before his own feelings; Classic: The Grand Betrayal)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Royal_Historian_Archesonus (female dwarf, High Seat; scholar, visually impaired; wrote The Fall of Lordaeron and the Scouring of the Eastweald and On Plagues, Curses, and Blights; offers to teach Ironforge's history; Classic: The Smoldering Ruins of Thaurissan)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Smoldering_Ruins_of_Thaurissan (the Dark Iron city where the Burning Steppes are now; dwarven civil war; a great blast turned the land to ash; survivors built Blackrock Spire and forts in Searing Gorge; rumours of an ancient evil controlling them; recent Dark Iron forts in the Badlands, expeditions into Uldaman, an attack on the Stonewrought Dam)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Muninn_Magellas (High Explorer and head of the Explorers' League, Hall of Explorers; maps the world, catalogues species, relics and treasures; runs the Prospectors; lifelong curiosity about history; grieved by Brann's loss; Classic: The Platinum Discs, Portents of Uldum, A Future Task)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Prospector_Stormpike (Library in the Hall of Explorers; Explorers' League; hot-tempered and curt, "prospector business"; Stormpike clan; Classic: Report to Ironforge, Powder to Ironband, Letter to Stormpike, Further Mysteries, Ironband Wants You!, Stormpike's Deciphering, The Lost Dwarves, The Hidden Chamber)

- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Innkeeper_Firebrew (male dwarf, Stonefire Tavern in the Commons; warm host: drinks, stories, kegs)

## Voices
- Mekkatorque: quick, warm, self-deprecating engineer; carries the guilt of approving the plan and says so plainly.
- Archesonus: learns visitors by voice; long-winded, exact; "I record that it's said."
- Magellas: eager, serious about discovery, frets over his Prospectors; less brogue than most dwarves.
- Stormpike: curt, hot-tempered, wants to be left alone with his reading.
- Stonehallow: brisk public servant with dirty boots; mild jokes about long speeches.
- Firebrew: jolly host; the Stonefire's rule is story for story.
