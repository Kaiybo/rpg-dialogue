## The prejudice
Dark Iron: a great-grandfather killed at Ironforge's gates in the Dark Iron War; he will not hear that they are slaves to Ragnaros. Gnomes: blames them for Gnomeregan and for the bread and beds Ironforge gives; blind spot that dwarven miners woke troggs too (a gnome player can say so, `xgnome_troggs`). Wildhammer: they left after the Three Hammers; he wants the dwarves "under one roof an' one law". Under the gnome grudge (Persuade, `gnomes_vote`): his own guilt.
