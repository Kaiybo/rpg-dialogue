Wiki pages read 2026-11-15 (warcraft.wiki.gg, raw wikitext). Classic only: Belgrum's later roles (Old Ironforge, the Council of Three Hammers), Gryth's *Shattering* episode and Hulfdan's Legion roles are not used. WoW: Forever adds Karnik's "For Further Study" (not used here).

# Curator Thorius  (key: thorius)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Curator_Thorius (read): wandering male dwarf in the Library of the Hall of Explorers; curator of the Ironforge museum; Classic quests Proof of Deed, Suntara Stones (2), Dwarven Justice, Rise, Obsidion!
- Quest pages read: Proof of Deed (gossip "Why hello there... Would you like a tour of the museum?"; Margol's horn, "the bane of every archaeologist that ever set foot in the Searing Gorge"; "May I keep it?"), Suntara Stones (2) (Dorius's singed letter: the stones exist, Lathoric the Black enslaves his team to build Obsidion; Thorius sobs; "In the name of the Light, he will have his wish. This I vow."), Dwarven Justice ("an empty, vacuous hole where my heart used to be"), Rise, Obsidion! (Lathoric and Obsidion slain).

## Their quests
- Proof of Deed: delight in trophies.
- Suntara Stones / Dwarven Justice / Rise, Obsidion!: grief, a vow, justice.

## Identity
Warm, enthusiastic, then grieving. Brother: Dorius Stonetender.
Authored, not canon: the raptor-or-dragon argument with Magellas; the horn's plaque and the thrown bun; a case for Dorius's notes.

## What they notice
Trophy: League explorers; high-level players before Proof of Deed.

# Librarian Mae Paledust  (key: mae_paledust)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Librarian_Mae_Paledust (read): female dwarf in the Library; Explorers' League; best finder of rare books (Exploring Azeroth; the SI:7 detail is not used).
- Mythology of the Titans (quest) (read): "The future is inevitable! We hurl ourselves towards it like falling raindrops to the Great Sea" (paraphrased, never quoted); the book in the Scarlet Monastery library; the Monastery a seminary for paladins, fallen to the Crusade, whose purifying goal was tainted by insanity.

## Their quests
- Mythology of the Titans: rescue knowledge from zealots.

## Identity
Calm, wise, wry. Authored: a footnote argument with Archesonus.

## What they notice
Lore: priests (the Monastery was a seminary); readers of The Seven Kingdoms.

# Historian Karnik  (key: karnik)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Historian_Karnik (read): male dwarf in the Library; gossip "Welcome to the Explorers' League. If you need information, and it is in book form, then I might be able to help you out." Winter Veil quests not used.
- Quest pages read: Prospect of Faith (2) (Karnik and Hammertoe Grez worked together on Uldaman's lost artifacts; the Shadowforge found out; progress text "I've no time to answer meaningless questions"; "We will have to take time to mourn his death later"), Passing Word of a Threat (he will speak to Hammertoe's spirit), Passing Word of a Threat (2), An Ambassador of Evil (Infernus at Angor Fortress, sent by Ragnaros to mind the Shadowforge).
- Existing: Badlands pack has Hammertoe.

## Their quests
- Prospect of Faith, Passing Word of a Threat, An Ambassador of Evil: grief deferred by duty.

## Identity
Brisk, serious, loyal; warms when Uldaman comes up.

## What they notice
Lore: League explorers.

# Hulfdan Blackbeard  (key: hulfdan)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Hulfdan_Blackbeard (read): male dwarf rogue trainer in the Forlorn Cavern; the guards say they often have trouble with him.
- Quest pages read: Road to Salvation (from Hogral; "So ye think yer a right scoundrel? Destined to be one o' Blackbeard's chosen few - an officer o' the Hidden Circle?"), To Hulfdan! ("Keep yer voice down, the guards may hear ye"), Kingly Shakedown (Magni's "blockheads" arresting Hidden Circle members over a heist while plotting "phase III"; "nobody puts the iron on old Blackbeard"; go to Renzik "The Shiv" in Old Town).
- Existing: Stormwind pack (Renzik: "Hulfdan in Ironforge sends dwarves; old friend of Renzik's").

## Their quests
- Road to Salvation, Kingly Shakedown: cheerful outlaw pride; loyalty to his people.

## Identity
Jovial, sharp, piratical speech ("scallywag", "wut", "ye"). Never tells the whole story.
Authored, not canon: the cart and the goat.

## What they notice
Stealth (stealthed players), priests.
