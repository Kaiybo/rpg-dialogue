**Corrections made in the pack:** Jordan's lines keyed on a quest titled "Verigan's Fist", which is the item; the quests are The Test of Righteousness. Belm's arrival line keyed on Bound for Kharanos, a Cataclysm quest; it now keys on Senir's Observations (the Coldridge report carried through Kharanos). Sten's mail line named Grelin as the addressee; the Classic quest sends the letters to Talin Keeneye first.

**Assumptions (confirm in game):** Forever keeps the Classic quest titles used here (The Test of Righteousness, Senir's Observations, The Reports, The Lost Dwarves, Letter to Stormpike, Mountaineer Stormpike's Task, A Visitor to Dun Morogh, Farsen's Watch, Old Ironforge Incursion, Prehistoric Prism). Forever's Senir Whitebeard also gives "Secure the Mountain" (no wiki page yet), not referenced. Forever lets dwarves be shamans (Teo Hammerstorm, Archaic Rune); a dwarf shaman gets a rune line from Sten. Whether Forever allows gnome priests is unconfirmed (Hallowed Memorandum suggests it); no line assumes it.

**Year 1 notes:** Magni is alive and king; Moira stays a whisper ("in the hands of the Dark Iron"; a level-50 line says others have gone into the mountain for her). The Classic Moira quests are Kharan Mighthammer's chain and The Princess's Surprise (Blackrock Depths); "The Princess Trapped" is an Arathi Highlands quest about Myzrael and isn't used. Brann is missing, Muradin believed dead. Mekkatorque plans a return but there is no Operation: Gnomeregan. Grimbooze is Ragnar's brother (Classic), not his father (Battle for Azeroth). The Ashbringer, Eimear and Mekkatorque's comic details are later-published backstory of events before Year 1; used lightly and never named where it would add a fact beyond what's cited.

**Authored, not canon:** Magni's eyes-shut tram rides and the Senate's seasonal reminders; his hint that the Frostmane cost his house dear; Mekkatorque's spare parts in the trophy case; Archesonus's wedding joke and her reading of the plague; Magellas keeping Brann's chair out of tidiness; Stormpike's "every cipher is written by somebody lazy"; Stonehallow's opinion that dwarven digging wakes the troggs; Firebrew's gryphon-wrestling greybeard and John Turner's coin-from-shame; Brandur's "same oaths, better beer"; John's "the months are very close together"; Jordan liking to watch the road; Eric's bean-eating hunter; Sten's wolves without a vote; Grelin's beard; Senir's warm posting; Ragnar's self-tapping keg; Belm pretending not to see the rogue in his kitchen.
