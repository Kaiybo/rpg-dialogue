## Their quests

## Identity
- **Haljan Oakheart** · Astranaar · male · general goods (service). Steady, plainspoken; supply carts from Maestra's Post, lives over flour.
- **Xai'ander** · Astranaar · male · weaponsmith (service). Intense, grim; angry at the Warsong.
- **Aeolynn** · Astranaar · female · clothier (service). Gentle, wistful for Ashenvale before the war.
- **Maliynn** · Astranaar · female · food and drink (service). Cheerful, motherly; wraps bread for Sentinels on the bridges.
