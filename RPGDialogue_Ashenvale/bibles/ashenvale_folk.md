## Voices
**Liladris Moonriver.** Dry, warm, exasperated and devoted; waits for a druid who can't stay awake.
**Shael'dryn (dryad).** Cheerful, gentle, giggles ("hehe", as in her quest text), grieves the treants that were Dartol's friends.
**Illiyana (dryad).** Grief turned to fury; not built for sadness, so she makes it anger.
**Talen.** Nervous, chatty scholar, braver about old things than about naga.
**Maluressian.** Calm, soft-spoken stable master; companions over weapons.

## Authored, not canon
- Liladris: she and Kerlonian lived in Auberdine before he slept; she moved to Maestra's Post with the Sentinels' supplies and wrote to him, and he never read the letter (reconciles Kerlonian's "going home to Auberdine" line in Darkshore `between.lua` with the quest ending at the Post); he won't speak of what he saw in the Dream; her opinion of Delgren.
- Shael'dryn: Dartol's friends broke and hid the rod after his death; the rod "lets its bearer wear another shape"; she saw Cenarius once from far away.
- Illiyana: she and Anilia grew up together; Anilia hid druids' boots as a joke.
- Talen: his mother disapproves; the statuette has a tiny Highborne lock opened by the Ring of Zoram.
- Maluressian: a bear that ate Astranaar's honey; sings to the sabers on bad nights.
