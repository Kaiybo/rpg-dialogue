## Their quests
- Raene: Onward to Ashenvale, An Aggressive Defense, Culling the Threat, Raene's Cleansing.
- Pelturas: Orendil's Cure, Elune's Tear, The Ruins of Stardust, Fallen Sky Lake.
- Relara: none (lines change after Fallen Sky Lake).
- Faldreas: Check in on the Edunes (lines general).
- Kimlya: none: innkeeper only.
- Delgren: The Tower of Althalaxx, Supplies to Auberdine.
- Senani: The Ashenvale Hunt, Ursangous's Paw, Shadumbra's Head, Sharptalon's Claw, The Hunt Completed.
- Mastok: The Befouled Element, Stonetalon Standstill (lines general).
- Kuray'bin: Ashenvale Outrunners.

## Voices
- Raene: fierce guardian of the forest; her animals are family.
- Pelturas: frightened, loving father; hope breaks through at the end.
- Relara: small, sick, brave; honey cakes.
- Faldreas: dutiful keeper of the roads.
- Kimlya: warm innkeeper on the island.
- Delgren: earnest young paladin far from home; hopes for peace in two languages.
- Senani: reverent hunter; the Hunt as respect.
- Mastok: prefers wagons to people.
- Kuray'bin: wary troll scout; both sides "be right a little".
