### Corrections made
- Raene's road in is Trek to Ashenvale (Sentinel Selarin), not the 2.0.3 Onward to Ashenvale. Korra is a saber cat (not specified as a wintersaber).
- Faldreas's Classic quest is Journey to Stonetalon Peak (Keeper Albagorm; the forest spirits tell him who is brave). Check in on the Edunes is a later quest; the line keeps its id with the Classic quest.
- Mastok's Classic quest is Stonetalon Standstill (the lake by the Talondeep Path came alive; scout the gazebo facing Silverwind); The Befouled Element is later, and that line now reacts to Standstill being done.
- Delgren learned Orcish to understand his enemies and finds their writing childish (The Tower of Althalaxx (6)); his earlier "for peace" reason was invented. He grew to respect the night elves (Tower (3)). His company is not named (the wiki gives both the Argent Dawn and the Silver Hand).

### Authored, not canon (this pass)
Raene: how Korra and Dagri tolerate each other; her answer to hunters about two companions; asking druids to look at the Thistlefur trees; Kimlya's inn as a hospice for wounded scouts. Pelturas: Orendil's remedies as prayers; Relara: the bog monsters, Raene's cat liking her, honey cakes for your pet. Faldreas: the forest spirits on Mystral Lake (the lake's elementals are canon from Mastok's quest); asking where home is. Delgren: hoping one day Orcish serves for more; praying before the satyrs' temples; "a better hammer than a priest". Senani: the war seen from both sides; the Earth Mother made hunter and hunted. Mastok: where the lumber goes; "for the Horde" grumble. Kuray'bin: the forest winning; Thrall's debt (the Darkspear's debt to Thrall is canon from Warcraft III); outrunners never look up.
