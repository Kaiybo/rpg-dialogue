## Authored, not canon
- Je'neu: Blackfathom as a drowned temple of the elves' goddess and the water "feeling sick" around Aku'mai; some in Orgrimmar find the Ring's neutrality strange.
- Marukai: came over on the same boats as Mitsuwa; lets Mitsuwa do the grieving.
- Mitsuwa: a loud-singing sister among the dead; one charm hung over the door for each; he told them the road was safe.
- Muglash: Vorsha took a boat and his shield-brother the first season at Zoram'gar; a blood oath on the beach.
- Locke: the Warsong want to burn the forest, Mastok counsels against it.
- Kaylisk: arrows through the roof twice this season. Vhulgra: riders and hippogryphs eyeing each other. Qeeju: sings to the beasts; a great black cat in the northern hills (Shadumbra, unnamed).
