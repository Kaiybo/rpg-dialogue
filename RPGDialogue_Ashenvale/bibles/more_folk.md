# Ashenvale, folk we had missed (keys: gurda, yama, burkrum, moonblaze, farsong, dalria, nantar)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg: Gurda Ragescar, Yama Snowhoof, Burkrum, Illiyana Moonblaze, Sentinel Farsong, Dalria, Nantar; quests The Lost Pages, Freedom to Ruul, Vanquish the Invaders!, Talismans of Merit, Sentinel Basic Care Package (quest).
- Classic placement: Gurda at a small camp southwest of Splintertree Post; Yama and Burkrum at Splintertree Post; Illiyana Moonblaze and Sentinel Farsong at Silverwing Grove (Warsong Gulch, patch 1.5); Dalria and Nantar in Astranaar.

- Yama: Freedom to Ruul (ends; her brother Ruul, a tauren druid, captured by the Thistlefur who thought he was a bear).

## Voices
**Gurda Ragescar.** Blunt orc, anxious about her overseer, not much of a reader; honest about it.
**Yama Snowhoof.** Gentle, worried tauren sister; calm on top; Earth Mother blessings.
**Burkrum.** Calm tauren armorer; grieved by the war, sells good armor anyway.
**Illiyana Moonblaze.** Roguish, wry supply officer; "belonged to a group not favored by most Sentinels"; rules are suggestions.
**Sentinel Farsong.** Proud, fierce Sentinel; every tree a grave; talismans, not tales.
**Dalria.** Brisk, wry trade goods seller; a few rare enchanting formulas (bracer, cloak).
**Nantar.** Warm, steady baker; measures the town's spirits in bread.

## Authored, not canon
- Gurda: chapter 3 is "how to stop it"; she'd rather face a Sentinel than her overseer.
- Yama came to Splintertree to keep Ruul fed; Ruul forgives the Thistlefur.
- Moonblaze: wouldn't name her group; care packages contents.
- Farsong's view of Grom as the slayer of Cenarius.
- Dalria's formulas came from an old enchanter who asked her not to copy them.
- Nantar: Raene eats his bread standing over a map.

## Continuity note
- `npcs/roads.lua` writes Ruul Snowhoof as a Thistlefur furbolg; in Classic he is Yama's tauren brother, a druid in bear form ("They thought I was a bear"). Yama follows the quest text.

## Never

### Voices
**Melyria.** Grave Sentinel; speaks of Velinde as still on duty; counts the Horde's road.
**Onaeya.** Brisk road-watch; no patience for excuses; dry.
**Feero.** Earnest human, Delgren's man; stubborn, says "friend"; the Light without preaching.
**Pixel.** Goblin hauler: prices everything, "pal", won't release unpaid blades.
**Aayndia.** Patient, exacting tanner; nothing wasted.
**Tandaan.** Easy-going; reads where you've been from your armor.
**Llana.** Soft-spoken, curious; keeps a notebook of what reagents are for.
**Fahran.** Dry, guarded; sells poisons and says so; on the town's side.
**Kylanna.** Brisk, cheerful, singed sleeves; brews for the Refuge.
**Andruk.** Gruff orc wind rider master; respects the trolls; mourns two lost riders.
