# RPG Dialogue: Ashenvale (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.21.0 (2026-11-17)
- New people to talk to (see the main changelog for 0.74.0). New files: restart the game fully (a /reload won't load them).

## 1.20.0 (2026-11-17)
- Writing pass: stock phrases ("Fair enough", "I won't pretend", "truly", neat little morals) rewritten in each speaker's own voice, and more grit and humour in the everyday lines.

## 1.19.0 (2026-11-17)
- People here now and then recognise where you come from (your origin) and react to it.

## 1.18.0 (2026-11-17)
- The town's leading people greet you differently when you come back after a fortnight or more, notice how far you've come since they last saw you, and have heard what you did with their neighbours. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.67.0.

## 1.17.0 (2026-11-17)
- People with a stake in a nearby dungeon or raid react once you've cleared it: a greeting, a reply to bring it up, and a conversation about what it means to them, with stances to take. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.58.0.

## 1.16.0 (2026-11-17)
- Every class this place could plausibly meet now gets a greeting and its own conversation, including this server's Forsaken paladins and dwarf shamans where they'd be welcome. Some people here also notice when you've been thinning out the local trouble.

## 1.15.0 (2026-11-17)
- Druids are noticed: 16 people greet a druid as one, with a question to ask and something to argue about. No new files. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.53.0.

## 1.14.0 (2026-11-17)
- Priests and paladins are noticed: 13 people greet an Alliance priest or a paladin as one, with a question to ask and something to argue about. No new files. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.51.0.

## 1.13.0 (2026-11-17)
- Warriors and hunters are noticed: about 31 new warrior and hunter conversations; people greet a warrior or hunter as one, with a question to ask and something to argue about, and hunters with a beast at their side hear it named. No new files. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.50.0.

## 1.12.0 (2026-11-17)
- Rogues, mages and warlocks get a greeting, a question and something to argue about with fourteen more people in Astranaar and on the roads.

## 1.11.0 (2026-11-16)

## 1.10.0 (2026-11-16)

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-15)

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)

Raene on Teronis and Dartol's rod, the Warsong and her two companions; Pelturas on Orendil and Fallen Sky Lake; Relara on her papa and the bog monsters (and your pet, by name); Delgren on the Dark Strand, the night elves and why he learned Orcish.
- Replies for hunters, druids and priests (Raene's wounded scouts), paladins and priests at Maestra's Post, travellers far from Stormwind or Ironforge (Faldreas), and on the Horde side hunters, tauren, orcs, Darkspear trolls and rogues.

- Voice pass: every line now speaks in whole sentences.
