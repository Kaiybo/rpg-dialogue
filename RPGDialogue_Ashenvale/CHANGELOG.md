# RPG Dialogue: Ashenvale (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.10.0 (2026-11-16)
- Horde shaman get their own conversation with 5 people at Splintertree and on the roads.

## 1.9.0 (2026-11-16)
- New people with no quests, who had nothing to say until now: Haljan Oakheart, Xai'ander, Aeolynn, Maliynn.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.8.0 (2026-11-16)
- New people: Kayneth Stillwind at Forest Song, Bhaldaran Ravenshade, Karang Amakkar at Zoram'gar and Ruul Snowhoof.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-16)
- Astranaar: Sentinel Thenysil and Daelyshia; Innkeeper Kimlya has more to say.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-15)
- Replies for the classes and races these people would really react to (warriors, hunters, rogues, mages, warlocks, druids, shamans, paladins; every race of the faction, and the Skyborne), each leading somewhere.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Every person here writes a first impression in your diary after you first talk (the journal's People page shows it too).

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)
- Depth: Raene, Pelturas, Relara, Faldreas, Kimlya and Delgren in Astranaar and Maestra's Post, and Senani, Mastok and Kuray'bin at Splintertree, are now conversations to explore. Raene on Teronis and Dartol's rod, the Warsong and her two companions; Pelturas on Orendil and Fallen Sky Lake; Relara on her papa and the bog monsters (and your pet, by name); Delgren on the Dark Strand, the night elves and why he learned Orcish.
- Replies for hunters, druids and priests (Raene's wounded scouts), paladins and priests at Maestra's Post, travellers far from Stormwind or Ironforge (Faldreas), and on the Horde side hunters, tauren, orcs, Darkspear trolls and rogues.
- Classic fixes: Trek to Ashenvale (Sentinel Selarin) replaces the later Onward to Ashenvale; Faldreas sends you to Stonetalon Peak (Journey to Stonetalon Peak) instead of the later Edunes; Mastok's errand is Stonetalon Standstill; Delgren learned Orcish to understand his enemies, as his quest says.
- Voice pass: every line now speaks in whole sentences.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits now bring personal lines (habits, worries, opinions of the neighbours), night-time lines, and a question or two about the person themselves, not only their quests.
- Faldreas's list, Kimlya, Relara, Kuray'bin, Mastok.

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- Astranaar: Raene Wolfrunner (and Korra and Dagri), Pelturas Whitemoon and his daughter Relara, Faldreas Goeth'Shael, Innkeeper Kimlya. Maestra's Post: Delgren the Purifier. Splintertree Post: Senani Thunderheart and the Ashenvale Hunt, Mastok Wrilehiss, Kuray'bin.
