# RPG Dialogue: Stranglethorn Vale (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.3.0 (unreleased)
- Town voices for Booty Bay, Grom'gol, the Rebel Camp and Nesingwary's Expedition, and one for the whole vale: where to go, the latest talk, and each town's take on the Alliance, the Horde, the trolls and the pirates.
- Every named NPC here answers Background, My trade and A little secret in their own words (the secret once they know you), and several have their own take on the Alliance, the Horde, Stormwind, the Holy Light or the Earth Mother.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)
- Nesingwary's Expedition: Hemet Nesingwary, Barnil Stonepot (and the scattered pages of The Green Hills of Stranglethorn), Ajeck Rouack (tigers, Sin'Dall), Sir S. J. Erlgadin (panthers, and why he left the House of Nobles).
- Booty Bay: Sprogger (Smotts's cook, the other survivor), Fin Fizracket (Stranglethorn Fever), Wharfmaster Lozgil; the Rebel Camp: Private Thorsen.
- Booty Bay: Captain Hecklebury Smotts and his three sea giants, "Shaky" Phillipe, First Mate Crazz, Crank Fizzlebub (and his arrangement with Kravel at the Raceway), Whiskey Slim, Drizzlik.
- Repeat-visit and night lines for everyone already here: Revilgaz, Seahorn, Krazek, MacKinley, Kebok, Aggro'gosh, Nimboya, Kin'weelay, Mok'thardin, Thulbek, Doren, Nimetz. Neighbours remark on each other (Seahorn on Crazz, Krazek on Smotts, Shaky on MacKinley, Erlgadin on Hemet).

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits bring personal lines (habits, worries, running jokes), and some NPCs get night-time lines. Skindle.

## 1.0.1 (unreleased)
- MacKinley knows when you've found Stoley; Krazek knows when you've met his cousin Tran'rek.

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- Booty Bay (neutral): Baron Revilgaz, Fleet Master Seahorn, Krazek, "Sea Wolf" MacKinley, Kebok and Innkeeper Skindle. The Booty Bay Bruisers.
- Grom'gol Base Camp (Horde): Commander Aggro'gosh, Nimboya, Kin'weelay, Far Seer Mok'thardin and Innkeeper Thulbek.
- The Rebel Camp (Alliance): Lieutenant Doren and Brother Nimetz.
