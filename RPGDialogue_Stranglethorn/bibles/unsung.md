## Their quests
- none: service NPCs with no quests or gossip; every conversation keeps BUSINESS.

## Identity
- Kizz: goblin, loud salesman of blunt weapons; buys bars from Jansen; sells to the Bruisers through the Baron.
- Rikqiz: goblin, sells salt, thread, dyes and the odd pattern; prices hides by eye.
- Glyx: goblin, fussy about clean vials; knows where Stranglethorn herbs grow; won't sell to the Bloodsail.
- Jansen Underwood: human from the north, plain-spoken, sells coal, flux and iron; points smiths to Zarena Cromwind.
- Rickle Goldgrubber: goblin, nervous, precise; the counting half of the bank beside Viznik.
- Qixdi Goodstitch: goblin, chatty; one of the Goodstitch family with Xizk.
- Kelsey Yance: cheerful cook selling spices and recipes; clams, cod and slow-cooked raptor; warns off troll cooking and the Vile Reef.
- Gnaz Blunderflame: excitable tinkerer-merchant; blew up the shop once; sells powder to ships and the Venture Company and feels bad about the latter.
- Jaquilina Dramet: human woman, brisk; sells axes to Hemet's hunters; respects Ajeck, wry about Erlgadin and Hemet.

## Authored, not canon
Jansen's past on the Stormwind docks; Jaquilina's timber-cutting father in Elwynn; Glyx's Ratchet workshop; Rickle's years keeping books in Gadgetzan; the Baron buying the Bruisers' clubs from Kizz; sharks as the Baron's deterrent; the Goodstitches as one family and Qixdi's spat with Xizk; Rickle and Viznik's relation left unstated; Kelsey's sunken merchant ship; Gnaz's explosion and the parrot. Kelsey's and Gnaz's race and gender are not stated. Genders not stated for the goblins (pronouns avoided).

## Never
Anything in the main Stranglethorn bible's Never list. No Cataclysm-era Booty Bay.

## What they notice
Kizz: holy classes, battered gear. Rikqiz: leatherworkers and skinners. Glyx: alchemists, drunkenness. Jansen: smiths and miners, fellow humans. Rickle: thin purses, rogues. Qixdi: casters, near-nakedness. Jaquilina: melee classes, chewed gear. Kelsey: cooks and fishers, hunger at dawn. Gnaz: engineers, gnomes.
