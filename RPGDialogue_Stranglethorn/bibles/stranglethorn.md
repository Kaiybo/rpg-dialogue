## Depth rewrite: Grom'gol (Horde towns pass, authored on sourced quests)
Sources read for this pass (warcraft.wiki.gg, Classic sections): Commander_Aggro'gosh, Nimboya, Kin'weelay, Far_Seer_Mok'thardin, Innkeeper_Thulbek, Grom'gol_Base_Camp, Yenniku, The_Defense_of_Grom'gol (1, 2), Hunt_for_Yenniku_(Classic), Headhunting_(Classic), Bloodscalp_Clan_Heads, The_Fate_of_Yenniku, The_Singing_Crystals, The_Mind's_Eye_(Classic), Saving_Yenniku_(Classic), Filling_the_Soul_Gem, Trollbane_(quest), Grim_Message, The_Troll_Witchdoctor, The_Vile_Reef_(quest), Bloody_Bone_Necklaces, Split_Bone_Necklace_(quest), Mok'thardin's_Enchantment (1-4), Trol'kalar_(2).
- Grom'gol: guards against jungle trolls, naga and Alliance coming from Booty Bay and Duskwood; zeppelins to Orgrimmar and the Undercity. Aggro'gosh: "we did not intend to wage war on the local tribes"; lashtail raptors outside the gate; Mosh'Ogg ogres in the Mizjah Ruins ambush caravans despite warnings; Bloodscalp steal rations.
- Yenniku: by Darkspear custom given to the Gurubashi of Zul'Gurub, lost when the Darkspear left with Thrall; Nimboya was sent from Orgrimmar. Headhunting: twenty shrunken heads inspected so Yenniku's isn't among them; a 600-year curse on the Bloodscalp chief Gan'zulah's spirit if he died by their hands. Bloodscalp Clan Heads: Gan'zulah's and Nezzliok's heads go in the bubbling cauldron by the north gate to force the truth "from their dead lips". Nezzliok: Zanzil the Outcast controls Yenniku body and soul. Kin'weelay: hope "faint, and tiny as the smallest insect". The Singing Crystals: Pulsing Blue Shards from Ironjaw Basilisks in the Crystalvein Mine; the Mind's Eye, held by the ogre mage Mai'Zoth in the Mosh'Ogg mound, whom Kin'weelay knew from when the Darkspear dwelt in Stranglethorn; reshaped into a soul gem. Saving Yenniku: the soul is freed, the body left a husk with Zanzil at the Ruins of Aboraz; "we will etch your tale in stone". Grim Message: the Witherbark were once Horde allies who betrayed Thrall; Nimboya's pike laden with skulls at Hiri'watha or Zun'watha. Nimboya's gossip: not afraid of Bloodscalp or Skullsplitter, but Grom'gol's high walls make him feel safe. Zengu sends Trol'kalar to Nimboya.
- Kin'weelay: the most powerful Darkspear witch doctor; gossip: the spirits say the player is here to aid his chief, "say it loud". Bloody Bone Necklaces are Bloodscalp (trophies give power; boiled and rendered to paste); Split Bone Necklaces are Skullsplitter (an old war, though the Darkspear no longer live there). The Vile Reef: an old troll city sunk off the Savage Coast, murlocs now; a tablet of Gri'lek, a hero of troll legend; a shard to enshrine in Orgrimmar. The Troll Witchdoctor: Nazeer sends Marg Nighteye's head; Kin'weelay sees an angry orc spirit with a task undone.
- Mok'thardin: rituals to harness the jungle's magic for the Horde; a ceremonial choker from Shadowmaw claws and a pristine tigress fang, an aged gorilla sinew from east of Booty Bay as the tether, jungle stalker feathers, and Holy Spring water, a spring every race claims was blessed for them, now with naga explorers. "I will thank the spirits for sacrificing such fine beasts."
- Fixed in this pass: Kin'weelay's Bloody Bone Necklaces line (Bloodscalp, not Skullsplitter) and Vile Reef line (murlocs, not sharks); Nimboya's Hunt for Yenniku and Headhunting lines (hunt and bring tusks; inspect heads), and his Yenniku line (the custom, not "the trees swallow him").
- Authored, not canon: Aggro'gosh's caravan counting, his view of an orc mage and of Skyborne; Kin'weelay's view of troll warlocks; Mok'thardin's view of an orc mage; Thulbek's toll of stories. Skyborne lines use only BRIEF §10 (Windshapers, debt to the wind spirits, the Earthen Ring, Thunder Bluff).

## The Salty Sailor's corners (keys: ianstrom, tokal, elril; npcs/booty_bay_tavern.lua)
Sources: Ian Strom (Rogue Trainer, Salty Sailor Tavern, trains both factions; canon gossip to non-rogues sends them to their own class trainer, paraphrased, never verbatim). Tokal (Season of Discovery chain: On the Lam from Bibbly F'utzbuckle in Desolace; Cherry for Your Thoughts?, a Cherry Grog for the rest of her story; No Honor Among Thieves, her note on Illari Duskfeather, the night elf who hired her crew, fed them camp intel, let them keep most loot, vanished when the Alliance marshal came, and once mentioned a hideout near Arathi). ### Their quests
- Ian Strom: none (trainer). Elril: none. Tokal: On the Lam (ends at her), Cherry for Your Thoughts?, No Honor Among Thieves (all TODO_QUEST until captured).
### Voices
- Ian Strom: low, dry Stormwind voice; counts exits; teaches anyone who leaves the war at the door.
- Tokal: blunt, wary, bitter at Illari; not a Horde loyalist; softens only for a Cherry Grog.
- Elril: warm, guarded, playful; coy about being a paladin, never confirms or denies ("the Light doesn't check your ears"); devoted to Elune.
### Authored, not canon
- Ian: Stormwind docks origin, came south on a Blackwater ship about eight years ago, left when lords wanted spies and scapegoats after the stonemasons went unpaid; pays Skindle for a room he hardly uses; nods nightly to a Bloodsail spy; the gnome student caught in the bank vault; poisons learned from a Darkspear woman; once a blindfolded guest at "a house in the Alterac hills" (Ravenholdt only hinted, never named).
- Tokal: struck a sergeant and left Durotar; crew of six (two orcs, an ogre who held a pass so they could run, two trolls, a human deserter cook); waits on a goblin captain who owes her a berth, avoiding the Ratchet ferry; the bar never waters the Cherry Grog because only she orders it; suspects Illari tipped off the marshal.
- Elril: female; temple-trained in Ashenvale without vows, carried water for the Sentinels, plate from a Menethil Harbor dwarf who charged double for the legs; the paladin question is a running joke she keeps alive (a sailors' song, Krazek's ten-gold offer); knelt once beside a human paladin and felt "something warm" answer, never sure whose. No night-elf paladin order is claimed.
- Never: confirming Elril as a paladin; Illari's fate or capture; anything about the chain's later rewards.
