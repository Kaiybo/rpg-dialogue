- Zul'Gurub, Hakkar's return, or the Zandalari: not yet in Year 1.

## Voices
- Revilgaz: a goblin who keeps books on everything, including you; dry, demanding, fond of people who spend.
- Seahorn: a tauren who went to sea and loves it; loud, laughing, nautical; fond of the Baron.
- Krazek: fast-talking secretary; knows everything, sells the rest.
- MacKinley: a gambler with a collector's patience; friendly until he isn't.
- Kebok: oily dealmaker; "business by subtraction".
- Skindle: wry innkeeper; the Baron lives upstairs.
- Aggro'gosh: blunt orc commander; counts caravans.
- Nimboya: wary Darkspear, one thing on his mind: the chief's son.
- Kin'weelay: playful witch doctor; the spirits are rude.
- Mok'thardin: calm far seer; asks before taking.
- Thulbek: warm orc innkeeper; a story for rent.
- Doren: wary officer who left his colonel; grief under duty.
- Nimetz: earnest priest-alchemist; glassware everywhere.
