# The Salty Sailor's regulars  (keys: catelyn, deeg, dizzy, slygarrett, pix)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Catelyn_the_Blade (read): human woman, Blackwater Raiders, Salty Sailor Tavern; daughter of Archmage Ansirem Runeweaver of the Six; Classic quests Ansirem's Key (ends with her), "Pretty Boy" Duncan, The Curse of the Tides (ends with Revilgaz). Her troll-made blade was lost to Duncan, a Bloodsail. Later novel material (her ship, Pandaria, marriage) is not used.

## Voices
**Catelyn the Blade.** Wry, quick and a little bruised, with a pirate's swagger over a scholar's vocabulary that shows when she forgets herself. She mocks her father and keeps every letter he sends; she is proud of her knife and sore about losing it. Calls the player "friend" or "love". Rogues get her respect and the story of the grey man and the raven's feather; mages get her embarrassment about Dalaran.

**Deeg.** Gruff dwarf burr ("ye", "aye", "cousin" for dwarves), grumbling without malice. Everything comes back to snuff, the stolen crates and the Bloodsail, and he treats a crew without tobacco as a crew half beaten. Warm to dwarves, wary of gnomes and their inventions, fond of Seahorn.

**Dizzy One-Eye.** Cheerful in a brittle way, sailor's "ye", talks about seeing with self-mocking jokes. Identifies people by footsteps and smell, hears ships by their rigging. Grateful and loyal; still waits for the Bloodsail who threw the pitch to walk in laughing.

**Sly Garrett.** Dry, plain, unembarrassed. Says exactly what each vial does and never calls it medicine; agrees with paladins that his trade is vile and sells anyway. Hillsbrad origin, distrusts tonic-sellers, quietly proud his poisons aren't watered like the dock goblins'.

**Pix Xizzix.** Goblin voice standard: fast, pushy, prices everything, "pal", "sweetheart", "buddy", oaths by ledgers and sprockets, casual about her brother's rockets. Young and ambitious rather than hard; loves Booty Bay because everyone in it is buying or selling. Undermine named only as home.

## Authored, not canon
- Catelyn earned "the Blade" by putting her knife through a Bloodsail's hand on her first boarding; she came south on a Menethil merchantman the Bloodsail took and the Blackwater retook.
- Catelyn and Duncan were close for a season before she found his red sash; she writes to her father once a year and keeps his letters under her bunk.
- A quiet man in grey (implied Ravenholdt) once asked to buy her knife and left a raven's feather; the Baron is "admiring" her knife beside the Stone of the Tides after the chain.
- Deeg was a Menethil deckhand whose captain sold his ship to the Bloodsail; he grinds his own snuff from Westfall leaf; a wife in Ironforge; Seahorn singes his snout lighting his pipe.
- Dizzy lost his eyes to Bloodsail pitch off the Wild Shore ("One-Eye" from the year he had one left, "Dizzy" from seasickness); the glass-eye wizard said he was from Dalaran; Dizzy hears more Bloodsail ships sailing dark.
- Sly Garrett was a Hillsbrad apothecary's apprentice who fled the plague; a goblin named him; a broken vial once cleared the tavern and its rats.
- Pix's brothers Rix (rockets) and Frix (insurance against the rockets) are working off the lost chest; the Baron, the Blackwater and the Bruisers each passed her robbery report along.
