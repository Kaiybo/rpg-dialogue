# Grom'gol: the camp's working hands (keys: durik, squibby, vharr, mudduk, nerrist)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg: Grom'gol_Base_Camp (NPC list), Durik (Stable Master, added 1.10.0), Squibby Overspeck (Tirisfal Glades Zeppelin Master, Steamwheedle), Vharr (Superior Weaponsmith), Mudduk (title "Superior Cook" before 4.0.3a, now Cooking Trainer), Nerrist (Trade Goods; sold vials before 4.0.3a). All orcs except Squibby (goblin); Nerrist is female.
- Skipped as later additions or unconfirmed for Classic: Nemeth Hawkeye (blood elf), Brawn (6.0.2), Kragg, Zudd, Angrun, Hragran, Nargatt (no Classic evidence found), Krakk (wiki infobox says orc, text says goblin).

## Voices
**Durik (orc).** Gruff with people, gentle with beasts. Plain, patient sentences. Values clean water and a fence the panthers can't climb.
**Squibby Overspeck (goblin).** Goblin standard: pushy, chatty, prices everything; slightly nervous about his Forsaken passengers, which he calls professionalism. Envies Nez'raz's Orgrimmar run.
**Vharr (orc).** Proud, blunt, distrusts goblin steel and goblin neutrality. Advises heavy weapons against the jungle trolls.
**Mudduk (orc).** Cheerful, feeds the whole camp from one pot, won't explain his ingredients.
**Nerrist (orc).** Dry and exact; fights the damp with salt, oilcloth and stones.

## Authored, not canon
- Durik: the stake fence built three times (a shadowmaw panther took a grunt's wolf; a gorilla pulled down the second); two raptor hatchlings from an abandoned lashtail nest; a scar from trying to ride one.
- Squibby: the tauren on the starboard rail and the landing in Hillsbrad; the "mostly sober" pilot; his ledger of every Forsaken passenger.
- Vharr: grunts confusing Darkspear with jungle trolls in night fighting (a worry, no incident named).
- Mudduk: a Darkspear numbing root in the stew; the glittering basilisk he cooked once.
- Nerrist: buys thread from goblins on the coast road against the commander's wishes; the grunt whose tent flooded.
