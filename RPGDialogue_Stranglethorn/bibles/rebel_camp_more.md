## Voices
**Corporal Sethman (human).** Fussy ex-barracks clerk; keeps a ledger of every watch because Kurzen burned his records. Discreet: will give the roster to Doren only.
**Private Merle (human).** Friendly, a little too curious, asks questions back, deflects. Never confesses. Dramatic irony only: an odd regret for Kurzen's speeches, a remark that Thorsen talked too much, red clay on his boots (gated to rogues and hunters).
**Corporal Bluth (human).** Weary, wry quartermaster of three crates and a tarp.

## Authored, not canon
- Sethman: was a pay clerk in the Stormwind barracks; Kurzen burned the expedition's records in a pot and told the men the jungle didn't need their names; a homecoming date pencilled in the ledger.
- Merle: misses the Colonel's speeches; red clay on his boots, which he blames on the river above the falls.
- Bluth: the camp raids Kurzen's supply runs for Stormwind biscuit; no pay since the Colonel stopped writing to Stormwind.
