Lore anchors: Steamwheedle Cartel, Blackwater Raiders, Bloodsail Buccaneers, Venture Company, Skullsplitter and Bloodscalp trolls, Ruins of Zul'Mamwe, Nesingwary's camp, the Rebel camp

## Voice
Flora speaks in long, soft sentences that circle back on themselves, like someone used to talking to plants and to an empty window. She is kind and slightly self-mocking, fond of the goblins without illusions about them, and precise the moment the talk turns to herbs: where they grow, when to cut, what sits beside them. Grief shows only at the edges, in how often she looks at the harbour and in the things she won't say.

## Authored, not canon
- Came down from Stormwind's Trade District with the sailor; he signed on a merchantman bound around the Cape of Stranglethorn "six rains ago" and the ship was never reported lost or taken. She never says his name aloud.
- Brannoc, a dwarf Goldthorn-seller, died going after a Purple Lotus in the Ruins of Zul'Mamwe; only his satchel was found. She tells every student about him.
- A kaldorei visitor taught her to take Wild Steelbloom without killing the plant; a tauren taught her to say a word over what she cuts.
- Has written twice to the Baron about the Venture Company stripping hillsides, and been thanked politely both times.
- Takes tea to Old Man Heming on bad evenings; they watch the sails together. Glyx sells her good vials "at a ruinous price"; mint over her door against the tannery smell.
