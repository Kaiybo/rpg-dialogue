# Booty Bay: visitors and newcomers  (file: npcs/booty_bay_visitors.lua; keys: zandalari_emissary, dudd, rix, bloads, ruzzgot, grizzlowe)

Six Booty Bay figures. Classic era only (Zandalar Tribe from patch 1.7): no Zandalari empire, no Zuldazar, nothing from Cataclysm or later; no Kezan, Gallywix or Lost Isles for the goblins (Undermine named only as home).

## Sources
- Zandalarian Emissary (15076): in-game gossip ("We, the Zandalarian trolls, traveled here from distant islands..."), Molthor on Yojamba Isle recruiting for Zul'Gurub; grants the Spirit of Zandalar when Hakkar falls (lore only, no fx).
- Dudd Fizzlescroll: no lore; fully invented.
- Rix Xizzix: warcraft.wiki.gg (Forever): <Lost and Found>, sells back misplaced quest rewards; brother of Pix and Frix; Show Me The Money! (from Pix). Kept consistent with Pix in npcs/booty_bay_salty.lua.
- Privateer Bloads (2494): Classic quest giver, Akiris by the Bundle (and its repeatable of the same name).
- Caravaneer Ruzzgot (3945): Classic; finishes Passage to Booty Bay, starts The Caravan Road (details kept vague).
- Grizzlowe (9559) <Shipmaster>: in-game gossip about Booty Bay's clam chowder and the Maiden's Fancy, the passenger ship to Ratchet.

## Voices

**Zandalarian Emissary** (key: zandalari_emissary) · no personal name ("My name is for my own people"). Proud, calm, ceremonious, a little condescending. Speaks of the Zandalar as the first tribe and the keepers of troll history and proper loa rites. Light accent: "de", "dat", "dem", "dis", the odd "mon" avoided; always whole, readable sentences. Calls Darkspear "little cousin" and Alliance players "child of de northern kingdoms"; respects the Horde for knowing a blood god when it sees one. Hakkar is a fallen loa who chose to be fed rather than honoured. Never mentions anything beyond Zul'Gurub and the Soulflayer.

**Dudd Fizzlescroll** (key: dudd) · Scrolls and Maps · address: "scholar" (to everyone, literate or not). Smooth, chatty, florid; sells while he talks and treats paper the way other goblins treat gold. Oaths by quills and ledgers. Shameless about his "aspirational" treasure maps and proud of the small print. Ex-Steamwheedle clerk.

**Rix Xizzix** (key: rix) · Lost and Found · address: "sport". Eager, cheerful, a little sheepish; Frix's twin, Pix's brother. Prices regret ("that's what regret costs"), and has a soft streak he hides behind business. Still dreams of rockets; eyebrows half grown back.

**Privateer Bloads** (key: bloads) · human · address: "shipmate". Jovial, brisk, sunburnt Westfall farm boy turned privateer. Proud of keeping to the letter of his marque; laughs at his own name before anyone else can.

**Caravaneer Ruzzgot** (key: ruzzgot) · address: "partner". Gruff, brisk, dusty Steamwheedle caravan boss who prices every danger on the jungle road (triple fare after dark past Mizjah Ruins). Talks about raptors, Bloodscalp, Skullsplitter, Kurzen's rebels and bandits like weather; hates the Venture Company's shredders. Fond only of his mule.

**Grizzlowe** (key: grizzlowe) · Shipmaster · address: "passenger" (to everyone, ticket or not). Jovial, chatty, obsessed with Booty Bay's clam chowder (Kelsey Yance's), steers every subject back to it. Proud of the Maiden's Fancy; friendly rivalry with Gazlowe in Ratchet.

## Authored, not canon
- Emissary: stands in Booty Bay because every ship of the South Seas calls there, and pays the Baron a docking fee to stand still; raised the Spirit of Zandalar from the dock when Hakkar fell. Darkspear were driven from the jungle to the Echo Isles by other jungle tribes (in his telling).
- Dudd: ex-clerk in the Baron's counting house, sacked for logging a departing ship as arriving (forty gold of Bruiser overtime, paid off copying contracts); scrolls scribed in Gadgetzan, not by him (his own attempt set his desk on fire); a dwarf once found a real Bloodsail chest at one of his Xs. Krazek buys his trail maps, Sly Garrett buys blank vellum (Dudd suspects forged letters of passage), Markel Smythe reads his small print aloud to customers.
- Rix: worked off the stolen chest hauling crates for the Bruisers, opened Lost and Found with what was left; buys from vendors, tavern leavings and wreck flotsam; Auctioneer Graves sends him unclaimed lots; refuses Bloodsail sellers. Frix sells insurance on the docks and complains Lost and Found hurts claims. Crazk Sparks says the wharf isn't big enough for two rocket goblins.
- Bloads: master of the sloop Saltpenny (named for his first coin at sea, sewn in his hat); left Westfall at fourteen; earned the Baron's letter of marque by bringing in a Bloodsail cutter full of the Baron's stolen rum without drinking any; independent of Seahorn's Blackwater; "Bloads" is his father's name, the crew say "boat loads". Akiris reed is used dried and twisted for caulking (pitch cracks, oakum rots); beds in the coastal shallows guarded by murlocs.
- Ruzzgot: mule boy for the Cartel at nine, twenty-two years on the road; his mule Collateral (borrowed against once, kicked two raptors to death, bit a Kurzen sergeant); lost three wagons at the bend below Mizjah Ruins; once paid a Skullsplitter chief a crate of rum for a clear road and was raided the next morning; carries Crazk's fireworks against raptors (one scar from a crate that went off early).
- Grizzlowe: offered Kelsey Yance forty gold and a free monthly crossing for her chowder recipe and was refused; thinks Menethil's chowder (with mushrooms) the worst and Ratchet's (river clams) a close second; the Fancy was bought secondhand from a human merchant, name included; a gnome once fell overboard measuring the wake; a Bloodsail sloop fled a boatload of orcs and dwarves three years ago, minus a mast; Cartel rule of no fighting aboard, enforced with buckets of seawater.
