# Booty Bay: the tradesfolk (keys: brikk, fargon, hurklor, grarnik, blixrez, oglethorpe)

## Sources
- Classic NPC roster of Booty Bay as remembered: Brikk Keencraft <Blacksmithing Trainer>, Fargon Mortalak and Hurklor (armorers, Plate-n-Chain), Grarnik Goodstitch <Tailoring Trainer> (A Tailor to Cities), Blixrez Goodstitch <Leatherworking Supplies> (Tan-Your-Hide Leatherworks), Oglethorpe Obnoticus <Gnome Engineering Trainer>. No gossip on record for any of them; wiki pages not re-read this pass.
- Oglethorpe: banished from Tinker Town over the Crowd Pummeler 9-60 (kept consistent with Overspark in `RPGDialogue_Ironforge/npcs/tinker_town.lua`); turn-in for the OOX homing robot quests (OOX-17/TN Tanaris, OOX-09/HL Hinterlands, OOX-22/FE Feralas) and the mechanical chicken reward ("An OOX of Your Own"), all as TODO_QUEST.
- Goodstitch kin kept consistent with Xizk (`booty_bay_more.lua`) and Qixdi (`unsung.lua`): one family, Qixdi prices low, goblin pronouns avoided for Xizk, Qixdi and Blixrez.

## Voices
**Brikk Keencraft (goblin).** Brisk, shrewd, proud of a trained ear: hears a flawed blade "go flat". Prices everything by the lesson and the pound, oaths by sparks and sprockets, "pal" and "chum". Casual about anchors through hulls and burnt awnings. Secretly builds to last and grumbles about the lost repeat trade. Respects Jansen's honesty and finds it suspicious; envies Fargon's riveting.

**Fargon Mortalak (human).** An old soldier-armorer, dry and stern, calls customers "soldier". Measured whole sentences, no goblin patter. Obsessed with fit ("a man who buys armor in a hurry gets buried in a hurry"). Quietly carries Kurzen's column with him; soft about Hurklor and the crab though he'd deny it. Fits Horde and Alliance alike, at some private cost.

**Hurklor (race not stated).** Huge, slow-spoken, gentle; plain sentences without contractions, never fragments. Reads dents like a story, says customers' names over rivets, keeps a hermit crab called Rivet in an old helm. Loyal to Fargon. A small sore spot: the Bruisers turned him down as too gentle.

**Grarnik Goodstitch (goblin).** Eldest of the Goodstitch clan and its self-appointed head. Smooth, vain, flattering, every compliment priced; "sweetheart". Insists "A Tailor to Cities" is a statement of ambition, not a misspelling. Dresses the Baron. Feuds with Qixdi over prices, despairs of Blixrez's leather, sells the odd red sash out the back.

**Blixrez Goodstitch (goblin).** The family's cheerful black sheep: blunt, earthy, proud of the smell. Twenty years in "the phase". Fast salesy patter with "pal" and "listen, listen", panics comically around tauren about kodo. Friendly rival of Rikqiz; buys pelts from Nesingwary's hunters and, at a profit, from Brikk.

**Oglethorpe Obnoticus (gnome).** Grandiloquent, wounded, eccentric; long technical sentences and "my friend". Insists he was framed over the Crowd Pummeler and suspects Overspark, without proof. Delights in goblin freedom from forms and safety rules. Proud of the OOX robots and his mechanical chicken; blames the crashes on Gnaz's fuel.

## Authored, not canon
- Brikk: Undermine foundry childhood (bellows boy from six); the Baron's forge contract for the whole cove; forged the brackets holding the shark's jaws above the gate and leg irons for Bloodsail prisoners; the anchor through the hull and the Baron's "court fee"; a "sea-iron" method; a shaman's fire burning Kizz's stall.
- Fargon: armorer to Colonel Kurzen's column, walked away when Kurzen went strange; learned riveting from a dwarf in Lordaeron's barracks in the Second War; linen-and-seawater padding trick from a troll scout; the goblin landlord who named the shop and charges a sign fee.
- Hurklor: saved Fargon from a falling crate of cannonballs nine years ago; Rivet the hermit crab; turned down by the Bruisers as too gentle; once carried Oglethorpe away from an "affectionate" prototype; rebuilt the shop door taller.
- Grarnik: eldest and head of the Goodstitch family, fourth generation; makes the Baron's coats with big gold buttons; the Stormwind lady and the sun hat; back-door sales of red sashes; copied Darkspear beadwork; the price feud with Qixdi, with Xizk siding with whoever's buying.
- Blixrez: twenty years as the leather Goodstitch, seated by the window at family suppers, Qixdi sits beside on purpose; the shop name as a joke threat; mangrove-bark tanning; rivalry and shared bottle with Rikqiz; the tauren and the crocolisk belt.
- Oglethorpe: three theories implicating Overspark; the Gadgetzan transporter prototype (chickens, trousers in Ratchet); Gnaz's fuel blamed for the OOX crashes; Brikk forges his casings.
