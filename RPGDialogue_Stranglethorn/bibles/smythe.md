## Authored, not canon
- From Stormwind's Old Town; was purser's mate on the merchantman *Ellery's Promise*, taken by the Bloodsail off the Cape about fourteen years ago.
- Held in a hulk off the Wild Shore for ransom nobody paid; the Bloodsail took his boots, and he still checks every Bloodsail corpse that washes up for them.
- The Blackwater Raiders took the hulk; the Baron ruled the crew "salvage" at a fee per head. Markel worked three years tallying crates for the Cartel to buy himself back, then stayed.
- Now a sworn witness for wagers, cargo counts, debts, pier duels and goblin marriages; keeps his book in a tarred tin under his bed. Swears on the Cartel's book and his mother's name.
- A Bloodsail spy posing as a cloth trader offered him forty gold to lie about a stolen rum cargo; he refused, got his right hand cut, and now signs with his left. The Bruisers threw the spy off the pier.
- Stands in the corner as witness twice a year when Cartel auditors check the Baron's books.
- Reads and writes the Bruisers' letters; three of them are courting the same girl in Ratchet in his handwriting.
- Hung a string of shells over his door when Ian Strom moved in; sometimes finds them rearranged.
- Stormwind posted the ship lost with all hands, so he is legally drowned; mother dead, a brother who never writes.
- "Common name, Smythe": no stated relation to any other Smythe.
