# Grom'gol, the rest of the camp  (keys: nezraz, thysta, uthok)

Kept apart from stranglethorn.md so the Booty Bay work can edit that file at the same time. The camp's main bible (Aggro'gosh, Nimboya, Kin'weelay, Mok'thardin, Thulbek) is in stranglethorn.md.

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Grom%27gol_Base_Camp (NPC list: Thysta <Wind Rider Master>, Nez'raz <Zeppelin Master>, Uthok <General Supplies>; zeppelins to Orgrimmar and the Undercity)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Nez%27raz (goblin; the ship from his tower drops off at Orgrimmar; fond of the sunny Stranglethorn coast)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Thysta (orc wind rider master; pre-Wrath)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Uthok (orc; Classic wares: drinks, bags, campfire reagents, ammunition and thrown weapons)

## Their quests
- none: all three are service NPCs; built from role, place and what Aggro'gosh and Thulbek already say (Bloodscalp ration theft, jungle trolls on the ridges).

## Never
- Nez'raz's brother Zapetta (the gossip's era isn't clear). Nemeth Hawkeye (blood elf, Burning Crusade). The Zandalari.

## Voices
- Nez'raz: chatty goblin, "pal", swears the zeppelins are safe a little too often.
- Thysta: calm, watchful; keeps her riders on high ground.
- Uthok: gruff supply orc; everything rots in a week.

## What they notice
- Nez'raz: race (a tauren's weight on deck), weariness. Thysta: race (Darkspear), class (a hunter's beast). Uthok: weariness, class (hunter's empty quiver).

The raptor that tried to climb the zeppelin tower; Thysta's axe by the pen; Uthok sleeping on his crates and the goblins' crate prices. Thulbek gains a "who keeps the camp" branch and a Darkspear reply.
Left out as unsure: Squibby Overspeck, Angrun, Kragg, Zudd, Durik, Mudduk, Brawn and the other vendors.
