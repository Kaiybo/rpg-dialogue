## Voices
Zandalari dialect as the emissary: "de/dat/dis/dem/dey", whole sentences, proud.
**Molthor.** Grave general; trusts deeds, not races; admits his elders think asking outsiders a disgrace.
**Exzhal.** Pompous; speaks as the king's mouth; ruthless with the prisoner; thinks in politics.
**Vinchaxa.** Calm priest of the altar; the coins as a prayer; warns against asking loa for favors.
**Jin'rokh.** Blunt dire troll, but in whole sentences; "my dealings are with warriors and heathens" (gossip paraphrase); jokes about being "a smaller fist".

## Authored, not canon
- Molthor: some Zandalari would capture and free the high priests rather than kill them; why Yojamba (water between them and Zul'Gurub, near Grom'gol and Booty Bay); the heart taken home to Zandalar.
- Exzhal: Molthor thinks him too hard on the prisoner; the heads as a message to every tribe; Rastakhan chose him for his memory.
- Vinchaxa: Zanza as keeper of elixirs and the spirit's strength (loose); bijous "scream" when unmade and whisper to those who keep them; some Zandalari would never teach outsiders the loa's names.
- Jin'rokh: Exzhal would keep the tribe's craft for the tribe; "sending a smaller fist".
