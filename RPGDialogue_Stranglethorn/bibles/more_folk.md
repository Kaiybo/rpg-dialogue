## Voices (authored, not canon)
- Angrun: calm, dry orc herbalist; the ruins grow the best herbs because of the trolls; the grey vine on troll spears; sends the poisoned to Kin'weelay. Honor stance on herb-lore versus glory.
- Hragran: gruff armourer at war with mould; crocolisk-fat oil. Honor stance on light armour as cowardice.
- Nargatt: jovial provisioner; boils rainwater; friendly feud with Mudduk over whose food feeds the camp. Order stance on carrying food versus foraging.
- Zudd: eager pet trainer; talks of beasts like promising grunts; disapproves of Nesingwary's trophy hunting. Light stance on the bond.
- Kragg: proud hunter trainer; "everything here hunts"; once let a great tiger the trolls call a spirit walk away. Honor stance on trophy hunting.

## What they notice
- Angrun: herbalists, alchemists. Hragran: battered kit. Nargatt: hunger, drink. Zudd: pets, cats. Kragg: troll hunters, pets.
