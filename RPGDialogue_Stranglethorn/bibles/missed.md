# Stranglethorn, missed folk  (keys: corporal_kaleb, cowardly_crosby, riggle_bassbait, jang, jaxin_chong, mcgavan, knaz, krakk, maywiki)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Stranglethorn_Vale_NPCs (roster scan); Classic-range NPC ids.
- Corporal_Kaleb (read: Rebel Camp cook; his mechanical pot, made by the goblin Krazek, broke; quests Krazek's Cookery, Return to Corporal Kaleb).
- Cowardly_Crosby (read: Tailoring Supplies on the Wild Shore, by the Bloodsail camps; trades with anyone).
- Riggle_Bassbait (read: Fishmaster, runs the Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza from a Booty Bay dock with Fishbot 5000 and Jang). Jang (read: Fishmaster Apprentice; buys Speckled Tastyfish five at a time).
- Jaxin_Chong (read: alchemy trainer, top level of Booty Bay; gossip about Glyx's prices, retold in our own words).
- McGavan (read: The Mithril Order, Booty Bay; sends smiths to Galvan the Ancient; quests The Mithril Order, In Search of Galvan).
- Knaz_Blunderflame (read: Engineering Supplies on a hidden hill south of the Mosh'Ogg Ogre Mound; relative Gnaz).
- Krakk (read: Superior Armorer, Grom'gol Base Camp; an orc in Classic, as the roster has him).
- Maywiki (read: Zandalari troll; the Cataclysm digsite and the later Dazar'alor role are not used; Classic has her on Yojamba Isle as Maywiki of Zuldazar, dealing with druids and shaman).
- Existing pack: rebel_camp.md / rebel_camp_more.md (Doren, Nimetz, Thorsen, Kurzen), stranglethorn.md (Krazek in Booty Bay), unsung.md (Glyx Brewright, Gnaz Blunderflame in Booty Bay), galvan.md, gromgol*.md, yojamba.md (heavy "de/dis/dat" Zandalari voice; "Al'tabim, Maywiki and Falthir trade with dose who walk other paths"), booty_bay_wharf.md and booty_bay_docks.lua (Sunday contest, forty tastyfish, "the Baron's man Riggle").

## Identity / voice
- Corporal Kaleb · human man · harried, good-humoured camp cook; mourns his goblin pot.
- Cowardly Crosby · human man · one long worried sentence at a time; alive because he ran.
- Riggle Bassbait · goblin man · carnival barker; "pal", "friend"; the contest fills the town and he says so.
- Jang · goblin woman · dry, exact; the real margin is in the losers' fish; wants the boss's job politely.
- Jaxin Chong · goblin man · aggrieved about Glyx's prices, and pays them anyway.
- McGavan · human man · blunt gatekeeper smith; judges work by the seams; cool to non-smiths.
- Knaz Blunderflame · gnome man · excitable, exact, counts seconds and percentages; failures are data.
- Krakk · orc man · proud, blunt; steel and honor; refers to himself by name once or twice.
- Maywiki of Zuldazar · Zandalari woman · serene, sly; "Maywiki would never say so"; warm only to druids and shaman.
