## Lore (authored, not canon)
- Her ship is the Gull's Wager, won from a goblin in Ratchet on a bet about a gull and a cannonball.
- She is a fisherman's daughter from the Westfall coast who went south when the Defias rose and the farms failed.
- She sails alongside the Blackwater Raiders rather than under them: her guns are the Baron's and she takes Seahorn's orders when the Bloodsail come, but she carries her own cargo in fair weather and pays the Baron a tenth of each cargo for anchorage.
- Crew: eleven hands (ten while Mathers is missing), bosun Old Tamsin (keeps a carved fish for luck), goblin twin gunners, and a parrot named Bilge that once bit Seahorn's thumb.
- Mathers was taken by Kurzen's men while ashore up the coast buying peppers; they kept him cooking for Kurzen's officers, and he over-peppered their stew. His best dish is turtle stew with Stranglethorn peppers.
- She lost two crewmen, Pell and Dory, to a Bloodsail broadside off the Wild Shore, and takes pressed Bloodsail sailors aboard when she can.
- A Theramore mage once froze a Bloodsail rudder for her ship.
