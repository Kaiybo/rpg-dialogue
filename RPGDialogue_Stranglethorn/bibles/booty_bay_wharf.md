# Booty Bay: the wharf and the trade halls  (file: npcs/booty_bay_wharf.lua)

Untitled-townsperson service NPCs with no quests of their own; built from place, trade and neighbours. All goblins, Steamwheedle Cartel, neutral to both factions. Classic era only: no Kezan, no Gallywix, no battle for Booty Bay. Each keeps their own address term.

## Voices

**Crazk Sparks** (key: crazk) · Fireworks Merchant · address: "buddy". A showman who talks faster than his fuses burn, sells while he talks and is cheerfully casual about explosions. No eyebrows, and proud of it ("product testing"). Lets slip the odd sentimental streak (paid for a hundred rockets himself) and covers it with a joke. Oaths by fuses and copper.

**Wigcik** (key: wigcik) · Superior Fisherman · address: "chum". Sour, smelly, practical fishmonger who haggles with the fish before he sells them. Grumbles about everyone and secretly protects Old Man Heming (pays him a copper more a pound, never says so). Distrusts anything that glows; keeps a jar for lost skiff crews.

**Myizz Luckycatch** (key: myizz) · Fishing Trainer · address: "sweetheart". Warm, quick and superstitious: never fish on a Thursday, never step over a rod, never whistle on a dock. The rare goblin who thinks patience is worth buying. Ex-counting-house clerk who chose a dock over gold and has no regrets. Argues amiably with Heming about which fish to throw back.

**Grimestack** (key: grimestack) · Stable Master · address: "boss". Gruff, grimy, few words for people and many for beasts. Ex-Venture Company muleskinner who quit with his fists over a mistreated mule. Kind to animals in a way he'd never call kindness. Keeps a stray raptor, Moody.

**Scooty** (key: scooty) · Chief Engineer · address: "pal". Eager, breathless, in total denial about his transporter's reliability; lists side effects as selling points ("a little to the left"). Loves his coils, fears the lever. Friendly rivalry with Oglethorpe Obnoticus, the gnomish engineering trainer.

**Auctioneer Graves** (key: bb_graves) · address: "friend", said flatly. Dour, deadpan, "not gloomy, accurate". Sells wreck salvage and the effects of people who didn't come back from the jungle, and treats them with a respect nobody else bothers with. Afternoon shift.

**Auctioneer Kresky** (key: bb_kresky) · address: "chief". Jittery, sleepless night-shift gossip who can't keep anything to himself and is half scared of what he knows. Repeats himself ("listen, listen", "quick, quick").

**Auctioneer O'reely** (key: bb_oreely) · address: "champ". Boisterous morning-shift storyteller who gives every lot a story and every story a new ending. Talks about his grandfather constantly.

## Authored, not canon
- Crazk: lost his eyebrows and his shop (roof, back wall, part of a dock and a crate of oranges) in Ratchet; Gazlowe still orders his fireworks yearly on condition they're set off from a boat far out. Keeps real stock in a cellar below the tideline by deal with the Bruisers (flooded twice); workshop shed beyond the tunnel; powder from Gadgetzan. Supplies the Baron a crate each Sunday for the tournament winner; put up two hundred rockets the night the Blackwater brought in a Bloodsail flagship's mast (paid for half himself).
- Wigcik: twenty years buying Heming's catch, pays him a copper extra a pound in secret; six skiffs that fish past the Cape, lost crews remembered with a copper jar and a round at the Salty Sailor. Fish caught near the Vile Reef sometimes glow faintly green at night (echoes Heming's glowing weed). Fished up a chest of unclaimed Stormwind letters and gave them to Markel Smythe. Old fish traps under the north pier are of troll make. Hinted he may have ghostwritten Heming's bass book.
- Myizz: real name, she insists; ex-clerk in the Cartel counting house in Gadgetzan; charms (shark's tooth, a Blackwater captain's ring, her grandmother's tin cricket, a Vile Reef pearl she won't discuss); an uncle lost to a Thursday grouper. Keeps the first fish of the day, throws back the second (Heming does the reverse). A gnome once won the Sunday tournament fishing off a raptor.
- Grimestack: drove pack mules for the Venture Company; knocked down a foreman who whipped a mule to death, and the Venture Company put a price on his ears that the Baron won't let them collect in town. Keeps Moody, an abandoned raptor who bites everyone once and likes only Grimestack, the Bruisers' cook and an old Ironforge dwarf. A panther has been eating parrots off Booty Bay balconies (ties to 0.73.0 colour); his trap failed. Won't stable summoned steeds.
- Scooty: transporter to Gadgetzan, goblin engineers only by Cartel rule, waiver with the fine print on page nine; side effects (an hour as a chicken, hair on fire, arriving "a little to the left", one dwarf came out as two). Cousin Fizzik minds the Gadgetzan pad and once let three caravans of sand through. The previous chief engineer went through the pad and never wrote back; nobody knows what the big lever does. Trades ideas with Oglethorpe Obnoticus.
- Auctioneers: Booty Bay's house is open to both factions with a steeper deposit and Cartel cut. Shifts: O'reely mornings, Graves afternoons, Kresky nights. Graves holds salvage lots thirty days for claimants (four in eleven years). Kresky noticed someone buying red cloth by the bolt through three runners (possible Bloodsail sashes; unresolved). O'reely's grandfather supposedly auctioned a ship with its crew aboard (the family still owes the Cartel for it); O'reely once sold a "lucky" wooden leg for eleven gold.
