# RPG Dialogue: Teldrassil (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.10.0 (2026-11-16)
- Rogues, mages and warlocks get their own conversation with 18 people in Shadowglen and Dolanaar.

## 1.9.0 (2026-11-16)
- New people with no quests, who had nothing to say until now: Jeena Featherbow, Kyra Windblade, Cyndra Kindwhisper, Malorne Bladeleaf, Nadyia Maneweaver, Radnaal Maneweaver, Nessa Shadowsong, Androl Oakhand.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.8.0 (2026-11-16)
- New people: Denalan, Iverron and Zarrin.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-15)
- Replies for the classes and races these people would really react to (warriors, hunters, rogues, mages, warlocks, druids, shamans, paladins; every race of the faction, and the Skyborne), each leading somewhere.

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-15)
- Every person here writes a first impression in your diary after you first talk (the journal's People page shows it too).

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.3.0 (unreleased)
- Depth: all eleven NPCs of Shadowglen and Dolanaar are now conversations to explore. Tenaron on Hyjal, the moonwell phial and the young ones he watches go down the path; Corithras tells the Crown of the Earth story; Athridas on the Druids of the Talon and Ursal; Tallonkai on Starbreeze Village; Syral on Zenn Foulhoof; Gilshalan on Githyiss and the oversized spiders; Lyreena on what each trade brings to a camp.
- Replies only some characters get: visitors who aren't night elves, characters back from the wider world, druids, priests, alchemists, herbalists and cooks, and quest states (Crown of the Earth, Iverron's Antidote, Twisted Hatred, Zenn's Bidding).
- Classic fixes: the Woodland Protector is the dryad Tarindrella, Twisted Hatred is about Lord Melenas in Fel Rock, A Troubling Breeze sends you to Gaerolas at Starbreeze, the dreamcatcher was Gaerolas's gift; Dirania no longer has an invented childhood with Iverron.
- Voice pass: every line, the Dolanaar Sentinels' included, now speaks in whole sentences.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits bring personal lines (habits, worries, running jokes), and some NPCs get night-time lines. Lyreena; Keldamyr on Corithras.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits now bring personal lines (habits, worries, opinions of the neighbours), night-time lines, and a question or two about the person themselves, not only their quests.
- Shadowglen and Dolanaar: Ilthalaine, Melithar, Tenaron, Dirania and Iverron, Gilshalan, Corithras, Athridas on the Gnarlpine, Tallonkai's dreamcatcher, Syral, Keldamyr.

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- Shadowglen: Conservator Ilthalaine, Melithar Staghelm, Tenaron Stormgrip, Dirania Silvershine and Gilshalan Windwalker.
- Dolanaar: Corithras Moonrage, Athridas Bearmantle, Tallonkai Swiftroot, Syral Bladeleaf, Innkeeper Keldamyr, and Lyreena Duskblade (Forever's camping). Teldrassil's Sentinels.
