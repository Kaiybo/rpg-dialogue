**Assumptions (confirm in game):** that Forever keeps the Classic Shadowglen quests (sigils from Ilthalaine, Woodland Protector from Melithar). Several titles are shared by two quests (Crown of the Earth x6, Iverron's Antidote x2, The Woodland Protector x2, The Balance of Nature x2); capture data will report them ambiguous. Gilshalan's Forever quest "Fang of Githyiss" and Tallonkai's Forever "Twisted Hatred (2)" have no wiki pages yet, so they aren't referenced.

**Year 1 notes:** nothing of the Burning of Teldrassil; no kinship between Melithar and Fandral or Athridas and Broll (wiki speculation); nobody blames Fandral for the tree's troubles beyond the dragons' refusal, which Corithras tells as Classic did.
