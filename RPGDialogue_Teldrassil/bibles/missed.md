# Teldrassil, missed folk  (keys: andiss, dellylah, freja_nightwing, janna_brightmoon, keina, khardan_proudblade, lyrai, orenthil_whisperwind, aldia, brannol_eaglemoon, byancie, dazalar, melarith, narret_shadowgrove, nyoma, sinda, alanna_raveneye, huntress_nhemai, huntress_yaeliura, sentinel_shaya)

Files: `npcs/missed.lua` (Shadowglen / Aldrassil), `npcs/missed2.lua` (Dolanaar, the Oracle Glade, the Darnassus road).

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg pages of each NPC via `tools/npc.py` (read: title, place; all Classic-range ids 3587-3614, 4265, 6091, 6094, 6781, 10604, 10606, 11942, 12429).
- Andiss (armorer & shieldcrafter), Dellylah (food & drink), Freja Nightwing (leather), Janna Brightmoon (clothier), Keina (bowyer), Khardan Proudblade (weaponsmith), Lyrai (general supplies): Aldrassil vendors.
- Orenthil Whisperwind: Classic placement is Shadowglen (moved to Dolanaar later); he took the Collector's Edition "Welcome!" voucher.
- Aldia (general supplies; Forever: Soft Saber Pelts), Brannol Eaglemoon (clothier), Byancie (first aid trainer; Forever: Camping 101: First Aid), Dazalar (hunter trainer), Melarith (no listed role), Narret Shadowgrove (trade supplies), Nyoma (cooking supplies), Sinda (leather): Dolanaar.
- Alanna Raveneye: enchanting trainer, Oracle Glade (Forever: Camping 101: Enchanting).
- Huntress Nhemai, Huntress Yaeliura: mounted on nightsabers beside Moon Priestess Amara on the Dolanaar-Darnassus road, by the path to Ursal the Mauler's den. Kept consistent with `amara.lua` (Nhemai noticed the Gnarlpine are frightened; both are quiet and sharp-eyed).
- Sentinel Shaya: south of Dolanaar; target of the priest lesson Garments of the Moon. Kept consistent with Laurna in `teldrassil_folk.lua` (hurt on patrol, refuses to come in).
- Existing pack: shadowglen.lua, aldrassil_trainers.lua, dolanaar.lua, unsung.lua (Lyreena's Camping 101, Malorne, Nadyia, Radnaal, Zarrin).

## Their quests
- Dazalar: The Hunter's Path, Taming the Beast (webwood lurker, nightsaber stalker, strigid screecher), Training the Beast (to Jocaste in Darnassus). Hunters only.
- Aldia: Soft Saber Pelts (Forever; details not captured, written loosely as nightsaber pelts for lining).
- Byancie, Alanna: end a Camping 101 lesson each (Forever), same pattern as Malorne.
- Orenthil: Welcome! (voucher only; no gift is promised in dialogue).
- Shaya: reacts to Garments of the Moon (Laurna's quest).
- The Cataclysm quests on the wiki (Byancie's Not Dead Yet chain, Nyoma's Reminders of Home, Orenthil in Dolanaar) are not used.
