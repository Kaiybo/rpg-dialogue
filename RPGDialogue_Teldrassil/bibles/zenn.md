## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Zenn_Foulhoof (read: friendly satyr east of Dolanaar, "up to no good"; the Council punishes him with fel cones; "I will have my vengeance!")
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Zenn's_Bidding and https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Seek_Redemption! (read)
- bibles/teldrassil.md "Never": what Zenn offers is not sourced, so he never says.

## Their quests
- Zenn's Bidding (gives/turn-in): nightsaber fangs, strigid owl feathers, webwood silk; "let's keep this our little secret"; "three cheers for the naive and gullible". Wants servants, secrecy, flattery.
- Seek Redemption! (turn-in, from Syral/Sentinels): the player feeds him fel cones and he becomes a frog. Vain, easily fooled by a snack, vengeful afterwards.
