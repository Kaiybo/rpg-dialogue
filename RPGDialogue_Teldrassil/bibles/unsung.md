# Teldrassil's tradespeople  (keys: jeena, kyra, cyndra, malorne, nadyia, radnaal, nessa, androl)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg (read, Classic and Forever parts only): Jeena Featherbow (bowyer, Dolanaar), Kyra Windblade (warrior trainer, Dolanaar; starts the Classic warrior quest Elanaria), Cyndra Kindwhisper (alchemy trainer, Dolanaar), Malorne Bladeleaf (herbalism trainer, Dolanaar; Forever: ends Camping 101: Herbalism), Nadyia Maneweaver (leatherworking trainer; Forever: ends Camping 101: Leatherworking; Classic gossip welcomes you to "our home" with Radnaal) and Radnaal Maneweaver (skinning trainer; Forever: ends Camping 101: Skinning), both northwest of Ban'ethil Hollow up the road from Dolanaar, Nessa Shadowsong (fishing supplies, Rut'theran Village; Classic quests Nessa Shadowsong, The Bounty of Teldrassil via Vesprystus, Flight to Auberdine to Laird, Return to Nessa), Androl Oakhand (fishing trainer, Rut'theran Village; his Classic "skill too high" line points to Old Man Heming's book in Booty Bay). All night elves.

## Their quests
- Kyra: Elanaria (gives; warriors go to Elanaria in Darnassus).
- Nessa: Nessa Shadowsong (ends), The Bounty of Teldrassil (gives), Return to Nessa (ends). Friendly courier network with Mydrannul, Vesprystus and Laird.
- Malorne, Nadyia, Radnaal (Forever): Camping 101 lessons from Lyreena Duskblade end with them.
- Jeena, Cyndra, Androl: none: vendor and trainers only.

## Identity
- **Jeena Featherbow** · Dolanaar · female · bowyer (service). Lively, frank; fallen wood only.
- **Kyra Windblade** · Dolanaar · female · warrior trainer (service). Steady, encouraging; sends students on to Elanaria.
- **Cyndra Kindwhisper** · Dolanaar · female · alchemy trainer (service). Soft-spoken; worries over the Gnarlpine.
- **Malorne Bladeleaf** · Dolanaar · male · herbalism trainer (service). Patient, reverent; leave the root.
- **Nadyia Maneweaver** · house up the road · female · leatherworking trainer (service). Hospitable, particular about small stitches.
- **Radnaal Maneweaver** · same house · male · skinning trainer (service). Quiet, gruff; minds waste.
- **Nessa Shadowsong** · Rut'theran · female · fishing supplies (service). Cheerful, chatty; loves the busy dock.
- **Androl Oakhand** · Rut'theran · male · fishing trainer (service). Slow, content.

- Jeena: hunters, warriors. Kyra: new warriors from Shadowglen, rogues. Cyndra: herbalists. Malorne: druids in bear or cat form. Nadyia: leatherworkers. Radnaal: a hunter's cat. Nessa: fishers. Androl: fishers past his teaching.

## Topics
- Jeena, Cyndra: furbolgs. Kyra: sentinels. Malorne: teldrassil, camping. Nadyia, Radnaal: camping. Nessa: teldrassil.
