## Voices
**Kyra Starsong.** Stern, precise, speaks for the Council of the Forest as if reading a scroll.
**Arynia Cloudsbreak.** Steady, grim; harpies and a lost runner.
**Laurna Morninglight.** Warm, earnest, would rather heal than teach; "child".
**Jannok Breezesong.** Jovial, hopeless romantic, oblivious; never mean.
**Seriadne.** Cheerful, talks to the animals first.
**Daryn Lightwind.** Chatty, precise, possessive of her books, delighted by turtles.

## Authored, not canon
- Kyra: the Council as Sentinel and druid elders under the Arch Druid and High Priestess; Zenn claims to want his old shape back (his own story in `zenn.lua`).
- Arynia: the runner's satchel found without the report; harpies blown over from the mainland.
- Laurna: Shaya refuses to come in when wounded; men serve Elune too, fewer of them.
- Jannok: thirty paintings and a statue facing the wall; the weather conversation remembered as meaningful.
- Seriadne: tells the animals stories of Cenarius, Aessina and the old spirit beasts (no later-named Wild Gods).
- Daryn: seasick on the Moonspray; her copy of Feralas: A History is the only one she's seen; argues with Greywhisker.

## Never
Cataclysm-era Teldrassil (Fidelio, Iranis, Irriende, Lor'danel boats), no Gilneas or worgen.
