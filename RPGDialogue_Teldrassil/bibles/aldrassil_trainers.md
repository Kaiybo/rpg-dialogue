# Aldrassil's class trainers (keys: mardant, alyissia, frahun, ayanna, shanda)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg: Mardant Strongoak, Alyissia, Frahun Shadewhisper, Ayanna Everstride, Shanda; quests Verdant Sigil, Simple Sigil, Encrypted Sigil, Etched Sigil, Hallowed Sigil (all Classic versions, from Conservator Ilthalaine), In Favor of Elune (Shanda to Laurna Morninglight).
- Kept consistent with `shadowglen.lua` (Ilthalaine and the sigils; Mardant upstairs) and the Dolanaar trainers (Kal, Dazalar, Jannok, Laurna).

## Their quests
- Each trainer is the end of their class's sigil quest; Shanda also gives In Favor of Elune. Service tier, with sigil lines in greetings.

## Voices
**Mardant Strongoak.** Slow, calm, translating from the trees; the forest's friend, not master or servant.
**Alyissia.** Stern drill-mistress, discipline first; a veteran.
**Frahun Shadewhisper.** Wry, smooth, amused by anyone who finds him.
**Ayanna Everstride.** Warm, steady; respect for the hunt, take only what you need.
**Shanda.** Gentle, devout; "child".

## Authored, not canon
- Mardant: Teldrassil "was not blessed as Nordrassil was" (the Classic-era worry, nothing later); the spirits "shout more than he'd like".
- Alyissia: fought at Hyjal against the Legion; trains so others needn't learn the way she did.
- Frahun: Jannok "writes poems about Syurna" (consistent with The Apple Falls and Syurna in Darnassus `enclave.lua`).
- Ayanna: carved the Etched Sigil herself.
- Shanda: the Sisterhood has always carried bows as well as prayers.

## Never
Cataclysm-era Shadowglen (Tarindrella, the class "Learning..." quests), anything about Teldrassil's later fate.
