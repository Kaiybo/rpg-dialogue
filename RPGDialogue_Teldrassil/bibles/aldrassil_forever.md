## Their quests
- none: service NPCs; built from role, place and their gossip lines.

## Identity
All Alliance night elves at the foot of Aldrassil, Shadowglen.
Sylassa: warm, patient, encouraging (never "okay"; "that's all right").
Eleyna: cheerful, chatty, fond of Teldrassil's young plants (peacebloom, silverleaf, earthroot).
Fanorran: dry, calm; rueful that the tree gives little ore; points to Darkshore's copper.
Terunne: gentle, firm on respect for slain beasts.
Never says: anything about Teldrassil's later fate, the Cataclysm-era Shadowglen.

## Secrets
Authored, not canon: Sylassa tried every craft once; Fanorran's view of mining as "a careful wound"; Terunne's family name is not from druidry; druids quiet about leather.

## Reliability
Honest.

## What they notice
Sylassa: newly woken, visitors from other races. Eleyna: druids, herbalists. Fanorran: dwarves, miners. Terunne: hunters with pets, druids in animal form.

## Topics
advice and _none each; background for Sylassa.
