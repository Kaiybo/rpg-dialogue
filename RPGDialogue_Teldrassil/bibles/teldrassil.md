## Their quests
- Ilthalaine: The Balance of Nature, Verdant Sigil. Melithar: The Woodland Protector.
- Tenaron: Tenaron's Summons, Crown of the Earth. Corithras: Crown of the Earth.
- Dirania: A Good Friend, A Friend in Need, Iverron's Antidote. Gilshalan: Webwood Venom, Webwood Egg.
- Athridas: Gnarlpine Corruption, The Relics of Wakening, Ursal the Mauler, A Troubling Breeze.
- Tallonkai: The Emerald Dreamcatcher, Ferocitas the Dream Eater, Twisted Hatred.
- Syral: Seek Redemption!, Denalan's Earth. Keldamyr: Dolanaar Delivery. Lyreena: The Great Outdoors.

## Never
- The Burning of Teldrassil, anyone's fate in it, or any hint of it.
- Fandral's later fall: Corithras says only that "the wells are less certain"; no one blames the Arch Druid.
- Kinship between Melithar and Fandral, or Athridas and Broll (speculation on the wiki).
- What Zenn Foulhoof offers, what the relics do, what the dreamcatcher does (not sourced).
- Gossip verbatim (Ilthalaine's "beauty of Shadowglen", Dirania's "coming back home", Syral's "embraces those who embrace the land", Tallonkai's "active part").

## Voices
- Ilthalaine: delighted, gentle conservator.
- Melithar: earnest young druid at the roots.
- Tenaron: warm elder atop Aldrassil; blessings.
- Dirania: wistful, worried for Iverron.
- Gilshalan: solitary spider-watcher, dry.
- Corithras: grave keeper of the moonwells.
- Athridas: stern druid who'd rather cure than kill.
- Tallonkai: approving, protective.
- Syral: composed messenger of the Council.
- Keldamyr: gentle innkeeper.
- Lyreena: easygoing camper (authored).

## Depth pass (0.24.0, authored, not canon)
Ilthalaine counts nightsaber cubs; Melithar listens to Aldrassil hum and measures days in leaves; Tenaron watches the young walk down to Dolanaar; Dirania and Iverron grew up together in the glade; Gilshalan studies spiders partly because they frighten him; Corithras keeps tasting the third well; the Gnarlpine once traded furs for herbs at Dolanaar's edge (authored; their corruption is sourced, the trade isn't); Tallonkai's dreamcatcher was a gift; Syral sorts the Council's orders; Keldamyr's Sentinels and their tea.
