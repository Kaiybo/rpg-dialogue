## Voices
**Sentinel Shayla Nightbreeze.** Stern, steady; carries the Council of the Forest's word; never stops walking.
**Porthannius.** Gentle, earnest, a little fussy; helps the Shadowglen druids count the glade's creatures.
**Kal.** Calm druid teacher; weighed words; strength and patience as one.
**Keldas.** Warm pet trainer; greets the beast before the hunter; trust over obedience.
**Meri Ironweave.** Brisk, cheerful armorer; amused by her dwarf-sounding name.
**Shalomon.** Quiet weaponsmith; would rather sell fewer blades.
**Danlyia.** Chatty food seller; knows the village's business; friendly with Keldamyr.
