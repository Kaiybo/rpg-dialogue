# More Wetlands folk (npcs/more_folk.lua)

Keys: mf_andrea, mf_comar.

## Sources
warcraft.wiki.gg via `tools/npc.py`, read 2026-11-18: Andrea Halloran (wife of James Halloran, mother of Jesse and Regina; scripted scene of Jesse climbing and Regina telling on him), Comar Villard (wanders near the Thandol Span searching for his lost wife; his heart was the target of the Horde warlock quest Hearts of the Pure, removed 4.0.3).

## Their quests
- None given. Comar is the objective of the Horde warlock quest Hearts of the Pure (not referenced in dialogue).

## Voices
- **Andrea**: harried, warm mother, half-listening while watching the children; teases James about the tannery smell. Consistent with `halloran.lua`.
- **Comar**: gentle, mournful, polite; never stops looking; thin and worn without noticing it.

## Authored, not canon
Comar's wife's laugh and bad singing. Nothing about where or how she was lost.

# Missed folk, added later (npcs/more_folk.lua)

## Sources
warcraft.wiki.gg via `tools/npc.py`, read 2026-11-18: each NPC's page. Fradd's later quests (Gizmos and Gadgets, I'll Call Him Bitey) and "Swiftgear Station" are post-Classic and NOT used; he and Wenna are written at their Classic roadside camp.

## Their quests
- none: Kersok Prond (Tradesman, Deepwater Tavern), Gruham Rumdnul (General Supplies), Dewin Shimmerdawn (night elf, Alchemy Supplies), Brak Durnad (Weaponsmith), Jennabink Powerseam (gnome, Tailoring Supplies & Specialty Goods, cart between the inn and the gryphons), Fradd Swiftgear (gnome, Engineering Supplies), Wenna Silkbeard (Special Goods Dealer), Kixxle (goblin, Potions & Herbs): vendors, built from role, place and neighbours.

## Voices
- **Kersok**: shrewd, genial dwarf; ships and tides. **Gruham**: weathered, wry; looks at boots. **Dewin**: serene night elf, "Ishnu-alah" once; finds the marsh beautiful. **Brak**: gruff, tireless; rust. **Jennabink**: bright gnome; wax, seams, percentages. **Fradd**: cheerful, distracted gnome; testing in bad weather. **Wenna**: canny, warm dwarf; watches the road. **Kixxle**: goblin per CONTENT_GUIDE 2: overhead, captive market, sells to both sides, "pal".

## Authored, not canon
Kersok buying at the dock; Gruham's eleven years and guards-at-cost; Dewin's willows and the draining question; Brak's fish hooks; Jennabink's unclaimed patterns; Fradd's bells and rust table, his partnership with Wenna; Wenna leaving bread for Comar Villard; Kixxle's herb pickers and jerky for the gnolls.
