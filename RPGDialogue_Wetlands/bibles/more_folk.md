# More Wetlands folk (npcs/more_folk.lua)

Keys: mf_andrea, mf_comar.

## Sources
warcraft.wiki.gg via `tools/npc.py`, read 2026-11-18: Andrea Halloran (wife of James Halloran, mother of Jesse and Regina; scripted scene of Jesse climbing and Regina telling on him), Comar Villard (wanders near the Thandol Span searching for his lost wife; his heart was the target of the Horde warlock quest Hearts of the Pure, removed 4.0.3).

## Their quests
- None given. Comar is the objective of the Horde warlock quest Hearts of the Pure (not referenced in dialogue).

## Voices
- **Andrea**: harried, warm mother, half-listening while watching the children; teases James about the tannery smell. Consistent with `halloran.lua`.
- **Comar**: gentle, mournful, polite; never stops looking; thin and worn without noticing it.

## Authored, not canon
Comar's wife's laugh and bad singing. Nothing about where or how she was lost.

# Missed folk, second pass (npcs/more_folk.lua)

Keys: mf_naela, mf_edwina, mf_falkan (vendors); mf_tidewater, mf_adam, mf_pompa (ship and piers); mf_hargin, mf_junder (Deepwater Tavern); mf_murphy, mf_timothy (keep); mf_caitlin (quest).

## Sources
warcraft.wiki.gg via `tools/npc.py`, read 2026-11-18: each NPC's page. Classic placements used: Murphy West in the keep's basement, Timothy Clark as a hopeful (not yet a guard or lieutenant), Junder Brokk inside the tavern, Don Pompa on the Auberdine pier (removed 3.0.2), Adam Lind on the Theramore pier, Bart Tidewater aboard the Maiden's Virtue (afloat, docked; in Classic she never sails). Nothing about the flood, sandbags or Northrend.

## Their quests
- **Caitlin Grassman** (house by the smithy): Forever quests Lost in the Thicket Things (written as given by her; ends at Ardin in the excavation) and Heartwoven (from Ardin, written as ending with her). Lines for active/done; see `wetlands_forever.md`. Who gives/ends each is inferred from Ardin's lines.
- none: Naela Trance and Edwina Monzor (Bowyers), Falkan Armonis (Reagents), Bart Tidewater (Captain of the Maiden's Virtue, docked navy ship; the Theramore boat is the Lady Mehley), Adam Lind (Theramore pier directions), Don Pompa (Auberdine pier directions), Hargin Mundar and Junder Brokk (tavern regulars), Murphy West (keep stores), Timothy Clark.

## Voices
- **Naela**: steady, wry; wax, strings, the damp. **Edwina**: brisk, proud; heavy bows, gull fletching. **Falkan**: courteous, fretful; sealing wax and salt boxes.
- **Tidewater**: weathered, even; watches passengers. **Adam**: easygoing, kind. **Don**: gruff, thinks about coppers.
- **Hargin**: merry dwarf drunk, the floodgate story (wiki: built a floodgate in Ironforge, trapped himself, was tunnelled out). **Junder**: dry, patient dwarf who watches the door.
- **Murphy**: idle, sardonic, works when it matters. **Timothy**: eager, earnest, a boy's run-on sentences.
- **Caitlin**: worried, capable, dry about her husband; consistent with Ardin in `wetlands_forever.lua` (alchemy, stew, rolling her eyes).

## Authored, not canon
Naela and Edwina's tea and their light/heavy bow argument; gull feathers from Stuart Fleming; Edwina billing Stoutfist; Falkan's glowing fish; Tidewater's Kul Tiran-built ship, its guns and the Bloodsail surrender; the dwarf who took the wrong boat; Don's copper a question; Hargin's handle on the wrong side and his sea wall drawing; Junder as a Loch Modan stonehauler who dug Hargin out; Murphy's rat and the powder on the stairs; Timothy under the cart at nine and Karl Boran's drill; Caitlin meeting Ardin over swiftthistle, the second bowl going to Brak Durnad.

Hargin: the drunk, gnomes. Junder: the green, dwarves. Murphy: plate-wearers, the green. Timothy: veterans, warriors. Caitlin: herbalists and alchemists, healers.
