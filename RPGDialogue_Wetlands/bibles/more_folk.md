# More Wetlands folk (npcs/more_folk.lua)

Keys: mf_andrea, mf_comar.

## Sources
warcraft.wiki.gg via `tools/npc.py`, read 2026-11-18: Andrea Halloran (wife of James Halloran, mother of Jesse and Regina; scripted scene of Jesse climbing and Regina telling on him), Comar Villard (wanders near the Thandol Span searching for his lost wife; his heart was the target of the Horde warlock quest Hearts of the Pure, removed 4.0.3).

## Their quests
- None given. Comar is the objective of the Horde warlock quest Hearts of the Pure (not referenced in dialogue).

## Voices
- **Andrea**: harried, warm mother, half-listening while watching the children; teases James about the tannery smell. Consistent with `halloran.lua`.
- **Comar**: gentle, mournful, polite; never stops looking; thin and worn without noticing it.

## Authored, not canon
Comar's wife's laugh and bad singing. Nothing about where or how she was lost.
