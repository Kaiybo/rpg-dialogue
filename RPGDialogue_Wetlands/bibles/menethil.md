## Voices
- Stoutfist: fierce dwarf captain on the wall; wants bare banner poles and Nek'rosh's head; proud of the name on the gate.
- Fitzsimmons: maudlin, generous drunk; the bay is a graveyard; jokes about his own smell; tries to keep your purse.
- Glorin: grumbling, overworked priest; prayer and wet socks; secretly glad of a colleague.
- Harlo: bitter, bedridden, proud; "face 'em, always"; asks after his regiment first.
- Karl: steady worker; the next crate; "get it home".
- Helbrek: jolly host; keeps Fitz's mead.
- Flagongut: eager, stranded archaeologist; raptors on the road, his stone in a raptor.
- Mikhail: quiet, watchful; watches the door; misses the old work and won't say so to Elling.
