- Fitzsimmons: The Third Fleet, The Cursed Crew, Lifting the Curse (The Greenwarden not referenced).
- Glorin: The Eye of Paleth, Cleansing the Eye.
- Harlo: Fall of Dun Modr.
- Karl: Claws from the Deep, Reclaiming Goods, Return the Statuette (lines stay general: their pages give no objectives).
- Helbrek: none: innkeeper only.
- Flagongut: The Absent Minded Prospector.
- Mikhail: The Missing Diplomat.

## Voices
- Stoutfist: fierce dwarf captain on the wall; wants bare banner poles and Nek'rosh's head.
- Fitzsimmons: maudlin, generous drunk; the bay is a graveyard; jokes about his own smell.
- Glorin: grumbling, overworked priest; prayer and wet socks.
- Harlo: bitter, bedridden, proud; "face 'em, always".
- Karl: steady worker; the next crate.
- Helbrek: jolly host; keeps Fitz's mead.
- Flagongut: eager, stranded archaeologist; orcs on his dig.
- Mikhail: quiet, watchful; watches the door; misses the old work (authored, from the wiki's hint) and won't say so to Elling.
