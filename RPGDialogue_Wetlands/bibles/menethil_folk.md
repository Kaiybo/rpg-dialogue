# Menethil Harbor folk (keys: wl_slim, wl_fremal, wl_telurinon, wl_bethaine, wl_stuart, wl_brahnmar)

Wiki pages read 2026-11-17 (warcraft.wiki.gg): Menethil Harbor, Tapoke "Slim" Jahn, The Missing Diplomat (11) and (12), Fremal Doohickey, Telurinon Moonshadow, Bethaine Flinthammer, Stuart Fleming, Brahnmar.

## Sources
- **Tapoke "Slim" Jahn** (male human rogue, Deepwater Tavern): subdued for "The Missing Diplomat" (part 11, Mikhail), then starts part 12. His confession: the Defias paid him to get their man, Private Hendel, aboard the king's ship in the harbor; the king was on his way to Theramore; "they're working with someone". Mikhail: "Even a rogue's got to have honor." Classic only (removed 4.0.3a). Uses the chain-wide `TODO_QUEST("The Missing Diplomat")` like Mikhail in `menethil.lua`.
- **Fremal Doohickey** (male gnome, first aid trainer, Deepwater Tavern); his high-skill gossip sends you to Doctor Gustaf VanHowzen in Theramore.
- **Telurinon Moonshadow** (male night elf, herbalism trainer).
- **Bethaine Flinthammer** (female dwarf stable master outside the Deepwater Tavern).
- **Stuart Fleming** (male human, fishing supplies and fish, northern dock).
- **Brahnmar** (male dwarf, armor vendor).

## Voices
- **Slim**: quick, evasive, funny, frightened underneath; never admits more than he must; insists he only "held the door".
- **Fremal**: brisk, kind, a gnome who gave up gears for linen; lectures on tourniquets.
- **Telurinon**: slow, polite, a little sad; winces at digging. "Ishnu-alah".
- **Bethaine**: sharp-tongued dwarf, losing a war with mud.
- **Stuart**: loud, friendly fishmonger; tells stories about the wrecks.
- **Brahnmar**: grizzled dwarf armorer obsessed with rust.

## Authored, not canon
- Slim: from Stormwind; ran small smuggling jobs (berths for runaways, lost crates); the man who paid him had "very clean hands" (a hint only, nobody named).
- Fremal: fled Gnomeregan with a lab of inventions and a pocket of bandages; three tourniquet designs nobody wants.
- Telurinon: stays because the harbor ships go to Auberdine and to study marsh plants; the dwarves are kind to him.
- Bethaine: imports straw from Dun Morogh at double price; dragons overhead spook the ponies.
- Stuart: the only fish seller on the dock; Third Fleet wreckage in the bay full of fish and rumoured drowned sailors.
- Brahnmar: oils his stock twice a day; worries about adventurers bound for Grim Batol.
