## Identity / voice
- Adam Lind · human man · patient dockside signpost; Auberdine pier north, Theramore pier south.
- Bart Tidewater · human man · gruff navy captain, proud of holding his mooring; touchy about never sailing.
- Brak Durnad · dwarf man · weaponsmith; rust is the enemy; burr.
- Caitlin Grassman · human woman · alchemist, warm and anxious; husband Ardin overdue from the excavation; writes to Llana of Astranaar.
- Derina Rumdnul · dwarf woman · harried mother, keeps the ledger; "where has that boy gotten to".
- Gruham Rumdnul · dwarf man · jovial general supplier; knows what travellers forget.
- Gimlok Rumdnul · dwarf boy · crabs, docks, plays with the Halloran children. Childlike, short.
- Dewin Shimmerdawn · night elf man · measured, formal; respects the marsh more than the townsfolk do.
- Edwina Monzor · human woman · brisk bowyer, fast hands; damp is her war.
- Naela Trance · human woman · quiet bowyer, reads the grain; shares a bench with Edwina.
- Falkan Armonis · human man · dry reagent seller, no magic himself.
- Jennabink Powerseam · gnome woman · chatty, measures thread strength; cart between the inn and the gryphon roost.
- Hargin Mundar · dwarf man · jolly tavern regular; the Ironforge floodgate was "an honest mistake".
- Junder Brokk · dwarf man · dry, teases Hargin about the floodgate; fond of him underneath.
- Kersok Prond · dwarf man · plain tradesman with a stall in the Deepwater Tavern.
- Jesse Halloran · human boy · climbs the wind tower to hide. Childlike.
- Regina Halloran · human girl · prim, tells on Jesse because it's dangerous. Childlike.
- Murphy West · human man · easygoing cellarman under Menethil Keep.
- Thomas Booker · human man · cheerful labourer; friendly feud with Tarrel Rockweaver.
- Kixxle · goblin man · potions and herbs at the crossroads; everything is location and margin.
- Mountaineer Thar · dwarf man · brisk sentry on the Dun Algaz road.
- Roggo Harlbarrow · dwarf man · grim rifleman of Longbraid's company; couldn't get word out when the Span fell.
- Terl Arakor · human man · possessive of his wagon; rumours of the marsh's beasts and the Dragonmaw.

## Authored, not canon
- Bart: his crew grumbles that he loves the mooring; he and Stoutfist split water and walls.
- Brak: whale oil against rust; friendly with Murndan Derth.
- Caitlin: brews for the guard, buys vials from Dewin; herb names along the water.
- The Rumdnuls came down from Loch Modan; Gimlok races crabs and named one Magni; hides in barrels.
- Dewin crossed on the Auberdine ship to study marsh plants.
- Edwina ships staves from Elwynn; sells to hunters who supply Halloran. Naela's bows are the ones the guard ask for.
- Falkan's grave-dust customer. Jennabink's snapped-spool tests and the gryphons stealing red silk.
- Hargin worked Ironforge's water channels; the floodgate/dining-hall tale is from the wiki's (Cataclysm-era) tavern banter, retold without the flood.
- Kersok's stall is in the tavern so craftsmen find him; Halloran's barrel of salt a month.
- Jesse wants to be a sailor in a crow's nest; Regina saw him fall once.
- Murphy keeps the keep's stores (wiki: he "hung out in the keep's basement"); Thomas hauls for the keep (wiki only has his Cataclysm sandbag duty and tossing dirt at Tarrel).
- Kixxle sells to both sides and keeps something in the cart that goes bang.
- Thar's posting details; Roggo's account of the Span (sappers from below) follows our Longbraid/Rhag bibles.
- Terl's rumours are his wiki gossip retold for Classic: Sarltooth leads the raptors at Whelgar's (not undead), Mosshide gnoll versus threshadon, Dragonmaw at Angerfang, the matriarch in Raptor Ridge.

## Never
- Flooded Menethil, sharks in the streets, the sunk Maiden's Virtue, Cataclysm quests.
- Twilight's Hammer at Dun Modr or anything past year 1.
