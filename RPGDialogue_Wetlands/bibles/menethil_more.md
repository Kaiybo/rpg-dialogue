## Their quests
- Valstag: The Algaz Gauntlet (turn-in), Report to Captain Stoutfist, Spoils of War (Forever). Worried by why the Dragonmaw surged.
- Tarrel: In Search of The Excavation Team (both parts), Unrequited Love (Forever, turn-in). Fear for Merrin; longs for peace.
- Vincent: James Hyal (both parts share the title; lines fit either). Proud of his brother; doesn't know about the Shady Rest (dramatic irony only).
- Red Jack: Fiora Longears. Romantic about the sea; recruiter's patter.
- Sida: Digging Through the Ooze. Private about the tomb and the bag; feuding with Einar.
- Riggs, Camerick, Unger: none.

## Identity
- Valstag Ironjaw · Menethil Keep · dwarf man · soldier. Gruff, dutiful, tired; dwarf burr ("an'", "o'", "ye").
- Tarrel Rockweaver · harbour bridge · dwarf man · citizen (a weaver, authored from the name). Anxious, loving; burr.
- Vincent Hyal · Deepwater Tavern · human man · citizen. Warm, proud, wistful.
- Red Jack Flint · Menethil Keep · human man · recruiter. Theatrical, romantic, sailor's patter.
- Sida · Menethil · dwarf woman · questgiver. Flustered, prickly, private; burr.
- Harold Riggs · Menethil · human man · fishing trainer. Jovial, chatty.
- Camerick Jongleur · Menethil · human man · minstrel. Cheerful, self-mocking, rhymes badly.
- Unger Statforth · Menethil · human man · horse breeder. Proud, careful.

- Sida: "throw yer shoe".
