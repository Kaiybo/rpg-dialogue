# Rethiel the Greenwarden  (key: wl_rethiel)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Rethiel_the_Greenwarden (read): bog beast at Greenwarden's Grove; gossip "The land suffers... It cries for mercy". Forever quests listed: Carnage, Horrors in the Highland.
- Quest text read: The Greenwarden (Fitzsimmons gives; Rethiel's completion), Tramping Paws, Fire Taboo, Blisters on The Land, Horrors in the Highland (Forever).
- First Mate Fitzsimmons (menethil.lua) already sends players to "the Greenwarden".

## Their quests
- The Greenwarden (turn-in): "The roots and ferns speak well of you, small one... I have many names, I answer to only a few... call me Rethiel"; the lands weep from festered wounds.
- Tramping Paws (gives): Mosshide gnolls were peaceful for years, now overgrown; they trample the fen and cut too much wood; "no longer in balance"; his faith in the player's people "has been taxed in the past".
- Fire Taboo (gives): the Mosshides burn the trees' flesh; "I will suffer this pain no longer"; smashes the flints and casts them into the sea.
- Blisters on The Land (gives): fen creepers diseased and fevered; they must be dispatched to settle back into the marsh.
- Horrors in the Highland (Forever, gives): corruption seeps through the mountain gully into unsalted highland waters; "an ancient energy stirs"; warn the excavators to stop digging.
