# RPG Dialogue: Wetlands (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.19.0 (2026-11-17)
- New conversations for 4 people who had none: Rhag Garmason, Rethiel the Greenwarden, James Halloran, Merrin Rockweaver. New files: needs a full game restart, not /reload. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.72.0.

## 1.18.0 (2026-11-17)

## 1.17.0 (2026-11-17)

## 1.16.0 (2026-11-17)

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)
- Depth rewrite: every Menethil NPC now has a conversation to explore (a hub with branches two or three deep, "what else?" lines, Ask-about takes on their own story and advice). All eight meet the depth standard.
- Captain Stoutfist: the Dragonmaw and Nek'rosh's grudge against the red dragons, Grim Batol behind its dragonkin, the harbour as the Alliance's back door and the king's name on the gate, the Dark Irons in the north, the night watch. Replies for dwarves (Ironforge's frontier), gnomes (the catapults), priests (the wounded and the dead), Lordaeron refugees (Terenas's name), and after Nek'rosh falls; the statuette goes straight to Fordragon (Forever's A Friend of the Family).
- First Mate Fitzsimmons: his dreams, the two wrecks and Captain Halyndor's strongbox, the Greenwarden (leave your purse with him), a flagon of mead; a toast if you've been drinking, a prayer if you're a priest, and what he hears after the Eye is cleansed.
- Glorin Steelbrow: the Third Fleet and the Flying Osprey's lightforge iron (for Bishop Farthing's errand, and Grimand Elmore in Stormwind), what an Eye of Paleth is and why he can't clean it, Fitzsimmons and the mead; he asks a priest where they trained.
- Harlo Barnaby: the Thandol Span ambush, Longbraid's horn, the wound; a priest can pray over it, a dwarf can promise Ironforge will hear, and news that Longbraid lives (or that Balgaras is dead) lands.
- Karl Boran: the Bluegill raid on the Blind Princess and the statuette of a young King Terenas (a Lordaeron refugee answers differently), the boats and the marines on payday, night elves off the Auberdine boat.
- Innkeeper Helbrek, Archaeologist Flagongut (the Stone of Relu in a raptor, the conference that laughed; League apprentices and night elves get their own replies) and Mikhail (the room he watches, Samor Festivus upstairs, Elling's errand, a rogue's eye).
- Corrected: the Dragonmaw camp is in the hills (Angerfang), and raptors, not orcs, sit on Whelgar's dig.
- Voice pass: every line in the pack, Menethil's guards included, now reads as whole sentences in the speaker's own voice (a dwarf's burr, a drunk sailor's run-ons, a retired spy's dry asides) instead of clipped fragments. Same meanings, facts and line ids.
