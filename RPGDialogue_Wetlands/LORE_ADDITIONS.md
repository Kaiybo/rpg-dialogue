**Assumptions (confirm in game):**
- Karl Boran's quests: the wiki merges Classic and Cataclysm versions (the pages read describe the Cataclysm flood and Gobbler). The Blind Princess and the statuette are taken as Classic (item 2625 is a Classic item ID); Claws from the Deep stays general.
- The Theramore boat is not named (the wiki's "Lady Mehley" may be the Classic name; not used).
- `visited = "Ironforge"` (Flagongut's notice, unchanged) depends on what subzone the client reports inside Ironforge.
- Forever's Stoutfist quests without wiki pages (Forced Disarmament, From the Ashes, Old Habits, This Land Was Their Land) are not referenced.

**Year 1 notes:** No Cataclysm (flood, crabs, Gobbler, Fitzsimmons dead, Horghast, the Twilight's Hammer in Dun Modr, Thargas Anvilmar's victory). No Night of the Dragon (raptor attacks from Grim Batol). Derek Proudmoore and the Battle of Crestfall left out (later sources). Mikhail never names the king: the Missing Diplomat parts share one title, so the engine can't tell the reveal from the start. Aman left out for the same reason.

**Corrections to earlier content (ids kept):** Stoutfist's `h_banners` and Flagongut's `h_default`, `h_stoutfist`, `d_default`, `tp_dragonmaw` said the Dragonmaw camped on Whelgar's dig; the sources put them at Angerfang in the hills, with raptors on the dig.

**Authored, not canon:** see "Depth rewrite (Wetlands 1.2.0)" in `bibles/menethil.md` (Stoutfist's night watch and burial ground, Fitzsimmons's dreams and mutiny joke, Glorin hiding the mead and asking where a priest trained, Harlo counting Dark Irons, Karl's lantern count, Helbrek's good mugs, Flagongut's two ales, Mikhail's full mug).
