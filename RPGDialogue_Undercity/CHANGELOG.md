# RPG Dialogue: Undercity (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Replies for the classes and races these people would really react to (warriors, hunters, rogues, mages, warlocks, druids, shamans, paladins; every race of the faction, and the Skyborne), each leading somewhere.

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-15)
- Every person here writes a first impression in your diary after you first talk (the journal's People page shows it too).

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.1.1 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: the Royal Quarter, the Apothecarium, the Magic Quarter and Norman's inn now talk in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments, each in their own voice (Sylvanas cold and controlled, Varimathras silky, Faranell gleeful, Bethor grieving, Norman dry). Same lines, same facts, same conditions.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits bring personal lines (habits, worries, running jokes), and some NPCs get night-time lines. Norman.

## 1.0.1 (unreleased)
- Sylvanas knows Executor Zygand's work in Tirisfal; Bethor hears Renferrel keeps finding Thule's name in Silverpine.

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- Lady Sylvanas Windrunner and Varimathras in the Royal Quarter; Master Apothecary Faranell in the Apothecarium; Bethor Iceshard in the Magic Quarter; Innkeeper Norman. The Undercity's guards are in the World pack.
