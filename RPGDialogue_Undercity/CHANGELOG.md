# RPG Dialogue: Undercity (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.25.0 (2026-11-18)
- 71 named NPCs the earlier passes missed now have conversations (`npcs/missed_a.lua`, `npcs/missed_a2.lua`, `npcs/missed_a3.lua`, `npcs/missed_b.lua`, `npcs/missed_b2.lua`, `npcs/missed_b3.lua`, `npcs/missed_estelle.lua`, `npcs/missed_genavie.lua`, `npcs/missed_oran.lua`, `npcs/missed_c.lua`). New files: restart the game fully.

## 1.24.0 (2026-11-18)
- "Where is...?" directions for the Undercity (`directions.lua`, new file: restart the game fully).

## 1.23.0 (2026-11-18)
- New NPCs from the Classic-era roster (`npcs/more_folk.lua`). New files: restart the game fully.
- NPCs notice what you have done: quests for their neighbours, notable kills, your deaths, rank and reputation.
- Writing pass: stance replies end on something concrete, fewer echoes and stock phrases.
- Druid greetings that assume a night elf or tauren no longer fire for other druid races.

## 1.22.0 (2026-11-18)
- Writing pass: repeated phrases cut and replaced with concrete detail.
- Clarity pass: answers answer the question, names are introduced, quest lines stand alone, fewer contradictions.
- Some NPCs of the opposite sex flirt with you, and remember how you answered (needs core 0.83.0).
- NPCs who had only a few lines now have full conversations.
- NPCs answer each stance reply before the talk moves on.

## 1.21.0 (2026-11-17)
- Named NPCs answer many more "Ask about" topics (3 to 6 each, more for leaders), mostly the new world topics added in World 1.36.0.
- Prejudices (0.80.0): Royal Overseer Bauhaus (the living). They let it show, you can push back or win them round, and it fades as they warm to you. No new files: a /reload is enough.
- New: Gordon Wendham, weapon merchant, holds every living thing in contempt, Horde allies included, since his apprentice joined the Scarlets and shot him. Undead players get a warmer welcome; a [Persuade] can crack him.
- New file: needs a full game restart, not /reload.
- People here notice your other characters: kin, look-alikes, faces they remember (needs core 0.80.0).
- New Forever-only characters (new files: full game restart): Fourteen Forever residents: Morbin Lightbane, Tanis Alderwood, Garen Largo, Glix Xizzix, Rugbul Boomfirst (Darkspear Islands), Alyz, Barrelmaker Ishtar, Malus, Angela Ward, Faith Duggins, Rakkamar, Anya Rince, Chadsworth, Zeglo Kruptorr.
- New people to talk to: Chemist Cuely, Chemist Fuely, Parqual Fintallas, Sharlindra, Jorah Annison, Patrick Garrett, Raleigh Andrean, Velora Nitely.
- New files: restart the game fully (a /reload won't load them).
- Origin nods (core 0.81.0): people here now notice the newer backgrounds from 0.70.0.
