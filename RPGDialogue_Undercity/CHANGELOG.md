# RPG Dialogue: Undercity (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.20.0 (2026-11-17)
- Every named NPC answers more "Ask about" topics (3 to 6 each, more for leaders), mostly the new world topics from World 1.35.0.

## 1.19.1 (2026-11-17)
- Writing pass on the characters added in 0.74.0: stock phrases ("fair enough", "truly", "worth more than") rewritten.

## 1.19.0 (2026-11-17)
- New people to talk to (see the main changelog for 0.74.0). New files: restart the game fully (a /reload won't load them).

## 1.18.0 (2026-11-17)
- Writing pass: stock phrases ("Fair enough", "I won't pretend", "truly", neat little morals) rewritten in each speaker's own voice, and more grit and humour in the everyday lines.

## 1.17.0 (2026-11-17)
- Lady Sylvanas has a great deal more to say: her kingdom, the Apothecarium, the Scarlet Crusade, her past and the Horde, with a new Persuade.
- People here now and then recognise where you come from (your origin) and react to it.

## 1.16.0 (2026-11-17)
- The town's leading people greet you differently when you come back after a fortnight or more, notice how far you've come since they last saw you, and have heard what you did with their neighbours. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.67.0.

## 1.15.1 (2026-11-17)
- Norman the innkeeper no longer repeats his full greeting when you come back to his questions. Dalaran is now spoken of as rebuilding, not sealed under its dome.

## 1.15.0 (2026-11-17)
- Some people here notice who you travel with: a greeting about your companions, a reply to bring them up, and a conversation with stances to take. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.59.0.

## 1.14.0 (2026-11-17)
- People with a stake in a nearby dungeon or raid react once you've cleared it: a greeting, a reply to bring it up, and a conversation about what it means to them, with stances to take. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.58.0.

## 1.13.0 (2026-11-17)
- Every class this place could plausibly meet now gets a greeting and its own conversation, including this server's Forsaken paladins and dwarf shamans where they'd be welcome. Some people here also notice when you've been thinning out the local trouble.

## 1.12.0 (2026-11-17)
- Troll and Forsaken priests are noticed: 17 people greet a Horde priest as one, with a question to ask and something to argue about. No new files. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.52.0.

## 1.11.0 (2026-11-17)
- Warriors and hunters are noticed: about 28 new warrior and hunter conversations; people greet a warrior or hunter as one, with a question to ask and something to argue about, and hunters with a beast at their side hear it named. No new files. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.50.0.

## 1.10.0 (2026-11-16)
- Horde rogues, mages and warlocks get their own conversation with 17 people in the Undercity, Sylvanas included.

## 1.9.0 (2026-11-16)

## 1.8.0 (2026-11-16)

- Innkeeper Norman now has replies for Horde warriors and hunters.

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-16)
