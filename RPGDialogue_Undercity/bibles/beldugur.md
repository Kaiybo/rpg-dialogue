# Keeper Bel'dugur  (key: beldugur)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Keeper_Bel%27dugur (read: Forsaken in the Apothecarium; gossip "Knowledge is both tool and weapon to those with the will to use it"; the wiki infers from a later quest that he may be Twilight's Hammer)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Book_of_Ur_(quest) (read)

## Their quests
- The Book of Ur (gives/turn-in): Ur, a great mage of Dalaran; his studies of other worlds are of value to "certain parties among the Forsaken"; "His knowledge was great, but his conscience held him from true power... We of the Forsaken cannot afford such weakness, if we are to survive... If we are to conquer." Ambitious, contemptuous of scruple, hints at hidden patrons.

## Identity
Keeper Bel'dugur · Apothecarium, Undercity · Undercity · Forsaken · keeper of books
Personality: cold, acquisitive, patient, contemptuous of scruple
Speech: aphorisms; soft, measured sentences
Knows and cares about: Ur, Arugal, Dalaran's forbidden studies, his collection
Relationships: "certain parties" he won't name
Default attitudes: warlocks are kindred; priests and paladins are timid
Lore anchors: Ur and the Book of Ur; Arugal of Dalaran
Never says: Twilight's Hammer, Old Gods, or anything that names his patrons
