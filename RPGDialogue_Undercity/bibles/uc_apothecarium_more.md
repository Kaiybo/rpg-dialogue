## Voices
**Chemist Cuely.** Cheerful, clinical, interested in Thersa's symptoms more than her comfort; "fascinating".
**Chemist Fuely.** Smiling, fervent; Sylvanas as a beacon; slimes as records of the land.
**Parqual Fintallas.** Sceptical, sharp, scornful of brawn; a starved reader.

## Authored, not canon
- Cuely and Fuely were chemists together in Lordaeron (Fuely: tinctures for the nobility); they share a bench and arguments.
- Parqual wrote histories of noble houses and knows which lied about their ancestors; his books were taken at the gate when he tried to fetch them (the wiki's novel section mentions smuggling and confiscation; his family is left out).
