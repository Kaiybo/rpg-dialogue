# Undercity Forever folk  (file: npcs/uc_forever_folk.lua)

Nine Forsaken added to the Undercity in World of Warcraft: Forever (patch 1.60.1). The wiki gives each a place and little else, so their lives are small and authored, built from where they stand and who stands near them.

## Their quests
- none: none of the nine gives or ends a quest.

## Identity
- **Alyz** (uc_alyz) · Canals. Odd, calm, fond of the slime. Fishes lost things from the canal and keeps them in a sack. Whole sentences, a little dreamy.
- **Barrelmaker Ishtar** (uc_ishtar) · Canals. Brisk, proud of her craft. Seals casks for the Apothecarium, the Deathguard and the zeppelin goblins and makes a point of not knowing what goes in them.
- **Malus** (uc_malus) · Canals to War Quarter. Dour, steady. Walks the round because when he stops he remembers.
- **Angela Ward** (uc_angela_ward) · Magic Quarter vendor. Prim, dry, exact about stitching; sells patterns, not bags.
- **Faith Duggins** (uc_faith_duggins) · Magic Quarter. Wry, warm mage; kept her living name; argues with Rakkamar to pass the nights.
- **Rakkamar** (uc_rakkamar) · Magic Quarter. Cold, proud; took a hard new name; studies the warlocks' binding circles without yet calling anything.
- **Anya Rince** (uc_anya_rince) · Apothecarium/Royal Quarter. Guarded, brisk runner who never opens what she carries.
- **Chadsworth** (uc_chadsworth) · War Quarter. Formal valet's English, dry humour, judges everyone's polish.
- **Zeglo Kruptorr** (uc_zeglo) · Rogues' Quarter. Sour, ambitious acolyte who feeds the trainers' imps.
Never says: anything after Year 1; no hints of the apothecaries' or Varimathras's later betrayals beyond Classic's own unease about the Society.

## What they notice   (2–3 things)
- Alyz: the living near the water; rogues. Ishtar: dwarves (rival coopers); casters feeling her ward. Malus: old soldiers' walk; Forsaken warriors. Angela: tailors; casters with loose reagents. Faith: Dalaran apprentices; mages. Rakkamar: warlocks; Forsaken paladins. Anya: rogues eyeing her satchel; the living. Chadsworth: broken armour; tauren. Zeglo: warlocks; imps near his chalk.

## Topics
- background, advice, _none for each; Ishtar also trade.

## Authored, not canon
- Alyz scrubbed pots in the palace kitchens; she scavenges the canals and keeps finds in a sack under a bridge; the wedding ring with two names; the man with the pipe.
- Ishtar's trade: barrels for the Apothecarium, Deathguard and zeppelin goblins; a sealing charm learned from a Dalaran pedlar; her father was a cooper for the king's cellars in the capital; one cask she looked into.
- Malus walked the night watch in the capital under Captain Landen (Garek Bandarion's real captain) on an invented "Canal Gate" beat, and was on the wall the night Arthas came home.
- Angela is Victor Ward's sister (the wiki only says relative); she and Carolyn Ward have never asked each other.
- Faith Duggins is a Dalaran-trained mage who kept her name; Rakkamar is a self-renamed caster who studies warlock binding and won't give his old name; the two argue nightly.
- Anya Rince is a runner carrying Faranell's sealed papers to the throne room; the previous runner vanished.
- Chadsworth was valet to an unnamed noble house and now keeps the War Quarter officers' kit.
- Zeglo Kruptorr is a warlock trainers' acolyte who feeds imps and wants a pact.
