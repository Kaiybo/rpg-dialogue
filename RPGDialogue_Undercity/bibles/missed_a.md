## Authored, not canon
- All former lives (clerk, fletcher, saddler's boy, parish record keeper, pawnbroker, physician, tavern keeper and the rest).
- Adrian not yet allowed a circle; Winifred's warlock relation; Victor writing everything down.
- Algernon's flowers being for someone he cannot remember; Boyle's predecessor and the mark on the floor.
- Alyssa's grudge against the Scarlets; her posting hopes; Eldin facing a chained ghoul in the yard.
- Andrew backing Edward and Chloe backing Tyler; the captives being Scarlets (Andrew only supposes so).
- The Hartwells (Andrew, Geoffrey, Anastasia), the Killians and the Van Talens treated as family; the wiki says only that they may be related (Chloe/Angela Curthas, Edrick/Brom/Sarah Killian, Helena/Nicholas Atwood and Elizabeth/Graham Van Talen are listed relatives). No exact relationship is stated.
- Every auctioneer's temperament and anecdotes (the gromsblood bidding, the snake, the sword called four times). Leeka having kept a shop of shields and maces follows a wiki speculation; no city is named.
- Vallia's satchel and the broken bottle on the bank stairs.
- Basil's "one flaw short" of journeyman; what a smith brings to a camp.
- Benijah's feathers from carrion birds; Francis buying salvage from Deathguard patrols and the royal longsword; Geoffrey refusing a sale.
- Cedric passing word to the Deathstalkers and his past in "a good many ports" (a light nod to a wiki speculation; nothing specific).
- Davitt's palace-garden walks; Selina's warlock relation.
- Ganoosh's journey, the apothecaries studying him, and his workbench on the third level.
- Gerard's past at Dalaran's fringes and who Theresa was; his method stays vague.
- Godrick losing his eyes to something in a summoning circle; his distance from Strahad Farsan (the wiki says only that they may be related).
- Gothard as a former Tirisfal tracker (the wiki notes he was a tracking trainer in the beta); Mennet sending him pupils.
- Helena aiming for the Bulwark and being kept out of the family gunroom; Hepzibah's debt calls (the wiki only guesses she is a rogue).
- Elizabeth's sand bucket and Graham's dragonling among the wall hangings.

## Never
- Anything after year one: Naxxramas (no play on Naxxremis's name), Cataclysm Undercity, the Wrathgate, later fates of any of these people.
- `Transmutation Master`, `Hearts of the Lovers`, `Green With Envy`.
- Promises of discounts, gifts or rewards; any detail of the New Plague beyond what the city already says aloud.
- Warmth by accident: these are Forsaken (and two zombies and a leper gnome), and kindness, where it shows, is small and dry.
