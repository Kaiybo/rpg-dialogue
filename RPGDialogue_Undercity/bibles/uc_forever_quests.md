# Undercity Forever quest givers and visitors  (file: npcs/uc_forever_quests.lua)

Garen Largo, Glix Xizzix, Tanis Alderwood, Morbin Lightbane and Rugbul Boomfirst, all added to the Undercity in World of Warcraft: Forever (1.60.1).

## Their quests
- A Lesson in Divinity (1) (turn-in, Tanis): he fears paladins and expects to be punished for begging.
- A Lesson in Divinity (2) (gives and takes): 10 Linen Cloth to wrap his wife until the apothecaries, busy with the Dark Lady's work, can stitch her.
- A Lesson in Divinity (3) (gives): gratitude; "you'll always have a friend in Tanis Alderwood".
- Light's Justice (Morbin, gives and takes): limbs from the Scourge in the Ruins of Lordaeron to stitch Forsaken soldiers; Garek Bandarion would wish it; "For Lordaeron!"
- Leonid's Letter (Glix, turn-in): old friend of Leonid from the war; stingy about tips.
- Garen Largo, Rugbul Boomfirst: none, trainer and battlemaster.

## Identity
- **Garen Largo** (uc_garen_largo) · stern, patient; the Light burns the dead and comes anyway. Offers solace to non-paladins.
- **Glix Xizzix** (uc_glix) · goblin standard: brash, "pal/buddy/bub/chum", prices everything, claims credit for the Forsaken's founding; "diversification".
- **Tanis Alderwood** (uc_tanis) · nervous, gentle, apologetic, devoted to his wife; one run-on nervous sentence at a time.
- **Morbin Lightbane** (uc_morbin) · fervent, proud; Light as a conduit for righteous fury; wants the Banshee Queen to watch him; "We are Forsaken!", "For Lordaeron!"
- **Rugbul Boomfirst** (uc_rugbul) · boisterous Darkspear, troll dialect ("be", "mon"), loyal to Thrall's peace but holding the beach.
Never says: anything after Year 1; nothing of Wrathgate or later Forsaken history; no Cataclysm Echo Isles reconquest.

## Agenda
- Glix: sell, and be remembered as a founder. Morbin: prove Forsaken paladins loyal. Tanis: get his wife mended.

## Secrets
- See "Authored, not canon".

## Reliability
- Glix exaggerates everything (the core of the Rot Hide story is true). Morbin is honest but partisan. Tanis is honest.

## What they notice   (2–3 things)
- Garen: Silver Hand knights and unsworn squires (origins); living paladins. Glix: balding Forsaken, troll hair, rogues near the lost and found. Tanis: the living, priests, a risen knight. Morbin: Forsaken paladins, Silver Hand origin, the living. Rugbul: trolls, honour rank.

## Authored, not canon
- Tanis's wife is named Merra, baked sweet buns in a village outside the capital, lost her jaw for three days; Tanis was a hedger and ditcher; the Scarlets burned the "Hendricks farm"; the Apothecarium set a guard on him after four visits.
- Glix is Pix, Rix and Frix's uncle ("my brother's girl"); he sold the rescued crates to the Forsaken; Leonid owes him for bandages and whetstones; vials as apothecary tips; a whole arm in the lost and found.
- Morbin wore the Silver Hand in life, served in the Deathguard under Garek and saw him first call the Light; apothecaries and Deathstalkers who distrust paladins (no names); Danitha "argues" with him at the keep.
- Garen's own past (carried the Light in life) is a light sketch.
- Rugbul: the Warsong tabard was a gift from an orc he pulled out of the surf, hidden from Vol'jin; "Boom first" family motto; Lady Proudmoore talks peace while Theramore's soldiers land on the islands (the wiki names only the Theramore Expeditionary Force).
- Quest IDs for A Lesson in Divinity (94427/94434/94435) come from the wiki, used because the three quests share a title.
