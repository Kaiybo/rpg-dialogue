# Undercity townsfolk  (keys: wmontague, maulray, archibald, vanbrunt, killian, lavinia_crowe, cward, graves, edras, cromwell)

The Undercity's service people: no quests, little or no gossip in the game. Built from role, place and neighbours (CONTENT_GUIDE §1, "almost nothing to go on").

## Sources
- In-game roles (vanilla 1.12, from memory): William Montague <Banker> (one of the Montague bankers, Trade Quarter); Anya Maulray <Stable Master>; Archibald <Weapon Master> and James Van Brunt <Blacksmithing Trainer> in the War Quarter; Brom Killian <Mining Trainer>; Lavinia Crowe <Enchanting Trainer> in the Magic Quarter; Carolyn Ward <Rogue Trainer> and Ezekiel Graves <Poison Supplies> in the Rogues' Quarter; Mary Edras <First Aid Trainer>; Armand Cromwell <Fishing Trainer>.
- Game rules used: one vault from every Horde bank; Forsaken use bandages; first aid's advanced test is Doctor Gregory Victor at Hammerfall (Triage); poison reagents (Dust of Decay, Essence of Pain, Empty Vials); enchanting disenchants items into dust and essences.
- Lore: the Undercity built in the catacombs and crypts beneath Lordaeron's capital; Arthas killed Terenas in the throne room; the Deathguard; the Scarlet Crusade in Tirisfal; the Syndicate (Alterac nobles) in Hillsbrad and Alterac; Brightwater Lake north of Brill; the green canals.

## Their quests
- none: all ten are service NPCs without quests.

## Identity and voices
- William Montague (banker): dry, exact clerk of a dead city; death is no excuse for sloppy accounts.
- Anya Maulray (stable master): gentle; spends her unlife persuading animals she is harmless.
- Archibald (weapon master): stern drill sergeant; wants Arthas at the end of a crossbow.
- James Van Brunt (blacksmithing trainer): gruff, work is all that's left and it's enough.
- Brom Killian (mining trainer): gallows cheer about being underground.
- Lavinia Crowe (enchanting trainer): proud, precise, talks of dust like spices.
- Carolyn Ward (rogue trainer): sharp, loyal to Sylvanas, measures everyone.
- Ezekiel Graves (poisons): cheerful shopkeeper selling death like sweets.
- Mary Edras (first aid trainer): brisk care; "love".
- Armand Cromwell (fishing trainer): calm; fishing as the dead's nearest thing to sleep.

## Authored, not canon
- Montague was a counting-house clerk three streets from the palace; asked for a pen on waking; the old kings "rearranged" by the apothecaries.
- Maulray raised horses near Brill; the Silverpine bear.
- Archibald was a sergeant on the wall when Arthas came home.
- Van Brunt watches the metal's colour because he can't feel it through the tongs.
- Crowe studied two summers in Dalaran.
- Ward's story of the rogue in the ditch outside Southshore.
- Graves's forgetting powder (vague; never named or sold in game).
- Edras was a midwife in the capital.

- Maulray: a hunter's named pet. Archibald: warriors' grip. Van Brunt, Killian, Crowe, Cromwell: skill in their own profession; Van Brunt and Edras: battered armour. Crowe: mages. Ward: stealth.
