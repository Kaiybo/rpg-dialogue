## Voices
**Andrew Brownell.** Grave, precise intelligence man; reports, liches as disease; won't name his agents.
**Luther Pickman.** Cold, condescending; control over power; shares the hall with Richard Kerwin, Carendin Halgar nearby.
**Killian Hagey.** Matter-of-fact; relearned his cuts with dead hands; skins beasts, not people.
**Mortimer Montague.** Fussy, counts twice; the Montague name came back without the family tree (no kinship claimed with William or Ophelia).
**Gillian Moore.** Sly; sells silence; doesn't ask.
**Joseph Moore.** Proudly dull; keeps a list of every customer; lied to an apothecary about it.
**Jonathan Chambers.** The one cheerful man on the canal; everything belongs in a bag.
**Lucille Castleton.** Refined, tart, "dear"; Lordaeron court fashion; misses colours.
**Sheldon Von Croy.** Pompous minor gentry, "capital V"; lost lands he won't visit.
**Zane Bradford.** Eager, scorched; tests every wand on himself.
**Sydney Upton.** Laconic; wood grain; Tirisfal plague yew vs. Silverpine.
**Morley Bates.** Gloomy joke about selling food to the dead; mushrooms from where cellars meet sewers.
**Mirelle Tremayne.** Blunt, sergeant-like; the dead don't heal, so wear plate.
**Christopher Drakul.** Formal; a guild is a chosen family; his staff came back with him.

### Voices
**Bragor.** Blunt orc on a long watch; trusts Forsaken blades, not their apothecaries; never turns his back on Varimathras.
**Genavie.** Bitter gentry; one subject; counts what was taken.
**Oran.** Precise, cool; would burn rival notes; Dalaran shut her out with her own books inside.
**Keever.** "Keever" for himself, "the one who thinks" for what was mislaid; disappointed by small animals.
**Lexington.** Dry, exact; distance is a clerical error; never leaves the city.
**Martha Strain.** Kennel keeper of demons; "half of demonology is removing excuses".
**Randolph.** The affable Montague; keepsakes in boxes; decides the four are cousins.
**Eunice.** Brisk, motherly, "dear"; cooks by eye, ear and clock; her mother's recipes written out with the salt measured.
**Tawny.** Soft, dreamy; tells her mushrooms the news.
**Victor.** Genial, hums four bars of a forgotten street song; presses every seam.
