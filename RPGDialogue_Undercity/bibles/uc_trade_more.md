# Undercity: the Trade Quarter, more (keys: uc_pgarrett, uc_raleigh, uc_velora)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg (Classic-only sections): Patrick Garrett (Reclaimed Treasures: sweeping the bat pens "is not befitting a Garrett"; family chest in Uldaman's South Common Hall "from when we Garretts toiled there as diggers"; the treasure is a broom, "the Garrett family weapon of choice"; possibly related to flight master Michael Garrett, not stated). ## Voices
**Patrick Garrett.** Pompous, cheerful, "I say", "bloody well"; dignity in the face of bats.
**Raleigh Andrean.** Flat, cold, precise; only revenge has warmth.
**Velora Nitely.** Dry, self-aware jokes that fall flat; honest about craft.

## Authored, not canon
- Patrick: coy about kinship with Michael Garrett; the Garretts dug for the dwarves' Explorers in Uldaman.
- Raleigh: head cook at a lord's table in Lordaeron, gave Deliah the ring that summer; can't taste, recites recipes; Valdred was his friend twenty years.
- Velora: made armour for the Silver Hand and court, once a breastplate for an unnamed prince (Arthas, unnamed); the tongs lesson for Forsaken smiths.
