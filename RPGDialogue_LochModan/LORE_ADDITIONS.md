**Wiki IDs seen (not used):** quests Rat Catching 416, Find Agmond 738, Ironband's Excavation 436.

**Corrections made in the pack:** Kadrell's Rat Catching line now describes kobolds in the western foothills, not cellar rats; the Mountaineers' pool listed gnolls among the loch's threats (gnolls are Cataclysm-era Loch Modan) and now lists kobolds.

**Assumptions (confirm in game):** Forever keeps the Classic quest titles used (The Trogg Threat, In Defense of the King's Lands, A Dark Threat Looms, Protecting the Shipment, Agmond's Fate, Ironband Wants You!, Report to Mountaineer Rockgar). Captain Rugelfuss has a Forever turn-in, "Banner of the Fallen (quest)", with no wiki page yet; not referenced.

**Year 1 notes:** the dam stands; Hinderweir is on it; no Twilight's Hammer at Mo'grosh, no Brann in Thelsamar.

**Authored, not canon:** Kadrell's dry socks for the Wetlands and the soot under a spy's nails; Rugelfuss digging trenches and "more chieftains than sense"; Hinderweir checking at noon and "who keeps the last stone"; the miners' song about the dam; Hearthstove's broken stool; Vidra straining the ichor and "bless the bears before".
