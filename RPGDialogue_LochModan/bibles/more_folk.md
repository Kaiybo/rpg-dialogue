# More Loch Modan folk (npcs/more_folk.lua)

Keys: mf_hammerfoot, mf_dargh, mf_deek, mf_greishan.

## Sources
warcraft.wiki.gg via `tools/npc.py`, read 2026-11-18: Pilot Hammerfoot, Dargh Trueaim, Deek Fizzlebizz, Greishan Ironstove. Quests original-WoW, unconfirmed for Forever; IDs TODO.

## Their quests
- **Pilot Hammerfoot** (North Gate Outpost, pre-Cataclysm location): The Lost Pilot, A Pilot's Revenge (Mangeclaw the bear). Grieves a comrade; wants the truth, then the beast dead.
- Dargh Trueaim (hunter trainer, Farstrider Lodge), Deek Fizzlebizz (gnome engineering trainer, Stonewrought Dam), Greishan Ironstove (male travelling bread vendor, Valley of Kings to Dun Algaz road): no quests.

## Voices
- **Hammerfoot**: grim, loyal; watches the hills; short on hope, long on loyalty.
- **Dargh**: slow, steady, dry; patience and the wind; a wounded beast is a hunter's worst sin.
- **Deek**: eager gnome, numbers and gears; loves the dam, worries about Dark Iron sabotage.
- **Greishan**: cheerful, chatty walker; the mountaineers look after him because they'd starve otherwise.

## Authored, not canon
Hammerfoot and the lost pilot flying together six years; Greishan bakes in Thelsamar and has a wife who calls him daft; Deek's "six thousand tons".
