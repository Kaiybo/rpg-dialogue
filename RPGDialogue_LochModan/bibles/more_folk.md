# More Loch Modan folk (npcs/more_folk.lua)

Keys: mf_hammerfoot, mf_dargh, mf_deek, mf_greishan.

## Sources
warcraft.wiki.gg via `tools/npc.py`, read 2026-11-18: Pilot Hammerfoot, Dargh Trueaim, Deek Fizzlebizz, Greishan Ironstove. Quests original-WoW, unconfirmed for Forever; IDs TODO.

## Their quests
- **Pilot Hammerfoot** (North Gate Outpost, pre-Cataclysm location): The Lost Pilot, A Pilot's Revenge (Mangeclaw the bear). Grieves a comrade; wants the truth, then the beast dead.
- Dargh Trueaim (hunter trainer, Farstrider Lodge), Deek Fizzlebizz (gnome engineering trainer, Stonewrought Dam), Greishan Ironstove (male travelling bread vendor, Valley of Kings to Dun Algaz road): no quests.

## Voices
- **Hammerfoot**: grim, loyal; watches the hills; short on hope, long on loyalty.
- **Dargh**: slow, steady, dry; patience and the wind; a wounded beast is a hunter's worst sin.
- **Deek**: eager gnome, numbers and gears; loves the dam, worries about Dark Iron sabotage.
- **Greishan**: cheerful, chatty walker; the mountaineers look after him because they'd starve otherwise.

## Authored, not canon
Hammerfoot and the lost pilot flying together six years; Greishan bakes in Thelsamar and has a wife who calls him daft; Deek's "six thousand tons".

# Missed folk, added later (npcs/more_folk.lua)

Keys: mf_vyrin (quest); mf_xandar, mf_irene, mf_cliff, mf_kat, mf_aldren (Farstrider Lodge traders), mf_gothor (Thelsamar), mf_nillen (Stonewrought Dam).

## Sources
warcraft.wiki.gg via `tools/npc.py`, read 2026-11-18: each NPC's page; quest pages Vyrin's Revenge and Vyrin's Revenge (2). Vyrin's innkeeper role, her husband Talithar and the later novel material are post-Classic and NOT used. Gothor Brumn is written in Thelsamar and Aldren Cordon at the Lodge (their Classic posts; the wiki lists later ones).

## Their quests
- **Vyrin Swiftwind** (high elf, Farstrider Lodge): Vyrin's Revenge (kill Ol' Sooty, "a little payback for His Boldness", Daryl), Vyrin's Revenge (2) (ends). Consistent with `farstrider.lua` (Marek: "Vyrin keeps the beds, an' she shoots better than any dwarf").
- none: Xandar Goodbeard (General Supplies), Irene Sureshot (Gunsmith), Cliff Hadin (human Bowyer), Kat Sampson (human Leather Armor Merchant), Aldren Cordon (Clothier), Gothor Brumn (Armorer), Nillen Andemar (Macecrafter): vendors, built from role, place and neighbours.

## Voices
- **Vyrin**: measured, dry, teasing; no contractions when she is being pointed; "His Boldness".
- **Xandar**: easygoing dwarf, proud of the beard. **Irene**: sharp, exacting, blames the shooter. **Cliff**: mild, slow, stubborn human. **Kat**: brisk, plain human. **Aldren**: fussy, kindly dwarf. **Gothor**: steady, blunt. **Nillen**: hearty, one idea (weight on a haft).

## Authored, not canon
Vyrin's view of Quel'Thalas (kept to "ash and silence since the Scourge"); the Irene/Cliff rifle-and-bow argument; Cliff coming north for island yew; Kat paying half for badly cut hides; Aldren's grey-green dye from Rann; Gothor "losing" bills for mountaineers; Nillen forging in Thelsamar and selling on the dam.
