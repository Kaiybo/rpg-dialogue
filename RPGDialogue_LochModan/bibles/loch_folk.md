# Loch Modan folk (keys: lm_huldar, lm_miran, lm_lina, lm_yanni, lm_vrok)

Wiki pages read 2026-11-17 (warcraft.wiki.gg): Huldar, Miran, Saean, Resupplying the Excavation (Classic), After the Ambush, Protecting the Shipment (Classic), Lina Hearthstove, Yanni Stoutheart, Vrok Blunderblast.

## Sources
- **Huldar** (male dwarf, quest giver by the powder caravan south of the loch): ends "Resupplying the Excavation", starts "After the Ambush". Gossip: deliveries are hard work, "we must do what we can against the Dark Irons". Completion text: Saean was a sympathizer, "working with him for almost a year".
- **Miran** (male dwarf, same caravan): ends "After the Ambush", starts the escort "Protecting the Shipment" (ends with Prospector Ironband).
- **Saean** (hostile, Dark Iron affiliation): betrayed Huldar and Miran; says "Sorry, Huldar... But it seems I've misled you." Kept consistent with Hornhelm's lines in `hornhelm.lua` and `thelsamar.lua`.
- **Lina Hearthstove** (female dwarf stable master outside the Stoutlager Inn; wiki: "may be related to" Innkeeper and Vidra Hearthstove).
- **Yanni Stoutheart** (female dwarf, general goods inside the Stoutlager Inn).
- **Vrok Blunderblast** (male dwarf gunsmith, Thelsamar).

## Voices
- **Huldar**: older, steady, proud of never losing a barrel; shaken and suspicious since the ambush. Thick Ironforge burr.
- **Miran**: young, nervous, decent; ties knots when anxious; wants to be braver than he is.
- **Lina**: hearty, talks to animals first; the Hearthstove who mucks out.
- **Yanni**: brisk shopkeeper who tells you what you forgot.
- **Vrok**: loud and half-deaf from test firing, proud of the family name.

## Authored, not canon
- Huldar: six seasons hauling for the League; Saean's unmet "cousin" in Thelsamar; Huldar and Ironband arguing over daily runs vs. escorts.
- Miran: from Kharanos, mother a brewer who thinks hauling is safe; Saean taught him to tie a load; Ironband once found a carved stone and sent it to Ironforge under guard.
- Lina: Innkeeper Hearthstove's sister and Vidra's aunt (wiki only says "may be related"); a dam worker owes her three weeks' stabling; the rams can open the feed shed latch.
- Yanni: sells flasks to people who drank the loch water.
- Vrok: grandfather earned the name at Grim Batol (and by blowing his workshop roof off).
