## Their quests
- None. All service tier.

## Identity
- Yanni Stoutheart: brisk, cheerful; sells "what ye need, never what ye want".
- Drac Roughcut: gruff, dry, honest; vials, flux, spice, thread; supplies Ghak and Vidra.
- Vrok Blunderblast: jovial gun enthusiast; mountaineers are his best customers.
- Rann Flamespinner: calm, wry, quiet dwarf in a loud town; notices clothes.
All speak the Khaz Modan burr. Alliance town; class lines kept general.

## Threads he could carry
- Dragonmaw on the Wetlands side of the pass (Drac's lost spice crates).

## Agenda
Sell, and stay out of trouble.

## Secrets
- Vrok knows dam engineers buy powder they've been told not to use near the dam.

## Reliability
Honest; Yanni's rope theory is a guess.

## What they notice
- kit, level (Yanni), craft (Drac, Rann), hunters and dwarf fighters (Vrok).

## Topics
stonewrought_dam, dragonmaw.

## Authored, not canon
- Yanni inherited the counter from an uncle with a bad back; rope sales for the dam road.
- Drac carted goods before settling; lost two crates of spice to the Dragonmaw this year; guesses Vidra's sausage secret.
- Vrok's grandfather double-loaded a barrel and earned the family name (and a roof hole).
- Rann's mother dyed forge-red cloth for Ironforge clans; the dye root grows beyond the Wetlands; a gnome paid for a harness repair in cogs.
