## Identity / voice
Lodge and traders
- Vyrin Swiftwind · high elf woman · innkeeper. Composed, dry, formal (no contractions), quietly fond of the dwarves; needles Daryl out of concern. Will not discuss Quel'Thalas beyond "the Scourge came".
- Claude Erksine · dwarf · pet trainer. Gentle with beasts, gruff with hunters; bitten hands; bears above all.
- Cliff Hadin · human · bowyer. Easy, wry; bows over guns; daily argument with Irene, whom he admires.
- Irene Sureshot · dwarf woman · gunsmith. Loud, exact, sure of herself; settles things at the range.
- Kat Sampson · human woman · leather armor. Brisk, practical; followed the hides north; cards with Cliff.
- Kelt Thomasin · human · hunter. Quiet, fixed on the loch; waiting for a great threshadon.
- Xandar Goodbeard · dwarf · general supplies. Cheerful, fussy, beard-proud; "rope, every time".
- Aldren Cordon · dwarf · clothier at Ironband's dig. Mild, weary, at war with dust.
- Nillen Andemar · dwarf · macecrafter on the dam. Blunt, hearty; no time for edges.
- Gothor Brumn · dwarf · armorer at Algaz Station. Grim, practical; "armour yer shoulders".
- Thorvald Deepforge · dwarf · smith, Valley of Kings. Proud, chatty; works under the statues' eyes.
- Dakk Blunderblast · dwarf · Thelsamar townsman. Jumpy, sleepless, spiders at the window; Vrok's kin.
- Ashlan Stonesmirk · dwarf · gunsmith at the Dun Modr camp. Wry, steady, crooked smile.

Thelsamar guards (one thing each)
- Bludd: the Stoutlager's door; thirsty. Cragg: oldest, weather knee, two wars. Dalk: watches the gryphons, wants to fly. Fazgard: watches the western ridge for troggs. Harn: night watch, knows the town by sound. Janha: watches hands at the shops. Kamdar: stands downwind of Vidra's kitchen. Modax: drill-master, inspects everyone. Ozmok: lochside, a fisherman at heart. Roghan: a father, minds houses and cellars. Swarth: dour, mistrusts magic, looks for Dark Iron spies. Uthan: bookish, at the League's door. Wuar: polices riders, loves rams.
- Roads: Stenn (shortest patrol in the loch, counts paces), Flint (fast walker, reads tracks), Droken (gives directions before asked).

Algaz and the tunnels
- Hammerfall: stern gate watch, tired of the orc-town joke. Luxst: silent marksman, ranges everything. Morran: keeps the station's tally. Yuttha: the friendly face, homesick for Thelsamar. Zwarn: "it'll be tonight". Ganin: road gossip between Algaz and the North Gate. Brokk: fierce, shows his face at Dun Algaz daily. Dokkin: hushed, listens to the tunnel.

Valley of Kings
- Naarh: solemn, keeps the feet of the kings. Tyraw: bored, on loan from Ironforge, misses the Great Forge. Kalmir: tracker, terse. Zaren: races her own patrol. Veek: old, unhurried, pipe, remembers the newcomers.

## Authored, not canon
- Vyrin stitched Daryl's face; Marek gave her the larder keys; she once outshot Irene with a bow.
- Claude trained only bears for years (from the wiki's beta trivia); the wolf-pup scar.
- Cliff cuts ash staves on the east shore; Kat broke three awls on a crocolisk hide; Irene's buzzard skull over the door.
- Kelt has seen a wake and a bitten crocolisk, nothing more; the creature is never confirmed.
- Xandar's goods come by cart from Yanni Stoutheart; Daryl dragged a boar home by the tail.
- Aldren was asked to the dig by Ironband; Nillen forges on the dam for the wind; Thorvald throws out blades under the statues' gaze; Thorvald has no in-game title recorded, so none is set.
- Dakk's lost boot; he tried sleeping at the inn.
- Ashlan had a shop inside Dun Modr, dries powder in a pan, and has singed his beard twice.
- All guard colour: Bludd fifteen years; Cragg's knee from an orc hammer at the gates; Dalk and the rider in the tree; Janha and the blunderbuss down a trouser leg; Kamdar swapped posts with Uthan; Modax drills Gwarth; Ozmok's one shot; Roghan the mason with three children; Wuar's courier and the hen; Stenn's 612 paces and the white stone; Flint's hobnailed prints; Hammerfall's family name; Luxst's 218 paces; Morran's open line in the book; Yuttha's chair at the Stoutlager; Zwarn's four years; Brokk's captured axe over Gothor's forge; Dokkin's hour in the dark; Naarh's nine years; Tyraw's hundred and six days; Kalmir's five scouting troggs; Zaren's times on her rifle stock; Veek's word to newcomers.
