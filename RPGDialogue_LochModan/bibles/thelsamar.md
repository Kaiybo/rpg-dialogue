## Their quests
- Kadrell: Stout to Kadrell, Rat Catching, Mountaineer Stormpike's Task, Stonegear's Search, Report to Mountaineer Rockgar.
- Ironband: Excavation Progress Report, Protecting the Shipment, Ironband Wants You! and the Agmond chain.
- Hearthstove: none: innkeeper only.
- Vidra: Thelsamar Blood Sausages.
- Rugelfuss: The Trogg Threat, In Defense of the King's Lands (Classic, four parts, ending with Grawmug).
- Hinderweir: A Dark Threat Looms (seven parts).

## Never
- Anything Cataclysm or later: the dam's destruction, Hinderweir's disappearance, Brann in Thelsamar, the Twilight's Hammer at Mo'grosh, gnolls in Loch Modan.
- What the "VII" means beyond "seventh of the name"; whether the Hearthstoves are related.
- That the dam was certainly Franclorn's work: Hinderweir hears the claim only from Archesonus's line, and dodges.

## Voices (whole sentences; the burr stays)
- Kadrell: hearty, welcoming mountaineer; tunnel rats and spies.
- Ironband: steady, honest digger; slow work, worth it; owns the possibility that his dig woke the troggs.
- Hearthstove: jolly innkeeper; best ale between Ironforge and the Wetlands.
- Vidra: brisk cook; don't ask about the sausages.
- Rugelfuss: formal officer, light brogue (an occasional ye, aye, {g:lad|lass}); numbers for the King; trenches, stone and the regiment's book.
- Hinderweir: anxious engineer who stumbles in long run-on sentences; checks the dam all day; light burr, stone and beard oaths ("thank the stone").

## Depth (Loch Modan 1.2.0)
- Kadrell: the spy (why the dam), the loch (what's in it), who needs a hand (Stormpike). Gated: the stout (after Stout to Kadrell), gnomes.
- Ironband: the dig (the idols, did we wake them?), why so slow (Jern), where the powder goes (Saean after the shipment). Gated: Stormpike sent you (Ironband Wants You!), miners.
- Hearthstove (service): the regulars (Vidra), the miners' song about the dam.
- Vidra: what's in the sausages (the ichor), who eats them, can I help. Gated: priests offering a blessing, cooks.
- Rugelfuss: where the soldiers went, who leads the troggs (Grawmug), the statues, why teeth. Gated: Mountaineers of Dun Morogh.
- Hinderweir: how old is the dam (if it goes), the Dark Iron threat (Seaforium after the chain), where the guards are, who built it. Gated: the stonewrought method (heard Archesonus), engineers.
