## Voices
- Kadrell: hearty, welcoming mountaineer; rats and spies.
- Ironband: steady, honest digger; slow work, worth it.
- Hearthstove: jolly innkeeper; best ale this side of the Wetlands.
- Vidra: brisk cook; don't ask about the sausages.
- Rugelfuss: formal officer; numbers for the King.
- Hinderweir: anxious engineer; checks the dam three times a day.
