# Ghak Healtouch  (key: lm_ghak)
## Identity
Dwarf healer. Gruff, warm. Scolds then mends. Knows every patient's gossip.
## Authored, not canon
- His mother mended the dam crews; he mended dwarves (and some orcs) on the loch in the Second War. Trogg bites from the excavation fester worse than the Valley's. Gives the "quiet" Mountaineers cold tea as a sleeping draught (disposition 65). The ogre club-print patient.
## What they notice
Wounds/ruined kit; priests; veterans past level 30.

# Torren Squarejaw  (key: lm_torren)
## Identity
Dwarf, Explorers' League field man kept in town by Jern to carry post. Eager, chatty.
## Authored, not canon
- Resents the post; suspects the fitted stone is older than any dwarf clan (no answer given, nothing later foreshadowed).

# Bailor Stonehand  (key: lm_bailor)
## Identity
Old dwarf weaponsmith, retired from the forge (shoulders); judges weapons by balance.
## Authored, not canon
- Forty years at the anvil; handed the forge to Morhan; proud of Jordan, says he'd deny it. Role in game not checked (written as retired, no BUSINESS).

# Brock Stoneseeker  (key: lm_brock)
## Identity
Dwarf mining trainer. Steady, dry. Sour about the Silver Stream Mine kobolds (Stormpike's Order).
