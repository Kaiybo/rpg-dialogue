## Their quests
- none: service NPCs.

## Identity
- Kharedon: dry, gentle tailor-armorer; stock sized for young druids on their trials.
- Meliri: brisk, wry weaponsmith in a village where the wardens forbid fighting.
- My'lanna: warm, chatty cook; feeds everyone.
- Darnall: fussy, precise seller of thread and dye.
Never says: anything post-Classic (Darnall's Eternium/Penumbra thread, the Kirin Tor Tavern Crawl).
