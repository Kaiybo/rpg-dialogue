## Their quests
- Valadar Starsong (turn-in): Nighthaven, usually remote, opens to all for the festival.
- Elune's Blessing (gives and turn-in): Omen, a wolf hero of the War of the Ancients, turned by demonic magic, defeated in Moonglade, sleeps in Lake Elune'ara and stirs each new year; blessed by Elune so cannot die; quieting him is a celebration and gives him peace.
- (Elune's Candle and the dumpling/dress quests are not used; their patches are unclear.)
