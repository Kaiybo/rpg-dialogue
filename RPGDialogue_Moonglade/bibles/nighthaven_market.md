# Nighthaven market  (npcs/nighthaven_market.lua; keys geenia, darnall, meliri, kharedon)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Geenia_Sunshadow (Speciality Dress Maker, robes), https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Darnall (Tailoring Supplies), https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Meliri (Weaponsmith), https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kharedon (Light Armor Merchant). All night elves in Nighthaven; no patch-added notes (taken as Classic).

## Their quests
- none: all four are vendors. Built from role, place and neighbours (Remulos's peace, Sindrayl's hippogryphs, Darnassus supply).

## Identity
- Geenia Sunshadow: warm, chatty dressmaker; thinks druids should dress as well as the forest looks; sells to both factions under the Keeper's peace.
- Darnall: dry, exact supplier; supplies Geenia; finds her exhausting and won't say so.
- Meliri: calm, wry weapon seller in a village where fighting is forbidden; arms travellers for the road out.
- Kharedon: cheerful leather seller; nothing hunted in Moonglade.

## Authored, not canon
- Geenia's secret Moonglade flower dyes and an unnamed Horde wool trader; struggling to cut hoods for tauren horns.
- Darnall's thread comes by hippogryph from Darnassus and Sindrayl complains about the weight.
- Kharedon's leather from Ashenvale/Feralas/Barrens hunters; some druids refuse to buy leather.
