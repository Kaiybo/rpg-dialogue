# RPG Dialogue: Moonglade (pack changelog)

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits now bring personal lines (habits, worries, opinions of the neighbours), night-time lines, and a question or two about the person themselves, not only their quests.
- Silva, Bunthen, the Great Bear Spirit, Loganaar.

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- Nighthaven: Keeper Remulos, Rabine Saturna, Dendrite Starblaze and the druid trials, Loganaar, the Great Bear Spirit, and the Circle's two flight masters, Silva Fil'naveth and Bunthen Plainswind. Nighthaven's defenders. Moonglade is neutral: everyone here speaks to Alliance and Horde alike, and druids get the warmest welcome.
