# The Blasted Lands  (keys: lungertz, mahar_ba, thadius, ardalan, enohar, alexandra, bernie, strumner, kumisha, drazial, lynnore, daio)

Levels 45-55. The Classic (pre-Cataclysm) zone only: everything below was read on warcraft.wiki.gg on 2026-11-15, Classic sections and Classic-only quest pages. The Cataclysm and later Blasted Lands (Surwich, the Shattershore, the Horde at Dreadmaul Hold, Mama Morton's inn, Commander Vines, Sergeant Krolan, Corporal Nobsy, the Lightbrew sisters, Keri Thunderbrew, Buttonwillow McKittrick, Leyan Steelson, the Okril'lon, Loramus and the bloodmages' Razelikh ritual, Kasim Sharim, the Iron Horde and Nethergarde's fall) are not used. Watch Commander Relthorn Netherwane and the Portal camp arrived in 2.0.3 and are left out. None of the NPC pages read has a World of Warcraft: Forever section, so the Classic versions stand (https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Forever checked: nothing on the Blasted Lands beyond Kazzak's part in the end-of-beta event, which is not used).

## Their quests
- Lungertz: The Lost Supplies (ends), Deliver the Shipment (ends), Supplies for Nethergarde (ends). Reveals: the keep lives on what gets through from Darkshire and Stormwind; every crate counts; worried about reagents and about Biggs; brisk, cheerful under pressure ("business as usual").
- Mahar Ba: Mazen's Behest (ends; the second step shares the title). Reveals: a scholar of the Portal who idolises Khadgar, feels forgotten by Stormwind's mages, impatient to read.
- Thadius: Thadius Grimshade (ends), Nekrum's Medallion, The Divination, Return to the Hinterlands (gives). Reveals: a Wildhammer who chose darker study but still loves gryphons; trades help for knowledge; wants long life without its price; candid about rituals going wrong.
- Ardalan: Petty Squabbles (gives). Reveals: wants Alliance and Horde to stop fighting each other and face the Legion; sends Alliance players to an orc.
- Enohar: Ragnar Thunderbrew (gives). Reveals: family loyalty, a Thunderbrew's outrage at stolen ale, a dwarf who needs a drink to bear the duty.
- Alexandra, Bernie, Strumner: none: service NPCs; built from role, the keep and its supply line.
- Kum'isha: Everything Counts In Large Amounts, To Serve Kum'isha (and their repeatables). Reveals: contempt, a lifetime's search, home, greed as the root of everything (including, as authored here, his own).
- Drazial: A Boar's Vitality, Snickerfang Jowls, The Decisive Striker (and repeatables). Reveals: an enthusiast of the body, cheerfully treating adventurers as test subjects.
- Lynnore: The Basilisk's Bite, Vulture's Vigor (and repeatables). Reveals: the mind and spirit of beasts; awe at the basilisks; "for a price".
- Daio: The Prison's Bindings, The Prison's Casing, Suppression (warlocks only). Reveals: an old teacher of control and sacrifice who prices power honestly.

## Identity and voices
- **Quartermaster Lungertz** (human, Stormwind, the keep's quartermaster, upstairs in the northwestern building). Brisk, cheerful, harried, practical, proud of keeping the keep fed. Talks in ledgers and lists; "business as usual"; counts crates. Knows: the Darkshire road, caravans, powerstones, Azoran crystals, who in the keep needs what. Relationships: Watcher Biggs (lost caravan), Sorigal in Darkshire, the mages upstairs, Bernie and Strumner (his to supply), Ardalan (another mouth to feed). Notices: priests and mages (useful), battered kit, dwarves. Never: names the keep's commanders (none are in game); Burning Crusade.
- **Watcher Mahar Ba** (human mage, Nethergarde). Earnest, scholarly, excitable about Khadgar, quietly lonely for Dalaran's scholarship. Long, careful sentences that run away with him. Knows: the Portal's residue, the taint in the soil, Khadgar's history, the black drake. Relationships: Mazen Mac'Nadir (overworked), Dellis (pitied), Thadius (respects, distrusts his methods), Ardalan (agrees, warily). Notices: mages, night elves (the old war), the fel on warlocks. Never: says the Portal will open; states the five heroes alive.
- **Thadius Grimshade** (Wildhammer dwarf diviner, the mage tower). Wry, hungry for knowledge, unapologetic, fond of gryphons; Wildhammer brogue ("ye", "tae", "o'", "cannae"). Knows: divination, Nekrum's medallion, Aerie Peak, darker study. Relationships: Talonaxe, Sharpbeak, the keep's mages (they tolerate him). Notices: dwarves (Wildhammer or Bronzebeard), priests (disapproval), mages. Never: becomes a warlock; the medallion's actual use beyond study.
- **Ambassador Ardalan** (blood elf, neutral envoy). Polished, patient, exasperated, sincere about the Legion; a diplomat's courtesy with a blade of impatience. Knows: the Legion's remnant, the Fallen Hero by the swamp, what the elves lost. Never: whom exactly he represents (left vague; authored), Kael'thas, Outland, Trebor's name.
- **Enohar Thunderbrew** (dwarf, Ironforge). Hearty, blunt, homesick, loyal to the Thunderbrew name; a mug always near. Notices: dwarves, the drunk, anyone carrying ale.
- **Alexandra Constantine** (human gryphon master). Calm, competent, fond of her birds; jokes about the heat by day and the cold by night.
- **Bernie Heisten** (human, food and drink). Chatty, a cook making do with caravan stores; knows everyone's order.
- **Strumner Flintheel** (dwarf armor crafter). Gruff and sparing with words but speaks in whole sentences; proud to be the only repair bench for leagues.
- **Kum'isha the Collector** (Lost One, near the Rise of the Defiler). Contemptuous, proud, bitter, obsessed; calls the player by race; speaks of himself as Kum'isha at moments of pride. Knows: draenethyst, the smell of greed, home. Never: Outland by name, the naaru, Shattrath, Exodar, how he came here.
- **Bloodmage Drazial** (blood elf, near the northern pass). Gleeful empiricist of the body; "test subject"; precise words. Never: Razelikh, Loramus, blood rituals (Cataclysm).
- **Bloodmage Lynnore** (blood elf, his sister). Cooler, wondering, a little superior; mind and spirit; "for a price". Never: the same.
- **Daio the Decrepit** (old human warlock, Tainted Scar). Dry, patient, grinning; teaches control and the price of power; "a long way from home". Knows: binding, prisons, Doomguard, the Scar. Never: his past (deflects); Balaadur, Arrexis.

## Agendas, claims, reliability
- Lungertz: honest, but plays down how short the keep runs in front of strangers (his Persuade reveals the real count).
- Mahar Ba: honest; his hope that the five heroes live is a hope, not a claim.
- Thadius: honest about prices; vague about why he wants long life.
- Ardalan: honest about the Legion; sincere but never says whose envoy he is.
- Kum'isha: one-sided (everyone smells of greed); his Persuade reveals he means himself too.

## Deepening pass (wastes questgivers)
No unwritten Classic named NPCs with gossip remain in the zone (the rest are hostile: Kruul, Razelikh's triad, the rares), so this pass deepens the three wastes questgivers.
- **Drazial** new branches: the scorpoks and The Decisive Striker (before/active/recent), his test subjects (stance: risk and consent), what Nethergarde makes of the elves and his view of Ardalan, the neighbours (Kum'isha refused him a blood sample; Daio), Quel'Thalas after the Scourge (stance: strength vs comfort). Topics: nethergarde, draenethyst.
- **Lynnore** new branches: Vulture's Vigor (her people's lost source, unnamed; stance on a tainted cure), "for a price" (organs, and her unease at killing thinking beasts), Nethergarde and Thadius (she suspects he fears age), Quel'Thalas and grief, Kum'isha (the most interesting mind in the wastes). Topics: nethergarde, draenethyst.
- **Daio** new branches: his students (one buried by the rock), what a Doomguard is and wants, Nethergarde looking away (stance: an honest teacher or none), Kum'isha ("a warlock in everything but the books"; Daio stopped wanting home). Topics: nethergarde, draenethyst, dreadmaul.
