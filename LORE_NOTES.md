## Lore sheet: Northshire Valley and Goldshire (vertical slice, M3)

Sources: [Elwynn Forest questing guide](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Elwynn_Forest_questing_guide), [Elwynn Forest (Classic) quests](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Elwynn_Forest_(Classic)_quests), [Elwynn Forest (Classic)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Elwynn_Forest_(Classic)), [Marshal Dughan](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Marshal_Dughan), [Innkeeper Farley](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Innkeeper_Farley), [Lion's Pride Inn](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lion's_Pride_Inn), [Goldshire](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Goldshire), [The Collector (Classic)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Collector_(Classic)), [Further Concerns](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Further_Concerns), [Wanted: "Hogger"](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Wanted:_%22Hogger%22), [Report to Goldshire](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Report_to_Goldshire). All are original-WoW (Classic) facts; **Forever may change quests**, so every quest ID comes from capture, never from these pages.

**Key NPCs and what they care about (from their quests)**
- Marshal McBride (Northshire Abbey): commands the garrison; kobolds in the woods and Echo Ridge Mine; sends recruits on to Marshal Dughan.
- Deputy Willem (outside the Abbey): first face for new recruits (A Threat Within); the Defias thieves in the valley (Brotherhood of Thieves, Bounty on Garrick Padfoot); sends people to Eagan Peltskinner and Milly Osworth.
- Eagan Peltskinner: wolves across the border (Wolves Across the Border).
- Milly Osworth: her vineyard harvest taken by the Defias (Milly's Harvest); the Grape Manifest goes to Brother Neals.
- Marshal Dughan (Goldshire): the law in Elwynn outside Northshire; the kobold mines (Fargodeep, Jasperlode), Westbrook Garrison, the murloc rumors (Further Concerns, after Remy's A Fishy Peril), the Hogger bounty, The Collector and Manhunt, sending people on to Gryan Stoutmantle in Westfall.
- Innkeeper Farley (Lion's Pride Inn): family inn since King Llane I's time; hospitality; Falkhaan Isenstrider's best friend (Rest and Relaxation); later the Legend of Stalvan (Stalvan stayed at the inn).
- Remy "Two Times" (Goldshire): buys gold dust from kobold mines (Gold Dust Exchange); heard the murloc rumors (A Fishy Peril).
- William Pestle (in the inn): kobold candles (Kobold Candles, Shipment to Stormwind); helps the young lovers Maybell Maclure and Tommy Joe Stonefield (Collecting Kelp, The Escape).
- Smith Argus <Journeyman Blacksmith>: Elmore's Task; later Return to Verner.
- Melika Isenstrider <Assistant Innkeeper> (Lion's Pride Inn, main room): no quests in the live game (in the original beta she ended Rest and Relaxation); a relative of Falkhaan Isenstrider, relation unstated, so never specify it. No gossip menu in Forever (user). Source: [Melika Isenstrider](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Melika_Isenstrider).

**Storylines**
- *Northshire troubles:* kobolds (McBride), wolves (Eagan), Defias thieves in the vineyards (Willem, Milly), then Report to Goldshire.
- *Mines and kobolds:* Fargodeep Mine, then Jasperlode Mine (Dughan); Kobold Candles (Pestle) and Gold Dust Exchange (Remy) on the side.
- *Maclures vs Stonefields:* Tommy Joe and Maybell's romance; William Pestle's potion helps them (The Escape).
- *A Fishy Peril:* Remy's murloc rumor, then Dughan's Further Concerns, then Guard Thomas at Eastvale. The rumor proves true (lost guards, Rolf's fate).
- *Gnolls and the Collector:* the Gold Pickup Schedule from gnolls shows gold from Elwynn's mines going to "the Collector" near the Brackwell Pumpkin Patch. Dughan's Manhunt follows. The wiki notes this links the gnolls to the Defias.
- *Hogger:* a huge gnoll in southwestern Elwynn with a bounty from the Stormwind Army; proof goes to Dughan.

**Year 1 status (Forever):** after the Forsaken Kingdom campaign; Defias active; VanCleef not yet defeated by default (the Deadmines are a dungeon the player may or may not have done); no Cataclysm-era Northshire (no worgen, no Blackrock invasion of the Abbey). Not used: Dughan's Emerald Nightmare dream (*Stormrage*, much later).

**Unsure / to confirm with capture:** whether Forever changed any of these quests; Brother Danil, Llane Beshere, Brother Sammuel and Brother Neals still in place; whether Goldshire has children NPCs; the exact names of guard NPCs in Northshire and Goldshire; Remy's in-game greeting (his voice is written from his quests and nickname, and should be checked against captured text).
