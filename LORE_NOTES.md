## Northshire Valley (partial; the full lore sheet comes in M3)

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Marshal (Douglas) McBride: human, a marshal of the Stormwind Army, commands Northshire Abbey's garrison and the valley's defense | [warcraft.wiki.gg: Marshal McBride](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Marshal_McBride) |
| He was concerned about kobolds infesting Northshire's woods and moving into Echo Ridge Mine, and marshaled Silver Hand paladins, warriors and other recruits against them | same page |
| His quests in original WoW: A Threat Within (turn-in), Kobold Camp Cleanup (kill 8 Kobold Vermin), Investigate Echo Ridge, Skirmish at Echo Ridge (kill 8 Kobold Laborers), Report to Goldshire, and the human class letters. **Unconfirmed for Forever.** | same page; [Kobold Camp Cleanup](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kobold_Camp_Cleanup); [Skirmish at Echo Ridge](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Skirmish_at_Echo_Ridge) |
| Skirmish at Echo Ridge's completion has McBride admit there may still be kobolds in the mine and say he'll send others (paraphrased, not for dialogue) | [Skirmish at Echo Ridge](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Skirmish_at_Echo_Ridge) |
