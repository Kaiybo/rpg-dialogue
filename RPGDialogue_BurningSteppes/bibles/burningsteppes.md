# The Burning Steppes  (keys: maxwell, helendis, oralius, jalinda, mayara, borgus, ragged_john, maxwort, tinkee, yuka, kibler, mathredis, vahgruk, cyrus)

Levels 50-58. The Classic (pre-Cataclysm) zone: everything below was read on warcraft.wiki.gg on 2026-11-15, Classic sections and Classic-only quest pages only. Morgan's Vigil and Flame Crest were rebuilt in Cataclysm (Morgan herself, John J. Keeshan, Colonel Troteman, Eitrigg and Ariok, the Vigil Guards, Chiselgrip, Flamestar Post, Gorzeeki's new quests); none of that is used.

## Their quests
- Maxwell: The True Masters (6) (sends you to John), Marshal Windsor (into the Depths), Abandoned Hope (ends; mourns), Jail Break! (ends), Stormwind Rendezvous (gives), Message to Maxwell (ends), Maxwell's Mission (Voone, Omokk, Wyrmthalak), General Drakkisath's Command (ends), General Drakkisath's Demise. Reveals: a soldier left at the end of the road, bitter that Stormwind waits for proof while his people die; dry humour about his own orders ("if he's dead, I want proof"); contempt for John's running; real grief for Windsor; relief that is careful not to become hope.
- Helendis: Dragonkin Menace (proves you first), The True Masters (the letter to Solomon), Put Her Down (Halycon). Reveals: a scholar-scout who reads the land and suspects the hand behind the Blackrock; tests people before trusting them; pity even for a corrupted beast.
- Oralius: Extinguish the Firegut, Gor'tesh the Brute Lord, Ogre Head On A Stick = Party, FIFTY! YEP!, The Good Stuff. Reveals: a soldier broken by war who still thinks in phases of attack; Captain Winky; terror as tactics; a collector's glee.
- Jalinda: Overmaster Pyron, Incendius!. Reveals: a gnome responsible for mages who keep coming back burned; frightened of Ragnaros's name; nobody else to send.
- Mayara: Mayara Brightwing (ends), Doomrigger's Clasp, Delivery to Ridgewell. Reveals: an agent with a noble patron and a collector's eye, practical about danger, proud of a find.
- Borgus: none: the gryphon master; carried by his roost and the roads he flies.
- Ragged John: Mother's Milk (gives and ends); spoken to for The True Masters (6). Reveals: survived by slipping away; tells a story his own way; loyal to Windsor; wants the deadliest poison in the Spire for ale.
- Maxwort: Tablet of the Seven, The Heart of the Mountain. Reveals: greed dressed as scholarship; patience; a goblin's love of a big, loud ending.
- Tinkee: Broodling Essence, Felnok Steelspring, Return to Tinkee, Tinkee Steamboil (sought out), Egg Freezing, Egg Collection, Leonid Barthalomew. Reveals: brilliant, excitable, proud of her gadgets, afraid of her patron, never asks what the eggs are for.
- Yuka: Yuka Screwspigot (sought out, from Yorba), Ribbly Screwspigot. Reveals: family business, bounty arithmetic, a cheerful lack of sentiment about her brother.
- Kibler: Kibler's Exotic Pets, En-Ay-Es-Tee-Why. Reveals: a salesman who loves animals, sends others into the Spire, worried about Bijou in a businesslike way, nagged by his parrot.
- Mathredis: the five Libram quests (Constitution, Resilience, Rumination, Tenacity, Voracity). Reveals: hunger, contempt, grief for a dead master and a lost kingdom, a high price.
- Vahgruk: none: the wind rider master; carried by his post and the Horde's roads.
- Cyrus: A Taste of Flame (1-3). Reveals: contempt, hunger for the mountain's fire, the flight watching what mortals do.

## Identity and voices
- **Marshal Maxwell** (human, Stormwind, commander at Morgan's Vigil). Weary, dry, stubborn, bitter at Stormwind's silence, kind under it. Speaks like an officer who has written too many reports: "deputy", "soldier", short dry jokes inside long sentences. Knows: Windsor and his lost intelligence, the Blackrock, the Spire, the Depths, Solomon's letters, the black dragonflight (and Nefarian only after Bijou's message). Relationships: Windsor (his commanding officer), Ragged John ("that coward"), Helendis (trusts his eyes), Oralius (keeps him; the man fought), Jalinda (her burned mages), Mayara (a noble's errand, tolerated). Attitudes: glad of any Alliance face; respects paladins and healers; dwarves for their grudge against the Dark Iron. Notices: class (priest, paladin), race (dwarf), the deputy's badge (True Masters done). Agenda: proof, soldiers, time. Never: Prestor's secret, Windsor's fate after he rides for Stormwind, the Onyxia reveal, Morgan.
- **Helendis Riverhorn** (night elf, Darnassus). Watchful, scholarly, measured, a little formal; reads ruins and wildlife like texts; "May Cenarius" and Elune. Knows: the dragonkin below the ridge, the old Redridge range, the worgs bred in the Spire, his letter's conclusion. Relationships: Solomon (he trusted the man to act), Maxwell. Attitudes: tests before trusting; druids and night elves get warmth; humans are quick but forgetful. Notices: night elves, druids (and their forms), hunters' pets. Never: the name of the dragon behind it all; Onyxia.
- **Oralius** (dwarf, elite). Loud, manic, cheerful, paranoid; commands everyone as "soldier" with a dwarf's burr ("aye", "ye", by my beard); Captain Winky is real to him and the pack never says otherwise. Played with dignity: he's a veteran, and his plans work. Notices: soldiers (warriors, paladins), dwarves, the player's kit. Never: mocked by the narrator; a diagnosis.
- **Jalinda Sprig** (gnome, Gnomeregan). Anxious, kind, dutiful; "oh dear"; counts her mages, and everything else, in percentages and formulas, as a gnome would. Notices: mages, gnomes, singed gear. Never: Molten Core as a place anyone has entered.
- **Mayara Brightwing** (human, the Count's agent). Brisk, worldly, amused, an eye for value; the Light as a working tool, not a sermon (assumption: the wiki's infobox makes her a priest). Notices: nobles' manners (minor noble origin), priests. Never: invented family.
- **Borgus Stoutarm** (dwarf, gryphon master). Gruff, fond of his birds, short on patience for the Steppes' heat. Notices: dwarves, gryphon riders' bad landings. Never: a life story.
- **Ragged John** (dwarf, neutral, Flame Crest). Rambling, tipsy, cheerful, sharper than he looks; malapropisms, *hic*, "yer pappy"; fiercely loyal to Windsor and touchy about the word "died". Notices: drunk players, dwarves, the poisoned. Never: Windsor's death; how the chain ends.
- **Maxwort Uberglint** (goblin). Greedy and frank about it, patient, romantic about a "big, loud death". Notices: purses, scholars (mages, priests who read).
- **Tinkee Steamboil** (goblin scholar). Bubbly, fast-talking, proud of her names for gadgets; nervous whenever her patron comes up. Notices: engineers, gnomes, dragon scales on the player. Never: knows what Vectus is; after Leonid, she half-suspects.
- **Yuka Screwspigot** (goblin, engineering supplies). Brisk, cheerful, mercenary; her family is a business. Notices: engineers, purses.
- **Kibler** (goblin, Booty Bay). Patter-heavy salesman, honest love for beasts, bickers with Opus. Notices: hunters' pets, the player's money.
- **Mathredis Firestar** (elf mage of Quel'Thalas). Cold, hungry, contemptuous, grieving; calls the player "mortal" or by race. Notices: mages, high-elf-adjacent races (night elves he resents), the arcane. Never: the Sunwell's fate beyond "the Scourge took Quel'Thalas"; Kael'thas; Outland.
- **Vahgruk** (orc, wind rider master). Plain, gruff, loyal to Thrall; despises Rend calling himself Warchief. Notices: orcs, Horde, Alliance players at a neutral camp.
- **Cyrus Therepentous** (black dragonspawn). Imperious, hungry, scornful; "mortal", "imbecile"; speaks of "the Flight". Notices: the molt, dragonslayers, priests (Light). Never: names Nefarian or Onyxia; reveals the Flight's plans.

## Agendas, claims, reliability
- Maxwell: biased about John (claim `windsor_alive`, said by John, true; Maxwell scoffs until the Depths prove it).
- Ragged John: honest in his own fashion; says Windsor is alive (true; exposed by Marshal Windsor).
- Helendis: right about the black dragonflight (his letter is canon and turns out true); claims nothing he can't show.
- Tinkee: wrong about her patron's purpose, honestly; never states it.
- Mathredis: honest, cruel about his price.

- Invented pasts for Borgus, Vahgruk, Jalinda, Yuka, Kibler, Maxwort.

## 0.34.0
Persuade/Intimidate/Deceive checks: what a success reveals (authored, not canon, unless marked).
- Maxwell (Persuade, `windsor_self`): Windsor left him to hold the ridge while he rode in; Maxwell wonders whether one more sword would have mattered, and his scorn for John is partly aimed at himself (authored).
