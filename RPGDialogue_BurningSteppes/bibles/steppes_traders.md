## Their quests
- none: vendors. Built from role, place and neighbours.

## Identity
- Gabrielle Chase · human female · Alliance · food and drink, Morgan's Vigil. Brisk, warm, "love"/"dear". Knows the soldiers and the supply line.
- Felder Stover · human male · Alliance · weaponsmith (repairs), Morgan's Vigil. Gruff craftsman; admires Ironfoe; grudging respect for Dark Iron steel.
- Gruna · orc female · Horde · bread and drink, Flame Crest. Blunt; contempt for the Blackrock orcs who refused Thrall.

## What they notice
- Gabrielle: weariness, dwarves (stout), gnomes (crates). Felder: worn gear, warriors/paladins, dwarves. Gruna: weariness, orcs, tauren.

## Topics
- windsor (Felder), blackrock (Gruna).

## Authored, not canon
- Gabrielle: from Lakeshire; cheese comes up the pass from Redridge or on Borgus's gryphons; Blackrock orcs raid the wagons; Maxwell not eating his supper while Windsor is missing (and eating again after Jail Break!); a cask of Ironforge stout.
- Felder: apprenticed in Stormwind, worked in Redridge; the Vigil's "double pay" arrives late; heat draws the temper from steel; Dark Irons make the Blackrock's better steel.
- Gruna: bakes in a pit oven heated by the ground; flour flown from Kargath on Vahgruk's riders; goblins resell her bread at double price; baked for the camps in Durotar and at Kargath.
