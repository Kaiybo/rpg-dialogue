# Mor'zul Bloodbringer  (key: morzul)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Mor%27zul_Bloodbringer (read: quest giver just west of the Altar of Storms; Classic dreadsteed chain; the Legion-era Dreadscar Rift role is not used). The wiki calls him human; RPGDialogue_Stormwind's warlock trainer calls him orcish. Lines never state his race.
- Quest pages read: Rage of Blood (7563), Wildeyes, Dreadsteed of Xoroth; Burning Steppes quests table (Doomsday Candle, Bell of Dethmoora, Wheel of the Black March, Arcanite)

## Their quests
- Mor'zul Bloodbringer (turn-in, from city warlock trainers).
- Rage of Blood (gives): glyph ink from maddened Winterspring owlkin blood; keeps some for himself, "its taste is quite euphoric".
- Wildeyes (gives): his servant Gorzeeki prepares the ink.
- Bell of Dethmoora, Wheel of the Black March, Doomsday Candle, Arcanite (give): ritual components.
- Dreadsteed of Xoroth (gives): perform each step exactly; "may the souls of your enemies swiftly find their way into your shard pouch".
