# Morgan's Vigil: the traders (keys: gabrielle_chase, felder_stover)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg Morgan's Vigil roster: Gabrielle Chase <Food & Drink> (human female; cheese and drink), Felder Stover <Weaponsmith> (human male). No quests or gossip on record.
- Morgan's Vigil: ruined village, tents, a crumbled mage tower; Morgan and many residents went into Blackrock Depths. Marshal Windsor's capture and "Jail Break!" as in `morgans_vigil.lua`/`flame_crest.lua`.

## Voices
**Gabrielle Chase.** Warm, weary, "love"; keeps a pot on and counts who comes back; refuses to be afraid of the Steppes.
**Felder Stover.** Steady, gruff Stormwind smith; complains the heat draws the temper out of steel; loyal to Maxwell.

## Authored, not canon
- Gabrielle: came up with her brother Tomas's militia company; Tomas went into the mountain after Morgan and she keeps his seat; kept an inn kitchen in Lakeshire; hangs her washing on the last standing wall (Oralius's line about it).
- Felder: learned in Stormwind's Dwarven District; re-tempers blades the heat has softened; the Dark Irons forge the Blackrock orcs' armour.

## Never
No Cataclysm Steppes (Keeshan/Troteman at the Vigil, Flamestar Post, Chiselgrip, Moira's Dark Iron settlers).
