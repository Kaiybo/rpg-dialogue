## 0.67.0 (2026-11-17)
Coming home, and a town that talks. No new files: /reload is enough if you have 0.65.0's packs in.

### "You've been away"
- **Towns remember when you were last there.** Walk back into a town after a week or more (logging out counts) and its people notice for a few hours: a guard at the gate, the innkeeper whose room has been let twice since, Bolvar, Thrall, Cairne, Jaina and about 290 others in every pack.
- **They notice how far you've come.** Mentors, trainers and quest givers who sent you out green remark on it once you've gained eight levels or more since you last spoke, and leaders notice when you come back from a long absence stronger.

### Word gets around
- **What you do with one person, the town hears.** When someone approves or disapproves of you strongly, when you win a Persuade, Intimidate or Deceive check, when a lie is seen through, or when you finish a quest for the town, everyone else there hears it, and the towns nearby hear it a day later.
- **They say so.** Townsfolk, guards and named people greet you with what they've heard for two weeks after, warm or wary depending on what it was.
- **It moves your standing a little.** Leaning on people or getting caught lying costs you a bit with the town, and winning someone over earns a bit. The note under the answer says "Word of this will get around Goldshire."

Packs: every pack gets a minor version (World 1.30.0).

### Fixes before release
- **A /reload no longer wipes "you've been away".** Reloading (or stepping out of the gate and back) within a few hours of coming home used to make the town forget you had just returned.
- **Time logged out counts properly.** Logging back in somewhere other than the town you logged out in no longer resets that town's clock to the login time.
- **Standing from word can't be farmed.** Repeating the same reply or check with the same person moves a town's standing only once a day.

## 0.64.0 (2026-11-17)
Core only; no pack changes. No new files: /reload is enough if you have 0.39.0 or later.

### Text to speech fixes
- **No more silent lines when the game's speech volume was never set.** If the game reports its text-to-speech volume as 0, RPG Dialogue now speaks the line itself at full volume, instead of handing it to the game's own routine, which played it silently.
- **Quest pages read only what the quest giver says.** Objectives, rewards and required items stay on the page and aren't read out.
- `/rpgd tts` now also shows the game's own volume setting next to the one we use, so a muted setting is easy to spot.

## 0.63.0 (2026-11-17)
Core only; no pack changes. Includes 0.62.0 (tidier settings, journal button). No new files: /reload is enough if you have 0.39.0 or later.

### Text to speech that actually speaks
- **It talks now.** The game changed how its speech call takes its arguments, and on newer clients our old call ended up playing every line at volume 0. RPG Dialogue now speaks through the game's own text-to-speech routine first, so it matches whatever your client expects.
- **Men and women sound different.** Men speak in the game's Standard voice and women in its Alternate voice (Options → Accessibility → Text to Speech, where you also set speed and volume). If both are set to the same voice, it picks a fitting one by name.
- **Clean reading.** Colour codes, links, icons and *<emotes in brackets>* are no longer read out.
- **Lines don't swallow each other.** Moving to the next line stops the old one and starts the new one a moment later, so the new line isn't cut off with it.
- **Quest and gossip pages are read too** (Settings → "Reading and accessibility" → "Also read quest and gossip pages aloud", on by default when reading aloud is on).
- **Test it:** the "Test the voices" button in settings, or `/rpgd tts`, reads one line in each voice and prints what it's using (voices found, which voice for men and women, speed, volume, and any error).
- Reminder: turn on "Read dialogue aloud" in Settings → "Reading and accessibility"; it's off by default.

### Dungeon clears count more reliably
- **A boss falling in front of you counts.** Before, a dungeon only counted as cleared if you or a party member landed the killing blow on its last boss. Now it also counts when you're inside and the boss dies to a pet, a totem, a raid member outside your party, or the fight itself.
