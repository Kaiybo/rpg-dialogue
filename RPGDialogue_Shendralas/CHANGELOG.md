# RPG Dialogue: Shen'dralas (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-17)
- Named NPCs answer many more "Ask about" topics (3 to 6 each, more for leaders), mostly the new world topics added in World 1.36.0.

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-17)
- Prejudices (0.80.0): Archmage Mordent Evenshade (night elves). They let it show, you can push back or win them round, and it fades as they warm to you. No new files: a /reload is enough.

## 1.2.2 (2026-11-17)
- The Magram and Gelkis now react to your standing with their clan, not only to how many of the other clan you've killed. Orgrul, Hagiak and Nerlokh have new greetings for anyone their clan hates.

## 1.2.1 (2026-11-17)
- Fix: one of Orgrul's replies was a question carrying alignment; it's a statement now.

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-17)
- Writing pass: stock phrases ("Fair enough", "I won't pretend", "truly", neat little morals) rewritten in each speaker's own voice, and more grit and humour in the everyday lines.

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-17)
- The town's leading people greet you differently when you come back after a fortnight or more, notice how far you've come since they last saw you, and have heard what you did with their neighbours. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.67.0.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-17)
- New pack. New folder: exit the game fully, copy it in, tick it and start the game again (/reload won't load it).
