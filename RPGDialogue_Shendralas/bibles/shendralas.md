# Shen'dralas  (keys: shd_evenshade, shd_orgrul, shd_hagiak, shd_nerlokh; pool: citizen_shendralas)

New Forever zone (patch 1.60.1): contested, south-east of Desolace, between Feralas and Mulgore, reached on foot through the Valley of Bones. Highborne ruins (Shen'dralar), the Magram centaur, the Bristleback quilboar. Everything below was read on warcraft.wiki.gg on 2026-11-17. The wiki's pages are thin: no quest pages exist yet for the zone's own chain, and the subzone pages (Bristleback Retreat, Evenshade Overlook, Magram Front, Outcast Hideaway, Shen'dralas subzone) are not written. The characters are kept to what their pages and quest titles support.

## Assumed (small, to confirm)
- Nerlokh stands at the Valley of Bones (his quest says "here at the Valley of Bones"; no NPC page).
- Outcast Necromancers are Magram necromancers outside the clan who steal the valley's dead (from the mob name, the quest title and Outcast Hideaway).
- Bristleback Retreat faces the Magram Front, and the quilboar and the Magram fight over the ground between them (Hagiak ends Bristleback Retreat and gives the starvation quests).
- The Magram Front is hungry (from Threat of Starvation and Long-Term Solutions).
- Corrupting Tears involves bottled sorrow (title only).
- Proof of Concept / Bone Raising have the player use a necromancy staff (Khan Jehn's "a familiar weapon ... last time you held it").

## Authored, not canon
- Mordent watched the rebels flee and believed them dead; he kept silent during the purge and stayed in the library.
- Mordent comes out at night for the moonlight on the temple; the spirits nod to him in the evenings.
- Mordent thinks the Magram's raised dead "were never asked", and watches the Bristleback "like ants".
- Hagiak's quiet doubt about Orgrul ("talks to bones more than to warriors").
- Orgrul's contempt for the Crusade's hammers; his questions for a Forsaken.
- The Bristleback were "pushed out of somewhere better" (Hagiak reading their camp's name; nobody states where from).

## Year 1 discipline
- Mordent is still of the cloistered order; no petition to Tyrande, no Darnassus, no reintegration, no Wolfheart, no Bel'ameth. He can only wish the kaldorei would hear him.
- Tortheldrin is alive and Immol'thar chained unless the player killed the Prince (`killed`). Immol'thar is never named; "a creature of the Nether".
- No Cataclysm Desolace (Burning Blade at Magram Village, Khan Kammah, perfect ones). Khan Jehn is the Magram khan.
- The Valley of Bones' RPG claims (Old God bones, cursed valley) are not used. Brother Anton is not mentioned (the Desolace pack has him alive).
- Skyborne: only BRIEF §10 (shen'dorei, the Shal'nan's rebels, High Order / Windshapers). Mordent is astonished; he states nothing new about them.

## Characters

### Archmage Mordent Evenshade (major)
- Scholar's voice, long sentences, gentle irony, grief under it. Says "my people", "the Prince", "Eldre'Thalas", never "Dire Maul" unless quoting others.
- Notices: night elves (old wound), Skyborne (astonishment), mages (the arcane), the Heirloom ring, Narassin's tome, Eluvi'ana, the Star.
- Agenda: see Luminarian's blades made whole; keep the city's name alive.
- Reliability: honest about the past, unsure of his own courage.

### Orgrul (major)
- Cold, unhurried, never raises his voice; "outsider" until the Khan Jehn ritual, then "Magram". Strength is the only law.
- Notices: servants of the Light, Forsaken, anyone who killed Khan Jehn.
- Agenda: the dead serve the clan; the outcasts are thieves; the khan comes back.

### Hagiak (quest)
- Blunt front-line officer; hunger and quilboar; little patience for talk or for bones.
- Notices: warriors, quilboar killers.

### Nerlokh (quest)
- Gelkis, devout to the Earth Mother, impatient with careful elders; acts without leave.
- Notices: shamans and druids, Magram friends.

### Pool: citizen_shendralas
- The Shen'dralar spirits: soft, half-remembering, courteous. No notices (broad pool).
