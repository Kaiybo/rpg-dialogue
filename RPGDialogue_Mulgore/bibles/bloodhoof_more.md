## Identity
- Skorn Whitecloud · Bloodhoof · old tauren hunter, hard of hearing; shame over Ghost Howl.
- Varg Windwhisper · Bloodhoof · tauren leather merchant; friendly, busy.
- Var'jun · Bloodhoof · Darkspear troll sent to find young troll priests; earnest, homesick.
- Kar Stormsinger · Bloodhoof · tauren riding trainer; kodo are kin.
- Seikwa · Bloodhoof · tauren stable master; gentle, fussy.

## Authored, not canon
- Skorn chased plainstriders before the Venture Company came; "half wolf" says his mother.
- Varg is the better seller and Ahanu the better leatherworker; he won't sell kodo hides; buys Bristleback boar hides.
- Var'jun misses the sound of the sea.
- Kar's reasoning for the exalted rule ("a kodo is family"); old kodo walk to the Barrens graveyard.
- Seikwa has rehomed eleven abandoned beasts and kept an old plainstrider nobody wants.
