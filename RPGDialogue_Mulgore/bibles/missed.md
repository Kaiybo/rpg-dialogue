## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg pages of the same names (via `tools/npc.py`), Classic ids 3210-3224 (Braves), 3069-3081, 3688, 5939, 5940, 6290, 6776, 11944.
- Braves, read: Greathoof wanders by the giant skeleton south of Camp Narache; Lightninghorn patrols the road just north of it; Proudsnout patrols the graveyard just west of it; Running Wolf patrols its southeast corner; Rockhorn patrols just south of Bloodhoof Village. The other eight are Bloodhoof Village guards who give directions (no post stated). Dawneagle is one of the oldest NPCs in the game (alpha).
- Bronk Steelrage (Armorer & Shieldcrafter), Marjak Keenblade (Weaponsmith), Varia Hardhide (Leather Armor Merchant): all in Camp Narache's armory tent. Kawnie Softbreeze: general goods, Camp Narache.
- Harn Longcast: fishing supplies near the bridge leading north out of Bloodhoof Village. Kennah Hawkseye: gunsmith. Moorat Longstride: general goods (bread, water, bags, arrows, shot, flint). Wunna Darkmane: trade goods, only seller of Recipe: Roasted Kodo Meat. Magrin Rivermane: stands in the Bloodhoof inn, no role.
- Chaw Stronghide (leatherworking), Yonn Deepcut (skinning), Vira Younghoof (bandages): trainers; Forever lists Camping 101: Leatherworking / Skinning / First Aid on them. Reban Freerunner: pet trainer. Vorn Skyseer: ends Welcome! (the gift-voucher quest); cannot be attacked and does not call the guards.
- Existing pack: Baine, Innkeeper Kauth, Kaga Wildhoof (camping), Uthan Stillwater, Yaw Sharpmane, Krang Stonehoof, Mahnott Roughwound, Jhawna Oatwind, Harutt Thunderhorn, Moodan Sungrain, Chief and Grull Hawkwind.

## Identity / voice (all tauren: slow, whole sentences, Earth Mother, plains imagery)
- Brave Greathoof · male · thoughtful, reverent; the great kodo bones south of Narache; watches cougars and stray battleboars, and the young who climb the bones.
- Brave Lightninghorn · male · brisk, proud, walks fast; the road north of Narache; counts who leaves the mesa.
- Brave Proudsnout · male · solemn, hushed; the burial ground west of Narache; keeps boars off it; wary courtesy to Forsaken.
- Brave Running Wolf · male · fierce, impatient; southeast corner of Narache facing Brambleblade Ravine; wants to fight the Bristleback and is told to hold.
- Brave Cloudmane · male · mild; north bridge of Bloodhoof; reads the weather; gives directions.
- Brave Darksky · male · wary, blunt; east side, night watch; distrusts the Venture Co. and stealthy strangers.
- Brave Dawneagle · female · steady elder brave; village centre; remembers the wandering years and the centaur; trained the others.
- Brave Ironhorn · male · stern drillmaster; by the trainers' lodges; judges posture and gear.
- Brave Leaping Deer · female · cheerful, youngest; the lake shore; chases swoops off calves; has a bow.
- Brave Rainchaser · female · patient tracker; west side toward the Palemane; reads the mud.
- Brave Rockhorn · male · stolid, few words; south road; greets the young down from Narache.
- Brave Strongbash · male · hearty, huge; outside the inn; wrestles; talks too much.
- Brave Swiftwind · female · alert runner; eastern road; collects news; the struck caravan.
- Bronk Steelrage · male · gruff shield-maker; "the strap fails, never the shield". Marjak Keenblade · male · calm; a first weapon should teach, not flatter. Varia Hardhide · female · practical, thrifty; plain dusty leathers. The three bicker amiably over whose work keeps a brave alive.
- Kawnie Softbreeze · female · warm, motherly; fills every waterskin.
- Harn Longcast · male · easygoing teller of fishing stories; friendly rivalry with Uthan.
- Kennah Hawkseye · male · curious; a tauren gunsmith who knows it looks odd; argues respectfully with the elders.
- Moorat Longstride · male · genial ex-trader of the Barrens trails. Wunna Darkmane · female · shrewd; guesses your craft; proud of the kodo recipe.
- Magrin Rivermane · male · quiet listener by the inn fire.
- Chaw Stronghide · male · patient; "the last stitch". Yonn Deepcut · male · dry; "the first cut"; thanks before the knife. They share a lodge and one long argument.
- Vira Younghoof · female · gentle and firm; checks everyone for hurts.
- Reban Freerunner · male · easy; greets the beast before the hunter.
- Vorn Skyseer · male · serene; reads clouds and birds; welcomes newcomers.

## Authored, not canon
- The posts of the eight Bloodhoof Braves without a wiki post (Cloudmane north bridge, Darksky east/night, Dawneagle centre, Ironhorn trainers' lodges, Leaping Deer lake shore, Rainchaser west, Strongbash inn, Swiftwind east road), and all the Braves' tempers.
- Dawneagle served through the wandering years and trained the other braves; Swiftwind runs messages to Thunder Bluff; Strongbash wrestles; Leaping Deer finished her rites two summers ago.
- Proudsnout's grandfather lies in the burial ground; scaffold burial as the Hawkwind custom.
- Kennah learned guns from the orcs during the centaur war; barrels come by caravan. Harn's wager with Uthan. Moorat traded between camps in the Barrens. Kawnie raised three calves. Magrin has no trade; he simply sits at the inn.
- Chaw's first vest; Vira's silverleaf wash; Reban once kept a plainstrider.
- What each Camping 101 craft adds to a camp is left vague on purpose (Forever details not captured).
- Vorn's voucher is "a token of welcome sent ahead by friends from far away".
