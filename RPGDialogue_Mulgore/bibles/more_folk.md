# Mulgore: more folk (keys: wildrunner, lanka, moodan, harant, uthan, rimblat)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg (Classic and Forever parts only): Brave Wildrunner (guard in Bloodhoof Village who gives directions; Forever: Longwalker Malah > Grim Tidings > Our Ancient Enemy). Lanka Farshot <Hunter Trainer> (Camp Narache; Classic: ends Etched Note, whose text gives "your bloodline is strong... the plains of Mulgore will be your home... teaching you how to tame your pet, how to fire your rifle... hunters are a strong part of our tradition"). Moodan Sungrain <Baker> (bread and drink, Camp Narache). Harant Ironbrace <Armorer & Shieldcrafter> (Bloodhoof Village). Uthan Stillwater <Fishing Trainer> (banks of Stonebull Lake west of Bloodhoof; Forever: ends Camping 101: Fishing, the Fish Bowl "to provide a bit of joy"). Rimblat Earthshatter (Forever: at Camp Gev'rek "where the Earthen Ring and skyborne Windshapers have come together"; a young Earthen Ring shaman who believes poisoned land can heal).
- BRIEF.md (Skyborne): Windshapers joined the Horde through the Earthen Ring; Muln Earthfury helped defeat Lorthuna.

## Their quests
- Wildrunner: Longwalker Malah, Grim Tidings, Our Ancient Enemy (TODO_QUEST; objectives not on record, so lines stay vague: Malah is a longwalker scout, the tidings are grim).
- Lanka: Etched Note (ends). (The Hunter's Path is Cataclysm; not used.)
- Uthan: Camping 101: Fishing (ends). Others: none.

## Voices
- **Brave Wildrunner.** Steady, earnest, duty first; hates being stuck at his post while others walk.
- **Lanka Farshot.** Sharp, sparing with praise; patience, steady hand, respect for the prey; the tribe before the self.
- **Moodan Sungrain.** Loud, jovial, feeds everyone; argues with Chief Hawkwind about flour.
- **Harant Ironbrace.** Gruff; reads dents; a shield is a promise to the line.
- **Uthan Stillwater.** Quiet, patient, speaks as if the fish can hear.
- **Rimblat Earthshatter.** Young, earnest, hopeful about healing the land; stays vague about Skyborne matters beyond the brief.

## Authored, not canon
Wildrunners guarding Bloodhoof since it was tents; Lanka's teacher buried near Red Cloud Mesa; Moodan's honey loaf; Harant's reading of tusk and claw marks; Uthan's uncaught fish on the northern bank; Rimblat's wish to see the eastern lands.

## Never
Anything after Year 1; no Cataclysm Mulgore; nothing about the Skyborne beyond BRIEF.md.

### Their quests
- Ravenfeather: Call of Earth (ritual salve from Bristleback shamans; sapta; Kodo Rock).
- Narm: Call of Fire, Call of Water (sends the shaman on; no detail beyond the names).
- Harb, Chaw, Pyall, Wunna: none: vendors and trainers, built from role, place and neighbours.

### Voices
- **Seer Ravenfeather.** Serene, exacting, long pauses; tests stillness where Graytongue tests courage.
- **Narm Skychaser.** Calm, a little dry; enjoys how far he sends his students.
- **Harb Clawhoof.** Patient and proud; a kodo is persuaded, never broken; asks the herd's leave.
- **Chaw Stronghide.** Gruff, exact, sparing with praise; nothing wasted.
- **Pyall Silentstride.** Quiet, kindly; hands you a spoon before he asks your name.
- **Wunna Darkmane.** Brisk and shrewd; keeps tally on knotted cord; will not buy from the Venture Company.

### Authored, not canon
Harb's grey cow and the grass fire; Chaw's crooked straps; Pyall's mother's pot; the Darkmanes as traders between the herds; Wunna's knotted cord.

## Added later (keys: kawnie, marjak, varia, bronk, reban, moorat, kennah, vira, yonn, harn)

### Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg (Classic and Forever parts only): Kawnie Softbreeze <General Goods>, Marjak Keenblade <Weaponsmith>, Varia Hardhide <Leather Armor Merchant>, Bronk Steelrage <Armorer & Shieldcrafter> (Camp Narache; the last three share the armory tent; Bronk is of the Steelrage tribe). Reban Freerunner <Pet Trainer>, Moorat Longstride <General Goods> (other Longstrides exist elsewhere), Kennah Hawkseye <Gunsmith>, Vira Younghoof <Bandage Trainer> (Forever: ends Camping 101: First Aid, the First Aid Kit), Yonn Deepcut <Skinning Trainer> (Forever: ends Camping 101: Skinning, the Camp Chair), Harn Longcast <Fishing Supplies> (near the bridge leading north out of Bloodhoof Village).

### Their quests
- Vira: Camping 101: First Aid (ends; TODO_QUEST). Yonn: Camping 101: Skinning (ends; TODO_QUEST). Both echo the quest's own completion text: camps belong out in the wild, not in the middle of town.
- Kawnie, Marjak, Varia, Bronk, Reban, Moorat, Kennah, Harn: none: vendors and trainers, built from role, place and neighbours.

### Voices
- **Kawnie Softbreeze.** Gentle, fussing; packs every young brave's bag and remembers what each bought.
- **Marjak Keenblade.** Exact and proud; plain weapons on purpose; the hand chooses the weapon.
- **Varia Hardhide.** Frank and wry; rough leather meant to be outgrown; lets the two smiths argue.
- **Bronk Steelrage.** Booming, earnest ("Ho!"); a shield before a weapon; secretly admires Marjak.
- **Reban Freerunner.** Patient, watchful; a pet is a partner who has agreed to stay.
- **Moorat Longstride.** Easygoing, talkative; trades goods for news of roads he has never walked.
- **Kennah Hawkseye.** Deliberate, thoughtful; sells guns and is still making up his mind about them.
- **Vira Younghoof.** Brisk and kind; no patience for braves who bleed quietly out of pride.
- **Yonn Deepcut.** Quiet, reverent; a word of thanks before the first cut. Consistent with Chaw Stronghide: Yonn takes the hide off, Chaw works it.
- **Harn Longcast.** Unhurried, droll; a fisher who sells things, at the best spot on Stonebull Lake.

### Authored, not canon
Kawnie keeping the names of braves who did not return; Marjak's hurried mace; Varia's yearly tooled belt; Bronk's split shield and the Steelrage as travelling smiths; Reban growling at meat; Moorat never having left Mulgore; Kennah as the bow hunter who first asked for a gun, and the brave called One-Brow; Vira's brave with the split hoof; Yonn's words of thanks; Harn's fish under the third post and his frost-day fishing with Uthan.
