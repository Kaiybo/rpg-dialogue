## Voices
- **Brave Wildrunner.** Steady, earnest, duty first; hates being stuck at his post while others walk.
- **Lanka Farshot.** Sharp, sparing with praise; patience, steady hand, respect for the prey; the tribe before the self.
- **Moodan Sungrain.** Loud, jovial, feeds everyone; argues with Chief Hawkwind about flour.
- **Harant Ironbrace.** Gruff; reads dents; a shield is a promise to the line.
- **Uthan Stillwater.** Quiet, patient, speaks as if the fish can hear.
- **Rimblat Earthshatter.** Young, earnest, hopeful about healing the land; stays vague about Skyborne matters beyond the brief.
