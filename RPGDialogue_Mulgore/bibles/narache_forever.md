## Their quests
- None (trainers and a guide).

## Identity and voices
- Karne: warm elder guide, "little one"; paraphrases the profession gossip; deflects the Mor Grayhoof question (the name is old and common; he is no hero).
- Garan: gentle; talks to plants; takes one in three like his grandmother.
- Nawka: quick, fierce; wastes nothing; thanks each beast before the cut.
- Vartha: steady as stone; asks the Earth Mother's leave; tauren leave the deep places to the dwarves.

## Invented details (authored, not canon)
- Garan's grandmother's rule; Nawka hunted with her mother; Vartha's family cut stone on the mesa for generations.

## What they notice
- Their own trade's progress; Karne: very new characters; Garan: druids; Nawka: beast pets; Vartha: Forsaken.
