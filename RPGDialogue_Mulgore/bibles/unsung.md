# Mulgore's unsung  (keys: harutt, meela, gart, krang, mahnott, jhawna)

People with no quests and little or no gossip in the game: trainers and shopkeepers. Written from memory of the vanilla world; no wiki page read in this pass.

## Sources
- Vanilla 1.12 rosters (memory): Harutt Thunderhorn (Warrior Trainer), Meela Dawnstrider (Shaman Trainer), Gart Mistrunner (Druid Trainer) at Camp Narache; Krang Stonehoof (Warrior Trainer), Mahnott Roughwound (Weaponsmith), Jhawna Oatwind (Baker) in Bloodhoof Village.
- bibles/mulgore.md for Grull and Chief Hawkwind, Baine, Ahab Wheathoof (Kyle's Gone Missing!), the Venture Company, Bael'dun, the Palemane gnolls.

## Their quests
- None of their own (class trainers receive the starting-zone class letters only; not written).

## Identity and voices
- Harutt: broad, slow-spoken; strength is patience; counts his braves home. Lanka Farshot named as Narache's hunter trainer.
- Meela: serene shaman; listen before asking; the elements are not tools; orcs found the spirits again through Thrall.
- Gart: quiet druid; Cenarius taught the tauren; Moonglade welcomes them though some elves resent the Horde (authored, not canon, in tone).
- Krang: gruff, proud of the tauren as Horde warriors; Venture Company, Palemane gnolls, the Bael'dun dwarves; the debt to Thrall for the centaur.
- Mahnott: dry weaponsmith; "a tauren with a little dagger looks like a kodo wearing a hat"; won't buy goblin iron (authored, not canon).
- Jhawna: warm baker; wheat from the farms around the village; feeds the braves (authored, not canon).

## Never
- Anything Cataclysm or later (Grimtotem coup, Magatha's betrayal, Cairne's death).

## What they notice
- Trainers: their own class, shapes (ghost wolf, bear), wounds. Mahnott: blacksmiths. Jhawna: wounds and hunger.
