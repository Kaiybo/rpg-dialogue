# RPG Dialogue: Mulgore (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.38.0 (2026-11-27): Every Voice Its Own
Deeper conversations: new branches for the NPCs with the fewest, shopkeepers' and trainers' disposition lines written for each person, and repeated lines rewritten in each speaker's own voice. No new files in this pack.

## 1.37.0 (2026-11-27): Word Gets Around
Disposition greetings for shopkeepers, trainers and other service folk; reputation and holiday lines where they fit. No new files in this pack.

## 1.36.1 (2026-11-25): Mending the Seams
- Bug pass: lines and replies that never showed (wrong condition keys or values, unremembered "heard" lines, duplicate line ids, NPCs sharing one key) fixed; stance replies phrased as statements.

## 1.36.0 (2026-11-25): Friends, Foes and Fools
- Major characters answer the new friendly topics and grievances in their own voice.

## 1.35.0 (2026-11-25): More to Say
- Thin conversations deepened: more topics, a longer thread, and a reply only some characters get.

## 1.34.0 (2026-11-24): Fewer Words, Same Voice
- NPC speeches over 60 words were trimmed so they fit the dialogue box; nothing they say was lost.

## 1.33.0 (2026-11-24): Old Grudges
- Major NPCs answer for a grudge you hold against them (or they against you), and let things slip when angry.
- Overlong speeches tightened.

## 1.32.0 (2026-11-23): The Overlooked
- Characters who had no conversation now have one (core 0.105.0).

## 1.31.0 (2026-11-23)
