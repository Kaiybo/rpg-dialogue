# RPG Dialogue: Mulgore (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.2.1 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: Camp Narache, Bloodhoof Village and Camp Gev'rek now talk in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments, slow and deliberate the tauren way (Hawkwind's resolve, Baine's diplomacy, Zarlman's calm, Mull's worry, Ahab's fretting over Kyle, Ayessa's steadiness). Same lines, same facts, same conditions.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)
- Neighbours remark on each other: Kauth on Mull, Harken on Maur.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits now bring personal lines (habits, worries, opinions of the neighbours), night-time lines, and a question or two about the person themselves, not only their quests.
- Bloodhoof: Baine on how the tauren came to Mulgore, Zarlman, Harken, Maur's name, Mull, Ahab and Kyle, Kauth's stew, Kaga. The Venture Company topic.

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- Camp Narache: Chief Hawkwind and Grull Hawkwind. The camp's braves.
- Bloodhoof Village: Baine Bloodhoof, Zarlman Two-Moons, Harken Windtotem, Maur Raincaller, Mull Thunderhorn, Ahab Wheathoof (back in Forever, still missing his dog) and Innkeeper Kauth. The village's braves.
- Camp Gev'rek: Ayessa Dawnsinger of the Windshapers (Skyborne facts from the lore brief only).
