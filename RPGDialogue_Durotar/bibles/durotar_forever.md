## Their quests
- Pa'zula, A Pain in the Neck (gives/turn-in, Enchanting): Zalazane binds his trolls' minds with pendants wrapped in binding spells; disenchant them for Luminous Residue. "Don't put one around your neck." Completion: a shame about the slain trolls, but their spirits can rest. Reveals grief for the hexed, pride in craft, patience.
- Pal'juh, Lost in the Shadows (gives; ends with Master Vornal, Sen'jin): scouted the crag alone, magic ran out, hiding. Vornal: promising hunter, "more like a raptor than a tiger." Reveals pride, impulsiveness.
- Turroc, For Honor (gives/turn-in): many Horde warriors died sacking Tiragarde Keep; humans reinforced the ruins, so their remains can't be honored; bring back the bow, battleaxe and shield to display in a place of honor. Reveals remembrance, duty.
- Kagil, Norzsh, Thedda, Zor'la, Slizzy: none (trainers, guide, flavor NPC).

## Identity and voices
- Kagil: gruff orc skinner; leather for the Horde; mild scorn for "talking about it" (Thedda). Notices skinners, beast pets.
- Norzsh: gruffer; "listen to the stone"; copper in the valley cliffs. Notices miners, warriors.
- Thedda: warm, patient guide; paraphrases her game gossip; recommends paired trades. Notices very new characters.
- Zor'la: slow-spoken Darkspear, "mon"; misses the Echo Isles; Durotar's herbs grow "angry". Notices herbalists, trolls.
- Pa'zula: sharp Darkspear enchanter; speaks of the Echo Isles as if still there (explains her gossip); binding steel vs binding minds. Notices enchanters, casters, trolls.
- Pal'juh: proud, quick trainee; raptor vs tiger; reports Kolkar gathering. Notices spirit/shadow casters, trolls.
- Slizzy Kreeg: fierce, gentle only with Morok'ra; "break its fear, keep its fury". Notices beast pets, mounts.
- Turroc: grim keeper of the barracks and of names; Thrall wants Durotar held, not a war on every shore. Notices warriors, veterans.

## Invented details (authored, not canon)
- Kagil lost hides to a wolf; the goblins at Razor Hill buy leather.
- Zor'la: picked herbs on the Echo Isles before Zalazane.
- Pa'zula: lived on the isles all her life; tiger/raptor saying borrowed from Vornal's text; studies residue to help the shamans undo Zalazane someday.
- Pal'juh: wanted to prove one quiet troll beats three loud trainees; returns to the crag after the quest to scout carefully.
- Slizzy found Morok'ra torn up in the scrub off the western road. Morok'ra's species is never named (unknown from the wiki).
- Turroc fought at Tiragarde himself; the weapons' owners (a hunter, a singing axeman, a gate-holder) and "old Gorrak" naming them are authored.
