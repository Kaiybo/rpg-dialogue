# Durotar's unsung  (keys: frang, kenjai, duokna, uhgar, thotar, kaplak, taijin, unthuwa)

People with no quests and little or no gossip in the game: trainers and shopkeepers. Written from memory of the vanilla world; no wiki page read in this pass.

## Sources
- Vanilla 1.12 rosters (memory): Frang (Warrior Trainer), Ken'jai (Priest Trainer) and Duokna (General Goods) in the Den, Valley of Trials; Uhgar (Weaponsmith), Thotar (Hunter Trainer), Kaplak (Rogue Trainer), Tai'jin (Priest Trainer) in Razor Hill; Un'Thuwa (Mage Trainer) in Sen'jin Village.
- bibles/durotar.md for the places, Gornek, Galgar, Kzan, Thazz'ril, Zureetha, Gar'Thok, Orgnil, Gadrin.

## Their quests
- None of their own (class trainers receive the starting-zone class letters only; not written).

## Identity and voices
- Frang: orc, loud, plain; guard first, charge later; judges recruits by getting up. Learned to fight with sticks in the internment camps (authored, not canon).
- Ken'jai: Darkspear troll, calm, mends Frang's recruits; "one river with many names" for spirits and Light; warns about shadow. Came to the valley because of the Darkspear's debt to Thrall (authored, not canon).
- Duokna: brisk shopkeeper; water first; nags Thazz'ril about the peons who haul the goods. Gender not stated in the lines (unsure).
- Uhgar: gruff orc weaponsmith; heavy for quilboar, quick for Kul Tirans; learned by mending guards' blades in the camps (authored, not canon).
- Thotar: calm orc hunter; beasts as people worth respecting; scorpids for loyalty, Barrens raptors.
- Kaplak: sly orc rogue in a burrow under Razor Hill; his students scout Tiragarde Keep and the Blade's caves for Gar'Thok and Orgnil (authored, not canon).
- Tai'jin: warm troll woman; family fishes off Sen'jin; the grunts call her auntie (authored, not canon); misses the Echo Isles.
- Un'Thuwa: dry troll mage; proud of troll magic (old empires, Zul'Aman, Stranglethorn); the witch doctors call his art cold, Gadrin respects it (authored, not canon).

## Never
- Anything Cataclysm or later (Northwatch war, Echo Isles retaken, Darkspear rebellion).
- Promises of discounts or free training.

## What they notice
- Trainers: their own class (race+class combos), wounds. Duokna: an empty purse. Uhgar: blacksmiths. Kaplak: stealth. Thotar: the hunter's pet.
