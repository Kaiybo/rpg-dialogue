## Their quests
- Telf: Call of Fire (the torch from Kranal), Fire Sapta (fire tar from the Razormane, a reagent pouch), then the fire spirit on the peak.
- The rest: none. Bom'bay's listed quests are Cataclysm; left out.

## Never
- Cataclysm Durotar: Kor'kron, Darkspear Rebellion, Garrosh, Bom'bay's Cataclysm quests, Raider Jhash's Sen'jin post, Season of Discovery runes.
- Bom'bay actually hexing the player (the addon can't); he only talks about it.

## Voices (authored, not canon)
- Telf: wry, lonely troll on a cold peak; the fire tests and grows the longer you wait (light stance on earning its respect); friendly disagreement with Kranal Fiss.
- Zjolnir: proud raptor breeder; raptors are family, sold only to Darkspear or exalted friends (order stance); Torka steals raptor eggs.
- Xar'Ti: stern; first lesson is falling; raptors obey from respect (light stance on fear vs trust).
- Bom'bay: eager apprentice, mishaps (a grunt turned frog); Gadrin taught Vol'jin and Zalazane, don't ask; wants to hex Zalazane (order stance on waiting).
- Hai'zan: jovial, pepper, murlocs steal his fish (light stance); rivalry with Torka.
- Grimtak: gruff, lost finger to grog and cleaver (order stance on working through pain).
- Cutac: dry, recruits need boots; Tiragarde, Burning Blade, Razormane (honor stance on Gar'Thok sending recruits).
- Dhugru: cold, soft-spoken warlock; Razor Hill avoids him; the Horde should use every weapon (light stance).
- Shikrik: steady, patient; the orcs forgot the spirits (light stance on the old debt); sends to Canaga and Searn.
- Zlagk: gruff feeder of recruits; boar circular economy (order stance on hardship).
- Jhash: blunt patrol rider; Burning Blade near Skull Rock, Neeru in the Cleft (honor stance on dull duty).

## What they notice
- Telf: shaman, trolls. Zjolnir: trolls, beast pets. Xar'Ti: trolls. Bom'bay: shaman, Forsaken. Hai'zan: tauren. Grimtak: cooks. Cutac: mages. Dhugru: shaman. Shikrik: shaman. Zlagk: hungry new recruits. Jhash: warriors.

## Added later (missed NPCs)
Sources: warcraft.wiki.gg pages for Ukor, Ruzan, Nartok, Jen'shan, Mai'ah, Rwag (Valley of Trials), Jark, Krunn (Razor Hill), Lau'Tiki (Darkspear Strand), Mishiki (Sen'jin Village). All pre-Cataclysm placements.

### Their quests
- Ukor: A Peon's Burden (carry his bundle of food to Innkeeper Grosk in Razor Hill). He brought it to the valley unasked and found it wasn't needed; too tired to carry it back, too honest to eat it.
- Ruzan: Vile Familiars (warlock version: a dozen vile familiars outside the Burning Blade Coven, then the imp). He judges who is fit; Nartok teaches.
- Nartok, Jen'shan, Mai'ah, Rwag: none beyond the class-letter hand-ins from Kaltunk (not written as quests here).
- Jark, Krunn, Lau'Tiki, Mishiki: none: vendors and profession trainers, built from role, place and neighbours.

### Voices (authored, not canon)
- Ukor: humble, kindly, whole sentences (no pidgin); proud of the Razor Hill gate; honor stance on eating the bundle, light stance on the patrol.
- Ruzan: wary, exacting; a demon is a tool that hates you (order stance), the Burning Blade as the warlocks' own shame (honor stance); trains out of sight at Gornek's request.
- Nartok: dry, even, treats the craft as bookkeeping; chalk circles; light stance on who pays the price; keeps downwind of Shikrik.
- Jen'shan: easy, sharp Darkspear; teaches orcs to wait (order stance); worries about Hana'zua; sends students on to Thotar.
- Mai'ah: bright, proud; self-taught until Orgrimmar; wins the orcs over by being useful (light stance); friendly rivalry with Un'Thuwa and Nartok.
- Rwag: wry, watchful; scouting as unseen honor (honor stance); won't have thieving from Duokna (order stance); sends students to Kaplak, then Shenthul.
- Jark: brisk, shrewd; goblin prices, Tiragarde shooting at carts (order stance on a wider war).
- Krunn: gruff miner; copper under Razormane ground (light stance on trade vs strength); learned the trade in a camp quarry.
- Lau'Tiki: unhurried fisherman; watches the Echo Isles, thinks he saw his hexed brother (authored; light stance on the hexed trolls).
- Mishiki: calm, particular; never take the last one (order stance); picks for Gadrin and Vornal; lets Bom'bay believe in moonlight.

### What they notice
- Ukor: warriors, the freshly resurrected. Ruzan: shaman, a warlock's imp. Nartok: mages, warriors. Jen'shan: beast pets, trolls. Mai'ah: trolls, warlocks. Rwag: stealth, warriors. Jark: thin purses, mages. Krunn: smiths, broken gear. Lau'Tiki: orcs, cooks. Mishiki: alchemists, druids.

## Added later (second pass of missed NPCs)
Sources: warcraft.wiki.gg pages for Huklah, Rarc, Hraug (Valley of Trials), Wuark, Ghrawt, Flakk, Dwukk, Harruk, Kitha, Mukdrak, Rawrk, Ophek, Furl Scornbrow (Razor Hill), K'waii, Trayexir, Tai'tasi, Miao'zan, Zansoa, Kali Remik (Sen'jin Village), Frezza and Snurk Bucksquick (zeppelin tower outside Orgrimmar, in Durotar before Cataclysm). All pre-Cataclysm placements.

### Their quests
- Ophek: Gan'rul's Summons (sends orc warlocks who have outgrown the imp to Gan'rul Bloodeye in the Cleft of Shadow; the voidwalker follows). He decides who is ready, and reports the ones who worry him.
- Furl Scornbrow: Carry Your Weight (eight canvas scraps from the Kul Tiras sailors at Tiragarde Keep; he sews a bag). A watchman whose horn has gone quiet, filling the hours with a needle.
- Kali Remik: The Hunter's Path (sends young hunters to Thotar in Razor Hill for the taming). She judges readiness. Written before/active/done; the giver's exact wording in Forever is unconfirmed.
- Dwukk, Mukdrak, Rawrk: Camping 101 (Forever) hand-ins get one greeting each, nothing more.
- Huklah, Rarc, Hraug, Wuark, Ghrawt, Flakk, Harruk, Kitha, K'waii, Trayexir, Tai'tasi, Miao'zan, Zansoa, Frezza, Snurk: none: vendors, trainers and zeppelin masters, built from role, place and neighbours.

### Never
- Cataclysm and later: Runda, Magga, Thonk, Burok, Gail Nozzywig, the flooded Southfury, Razor Hill under the Kor'kron, the Darkspear Rebellion (Dwukk's and Yelnagi's wiki text), the Orgrimmar Skyway and Squibby Overspeck. The zeppelin tower stands outside Orgrimmar's gate with one platform each for Tirisfal and Grom'gol.

### Voices (authored, not canon)
- Huklah: patient valley tailor; tans downwind of the Den; recruits should earn better armor (honor stance).
- Rarc: gruff shield fitter; sells the too-big shield and lets the shoulder teach (order stance against Frang's view).
- Hraug: scarred, short-tempered; never ask a demon, tell it; each leashed imp a debt repaid (light stance).
- Wuark: steady garrison armorer; reads dents; rifle balls and Gar'Thok's refusal of guns (honor stance).
- Ghrawt: quiet bowyer in a land with no trees; Ashenvale staves from the Warsong (light stance); taught by a Darkspear on the voyage (authored).
- Flakk: cheerful, asks what everyone is making; keeps a count of Mukdrak's powder for Orgnil (order stance).
- Dwukk: stern, fair; a nail first; a smith answers for where his steel goes (honor stance; echoes the wiki's later note that he minded whom his steel served, without the later events).
- Harruk: calm, prefers wolves; refused to train for hunters who beat their beasts (order stance).
- Kitha: cool, sardonic; lets Orgnil search her shelves (order stance); voidwalker silence.
- Mukdrak: eager orc tinker who swept floors in Ratchet (authored); rifles for the Tiragarde watch (order stance).
- Rawrk: brusque, gentle hands; a Horde that carries its wounded (light stance).
- Ophek: grave watcher for Gan'rul; named two students to Orgnil, one went to the Burning Blade (authored; order stance).
- Furl Scornbrow: wry, idle; offered the Tiragarde watch (honor stance); Cutac taught him to double a seam.
- K'waii: easy, sharp; sells what washes up from wrecks; the two half-drowned sailors (light stance).
- Trayexir: proud, teasing; balance over weight; what to tell the young about the hexed kin (honor stance).
- Tai'tasi: warm, wry; the wiki only hints she is Lar Prowltusk's mate, so she says he "shares my fire" and no more; kept him at his post (honor stance).
- Miao'zan: merry, restless; dances because his grandmother did; Vornal wants reasons (order stance).
- Zansoa: dry, unhurried; Tiragarde fires on the boats; young fishers want to answer at night (order stance).
- Kali Remik: keen, teasing; the boy whose raptor turned on him (authored; light stance).
- Frezza: brisk, officious goblin; the safety rules; banned an imp and was overruled (order stance).
- Snurk Bucksquick: flirtatious, shrewd goblin; flattery as good business (honor stance); teases her brother.
