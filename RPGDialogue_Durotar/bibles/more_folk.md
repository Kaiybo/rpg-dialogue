## Voices (authored, not canon)
- Telf: wry, lonely troll on a cold peak; the fire tests and grows the longer you wait (light stance on earning its respect); friendly disagreement with Thun'grim.
- Zjolnir: proud raptor breeder; raptors are family, sold only to Darkspear or exalted friends (order stance); Torka steals raptor eggs.
- Xar'Ti: stern; first lesson is falling; raptors obey from respect (light stance on fear vs trust).
- Bom'bay: eager apprentice, mishaps (a grunt turned frog); Gadrin taught Vol'jin and Zalazane, don't ask; wants to hex Zalazane (order stance on waiting).
- Hai'zan: jovial, pepper, murlocs steal his fish (light stance); rivalry with Torka.
- Grimtak: gruff, lost finger to grog and cleaver (order stance on working through pain).
- Cutac: dry, recruits need boots; Tiragarde, Burning Blade, Razormane (honor stance on Gar'Thok sending recruits).
- Dhugru: cold, soft-spoken warlock; Razor Hill avoids him; the Horde should use every weapon (light stance).
- Shikrik: steady, patient; the orcs forgot the spirits (light stance on the old debt); sends to Canaga and Searn.
- Zlagk: gruff feeder of recruits; boar circular economy (order stance on hardship).
- Jhash: blunt patrol rider; Burning Blade near Skull Rock, Neeru in the Cleft (honor stance on dull duty).
