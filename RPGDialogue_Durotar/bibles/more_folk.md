- The rest: none. Bom'bay's listed quests are Cataclysm; left out.

- Cataclysm Durotar: Kor'kron, Darkspear Rebellion, Garrosh, Bom'bay's Cataclysm quests, Raider Jhash's Sen'jin post, Season of Discovery runes.

## Voices (authored, not canon)
- Telf: wry, lonely troll on a cold peak; the fire tests and grows the longer you wait (light stance on earning its respect); friendly disagreement with Thun'grim.
- Zjolnir: proud raptor breeder; raptors are family, sold only to Darkspear or exalted friends (order stance); Torka steals raptor eggs.
- Xar'Ti: stern; first lesson is falling; raptors obey from respect (light stance on fear vs trust).
- Bom'bay: eager apprentice, mishaps (a grunt turned frog); Gadrin taught Vol'jin and Zalazane, don't ask; wants to hex Zalazane (order stance on waiting).
- Hai'zan: jovial, pepper, murlocs steal his fish (light stance); rivalry with Torka.
- Grimtak: gruff, lost finger to grog and cleaver (order stance on working through pain).
- Cutac: dry, recruits need boots; Tiragarde, Burning Blade, Razormane (honor stance on Gar'Thok sending recruits).
- Dhugru: cold, soft-spoken warlock; Razor Hill avoids him; the Horde should use every weapon (light stance).
- Shikrik: steady, patient; the orcs forgot the spirits (light stance on the old debt); sends to Canaga and Searn.
- Zlagk: gruff feeder of recruits; boar circular economy (order stance on hardship).
- Jhash: blunt patrol rider; Burning Blade near Skull Rock, Neeru in the Cleft (honor stance on dull duty).

## What they notice
- Telf: shaman, trolls. Zjolnir: trolls, beast pets. Xar'Ti: trolls. Bom'bay: shaman, Forsaken. Hai'zan: tauren. Grimtak: cooks. Cutac: mages. Dhugru: shaman. Shikrik: shaman. Zlagk: hungry new recruits. Jhash: warriors.

## Added later (missed NPCs)
Sources: warcraft.wiki.gg pages for Ukor, Ruzan, Nartok, Jen'shan, Mai'ah, Rwag (Valley of Trials), Jark, Krunn (Razor Hill), Lau'Tiki (Darkspear Strand), Mishiki (Sen'jin Village). All pre-Cataclysm placements.

### Their quests
- Ukor: A Peon's Burden (carry his bundle of food to Innkeeper Grosk in Razor Hill). He brought it to the valley unasked and found it wasn't needed; too tired to carry it back, too honest to eat it.
- Ruzan: Vile Familiars (warlock version: a dozen vile familiars outside the Burning Blade Coven, then the imp). He judges who is fit; Nartok teaches.
- Nartok, Jen'shan, Mai'ah, Rwag: none beyond the class-letter hand-ins from Kaltunk (not written as quests here).
- Jark, Krunn, Lau'Tiki, Mishiki: none: vendors and profession trainers, built from role, place and neighbours.

### Voices (authored, not canon)
- Ukor: humble, kindly, whole sentences (no pidgin); proud of the Razor Hill gate; honor stance on eating the bundle, light stance on the patrol.
- Ruzan: wary, exacting; a demon is a tool that hates you (order stance), the Burning Blade as the warlocks' own shame (honor stance); trains out of sight at Gornek's request.
- Nartok: dry, even, treats the craft as bookkeeping; chalk circles; light stance on who pays the price; keeps downwind of Shikrik.
- Jen'shan: easy, sharp Darkspear; teaches orcs to wait (order stance); worries about Hana'zua; sends students on to Thotar.
- Mai'ah: bright, proud; self-taught until Orgrimmar; wins the orcs over by being useful (light stance); friendly rivalry with Un'Thuwa and Nartok.
- Rwag: wry, watchful; scouting as unseen honor (honor stance); won't have thieving from Duokna (order stance); sends students to Kaplak, then Shenthul.
- Jark: brisk, shrewd; goblin prices, Tiragarde shooting at carts (order stance on a wider war).
- Krunn: gruff miner; copper under Razormane ground (light stance on trade vs strength); learned the trade in a camp quarry.
- Lau'Tiki: unhurried fisherman; watches the Echo Isles, thinks he saw his hexed brother (authored; light stance on the hexed trolls).
- Mishiki: calm, particular; never take the last one (order stance); picks for Gadrin and Vornal; lets Bom'bay believe in moonlight.

### What they notice
- Ukor: warriors, the freshly resurrected. Ruzan: shaman, a warlock's imp. Nartok: mages, warriors. Jen'shan: beast pets, trolls. Mai'ah: trolls, warlocks. Rwag: stealth, warriors. Jark: thin purses, mages. Krunn: smiths, broken gear. Lau'Tiki: orcs, cooks. Mishiki: alchemists, druids.
