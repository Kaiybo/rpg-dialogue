## Their quests
- Vornal: A Solvent Spirit (makrura eyes, crawler mucus); Forgotten Loa Idols (to Gadrin); Lost in the Shadows (Pal'juh rescued).
- Vel'rin: Practical Prey (4 tiger furs, islands south); Legging It (spiders on the north road, report to Gar'Thok).
- Ula'elek: level 30 warrior chain from Thun'grim; 7 uncloven satyr hooves from northeastern Ashenvale.

## Identity
- Master Vornal · Sen'jin · troll man · alchemist/questgiver. Proud, dry, half-blind; fought beside Sen'jin; won't take apprentices.
- Vel'rin Fang · Sen'jin · troll man · hide-man. Steady, practical, careful about overhunting.
- Ula'elek · Sen'jin · troll man · smith. Grave, mystic; says the spirits tell him things.

## Authored, not canon
- Vornal's tincture is for his failing eyes; he cites Zalazane (Gadrin's student) as his reason for refusing apprentices; he sits facing the isles every morning; a young troll who touched his bottles went blue for a week.
- Vel'rin counts tracks at the waterholes each new moon; teasing rivalry with Gar'Thok ("orcs be tired because they never laugh").
- Ula'elek's smith grandfather on the isles; he and Thun'grim each keep a tally of gauntlets made, loser buys the rum.

## Reliability
All honest; Ula'elek's "the spirits say" is half showmanship.
