## Identity / voice
- Nartok · orc man · cold, watchful; demons as a chained beast; remembers the blood curse and the camps.
- Rwag · orc man · sly, quiet; Shattered Hand loyalty; watches Nartok.
- Hraug · orc man · dry bookseller in gloves; "lessons for demons, not for you".
- Huklah · orc man · plain, patient; reads a fight from the holes in a shirt.
- Rarc · orc man · gruff, proud; shields above all; teases Huklah's leather (and is teased back).
- Magga · orc woman · warm greeter; a basket of odd small companions for voucher-bearers.
- Ophek · orc man · uneasy, wry messenger; wary of the warlock he serves.
- Rawrk · orc man · gruff field medic; "bind it after the fight".
- Grunt Kor'ja · orc woman · brisk, stubborn; won't leave her road.
- Rokar Bladeshadow · old orc man · weathered, wry; a retired blade who grows vegetables.
- Harroc · young orc man · earnest, restless; wanted to fight, learned to dig.
- Javnir Nashak · orc man · solemn, generous; the feast and the dead.
- K'waii · troll woman · cheerful, shrewd. Miao'zan · troll man · merry, dances while he brews. Mishiki · troll woman · patient, hard bargainer. Trayexir · troll man · cool ex-headhunter; balance. Zansoa · troll man · talkative net-mender. Lau'Tiki · troll man · calm, teasing; "the fish decides".
- Troll lilt kept light: "mon", "ya", "dat", "I be", once or twice a line.

## Authored, not canon
- Nartok and Hraug dividing the work (warlock vs. the demon); the Orgrimmar bookseller who hears his stock whisper.
- Huklah stitched tents in the camps; Rarc learned riveting from a Blackrock smith; ironwood planks come down from the northern lumber camps.
- Magga's basket (the three Collector's Edition companions described without names); she came with the first wagons.
- Ophek was a warband runner; the summons that "can go either way".
- Rawrk carried a litter in the last war. Kor'ja's fight with four marines.
- Rokar's past as one of the Horde's quiet blades (from his name and the beta rogue quest), and his lesson with the hoe; Harroc born in a camp; hauling water from the Southfury.
- Javnir once saw Grom across a camp.
- The Sen'jin traders' losses on the Echo Isles (K'waii's hut, Mishiki's garden, Zansoa's boat); Miao'zan's cousin with hairy tusks; Mishiki supplying Miao'zan; Lau'Tiki sending pole-breakers to Zansoa.
