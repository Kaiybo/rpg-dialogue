# RPG Dialogue: Durotar (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Replies for the classes and races these people would really react to (warriors, hunters, rogues, mages, warlocks, druids, shamans, paladins; every race of the faction, and the Skyborne), each leading somewhere.

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Every person here writes a first impression in your diary after you first talk (the journal's People page shows it too).

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.1.1 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: the Valley of Trials, Razor Hill and Sen'jin now talk in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments, each in their own voice (Gornek's drill, Gar'Thok's terse orders, Orgnil's grimness, Thazz'ril's exasperation, Grosk's dry humour, Gadrin's troll lilt). Same lines, same facts, same conditions.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits bring personal lines (habits, worries, running jokes), and some NPCs get night-time lines. Kaltunk's and Galgar's stories from the camps; Thazz'ril, Kzan, Grosk.

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- The Valley of Trials: Kaltunk, Gornek, Zureetha Fargaze, Foreman Thazz'ril, Galgar and Kzan Thornslash (with Forever's Wayward Weapons).
- Razor Hill: Gar'Thok, Orgnil Soulscar and Innkeeper Grosk. Razor Hill's grunts.
- Sen'jin Village: Master Gadrin. Sen'jin's watchers.
