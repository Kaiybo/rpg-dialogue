# RPG Dialogue: Durotar (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.12.0 (2026-11-17)
- Troll and Forsaken priests are noticed: 21 people greet a Horde priest as one, with a question to ask and something to argue about. No new files. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.52.0.

## 1.11.0 (2026-11-17)
- Warriors and hunters are noticed: about 35 new warrior and hunter conversations; people greet a warrior or hunter as one, with a question to ask and something to argue about, and hunters with a beast at their side hear it named. No new files. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.50.0.

## 1.10.0 (2026-11-16)
- 29 people in the Valley of Trials, Razor Hill, Sen'jin and on the roads notice rogues, mages and warlocks (43 new branches), and orcs remember what fel magic cost them.

## 1.9.0 (2026-11-16)
- Horde shaman get their own conversation with 19 people in the Valley of Trials, Razor Hill and along the roads.

## 1.8.0 (2026-11-16)
- Druids are noticed: eight people in Razor Hill, Sen'jin and on the roads (tauren druids) greet a druid as one and have something to ask and argue about.

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-16)
- New people with no quests, who had nothing to say until now: Frang, Ken'jai, Duokna, Uhgar, Thotar, Kaplak, Tai'jin, Un'Thuwa.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-16)
- New people: Misha Tor'kren, Margoz, Swart and Tarshaw Jaggedscar.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Replies for the classes and races these people would really react to (warriors, hunters, rogues, mages, warlocks, druids, shamans, paladins; every race of the faction, and the Skyborne), each leading somewhere.

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Every person here writes a first impression in your diary after you first talk (the journal's People page shows it too).

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.1.1 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: the Valley of Trials, Razor Hill and Sen'jin now talk in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments, each in their own voice (Gornek's drill, Gar'Thok's terse orders, Orgnil's grimness, Thazz'ril's exasperation, Grosk's dry humour, Gadrin's troll lilt). Same lines, same facts, same conditions.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits bring personal lines (habits, worries, running jokes), and some NPCs get night-time lines. Kaltunk's and Galgar's stories from the camps; Thazz'ril, Kzan, Grosk.
