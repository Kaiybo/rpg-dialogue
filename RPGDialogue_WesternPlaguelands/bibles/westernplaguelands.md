## Authored, not canon
- Valorfist counts the camp every morning and night and writes the letters himself; he hears Andorhal's bells at night. MacDonnell labels the sample jars in her own hand. Arbington's quickener threatens his eyebrows, and he washes his hands after reading. Weldon sleeps with his back to the cliff and a sword under his blanket. Bibilfaz tells the gryphons about Ironforge; one gryphon is called Snowtail. Dumah's father reached Stormwind and never stopped talking about the mill (the wiki says only that they fled).
- Derrington prefers winning to honor and finds living allies' appetites reassuring. Vandis prayed once and was answered, once. Dithers's knee forecast is canon; the Society wanting to study it is not. Alexi had the family signet resized. Garush spent years in the internment camps (orcs of his age mostly did; his own past is unknown) and writes to Pureheart.
- Janice wanted to write to Emma (her sister in Stormwind, canon; the letter is authored). Mulgris dreams of Mulgore and felt the cauldrons from Moonglade. Marlene remembers Joseph and Pamela stopping at her house (their relation beyond aunt and niece isn't stated). Kirsta saw the Crusade hang a farmer; the cheese jokes play on her canon slip about Elling. Eva calls Jandice Barov a quiet girl, once.

## Voices
- Valorfist: soldier-commander, brisk, bellows when proud, "Well played" style briskness without copying it, counts everything.
- MacDonnell: priestess with a fighter's relish; gleeful about poisoning the poisoners, warm to her people.
- Arbington: put-upon scholar, nervous, says what he shouldn't, genuinely awed by heroes.
- Weldon: spiteful, frightened, entitled, a nobleman in a mud camp.
- Pureheart: plain, devout, firm; "For the Dawn".
- Dumah: earnest son of a mill-owner, grief kept tidy, a small mean streak for the Crusade.
- Bibilfaz: cheerful gnome, fond of his gryphons, homesick.
- Derrington: cold, practical, contemptuous of politics, a tactician who sets enemies on each other.
- Vandis: dry, faithless shadow priestess, interested in results.
- Dithers: rambling apothecary, morbid humor about his own body.
- Alexi: languid, sneering nobleman, bored by murder.
- Garush: blunt orc veteran who found a cause bigger than the Horde.
- Janice: confused ghost, fragments, calls you John.
- Mulgris: dying druid, slow, gentle, proud of his people.
- Marlene: gentle ghost, one question: where is Pamela?
- Kirsta: wry wounded scout, keeps secrets badly.
- Eva: formal servant's courtesy over terrible memory.

## 0.34.0
Persuade/Intimidate/Deceive checks: what a success reveals (authored, not canon, unless marked).
- Valorfist (Persuade, `need_truth`): privately he believes Stormwind sent him to keep the plague off Southshore, not to win; Andorhal will fall to volunteers, not to the regiment he asked for and never got (authored opinion).
- MacDonnell (Persuade, `priest_fear`): the Light answers her curses on the dead hot and fast and her prayers for the living soft, and she isn't sure what that says about her (authored confidence).
- Weldon (Intimidate, `claim_truth`): nobody has read the deeds since the Scourge came, so he doesn't know whose name is on them; each brother swears Father meant them for him. The question stays open.
- Alexi (Persuade, `hate_truth`): what he can't forgive is that Weldon spared him as one spares a dog while Alexi was still himself inside; the deeds' question stays open (authored feeling, canon spare).
- Kirsta (Persuade, `job_rest`): Stormwind wants to know what the Crusade keeps behind Mardenholde Keep (Hearthglen is run from it, canon); her reason is authored.
- Eva (Persuade, `jandice_truth`): she heard Lord Barov promise things to each son in turn, can't say which was meant to inherit, and thinks neither deserves it (authored; keeps the fortune open).
