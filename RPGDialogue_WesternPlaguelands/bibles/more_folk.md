# Caer Darrow's other ghosts (keys: renfray, rory_darrow, joseph_dirte, baker_masterson)

Wiki pages read 2026-11-18 (warcraft.wiki.gg, via tools/npc.py): Artist Renfray, Rory, Joseph Dirte, Baker Masterson; quest pages Of Love and Family (1) and (2). Neighbours: Eva and Lucien Sarkhoff (wilds.lua, lucien.lua). Rory, Joseph and Masterson are seen only with the Spectral Essence (Kirtonos the Herald) or a priest's Eye of Divinity.

## Sources
- **Artist Renfray** (female human ghost, western Caer Darrow): Of Love and Family (1) turn-in, (2) start. Painted the Fordrings (Tirion, Karandra, Taelan) on Caer Darrow's beach; died of the plague out of Scholomance, not the Scourge. Quest text: "Many of the spirits on this island are cursed to relive their last happy memory... I am one of the few that remembers all"; "Perhaps this thing I tell you shall set me free"; painting hung in her workshop in the Order's barracks; hidden after Tirion's trial under a painting of the twin moons, now in the Scarlet Bastion. Gossip: "Tirion my old friend, is that you? have you come to save us?" Fourth War note not used.
- **Rory** (male human ghost fisherman): gossip: they don't realise they're dead; "You thought the Scourge did this? The Scourge did not yet exist and if they had, we would have at least put up a damned fight"; a plague out of Scholomance; "Now leave me to my sorrows."
- **Joseph Dirte** (male human ghost, Waste Management, at a shed): gossip: "Waste Management Specialist"; "sound enhancing devices attached to my FISTS OF FURY"; "my sweet Brandy, I miss her so!"; "The Barov house sure does keep me busy."
- **Baker Masterson** (male human ghost, bread and drink vendor).

## Their quests
- Renfray, Of Love and Family (turn-in, then gives): loyal to Tirion; protective of the portrait; hopes finishing it frees her.
- Rory, Joseph, Masterson: none: gossip only (Masterson is a vendor).

## Voices
- **Renfray**: gentle, wistful painter; light and colour; "Tirion, my old friend".
- **Rory**: grim, bitter, knows he's dead; dismissive close.
- **Joseph**: cheerful, touchy about his title; denies he's dead.
- **Masterson**: warm, motherly-busy baker ("dear"); doesn't know he's dead.

## Authored, not canon
- Renfray hasn't heard what became of Taelan and doesn't want to.
- Rory watches students ferried to Scholomance.
- Joseph hauls bones and robes from the Barov house; Brandy "went to see her sister".
- Masterson thinks the town is quiet because of a fever in the Barov cellars; flour never runs out.

## What they notice
- Renfray: paladins; the portrait returned. Rory: fishers. Joseph: battered kit. Masterson: hunger.
