# Western Plaguelands, between the towns  (keys: myranda, chromie)

## Sources (warcraft.wiki.gg, Classic sections)
- Myranda_the_Hag, Find_Myranda, Scarlet_Subterfuge, Myranda_the_Hag_(quest): a hag on Sorrow Hill, a former student of the Scarlet Crusade's priests turned to darker arts; for Tirion Fordring's chain she casts an illusion so the player can walk into Hearthglen looking like a Crusader and reach Taelan Fordring.
- Chromie (Classic): a bronze dragon in the guise of a gnome in Andorhal; the Darrowshire chain sends Joseph Redpath's ring and the Annals of Darrowshire to her; she speaks of time and of the battle of Darrowshire.

## Their quests
- Myranda: Find Myranda (turn-in), Myranda the Hag (illusion for Hearthglen).
- Chromie: The Annals of Darrowshire, Brother Carlin; A Strange Historian (Joseph Redpath's ring, turn-in).

## Never
- Chromie's later Caverns of Time role, Wrathgate or anything after Classic; Taelan's death beyond what the chain says.

## Authored, not canon
- Myranda's dislike of being called a witch; Chromie's habit of answering before you ask.
