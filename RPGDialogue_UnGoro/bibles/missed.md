# Un'Goro, missed  (keys: morvek)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg zone roster scan; Mor'vek read with `tools/npc.py` (jungle troll, Ravasaur Trainers / Darkspear, Un'Goro Crater 71.6 73.6; relatives: a wife; his quests Toxic Tolerance, Venomhide Eggs and They Grow Up So Fast are a later addition and are not offered here).
- Existing pack: crater.lua (Torwa Pathfinder, devilsaurs), refuge.lua (Marshal's Refuge).

## Their quests
- None in Classic. He only talks about the ravasaurs.

## Identity / voice
- Mor'vek · Darkspear man · wry, patient, quiet-voiced by necessity; a light lilt ("mon", "dat", "I be"), whole sentences. Neutral to the Alliance down in the crater.

## Authored, not canon
- That he came to see whether a venomhide could be befriended, sits at a distance from one old female, and takes small doses of venom. These echo his later quest chain in outline (tolerance, eggs) but he promises no mount and asks for nothing.
- His wife sitting up with him (the wiki notes only that he has one). Community `ungoro_wilds` is new.

## Never
- Offering the Venomhide Ravasaur, its quests or any reward. Anything after Classic.
