# Un'Goro Crater  (keys: williden, linken, jdcollie, spraggle)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Marshal's_Refuge (neutral camp at the northern edge of Un'Goro; Marshal Expeditions led by Williden and Hol'anyee Marshal underestimated the crater, their base camps were overrun and they retreated to the higher ground of Marshal's Refuge; Classic NPCs Williden, Linken, J.D. Collie, Spraggle Frock, Ringo at Fire Plume Ridge; guards are Gadgetzan Bruisers)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Williden_Marshal (male human, leader of the Marshal Expedition; warm; warned adventurers about dangerous animals, especially King Mosh; Hol'anyee is his sister; scientists and explorers after crystals, rare plants, research and money; adventurers brought supplies and his journal; Expedition Salvation, Williden's Journal, remain in Classic)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Linken (male gnome warrior with lost memory, no interest in gadgets; came from a distant homeland to defeat Blazerunner and recover the Golden Flame; raft wrecked in the marshlands, blow to the head; a half-remembered piece of advice guided him to the Refuge; sword, Donova Snowden, Gaeriyan, Eridan Bluewind, Aquementas; hands the Golden Flame to the adventurer; It's a Secret to Everybody, Linken's Sword, A Gnome's Assistance, Linken's Adventure, It's Dangerous to Go Alone)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/J.D._Collie (female gnome, Marshal Expeditions, neutral; lives in a cave; studies Un'Goro's crystals, curious and persistent through failures; A Gnome's Assistance, Making Sense of It, Linken's Memory, Aquementas, Linken's Adventure)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Spraggle_Frock (female goblin, Marshal Expeditions, neutral; friendly; worried about her friend Ringo who went missing at the volcano; Beware of Pterrordax remains in Classic; Lost!, A Little Help From My Friends)

## Their quests
- Williden: Williden's Journal, Expedition Salvation.
- Linken: It's a Secret to Everybody, Linken's Sword, Linken's Adventure, It's Dangerous to Go Alone.
- J.D. Collie: Making Sense of It, A Gnome's Assistance, Linken's Memory, Aquementas.
- Spraggle: Lost!, A Little Help From My Friends, Beware of Pterrordax.

- Brother Livingston; the Fourth War.

## Voices
- Williden: warm leader, honest about how badly the expedition went.
- Linken: earnest, cheerful amnesiac warrior.
- J.D. Collie: eager crystal scholar, cheerful about failures. She, not he.
- Spraggle: anxious, kind goblin, Ringo on her mind.
