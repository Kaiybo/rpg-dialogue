**Assumptions (confirm in game):**
- Quest titles as the client shows them in Forever: "Seek out SI:7" (the wiki spells it "Seek out SI: 7"; existing line kept), "The Shamed Lieutenant" (no wiki page yet), "Grimand Elmore" (a warrior quest named after the smith), "Southshore" (Milton's quest).
- Several quests share one title across steps (The Missing Diplomat, The Defias Brotherhood, The Tome of Divinity, A Friend of the Family). Lines tied to them stay general enough for any step; Dashel's "no hard feelings" line now also needs his own "never met one" line heard first.
- Forever keeps the Classic Stockade, Lescovar chain and Missing Diplomat (the NPC pages carry Classic sections and Forever loot tables for Thredd, Targorr and Dextren Ward).

**Year 1 notes:** Varian is missing on the road to Theramore (Shaw, Samuelson) and the Missing Diplomat lines never say who the diplomat is; Prestor is trusted by Shaw and receives Baros's report (dramatic irony only); Windsor's fate is unknown to Marcus; nothing of Samuelson's later betrayal, Marcus's death, Renzik's Undermine past or death, Grayson's sister or Thelwater's Cataclysm-era Hogger story is used. Tiffin's death is pre-Year-1 backstory (15 ADP).
