## Identity
King Anduin Wrynn · Stormwind Keep · Alliance · Human · noble
Personality: earnest, curious, kind, trying hard to be kingly, ten years old
Speech: formal phrases he's been taught, then a child's honesty underneath
Knows and cares about: his father, his people's fears, Bolvar, what the grown-ups won't say
Relationships: Bolvar (guardian), Lady Prestor (he does as she says, mostly), Varian (missing father)
Default attitudes: fascinated by everyone from far away
Lore anchors: Year 1. Not used: his later priesthood, Prophet Velen, anything after
Never says: anything grim or political beyond a child's understanding; anything that suggests Prestor is not what she seems
