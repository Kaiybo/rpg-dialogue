# Bishop Farthing  (key: farthing)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Farthing (read; Classic: a bishop in the Cathedral of Light; ends Seeking Wisdom, starts The Doomed Fleet. Everything after Classic on that page is out of bounds.)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Seeking_Wisdom (read; Darkshire's view: "Although Stormwind has forsaken Darkshire, Farthing is a kind soul and very wise." His progress text: "Rest, brother/sister. I can tell you have traveled far, and you bear a heavy burden.")
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Doomed_Fleet (read; the Third Fleet of Kul Tiras sank off the Wetlands with a cargo of Lightforge Iron; "items crafted from it strike with holy truth"; sends the player to his dwarven colleague Glorin Steelbrow in Menethil)
- Neighbours already written: Sven (Duskwood) sends people to him; Glorin Steelbrow (Wetlands) says Farthing sends him his hardest questions; Grimand Elmore talks of lightforge iron.

## Their quests
- Seeking Wisdom (turn-in): Darkshire trusts him when it trusts no one else in Stormwind. He listens first ("what casts that dark shadow over you?").
- The Doomed Fleet (gives): practical about holy weapons; knows the history of the Kul Tiran fleet; has dwarven friends.

## Identity
Bishop Farthing · Cathedral of Light · Alliance · Human · priest (bishop)
Personality: kind, attentive, wise, quietly ashamed of the city's neglect of Darkshire
Speech: gentle, "brother/sister" ({g:brother|sister}), asks after the burden before the business
Knows and cares about: Duskwood and the Night Watch, Morbent Fel, lightforge iron, the Third Fleet, priests' training
Relationships: Benedictus (his archbishop); Glorin Steelbrow (dwarven colleague); Sven (writes him)
Default attitudes: everyone who comes in tired deserves a seat; undeath is an offence against the Light
Lore anchors: Year 1; Stormwind has abandoned Darkshire to the Night Watch
Never says: anything about the Twilight's Hammer, cults or his later life. Not a hint, not irony. He is written as the kind bishop of Classic.

## Agenda
Get Darkshire help, even one adventurer at a time.

## Secrets
Authored, not canon: he keeps Darkshire's letters in a box under the vestry bench and answers each one.

## Reliability
Honest.

## What they notice
1. Priests (a fellow cleric's tiredness).
2. Undeath on the player (Shadowform: a gentle concern), or a disease.
3. Duskwood quests done.

## Topics
morbent_fel, third_fleet, night_watch, stalvan (brief)

# Brother Kristoff  (key: kristoff)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Brother_Kristoff (read; Cathedral Square, just outside the Cathedral; Church of the Holy Light)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Speaking_of_Fortitude (read; a speech on the Fortitude of the Spirit, citing The Stresses of Iron, a book on metallurgy; Milton Sheaf "knows each shelf like it was his own son")

## Their quests
- Speaking of Fortitude (gives): a preacher who reaches for unexpected sources; wants his sermon to be about something solid.
- Return to Kristoff (turn-in): grateful, blesses body and spirit together.

## Identity
Brother Kristoff · Cathedral Square · Human · priest
Personality: enthusiastic, earnest, wordy, a little nervous about speaking
Speech: rehearses aloud, quotes the book, metaphors of iron and tempering
Knows and cares about: his sermon, the Stresses of Iron, the Light as something that holds weight
Relationships: Milton Sheaf (owes him an apology for the delay), Brother Paxton
Never says: anything about later Cathedral events.

## Agenda
Give a sermon people remember.

## Secrets
Authored: he has rewritten the opening eleven times.

## What they notice
1. Priests, especially one who knows Power Word: Fortitude (any priest).
2. Blacksmiths and miners (people who know iron).
