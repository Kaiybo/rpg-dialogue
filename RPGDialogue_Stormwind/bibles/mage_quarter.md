# The Mage Quarter  (keys: maginor, elsharin, larimaine, zardeth, ursula, spackle)

The Wizard's Sanctum upstairs and the Slaughtered Lamb's cellar below. File: npcs/mage_quarter.lua.
All wiki pages read (warcraft.wiki.gg, raw source), November 2026. Jennea Cannon and Gakin the Darkbinder are already written (city.lua); these are their neighbours.

# Maginor Dumas  (key: maginor)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Maginor_Dumas (read; Master Mage, Wizard's Sanctum; "one of Stormwind's most renowned mages"; wrote "Magical Flows and How They Effect the World"; Classic quest: Magecraft. Gossip to non-mages: "As you travel the world, be wary of magic for it will burn the untrained." Everything tied to Varian, Hogger's teleport and Cataclysm quests is later and out of bounds.)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Magecraft (read; Archmage Xylem in Azshara asks for someone of experience; his tower atop the cliffs, reached by a teleport from his arrogant servant Sanath Lim-yo)
- RPGDialogue_Azshara/npcs/xylems_tower.lua (Xylem's side of Magecraft).

## Their quests
- Magecraft (gives, mages ~45+): respects Xylem's power, dislikes his manners; sends his best students to learn where he can't teach.

## Identity
Maginor Dumas · Wizard's Sanctum · Human · trainer (Master Mage)
Personality: dignified, cautious, dry, scholarly, proud of the Sanctum
Speech: lecturing cadence, "be wary", measured warnings, a scholar's precision
Knows and cares about: magical flows, discipline, the Kirin Tor's absence, Xylem, students who burn themselves
Relationships: Jennea Cannon and Elsharin (colleagues), Larimaine Purdue (portals)
Never says: anything about Varian's return, the Cataclysm-era quests.

## What they notice
1. Mages (a student's progress).
2. Priests (the Light as another kind of discipline).
3. Gnomes (Gnomeregan's mages).
