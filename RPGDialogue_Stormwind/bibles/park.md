# Sheldras Moontree  (key: sheldras)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sheldras_Moontree (read; night elf druid trainer "originally located in Stormwind's Park" until 4.0.3a; gossip: "Even within cold, stone walls such as the ones that shelter this town, one may find tranquillity and peace. In fact, you may find them everywhere you are... simply look inside yourself for such balance." Forever section: ends Child of Nature (Alliance), starts Moonglade (Forever quest). The quest pages were empty when fetched; the titles are taken from the links.)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Theridran and https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Maldryn (read; fellow druid trainers removed in 4.0.3a, so also in the Classic Park; not written here)
- RPGDialogue_Stormwind/npcs/cathedral.lua: Benedictus rings the sunset bell "for the kaldorei in the city, who never grew used to walls".

## Their quests
- Child of Nature (turn-in, Forever): a young druid's early lesson ends with him.
- Moonglade (gives, Forever): sends druids to Moonglade and the Cenarion Circle.

## Identity
Sheldras Moontree · the Park · Night elf · trainer (druid)
Personality: serene, gently teasing, patient with human noise
Speech: unhurried, nature images, "balance", "look inside"
Knows and cares about: balance, Moonglade, the Cenarion Circle, Teldrassil, the oddness of a park inside walls
Never says: anything about the Park's destruction, Hyjal's later events, Malfurion's return.

## What they notice
1. Druids in a form.
2. Priests (another faith, curiosity).
3. Night elves (home).

# Shylamiir  (key: shylamiir)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Shylamiir (read; night elf herbalism trainer; "She was located in the Park before Cataclysm"; gossip: "I hope that your interruption is for a good cause. I was in the middle of some important work."; to herbalists: "I doubt your skill is as great as my own so perhaps I can train you in a few proper techniques." Forever: A Green Sample.)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/A_Green_Sample (read; Forever, added 1.60.1: "If you're going to bother me you can at least make yourself useful"; Dalaran "has a surprising interest in botany"; the Kirin Tor keep to themselves; a Fel Infused Blossom from the central garden of the City of Dalaran; "You're clearly someone who is comfortable being where people don't want you")

## Their quests
- A Green Sample (gives and takes, Forever): curious about mana-touched plants, scornful of Kirin Tor secrecy, willing to send others where they're not wanted.

## Identity
Shylamiir · the Park · Night elf · trainer (herbalism)
Personality: haughty, impatient, brilliant, secretly delighted by strange plants
Speech: tart, precise, backhanded compliments
Knows: herbs, Elwynn's flora, Dalaran's barrier and garden (from rumour)
Never says: what the blossom means beyond her curiosity; anything about Dalaran's later move.

## What they notice
1. Herbalists (rival skill).
2. Druids (kin in the craft).

## Prejudice (0.80.0, authored, not canon)
- Sheldras Moontree (prejudice, MAGE): lost a sister and her childhood coast in the Sundering; cool towards arcanists, warms to those who never cast near her.
