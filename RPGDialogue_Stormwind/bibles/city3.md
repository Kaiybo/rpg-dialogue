# Stormwind, part three  (keys: lescovar, dashel, furen, topper, thelwater)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gregor_Lescovar (senior member of the House of Nobles, found in the Royal Library; secretly allied with the Defias; aide Marzon; exposed through the Lescovar Incident ordered by Elling Trias; Classic and Forever only)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Dashel_Stonefist (dwarf rogue brawler in Old Town, nicknamed "Fist" for his fights; the Missing Diplomat chain has you subdue him; Classic only)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Furen_Longbeard (dwarf shieldsmith and armorsmith in the Dwarven District; taught by Grimand Elmore; Classic: Stormpike's Order, The Shieldsmith, Fire Hardened Mail, Furen's Armor)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Topper_McNabb (beggar and former farmer behind the Auction House in the Trade District; comically dramatic pleas; offers to shine armour; blames the scout towers or the orcs who burned his farm)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Warden_Thelwater (runs the Stockade under the canals; caught unprepared by the riots, kept them quiet; anxious, worried about blame and the Captain; Classic: Bazil Thredd, Quell the Uprising, The Stockade Riots, The Curious Visitor)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Crime_and_Punishment_(Classic) (Dextren Ward held in the Stockade by the House of Nobles; Darkshire wants him dead)

## Their quests
- Lescovar: none he gives; the target of the Lescovar Incident (Infiltrating the Castle, The Attack!). He never knows it's coming.
- Dashel: The Missing Diplomat (the parts where he's subdued).
- Furen: Stormpike's Order, The Shieldsmith, Furen's Armor (lines stay general).
- Topper: none: beggar.
- Thelwater: Bazil Thredd, The Stockade Riots, The Curious Visitor, Quell the Uprising.

## Never
- Lescovar never admits anything. His lines are dramatic irony: disdain for the masons, curiosity about Deadmines papers, admiration for Prestor.
- Lescovar's son, or Twilight's Hammer rumours.
- What Thredd's visitor is; Dashel's employer by name.
- Anything Legion or later (Topper as a rat, the Veiled Hand).

## Voices
- Lescovar: smooth, condescending, "How generous of..."; money and the word "no".
- Dashel: cheerful violence, Old Town ale, respect for anyone who hits back.
- Furen: earnest student, proud of a hard master.
- Topper: theatrical beggar, every story negotiable.
- Thelwater: anxious, minimising ("a small disturbance"), wants no reports filed.
