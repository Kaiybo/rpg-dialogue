- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Crime_and_Punishment_(Classic) (Dextren Ward held in the Stockade by the House of Nobles; Darkshire wants him dead)

## Their quests
- Lescovar: none he gives; the target of the Lescovar Incident (Infiltrating the Castle, The Attack!). He never knows it's coming.
- Dashel: The Missing Diplomat (the parts where he's subdued).
- Furen: Stormpike's Order, The Shieldsmith, Furen's Armor (lines stay general).
- Topper: none: beggar.
- Thelwater: Bazil Thredd, The Stockade Riots, The Curious Visitor, Quell the Uprising.

## Never
- Lescovar never admits anything. His lines are dramatic irony: disdain for the masons, curiosity about Deadmines papers, admiration for Prestor.
- Lescovar's son, or Twilight's Hammer rumours.
- What Thredd's visitor is; Dashel's employer by name.
- Anything Legion or later (Topper as a rat, the Veiled Hand).

## Voices
- Lescovar: smooth, condescending, "How generous of..."; money and the word "no".
- Dashel: cheerful violence, Old Town ale, respect for anyone who hits back.
- Furen: earnest student, proud of a hard master.
- Topper: theatrical beggar, every story negotiable.
- Thelwater: anxious, minimising ("a small disturbance"), wants no reports filed.
