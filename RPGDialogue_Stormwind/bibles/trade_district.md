# Trade District  (keys: renato, elaine_trias, mullby, gunther_weller, crier_goodman, dungar, morgan_pestle, harlan)

The shopkeepers and criers a player walks past between the Valley of Heroes and the canals. Classic layout: the central square with the well, the auction house, Weller's Arsenal, Lionheart Armory, Everyday Merchandise, Pestle's Apothecary, Trias' Cheese and the Gryphon Roost. Lines in `npcs/trade_district.lua`.
