# Stormwind quest givers (keys: shoni, argos, mazen)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg: Shoni the Shilent (gnome, Dwarven District, commander of the Gnomeregan underground assault crew; Classic: Speak with Shoni, Underground Assault (the Gnoam Sprecklesprocket from the goblin shredder in the Deadmines, to power the gyrodrillmatic excavationators), Gyrodrillmatic Excavationators (24 Robo-Mechanical Guts from Gnomeregan's bots "like cattle on an open range")).
- Argos Nightwhisper (night elf; in the Park before Cataclysm; Classic: The Corruption Abroad, sending the player to his brother Gershala Nightwhisper in Auberdine: "I have traveled from afar to seek help").
- Mazen Mac'Nadir (human mage, caretaker of the Academy of Arcane Arts and Sciences' library in the Canals; Classic: Mazen's Behest, to his assistant Acolyte Dellis, "quite able in the library but incompetent when he leaves the gates"; completion "It wasn't my fault. Really it wasn't.").
- Consistent with Gnoarn's line about Shoni (Ironforge `tinker_town.lua`) and Collin Mauren (`collin_mauren.lua`).

## Voices
**Shoni (gnome).** Brisk, chatty, tired of jokes about her name; pragmatic about goblin parts.
**Argos (night elf).** Calm, weary, a stranger who has learned to like human bread; quietly afraid for his brother.
**Mazen (human).** Fussy, kindly librarian; despairs of Dellis with affection; guards dangerous books.

## Authored, not canon
- Shoni: the nickname came from a mispronounced joke ("the Silent") after a twelve-hour shift at the Institute; the drill chewed a dwarf's boot; a lead apron by the door; old service shafts under Gnomeregan's eastern wing.
- Argos: Darkshore's corruption described through Gershala's letters (beasts, furbolgs, the land itself); a debate among the Kaldorei between burning and healing; three seasons away from Auberdine.
- Shoni (expansion): drill "Bessie", named for her aunt; one drill ended in the canal ("a new fountain"); crew of six gnomes, two dwarves who lost a bet and a Steamwheedle goblin wirer passed off as a sunburnt dwarf; the High Tinker's blessing, funding and stern look; Overspark wants the front door; she once worked two floors under Thermaplugg; digs from Stormwind for room and borrowed tools; drill bits quenched wrong (oil, not water); new servo arms from the robo-mechanical guts. Gated replies: dwarf, Deadmines cleared, Gnomeregan refugee, rearguard, tinker apprentice, Engineering, after Gyrodrillmatic Excavationators.
- Argos (expansion): kept the moonwell records in Auberdine ("a scribe with a scribe's courage"); Gershala's letters (savage bears, Blackwood furbolgs, moonwells tasting wrong, a letter ending mid-sentence); elders arguing over Teldrassil; heard an old tree fall in Ashenvale; a child touched his ear in the Trade District; a baker who overcharged him then gave a loaf free; means to take the next boat home. Gated replies: Auberdine watch origin, druid, priest, human, after The Corruption Abroad and Researching the Corruption.
- Mazen: a book on binding stolen years ago by a student later found in Duskwood; the third shelf that bites; Collin Mauren never reshelves; Dellis lost the Academy's supplies outside the walls (the quest says only "a horrible mistake").

## Never
No Cataclysm (the Park destroyed, Argos at the harbor). No later Gnomeregan (Operation: Gnomeregan).
