## Authored, not canon
- Dellis: the no-lending rule, the Explorers' League dwarf who argues with the maps.
- Adam took the crab home in his hat; "the cheese man's wife" gives the boys broken bits.
- Aedis came south to help rebuild and stayed; who threw what in the Lordaeron brawl; Christoph carried rubble out of Old Town.
- Carla once fitted robes for the Academy's students. Allan was never much of a caster; woodwrights turn the shafts.
- Andi's favourite statue (the elven ranger at the gate), Geoff's stick, Jimmy's bakery by the fountain and his sad look.
- Gaiman was at the fall of the old city and advises the army; the Champions' Hall is treated as a hall for officers who earned rank in the field (no battleground or rank-system specifics).
- Hammon's lord's son; Quine is Old Town born.
- Borgus tests blades on a block; Magni sent smiths south. Dane's father shod horses; he asked three times to carry coal.
- Brooke came with the first crews from Dun Morogh. Einris was raised on Dun Morogh powder.
- Roman caught a hat; Justin's cousin in the guard; the log with eyes.
- Folsom noticed the tutor's attention and said nothing; he would rather not hear how it ends. The Flintridge daughter is called Tilloa after the Tear of Tilloa.
- Connor was a pot boy under James Hyal; James never shouted.
- Corbett carries padded shirts to Lionheart Armory and keeps the books.
- Erich's shout is his own idea; Steven disapproves.
- Duncan started with one table and a borrowed sign.
- David's role as company for the regulars; Jenn runs the tavern's errands (the wiki only has her walking Old Town); prices up since Westfall went quiet.
- Erika cooked for a company of foot; the paladin who tried to bargain.
- Betsy's wool hair, blue dress and button eye.
- Edna reads the city by what crafters buy.
- Stonebridge takes riders' reports at the gate and asks fighters about the Blackrock orcs.
- Gil's reasoning about Fizzles reading the spell books; Paige looks in dress shops.
- Gregory's father named the shop when it sold only maces.
- Janey wants water breathing, Lisan wants height, Suzanne wants no trouble; third-year student.
- Jorgen's "work never written in any ledger" with Elling and DeLavey stays unnamed; the soft-handed labourers at the gate.
