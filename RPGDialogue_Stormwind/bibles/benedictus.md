# Archbishop Benedictus  (key: benedictus)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Archbishop_Benedictus (leads the Church of the Holy Light; student of Archbishop Alonsus Faol, vowed to continue his work; helped build the Cathedral of Light and trains young priests there; kind, wise, soft-spoken; looks more like a farmer than a churchman; the fall of Lordaeron shook his faith; Classic quests include Cleansing the Eye)

## Their quests
