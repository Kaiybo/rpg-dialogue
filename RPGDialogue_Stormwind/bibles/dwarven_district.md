# The Dwarven District  (keys: therum, kaita, lilliam, gelman)

Smiths, engineers and miners by the Deeprun Tram. File: npcs/dwarven_district.lua.
All wiki pages read (warcraft.wiki.gg, raw source), November 2026. Grimand Elmore and Furen Longbeard (city2/city3) are their neighbours.

# Therum Deepforge  (key: therum)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Therum_Deepforge (read; dwarf expert blacksmithing trainer in the Dwarven District; "the royal armorer of the House of Wrynn". Gossip: "So ye want to be a Blacksmith eh? Grab an apron and a hammer"; "Soot under the nails, steel in yer bones." Classic gossip: trained under Grumnus Steelshaper at the Great Forge (armour); "The blades and hammers ye make surpass even my own... Only person I ever met with skill even greater is Borgus Steelhand." His listed quests are post-Classic and left out.)

## Their quests
- none in Classic: trainer; built from his gossip.

## Identity
Therum Deepforge · Dwarven District · Dwarf · blacksmith (trainer)
Personality: gruff, generous with praise when it's earned, proud of his teachers
Speech: heavy burr ("ye", "yer", "tae", "cannae"), smithing metaphors
Knows and cares about: armour and weapons, his teachers in Ironforge, the King's armour
Relationships: Kaita Deepforge (shares the name; relation unstated on the wiki, so unstated here), Grumnus Steelshaper, Borgus Steelhand
Never says: that Kaita is his kin (unknown).

## What they notice
1. Blacksmiths (soot under the nails).
2. Broken armour.

# Lilliam Sparkspindle  (key: lilliam)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lilliam_Sparkspindle (read; gnome engineering trainer in the Dwarven District; gossip "Let's see... fourteen plus seven, carry the two... oh, yes?"; starts Gnome Engineering and Goblin Engineering (Stormwind City versions, Classic until 4.0.1))
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gnome_Engineering_(Stormwind_City) (read; at 200 skill, expert engineering splits into gnome and goblin disciplines; the manual goes to Tinkmaster Overspark in Ironforge; "membership is permanent")

## Their quests
- Gnome Engineering (gives): partisan for gnomish engineering, honest that the choice is permanent.
- Goblin Engineering (gives): sends people to the goblins without much grace.

## Identity
Lilliam Sparkspindle · Dwarven District · Gnome · trainer (engineering)
Personality: absent-minded, arithmetic-obsessed, kind, quietly grieving Gnomeregan
Speech: sums out loud, trailing off, sudden enthusiasm
Never says: anything about Gnomeregan's later reclamation.

## What they notice
1. Engineers.
2. Gnomes.

## Identity
Gelman Stonehand · Stonehand Mining · Dwarf · trainer (mining)
Personality: grumpy, impatient, secretly conscientious, homesick for Ironforge
Speech: burr, "blasted", short temper in long sentences

Never says: invented family.

## What they notice
