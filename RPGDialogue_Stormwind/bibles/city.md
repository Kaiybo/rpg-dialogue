## Shaw
Cynical, dry, precise, protective of the crown. Knows more than he says, and says so. Lines about Prestor carry dramatic irony only: he trusts her, as canon has it. Never says: anything about Varian's disappearance beyond what Bolvar says; anything later (Cataclysm and after). Notices: stealth (the creaking step), the Deadmines (reports).

## Jennea
Formal, exacting, fond of good students, sharper than Wefhellt (who says so). Notices: Skyborne High Order mages (brief §10 only).

## Gakin
Guarded, transactional, wounded by Surena. Notices: voidwalkers; non-warlocks who look like informants. Never says: what the choker is.

## Allison
Warm, busy, a little gossip from the breakfast tables. Notices: returning guests, drunks. Authored: "the Pig and Whistle first".
