# Nara Meideros  (key: nara_meideros)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Nara_Meideros (read; night elf Priestess of the Moon, Sisterhood of Elune, priest trainer in the Park before the Cataclysm; non-priest gossip "Elune be with you, <class>.")
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Returning_Home_(Nara_Meideros) (read; night elf priest, level 10: a messenger of "our order" came looking for you; go to Priestess Alathea at the Temple of the Moon; completion mentions Tyrande and asks whether Elune still blesses your travels)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Desperate_Prayer_(Park) (read; human priest, level 10: one of your priests came looking for you; go to High Priestess Laurena at the Cathedral; "His demeanor seemed positive"; "Elune be with you, good <race>")
- RPGDialogue_Stormwind/npcs/cathedral.lua: Benedictus's sunset bell for the kaldorei; Laurena and the Archbishop "agree only that both teach mercy".

## Their quests
- Returning Home (gives): she is the Sisterhood's post office in Stormwind; proud of her students, glad when they come home safe.
- Desperate Prayer (gives): she passes the Cathedral's messages too, kindly and without rivalry; the two faiths share students.

## Identity
Nara Meideros · the Park · Night elf · trainer (priest)
Personality: gentle, warm, courteous, quietly homesick, a little wry
Speech: measured kaldorei formality, "Elune be with you", small mercies, moon imagery
Relationships: Laurena (friendly rival over tea), Sheldras (came the same year; complain about pigeons), Shailiea (worries about her), the Grants (fond)
Never says: anything about the Park's destruction, the harbour, or later events.

## Agenda
Keep young priests from going astray, and keep the two faiths on speaking terms.

## Secrets (authored, not canon)
- She has never set foot inside the Cathedral, though she sends students there and has stood at its doors many evenings.

## What they notice
1. Shadowform (unease).
2. Night elves (home).
3. Human priests (Laurena's students).

# Imelda  (key: imelda)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Imelda (read; human, level 25, in the Park "to the north of the well"; nothing else known.)

## Their quests
- none: no quests or gossip; built modestly from her spot by the well and her level.

## Identity (authored, not canon: all of it)
Imelda · north of the Park's well · Human · citizen
A nursemaid to a Trade District wool merchant's two children (a boy who wants the Guard, a girl of six who wants to be a night elf). Daughter of a Second War footman from Old Town who drilled her with the sword (her level); he died four winters ago and she keeps his blade oiled. About thirty, never been past Goldshire, dreams of seeing the sea.
Personality: warm, steady, observant, wistful. Speech: plain, friendly Stormwind, a soldier's daughter's sayings.
Knows: everyone who stops at the well; the Grant sisters' weekly row; the elves' kindness.

## What they notice
1. Warriors (her father's grip).
2. Minor nobles (manners she teaches).

# Kimberly Grant  (key: kimberly_grant)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kimberly_Grant (read; human, the Park before the Cataclysm; sister Kelly; Forever: ends Packaged Pristine Pelts)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kristy_Grant (read; human, level 45, Thalanaar, Feralas, Classic; sister of Kelly and Kimberly per Forever; Forever: Pristine Pesterhide Pelts, starts Packaged Pristine Pelts)
- Packaged Pristine Pelts quest page does not exist on the wiki yet; flow inferred from the NPC pages.

## Their quests
- Packaged Pristine Pelts (turn-in, Forever): Kristy at Thalanaar ships Pesterhide hyena pelts home to her; she is the family's seller and worrier.

## Identity
Kimberly Grant · the Park · Human · questgiver (turn-in)
Personality: brisk, practical, proud, anxious under it. Speech: prices and accounts, quick, dry.
Never says: Lion's Rest, the Cataclysm, Thalanaar flooding.

## Authored, not canon
- Birth order: Kimberly eldest, Kristy middle, Kelly youngest.
- The family is in the fur trade; their late father Thomas Grant was a Trade District furrier (died of a fever two winters ago); the sign still bears his name.
- Pelts packed "in salt and cedar the way Mother taught us"; sold to Trade District tailors, who pass them off to nobles as something exotic.
- Kristy's password: "the ledger balances and Kelly still can't whistle."

## What they notice
1. Skinners. 2. Hunters (tell Kristy to write).

# Kelly Grant  (key: kelly_grant)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kelly_Grant (read; human, the Park before the Cataclysm; sister Kimberly.)

## Their quests
- none on the wiki; she reacts to her sisters' Packaged Pristine Pelts.

## Identity (authored, not canon)
Kelly Grant · the Park · Human · citizen
Youngest sister, restless, dreams of Darnassus and of joining Kristy. Learns Darnassian words from Shailiea (Ishnu-alah, Elune-adore, shan'do), sits quietly while Nara prays, trying to learn Sheldras's bird whistle. Saves coppers in a sock under the floorboard; keeps a list of beautiful places in the back of Kimberly's ledger.
Personality: eager, chatty, warm, a run-on talker. References Kimberly (via heardLine on Kimberly's lines).

## What they notice
1. Night elves. 2. Well-travelled characters (level 40+).

# Shailiea  (key: shailiea)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Shailiea (read; night elf, level 35, "wandered the Park before Cataclysm"; "During the original beta, she was a Tracking trainer.")

## Their quests
- none: built from her wandering and the beta tracking-trainer note.

## Identity (authored, not canon)
Shailiea · the Park paths · Night elf · citizen
A tracker for the Sentinels in Ashenvale (never sworn to them) who came to Stormwind guiding kaldorei envoys and stayed when they left. Walks the Park in circles reading tracks: tells players where they came in, the apprentices' shortcuts, the napping guard. A failed tip to the guards about a cutpurse. Feels guilty for not going back to fight the Warsong loggers. A big cat's track that ends at the canal (she assumes a druid of Sheldras's).
Personality: dry, restless, blunt, secretly fond of Kelly. Speech: short-tempered kaldorei formality, tracks and ground imagery.

## What they notice
1. Beast pets. 2. Stealth. 3. Night elves.
