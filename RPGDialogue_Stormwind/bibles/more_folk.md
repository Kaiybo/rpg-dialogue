## Identity / voice (one line each)
- Hank the Hammer · human armorsmith · gruff, loyal; talks in rivets and promises, reveres Galvan.
- Royal Factor Bathrilor · human census factor · fussy, precise, discreet; "within reason and the law".
- Doc Mixilpixil · gnome physician · manic, cheerful, percentages, addresses the squirrel; never explains SI:7 business.
- Clavicus Knavingham · human quartermaster · earnest civic booster; "friend", ledgers, bandages.
- High Sorcerer Andromath · human mage · grave, quiet, superior; wards, Morganth's fall as a warning.
- Donyal Tovald · human librarian · mild, fussy about shelves, quietly for literacy.
- Garion Wendell · human librarian · eager, bookish, can't bear a lost page.
- Karrina Mekenda · dwarf pet trainer · hearty burr; greets the beast first.
- Thulman Flintcrag · dwarf gun seller · proud, salesman's burr; guns over bows.
- Joachim Brenlow · human bartender · smooth, discreet; mentions Angus Stern.
- Evan Larson · human hatter · cheerful, judges heads; mock rivalry with Wynne.
- Heinrich Stone · human blade merchant · dry ex-soldier; sells to footmen by the barracks.
- Julia Gallina · human wine seller · warm, a little teasing; relation to Suzetta not stated.
- Ol' Beasley · human beggar · wry, observant, no self-pity.

## Authored, not canon
- Hank kept his promise "twenty years"; Galvan's anvil is in his shop.
- Bathrilor's wards built with the Sanctum's help; he won't say how.
- Mixilpixil's squirrel nested in Ol' Emma's thread.
- Clavicus's cloth goes to the Cathedral infirmary, refugee houses and tabards.
- Andromath chose Stormwind over Dalaran; the Sanctum keeps dangerous books sealed.
- Garion's demon-pet guide on the highest shelf.
- Karrina from Dun Morogh; Thulman's grandfather a Bronzebeard rifleman; Ol' Beasley carried stone for the masons on the walls (ties to the Stonemasons, without excusing the Defias).
