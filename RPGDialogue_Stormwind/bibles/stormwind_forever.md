# Abigail Lawson  (key: abigail_lawson)
## Their quests
- Delivering Closure (turn-in; from Demar Brant, after Dying Wish): Ryan's crest. Progress: "only a matter of time before someone came asking about Ryan"; his family killed in the First War; duty warped into hatred; she asked him to live with her parents and her in Stormwind; he swore to stay until he purged his home of orcs; they hadn't spoken since. Completion: Demar a coward; "heroes excel at getting other people killed"; take care of yourself; clutches the crest; "Idiot..."
## Identity
Cathedral Square · human woman · Ryan's betrothed (wiki infobox says husband; the quest says betrothed, used here). Sharp, proud, grieving in advance. She replaced Terry Lawrence (skipped).
## Invented
Sits by the Cathedral most days; mother asks questions; the argument ("he loved his grudge more than me"); crest under her pillow; lights a candle after.
## What they notice
Warriors/paladins 36+ (may have been to Farholde), priests.

# Gilbert Gray  (key: gilbert_gray)
## Their quests
- Fishin' Time (gives/turn-in): title only; written as "an hour or two with a rod". **Uncertain.**
## Identity
A pier in Stormwind Harbor · human man · fisher. Jovial, patient. Wiki: "may be related to Calder Gray and Sergeant Gray" (speculation, not used).
## Invented
Fished here since before the First War; fled north on one of the last boats with Lothar's survivors; came back and fished until the new pier was built; never had a lesson (teases Arnold Leland).

# Idrieth Mossgrove  (key: idrieth)
Stormwind Harbor · night elf man (Darnassus). Wiki lines used: travel to Auberdine; "Our people welcome any Alliance members who wish to trade, travel, or aid others in our lands"; "the boat arrives frequently". Calm, courteous.
## Invented
Born near Ashenvale; studies humans; Darkshore's furbolgs and the Sentinels short of hands.

# Roy Lewells  (key: roy_lewells)
## Their quests
- Reading Room (gives), Shelf Picked (turn-in): titles only. Lines stay vague: "somewhere proper to read", "things are back where they belong". **Uncertain.**
## Identity
The Park · human man. Gentle, bookish, distracted.
## Invented
Wife who calls reading a sickness; clerk by trade; reading a rude history of the Second War; Sheldras's long story; Milton Sheaf won't lend books.

# Aendaril Brookshot  (key: aendaril)
Valley of Heroes · night elf druid (Darnassus). **Gender blank on the wiki: no pronouns anywhere.** Calm, curious; came to see how humans remember their dead.
## Invented
Cenarion Circle member trained in Ashenvale; the druids slept in the Emerald Dream; won't call the Expedition dead without a grave.

# Fyrenz Vishonar  (key: fyrenz)
Stormwind (48.0, 84.6) · night elf woman (Darnassus) · Transmogrifier. Wiki gossip used: "Greetings, <class>. I can show you how to weave magic about yourself to change the appearance of your equipment". Smooth, proud, a weaver's eye.
## Invented
"A thread of magic laid close over the metal"; came because Stormwind has more badly dressed adventurers than Darnassus.
