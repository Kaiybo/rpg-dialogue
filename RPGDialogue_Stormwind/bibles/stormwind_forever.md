# Abigail Lawson  (key: abigail_lawson)
## Their quests
- Delivering Closure (turn-in; from Demar Brant, after Dying Wish): Ryan's crest. Progress: "only a matter of time before someone came asking about Ryan"; his family killed in the First War; duty warped into hatred; she asked him to live with her parents and her in Stormwind; he swore to stay until he purged his home of orcs; they hadn't spoken since. Completion: Demar a coward; "heroes excel at getting other people killed"; take care of yourself; clutches the crest; "Idiot..."
## Identity
Cathedral Square · human woman · Ryan's betrothed (wiki infobox says husband; the quest says betrothed, used here). Sharp, proud, grieving in advance. She replaced Terry Lawrence (skipped).
## Invented
Sits by the Cathedral most days; mother asks questions; the argument ("he loved his grudge more than me"); crest under her pillow; lights a candle after.
- Expansion (Authored, not canon): met Ryan when he shouted in Cathedral Square after petitioning the House of Nobles for soldiers (they gave him a supper, no men); an elder brother, nineteen, in the city watch, lost on the gate when the Horde took Stormwind (mother kept his room ten years); prays on the same pew each morning; won't visit Farholde; after the crest, helps the Matron with the orphans three mornings a week. Gated replies: squire origin, Lordaeron refugee origin, priests, level 36+ (Farholde).
## What they notice
Warriors/paladins 36+ (may have been to Farholde), priests.

# Gilbert Gray  (key: gilbert_gray)
## Their quests
- Fishin' Time (gives/turn-in): title only; written as "an hour or two with a rod". **Uncertain.**
## Identity
A pier in Stormwind Harbor · human man · fisher. Jovial, patient. Wiki: "may be related to Calder Gray and Sergeant Gray" (speculation, not used).
## Invented
Fished here since before the First War; fled north on one of the last boats with Lothar's survivors; came back and fished until the new pier was built; never had a lesson (teases Arnold Leland).
- Expansion (Authored, not canon): a widower (wife died of a fever eight winters ago; she hated fish, made mutton pie); released a sea turtle that towed him down the pier ("the turtle man"); gutted fish for three hundred on the voyage north; Lordaeron refugee camps on the coast, threw a local fisher in the sea; feeds a widow two doors down and an old sailor upstairs (the reason for his job); a running bait argument with Arnold Leland. Gated replies: Fishing, Lordaeron refugee, canal pickpocket, night elf (spear-fishing), dwarf (Loch Modan).

# Idrieth Mossgrove  (key: idrieth)
Stormwind Harbor · night elf man (Darnassus). Wiki lines used: travel to Auberdine; "Our people welcome any Alliance members who wish to trade, travel, or aid others in our lands"; "the boat arrives frequently". Calm, courteous.
## Invented
Born near Ashenvale; studies humans; Darkshore's furbolgs and the Sentinels short of hands.
- Expansion (Authored, not canon): asked for the posting to understand the people who stood with the kaldorei at Hyjal; attended a human funeral where the widow made the room laugh; elders who want the kaldorei kept to the forest; dead sea beasts and carved relics washing up on Darkshore's beaches; the crossing keeps wide of the Maelstrom; an old sentinel who sings to the moon on Auberdine's north tower. Real names used: Gershala and Argos Nightwhisper, Caylais Moonfeather, the Blackwood furbolgs, the Temple of the Moon. Gated replies: Auberdine watch/smuggler origins, night elf, human, dwarf (Explorers' League in Darkshore), hunter, non-kaldorei priest.

# Roy Lewells  (key: roy_lewells)
## Their quests
- Reading Room (gives), Shelf Picked (turn-in): titles only. Lines stay vague: "somewhere proper to read", "things are back where they belong". **Uncertain.**
## Identity
The Park · human man. Gentle, bookish, distracted.
## Invented
Wife who calls reading a sickness; clerk by trade; reading a rude history of the Second War; Sheldras's long story; Milton Sheaf won't lend books.
- Expansion (Authored, not canon): clerk to a grain merchant in the Trade District, done at four bells; his wife reads endings first and reads aloud well; the history's author signs as "a soldier of King Terenas" and loves his horse Buttercup; a romance where the red dragon is the only sensible character; Sheldras's story of an Ashenvale tree grown round a sword; Milton Sheaf let him read twice, standing behind his chair; a Mage Quarter apprentice told him the books bite (echoes Mazen's third shelf); Brother Neals's bad directions. Gated replies: mage/Mage Quarter, Abbey novice, gnome forbidden texts, minor noble, warlock, dwarf, after Shelf Picked.

# Aendaril Brookshot  (key: aendaril)
Valley of Heroes · night elf druid (Darnassus). **Gender blank on the wiki: no pronouns anywhere.** Calm, curious; came to see how humans remember their dead.
## Invented
Cenarion Circle member trained in Ashenvale; the druids slept in the Emerald Dream; won't call the Expedition dead without a grave.
- Expansion (Authored, not canon): learned under Mardant Strongoak long ago; sits silent with Sheldras Moontree in the Park; stopped walking the Trade District in cat form because of the guards; stands longest at Khadgar's statue; feels the city's burned ground "like weather in a healed bone". Statues described: Turalyon, Khadgar, Kurdran, Danath, Alleria. Still no pronouns for Aendaril. Gated replies: human, Wildhammer kin, druid, Shadowglen initiate, dreamer kin, paladin, mage.

# Fyrenz Vishonar  (key: fyrenz)
Stormwind (48.0, 84.6) · night elf woman (Darnassus) · Transmogrifier. Wiki gossip used: "Greetings, <class>. I can show you how to weave magic about yourself to change the appearance of your equipment". Smooth, proud, a weaver's eye.
## Invented
"A thread of magic laid close over the metal"; came because Stormwind has more badly dressed adventurers than Darnassus.
- Expansion (Authored, not canon): glamours only like over like; the craft came down from a teacher whose teacher watched Azshara's court from the servants' stair; she calls it a weaving, not a spell (the Sentinels accept it, the priestesses don't); many Uther looks for paladins; a dwarf whose plate she made look like a blue dress; told a guard she'd turn his tabard pink; a hunter's wolf ate a bolt of silk; noble tailors complain she stole their custom. Gated replies by class (rogue, warrior/paladin, priest, mage, druid, hunter), nearly naked, minor noble, night elf.
