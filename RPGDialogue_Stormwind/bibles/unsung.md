## What they notice
- Bankers: a heavy or empty purse, a rogue's hands, a human (the old bank). Auctioneers: crafters, new sellers.
- Trainers and vendors: their profession and the classes that buy from them (priest light feathers and candles, mage runes, druid seeds, paladin symbols; priests and mages for robes and wands; warriors and paladins for heavy steel; hunters' pets).
