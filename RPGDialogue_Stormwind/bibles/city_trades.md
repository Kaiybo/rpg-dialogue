# Stormwind's skipped trades  (keys: tannysa, shaina, stover, felicia_gump)

## Sources
- From memory of original WoW (1.x): Tannysa, Herbalism Trainer in the Park; Shaina Fuller, First Aid Trainer near the Cathedral; Frederick Stover, Bow & Arrow Merchant in the Trade District; Felicia Gump, the florist (Duskwood's Chef Grual line already sends players to her shop). Exact placements unconfirmed; confirm with capture.

## Their quests
- none: trainers and vendors. They react to class, profession and the city's state in Year 1 (the stonemasons' unpaid wages, the Park's quiet).

## Voices (authored, not canon)
- Tannysa: night elf, patient, quietly amused by human gardens.
- Shaina: brisk, kind; bandages more refugees than soldiers.
- Stover: cheerful salesman of bows, proud of his yew.
- Felicia Gump: chatty florist; flowers for weddings and, lately, for funerals.
