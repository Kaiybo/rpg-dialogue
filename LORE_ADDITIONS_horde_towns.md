# Lore additions: Horde towns in the 30-60 zones (depth rewrite)

Notes for `LORE_NOTES.md`, from the depth rewrite of Grom'gol, Kargath, Stonard, Brackenwall, Camp Mojache, Hammerfall, Bloodvenom Post (with Maybess Riverbreeze) and Ghost Walker Post. Every fact below was read on warcraft.wiki.gg (raw page source, Classic sections and Classic quest pages) during this session. The full per-NPC detail is in each pack's bible, under the new "Depth rewrite" section.

## Sources and facts

### Grom'gol Base Camp (Stranglethorn)
- Grom'gol guards a supply chain to Stonard; it is fortified against jungle trolls, naga and Alliance coming from Booty Bay and Duskwood. Zeppelins to Orgrimmar and the Undercity. (Grom'gol_Base_Camp)
- The Defense of Grom'gol (1, 2): lashtail raptors; Mosh'Ogg ogres in the Mizjah Ruins ambush caravans despite warnings; "we did not intend to wage war on the local tribes".
- Yenniku, Vol'jin's youngest son, was given to the Gurubashi of Zul'Gurub by custom and lost when the Darkspear left with Thrall (Hunt for Yenniku (Classic)). The chain: Bloodscalp tusks; twenty shrunken heads inspected (and a 600-year curse on Gan'zulah's spirit); Gan'zulah's and Nezzliok's heads in the bubbling cauldron; Nezzliok names Zanzil the Outcast; Kin'weelay's hope "tiny as the smallest insect"; Pulsing Blue Shards from the Crystalvein Mine; the Mind's Eye from the ogre mage Mai'Zoth in the Mosh'Ogg mound (Kin'weelay knew him when the Darkspear lived there); the soul gem; Yenniku's soul freed, his body left with Zanzil at the Ruins of Aboraz.
- Grim Message: the Witherbark were once Horde allies who betrayed Thrall; Nimboya's pike at Hiri'watha or Zun'watha.
- Kin'weelay: Bloody Bone Necklaces (Bloodscalp), Split Bone Necklace (Skullsplitter, an old war); the Vile Reef: a troll city sunk off the Savage Coast, murlocs, the tablet of Gri'lek, a shard to enshrine in Orgrimmar; The Troll Witchdoctor brings him Marg Nighteye's head from Dustwallow.
- Mok'thardin's Enchantment (1-4): a ceremonial choker from Shadowmaw claws, a pristine tigress fang, an aged gorilla sinew, jungle stalker feathers and Holy Spring water (claimed by every race; naga explorers).
- Trol'kalar (2): Zengu sends the sword to Nimboya at Grom'gol.

### Kargath (Badlands)
- Named for Kargath Bladefist of the Shattered Hand. The Kargath Expeditionary Force came on Thrall's orders, found Uldaman, sought a route through the Searing Gorge to Blackrock Mountain to strike Rend Blackhand, and clashed with the Dark Irons. Its flight path tied Hammerfall and the Undercity to Grom'gol, Stonard and Booty Bay. (Kargath)
- Warlord Goretooth's first name is Lunk (bonfire scene). His Classic gossip: a warrior bound by duty, envies those at the front, dreams of dying a thousand ways in battle. KILL ON SIGHT: General Angerforge's men massacred the 109th division; "Grandmaster Lexlort, head of the K.E.F. reconnaissance division". Warlord's Command, then Eitrigg's Wisdom ("Rend lives?").
- Theodora: The Rise of the Machines; her enormous monocle; initials G.L.A., rank IV; Lotwil.
- Vivian: the Apothecarium; Essence of the Elements for abominations; Hurley Blackbreath's stolen Thunderbrew recipe; Urheld Darkstone's tablet in Blackrock Spire's former Hall of Fortune.
- Lexlort: Grark Lorkrub, a traitor who gave the Blackrock the K.E.F.'s daily operations; Operative Bijou, a goblin two-timing with the pet dealer Kibler.

### Stonard (Swamp of Sorrows)
- Old Horde outpost in the Black Morass after the Dark Portal first opened, one of the oldest orc settlements; its sister Rockard is now the Dreadmaul ogres' hold; a great hall; wayfinders and cartographers map the uncharted swamp (Classic). (Stonard)
- The Fallen Hero of the Horde's chain: The Disgraced One (Thrall's Grasp, turned in to Ruag), The Missing Orders (Ruag insists he relayed them; murlocs ambushed the carriers; Bengor survived, can hardly speak, can't walk, is fed through a tube), The Swamp Talker (the murloc who took the orders; nineteen of the hero's men lost).
- Fel'zerul: the temple was already sunk when they arrived; green dragons guard it; the Atal'ai exile among the Witherbark; Jammal'an's prophecy that Hakkar's return grants immortality ("foolish trickery to bring about a premature doomsday"); the fetishes.
- Helgrum: Neeka Bloodscar is Kargath's head scout; outrunners between Stonard and Kargath go missing near Loch Modan; Report to Helgrum: some enemies may be shared with the Alliance.

### Brackenwall Village (Dustwallow)
- Krog's Classic gossip: the Warchief ordered the humans left alone; he investigates the burned Shady Rest. Horde clue chain (pre-2.3): hoofprints (quilboar or centaur?; the owner's wife and child died; Kagoro follows the tracks), Paval Reethe's badge (a deserter; Ogron tracks him; Theramore soldiers kill him), the black shield (Do'gol, then Mosarn of Thunder Bluff). Patch 2.3 added Inspecting the Ruins and Return to Krog (Mosarn: the Grimtotem are his best customers).
- Brackenwall is the Stonemaul ogres' village; black dragons destroyed their old village.
- Nazeer: Marg Nighteye, The Lost Report, The Severed Head, The Troll Witchdoctor (Kin'weelay), Marg Speaks (a muckshell; a noble's pendant on a Stormwind soldier), Report to Zor (Zor Lonetree knows Alliance politics).

### Camp Mojache (Feralas)
- Rok Orhan is a female orc warrior; Belgrom Rockmaul sends players to her; the Gordunni are Feralas's dominant force; the High Wilderness ruins; the scroll, the dark ceremony, the orb for Uthel'nay in Orgrimmar's Darkbriar Lodge. Orwin Gizzmick: a goblin who just arrived, Gordunni cobalt buried under a blue glow. Camp Mojache sits over Wildwind Lake with a stream through it, known for thick hides and braves who guard travelers; Greul is a female tauren.

### Hammerfall (Arathi)
- After the Third War the ruins were reoccupied as an unofficial refuge for orcs who felt they had no place in the new world (Hammerfall).
- Drum Fel: Tallow sends players from Tarren Mill; Witherbark and Boulderfist work together; the Boulderfist lords hold the ruins of Stromgarde. The Real Threat (Classic): his wife Korin Fel reads auguries and hides the ogre Or'Kalar's presence from him; Or'Kalar hates Drum for defeating him years ago.
- Zengu: Trol'kalar ("troll slayer"), Lord Ignaeus of Strom (Trollbane), the sigils spread among Stromgarde's survivors (the Syndicate, the militia's eleven pieces of Thoradin's sigil rebound by Tor'gan, Lieutenant Valorcall, Prince Galen).

### Felwood
- Maybess speaks "Taurahe?" to Alliance players; Cleansing Felwood (Horde): blood amber from the Warpwood elementals of the Irontree Cavern, to earn trust; then the Cenarion beacon and plant salve.
- Winna Hazzard (Classic): corrupts her kitten with the Jadefire satyrs' moonwell water at the Ruins of Constellas to use against the Alliance ("Some may say that I am exceptionally cruel").
- Trull Failbane: Winterspring's wildkin guard night elf secrets; berserk owlbeasts by the southern caves; the Blue-Feathered Necklace goes to Nara Wildmane, who says Elune made the wildkin.
- Bloodvenom Post seems tauren-founded and is guarded by tauren braves.

### Ghost Walker Post (Desolace)
- Takata's Classic gossip on the Burning Blade; Alliance Relations (Keldran fears the Alliance will think the Horde made pacts with the Legion; Maurin Bonesplitter handles the Blade's warlocks; Takata hopes to go home "before the seasons change"); Befouled by Satyr (how evil gains a foothold); The Corrupter (Lord Azrethoc and Jugkar Grim'rod; Takata won't leave his post for his own honor).
- Nataka: Family Tree (a year since Cliffwatcher's last letter, unusual centaur activity), Catch of the Day (Bloodbelly fish from Jinar'Zillen at Shadowprey). The Kodo Graveyard is the kodo's resting place, with kodo apparitions among the bones; Desolace's centaur tribes fight each other as much as anyone.

## Assumptions to record
1. **Quests added after Classic 1.12 are written for but not assumed.** Krog's Inspecting the Ruins, Return to Krog, The Grimtotem Plot, Justice Dispensed and Warchief's Command, Nazeer's Theramore Spies, Theodora's The 109th Division and The Grim Guzzler come from patch 2.3 or Cataclysm. Their lines are kept (or added) on `TODO_QUEST` conditions, so they only fire if Forever has those quests. The 109th Division's massacre itself is Classic (the KILL ON SIGHT postings), so Theodora's grief stays.
2. **Same-titled quest steps share one `TODO_QUEST`.** The Defense of Grom'gol, Call to Arms, The Ogres of Feralas, Mok'thardin's Enchantment, Wild Guardians, Guarding Secrets and Alliance Relations each have several steps with one title; lines are written to fit any step (Trull's necklace reply speaks of Nara Wildmane conditionally for that reason).
3. **Korin Fel and Or'Kalar are now used** (The Real Threat is a Classic-only quest), replacing the old bible note that they weren't sourced. Drum Fel never learns of the trap.
4. **Neeka Bloodscar is Kargath's head scout and Helgrum's colleague**, not his mate (that's Cataclysm gossip). Their teasing is authored.
5. **Bengor's speech**: the quest says he can hardly speak; his lines are whole sentences slowed by commas rather than fragments, to satisfy CONTENT_GUIDE §2.
6. **Winna is written as cheerfully ruthless**, as her Classic quests show (the old bible said "harmless"); nothing from her Cataclysm slime experiments.
7. **Fel'zerul names Jammal'an's prophecy and Hakkar** only after Return to Fel'Zerul, as the quest text does, and as a belief; nothing about Hakkar's actual return or Zul'Gurub's raid.
8. **Skyborne replies** (Aggro'gosh, Kin'weelay, Mok'thardin, Rok Orhan) use only BRIEF §10: Windshapers honor the elements and feel a debt to the wind spirits, came to the Horde through the Earthen Ring, Thunder Bluff vouches for them, their spirits went quiet, and their ancestors came from Eldre'Thalas in Feralas. NPCs stay curious or polite and state nothing else about them.
9. **Forever combinations**: orc mages (Aggro'gosh, Mok'thardin, Vivian, Takata), troll warlocks (Kin'weelay, Fel'zerul), a Forsaken paladin (Theodora). Reactions are authored from each NPC's stance (Mok'thardin "asks before taking", Takata distrusts warlocks, Vivian loves rare specimens).
10. **Maybess and an Alliance player**: her wiki quote to the Alliance is "Taurahe?"; the addon keeps her existing line pointing them to Arathandris in Common, and her new reply says she can't hand them the work herself. The other towns are Horde-only, so no Alliance replies there.
11. **Small authored details** (marked in each bible): Rok Orhan eats standing up, Karakul's bowl for late scouts, Thulbek's and Shul'kar's story toll, Adegwa humming, Zengu's patience lesson, Trull's unread reports. No invented family or backstory for any minor NPC; "Their story" answers deflect where the wiki is silent.
12. **Orwin Gizzmick's "on Orgrimmar's business"** (an existing line) isn't on the wiki; his quest rewards Orgrimmar reputation, so it was kept.
