# Kargath: the traders (keys: greth, sranda)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg: Greth <Stable Master> (orc, id 10058), Sranda <Light Armor & Weapons Merchant> (orc female; "Before the Cataclysm, she was in Kargath"). No quests or gossip on record.

## Voices
**Greth.** Gruff, steady; water and shade before anything; the beasts first, the grunts second.
**Sranda.** Brisk and sharp; no haggling, fair prices; sells light gear because plate cooks in the Badlands.

## Authored, not canon
- Greth: a slow spring behind Kargath and water barrels flown in by wind riders; would send the beasts back to Orgrimmar if the spring failed; once tended Frostwolf wolves.
- Sranda: stores leather in a cellar under Shul'kar's inn for a share of profits; sold armor in Orgrimmar's Drag before.

## Never
No New Kargath or Cataclysm Badlands.
