# Badlands  (keys: goretooth, theodora, vivian, lexlort, shulkar, ryedol, lotwil)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kargath (the only Horde outpost in Khaz Modan; the Kargath Expeditionary Force went into the Badlands on Thrall's orders, found Uldaman, and worked toward Blackrock Mountain to strike at Rend Blackhand, bringing conflict with the Dark Irons; leader Warlord Goretooth; Classic NPCs Lexlort, Innkeeper Shul'kar, Hierophant Theodora Mulvadania, Shadowmage Vivian Lagrave; Kargath Grunts guard it; remains in Classic and Forever)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Warlord_Goretooth (boisterous, proud, loyal; a warrior in youth, now commands; frustrated duty keeps him from the fight; dreams of a glorious death in battle; flirts with Theodora and Vivian at the bonfire; Classic quests KILL ON SIGHT: Dark Iron Dwarves, KILL ON SIGHT: High Ranking Dark Iron Officials, Operation: Death to Angerforge, Warlord's Command, Eitrigg's Wisdom)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Theodora_Mulvadania (female Forsaken Hierophant, Kargath Expeditionary Force, Undercity; dramatic and gossipy; teases Goretooth; The 109th Division, The Grim Guzzler, The Rise of the Machines)

- Vivian: The Last Element, Vivian Lagrave, Lost Thunderbrew Recipe, Vivian Lagrave and the Darkstone Tablet, The Darkstone Tablet.
- Lexlort: Grark Lorkrub, Precarious Predicament. Shul'kar: none.
- Ryedol: A Dwarf and His Tools, A Sign of Hope, Prospect of Faith. Lotwil: Study of the Elements: Rock, This Is Going to Be Hard, The Rise of the Machines.

## Voices
- Goretooth: loud, bored, dreams of glorious death; secretly sweet on the two Forsaken.
- Theodora: dramatic gossip, calls people darling.
- Vivian: theatrical, insists she's composed, isn't.
- Lexlort: few words, all of them useful.
- Shul'kar: warm orc innkeeper, wants your war stories.
- Ryedol: weary dwarf prospector, grieving his partner.
- Lotwil: absent-minded gnome, everything is fascinating.
