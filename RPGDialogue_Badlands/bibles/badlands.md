## Depth rewrite: Kargath (Horde towns pass, authored on sourced quests)
Sources read for this pass (warcraft.wiki.gg, Classic sections): Warlord_Goretooth, Theodora_Mulvadania, Vivian_Lagrave, Lexlort, Innkeeper_Shul'kar, Kargath, KILL_ON_SIGHT (both), Operation:_Death_to_Angerforge, Warlord's_Command, Eitrigg's_Wisdom, The_109th_Division, The_Rise_of_the_Machines (1, 2), The_Last_Element, Vivian_Lagrave_(quest), Lost_Thunderbrew_Recipe_(quest), Vivian_Lagrave_and_the_Darkstone_Tablet, The_Darkstone_Tablet, Grark_Lorkrub_(quest), Precarious_Predicament, Operative_Bijou, Bijou's_Reconnaissance_Report_(quest).
- Goretooth's first name is Lunk (Theodora calls him that in the bonfire scene). Classic gossip (paraphrased, never verbatim): a warrior of the Horde, enraged that enemies sit in a fortress less than a day's ride away; envies those at the front; in his youth lived the dreams that make old age bearable, and now feels victory and defeat through his soldiers; dreams of a thousand deaths in battle, each with ten thousand foes falling; "you remind me of myself". KILL ON SIGHT: Angerforge's forces massacred the 109th division of the K.E.F.; "Grandmaster Lexlort", head of the K.E.F. reconnaissance division, found where the officials train "through locked gate, near the heart of the city"; Operation: Death to Angerforge, "the Butcher of Blackrock"; Warlord's Command: Omokk, Voone, Wyrmthalak and important documents, after which Goretooth's face goes pale and Eitrigg's Wisdom follows ("Rend lives?").
- Kargath: named for Kargath Bladefist of the Shattered Hand; the K.E.F. found Uldaman and sought a route through the Searing Gorge to Blackrock Mountain to strike at Rend Blackhand, and clashed with the Dark Irons; its flight path linked Hammerfall and the Undercity with Grom'gol, Stonard and Booty Bay.
- Theodora: The Rise of the Machines: golems first met in the Searing Gorge, imported from the Burning Steppes and perhaps Blackrock Depths; she examines the shards "with an enormous monocle": initials G.L.A. and the rank IV; she sends the player to Lotwil, who goes white.
- Vivian: misses the Apothecarium's amenities; The Last Element: essence that powers Dark Iron golems, for Forsaken abominations, sent by courier to the Undercity ("Keep the change, you filthy beast!"); Lost Thunderbrew Recipe: Hurley Blackbreath stole it, Forsaken applications, "perhaps death has an effect on one's sense of humor"; the Darkstone Tablet: alchemical recipes of the long-dead dwarf Urheld Darkstone, thought insane, in Blackrock Spire's former Hall of Fortune.
- Lexlort: tracked Grark Lorkrub across the Gorge, through the mountain into the Burning Steppes; Grark was caught red-handed giving the Blackrock the K.E.F.'s daily operations and talks of the power of joining the Blackrock legion. Operative Bijou: a goblin, one of the slipperiest operatives, two-timing with the pet dealer Kibler and spending Horde funds; "Never trust a goblin"; her reports turn out invaluable.
- Fixed in this pass: Vivian's Darkstone Tablet line (alchemical recipes, no curse); Goretooth's Warlord's Command line (the Spire's lieutenants and their papers, then Orgrimmar).
- Authored, not canon: Goretooth's reply about the Forsaken ladies, Theodora on a Forsaken paladin, Vivian on an orc mage, Lexlort's runners, Shul'kar's story toll.
