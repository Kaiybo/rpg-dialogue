## Their quests
- Thunderheart: Disharmony of Flame, Disharmony of Fire. Grieves for bound spirits.
- Galamav: Commander Gor'shak, The Pack Mistress. Watcher, exact, sceptical.
- Razal'blade, Gargal, Amakkar: none (service).

## Identity
- Thunderheart: tauren shaman; calm, grim, wise; slow formal speech, "young one", the Earth Mother.
- Galamav the Marksman: tauren hunter; steady, dry; watches Blackrock down a rifle.
- Razal'blade: Darkspear troll scout; wry, fierce; troll cadence ("mon", dropped verbs, still whole sentences).
- Grunt Gargal: big orc shield-grunt; gruff, protective, few words in full sentences.
- Initiate Amakkar: young orc rogue; eager, sharp-tongued, wants into the mountain.
Horde only (Kargath is hostile to the Alliance). Never says: anything after Year 1; Bad Dogs mercenary content.

## Threads he could carry
- Blackrock clan and Rend under a black dragon (Spire); Dark Iron fire-binding; the Firelord.

## Agenda
Watch Blackrock for the Warchief; calm the fire (Thunderheart); bring Gor'shak home (Galamav).

## Secrets
- Amakkar is glad Gargal stands in front of him and won't say so.

## Reliability
Galamav doubts Gor'shak openly; Thunderheart doesn't claim to know if Ragnaros can fall.

## What they notice
- shamans, tauren, Forsaken (Thunderheart); hunters, pets, tauren (Galamav); trolls, rogues (Razal'blade); warriors, slight races (Gargal); rogues, veterans (Amakkar).

## Authored, not canon
- Thunderheart came at the Warchief's request on Cairne's word; keeps sweetroot in a pouch for Mulgore.
- Galamav: an orc nicknamed him "the Marksman" mockingly after he shot a buzzard off his supper; learned from a Ratchet goblin who charged a copper a lesson; a hunter lost a hand to a raised worg pup; his view of Goretooth.
- Razal'blade scouts the ridge at night, counts slaves going in; "mojo goes cold" near the mountain; serves because Vol'jin said go.
- Gargal's shield is Dark Iron steel taken in battle; he was in the internment camps; he shields Amakkar and the shaman.
- Amakkar's father fought at Blackrock in Doomhammer's Horde; initiates carry water.
