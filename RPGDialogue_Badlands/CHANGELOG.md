# RPG Dialogue: Badlands (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.20.0 (2026-11-17)
- People with a stake in a nearby dungeon or raid react once you've cleared it: a greeting, a reply to bring it up, and a conversation about what it means to them, with stances to take. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.58.0.

## 1.19.0 (2026-11-17)
- Every class this place could plausibly meet now gets a greeting and its own conversation, including this server's Forsaken paladins and dwarf shamans where they'd be welcome. Some people here also notice when you've been thinning out the local trouble.

## 1.18.0 (2026-11-17)
- Druid replies for every named person here who had none: a greeting, a question and something to argue about. Night elf and tauren druids are both spoken to where it fits.

## 1.17.0 (2026-11-17)
- Warriors and hunters are noticed: 13 people greet a warrior or hunter as one, and have a question to ask and something to argue about; hunters with a beast at their side hear it named. No new files. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.49.0.

## 1.16.0 (2026-11-16)
- Priests and paladins are noticed: 3 people greet an Alliance priest or paladin as one, and have something to ask and argue about.

## 1.15.0 (2026-11-16)
- Horde priests are noticed: 10 people greet a Forsaken or troll priest as one and have something to ask and argue about. Some also gain lines for Horde mages, warlocks or rogues where they had none.

## 1.14.0 (2026-11-16)
- Rogues, mages and warlocks are noticed: named people here greet them as such, with a question to ask and something to argue about. No new files. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.43.0.

## 1.13.0 (2026-11-16)

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-15)

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-15)

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-15)
- Depth for Kargath: Warlord Goretooth, Hierophant Theodora Mulvadania, Shadowmage Vivian Lagrave, Lexlort and Innkeeper Shul'kar, built from their Classic quests (KILL ON SIGHT and the 109th Division, Operation: Death to Angerforge, Warlord's Command and Eitrigg's Wisdom, The Rise of the Machines and the G.L.A. initials, The Last Element, the Thunderbrew recipe, the Darkstone Tablet, Grark Lorkrub, Operative Bijou).
- Goretooth's dreams of a thousand deaths, his first name (Lunk), why the Expeditionary Force holds Kargath, and who serves under him; Theodora's monocle and gossip; Vivian on abominations and Urheld Darkstone; Lexlort on traitors and goblins; Shul'kar on Kargath Bladefist and the wind rider routes.
- Replies for who you are: warriors, Forsaken (Goretooth teased about the ladies, Vivian on the Apothecarium, Theodora on orc company), a Forsaken paladin (Theodora is delighted), mages and warlocks, orc mages (Vivian), rogues (Lexlort); replies after Angerforge falls, Grark is taken and Bijou's report arrives.
- Fixes: Vivian's Darkstone Tablet line (it holds a dead dwarf alchemist's recipes, not a curse); Goretooth's Warlord's Command line (kill Omokk, Voone and Wyrmthalak and bring their papers).
