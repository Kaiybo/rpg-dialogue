# RPG Dialogue: Thunder Bluff (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.13.0 (2026-11-17)
- Troll and Forsaken priests are noticed: 17 people greet a Horde priest as one, with a question to ask and something to argue about. No new files. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.52.0.

## 1.12.0 (2026-11-17)
- Warriors and hunters are noticed: about 32 new warrior and hunter conversations; people greet a warrior or hunter as one, with a question to ask and something to argue about, and hunters with a beast at their side hear it named. No new files. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.50.0.

## 1.11.0 (2026-11-16)
- Horde rogues, mages and warlocks get their own conversation with 16 people in Thunder Bluff, Cairne and Magatha included.

## 1.10.0 (2026-11-16)

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-15)

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-15)
- Every person here writes a first impression in your diary after you first talk (the journal's People page shows it too).

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-15)
