# RPG Dialogue: Thunder Bluff (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-15)
- Hamuul Runetotem asks after Mulgris Deepriver once you've met him in the Western Plaguelands.

## 1.2.1 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: the High Rise, Elder Rise and the Cat and the Shaman now talk in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments, slow and deliberate the tauren way (Cairne's gravity, Hamuul's wry calm, Turak's patience, Magatha's open ambition, Pala's mothering). Same lines, same facts, same conditions.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)
- Pala remarks on Turak.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits now bring personal lines (habits, worries, opinions of the neighbours), night-time lines, and a question or two about the person themselves, not only their quests.
- Turak Runetotem's story (the stag), a line for Skyborne druids, Mu'sha at night; Pala, Hamuul.

## 1.0.2 (unreleased)
- Cairne knows when you've met Baine in Bloodhoof; Hamuul knows Rau Cliffrunner at Freewind.

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-14)
- Magatha answers when asked about the Grimtotem clan (a new topic from Freewind Post).

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- High Chieftain Cairne Bloodhoof; Arch Druid Hamuul Runetotem and Turak Runetotem on Elder Rise; Magatha Grimtotem, the Elder Crone; Innkeeper Pala. The Bluffwatchers are in the World pack. The first Horde capital with named NPCs.
