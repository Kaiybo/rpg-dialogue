# Hunter Rise trainers  (keys: sark, torm, urek, kary, hesuwa, taim)

## Sources
- In-game roles (vanilla, from memory): Sark and Torm Ragetotem <Warrior Trainer> (with Ker); Urek and Kary Thunderhorn <Hunter Trainer> (with Holt); Hesuwa Thunderhorn <Pet Trainer>.
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Hesuwa_Thunderhorn (read): Hunter Rise pet trainer (male per wiki).
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Taim_Ragetotem (read): <Alterac Valley Battlemaster>, added 1.6.0, Hunter Rise, Frostwolf.

## Their quests
- none: service NPCs.

## Identity and voices
- Sark: loud, blunt; knowing when to stop.
- Torm: quiet, exact; footwork and balance.
- Urek: jovial storyteller, often lost.
- Kary: dry tracker; won't hunt people for coin.
- Hesuwa: gentle, prefers beasts; never lie to a companion.
- Taim: brisk recruiter; debt to Thrall paid in the snow.

## Authored, not canon
- Sark/Torm/Taim as cousins of one Ragetotem family; Urek's three days lost in the Barrens; Hesuwa raised orphaned calves.
- Kary written female; unverified.

## What they notice
- Sark: Forsaken warriors. Torm: orc warriors. Urek, Hesuwa: pets. Kary: troll hunters. Taim: PvP rank.
