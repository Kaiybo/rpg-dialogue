## Secrets
Authored, not canon: her son Tahote hunts for her stall; a Venture Company shredder crushed his leg when he went with the hunters to drive them out of northern Mulgore; the shamans' poultices failed and they spoke of taking the leg; a Forsaken apothecary from the Pools burned the rot out in three days. Kaga told Tahote (feverish, remembers nothing) it was the Earth Mother and never thanked the apothecary. She cures her meat with Steamwheedle salt from Ratchet (her goblin blind spot).
Payoffs: Persuade success (`told_secret`); then the player either urges her to thank the apothecary (`urged_thanks`: next visit she did, and the apothecary found it "interesting work") or tells her to keep it (`kept_secret`: she frets that it makes her a liar).

## Reliability
