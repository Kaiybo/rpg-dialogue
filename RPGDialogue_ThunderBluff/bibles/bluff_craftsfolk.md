# Thunder Bluff: craftsfolk (keys: orm_stonehoof, karn_stonehoof, ahanu, bulrug)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg: Orm Stonehoof (Classic-only: Orm Stonehoof and the Brutal Helm from Thun'grim Firegaze, Chimaeric Horn: slay a chimaera matriarch in the Charred Vale of southwestern Stonetalon, "free her of her misery", bring a Galvanized Horn; Brutal Helm; works "near the pool by the city's great, central totem"). Karn Stonehoof <Blacksmithing Trainer> (roster only). Ahanu <Leather Armor Merchant> at Hewa's Armory at the base of the wind rider tower (Classic: Ride to Thunder Bluff turn-in, Bundle of Hides; Tal the Wind Rider Master: leather goods for Jahan Hawkwing at the Crossroads). Bulrug <Stable Master>, Lower Rise (roster only).
- Consistent with Jahan Hawkwing (Barrens), Tal, Velora Nitely (Undercity), Thun'grim Firegaze (Barrens), Melor Stonehoof.

## Voices
**Orm Stonehoof.** Slow, weighty, proud; only the worthy get a helm.
**Karn Stonehoof.** Patient teacher; likes beginners.
**Ahanu.** Warm, fussy, thankful to every beast; a good stitcher and a poor seller.
**Bulrug.** Unhurried; talks to beasts more than owners; sings to them.

## Authored, not canon
- Orm and Karn are brothers; Melor is their cousin; Orm's family worked by the totem since the bluff was a camp; Orm and Thun'grim friends who disagree on all but iron.
- Karn learned at an orc's anvil in Durotar; thanks the ore before melting it.
- Ahanu: bad knees, buys from hunters; mother tanned hides at Bloodhoof; long partnership with Jahan.
- Bulrug: herded kodo for the Bloodhoof; a cat whose hunter never came back from the Barrens.

## Never
No Cataclysm or Legion Thunder Bluff (no Highmountain feast, no Baine as chieftain in Year 1).
