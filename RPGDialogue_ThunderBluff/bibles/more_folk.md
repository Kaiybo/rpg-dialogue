## Voices
- **Tah Winterhoof.** Old carver, calm and dry; colour as story (red the hunt, white the clouds, black the ancestors' night); keeps his opinions of the Grimtotem in his mouth.
- **Komin Winterhoof.** Gentle, unhurried; thanks every herb aloud; uneasy selling to the Forsaken apothecaries.
- **Brek Stonehoof.** Gruff, stubborn, a little defensive about mining the Earth Mother; family-proud.
- **Mooranta.** Blunt and sparing; "the beast gives itself twice"; nothing wasted.
- **Netali Proudwind.** Cheerful, chatty, passes on only the kind gossip; pragmatic about the Forsaken below.
- **Sunn Ragetotem.** Proud of the Ragetotem's warrior name; shakes rainsticks at customers; leaves the clouds to the Earth Mother.
- **Jyn Stonehoof.** Brisk, sharp; balance before shine; sells what her cousins forge.
- **Fyr Mistrunner.** Warm and slow; "little one"; plain honest bread; teases Aska behind her back.
