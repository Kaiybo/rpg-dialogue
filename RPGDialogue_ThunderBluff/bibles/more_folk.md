## Voices
- **Tah Winterhoof.** Old carver, calm and dry; colour as story (red the hunt, white the clouds, black the ancestors' night); keeps his opinions of the Grimtotem in his mouth.
- **Komin Winterhoof.** Gentle, unhurried; thanks every herb aloud; uneasy selling to the Forsaken apothecaries.
- **Brek Stonehoof.** Gruff, stubborn, a little defensive about mining the Earth Mother; family-proud.
- **Mooranta.** Blunt and sparing; "the beast gives itself twice"; nothing wasted.
- **Netali Proudwind.** Cheerful, chatty, passes on only the kind gossip; pragmatic about the Forsaken below.
- **Sunn Ragetotem.** Proud of the Ragetotem's warrior name; shakes rainsticks at customers; leaves the clouds to the Earth Mother.
- **Jyn Stonehoof.** Brisk, sharp; balance before shine; sells what her cousins forge.
- **Fyr Mistrunner.** Warm and slow; "little one"; plain honest bread; teases Aska behind her back.

## Authored, not canon
Tah's father carved before him; goblin paint runs in the rain. Komin gathered on the plains before teaching; Nida sorts leaves beside him. Brek's family arguing at supper. Mooranta's trade in bones and horn. Netali's candle boxes from Bloodhoof. Sunn's high-grove staves. Jyn as the family's counter. Fyr's mother's stone oven on a kodo.

### Voices
- **Torn.** Young, quick, earnest; knots a cord tally beside the goblins' paper one.
- **Thrumn.** Dry and proud; a tabard is a totem made light enough to wear; enjoys the quarrel with Krumn.
- **Auctioneer Gullem.** Low-voiced, wry; the flute to Stampi's drum; uneasy about prices nobody decides.
- **Shadi Mistrunner.** Brisk, kind; the one Mistrunner who cannot cook; guesses your craft.
- **Chepi.** Calm, watchful; seeds are sleeping lives; keeps the mages' powders apart.
- **Pakwa.** Cheerful, practical; everyone carries too much; a shop is a kodo that stands still.
- **Hewa.** Careful, fond; grumbles at Ahanu's giving and quietly writes it off as spoiled stock.
- **Elki.** Young, quiet, diligent; oils every ring; notices more than he says.
- **Thrag Stonehoof.** Loud, hearty, family-proud; many patient blows; lives for Karn's nod.
- **Taur Stonehoof.** The calm Stonehoof; chose to feed the forge; dry.
- **Kurm Stonehoof.** Blunt, good-humoured; a cheap pick costs more; defends Brek.
- **Nan Mistrunner.** Teasing, generous; swears it is ripe; presses fruit on everyone.
- **Naal Mistrunner.** Easygoing, wry; fetches for Aska and would run twice as far for her pot.
- **Sewa Mistrunner.** Placid, droll; finds adventurers' hurry gently funny.
- **Tand.** Mild, absorbed; never stops weaving; quiet work that lasts.
- **Halpa.** Soft-spoken; finds homes, does not sell; the tribe under the grass.
- **Kard Ragetotem.** Proud, exacting; a blade is a promise; lectures.
- **Delgo Ragetotem.** Hearty, boastful, laughs; the axe does everything; sober only about the centaur.
- **Etu Ragetotem.** Thoughtful, dry; waits, then ends the argument in one sentence.
- **Ohanko.** Immense, slow; not a Ragetotem but one of the hall; settles disputes by lifting something.
- **Sura Wildmane.** Firm, motherly; will not sell a harness unfitted; "young one".
- **Hogor Thunderhoof.** Eager and a little defensive; honour is in why you shoot.
- **Fela.** Frank, sturdy; knocks on your armour; a dead warrior defends nobody.
- **Grod.** Easy, sly; an old scout; the best armour is not being there.
- **Tagain.** Serene, observant; chooses a robe by your walk; buys beginners' work.
- **Tarn.** Gentle, encouraging; praise first, then the truth.
- **Mak.** Practical, talkative; the one who can count, so he got the stores.
- **Vhan.** Talkative, eager; one breathless run-on at a time; loses his row.
- **Mahu.** Even, diplomatic; sells to both tents and takes neither side.
- **Teg Dawnstrider.** Grave, deliberate; enchantment is asked for; unmake nothing idly.
- **Mot Dawnstrider.** Bright, impatient; measures what his father reveres.
- **Nata Dawnstrider.** Shrewd and warm; hides the coin in the ledger; weighs every pouch.
- **Kray.** Careful to the point of worry; labels twice.
- **Mani Winterhoof.** Unflappable; knows the colours of the smoke.
- **Birgitte Cranston.** Forsaken; dry, exact, cultured; unsure what to make of tauren kindness.
- **Kergul Bloodaxe.** Orc; fierce, direct; Warsong pride, respect for tauren.
- **Martin Lindsey.** Forsaken; cold, sardonic; unsettled by being asked whether he is comfortable.
