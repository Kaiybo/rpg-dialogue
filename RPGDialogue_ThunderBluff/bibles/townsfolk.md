# Thunder Bluff townsfolk  (keys: chesmu, tal, ansekhwa, kuna, kah, aska, bena, nida, una_tb)

Thunder Bluff's service people: no quests, little or no gossip in the game. Built from role, place and neighbours (CONTENT_GUIDE §1, "almost nothing to go on").

## Sources
- In-game roles (vanilla 1.12, from memory): Chesmu <Banker>; Tal <Wind Rider Master> on the central mesa's tower; Ansekhwa <Weapon Master>; Kuna Thunderhorn <Bowyer & Fletcher> on Hunter Rise; Kah Mistrunner <Fishing Trainer>; Aska Mistrunner <Cooking Trainer>; Bena Winterhoof <Alchemy Trainer>; Nida Winterhoof <Herbalism Trainer>; Una <Leatherworking Trainer>.
- Game rules used: one vault from every Horde bank; Thunder Bluff's flight paths (Crossroads, Camp Taurajo, Sun Rock Retreat), none to Bloodhoof Village; Minor Healing Potion from Peacebloom and Silverleaf; Mulgore herbs (Peacebloom, Silverleaf, Earthroot) and the Barrens' (Mageroyal, Briarthorn); prairie wolf meat as a first recipe.
- Lore: the tauren were nomads until Cairne built the Bluff against the centaur; Mulgore's Stonebull Lake and the Palemane gnolls; the Winterhoof Water Well and its cleansing (Winterhoof Cleansing, Mulgore); the Forsaken apothecaries on Spirit Rise, admitted when Hamuul spoke for them; the Grimtotem's sympathy for the Forsaken (pack bible).

## Their quests
- none: all nine are service NPCs without quests. Bena reacts to Winterhoof Cleansing (her clan's well).

## Identity and voices
- Chesmu (banker): calm; wealth used to be the herd and the friends who guarded it.
- Tal (wind rider master): steady; the sky shows how small troubles are.
- Ansekhwa (weapon master): steady, honourable; weapons protect the herd; prefers the mace.
- Kuna Thunderhorn (bowyer): warm craft pride; asks a tree before cutting it.
- Kah Mistrunner (fishing trainer): easy, laughing; "Ho, friend".
- Aska Mistrunner (cooking trainer): warm, bossy, "dear".
- Bena Winterhoof (alchemy trainer): sharp; alchemy is for healing; wary of the Forsaken and the Grimtotem, deferential to Cairne.
- Nida Winterhoof (herbalism trainer): gentle; there are no weeds.
- Una (leatherworking trainer): steady; use everything, waste nothing.

## Authored, not canon
- Kah and Aska are brother and sister; Bena and Nida are cousins; Kuna and Holt are "both Thunderhorns" (no closer kinship claimed).
- Tal's story of the orc who kicked a wind rider.
- Kah keeps a club by his pole for the gnolls.

## Never
- Anything after Year 1 (Magatha's coup, Cairne's death, Baine).
- Accusations against Magatha beyond wariness.
- Pronouns for Chesmu or Kuna (gender unconfirmed).

## What they notice
- Tal: mounts on the tower, druids in travel form. Ansekhwa: druids. Kah, Aska, Bena, Nida, Una: skill in their own profession. Bena: Forsaken. Nida: druids in beast shape.
