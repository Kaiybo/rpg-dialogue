# Spirit Rise and Pools of Vision trainers  (keys: tigor, beram, siln, shymm, ursyn, thurston, miles_welsh, malakai, cobb)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Thunder_Bluff (read): the Skychaser tribe kept Spirit Rise before Cataclysm.
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tigor_Skychaser (read): shaman trainer on Spirit Rise (his later fate is post-Year 1: never mention).
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Archmage_Shymm (read): Forsaken mage trainer, Pools of Vision; "magic will burn the untrained".
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Miles_Welsh (read): Forsaken priest trainer, Pools of Vision, Cult of Forgotten Shadows; teaches Touch of Weakness, Devouring Plague, Shadowguard.
- Beram and Siln Skychaser, Ursyn Ghull, Thurston Xane, Malakai Cross, Father Cobb: vanilla roles from memory, not individually verified.

## Their quests
- none: service trainers.

## Identity and voices
- Tigor: stern elder; ask the spirits, never compel.
- Beram: practical totem-carver; tactics and reverence.
- Siln: young, curious; visits the Forsaken below.
- Shymm: dry, superior; power kept from fools.
- Ursyn: careful, homesick for Dalaran.
- Thurston: cheerfully morbid; chose kindness.
- Miles Welsh: calm Forgotten Shadow priest; the Light closed its door.
- Malakai: fervent, Dark Lady loyalist; Forsaken watch their allies.
- Father Cobb: gentle ex-village priest; still prays.

## Authored, not canon
- Skychasers as kin; Siln's visits below; Thurston's jar of flowers; Cobb's northern parish; Malakai openly saying the Forsaken report what they hear.

## Never
- Tigor's or the Skychasers' later fate; anything after Year 1.

## What they notice
- Tigor: orc shaman. Beram: troll shaman. Siln, Malakai: Forsaken. Thurston: tauren. Shymm: troll mages, arcane. Ursyn: Dalaran-apprentice origin. Miles: troll priests. Cobb: Forsaken priests.
