# Thunder Bluff: those who send people onward (keys: xanis, windstrider, ghede)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg (Classic-only sections): Xanis Flameweaver (Spirit Rise; Call of Fire to Kranal Fiss north of the Crossroads, Call of Water to Islen Waterseer by her fishing hut south of Ratchet, Call of Air to Prate Cloudseer in Thousand Needles; quest texts "Long has it been since you were last tested", "your spirit is already warmed by fire"). Bluff Runner Windstrider (town crier; The Ashenvale Hunt to Senani Thunderheart at Splintertree, A Call to Arms: The Plaguelands!, The New Frontier, Camp Mojache; crier gossip "Greetings and salutations, hero!", 1.4.0). Ghede (Elder Rise; Un'Goro Soil (Horde) for the Cenarion Circle; "Oh that's right Private <name>, don't make any effort to help me unload this soil...!").
- Kranal Fiss, Telf Joolam (Durotar), Islen Waterseer, Prate Cloudseer (female) consistent with Barrens/Needles.

## Voices
**Xanis Flameweaver.** Warm, grandfatherly; the four elements' lessons.
**Bluff Runner Windstrider.** Breathless, boastful about speed, carries news without choosing it.
**Ghede.** Drill-sergeant sarcasm, "Private"; secretly fond of the work.

## Authored, not canon
- Xanis: the name from burning half a tent at Bloodhoof showing off with a woven fire; Islen is his oldest friend.
- Windstrider: ran messages for Cairne's war bands on the plains.
- Ghede: drilled braves for Cairne's war bands against the centaur for thirty years; thinks Hamuul wants a better squash.

No Cataclysm-era Spirit Rise changes; the Silithus line stays a rumour.
