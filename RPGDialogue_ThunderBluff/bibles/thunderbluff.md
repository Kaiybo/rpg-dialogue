# Thunder Bluff  (keys: cairne, hamuul, turak, magatha, pala)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Cairne_Bloodhoof (High Chieftain of the united tribes, chieftain of the Bloodhoof, on the High Rise; wise, gentle beneath his strength, would rather live on the plains; formal, invokes the Earth Mother and honor; met Thrall in the Third War when Thrall saved a tauren from centaurs, pledged the tauren to the Horde, led them to Mulgore and built the Bluff because nomadic life left them exposed; keeps Magatha on Elder Rise to keep a rival close; Classic: Journey into Thunder Bluff, Rites of the Earthmother)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Hamuul_Runetotem (Arch Druid, elder on Elder Rise, mentors young druids, leads the tauren of the Cenarion Circle, advises Cairne; asked Malfurion to teach him after the Third War, Malfurion agreed over Fandral's objection; first tauren druid in nearly twenty generations; motto about tempering strength with wisdom (paraphrased, not quoted))
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Turak_Runetotem (druid trainer in the Hall of Elders, Hamuul's assistant; wise, patient; the Great Balance, a clear mind and strong heart; Heeding the Call, Back to Thunder Bluff (the Great Bear Spirit), Moonglade, Body and Heart, Lessons Anew, Power over Poison (after work in the Crossroads), A Lesson to Learn, Aquatic Form)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Magatha_Grimtotem (Elder Crone of the Grimtotem on Elder Rise; cunning, proud, impatient with outsiders; believes she should lead the tauren and argues with Cairne openly; wants the tauren's ancestral lands; her followers expect the lesser races driven out; supports the Forsaken, motives doubted; The Elder Crone, Forsaken Aid, Sacred Fire, Arikara)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Innkeeper_Pala (tauren innkeeper at the Cat and the Shaman on the lower rise; warm welcome)
- Brief §10 (Windshapers joined the Horde through the Earthen Ring, Camp Gev'rek in Mulgore, Friendly with Thunder Bluff)

- Hamuul: none referenced.
- Turak: Heeding the Call, Back to Thunder Bluff, Power over Poison, Aquatic Form.
- Magatha: The Elder Crone, Forsaken Aid, Sacred Fire (lines stay general).
- Pala: none: innkeeper only.

## Voices
- Cairne: formal, warm, old warrior's gravity; the Earth Mother, honor, home.
- Hamuul: calm, wry, grateful to Malfurion; teases Fandral gently.
- Turak: patient teacher of the Great Balance.
- Magatha: cunning, proud, openly ambitious, contemptuous of outsiders; says her rivalry out loud, never anything more.
- Pala: motherly host.
