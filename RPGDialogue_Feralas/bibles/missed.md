## Their quests
- None of their own in Classic. Kylanna, Hahrana, Brannock are trainers; the rest vendors or residents.

## Identity / voice
- Bethan Bluewater · night elf woman · quiet, rooted; keeps the druids' tree-house and watches the sea.
- Brannock · night elf man · calm fishing master; "softly, if you please".
- Savanne · night elf woman · wry, honest about tackle; long-running bait argument with Brannock.
- Dulciea Frostmoon · night elf woman · gracious, proud of her cloth; pities travelling clothes.
- Faralorn · night elf man · orderly quartermaster; tells you what you forgot to pack.
- Kylanna Windwhisper · night elf woman · exacting master alchemist; "touch nothing on the table".
- Logannas · night elf man · mild, careful; wraps glass in moss; content in Kylanna's shadow.
- Mardrack Greenwell · night elf man · warm and merry for a night elf; feeds everyone.
- Marli Wishrunner · night elf woman · bright, forgetful; keeps the camp at the foot of the Colossal.
- Kalin Windflight · night elf man · dry, patient; watches from the top and waits for Marli to remember him.
- Bronk · tauren man · gentle; huge slow hands, never broke a vial.
- Cawind Trueaim · tauren man · steady bowyer who sells rifles with a shrug.
- Hahrana Ironhide · tauren woman · firm, respectful of the hide; judges by hands and seams.
- Worb Strongstitch · tauren man · easy, plain-dealing; pulls every seam.
- Sage Palerunner · tauren man · very old, grave and kindly; remembers the wandering years; uneasy about the elven ruins.
- Orik'ando · Darkspear troll man · easy, sly, watchful; misses the rain of the islands.
- Blaise Montgomery · Forsaken man · dry, cold; offended by greenery and by tauren kindness.
- Griniblix the Spectator · goblin man · loud, eager; everything is odds and overhead.

## Authored, not canon
- Bethan keeps house for Quintis and watches the tides; she came with the Sentinels when the stronghold was raised.
- Brannock and Savanne's bait argument; the nets cut by naga off the northern point.
- Dulciea oils her cloth against the damp; stock by ship from Auberdine and Darnassus.
- Faralorn and Mardrack ration together when the boat is late; Faralorn kept stores in Ashenvale.
- Kylanna came to Feralas for the fresh herbs; Logannas followed her and brews a little in the evenings.
- Marli and Kalin as a two-person watch on the road reporting to Feathermoon; Marli knows "the old way" up and once forgot Kalin overnight (a light nod to the later teleport, nothing promised to the player).
- Bronk's glass comes by kodo from Thunder Bluff; his father was a ploughman.
- Cawind's dislike of rifles; his father and grandfather were bowyers in Mulgore.
- Hahrana learned from her grandmother; Worb's mother stitched tents and gave him the name; Worb sells her students' work.
- Sage Palerunner's uneasy dreams of the ruins are vague on purpose (no amulet, no quest).
- Orik'ando is a hunter who came down from Durotar to see the jungle; Blaise writes reports for the Undercity.
- Griniblix followed the noise to the pit, has watched fights in Booty Bay and Gadgetzan, and keeps a book in his head with nobody to bet against.
