## Their quests

## Identity
- Fyldren: night elf, calm, devoted to the hippogryphs; reports what the birds see of the naga to Shandris.
- Vivianna: night elf, soft-spoken quartermaster of trade goods; boats and birds bring the stock.
- Ruw: tauren, slow and warm ("little one"); take the leaf, leave the root.
- Kulleg Stonehorn: tauren, blunt; waste dishonors the hunt.
- Sheendra Tallgrass: tauren, friendly, practical; kodo caravans from Thunder Bluff through Thousand Needles.
