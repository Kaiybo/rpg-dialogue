## Voices (authored, not canon)
- Xylinnia: measured, precise; enchanting as restraint, the Well's lesson; wards on her door against the naga. Order stance on restraint versus power.
- Harklane: dry fisherman; the Hatecrest tear his nets; Mardrack bakes, he catches. Light stance on the naga (drive them off versus peace).
- Antarius: gentle; beasts hate the crossing; warmth, food and quiet. Order stance on taming.
- Jadenvis: watchful dock-keeper; counts the boats; Gordunni rafts that never land; warns the curious off the old city in the east (Dire Maul, unnamed). Order stance on watching allies.
- Loorana: motherly; flour by kodo through the Needles, acorn bread when it doesn't come; feeds Rok Orhan anyway. Light stance on food as a blessing.
- Tarhus: slow, gathers from the forest; "ask before you take". Light stance on taking from the forest.
- Shyrka: blunt orc among tauren; her clan rode wolves; her old wolf Gnaw chews boots. Honor stance on trust versus fear.
- Jannos: calm druid; the Dream close to the ground in Feralas; tauren and kaldorei roots drink the same water. Order stance on balance versus taming the wild.

## What they notice
- Xylinnia: enchanters, mages. Harklane: fishers, the hungry. Antarius: pets. Jadenvis: druids, the battered. Loorana: hunger, tauren. Tarhus: shamans, druids. Shyrka: pets, wolves. Jannos: shapes, kaldorei druids.

### Their quests
- Troyas: In Search of Knowledge (the history kept by Daryn Lightwind in Darnassus), The Stave of Equinex (four monolith flames in the Ruins of Ravenwind), The Morrow Stone. He explains each step and is afraid of hurrying a relic.
- Runethorn: Elven Legends (find what became of Telmius Dreamseeker in Dire Maul). Proud, exact, will not lose the race to Korolusk and likes him anyway.
- Rockbiter: The Giant Guardian (ends with him), Wandering Shay (find Shay, ring her bell, bring her back). Gentle, slow, devoted.
- The rest: none; written from role, place and neighbours.

### Voices (authored, not canon)
- Troyas: patient scholar; Jewel sleeps on his notes; he and Angelas disagree on whether the stone should go to the Sentinels. Order stance on understanding before use.
- Runethorn: brisk; "call it Eldre'Thalas"; fond of Korolusk's notes. Order stance on sealing the ruins versus opening them.
- Rockbiter: names himself, simple whole sentences; his kin in the south are angry and he does not know why. Light stance on the angry giants.
- Kylanna: exacting; judges the naga war by how fast her shelves empty. Order stance on battle elixirs.
- Brannock: wry; lost a rod to something in the deep channel; Harklane sold him the new one. Order stance on rest versus the wall.
- Mardrack: warm baker; sends a tray to Shandris every evening. Light stance on feeding soldiers well.
- Hahrana: proud, steady; will not work hippogryph hide. Honor stance on respect for the beast.
- Cawind: quiet bowyer who sells rifles and keeps his opinion; one limb a season from the old yew. Order stance on bow versus rifle.
- Bronk: cheerful, fussy; sniffs everything; keeps Uzer'i's roots in a separate basket. Order stance on careful versus strong potions.
- Thyssiana: watches the dust over the Needles; perches turned west. Order stance on holding a small post.

## Kaldorei factions (Runethorn)
Disdains the quel'dorei as the Highborne exiles' children who built a second Well (node `quel`), though she chases one of them; she knows it looks like envy and says so. Wants to question the Shen'dralar of Eldre'Thalas, which the Sentinels would rather burn (node `shendralar`).
