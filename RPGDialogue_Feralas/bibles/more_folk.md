- none: all are vendors, trainers and stable masters, written from role, place and neighbours.

## Never

## Voices (authored, not canon)
- Xylinnia: measured, precise; enchanting as restraint, the Well's lesson; wards on her door against the naga. Order stance on restraint versus power.
- Harklane: dry fisherman; the Hatecrest tear his nets; Mardrack bakes, he catches. Light stance on the naga (drive them off versus peace).
- Antarius: gentle; beasts hate the crossing; warmth, food and quiet. Order stance on taming.
- Jadenvis: watchful dock-keeper; counts the boats; Gordunni rafts that never land; warns the curious off the old city in the east (Dire Maul, unnamed). Order stance on watching allies.
- Loorana: motherly; flour by kodo through the Needles, acorn bread when it doesn't come; feeds Rok Orhan anyway. Light stance on food as a blessing.
- Tarhus: slow, gathers from the forest; "ask before you take". Light stance on taking from the forest.
- Shyrka: blunt orc among tauren; her clan rode wolves; her old wolf Gnaw chews boots. Honor stance on trust versus fear.
- Jannos: calm druid; the Dream close to the ground in Feralas; tauren and kaldorei roots drink the same water. Order stance on balance versus taming the wild.

## What they notice
- Xylinnia: enchanters, mages. Harklane: fishers, the hungry. Antarius: pets. Jadenvis: druids, the battered. Loorana: hunger, tauren. Tarhus: shamans, druids. Shyrka: pets, wolves. Jannos: shapes, kaldorei druids.
