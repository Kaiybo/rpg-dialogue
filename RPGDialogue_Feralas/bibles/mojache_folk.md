## Voices
- **Krueg Skullsplitter** (orc): blunt grunt, follows Belgrom's orders without asking twice.
- **Sage Korolusk** (tauren): mild, obsessive old scholar; calls his stubbornness a pilgrimage.
- **Shyn** (tauren woman): bright, brisk; loves her riders and the rain.

## Authored, not canon
- Krueg's guess that the Grimtotem insulted the Warchief's name and Belgrom took it personally; Hadoken's dislike of his sacks.
- Korolusk travelled with Runethorn until the sea, each sure the other would arrive second; students call him an old kodo who won't stop walking.
- Shyn: riders want to fight the Sentinels' hippogryphs over the coast; they dive into Wildwind Lake for fish.
