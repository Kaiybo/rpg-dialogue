## Their quests
- A Strange Request (gives): a dwarf's shrunken head for Neeru's herbs. Reveals: macabre humour, trades in grisly coin.
- Return to Witch Doctor Uzer'i (ends), Testing the Vessel (wildkin in the Hinterlands, "once pets of Elune"), Hippogryph Muisek, Faerie Dragon Muisek, Treant Muisek, Mountain Giant Muisek: shrink the slain beast before its muisek escapes and seeks revenge. Reveals: method, pride in his ritual, testing the player's ability as much as the vessel.
- Natural Materials, Weapons of Spirit: logs, minerals, sinew, metal; the muisek sealed forever into a weapon. Reveals: craftsman's patience behind the grin.
