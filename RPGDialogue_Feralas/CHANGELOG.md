# RPG Dialogue: Feralas (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.12.0 (2026-11-16)
- Druids are noticed: nine people at Feathermoon, Camp Mojache and in the wilds greet a druid as one and have something to ask and argue about.

## 1.11.0 (2026-11-16)
- New people with no quests, who had nothing to say until now: Fyldren Moonfeather, Vivianna, Ruw, Kulleg Stonehorn, Sheendra Tallgrass.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.10.0 (2026-11-16)
- New people: Gregan Brewspewer, Zorbin Fandazzle, Kindal Moonweaver, Angelas Moonbreeze and the Swiftstriders.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.9.0 (2026-11-15)
- Replies for the classes and races these people would really react to (warriors, hunters, rogues, mages, warlocks, druids, shamans, paladins; every race of the faction, and the Skyborne), each leading somewhere.

## 1.8.0 (2026-11-15)
- Every person here writes a first impression in your diary after you first talk (the journal's People page shows it too).

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Depth for Camp Mojache: Rok Orhan, Orwin Gizzmick and Innkeeper Greul, built from their Classic quests (A Threat in Feralas, The Ogres of Feralas, the Gordunni scroll, ceremony and orb, Gordunni Cobalt).
- Rok Orhan on the Gordunni, the scarred High Wilderness, Uthel'nay and the orb, and fighting beside the tauren; Orwin on buried blue rocks and buyers; Greul on the camp, its braves and the loud orc at the gate.
- Replies for who you are: warriors, tauren, mages and warlocks, Skyborne (whose people came from these hills), miners.

## 1.4.0 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: Shandris, Pratt, Shyria, Rok Orhan, Orwin and Greul now talk in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments, each in their own voice; what they say is unchanged.

## 1.3.0 (unreleased)

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
