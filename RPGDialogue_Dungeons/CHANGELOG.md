# RPG Dialogue: Dungeons changelog

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-27): Word Gets Around
Disposition greetings for shopkeepers, trainers and other service folk; reputation and holiday lines where they fit. No new files in this pack.

## 1.5.1 (2026-11-25): Mending the Seams
- Bug pass: lines and replies that never showed (wrong condition keys or values, unremembered "heard" lines, duplicate line ids, NPCs sharing one key) fixed; stance replies phrased as statements.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-25): Friends, Foes and Fools
- Major characters answer the new friendly topics and grievances in their own voice.

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-25): More to Say
- Thin conversations deepened: more topics, a longer thread, and a reply only some characters get.

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-24): Old Grudges
- Major NPCs answer for a grudge you hold against them (or they against you), and let things slip when angry.
- Overlong speeches tightened.

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-23): The Overlooked
- Characters who had no conversation now have one (core 0.105.0).

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-23)
- 27 friendly dungeon characters deepened (Celebras, Naralex, Nefarius, Andorgos and quest-givers). The three dragons at Ahn'Qiraj are now Brood of Malygos, Ysera and Alexstrasza; Ysida no longer calls Anthion alive.

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-23)
- Hakkar's quest greeting fixed (Signets of the Zandalar).

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-23)
- New pack: dialogue for the people inside every Classic dungeon and raid. New files: needs a full game restart.
