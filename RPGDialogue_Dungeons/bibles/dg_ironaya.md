# Ironaya (Uldaman)
Sources: warcraft.wiki.gg Ironaya; vanilla Uldaman. Stone keeper guarding the vault before the Map Room; awakens when the door is opened ("None may steal the secrets of the Makers!").
Quests: none directly (vault leads toward Archaedas and the Discs).
Voice: cold, formal, mechanical patience; "the Makers"; sees dwarves as earthen gone soft. No later-expansion lore.
