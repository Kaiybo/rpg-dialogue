# Shade of Eranikus (Sunken Temple)
Sources: warcraft.wiki.gg Eranikus; vanilla ST final boss: Ysera's consort set to guard the Atal'ai, corrupted by the Nightmare's shadow. Classic only; no Moonglade event foreshadowing.
Quests: The Essence of Eranikus (from Essence item), Into the Temple / Haze of Evil chain (Itharius). Unconfirmed for Forever.
Voice: vast, sorrowful, proud, fraying; "little one", "mortal"; fights temptation.
