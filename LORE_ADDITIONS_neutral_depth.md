## Assumptions to record

1. Rabine's two Cataclysm-quest lines were moved to his Classic quests (ids kept, conditions changed). If the user would rather keep the ids untouched and silent, revert those two conditions.
2. Sun Rock, Malaka'jin and Freewind are Horde-only: an Alliance character can't speak to them, so they have no Alliance or human-priest replies. Their priest replies reach Forsaken and troll priests. The human priest's content in these packs is at Stonetalon Peak (Albagorm, Faralia), Moonglade (Remulos, Rabine, the Great Bear Spirit, Dendrite) and, as a non-Skyborne visitor, Zephras Isle (Talaanis's "who are the Skyborne" branch).
3. Albagorm and Faralia are Alliance in Classic; the bible called Albagorm neutral. His Horde notice is kept but should rarely fire.
4. "Free At Last" was respelled "Free at Last" (the quest's real title); title matching is normalized, so nothing breaks.
5. Authored, not canon, small touches are listed at the end of each bible's depth-pass section.
