# Spoogledorf (World, `wanderers/spoogledorf.lua`)

Forever NPC 276591. A hunter of the adventuring company <Uber Town> (the name is from the in-game character; the retail cameo is a later expansion and is never referenced). His post, race and trade in Forever weren't readable when this was written (Wowhead blocked the fetch), so no line names a zone, his race or a vendor/quest role. If he turns out to sell things or give quests, add those lines.

## Authored, not canon
- The name is a family name passed down from his grandfather; people mangle it (Spooglewort, Spudgedorf, "Spoogle the Dwarf" on a wanted poster over a stolen pie he paid for anyway).
- Uber Town: six members on paper. A warrior who opens the wrong doors, a rogue who vanishes with the loot (buys bandages), a priest (she) who doesn't heal him after he said her prayer sounded like a cat in a well, a leader with two ledgers (coin and promises). "Uber" is a gnome tinker's word; they mean to buy a whole town and have saved eleven gold.
- Pet Biscuit (she), tamed from a trapper's snare after two days; bites everyone but him. He broke the trapper's snares.
- Best job: finding a farm girl's dog ruling a wolf den, paid in apples.
- Wants to be "in the room when something great happens" and in the songs (no named future event).
