# Spoogledorf (World, `wanderers/spoogledorf.lua`)

Forever NPC 276591. Seen in game (user screenshot, 0.80.1): an old gnome, bald, big grey fanned eyebrows and a long combed beard, red-and-purple robes, friendly, indoors by wooden stairs in Stormwind. No subtitle. The <Uber Town> name is a nod to the retail cameo (a later expansion, never referenced). His trade (vendor/quest) is unknown; no line assumes one.

## Authored, not canon
- Name: a "spoogle" is a tiny watch spring, a "dorf" a workshop; his great-grandfather made Tinker Town's best springs. A Stormwind tax notice calls him "Mister Dorf", whom he disowns. A gate clerk suggested "Spencer".
- Seventy years in Tinker Town (fourth spiral down), kept the books for the spring works; fled with a ledger and a singed beard. Won't talk about the gas.
- Hates Stormwind's tall stairs, carries a stool to the bank, winds clocks at night because the city is too quiet.
- Uber Town: he is founder, treasurer and oldest member. Five young members: a warrior who opens the wrong doors, a rogue who is always elsewhere, a priest (she) who won't heal the hunter, a hunter with a cat called Biscuit who said her prayers sound like a cat in a well. Two ledgers (coin, promises: a farmer's sheep, a widow's roof, an apology to a dwarf in Thelsamar). "Uber" = old Tinker Town word for a thing better than its blueprint; they mean to buy a town, eleven gold saved.
- Wants the last verse of the song: the old gnome who kept the books.
