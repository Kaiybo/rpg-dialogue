# Timbermaw Hold  (keys: gorn_one_eye, meilosh)

## Sources
- From memory, not checked against a wiki this pass: Gorn One Eye and Meilosh are Timbermaw furbolgs inside Timbermaw Hold (Classic); Meilosh sells Timbermaw patterns/plans gated on Timbermaw Hold reputation. The Timbermaw are the one furbolg tribe untouched by the corruption and hold the tunnels between Felwood, Winterspring and Moonglade; Deadwood (Felwood) and Winterfall (Winterspring) are corrupted kin. Grazle and Nafien (this pack) and Salfa (Winterspring pack) are the doors.

## Their quests
- Not written against specific quest titles (unsure which Classic quests Gorn gives); lines gate on Timbermaw Hold reputation and Deadwood kills instead.

## Identity
- Gorn One Eye: male furbolg elder, quest giver. Gruff, wary, patient; speaks of himself in the third person (as Grazle does). Grieves the Deadwood as family.
- Meilosh: furbolg trader (gender unconfirmed; lines avoid pronouns). Warm to friends, firm about reputation; third person too.

## Agenda
- Gorn: keep the hold sane and the road under Timbermaw control; asks outsiders to thin the Deadwood quickly and without enjoying it.
- Meilosh: keep the hold's patterns out of enemy hands.

## Reliability
Honest. Gorn does not know who supplies the Winterfall's drink and says so.

## What they notice
- Gorn: many Deadwood kills, tauren, druids.

## Topics
furbolgs, satyrs, background, advice (Gorn); furbolgs (Meilosh).

## Authored, not canon
- Gorn lost his eye to a Deadwood cousin he had taught to fish, and let him run; he was a fisher, then a fighter; the elders give him the job of talking to strangers.
- Gorn: the Timbermaw take no toll from Everlook's goblins and choose friends over coin.
- Meilosh once saw a Deadwood wearing a Timbermaw-woven cloak taken from a dead trader; the hold buys salt and iron from Everlook with outsiders' coin.

## Their quests
- Gorn: The Root of All Evil (gives/turn-in): the Winterfall are poisoned, not wicked; wants the demon dead, not the puppet chief. The Brokering of Peace (gives): the hold finally answers outsiders who reached out; asks nothing but to be left alone.
- Kernda: Deadwood Ritual Totem, Winterfall Ritual Totem (turn-in): studies the taint in the carvings.
- Meilosh: none; trades with friends of the hold (reputation).

## Identity
- Gorn One Eye · Timbermaw Hold · Timbermaw · furbolg, male · goods + quests. Gruff, steady, protective; plain first-person speech; says "outsider". Grieves the Winterfall like kin.
- Kernda · Timbermaw Hold · furbolg · totem reader. Calm, sharp, devout, curious; addresses the player by {class} (from the gossip). No pronouns used for Kernda (gender unknown).
- Meilosh · Timbermaw Hold · furbolg, male · trader to proven friends. Wary, proud; speaks of himself in the third person (like Grazle).
Default attitudes: wary of all outsiders; warm by reputation; hard on warlocks (Gorn) because a demon poisoned the Winterfall.
Never says: Cataclysm's Grolnar, Blackmaw Hold, anything about later Timbermaw history.

## Threads he could carry
- The Winterfall's poisoning and healing (Winterspring pack: Donova Snowden, Salfa).

## Agenda
Gorn: heal the Winterfall, keep the tunnels clean. Kernda: understand the carvings. Meilosh: keep the old craft for real friends.

## Secrets

## Reliability
Honest; Kernda says when guessing.

## What they notice   (2–3 things)

## Topics
furbolgs, burning_legion, satyrs, background, advice.

## Authored, not canon
- Gorn lost his eye to a Deadwood spear thrown by a former friend (white streak on his arm); "drawing a thorn" image; the Timbermaw ask nothing in return for peace and won't fight in the faction war.
- Gorn's line that "the oldest stories" say night elves and furbolgs shared the woods.
