# Timbermaw Hold  (npcs/timbermaw_hold.lua; keys gorn_one_eye, meilosh)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gorn_One_Eye, https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Root_of_All_Evil (Classic: High Chief Winterfall's cave, demon Xandivious), https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Brokering_of_Peace_(Alliance) / (Horde) (exalted; Magni / Shok Narnes)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Meilosh, https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Runecloth_(quest)

## Their quests
- Gorn One Eye: The Root of All Evil (plant the Demon Summoning Torch, kill Xandivious, bring its essence: the healing of the Winterfall begins); The Brokering of Peace (exalted: carry the Timbermaw Offering of Peace to the player's leaders).
- Meilosh: Runecloth (30 runecloth; a rare night elf friend wore a runecloth cloak and now everyone wants one); trades only with friendly or better.

## Identity
Furbolg voice: third person, plain, whole sentences ("Gorn sees you").
- Gorn: gruff, watchful provisioner, protective of the hold.
- Meilosh: gentle, fussy tailor; slow Timbermaw way of trading.

## Authored, not canon
- Gorn lost his eye to a Deadwood cousin early in the corruption, because he waited too long to strike.
- Some elders want every Winterfall killed; some elders think outsiders' cloth brings outsiders' trouble.
- Meilosh won't name the night elf who wore the cloak.
