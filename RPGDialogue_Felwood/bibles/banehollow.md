# Lord Banehollow  (key: banehollow)

## Sources
- From memory plus this repo's Burning Steppes bibles (gorzeeki.md, morzul.md): Lord Banehollow leads the Shadow Council in Jaedenar, Felwood, from Shadow Hold; the warlock quest "Lord Banehollow" (from Gorzeeki Wildeyes) sends the player to buy Xorothian Stardust from him for the dreadsteed ritual; Gorzeeki's shadowy potions let the player walk through Jaedenar as one of the Council. Shadow Lord Fel'dan and the succubus Rakaiah are in Shadow Hold (Rakaiah per jessir/arkonarin). His race is not stated in the lines (unconfirmed).

## Identity
Courteous, cold, amused; a merchant of damnation. Speaks in long, polished sentences; calls the player "little one", "child of the moon" (night elves). Sees through the disguise and trades anyway.

## Agenda
Keep Felwood's wound open for patient paymasters (the Legion, never named as returning).

## Reliability
Unreliable: claims he let Arko'narin's rescue happen (a boast, not true as far as anyone knows).

## What they notice
paladins, night elves.
