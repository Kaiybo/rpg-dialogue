# Talonbranch Glade keepers  (npcs/talonbranch_folk.lua; keys kaerbrus, malygen, mylini)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kaerbrus (hunter trainer, pet Shi'alune), https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Malygen (general goods), https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Mylini_Frostmoon (weapon merchant, added 1.8.0)

## Their quests
- none: all three are service NPCs (trainer, vendors). Kaerbrus points hunters to the Irontree Ancients (Vartrus).

## Identity
- Kaerbrus: quiet night elf hunter trainer; looks at your pet first; puts corrupted beasts down quickly.
- Malygen: fussy night elf provisioner; sells nothing grown in Felwood; obsessed with clean water.
- Mylini Frostmoon: blunt night elf weapon seller; arms the Sentinels against satyrs.

## Authored, not canon
- Shi'alune found as a cub by a dead moonwell in Ashenvale; taught to smell the sickness.
- Malygen's caravans from Ashenvale, three wagons lost to satyrs; a Sentinel sick for a month after drinking from Bloodvenom Falls on a dare.
