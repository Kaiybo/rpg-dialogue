## Their quests
- Ancient Spirit (gives, escort): still trapped as a spirit and bearing the land's suffering; wants to be led to safety, then sends the player to Kayneth Stillwind at Forest Song. Completion hope: one day Felwood may be reclaimed; every kind deed helps.
- (before) Felbound Ancients / Purified!: Eridan Bluewind purifies the flute with Elune's water, breaking its link to the ancient spirits.

## Identity
Arei · Irontree Woods, Felwood · Alliance (Darnassus) · spirit of an Ancient of War · quest giver
Personality: calm, patient, sorrowful, kind
Speech: slow, old, calls the player "little one"; images of roots, seeds, smoke.
Knows and cares about: Felwood before the Legion, his corrupted kin, the druids' work.
Default attitudes: tender to druids and night elves; tolerates warlocks with sorrow.
Lore anchors: Legion and the skull of Gul'dan corrupted Felwood; spirit trapped there.
Never says: anything from the novel Stormrage or the Emerald Nightmare war.

## Agenda
To be led out and to rest.

## Secrets
- none: a spirit with nothing hidden.

Truthful; old memory, possibly rosy.
