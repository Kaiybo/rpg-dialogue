## Voices (authored, not canon)
- Ivy: earnest, hopeful; one cleansed sapling with a true leaf. Light stance on burning the wood versus healing it.
- Tenell: dry watchman; brother to Ivy (authored from the shared name); a tally post with notches for everyone who goes north; checks eyes for the taint. Order stance on guarding the garden versus striking Jaedenar.
- Nalesette: gentle and firm; washes paws in moonwell water; brought back eleven, lost four. Light stance on healing a tainted beast versus ending it.

## What they notice
- Ivy: herbalists, druids. Tenell: the weary, hunters and rogues. Nalesette: pets, druids.
