# Felwood  (keys: eridan, greta, maybess, arathandris, winna, trull, golhine)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Felwood (once a peaceful forest north of Ashenvale; in the Third War Archimonde had the forest around Hyjal corrupted; the dreadlord Tichondrius used the Skull of Gul'dan; mutated wildlife, corrupted ancients and treants, polluted land; satyrs and infernals; the Shadow Council in Jaedenar under Lord Banehollow; Xavathras leads satyrs from Jadefire Run; Bloodvenom Post the only Horde settlement and Talonbranch Glade the only Alliance one by Year 25; Greta Mosshoof fights satyrs at the Emerald Sanctuary)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Emerald_Sanctuary (Emerald Circle camp, Cenarion affiliation, southeast Felwood; investigate and stop corruption at Jaedenar; Classic NPCs Arathandris Silversky, Maybess Riverbreeze; Eridan, Greta)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Eridan_Bluewind (female night elf druid, Emerald Circle, neutral; hopeful; The Corruption of the Jadefire, Further Corruption, Flute of Xavaric, Felbound Ancients, Purified!; Classic)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Linken (a totem from Eridan Bluewind, made with Aquementas's help)

- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Maybess_Riverbreeze (female tauren, Emerald Circle; earnest; Cleansing Felwood for the Horde, Alliance sent to Arathandris; salve dailies via disenchanting, gathering, hunting, mining, skinning)

## Their quests
- Eridan: The Corruption of the Jadefire, Further Corruption, Flute of Xavaric, Felbound Ancients, Purified!
- Greta: Forces of Jaedenar, Collection of the Corrupt Water, Seeking Spiritual Aid, Cleansed Water Returns to Felwood, Dousing the Flames of Protection, A Final Blow.
- Maybess and Arathandris: Cleansing Felwood and the salves.
- Winna: A Strange One, Well of Corruption, Corrupted Sabers. Trull: Wild Guardians, Guarding Secrets (their stories aren't on the wiki pages, so the lines stay general).
- Golhine: none (trainer).

## Never
- Winna's Cataclysm slime experiments and the Slimeslaves: not a word, not a hint. Her laughing and her kitten are Classic; her curiosity stays harmless.
- Talonbranch's worgen (Denmother Ulrica, James Hallow and the rest are later).
- Kelek Skykeeper and the rest of the Cataclysm sanctuary roster; Ruins of Constellas as Eridan's home (later move).
- Islen Waterseer's location (not given).
- Story details of Wild Guardians and Guarding Secrets, and what A Strange One's "strange one" is.

## Authored, not canon
- Eridan plants a seed at the sanctuary's edge every morning. Greta keeps a Mulgore stone in her pocket. Arathandris watches one clean white flower. Maybess won't live to see the forest clean and doesn't mind. Winna's kitten has no name. Trull doesn't know what Winna's doing either. Golhine's hood keeps the ash off.

## Voices
- Eridan: hopeful, soft, grieving the forest.
- Greta: welcoming but wary; warns about the power.
- Maybess: earnest, practical, kind.
- Arathandris: determined, stubborn as the curse.
- Winna: cackling, delighted by wrongness, harmless on the page.
- Trull: grim, dry Horde intelligence man.
- Golhine: quiet, few words.
