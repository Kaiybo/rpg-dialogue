## Taronn Redfeather (taronn) · tauren male druid, Emerald Circle, Emerald Sanctuary · quest
### Their quests
- Verifying the Corruption (gives/ends): checks Shatter Scar Vale's craters and entropic beasts; hoped for one pure water source, found none, turns to saving the creatures he can.
### Identity
Slow, patient, a little weary; plants for the ones who come after. Speaks of the Earth Mother and Mulgore.
### What they notice
- tauren: another of the People this far north.
- druid: warns them about the Dream in Felwood.

## Kelek Skykeeper (kelek) · night elf male druid, Emerald Circle, Emerald Sanctuary · quest
### Their quests
- Words of the High Chief (ends; from Donova Snowden): takes the Winterfall High Chief's Crudely-Written Log to translate; very little is known about the Winterfall.
### Identity
Dry, patient scholar of furbolgs; prefers them to people; records the Deadwood's old ways for a future cure.
### What they notice
- timbermaw: Timbermaw Hold reputation friendly or better.

## Authored, not canon
- Taronn wears Horde colors on his bracers; Greta and he are the only tauren at the sanctuary; the Horde at Bloodvenom drink from the falls and ignore his warnings.
- Kelek's reading of the log (a "gift", a "voice" teaching the recipe) anticipates the Xandivious reveal without naming it; his forty years among furbolgs.
- "Some in the Circle" want to burn more of Felwood.
