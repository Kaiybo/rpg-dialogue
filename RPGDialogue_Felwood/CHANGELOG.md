# RPG Dialogue: Felwood (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.1.2 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: the Emerald Sanctuary, Bloodvenom Post and Talonbranch Glade now talk in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments. Golhine is still a druid of few words and Winna still cackles, each in a sentence or two rather than a string of scraps.

## 1.1.1 (unreleased)
- Felwood's guards react only to beast shapes, never to a priest's Shadowform.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Depth: Eridan, Greta, Arathandris and Golhine, with their own story and advice.

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- Emerald Sanctuary (both factions): Eridan Bluewind, Greta Mosshoof, Maybess Riverbreeze (Horde), Arathandris Silversky (Alliance).
- Bloodvenom Post (Horde): Winna Hazzard and her kitten, Trull Failbane; the Bloodvenom Post Braves.
- Talonbranch Glade (Alliance): Golhine the Hooded, druid trainer.
