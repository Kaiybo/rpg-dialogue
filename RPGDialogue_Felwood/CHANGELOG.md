# RPG Dialogue: Felwood (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.15.0 (2026-11-17)
- Every class this place could plausibly meet now gets a greeting and its own conversation, including this server's Forsaken paladins and dwarf shamans where they'd be welcome. Some people here also notice when you've been thinning out the local trouble.

## 1.14.0 (2026-11-17)
- Druid replies for every named person here who had none: a greeting, a question and something to argue about. Night elf and tauren druids are both spoken to where it fits.

## 1.13.0 (2026-11-17)
- Warriors and hunters are noticed: 13 people greet a warrior or hunter as one, and have a question to ask and something to argue about; hunters with a beast at their side hear it named. No new files. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.49.0.

## 1.12.0 (2026-11-16)
- Troll and Forsaken priests are greeted as priests by 11 Horde, Timbermaw and Cenarion people in Felwood, who talk about the corruption.

## 1.11.0 (2026-11-16)
- Priests and paladins are noticed: 6 people greet an Alliance priest or paladin as one, and have something to ask and argue about.

## 1.10.0 (2026-11-16)
- Shaman are noticed: 9 people greet a shaman as one and have something to ask and argue about the land's elements.

## 1.9.0 (2026-11-16)
- Rogues, mages and warlocks are noticed: named people here greet them as such, with a question to ask and something to argue about. No new files. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.43.0.

## 1.8.0 (2026-11-16)
- New people with no quests, who had nothing to say until now: Mishellena, Brakkar, Bale.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-16)
- New people: Grazle and Nafien of the Timbermaw, and Niby the Almighty.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-15)
- Replies for the classes and races these people would really react to (warriors, hunters, rogues, mages, warlocks, druids, shamans, paladins; every race of the faction, and the Skyborne), each leading somewhere.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Every person here writes a first impression in your diary after you first talk (the journal's People page shows it too).

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-15)
- Depth for Bloodvenom Post and Maybess Riverbreeze: Winna Hazzard, Trull Failbane and Maybess, built from their Classic quests (Well of Corruption and Corrupted Sabers, Wild Guardians and Guarding Secrets, Cleansing Felwood and the salves).
- Winna on her kitten, the Constellas moonwell and why she'd use the corruption against the Alliance; Trull on Winterspring's wildkin and what they guard; Maybess on the salve, the Cenarion beacon and why she makes you kill elementals first.
- Replies for who you are: Forsaken, hunters and tauren (Winna), druids (Trull), herbalists, an Alliance player at the sanctuary, and players who've met Winna (Maybess).
- Fixes: Maybess's Cleansing Felwood line (the quest asks for blood amber); Winna's moonwell and saber lines (she corrupts her kitten on purpose, she isn't saving sick cubs); Trull's Guarding Secrets line (Winterspring).

## 1.1.2 (unreleased)
