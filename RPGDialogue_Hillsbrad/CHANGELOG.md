# RPG Dialogue: Hillsbrad Foothills (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)
- Depth rewrite: every Southshore and Tarren Mill NPC now has a conversation to explore (a hub with branches two or three deep, "what else?" lines, Ask-about takes on their own story and advice). All nine meet the depth standard.
- Marshal Redpath: the boats from Stormwind, Lothar and Khadgar; the Crushridge ogres in Alterac's ruins, the Syndicate in Durnholde's rubble, Tarren Mill across the valley; the town and who lives in it; what he really thinks of Farren. Lordaeron refugees are told there's room; paladins hear what became of Southshore's old knights; priests hear how the town buries its dead now; after the Warmongers he asks how the old city looks.
- Magistrate Maleb: his murdered predecessor, the Syndicate's nobles and their supply list (stolen Southshore uniforms among it), signet rings, the Argus Wake behind them, and Baron Vardus's contract on his life. Replies for rogues and for minor nobles.
- Lieutenant Farren Orinelle: murlocs, naga and promotion, the City Guard and Major Samuelson, a conscience somewhere; soldiers can tell him to get out of his chair, and anyone fresh from Stormwind gets asked about the canal lamps.
- Brewmeister Bilger: the Silver Hand's end, Foggy MacKreel's moonshine debts; paladins, Silver Hand squires, priests and dwarves each get their own conversation.
- Loremaster Dibbs: the Arm of Gri'lek, the ogres as critics, Prospector Stormpike and the Syndicate's ciphers; mages and Lordaeron refugees get their own replies.
- Innkeeper Anderson: the regulars, Farren's usual, the missing horses.
- Tarren Mill: High Executor Darthalia's orders (the farms, then the smith, then Southshore), the Syndicate and Durnholde, Varimathras; Forsaken, orcs, trolls and tauren each get a reply. Apothecary Lydon's Umpi, the new plague, his misery at the fresh air, his reagents for alchemists; tauren, trolls and Forsaken get their own replies. Innkeeper Shay remembers the old town like a map.
