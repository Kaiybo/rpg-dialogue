# More Hillsbrad folk (keys: hb_kris_legace, hb_mallen_swain, hb_jason_lemieux)

Wiki pages read 2026-11-18 (warcraft.wiki.gg, via tools/npc.py): Kris Legace, Mallen Swain, Jason Lemieux.

## Sources
- **Kris Legace** (female human, Freewheeling Tradeswoman, Durnholde Keep): leather armour vendor, friendly to both Horde and Alliance; hides just behind the ruins of the keep's main building, very near one of the captured orcs. (The post-Cataclysm note about the Syndicate losing the keep is not used.)
- **Mallen Swain** (male Forsaken, Tailoring Supplies, Tarren Mill). Legion-invasion notes not used.
- **Jason Lemieux** (male Forsaken, Mushroom Seller, Tarren Mill; model updated 2.0.1, so present in 1.x). Legion rescue not used.

## Their quests
- none: all three are vendors. Kris's lines touch Krusk's The Rescue (the orcs held in Durnholde) from the side.

## Voices
- **Kris**: low-voiced, quick, amused; a smuggler's ease; "friend"; loyal to her stock and her skin.
- **Mallen**: fussy, dry Forsaken tailor; proud of neat stitches in cloth and in flesh.
- **Jason**: the cheerful Forsaken; gallows jokes to watch the living go pale, then owns up.

## Authored, not canon
- Kris: some Syndicate buy from her behind their captain's back; the Syndicate's best-booted were Alterac nobles; an orc prisoner sings at night.
- Mallen: a tailor on Hillsbrad's green in life; his wife chose the colours; stitches deathguards' limbs; Apothecary Lydon calls it vanity.
- Jason: grows mushrooms in a cellar under the old mill on straw and leaves.

## What they notice
- Kris: battered kit, rogues. Mallen: tailors. Jason: hunger.

## Topics
_none for each.

## Depth pass and missed folk (keys added: hb_daryl_stack, hb_derak_nightfall, hb_aranae, hb_fahrad, hb_bront, hb_aebischer, hb_raycroft, hb_lanyda)

### Sources (warcraft.wiki.gg via tools/npc.py, read 2026-11-18)
- Daryl Stack <Tailoring Trainer>, Derak Nightfall <Cook>, Aranae Venomblood <Herbalism Trainer, in front of the building west of the inn>: Forsaken, Tarren Mill. Legion-invasion notes and Derak's later quest (Stagwiches) not used.
- Fahrad <Grand Master Rogue>, Ravenholdt Manor: Junkboxes Needed ("the stuff Zan makes us is unfit for all but the lowliest neophytes"; leave a few coins in each box). His later-revealed nature (Cataclysm) is not used or hinted.
- Bront Coldcleave <Butcher>, Robert Aebischer <Superior Armorsmith>, Sarah Raycroft <General Goods>, Jaysin Lanyda <Poisons & Reagents>: humans, Southshore (pre-Cataclysm).

### Their quests
- Fahrad: Junkboxes Needed. Everyone else: none (service).

### Voices
**Daryl.** Quiet, exacting; asked to be posted here; knows soldiers by their measurements.
**Derak.** Gruff; "it's stew"; believes nobody who praises it; Krusk complains about salt daily.
**Aranae.** Sharp, patient teacher; picks what Lydon asks for and does not pretend not to know why.
**Fahrad.** Cold, amused; patience over speed; the house serves itself.
**Bront.** Bluff, hearty, "friend"; counts the farms that still send cattle; draughts with Hal McAllister.
**Robert.** Steady craftsman; reads fights from dents; admires dwarven wire.
**Sarah.** Warm, "love"; reads the town from what it buys; sells trunks to those leaving.
**Jaysin.** Sly, genial; "the cupboard they keep the ugly tools in".

### Authored, not canon (this pass)
- Kris: a tanner in the Uplands; the east wall and the kennel gap. Mallen: the shop with green shutters on Hillsbrad's green; violet dye from Lydon's slops. Jason: carted flour from the mill to Southshore in life; Samsa's weekly mushroom.
- Daryl made a banner for the Dark Lady; Derak kept the Tarren Mill inn's kitchen in life; Aranae was a schoolmistress in the valley and chose her name on rising.
- Bront's wife and grandfather; Aebischer's Stormwind guild letter and the dwarven coif; Sarah's sister in Menethil and nine trunks sold; Jaysin and the Syndicate poisoning wells two summers back.

## Ravenholdt's household and the towns' remaining folk (keys added: hb_winstone, hb_myrokos, hb_smudge, hb_zan, hb_kang, hb_carlo, hb_simone, hb_tamara, hb_candarte, hb_hewitt, hb_shara, hb_zandin, hb_coldgaze)

### Sources (warcraft.wiki.gg via tools/npc.py and the Ravenholdt Manor, Southshore, Tarren Mill pages, read 2026-11-18)
- Ravenholdt Manor: Winstone Wolfe <The Wolf>, Myrokos Silentform (elven rogue), Smudge Thunderwood <Poison Supplies> (dwarf, cellar; brother Dirk), Zan Shivsproket <Speciality Engineer> (gnome, cellar; the only seller of the Gnomish Cloaking Device schematic), Master Kang (orc, trains the sentries), Carlo Aurelius <Assassin>, Simone Cantrell <Landscape Architect> (night elf). Their Cataclysm and Legion quests and roles are not used. Lord Jorach Ravenholdt was already written (alterac_roads.lua).
- Southshore (pre-Cataclysm list): Tamara Armstrong (with Hemmit Armstrong).
- Tarren Mill: Craig Hewitt, Hans Zandin, Tara Coldgaze (townsfolk), Shara Blazen (inside the old church; Forever quest "Strahnbrad Mystery", text not on the wiki).
- George Candarte <Leatherworking Supplies>: Forsaken, behind a hill by Thoradin's Wall in the far north-east of the zone.
- Not written: Hemmit Armstrong, Farmer Kent, Apprentice Honeywell, Delia Verana, Vinna Wayne (bystanders with no service or quest); Milton Beats (never met in play); Huraan and the other later additions.

### Their quests
- Shara Blazen: Strahnbrad Mystery (Forever). The wiki has only the title, so her lines assume she wants the Syndicate's digging at Strahnbrad looked into, which matches Gol'dir's lines in alterac_roads.lua. Correct her once the quest text is captured.
- Everyone else: none.

### Voices
**Winstone.** Laconic watchman; boots before faces; the second bend. **Myrokos.** Weighed sentences; a word spoken cannot be recovered. **Smudge.** Jolly dwarf burr, "wee", "{g:lad|lass}"; names his rats. **Zan.** Runs on at speed; locks are "fundamentally sincere". **Kang.** Measured orc; the reed and the thousand cuts. **Carlo.** Urbane; "conclusions"; one blade and good shoes. **Simone.** Night elf: serene, sly; a garden that is a wall; sings to moonpetal.
**Tamara.** Anxious, stubborn; bakes when frightened. **George.** Furtive, genial; "set up at a distance". **Craig.** Plain farmer; black barley. **Shara.** Intense; sweeps an empty church; watches the hills. **Hans.** Bitter, sardonic; nine names and a dog. **Tara.** Still; dust, birds and frogs.

### Authored, not canon (this pass)
- Winstone's brother lost to the Syndicate and the spyglass on the mantel; Myrokos abroad when Quel'Thalas fell, and his friendship with Simone; Smudge's mother who thinks he is a cooper, the chalk line and the rats; Zan's false-set lock and his ventilator; Kang a blademaster's student, a prisoner at Durnholde, found breaking horses at Strahnbrad; Carlo's three rules and the refused contract; Simone's three true paths, the leaning oak and the moonpetal.
- Tamara and Hemmit as husband and wife of thirty years, and the chemist's smell on the north wind (from Apothecary Lydon's Classic quests); George's unpaid debt to Christoph Jeffcoat; Craig's north strip and the ditch he has not dug; Shara's baptism at the font and the wrapped chalice; Hans's tollhouse, his list and the dog Penny; Tara the trapper and the six lost in the rain.
