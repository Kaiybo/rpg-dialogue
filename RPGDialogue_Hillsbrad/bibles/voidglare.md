## Their quests
- Voidglare, Prison Break In (gives): came to Tarren Mill to research, now resolving a crisis; four Forsaken (Kegan among them) stole artifacts from the Dark Lady's vault; fled to Dalaran; quarantined in the Lordamere Internment Camp. Loyal to Sylvanas, irritated by distractions.
- Voidglare, Dalaran Patrols (gives): the dome was for protection, or for solitude "to study and plot"; he cares only that the ruins may hide treasures. Acquisitive.
- Voidglare, Key to the City (Forever, gives): Archimonde flattened the city "like a child's plaything"; rebuilt "on the Dark Lady's very doorstep"; the sewer key ("thankfully I am not the one who will be using this").
- Voidglare, Blood in the Streets (gives): the barrier is down, the city in turmoil; sewers on the northern border.
- Voidglare, Shrewd Negotiations (gives): "My brilliant work still pales in comparison" to the Kirin Tor's wisdom; bargain with the mote. Vain and envious.
- Bel'varil, Stone Tokens (gives): tokens command elementals; the internment camp is safer than Dalaran; inert specimens first.
- Bel'varil, Bracers of Binding (gives): bracers on the elementals' wrists; "death has not only stopped my heart, but robbed me of my comedic timing".
