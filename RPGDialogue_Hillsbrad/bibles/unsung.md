# Hillsbrad: the unsung (0.39.0; keys: lindea, nandar, micha, zixil)

## Sources
- Classic NPC roster of Southshore and the Hillsbrad road as remembered (no quests, no gossip on record): Lindea Rabonne <Fishing Supplies>, Nandar Branson <Alchemy Supplies>, Micha Yance <Trade Supplies> (Southshore); Zixil <Merchant Supplier>, a goblin who walks between Southshore and Tarren Mill with two Overwatch Mark I bodyguards. Wiki pages not re-read this pass.

## Their quests
- none: service NPCs; Lindea reacts before and after Down the Coast (Torn Fin on the Western Strand), as Donald does.

## Identity
- Lindea: Southshore-born, wry, sells fishing gear beside Donald Rabonne; plays along with Donald's joke that nobody knows how they are related.
- Nandar: anxious alchemist, frightened of what Tarren Mill's apothecaries brew; the sickness in Hillsbrad Fields.
- Micha: brisk shopkeeper; carts from Menethil over the Thandol Span; the Syndicate at Durnholde.
- Zixil: goblin peddler, sells to both towns, proud of the Overwatch units.

## Authored, not canon
Lindea's guessing game with Bilger, Kelly and the magistrate; Nandar's training in Lordaeron and the farmer's jar of blood; Micha taking over the shop and Hillsbrad wool going unsold; Zixil buying the robots used. Micha's and Zixil's genders are not stated (pronouns avoided).

## Never
Southshore's later fate, the Forsaken's later plague projects by name, anything after Year 1.

## What they notice
Lindea: fishers, near-drowning. Nandar: alchemists and herbalists, Forsaken. Micha: crafters. Zixil: heavy purses, gnomes.
