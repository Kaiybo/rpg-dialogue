# Hillsbrad and Alterac, between the towns  (keys: ravenholdt, ansirem, goldir, hinott)

## Sources (warcraft.wiki.gg, Classic sections)
- Lord_Jorach_Ravenholdt, Ravenholdt_Manor, Syndicate_Emblems: master of the hidden assassins' house in the Alterac hills; enemy of the Syndicate; collects Syndicate emblems for Ravenholdt reputation.
- Ansirem_Runeweaver (Classic): Kirin Tor archmage outside Dalaran's sealed dome in Alterac.
- Gol'dir_(quest), Krusk: Gol'dir led Krusk's expedition from Durotar, captured by the Syndicate and forced to haul and dig at Strahnbrad.
- Serge_Hinott (Classic): Forsaken alchemy trainer in Tarren Mill.

## Their quests
- Ravenholdt: Syndicate Emblems.
- Ansirem: none: no quest kept in the pack; he watches the dome.
- Gol'dir: rescued in Krusk's Gol'dir.
- Hinott: none: alchemy trainer.
