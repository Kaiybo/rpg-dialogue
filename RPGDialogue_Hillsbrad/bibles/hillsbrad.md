# Hillsbrad Foothills  (keys: redpath, maleb, farren, bilger, dibbs, anderson, darthalia, lydon, shay)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Southshore (quiet Alliance port, one of the few human towns left in Lordaeron; feud with Tarren Mill; the Syndicate at Durnholde and the Forsaken steal horses and shipments; murlocs, naga, Crushridge ogres; roster incl. Robert Aebischer)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Marshal_Redpath (headman of Southshore when Lothar, Khadgar and the Stormwind refugees landed; sent his fastest rider Willem to King Terenas; marshal after the Third War; wants the murlocs, ogres and naga gone; Crushridge Bounty, Crushridge Warmongers)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Magistrate_Henry_Maleb (Southshore's magistrate; elated to get the post young, wonders if he'd have done better in Stormwind; Syndicate Assassins, Foreboding Plans, Dark Council and the rest of the chain)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lieutenant_Farren_Orinelle (resents his transfer from the Stormwind City Guard, hates Southshore, sits in the inn and sends adventurers after the murlocs; Farren's Proof, Stormwind Ho!, Reassignment)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Brewmeister_Bilger (dwarf, once a strict Silver Hand paladin, now a hiccupping brewmeister in the inn cellar; MacKreel's Moonshine)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Loremaster_Dibbs (unimpressed by the Crushridge ogres' damage to Alterac's books; Preserving Knowledge, The Ensorcelled Parchment, Return to Milton)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Innkeeper_Anderson (human innkeeper; personality authored)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/High_Executor_Darthalia (Forsaken warrior leading the war on the humans from Tarren Mill under Sylvanas and Varimathras; single-minded; Time to Strike, WANTED: Syndicate Personnel, Battle of Hillsbrad)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Apothecary_Lydon (Forsaken apothecary in Tarren Mill; depressed and bored; devoted to the Dark Lady; finds joy only in the suffering of the living; his frog Umpi; works with Master Apothecary Faranell; Elixir of Pain, Elixir of Suffering, Elixir of Agony)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Innkeeper_Shay (Forsaken innkeeper; personality authored)

- Farren: Farren's Proof, Stormwind Ho!, Reassignment.
- Bilger: MacKreel's Moonshine.
- Dibbs: Preserving Knowledge, The Ensorcelled Parchment.
- Anderson, Shay: none: innkeepers only.
- Darthalia: Time to Strike, WANTED: Syndicate Personnel, Battle of Hillsbrad.
- Lydon: Elixir of Pain, Elixir of Suffering, Elixir of Agony.

## Voices
- Redpath: steady old marshal; remembers Lothar and the boats.
- Maleb: weary, decent magistrate buried in Syndicate papers.
- Farren: sulky, comic, homesick for Stormwind; has a conscience somewhere.
- Bilger: hiccupping fallen paladin, rueful, kind underneath.
- Dibbs: dry scholar; ogres as critics.
- Anderson: practical innkeeper; fish stew; kind to Bilger.
- Darthalia: cold Forsaken commander; arithmetic of war.
- Lydon: glum poisoner; Umpi the frog.
- Shay: deadpan Forsaken innkeeper.
