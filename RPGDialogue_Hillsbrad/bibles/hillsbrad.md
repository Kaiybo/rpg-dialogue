## Voices
- Redpath: steady old marshal; remembers Lothar and the boats; lets Farren think he doesn't know.
- Maleb: weary, decent magistrate buried in Syndicate papers; bolts his shutters; "small justice is still justice".
- Farren: sulky, comic, homesick for Stormwind; has a conscience somewhere; writes to Samuelson often and politely.
- Bilger: hiccupping fallen paladin, rueful, kind underneath; Foggy pays in moonshine.
- Dibbs: dry scholar; ogres as critics; sends Stormpike the hard ciphers.
- Anderson: practical innkeeper; fish stew; kind to Bilger; knows Farren's usual.
- Darthalia: cold Forsaken commander; arithmetic of war; "food, iron, nerve".
- Lydon: glum poisoner; Umpi the frog (she); hates fresh air.
- Shay: deadpan Forsaken innkeeper.

## Depth rewrite (Hillsbrad 1.2.0, authored, not canon)
- Redpath: keeps a lantern by the stables at night; Southshore watches its new graves "a few nights, to be sure"; lets Farren think he doesn't know who does his murloc work; Khadgar "had an old man's eyes even then".
- Maleb: bolts the shutters, checks the guard, reads one more report; a fisherman's thanks over a net keeps him in Southshore.
- Farren: asks whether the canal lamps still come on at dusk; would write the town "a long letter with apologies".
- Bilger: "barley, hops and forgetting"; Foggy's moonshine "strips paint off a church door"; misses the mornings of the old Hand; plate taken in for dwarves.
- Dibbs: the useful part of history is the part that embarrassed people; asks a Lordaeron refugee whether anyone carried books out.
- Anderson: a bed for northern refugees, pay when you can; can't think what the dead want with horses.
- Darthalia: Syndicate executions "save posting guards"; her reports to the Undercity "have grown pleasantly short"; answers a tauren's scruples coldly.
- Lydon: remembers liking things but not what it felt like; "frogs complain less, and they come back".
- Shay: remembers the old town "the way you remember a map"; keeps the cellar locked for the living.
- Corrected: Umpi is "she" (the wiki); Anderson's "my father said" about Bilger became "folk here say" (no invented family).

Secrets revealed by Persuade/Intimidate/Deceive checks (authored, not canon):
- Redpath (Persuade, lantern): fears Southshore goes the way of Tarren Mill (he knew folk there; they fished the same river); he is the one holding the stable lantern most nights, watching the hill road, while the town thinks he's seeing to the horses.
- Farren (Intimidate, strand_truth): his first week here a Torn Fin nearly took his leg on the Western Strand, and he hasn't gone back; he isn't sure he could.

## Humans (human patch)
- Redpath, humans: stood on the beach when Lothar's boats landed; bitter that Stormwind sent 'three men and a cook' when Lordaeron's refugees came south; the ones who stayed are Southshore folk now.
- Maleb, humans: the Syndicate are Alterac's nobility, Perenolde's houses; the other kings carved Alterac up; Gilneas walled itself in, Stromgarde marched home. Non-humans: short lives, long grudges.
