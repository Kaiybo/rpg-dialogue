## Their quests
- The Crown of Will (turn-in from Sharlindra): "I don't waste much time with idle chatter"; Mug'thol is "an irritant", to be dealt with "quickly, thoroughly, and without incident".
- The Crown of Will (2): thin the Crushridge; "The snow blooms red".
- The Crown of Will (3): Targ, Muckrake and Glommus; heads on pikes above Alterac; "I want their heads, not excuses."
- The Crown of Will (5): deliver the crown to Sharlindra; warns against using it.
- Sharlindra's text: Mug'thol tried the crown on and resisted the possession placed upon him.
- The Grave Knight (Forever, gives): a necromancer traitor in the mage city's underbelly; she cares not for the mage city; "no one is beyond the reach of the Dark Lady".
