## Their quests
- Jacques: An Alarming Request (Forever): Dalaran's ley lines, a proud council, an emissary asking strangers.
- Kundric: Strahnbrad Mystery (Forever, turn-in): wants Alterac's buried stories.
- Kelly: Talk of the Town (Forever, start): keeps track of what's said.
- Phin: Hints of a New Plague? (start and final turn-in): fears for friends; suspects the Forsaken.
- Malvew: Costly Menace: loves the horses; hates the lions.
- Jessen: Soothing Turtle Bisque: pride in the town's cooking; the hills too dangerous now.
- Merideth: Manna-Enriched Horse Feed (paladins, 60): vindication; grudge against the Silver Hand.
- Rabonne: none; reacts to Down the Coast.

## Identity
- Emissary Jacques · Southshore Town Hall · human man · noble (ambassador). Formal, careful, quietly frightened; dry humour about constructs.
- Kundric Zanden · Southshore · human man · scholar. Chatty, footnote-loving, kind about refugees.
- Barkeep Kelly · Southshore inn · human man · drink vendor. Jovial gossip; "the real business of any inn".
- Phin Odelic · Southshore · human man · ex-Kirin Tor mage, questgiver. Sharp, curious, loyal; bored by committees.
- Darren Malvew · Southshore stables · human man · stablehand questgiver. Gruff, folksy ("ain't", "'em", "dang").
- Chef Jessen · Southshore · human man · cook. Proud, generous, teasing.
- Merideth Carlson · Southshore · human woman · horse breeder. Proud, prickly, vindicated; capitals when stung ("PALADIN").
- Donald Rabonne · Southshore docks · human man · fishing trainer. Calm, sly about Lindea.

## Agenda / Secrets (authored, not canon)
- Jacques: thinks the charade can't last; "a broom that attacks you is funny exactly once".
- Kundric: wants Southshore to remember it once took in refugees; writes carefully about the Forsaken.
- Kelly: gossip as a way to stop fights and save horses.
- Phin: left the Kirin Tor for "questions, not committees"; reads Jacques's errand as a sign it's bad.
- Malvew: locks the stable every night, and some mornings it's still open.
- Jessen: didn't charge a weeping fisherman; won't share the recipe.
- Merideth: "I'll remember every name".
- Rabonne: he and Lindea never tell anyone how they're related.
