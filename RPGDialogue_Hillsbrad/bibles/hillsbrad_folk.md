# Hillsbrad folk (keys: hb_monika, hb_christoph, hb_theodore, hb_ott, hb_darla, hb_wesley, hb_hal, hb_neema)

Wiki pages read 2026-11-17 (warcraft.wiki.gg): Southshore, Tarren Mill, Monika Sengutz, Vorrel's Revenge, Christoph Jeffcoat, Soothing Turtle Bisque (Horde), Theodore Mont Claire, Ott, Darla Harris, Wesley, Hal McAllister, Neema.

## Sources
- **Monika Sengutz** (female Forsaken, Tarren Mill): ends "Vorrel's Revenge" (Classic; from Vorrel Sengutz in the Scarlet Monastery: Interrogator Vishas tortured him, gave his wedding ring to his wife Nancy, who lives by Lake Lordamere; return the ring to Monika). Progress: "What do you want, stranger?" Completion: "Vorrel led such a troubled existence. And what a good mate he was... Damn those filthy humans!"
- **Christoph Jeffcoat** (male Forsaken, Trade Goods, Tarren Mill): starts/ends "Soothing Turtle Bisque" (Horde cooking, 10 Turtle Meat from the snapjaws at Lake Lordamere; "I've known folks that would come back from the dead just to enjoy a bowl"; can't leave his work). Gossip: "Can I show you what I have for sale?" Shares its title with Chef Jessen's Alliance quest (`southshore_more.lua`).
- **Theodore Mont Claire** (male Forsaken, Stable Master, Tarren Mill).
- **Ott** (male orc, Weaponsmith, Tarren Mill). His wiki quests ("An Underrated Talent") look post-Classic and are not used.
- **Darla Harris** (female human Gryphon Master in Classic Southshore; became Forsaken only in Cataclysm, not used).
- **Wesley** (male human Stable Master, beside the mailbox in front of Southshore's inn).
- **Hal McAllister** (male human Fish Merchant, Southshore; in Old Hillsbrad sceptical of a fisherman's tall tales).
- **Neema** (female human waitress, food and drink, Southshore inn).

## Voices
- **Monika**: cold, dry Forsaken grief; still waiting on the road; won't hear the word "dead".
- **Christoph**: cheerful Forsaken cook-merchant; "heh"; longs for taste.
- **Theodore**: courtly Forsaken ex-nobleman who bred horses.
- **Ott**: few words, orc among the dead, uneasy.
- **Darla**: brisk gryphon master watching the north for bats.
- **Wesley**: young, jumpy, kind.
- **Hal**: dour sceptic of every fish story.
- **Neema**: quick, warm, "love", knows all the gossip.

## Authored, not canon
- Monika: Vorrel was a carter before and after death, carrying messages for the Deathstalkers; he hummed badly; she wears the ring on a cord.
- Christoph: knew Chef Jessen from old harvest fairs; tastes only very salty, hot food; carts from Undercity shot at by Southshore.
- Theodore: bred greys on an estate north of Lordamere; sat three nights in the straw until the horses accepted him.
- Ott: came to see Durnholde, where Thrall was a slave; laughed at the Syndicate from its walls; distrusts the apothecaries' cellars.
- Darla: the Wildhammer lent Southshore its gryphons after the Second War; learned the whistles from a rider called Gorran.
- Wesley: an aunt who lived in Tarren Mill; the old stable master went to Stromgarde with the League; has kept the stalls two summers.
- Hal: sold a herring to a man who claimed to have caught a talking fish.
- Neema: gossip about Bilger watering the ale and Forsaken carting barrels in the hills.
