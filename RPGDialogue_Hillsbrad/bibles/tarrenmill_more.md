# Tarren Mill, more people  (keys: krusk, thaivand, humbert, samsa, tallow, zarise, kayren)

## Sources (warcraft.wiki.gg, Classic sections)
- Tarren_Mill (NPC list), Krusk, The_Rescue, Infiltration_(Classic), Gol'dir_(quest): Krusk, an orc warrior sent by Thrall from Durotar with others to retrieve an object from Durnholde and investigate the Syndicate; ambushed by a large number of humans, some taken captive, he escaped. The Rescue (Drull and Tog'thar held in Durnholde, southeast across the river), Infiltration (documents at the large Syndicate camp north of Tarren Mill), Gol'dir (held in a Syndicate base north, in Alterac; Gol'dir led the expedition, forced to haul and dig in Strahnbrad; Krusk undoes the lock himself).
- Novice_Thaivand, Helcular's_Revenge, Helcular's_Revenge_(2), Helcular's_Rod_(Classic): apprentice of Helcular, a wizard nearly transformed into a lich when a human lynch mob found his ritual lair in the hills and killed him; buried in Southshore's cemetery; rod taken by yeti (Alterac); flames of Azel and Veraz in the Foothill Caverns, Uzel in a cave above; drive the charged rod into the grave; Helcular rises and summons skeletons.
- Deathguard_Humbert, Humbert's_Sword_(quest): returned to camp to find his gear missing; dwarven scouts from Dun Garok made off with it.
- Deathguard_Samsa, Souvenirs_of_Death: jealous of those enlisted by Darthalia; stuck on guard; collects 30 human skulls.
- Tallow, The_Hammer_May_Fall: Forsaken warrior; "weaklings"; Hammerfall under attack; test yourself at the Boulderfist ogre mound northeast of Stromgarde, then report to Drum Fel (Drum's line about Tallow already in the Arathi pack).
- Zarise (bat handler), Kayren_Soothallow (general goods).
