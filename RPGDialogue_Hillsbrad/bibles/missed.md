# Hillsbrad and Alterac, missed folk  (keys: hb_drull, hb_togthar, hb_elysa, hb_henchman_valik, hb_carlo_aurelius, hb_simone_cantrell, hb_warden_belamoore, hb_brokin, hb_george_candarte, hb_jekyll_flandring, hb_rizz_loosebolt, hb_rousch, hb_tamra_stormpike)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg via `tools/npc.py` (read 2026-11-18): each NPC's own page; quest pages Valik, Lord Aliden Perenolde, Taretha's Gift.
- Classic placement: Drull and Tog'thar are prisoners in Durnholde Keep (Eastpoint Tower and "Durnholde Syndrome" are Cataclysm); Elysa is alive upstairs at Dandred's Fold; Valik lies on Sofera's Naze; Belamoore commands the Lordamere Internment Camp.
- Existing pack: tarrenmill_more.lua (Krusk, who wants Drull and Tog'thar rescued), alterac_roads.lua (Lord Ravenholdt, Gol'dir), voidglare.lua (the Forsaken thieves held at the Internment Camp). Dalaran pack: Thanthaldis Snowgleam, the Stormpike counterpart to Jekyll Flandring.

## Their quests
- Drull, Tog'thar: freed in The Rescue (Krusk's quest).
- Elysa: Lord Aliden Perenolde (turn-in), Taretha's Gift (start).
- Henchman Valik: Valik (bring a mug of Southshore Stout; he hands over a Syndicate missive).
- The others have none. Tamra's rifle and Belamoore's pendant are drops, not conversations.

## Identity / voice
- Drull · orc man · blunt, ashamed to be taken alive; jailor Eston.
- Tog'thar · orc man · grim, dry, counts the guard's steps; jailor Marlgen.
- Elysa · human woman · frightened, worn down; never chose Perenolde; wants to be nobody again.
- Henchman Valik · human man · wry and resigned; one last drink.
- Carlo Aurelius · human man · smooth Ravenholdt assassin; polite menace.
- Simone Cantrell · night elf woman · serene, sly; her "landscaping" is the manor's defence.
- Warden Belamoore · human woman · stern Kirin Tor jailer; everything catalogued.
- Bro'kin · goblin man · cheerful salesman above a city of ogres; friend of Lotwil Veriatus.
- George Candarte · Forsaken man · dry recluse; hides by Thoradin's Wall to avoid company.
- Jekyll Flandring · Forsaken man · officious clerk lent to the Frostwolves; supplies by standing.
- Rizz Loosebolt · gnome man · frantic, still selling from the ogres' cage.
- Rousch · dwarf man · gruff, takes no side, tells nothing.
- Tamra Stormpike · dwarf woman, a ghost · fierce, distant, still on watch.

## Authored, not canon
- Tog'thar's overheard details (guard changes at dusk, orders from a manor to the north).
- Elysa's family holding by the lake and her plan to walk to Southshore; Aliden's supper talk.
- Valik grew up near the Southshore brewery.
- Carlo and Simone's banter about Syndicate scouts; Simone's sight-lines on the path.
- Belamoore's manner; she does not mention the pendant.
- Bro'kin's ogre who walked off the wall; George was a tanner in life; Jekyll was a quartermaster's clerk.
- Rizz mended the ogre chieftain's cook-pot and is kept as lucky (wiki: he asks to be saved and can't be).
- Rousch: the wiki gives only "a dwarf south of Dun Garok", neutral. Everything else (keeps to himself, refuses a side) is invented and kept deliberately thin.
- Tamra: the wiki gives a rare ghostly Stormpike dwarf in Dun Garok's inner level. That she fell defending the fort and stays on watch is invented.
