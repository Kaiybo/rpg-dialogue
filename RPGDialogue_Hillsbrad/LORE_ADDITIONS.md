**Assumptions (confirm in game):**
- `visited = "Trade District"` stands for "has been to Stormwind" in Farren's reply.
- `questActive = TODO_QUEST("Farren's Proof")` matches all three parts (same title); Redpath's reply is worded to fit any of them.
- `questDone = TODO_QUEST("Battle of Hillsbrad")` fires after the first part; Darthalia's reply is worded for any point in the chain.
- The title is "Time To Strike" on the wiki; the file keeps the existing `TODO_QUEST("Time to Strike")` (matching normalises case, I believe; check).
- Bilger's Silver Hand past (already in pack 1.0.0) comes from his Old Hillsbrad appearance (Burning Crusade). The Southshore page's Classic section does say some residents were once Silver Hand knights. Kept as backstory, nothing from the Old Hillsbrad dungeon is referenced; the lead may want to confirm.

**Year 1 notes:** Nothing from Southshore's destruction, the Hillsbrad worgen, Lydon's promotion or the Sludge Fields, Edge of Night, Southpoint Gate, Old Hillsbrad as an event, or the Battle for Azeroth return. Varimathras is Darthalia's commander with no hint of his betrayal. The Sunwell Trilogy's human Tarren Mill isn't used.

**Corrections to earlier content (ids kept):** Lydon's `u_default` called Umpi "he"; the wiki says "her". Anderson's `h_bilger` gave him a father who knew Bilger; now "folk here say".
