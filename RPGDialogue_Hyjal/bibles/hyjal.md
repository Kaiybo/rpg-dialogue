New Forever zone (level 60, contested), reached on foot from Winterspring through Darkwhisper Gorge. Everything below was read on warcraft.wiki.gg on 2026-11-17. The Forever pages are thin: the subzone pages don't exist yet, only six NPCs and a handful of quests have pages, and the raids open on December 9. Where the wiki is silent, the characters stay modest and talk about their work, their place and each other.

## Year 1 discipline
- Set a few years after the Battle of Mount Hyjal (Third War). Archimonde is dead; Nordrassil is scorched and mending; the night elves are mortal; Teldrassil is their capital; Malfurion sleeps in the Emerald Dream (body in the Moonglade barrow dens); Felwood rots below.
- Never: anything from Cataclysm Hyjal (Ragnaros, the Firelands, the Twilight's Hammer assault on Nordrassil, Malfurion awake, Aviana reborn, the Wormwing harpies, Sethria, Guardians of Hyjal as a faction, Sanctuary of Malorne quest hubs), Illidan's later fate (Outland, the Illidari), the Legion invasion, anything later.
- No dates or year counts: "a few years", "since the battle".
- The raids (Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit) are not open yet and their bosses have no pages. Nobody names a boss. Maerion only says the deeps under the mountain are deep and not as empty as the Circle hopes.

## Voices
- **Thisalee Crow** (major): young, quick, headstrong Druid of the Talon; loves the mountain, chafes at Skylord Omnuron's caution, likes Yashiro because he does what she would. Speaks fast, warm, a little rude; "the owls" are her excuse for everything. Notices druids, forms, night elves, warlocks (hostile), kit.
- **Yashiro** (major): orc blademaster, quiet, formal, whole sentences, no slang; "the will of the mountain", wind and stillness. Vengeance he admits to on bad days. Respects healers (a shaman saved him), honours the Hyjal alliance, sad about Ashenvale. Lok'tar to the Horde.
- **Maerion Thaelemaches** (major): night elf demon hunter; grim, precise, dry; never contractions-heavy; "if this is what we call a victory" is his creed (paraphrased in his hello, not copied verbatim). Distrusted by Sentinels and expects it. Will not say Illidan's name.
- **Jereman** (quest): dread raven Wild God of the Cradle; slow, sideways answers, "little one"; remembers accurately because "the songs never do".
- **Marrosis** (major): vulture Wild God at Mourning's Rest; dry, grumbling, funny about food, serious about the cycle; "little meat". Two of its documented lines are paraphrased in the hello (the blight on the cycle; not chasing meals).
- **Thylaen** (quest): tends the poisoned growth; stern gardener; cutting is healing. Race and gender unknown: no line states either (impression says "they").
- **Zet'gan** (quest): troll, Horde camp; tired, loyal, joking; troll dialect kept to whole sentences. Likely Horde-only to talk to.
- Pools: sentinels/wardens/watchers (guard), druids and tenders (citizen), traders (vendor). All `zone = "Mount Hyjal"`.

## Assumed
- The way through Darkwhisper Gorge is open now; nobody in the pack knows who opened it or why (deliberately open).
- The Shrine of Aviana is a Druids of the Talon place in Forever (its page is Cataclysm-era; the Forever subzone exists).
- The hostile furbolgs at Malorne's Retreat are "soured"; which tribe is not said.
- The dead at Mourning's Rest include the humans of Jaina's force and night elves who fell at the defenders' camp; who raised them is left open (Legion rot or Scourge).
- Mourning's Rest is west, Dae'gun north-east, the Cradle north (from map coordinates).
- The Hyjal guards and tenders are night elves (pool greetings "Ishnu-alah", "Elune's light").
- Zet'gan and his camp are Darkspear trolls of the Horde.
- The unnamed Alliance Feathers in Flight giver is "the fletcher" in the citizen pool.

## Authored, not canon
- Thisalee: "a Talon as young as me"; she carried messages over the lines during the battle; her distrust of fel; her liking for Yashiro.
- Yashiro: three blademaster brothers lost when the wisps came (Persuade secret); his master never told his true name; an old shaman gave him the spirit bead and saved his life once; he lights Dae'gun first because the Horde camped there.
- Maerion: lost his name among the Sentinels and his family's door; hears the fel bargaining in his own voice (Persuade secret); the first demon hunters learned from the prisoner of the barrows; Jereman tolerates him in the Cradle.
- Jereman: watched the Legion's coming; flew blind for a day after the wisps; Wild Gods "died in the first war".
- Marrosis: the "kindness" of death; the "pretty" Wild Gods tolerating it; the wailers' crying.
- Thylaen: keeps Celastrus's frond on a shelf.
- Zet'gan: Yashiro's visits keep demons from the camp a week.
- Pools: couriers and other quest hubs not mentioned; vendor stock from Everlook via Azzleby.
