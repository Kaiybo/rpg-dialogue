# Talk to the people of Azeroth

**RPG Dialogue** turns NPC interactions in World of Warcraft: Forever into short, reactive, CRPG-style conversations. Right-click someone as usual, and instead of a wall of quest text you get a real exchange: their words, your replies, and consequences that last.

People notice who you are: your race, class, level, what you're wearing and what you've done. They remember you between visits, talk about each other, and sometimes lie to you.

Everything is pre-written and ships with the addon. Nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing you do shows up for anyone else.

## What you get

- **Branching conversations** with hundreds of named NPCs across 49 zone and city packs, Alliance and Horde, from Northshire to Silithus, plus Forever's Dalaran, Mount Hyjal, Shen'dralas, Zephras Isle and the Riverglades.
- **Replies that fit you.** Every class, race and gender gets lines of its own. Your stances shape your alignment, and people react to it.
- **A memory.** NPCs recall your past visits, the deeds you've done, the dungeons you've cleared and who you travel with.
- **Ask about...** Names of people, places and troubles are highlighted as people speak. Pick them up and ask anyone about them.
- **The journal:** your chronicle by day and place, the people you've met, your character sheet and origin, topics, deeds and your own notes.
- **Read aloud** (optional): conversations and quest text spoken with the game's text-to-speech voices.
- **Quests and services stay one click away.** Quest, vendor, trainer and flight options sit right in the box, and "Get on with it" (or Escape) takes you straight to the normal window.

## Plays well with others

- **DialogueUI:** conversations happen inside DialogueUI's own window.
- **Immersion:** RPG Dialogue steps back and shows only a short line, with the full conversation on a keybinding.
- **Questie** and other quest or vendor addons: unaffected.
- **Total RP 3** (optional): NPCs call you by your roleplay name and title.

## Accessible

Adjustable text size, spacing and fonts (including Atkinson Hyperlegible), text speed, instant text, auto-advance, window scale, a high-contrast theme, full keyboard and gamepad control, and one **reduce motion** switch. Nothing relies on color alone.

## Getting started

1. Install, then make sure **RPG Dialogue** and the zone packs are ticked in the AddOns list. Packs only load in their own zone, so keep them all on.
2. Right-click an NPC.
3. `/rpgd` lists every command. `/rpgd settings` opens the settings, and `/rpgd journal` opens the journal.

**Back up your WTF folder now and then.** Everything NPCs remember about your characters lives in SavedVariables there. `/rpgd export` gives you a copyable backup of one character's story.

## A note from the author

As a disclaimer, I wrote this addon mainly for my own enjoyment, and it was built with heavy help from AI, so the language may feel stiff at times. I'm still improving the UI and adding more content, but the fundamentals are there. I hope you enjoy it.
