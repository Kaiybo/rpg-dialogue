# RPG Dialogue: Darkshore (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)
- Depth: Thundris, Terenthis, Gwennyth, Glynda, Cerellean and Shaussiy are now conversations to explore. Thundris on Felwood's sickness, the Highborne ruins, the Twilight's Hammer at the Master's Glaive and why he trusts the Silver Hand; Terenthis on Fel Moss, Volcor and Grimclaw; Gwennyth on the beached threshers and whether they flee Teldrassil; Glynda on the moonkin and what the red crystal hides; Cerellean on finding Anaya's spirit; Shaussiy on the dwarves and the boats.
- For a priest: Thundris and Gwennyth ask for a healer's eye on the shore. Humans, dwarves and gnomes off the Bravery from Menethil get Thundris's welcome; paladins hear about the Kor gem.
- Year 1 fix: Onward to Ashenvale (added in 2.0.3) is no longer used; Thundris points level 18+ characters to Raene, and Raene remembers it.
- Voice pass: every line, the Auberdine Sentinels' included, now speaks in whole sentences.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits now bring personal lines (habits, worries, opinions of the neighbours), night-time lines, and a question or two about the person themselves, not only their quests.
- Shaussiy, Gwennyth, Glynda, Terenthis.

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- Auberdine: Thundris Windweaver, Terenthis and the Fel Moss, Gwennyth Bly'Leggonde and the beached creatures, Sentinel Glynda Nal'Shea, Cerellean Whiteclaw on the dock, Innkeeper Shaussiy. Auberdine's Sentinels.
