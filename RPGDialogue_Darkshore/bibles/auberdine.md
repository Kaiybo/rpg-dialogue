- Glynda: urgent, protective Sentinel.

- Shaussiy: warm innkeeper; tea for Cerellean.

### Year 1 notes
- Onward to Ashenvale was added in patch 2.0.3 (Burning Crusade); it is no longer used, nor the friendship it describes between Thundris and Raene. Classic's road from Darkshore to Astranaar is Sentinel Selarin's Trek to Ashenvale.
- The Way to Auberdine (draenei) is Burning Crusade; not used.
- In Classic the Bravery sails to Menethil and the Moonspray to Rut'theran; Auberdine's Sentinels guard the Bravery's crossings; dwarves keep a small smithy in town; the Explorers' League digs at Remtravel's Excavation.

### Authored, not canon (this pass)
Thundris's welcome to people off the Bravery, his question about Menethil's damp, his late lamps, "I know the smell of it"; Terenthis's account of Grimclaw standing in the road; Gwennyth's offer to walk the shore with a priest; Glynda's "people who write poems about them"; Cerellean's advice to tell people you love them today; Shaussiy's guests and the dwarves who complain about the damp.

## 0.37.0: more of Auberdine (npcs/auberdine_more.lua)

- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tharnariun_Treetender (night elf druid in Auberdine; Plagued Lands (trap and bring back a rabid thistle bear), Cleansing of the Infected, Tharnariun's Hope (the Den Mother))

### Their quests

- Elissa: Supplies to Auberdine (turn-in); a line for The Tower of Althalaxx in progress.

### Voices
- Tharnariun: patient healer of beasts; wants the sick bear alive; doubts Terenthis's one-cause theory, gently.
- Elissa: brisk Sentinel quartermaster; counts crates and kindnesses; came to respect Delgren's humans.
- Caylais: cheerful hippogryph keeper; talks of the birds' moods like weather.
### What they notice
- Tharnariun: a pet near sick bears; a druid in bear form.
- Elissa: humans and paladins (Delgren's company).
- Caylais: dwarves and gnomes off the Bravery.
### Never
- Tharnariun or anyone naming the bears' sickness's source (the game doesn't). No druid flight form (Classic has none).
### Authored, not canon
Tharnariun sitting with the caged bear each evening, the sickness worse at the dark of the moon, his friendly argument with Terenthis; Elissa's doubts about outsiders and "one crate at a time"; Caylais's hippogryph Feather-in-the-Mist, "lean left", the birds refusing to fly when the moonkin are restless. Shaussiy (0.37.0): seasick guests from Menethil, her word to Elune at the fire.

## Prejudices (0.80.0, authored, not canon)
- Sentinel Glynda Nal'Shea distrusts dwarves (`prejudice` Dwarf 10): Remtravel's diggers carry carvings out of Ameth'Aran and Mist's Edge, and she once found her mother's door lintel propping up a dwarf's cookpot at the dig. Earned affinity wins her round.

## Kaldorei factions (authored, not canon)
- Elissa: values the Alliance by her ledgers (dwarven iron, Delgren's paladins, gnomish lamp oil) and argues with Glynda and Tysha. Thundris: has made peace with mortality since Hyjal and argues that mortal peoples need each other; names Auberdine's banks (Glynda, Tysha, Elissa, Barithras, Allyndia).
- Night elves (0.94.0 work): Remtravel thinks the kaldorei forbid digs to hide their past, not out of reverence (claim `remtravel_elves_hide_history`, partly, open); admits the lintel-and-cookpot business with Glynda. A night elf can send him to Thundris (`[Persuade]`, npcFlag `remtravel_soft`), after which every crate goes past Thundris first.


## Humans (human patch)
- terenthis: Bitter, intent: humans caged then freed the orcs, and a human necromancer called the Legion into Dalaran; concedes the Highborne first called the Legion. Softens if a human offers to help heal the ground. Flag `terenthis_human_soft`.
- remtravel: Wry, scatterbrained: humans are splendid diggers who never look; two Westfall lads shovelled through a titan carving. Softens if a human promises to go slowly. Flag `remtravel_human_soft`.
