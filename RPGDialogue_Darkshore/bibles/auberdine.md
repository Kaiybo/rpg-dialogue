# Auberdine  (keys: thundris, terenthis, gwennyth, glynda, cerellean, shaussiy)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Auberdine (the Alliance's northern port: boats to Rut'theran and Menethil; moonkin to the east maddened by a red crystal from Felwood, Blackwood furbolgs corrupted by Fel Moss to the south, murlocs sinking ships; the village council hires adventurers)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Thundris_Windweaver (night elf druid, village elder; wise, fair; doesn't judge a group by one member; trusts the Silver Hand more than some of his own kin; Bashal'Aran, Tools of the Highborne, The Cliffspring River (Felwood), The Blackwood Corrupted (Xabraxxis), Seeking the Kor Gem (paladins; Blackfathom Deeps), Onward to Ashenvale (Raene))
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Terenthis (night elf druid studying the spread of Fel Moss; How Big a Threat?, A Lost Master (the bear Grimclaw, whose master furbolgs attacked), Escape Through Stealth, Escape Through Force)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gwennyth_Bly%27Leggonde (priestess of the Sisterhood, sent from the Temple of the Moon to help Auberdine; Washed Ashore, Beached Sea Creature, Beached Sea Turtle)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sentinel_Glynda_Nal%27Shea (Sentinel in Auberdine; corruption creeping into the groves; The Red Crystal, As Water Cascades, The Fragments Within, WANTED: Murkdeep!)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Cerellean_Whiteclaw (night elf druid on the docks by the inn; loved Anaya Dawnrunner of Ameth'Aran, who died in the War of the Ancients; slept with Malfurion and dreamed of her; couldn't free her spirit himself; when freed she promised they'd meet again; For Love Eternal)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Innkeeper_Shaussiy (night elf innkeeper; tired spirits, rest, Elune guide your dreams)

## Their quests
- Thundris: Bashal'Aran, Tools of the Highborne, The Cliffspring River, The Blackwood Corrupted, Seeking the Kor Gem, Onward to Ashenvale.
- Terenthis: How Big a Threat?, A Lost Master, Escape Through Stealth.
- Gwennyth: Washed Ashore, Beached Sea Creature, Beached Sea Turtle.
- Glynda: The Red Crystal, As Water Cascades, WANTED: Murkdeep!
- Cerellean: For Love Eternal.
- Shaussiy: none: innkeeper only.

## Never
- Anything Cataclysm or later: Auberdine's destruction, the deaths on the beach, Lor'danel, Cerellean adopting Grimclaw, Glynda as a ranger.
- What the beached creatures' deaths mean (Gwennyth doesn't know).

## Voices
- Thundris: wise, fair elder; the land is sickening and it comes from the north.
- Terenthis: intent druid chasing the moss; the furbolgs are sick, not evil.
- Gwennyth: gentle, observant priestess; the sea giving up its dead.
- Glynda: urgent, protective Sentinel.
- Cerellean: melancholy lover on the dock; ten thousand years of waiting.
- Shaussiy: warm innkeeper; tea for Cerellean.
