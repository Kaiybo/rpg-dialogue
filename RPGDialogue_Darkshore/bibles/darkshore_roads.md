# Darkshore off the road (keys: balthule, tysha)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg: Balthule Shadowstrike, Sentinel Tysha Moonblade; quests The Tower of Althalaxx (1-3), The Fall of Ameth'Aran (quest). Classic versions only (both removed in 4.0.3a).
- Delgren is the human paladin at Maestra's Post (`RPGDialogue_Ashenvale/npcs/astranaar.lua`); Elissa Starbreeze sends the player to Balthule (`auberdine_more.lua`).

## Voices
**Balthule Shadowstrike.** Watchful, earnest, a little guilty about not reporting; a kaldorei scout learning the Light from Delgren and shyly proud of it.
**Sentinel Tysha Moonblade.** Steady, dry, honest that the spirits drove her off; keeps the ruins' memory for Darnassus.

## Authored, not canon
- Balthule: the Sentinels lent him to Delgren "mostly to be rid of him"; elder Sentinels needle him for the human faith; Delgren wondering if the Light and Elune are two faces of one thing; the parchments name a gathering place far to the south.
- Tysha: her reading of the tablets (the city sang of its beauty until it drowned in its power; the spirits never heard the song end).

Cataclysm-era Darkshore (Mathystra relocation, Shatterspear quests, Lor'danel).
