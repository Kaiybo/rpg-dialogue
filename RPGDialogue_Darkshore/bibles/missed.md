# Darkshore, missed folk  (keys: jaelysia, yalda, ullanna)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg via `tools/npc.py`: Jaelysia (stable master; the wiki lists Lor'danel, a Cataclysm town; her Classic-range id 10085 and trade put her at Auberdine's stables), Yalda (id 6887; a ghost in the Ruins of Auberdine after the Cataclysm, "no functional purpose" before it, so written as a living resident of Auberdine), Ullanna (trade supplies; the wiki lists the Auberdine Refugee Camp and says she stood at the Grove of the Ancients before the Cataclysm).
- Existing pack: auberdine.lua, auberdine_folk.lua (Gwennyth and the dead sea creatures, Tharnariun and the rabid thistle bears, Thundris, the Sentinels), between.lua (Onu at the Grove; the Twilight's Hammer at the Master's Glaive).

## Their quests
- None.

## Identity / voice
- Jaelysia · night elf woman · gentle, observant; greets the beast first; trusts animals' unease.
- Yalda · night elf woman · wistful, soft-spoken; born in Auberdine; watches the boats.
- Ullanna · night elf woman · hushed, content; keeps shop in Onu's shadow and lowers her voice for him.

## Authored, not canon
- Yalda's whole manner and history (born on the shore, gathered shells at the Long Wash, her mother traded with the Blackwood).
- Ullanna came to the grove to rest and stayed; her reasons for liking a lonely shop.
- Jaelysia's views on which beasts suit a hunter.

## Never
- Lor'danel, the Auberdine Refugee Camp, the ruin of Auberdine, Yalda as a spirit, or any hint of the Cataclysm.
