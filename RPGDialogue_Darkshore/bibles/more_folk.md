# Darkshore, Auberdine's shopkeepers we had missed (keys: harlon, kyndri, allyndia, shaldyn, mavralyn)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg: Harlon Thornguard (Armorer & Shieldsmith, in Auberdine before the Cataclysm), Kyndri (Baker, drink and bread, Auberdine), Allyndia (Food & Drink, Auberdine), Shaldyn (cloth armor vendor, Auberdine), Mavralyn (leather armor and leatherworking vendor, Auberdine).
- Classic placement: all five in Auberdine. Their later fates (Auberdine's destruction, Lor'danel) are Cataclysm and are never used.

## Their quests
- none: service NPCs. (Shaldyn's "The Last Wave of Survivors" is Cataclysm; not used.)

## Voices
**Harlon Thornguard.** Gruff, steady armorer; the coast is hard; sells thick plate to outlanders.
**Kyndri.** Warm, brisk baker; frets about late flour ships from the south.
**Allyndia.** Cheerful, chatty food seller; likes the outsiders livening up Auberdine.
**Shaldyn.** Gentle, precise clothier; proud of her shore dyes.
**Mavralyn.** Dry, practical leather seller; pays for good skinning, won't buy sick hides.

## Authored, not canon
- Harlon on the Blackwood as old neighbours; outlanders buy his heavy plate.
- Kyndri's flour from the south and late ships; naga off the northern coast (kept as rumour).
- Allyndia on the elders grumbling about outsiders.
- Shaldyn's dyes (kelp blue, moss green, moon-flower silver).
- Mavralyn refuses hides from near the furbolg dens; "murloc-chewed" bad skinning.

## Never
Cataclysm Darkshore (Lor'danel, Deathwing, Auberdine's ruin), boats to Azuremyst or Exodar, anything after Classic.

### Voices
**Wizbang.** Fast, precise, apologetic gnome; percentages; won't dwell on Gnomeregan.
**Aynasha.** Terse, proud; asks for help as a statement of fact.
**Therylune.** Shaken but resolute; whispers; her race is deliberately not stated in dialogue.
**Delfrum.** Dwarf burr, hearty; complains of the damp; proud of his elven students.
**Grondal.** Courteous, never satisfied; teaches students "to be forgotten".
**Gorbold.** Jovial dwarf trader; exact about losses; Steelhand family pride.
**Thelgrum.** Plainspoken; baffled by unmined hills.
**Valdaron.** Mild; waits on the boats.
**Jaelysia.** Gentle with beasts, firm with owners.

### Authored, not canon

Deepened entries (Allyndia, Harlon, Kyndri, Mavralyn, Shaldyn, Thamarian): neighbours, trade and the coast's troubles only.
