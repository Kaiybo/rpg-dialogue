## Voices
**Hollee (dwarf, Explorers' League).** Young, anxious, chatty, loyal to Remtravel to the point of hero worship; speeds up when worried; homesick for proper accents.
**Gershala Nightwhisper (night elf scholar).** Grim, precise, unflinching; studies how magic changed the satyr and naga and fears the blood elves are next (from the quest text). Fond of his brother in a dry way.
**Sentinel Selarin.** Brisk, steady, proud of serving Raene Wolfrunner; turns travellers south.
**Thamarian.** Cheerful dock guide who loves the ships and can't stand sailing.
**Kurdram Stonehammer (dwarf).** Gruff, dry, damp-kneed; "ye" and "yer"; respects the elves' tree rule grudgingly.
**Jenna Lemkenilli (gnome).** Eager, quick, a Gnomeregan refugee glad of any roof.

## Authored, not canon
- Hollee: studied at the academy in Ironforge; Greywhisker assigned her to Remtravel; Remtravel dated a pot shard while standing in the cook fire; a bargain not to dig elf ruins, a Sentinel counting their shovels; Gwennyth's kindness and a low stool at the inn; three letters to Greywhisker.
- Gershala: he and Argos have lived apart for a very long time; Argos's letters smell of chimney smoke.
- Selarin: a girlhood drinking from Ashenvale moonwells; Raene gave her the dock posting for her patience; strangers in dark robes camping by the Master's Glaive (kept vague on purpose).
- Thamarian: sailed once to Menethil and was sick the whole way; prays to Elune for each departure.
- Kurdram: Thelgrum Stonehammer (mining supplies) is his cousin; the rule "mine around the trees" and Sentinels' warning arrows.
- Jenna: crossed the sea on an elf captain's offer after Gnomeregan; Thundris asked her to keep her smoke away from the moonwell; Gelkak once paid her in sea-shells.

## Never
Auberdine's destruction or anything Cataclysm and later; no retaking of Gnomeregan; no Lor'danel.

## Kaldorei factions (authored, not canon)
- Gershala: distrusts the arcane in the Alliance. Dalaran learned from the high elves, who descend from Highborne exiles; fears the quel'dorei starving for magic since the Sunwell fell. Argues with his brother Argos (Stormwind), who values the Alliance.
