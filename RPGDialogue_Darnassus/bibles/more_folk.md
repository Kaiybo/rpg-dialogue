# Darnassus, folk we had missed (keys: manados, shaedlass, lotherias, alegorn, fylerian, cylania, shylenai, talaelar)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg: Argent Guard Manados, Dawnwatcher Shaedlass, Lotherias, Alegorn, Fylerian Nightwing, Cylania, Shylenai, Talaelar; quests Twilight Falls, In Search of Thaelrid, Moonglow Vest (quest).
- Classic placement: Manados and Shaedlass on the Craftsmen's Terrace (Argent Dawn); Lotherias and Alegorn on the Craftsmen's Terrace; Fylerian in the Cenarion Enclave; Cylania and Shylenai on the Warrior's Terrace; Talaelar in the Temple Gardens.

## Their quests (TODO_QUEST by title)
- Manados: Twilight Falls (3 Twilight Pendants from the Twilight's Hammer in Blackfathom Deeps; Darnassus reputation).
- Shaedlass: In Search of Thaelrid (find Argent Guard Thaelrid in Blackfathom Deeps; leads to Blackfathom Villainy).
- Lotherias: Moonglow Vest (leatherworking 70: embossed leather gloves, 5 boots, 5 cloaks; teaches the pattern).
- Alegorn, Fylerian, Cylania, Shylenai, Talaelar: service only.

## Voices
**Argent Guard Manados.** Stern, plain-spoken soldier of the Dawn; Blackfathom is his worry; a guest who knows it.
**Dawnwatcher Shaedlass.** Calm, precise gatherer of rumours; the calm thins over his missing scout Thaelrid.
**Lotherias.** Brisk, warm, overworked leatherworker; judges by stitching; masters teach by orders.
**Alegorn.** Warm, easygoing cooking teacher; a good meal settles quarrels; feeds hungry Sentinels.
**Fylerian Nightwing.** Calm, slow druid teacher; balance between the shapes.
**Cylania.** Brisk, proud armorer; fit matters; forest armor is light for a reason.
**Shylenai.** Soft-spoken owl keeper; owls are Elune's birds.
**Talaelar.** Easygoing, wry fishmonger who fishes the Temple lake when the priestesses aren't looking.

## Authored, not canon
- Manados and Shaedlass work as a pair; Manados worries about Shaedlass's sleep.
- Lotherias buys only hides taken properly; Sentinels complain about boots in detail.
- Alegorn feeds Sentinels on watch (wiki mentions it); human spices from Stormwind.
- Fylerian is noncommittal on kinship with Freja Nightwing (the wiki only says "may be related").
- Shylenai's owl that glares at the door; the Sentinel saying about owls and quiet nights.
- Talaelar once stood watch on Ashenvale's border (small, kept vague).

## Never
Later fates (the Burning of Teldrassil, Amirdrassil, Dragon Isles), Cylania's later quests, anything from Wrath or later.

## Added later: missed folk (same file, npcs/more_folk.lua)

### Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg: Milla Fairancora, Taladan, Me'lynn, Eladriel, Ulthir, Vaean (Craftsmen's Terrace); Astaia, Voloren (Temple Gardens); Anishar (Cenarion Enclave). All service; no quests used (wiki lists later-era fishing dailies for Astaia: not Year 1).

### Voices
**Milla.** Eager apprentice teaching beginners; measures twice; in awe of Ainethil.
**Taladan.** Grave; teaches enchanting as restraint; "what does it cost, and who pays".
**Me'lynn.** Proud, generous; sewing as devotion.
**Eladriel.** Blunt; students thank the animal aloud; "slower".
**Astaia.** Serene former Sentinel; hates clams, loves octopus (wiki); turns a blind eye to Talaelar.
**Anishar.** Wry, watchful; the "root cellar"; careful about Staghelm.
**Ulthir.** Genial, orderly; kind to apprentices.
**Vaean.** Cool, exact; keeps the Temple's ledger of buyers.
**Voloren.** Cheerful, talkative; fishes while he sells.

### Authored, not canon
The Temple's ledger condition on enchanting (Vaean, Taladan); Taladan's dismissed student; Me'lynn's mother and the needle; Milla's forty years of stirring the wrong way; Ulthir's notes to the Enclave about spoiled herbs; Anishar's letter from Stormwind's SI:7; Voloren's whittled reel. Deepened entries (Alegorn, Cylania, Fylerian, Lotherias, Shylenai, Talaelar): neighbours and craft only; Fylerian stays careful, never disloyal, about Staghelm.

## Second sweep (Craftsmen's and Tradesmen's Terraces, Enclave; `npcs/more_folk.lua` tail and `npcs/more_folk2.lua`)

### Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg: Fyldan, Elynna, Saenorion, Telonis, Faldron, Trianna, Sylvanna Forestmoon, Lalina Summermoon, Mythrin'dir (Craftsmen's Terrace); Chardryn (by the Temple of the Moon); Auctioneers Tolon, Golothas, Cazarez; Ealyshia Dewwhisper, Merelyssa, Kieran, Turian, Glorandiir, Mythidan, Andrus, Caynrus, Melea, Vinasia, Anadyia, Yldan, Ellandrieth (Tradesmen's Terrace); Dendrythis; Kyrai, Cyroen (Cenarion Enclave); Ellaercia. All service; no quests.
- Left out: the three battlemasters (their Forever placement is unknown), Lewin Starfeather (no data), Sentinel Dalia Sunblade (a Forever-only quest we have no text for), Emissary Valustraa and the jewelcrafters (later).

### Voices
**Fyldan.** Placid, droll; has counted Alegorn's forty-one kind words; salts at the start.
**Elynna.** Candid; says the hard things about colour that Me'lynn won't.
**Saenorion.** Meticulous, mild; the one tidy bench; notches his awls.
**Telonis.** Grave master; teaches only when the pride has gone; misses Ashenvale stag hide.
**Faldron.** Gentle beginner's teacher; "begin with a strap"; quietly proud of Darianna.
**Trianna.** Quick, ambitious; collects outlander sleeves; rushes her black.
**Sylvanna Forestmoon.** Precise, dry; ten identical draughts; respects a scale.
**Lalina Summermoon.** Earnest, careful; asks every student why; "when it feels easy, stop".
**Mythrin'dir.** Shrewd, genial; stocks hammers in a city with no forge; orders ahead and says nothing.
**Chardryn.** Soft-spoken; believes the pots listen; keeps Ashenvale seed apart from Teldrassil seed.
**Tolon.** Formal, unhurried; asks sellers of old things twice.
**Golothas.** Terse; reads the city's fears in prices.
**Cazarez.** Affable, curious; has built the whole world in his head from travellers' talk and never left the tree.
**Ealyshia Dewwhisper.** Composed former Sentinel; held a line at Hyjal beside an orc; the thousand swings.
**Merelyssa.** Quick, sardonic; reads owners by their edges.
**Kieran.** Easygoing; "the first step of the stair"; sends buyers to his rivals.
**Turian.** Playful, exacting; knotholes now, satyrs once; the two-bladed glaive under the counter.
**Glorandiir.** Bluff; sells axes among night elves and knows how that sounds.
**Mythidan.** Thoughtful; knows each priestess by her staff.
**Andrus.** Scholarly carver; the raindrop staff.
**Caynrus.** Stolid; "the only honest weapon"; learned at Andiss's bench.
**Melea.** Brisk former quartermaster; doubles the collar for Ashenvale.
**Vinasia.** Graceful, tactful; dresses people toward who must trust them.
**Anadyia.** Serene, opinionated; the old pattern as a kind of faith.
**Yldan.** Dry, methodical; a pack is a character turned inside out.
**Ellandrieth.** Chatty, warm; directions unasked; the extra loaf.
**Dendrythis.** Soothing, wry; feeds people before and after they are advised; collects sighs.
**Kyrai.** Cool, matter-of-fact; arrow venom for the Sentinels, then the rogues' den.
**Cyroen.** Patient, kindly; a reagent is a small promise.
**Ellaercia.** Decisive; won't draw until a company knows what it is for.

### Authored, not canon
Ainethil's coarse salt (moonwell clippings, for the Temple); Trianna's human-shouldered robe; Sylvanna's three-year pupil and the gnome's alembic; Lalina's question and Taladan's "what does it cost, and who pays"; the druid who inspected Mythrin'dir's hammers; Chardryn's two sets of pots and the ironwoods' thinning seed (Cyroen): quiet signs of the tree's sickness, never stated as fact. The auction house's hundred-year ban, the ticking box and the sold glaive; Ealyshia, Glorandiir and Melea at Hyjal (one or two lines each, never a tale); the apple contest (Turian, Landria, Jaeana); the matron and the axe; the raindrop staff; the bitten shield at Maestra's Post; Caynrus as Andiss's former apprentice; the Temple paying for the rogues' rooms (Kyrai's claim); Dendrythis's sister near Astranaar; the White Rose tabard.
