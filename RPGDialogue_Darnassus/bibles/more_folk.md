## Voices
**Argent Guard Manados.** Stern, plain-spoken soldier of the Dawn; Blackfathom is his worry; a guest who knows it.
**Dawnwatcher Shaedlass.** Calm, precise gatherer of rumours; the calm thins over his missing scout Thaelrid.
**Lotherias.** Brisk, warm, overworked leatherworker; judges by stitching; masters teach by orders.
**Alegorn.** Warm, easygoing cooking teacher; a good meal settles quarrels; feeds hungry Sentinels.
**Fylerian Nightwing.** Calm, slow druid teacher; balance between the shapes.
**Cylania.** Brisk, proud armorer; fit matters; forest armor is light for a reason.
**Shylenai.** Soft-spoken owl keeper; owls are Elune's birds.
**Talaelar.** Easygoing, wry fishmonger who fishes the Temple lake when the priestesses aren't looking.
