## The prejudice
Every dwarf is a shovel looking for a hill. Blind spot: he digs earthroot himself, and his own people grew Teldrassil without the Aspects' blessing and it is sick; the player can put both to him. Softens through the [Persuade] for dwarves (flag `firodren_soft`); later he greets Greywhisker and shows dwarves where the roots run.

## Agenda
Keep the roots of Teldrassil and the ruins of Darkshore whole.

## Secrets
Authored, not canon: his prejudice against dwarves; his grove south of Auberdine (oaks over a ring of old stones) cut down two summers ago by an Explorers' League crew for trench shoring; the young dwarf with the lantern who apologised and wasn't believed; grey-veined herbs near Dolanaar; that he never speaks to Greywhisker.

## Reliability
Biased and grieving; the grove story is true as he tells it.
