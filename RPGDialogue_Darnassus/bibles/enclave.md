## Their quests
- Jenal: Un'Goro Soil (turn-in): he logs and sorts the soil; resents being given dirt to move; proud, self-pitying, comic.
- Syurna: The Apple Falls (turn-in): Jannok's roses irritate her; she sees use in his courier. Destiny Calls (gives): fears the sickness spreading from Darkshore; trusts stealth where force and talk failed. (A Simple Request not used: unsure of its patch.)
- Jocaste: The Hunter's Path (gives), Training the Beast (turn-in): stern but proud of young hunters; balance.
- Denatharion: Heeding the Call (gives): sends young druids to Mathrengyl.

## Identity
- **Jenal** · outside the Enclave · night elf · worker (quest). Grandiose, self-pitying, chatty, harmless. Speech: "a visionary", "conspiracies abound", "don't step in the dirt". Relationships: Fandral (resented, feared). Never: real proof of any conspiracy.
- **Syurna** · Enclave · night elf · rogue trainer (quest). Cold, sharp, guarded; talks only grudgingly to non-rogues; cares about Teldrassil's health.
- **Jocaste** · Enclave · night elf · hunter trainer (quest). Stern, steady, proud of her students; balance as law.
- **Denatharion** · Enclave · night elf · druid trainer (quest). Calm, patient, tactful about Fandral.

## Threads they could carry
- Jenal and Fandral: the Arch Druid's manner seen from the bottom of the Enclave.
- Syurna: the corruption creeping into Teldrassil (Sethir; ties to Darkshore and the Oracle Glade).

## Agenda
- Jenal: be recognised. Syurna: keep the forest's dark corners watched. Jocaste: raise hunters who keep the balance. Denatharion: send good druids up to Mathrengyl.

## Secrets
- Jenal (authored): he has thought of many retorts to Fandral, all after the fact.
- Denatharion (authored): admires Fandral on rested days, finds him tiring on others.
