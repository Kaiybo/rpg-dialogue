## Their quests
- Alathea: Desperate Prayer (gives, to human priests: "Praise be to Elune, and to your Light"; sends them to Laurena; respectful of the other faith). A Lack of Fear (gives, to dwarf priests: sends them to Rohan; wishes she could witness their rites). Elune's Grace and Stars of Elune (night elf priests: Elune's gifts for the devoted). Arcane Feedback (her distrust of arcane magic, divine or not; allies matter more than her unease).
- Astarii: Prayer to Elune (turn-in): Zamael Lunthistle's golden prayer of repentance, carried from the Searing Gorge; "you have returned a lost soul to her". Believes in mercy for the repentant.
- Jandria: none: priest trainer only.
- Lariia: none: priest trainer only.
- Aquinne: The Temple of the Moon (Classic) (gives): a once great creature of the forest needs help; sends the player to A'moora (Sathrah). Earnest, tender-hearted.
- Idriana: Calm Before the Storm (turn-in, step 2): pays Gracina's reward from her account. Courteous, exact. Lines fit either step of the same-titled chain.

## Identity
- **Alathea** · Priestess Alathea · Temple of the Moon · Darnassus · night elf · priest trainer (major). Personality: gracious, formal, devout, wary of the arcane, protective of her students. Speech: measured, "Praise be to Elune", "young priest". Knows: the Sisterhood, Laurena by letter, Rohan, the priest gifts, Tyrande's routine. Relationships: Laurena (letters), A'moora, Tyrande. Attitudes: welcomes priests of the Light as allies; keeps her hands folded around mages. Never says: that Elune and the Light are the same (SoD only); anything after Year 1.
- **Astarii Starseeker** · Temple · night elf · priest trainer (quest). Gentle, earnest, merciful; a listener; "soft" by her own admission. Never: anything about her later fate.
- **Jandria** · Temple · night elf · priest trainer (service). Brisk, practical; teaches mana discipline and field healing.
- **Lariia** · Temple · night elf · priest trainer (service). Quiet, watchful, honest about what she hasn't seen.
- **Sister Aquinne** · Temple Gardens · night elf · sister, not yet priestess (quest). Earnest, young in the Temple, easily moved, a little in awe of Tyrande.
- **Idriana** · Bank of Darnassus, Temple Gardens · night elf · banker (service). Courteous, exact, discreet.

## Agenda
- Alathea: keep the young priestesses steady; keep the alliance between the two faiths courteous.
- Astarii: see that the repentant are heard.

## Secrets
- Alathea (Persuade, authored, not canon): the young priestesses fear death since the loss of immortality, and some nights her prayers aren't enough for her either; she asks you not to tell Tyrande.
- Idriana (authored): Gracina keeps more specimens than coins in her box.

## Reliability
- All reliable. Lariia says plainly she has seen nothing in the pool; Alathea says plainly she doesn't know whether the Light and Elune are one.

## What they notice
- Alathea: priests (human priests first, Shadowform in the Temple, night elf priests), humans, mages (arcane).
- Astarii: priests of the Light; the Light in human priests and paladins.
- Jandria: priests of the Light. Lariia: worn gear, human priests. Aquinne: priests of the Light, low-level beginners. Idriana: a heavy purse.
