## Voices
**Darnath Bladesinger.** Proud, steady, honor as a kept blade; respects worthy foes and human courage at Hyjal.
**Silvaria.** Gentle; speaks to the beast before the hunter.
**Alassin.** Dry stable master, never lost an animal.
**Elissa Dumas (human mage).** Brisk, wry, quiet around priestesses; Stormwind born, now half nocturnal.
**Lairn.** Precise, slow-counting banker; memories over gold.
**Landria.** Cheerful, proud bowyer-merchant; only fallen or spared wood.

## Authored, not canon
- Darnath: trains roughly as many men as Sildanair trains women; his view that warriors of both sides understand each other.
- Silvaria: hunters who grieve a companion like a sister, others who buy a new one the same afternoon.
- Alassin: the dwarf hunter's Darkshore crab that pinched him every morning.
- Elissa Dumas: volunteered because the kaldorei won't teach the arcane; Stormwind born.
- Lairn: kaldorei keep memories in their vaults (a Hyjal letter, an old glaive, seeds of a vanished tree).
- Landria: friendly rivalry with Jeena Featherbow; bows only from fallen limbs or branches the druids say can be spared.
