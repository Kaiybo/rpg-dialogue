# Darnassus  (keys: tyrande, fandral, mathrengyl, amoora, gracina, saelienne)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tyrande_Whisperwind (High Priestess of Elune, leads the night elves alone while Malfurion's spirit is lost in the Emerald Dream and his body sleeps in his barrow; fierce, protective, blunt, acerbic when his absence is questioned; concerns: mortality and those who want immortality back, corruption in Teldrassil and the Dream, the Horde in Ashenvale, quarrels with Fandral)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Fandral_Staghelm (Archdruid, leads the Cenarion Circle from the Cenarion Enclave while Malfurion is lost; abrasive, hot-tempered, contentious; calls Moonglade's druids fools, distrusts Remulos, quarrels with Tyrande; drove the plan for Teldrassil without the Aspects' blessing; objected to teaching the tauren; Classic: Crown of the Earth)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Mathrengyl_Bearwalker (druid trainer in the Enclave, once Fandral's second; wise, dignified; dismisses talk of strife in the Circle and praises Fandral; Heeding the Call, Body and Heart (the Great Bear Spirit, then Lunaclaw), Lessons Anew and Power over Poison (after the problems in Auberdine), A Lesson to Learn, Aquatic Form)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Priestess_A%27moora (Sisterhood of Elune, Temple of the Moon before the Cataclysm; The Temple of the Moon, Tears of the Moon, Sathrah's Sacrifice)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gracina_Spiritmight (priestess and scholar in the Temple of the Moon; studies Azeroth's unique life and the silithid; Rise of the Silithid and later silithid quests)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Innkeeper_Saelienne (night elf innkeeper on the Craftsmen's Terrace; warm: rest, and Elune guide your dreams)
- Brief §10 (Skyborne: Eldre'Thalas, exiled by Prince Tortheldrin's purge; druids favour the elements over Elune)

## Voices
- Tyrande: commanding, formal, fierce; tired of waiting for Malfurion; dry about Fandral.
- Fandral: abrasive, impatient, proud of the tree, dismissive of Moonglade, Tyrande and non-kaldorei druids.
- Mathrengyl: calm, dignified mentor; loyal to Fandral, insists the Circle isn't divided.
- A'moora: gentle, "little star".
- Gracina: intent naturalist, unsettled by hive minds.
- Saelienne: warm innkeeper; the branches rock at night.
