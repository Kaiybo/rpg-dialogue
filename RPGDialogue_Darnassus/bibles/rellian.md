## Their quests
- Rellian Greenspyre (turn-in, from Denalan): he wanted to grow a timberling from a seed; the seeds carry their parent's taint, beyond his talents. Humble, worried, admires Denalan.
- Tumors (gives): kill the most tainted timberlings at Wellspring Lake, bring mossy tumors to be burned. Reluctant culler; wants the poison out of "our new land".
- Return to Denalan (gives): Denalan may have found the cause. Urgent, hopeful.
