# Darnassus, the unsung  (keys: garryeth, sildanair, ariyell, lelanai, jartsam, lysheana, shalumon, jaeana, dannelor, ainethil)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg (read, Classic parts only): Garryeth and Lairn (bankers, Temple Gardens), Sildanair and Darnath Bladesinger (warrior trainers, Warrior's Terrace), Ariyell Skyshadow (weapon merchant, Warrior's Terrace), Landria (bow merchant, Tradesmen's Terrace), Lelanai (saber handler) and Jartsam (riding trainer) side by side at the Cenarion Enclave, Lysheana (guild master) and Shalumon (tabard vendor) on the Craftsmen's Terrace, Jaeana (meat vendor, Tradesmen's Terrace), Dannelor (first aid trainer) and Ainethil (alchemy trainer) on the Craftsmen's Terrace. All night elves.
- Game rule used: nightsabers and frostsabers sold to night elves, or to others exalted with Darnassus; riding at level 40.

## Their quests
- none: all ten are service NPCs with no Classic quests. Built from role, place and neighbours (section 1).

## Identity
- **Garryeth** · Temple Gardens bank · male · banker (service). Dry, precise; the bank stands on the safest ground in the city.
- **Sildanair** · Warrior's Terrace · female · warrior trainer (service). Brisk drillmistress; shield before sword; the practical foil to Arias'ta's "warrior's spirit".
- **Ariyell Skyshadow** · Warrior's Terrace · female · weapon merchant (service). Cheerful, proud of fit and balance; sells the right blade, not the dear one.
- **Lelanai** · Cenarion Enclave · female · saber handler (service). Warm and protective of her cats; sells only to kaldorei or to those exalted with Darnassus.
- **Jartsam** · Cenarion Enclave · male · riding trainer (service). Easygoing, wry; teaches riders to trust the saber.
- **Lysheana** · Craftsmen's Terrace · female · guild master (service). Formal; asks what a guild is for before she writes its name.
- **Shalumon** · Craftsmen's Terrace · male · tabard vendor (service). Fussy, enthusiastic about colors.
- **Jaeana** · Tradesmen's Terrace · female · meat vendor (service). Brisk, practical; defends hunting against Enclave disapproval.
- **Dannelor** · Craftsmen's Terrace · male · first aid trainer (service). Gentle; the healer isn't always beside you.
- **Ainethil** · Craftsmen's Terrace · female · alchemy trainer (service). Precise, a little distracted; a hundred clean potions before the next lesson.
