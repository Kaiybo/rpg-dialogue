# Rut'theran's hippogryph master and two trainers  (keys: vesprystus, ilyenia, jeenra)

## Sources
- From memory of original WoW (1.x): Vesprystus, Hippogryph Master at Rut'theran Village below Teldrassil; Ilyenia Moonfire, Weapon Master in Darnassus; Jeen'ra Nightrunner, Hunter Trainer in Darnassus. Confirm with capture.

## Their quests
- none: flight master and trainers. Class quests not attached here.

## Voices (authored, not canon)
- Vesprystus: calm, old, loves the sea wind; the gate between Teldrassil and the world.
- Ilyenia: exacting, proud of the glaive and the Sentinels' discipline.
- Jeen'ra: quiet tracker, bonded to the wilds of Teldrassil.
