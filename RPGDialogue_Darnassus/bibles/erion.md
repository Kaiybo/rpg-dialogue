## The prejudice
Humans are short-lived oath-breakers who promise like they'll live forever. Blind spot: the Highborne were kaldorei and betrayed the world worse than any human; a night elf player can make him admit he never thinks of them. Softens through the [Persuade] for humans (flag `erion_soft`), which pays off in later greetings and the `xsoft` branch.

## Agenda
