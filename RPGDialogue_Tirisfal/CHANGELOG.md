# RPG Dialogue: Tirisfal Glades (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-16)
- Horde warriors and hunters get their own question and short conversation with several people here.

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-16)
- New people: Deathguard Saltain, Novice Elreth, Calvin Montague and Junior Apothecary Holland.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Replies for the classes and races these people would really react to (warriors, hunters, rogues, mages, warlocks, druids, shamans, paladins; every race of the faction, and the Skyborne), each leading somewhere.

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Every person here writes a first impression in your diary after you first talk (the journal's People page shows it too).

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.1.1 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: Deathknell and Brill now talk in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments, each in their own voice (Mordo's matter-of-fact welcome, Sarvis' sardonic edge, Arren's and Zygand's cold command, Coleman's courteous bitterness, Gretchen's chill, Renee's warmth). Same lines, same facts, same conditions.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits bring personal lines (habits, worries, running jokes), and some NPCs get night-time lines. Gretchen Dedmar.

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- Deathknell: Undertaker Mordo, Shadow Priest Sarvis, Executor Arren, and Aramis Hammerhand for Forever's Forsaken paladins. Deathknell's Deathguards.
- Brill: Executor Zygand, Magistrate Sevren, Apothecary Johaan, Coleman Farthing, Gretchen Dedmar, Innkeeper Renee, and Eleanor Shackleton (Forever's camping, on the road outside town). Brill's Deathguards.
