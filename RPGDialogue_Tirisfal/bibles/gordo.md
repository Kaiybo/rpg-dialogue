# Gordo  (key: gordo)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gordo (read: abomination; classic: patrols the road between Deathknell and Brill; reaps Gloom Weed violently)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gordo%27s_Task_(Classic) (read: quest text and Holland's completion)

## Their quests
- Gordo's Task (gives; ends with Junior Apothecary Holland): "Master want weeds. Gordo gots big hands and not good at picking." Holland wanted doom weed, not gloom weed, and is exasperated. Gordo is eager, clumsy, and loyal to a master who barely notices him.
- Doom Weed (follows from Holland): Gordo is told not to help.

## Identity
Gordo · the road between Deathknell and Brill · Undercity · abomination · weed-gatherer
Personality: simple, earnest, gentle, a little sad
Speech: calls himself "Gordo" and "us"; simple grammar ("you is", "us is") but whole run-on sentences, never stacked fragments
Knows and cares about: weeds, Master Holland, the road, the "red men" who shoot at him
Relationships: Junior Apothecary Holland (master; shouts, sometimes says thank you)
Default attitudes: likes everyone who is kind to him; tauren are "almost Gordo big"
Lore anchors: abominations are stitched from many corpses
Never says: anything cruel; never threatens except the canon "we not hurt you"
