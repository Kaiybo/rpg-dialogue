## Identities
- **Abigail Shiel** (tir_abigail) · Forsaken (female) · trade supplies. Brisk, sharp, fair; vials sell, dye doesn't; half-remembers a cloth shop with a green door.
- **Mrs. Winters** (tir_winters) · Forsaken (female) · general supplies. Warm, chatty, "dear"; keeps manners after death; keeps a little bread for living Horde.
- **Morganus** (tir_morganus) · Forsaken (male) · stable master. Gruff, steady; misses living horses that knew his voice.

## Topics
- trade (Abigail), scarlet_crusade (cart ambushes).
