- Yvette: A Letter Undelivered (turn-in). Mentions Coleman's Deaths in the Family / The Haunted Mills.

## Voices
**Yvette Farthing.** Cold, proud, bitten-off grief; "regrets are for the weak"; lets a little through about Thurman.
**Ageron Kargal.** Dry, precise; demons as hounds on a leash; teaches demons "manners".
**Caretaker Caice.** Solemn and odd; speaks of the risen's freedom; keeps his door shut.
**Deathguard Terrence.** Wary road-walker; never stops; distrusts the Dawn unsupervised.
**Deathguard Cyrus.** Stiff, proper, recites directions; duty is what's left.
**Deathguard Lawrence.** Leans, grumbles, near the top of Zygand's ledger.
**Deathguard Mort.** Gloomy, likes his upstairs window; Garren's Haunt watched him back.
**Deathguard Royann.** Sardonic cellar guard; sends drinkers back upstairs with a glare.
**Eliza Callen.** Brisk; judges by boots; hides from Glades beasts and Crusade scrap.
**Oliver Dwor.** Earnest apprentice with no master left; plain work, good edges.
**Hamlin Atkins.** Cheerful rambler; barley once, mushrooms now; uneasy about apothecaries.
**Blacksmith Rand.** Gruff, patient; melts Crusader helms into Forsaken greaves.
**Abe Winters.** Mild, fond of Mrs. Winters; learning smithing late.

## Authored, not canon
- Yvette meant to marry Thurman; Coleman stayed when the Agamands asked (squares her letter with his quests loosely); Brand left unexplained.
- Ageron studied quietly in Lordaeron; imps singe tablecloths.
- Lawrence's leaning; Mort moved upstairs by Zygand; Royann's closing-time drinkers.
- Oliver's master stayed dead; Hamlin farmed barley past the Brightwater; Rand's Crusader scrap; Abe was no smith before the Plague, the forgotten wedding "was lovely" (matches tir_winters).
