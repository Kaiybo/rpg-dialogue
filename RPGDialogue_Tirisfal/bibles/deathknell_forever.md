# Deathknell, Forever-only folk  (file: npcs/deathknell_forever.lua)

Keys: tf_clarence, tf_florence, tf_margaret, tf_walter. All Forsaken, Undercity, Deathknell; added in Forever 1.60.1.

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Clarence_Gillian (read: tradeskill guide; gossip on what professions are, primary vs secondary, two primary max, dropping one loses it, directions to trainers, "you can always return here")
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Florence_Nightshade (read: Junior Herbalism Trainer; "may be related to Vera Nightshade")
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Margaret_Weaver (read: Junior Skinning Trainer; sells Skinning Knife, Salt, Coarse Thread, skinning satchel; gossip "Care to learn how to gather leather from slain beasts? Or do you need some new tools?")
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Walter_Mason (read: Junior Mining Trainer; sells Mining Pick, mining pack, Blacksmith Hammer; gossip "How can I help you?")
- Classic Tirisfal: peacebloom, silverleaf, earthroot, copper; duskbats, Night Web's Hollow spiders.

## Their quests
- none: all four are trainers/guides with no quests. Built from role, place and neighbours.

## Identity
- **Clarence Gillian** (service, role citizen): eager, warm, stubbornly cheerful guide. Speaks in whole friendly sentences ("I'm glad you asked!"). Believes learning a trade proves the Forsaken own themselves again. Points to Florence, Walter, Margaret, then Brill and the Undercity.
- **Florence Nightshade** (service): brisk, particular, loves plants more than pickers; scolds root-tearing. Sells to the Royal Apothecary Society and tries not to think about what for.
- **Margaret Weaver** (service): gentle, patient, firm about waste; dry joke that the dead are good at keeping things from rotting.
- **Walter Mason** (service): cheerful, talks copper; sells the hammer because new miners always come back wanting the forge.
- Never says: anything about the Year-1 apothecary plague plot beyond hints; no promises of discounts.

## Agenda
Get the newly risen working and feeling like themselves.

## Secrets

- Florence: craft (herbalism). Margaret: pet, craft (skinning). Walter: race (dwarf), craft (mining).

background, advice, _none for each.

## Stances
- Clarence choose: planner (order +1) / take what comes (order -1).
- Florence buyers: poisons for enemies (light -1) / poultices (light +1).
- Margaret trouble: use all of it, respect (light +1) / resource only (light -1).
- Walter buyers: arm the Forsaken (order +1) / sell to highest bidder (order -1).

## Authored, not canon
- Clarence woke in Deathknell "like everyone else"; his "work proves we're ours" view.
- Florence: "Nightshade" is just a family name and she has never picked the plant; she can't smell herbs any more; Tirisfal herbs pale and slow to rot.
- Margaret: Deathguard wear out boots; declines to talk about her past.
- Walter: his grandfather was a stonemason; mine the vein from the edges; copper in Night Web's Hollow; Tirisfal copper ends up on the Bulwark's walls.
