# Tirisfal, more people  (keys: saltain, elreth, montague, holland)

## Sources (warcraft.wiki.gg, Classic sections)
- Deathguard_Saltain, Scavenging_Deathknell: Deathknell guard who sends new Forsaken to gather scraps of cloth and gear from the ruined town.
- Novice_Elreth, The_Damned, Marla's_Last_Wish: Deathknell novice; the Damned are the mindless undead in the fields; Marla Fowler's grave at Solliden Farmstead, and her husband Samuel Fipps among the Scarlets.
- Calvin_Montague, A_Rogue's_Deal: a living human outside Deathknell who picks a fight with a new Forsaken and pays up if beaten.
- Junior_Apothecary_Holland, Doom_Weed: Brill apothecary's assistant who wants doom weed gathered from the fields.

## Their quests
- Saltain: Scavenging Deathknell.
- Elreth: The Damned, Marla's Last Wish.
- Montague: A Rogue's Deal.
- Holland: Doom Weed.

## Never
- Cataclysm Tirisfal (Agatha, the val'kyr, Lilian Voss); anything after Classic.

## Authored, not canon
- Saltain's tidy piles; Elreth's doubts about the Damned; Montague's wounded pride; Holland's ambitions in the Society.
