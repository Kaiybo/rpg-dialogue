npcs/brill_folk.lua. One quest giver and the Gallows' End trainers, riding trainer, herbalist and two shopkeepers.

## Their quests
- Burgess: Proof of Demise (before/during/after lines; also reacts to At War With The Scarlet Crusade done).
- Beryl: In Favor of Darkness (turn-in), Garments of Darkness (priests).
- Cain Firesong: Speak with Anastasia (mages). Marion Call: Mennet Carkad (rogues).
- Others: none.

## Voices
- Burgess: proud, zealous, a little menacing; writes down the names of anyone whose loyalty is quiet. Speaks of Varimathras with plain respect (no hint of anything later).
- Beryl: calm, devout Forgotten Shadow priest; pity for the Light rather than anger.
- Cain: wry, a touch vain; the scorch marks, Renee's bucket.
- Marion: sly, low-voiced, loyal to Carkad; never interrupts.
- Velma: brisk and practical with a soft spot for the horses.
- Mrs. Winters: warm, motherly ("dear"), keeps her living habits.
- Abigail: brisk ledger-keeper. Faruza: quiet, careful, gloves on.

## Never
- Varimathras's later betrayal or the Wrathgate; the New Plague's purpose spelled out (Faruza only finds the apothecaries' interest unsettling).

## Authored, not canon
- Burgess's brother-in-law among the Crusaders at Solliden; the rings sent to the Undercity in a sealed box; he keeps a list of muttering deathguards.
- Beryl's village died of the plague. Cain studied three winters in Dalaran and set the Magistrate's curtains alight. Marion once killed a Brill smith who talked to the Crusade, for Carkad.
- Velma's horses stopping at field gates. Mrs. Winters is married to Abe Winters (the Brill armorer); both died in the same week and rose a month apart. Mrs. Winters's morning crust at the window.
- Abigail's ledger of debts; goods off looted Crusade wagons. Faruza once picked a plague-tainted plant and the apothecaries came asking; Holland still asks.
