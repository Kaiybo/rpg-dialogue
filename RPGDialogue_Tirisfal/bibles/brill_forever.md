## Their quests
- Continue Your Training (Shari turn-in, from Aramis): welcome to Brill, find work, grow in the Light, return for training; Aramis warns that many in the Dark Lady's service distrust paladins.
- A Second Home (Shari gives, Forsaken paladin, level 8): "community and shared faith"; a new order in Tirisfal; Bandarion Keep on the bluffs NW of Solliden Farmstead, edge of the Whispering Woods; report to Breton Samuels.
- Seeking Refuge (Shari turn-in, from Bareth Dawnstone): Bareth captured carrying a message to her; Scarlets wield the Light with hatred; "Our order is still taking root".
- Camping 101: Cooking (Pickman turn-in, from Eleanor Shackleton): the campfire is the foundation of a camp; raise cooking for bigger fires; other trainers teach furnishing.
- Angie: none (no quests, no gossip); built from Brill's place and neighbours.

## Identity
- **Shari Stilwell** (quest tier, trainer): steady, devout, recruits for Danitha Morr's order; the Light burns her and she calls it a fever of mending. Knows Aramis, Breton, Danitha, Garek's story. Careful about Forsaken mistrust.
- **William Pickman** (service): cheerful cook who can't taste; cooks by memory and watching faces; feeds the living off the zeppelin.
- **Angie Cilas** (service): dry town-watcher; knows who comes and goes; undecided about the paladins.
- Never says: anything later than Year 1; no promises of free food.

## Agenda
- Shari: earn Forsaken trust for the paladins; send new paladins to the Keep.

## Secrets
- Shari (authored): doubts every morning whether the Light really answers; told only on a Persuade success.
