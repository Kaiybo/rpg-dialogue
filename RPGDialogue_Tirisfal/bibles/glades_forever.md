## Their quests
- The Argent Emissary (Hadric turn-in, from Deathguard Terrence): Terrence distrusts the Dawn unsupervised; "Stay low and try not to be seen".
- Remnants of War (Hadric gives): Naxxramas bore down on the Undercity (Forsaken Kingdom); its necrotic focusing crystal shattered over the fields; both Forsaken and Dawn lost much; "pulling these crystals out of the ground for years".
- The Cult of the Damned (Hadric gives): "special disgust for living servants of the Scourge"; "if we do not wish to see Lordaeron's history repeat itself".
- Bandarion Keep (Hadric gives): he and his commander were headed to the Keep; meet Leonid there (Dawn's Watch; Danitha named it for Garek).
- Patience (Kristof is an objective): his report says new Scourge are being made inside the borders; "Come see me again... a willing blade".
- Discipline (Kristof, Lizabetha are targets): Zygand's motivator; Deathguard reply "There is no escape, even in death" etc.
- The Wrath of Rath'mael (Kristof gives): unholy fog at Lordamere Overlook in the Ruins of Lordaeron; necromancer Rath'mael; "something of his kind will be replaced".
- A Lesson in Divinity (5) (Falgan turn-in): Billmuth stepped on a twig; Falgan stayed; raised by a paladin with a Symbol of Life, not seen "since before the war".
- A Lesson in Divinity (6) (Falgan gives): Scarlet attack plans on Brill (zeppelin tower fire, attack from the north, Magistrate and civilians targeted).
- Deyrek, Relreo, Adelbert, Rickard, Lizabetha: none of their own; built from place and neighbours.

## Identity
- **Hadric Harlson** (quest): steady, devout Argent Forsaken; cold toward cultists; treats undeath as Leonid does on good days. Notices Forsaken paladins (Danitha's knights) and living Horde.
- **Deathguard Kristof** (quest): grim, sharp, sure the enemy is inside; spars with Dillinger's view; dry about Zygand's motivator. Gives directions.
- **Deathguard Lizabetha** (service, guard): clipped, dutiful, sour about the motivator and about people who don't read signposts.
- **Deathguard Falgan** (quest): wry, brave, embarrassed by his own heroism; rethinking paladins.
- **Deyrek Duke** (service): dry zeppelin-watcher who won't ride one.
- **Relreo Emberlight** (service): gentle hermit; living dog Max; feels warm near the Keep without wielding the Light.
- **Adelbert Edmunds / Rickard Hardee** (service): a bickering pair of old watchmen at the Bulwark; Rickard half-blind and the better watcher.

## Agenda
- Hadric: deprive the cult of crystals; bring the Keep's paladins closer to the Dawn (Danitha declines).
- Kristof: prove the Scourge are being made inside Tirisfal.

## Secrets
- Hadric (authored): lost a newly risen squad at a gate during the Naxxramas battle; revealed on Persuade.

## Reliability
- Kristof vs Dillinger is a real disagreement (Patience reports); neither is settled here.
- Adelbert's "walked into a door" story about Rickard's eye is a joke; truth left open.
