## Their quests
- A Putrid Task (gives/turn-in): Scourge trickling in west of Brill near the old bridge; he regrets not being there to see them smashed. Wants to be in the fight, not on the post.
- The Mills Overrun (gives/turn-in): the Scourge trying to make a base at Agamand Mills; "I will not thank you for doing what is necessary". Dutiful, sparing with praise, but remembers names.
- Speak with Dillinger (turn-in, warrior quest from Austil de Mon) and Ulag the Cleaver (gives): a captured Scourge with a free mind but still enthralled; the Deathguard tried to break the Lich King's hold and failed. The thin line between Ulag and every Forsaken bothers him.

## Identity
Deathguard Dillinger · Brill, west end by the graveyard · Undercity / Deathguard · Forsaken · soldier
Personality: dutiful, terse, restless, quietly troubled
Speech: soldier's plain sentences; "the west road", "the Mills"; never says thank you for duty
Knows and cares about: the west road, Agamand Mills, the bridge, Ulag, Brill's graves
Relationships: Austil de Mon sends him warriors; Zygand commands the Deathguard; points to Coleman Farthing for the Agamands' story
Default attitudes: living allies are welcome but should keep to the main street; warriors get his respect first
Lore anchors: Scourge at Agamand Mills; Ulag the Cleaver
Never says: anything about the Forsaken's future beyond Year 1
