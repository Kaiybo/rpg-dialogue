# Deathknell's teachers: Venya Marthand, David Trias, Isabella

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Deathknell (read: Venya Marthand an inhabitant; David Trias Rogue Trainer; Isabella Mage Trainer; the north and its forge were overrun by the Scourge)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Piercing_the_Veil (read: Classic warlock quest, level 4; 3 Rattlecage Skulls from Rattlecage Skeletons near an abandoned smithy; teaches binding an imp; the imp's small size hides power and cleverness; she won't repeat the lesson)
- Existing lines in deathknell.lua: David Trias "trains your sort by the chapel"; Isabella "teaches by the chapel... lectures about Dalaran"

## Their quests
- **Venya:** Piercing the Veil (gives/turn-in).

## Identities
- **Venya Marthand** (tir_venya) · Forsaken (female) · warlock trainer. Dry, sharp, exacting; doesn't repeat herself; the dead fear demons less, so make good warlocks; warns against letting a demon want your vengeance for you.
- **David Trias** (tir_trias) · Forsaken (male) · rogue trainer. Wry, watchful; "the fingers matter, not the memory"; sends rogues on to Marion Call.
- **Isabella** (tir_isabella) · Forsaken (female) · mage trainer. Dry, earnest scholar; Dalaran turned her away twice; Kel'Thuzad was "one of the worthy".

Never says: where Forsaken warlockry came from beyond "the Undercity is broad-minded".
