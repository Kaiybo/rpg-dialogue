# Deathknell's other residents  (keys: venya, kayla_smithe, kava)

npcs/deathknell_folk.lua.

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Deathknell (roster)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Venya_Marthand and Piercing_the_Veil (Classic only, removed 3.3.0: warlock quest; bring 3 Rattlecage Skulls from near the abandoned smithy to learn Summon Imp; she calls the player "child"; knowledge as power over the void)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kayla_Smithe (demon trainer with an Imp Minion), Archibald_Kava (cloth and leather armor merchant)

## Their quests
- Venya: Piercing the Veil (warlocks only; before/during/after).
- Kayla, Kava: none.

## Voices
- Venya: measured, patient, faintly maternal and faintly cold; "child"; knowledge, will, doors kept small.
- Kayla: wry and fond of her bad-tempered imp.
- Kava: dry tailor's humor, quietly kind.

## Authored, not canon
- A warlock in the Dark Lady's service found Venya in the graves and asked what she wanted; she studies the Scourge's binding to understand what was done to her.
- Kayla's imp has been with her since she rose; it steals her ink and has cost her two grimoires and a pair of boots.
- Kava unpicks the red from Scarlet coats; Novice Elreth sends him the risen who have gone quiet, and he finds them a hat.
