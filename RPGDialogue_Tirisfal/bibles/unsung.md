# Tirisfal's unsung  (keys: dannal, duesten, kien, boch, carolai, neela, zachariah)

People with no quests and little or no gossip in the game: trainers and shopkeepers. Written from memory of the vanilla world; no wiki page read in this pass.

## Sources
- Vanilla 1.12 rosters (memory): Dannal Stern (Warrior Trainer), Dark Cleric Duesten (Priest Trainer), Joshua Kien (General Goods) in Deathknell; Rupert Boch (Warlock Trainer), Carolai Anise (Alchemy Trainer), Nurse Neela (First Aid Trainer), Zachariah Post (Undead Horse Merchant) in Brill.
- bibles/tirisfal.md for Arren, Sarvis, Aramis Hammerhand (Forever paladins), Johaan, Renee, the Scarlet Crusade, the Rattlecages and the mindless dead.

## Their quests
- None of their own (class trainers receive the starting-zone class letters only; not written).

## Identity and voices
- Dannal: grim ex-soldier of Lordaeron; teaches the risen to check their own bodies since they feel no pain; loyal to Sylvanas because she came for him (authored, not canon).
- Duesten: cold, proud Cult of Forgotten Shadows priest; faith in the will; died "between two prayers" (authored, not canon); disdains the Light, wary of Forever's undead paladins.
- Joshua Kien: dry shopkeeper; the dead eat to help the flesh knit; kept a candle-and-thread shop near the capital in life (authored, not canon).
- Rupert Boch: smooth, amused warlock; "a demon is a contract with teeth".
- Carolai Anise: brisk alchemist; practical medicine; keeps out of the Apothecary Society's business.
- Nurse Neela: warm, firm nurse; nursed plague victims in life (authored, not canon); linen from the Crusade.
- Zachariah Post: dry horse merchant; skeletal horses for the Forsaken (others need Undercity reputation), level 40 riding as in vanilla.

## Never
- The New Plague's true purpose spelled out (dramatic irony only), Wrathgate or anything later.

## What they notice
- Trainers: own class, wounds; Duesten: the Light on paladins; Boch: a warlock's demon; Carolai: herbalists; Zachariah: mounts and purses.
