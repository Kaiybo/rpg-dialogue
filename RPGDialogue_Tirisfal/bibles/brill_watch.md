## Their quests
- **Simmer:** Fields of Grief (gives). Johaan wants the plague-soaked pumpkins (Johaan's own line in brill.lua).
- **Burgess:** Proof of Demise (gives/turn-in). Loyal to Varimathras and the Dark Lady.
- **Linnea:** Forsaken Duties (turn-in, from Sevren), Return to the Magistrate (gives), Rear Guard Patrol (gives/turn-in).
- **Zealot:** none.

## Identities
- **Deathguard Simmer** (tir_simmer) · Brill, west road · Forsaken (male). Dry, gruff, secretly amused; thinks he was a farmer because his hands know pumpkins. Calls the risen "corpse".
- **Deathguard Burgess** (tir_burgess) · Brill · Forsaken (male). Fierce, proud, devoted to the Dark Lady and Varimathras; "she cut the strings". Rings count better than ears.
- **Deathguard Linnea** (tir_linnea) · east road toward the Bulwark · Forsaken (female). Steady, wry, tired; knows Sevren skims reports; would rather end the horrors herself than leave them to the Crusade.
- **Captured Scarlet Zealot** (tir_zealot) · Brill · Human, Scarlet Crusade. Fierce and frightened; prays at the dead; refuses to give names.

Never says: anything of Varimathras's later betrayal or what the New Plague is really for (no Wrathgate hints); nothing of Cataclysm Tirisfal.

## Authored, not canon
- Simmer: keeps the "west road"; his farmer's hands; the mindless at Solliden still pulling weeds; Zygand has more Crusade than Deathguard, so they bleed them slowly.
- Burgess: rings go to the Undercity in a box and Varimathras looks at each; he may have known Crusaders in life.
- Linnea: she was one of the mindless "out east" before Brill was retaken; burning Balnir was refused because "the Crusade burns farms and we don't"; Scourge slip through the woods south of the wall and along the river. Her post's exact place is left vague (retail puts her at a Cataclysm-era waystation).
- Zealot: the apothecaries bring a new potion every few days; a sister at the monastery.
