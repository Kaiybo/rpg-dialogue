# Bandarion Keep  (keys: danitha, breton, jorin, ephram, bareth, hilda, angus, ander, billmuth, evin)

The Forever-only order of Forsaken paladins on the bluffs northwest of the Solliden Farmstead, plus Bareth Dawnstone, held captive at the Crusader's Outpost. File: `npcs/bandarion_keep.lua`. Aramis Hammerhand (Deathknell, `deathknell.lua`, bible `tirisfal.md`) is Angus's kin and the first step on the same paladin road.

## Their quests
- Touring the Grounds (Breton gives, Danitha takes; Hilda, Jorin, Ander are objectives): the gossip of each is their self-portrait. Danitha: "the closest thing this keep has to a commander".
- A Second Home (Shari Stilwell gives, Breton takes): "Welcome to Bandarion Keep, brother/sister." Breton is the welcomer.
- Murlocs at the Gates (Breton): the Vile Fin spawned here before the paladins drove them out; rebuilding is hard enough without spears in the back.
- Making Repairs (Jorin): far from the Undercity, a dire shortage of materials; "location is everything... an unfortunate oversight on our part". Will send Breton and recruits to clear the cellar.
- The Tarnished (Danitha): risen paladins who kept their prejudices and kill Forsaken; it pains her to fight old brothers; trust may take "years, or decades".
- A Token of Good Faith (Danitha gives): Gelhardt's head to Sylvanas, who "all but openly disparages us"; Sylvanas: "We will be watching."
- A Lesson in Divinity 1-7 (Danitha; Billmuth 4-7): divinity is "the pursuit of good itself, for good's sake"; Tanis the beggar; the Symbol of Life; the priests waved Billmuth away; Falgan raised; Scarlet plans to burn Brill's zeppelin tower; "win the hearts and minds of our fellow Forsaken".
- Diplomatic Incident (Danitha): missing Earthen Ring dignitaries in Silverpine; Trevan Rol at the Sepulcher; a chance at legitimacy with the Horde.
- A Righteous Cause (Leonid): Danitha refuses the Argent Dawn; rejects the idea that being Forsaken is a condition to be cured; "That is why it is so important that we stay."
- The One That Got Away (Ephram): a cultist fled with a piece of a focusing crystal from near the Naxxramas battlefield; somewhere under Shadowvale; on turn-in, "We SMASH IT WITH A HAMMER!"
- As Above, So Below (Hilda): Scourge stirring in the Shadowvale cellars; glowing bones to study; "your body on a stretcher"; "We might just make something out of you yet."
- Seeking Refuge (Bareth): captured en route to Shari in Brill; Crusaders wield the Light with hatred; "to try me for my crimes, of existing at all"; "a small matter to attend to first".
- Discipline (Zygand; Billmuth is a valid target): the Executor's motivator.

## Identity
- **Danitha Morr** (major): leader, former Knight of the Silver Hand; friend of Garek Bandarion and Leonid; found Cormack. Warm, steady, certain, politically careful. Speaks in full, measured sentences; "brother/sister".
- **Breton Samuels**: the greeter and murloc-fighter; kindly, tired, earnest.
- **Jorin Croge**: provisioner, "something of a quartermaster"; dry, put-upon, careful.
- **Ephram Barbaro**: stable master; "Oi"; gruff, blunt, loves hammers, distrusts scholars.
- **Bareth Dawnstone**: young paladin, captive; earnest, fierce; "My pain is my strength."
- **Hilda the Breaker**: paladin trainer; drill-sergeant; dry menace; secretly warm.
- **Angus Hammerhand**: blacksmith, kin to Aramis; steady, dry, salvage-minded.
- **Ander Solliden**: lookout from the Solliden family; quiet, sad, steady.
- **Deathguard Billmuth**: nervous visitor; run-on apologetic sentences; loyal to Falgan.
- **Evin Schmidtt**: "Keeper of Bandarion Keep"; fussy, fond of the old building.
Default attitudes: all are Forsaken loyalists who know the Forsaken mistrust paladins. Never says: anything after Year 1; never claims Sylvanas's favour; no cure for undeath talk beyond Leonid's offer.

## Authored, not canon
- Danitha: the Light came easily in life and she took it for granted; tried to redeem the first Tarnished and lost two brothers doing it; argued with Garek; found Cormack "at his own grave" (the story has him at his grave, but her finding him there is our framing); her fear that Sylvanas will end the order.
- Breton: woke in a field near Brill with his shield strapped on; Shari Stilwell found him; murlocs once stabbed the keep's cook.
- Jorin: was a clerk in the Lordaeron army quartermaster's office; holds the Light "a little".
- Ephram: kept horses at Dawn's Watch in life; his old horse Cinder waiting in the yard; once tried to shoe a ghoul (Hilda's claim); he and Angus agree only about hammers.
- Bareth: a squire who never took his vows; Crusader "testing" with holy water; the "small matter" was leaving something for a farmhand who gave him water.
- Hilda: broke warhorses for the Silver Hand's stables in life; three trainees "couldn't stop a murloc with a door".
- Angus: Aramis is his brother or near kin who grew up with him ("since we were boys", "our father's forge"); salvage sources.
- Ander: the living Farmer Solliden is his kin (wiki lists Farmer Solliden only as a relative); keeps buttons and spoons from the furrows.
- Billmuth: "in the Executor's ledger twice"; spreading word among the Deathguard (canon intent, our phrasing).
- Evin: was a groundskeeper in life and holds the Light; murloc spawn in the barracks; what "keeper" means.
