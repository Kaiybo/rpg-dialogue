## Their quests
- Return the Book (turn-in from Bethor): "So you were the thief"; recognises Bethor's enchantment. Suspicious first, then astonished.
- Proving Allegiance (gives/turn-in): Lillith Nefara, a Scourge agent at the altar off his southern shore. He needs proof before trust; "I had believed I was the only Undead with free will".
- The Prodigal Lich Returns (gives): a Nether Gem for Bethor so they can talk. Lonely; wants his friend back.
