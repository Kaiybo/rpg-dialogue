## Their quests
- Maximillion: Tainted Scroll (ends with him). Kel: reacts to Garments of Darkness (Beryl's). Captured Mountaineer: reacts to A New Plague (Johaan's). The rest have none.

## Identity / voice
- Deathguard Bartrand · Forsaken man · dry; counts everyone on the hill.
- Deathguard Oliver · Forsaken man · wry, gallows humour; old Lordaeron garrison.
- Deathguard Phillip · Forsaken man · stern, no patience; watches for red tabards.
- Deathguard Randolph · Forsaken man · calm, slow, the old hand; tells the newly risen they are free.
- Deathguard Lundmark · Forsaken man · brisk; directions given like orders, never stops walking.
- Deathguard Kel · Forsaken man · gruff, wounded, won't leave his field.
- Harold Raims · Forsaken man · dry and earnest apprentice; plain steel.
- Maquell Ebonwood · Forsaken woman · cold, sharp, keeps up standards.
- Maximillion · Forsaken man · smooth, cold, enjoys being right; formal, no contractions when lecturing.
- Captured Mountaineer · dwarf man · defiant burr; warms only to the Alliance.
- Ratslin Maime · Forsaken man · watchful, answers with questions.
- Zapetta · goblin man · brisk pitchman of the Orgrimmar line.
- Hin Denburg · goblin man · jovial, terrified of sparks.

## Authored, not canon
- Oliver served on a wall for Terenas; Randolph was old when he died; Lundmark carried messages in life.
- Kel's wound from a darkhound; Burgess has the town, Kel the field.
- Harold's master never woke; he borrows Rand's forge.
- Maquell kept a respectable house in Tirisfal. No role is given for her on the wiki.
- Maximillion's wager with Kayla. (deathknell_folk.lua calls him "Maximillion Crowe"; the in-game name is only "Maximillion", so no surname is used here.)
- The mountaineer was carrying word north when taken; he gives no name.
- Ratslin as Brill's listener (the wiki gives him no role in Classic).
- Nez'raz owes Zapetta twelve silver; Hin's fear of fire (a nod to his name).
