- **Faruza:** none.

## Identities
- **Dark Cleric Beryl** (tir_beryl) · Forsaken (female) · Cult of Forgotten Shadow. Calm, cold, exact; the Light is a tool, "men burned us, not the Light"; argues theory with Sarvis.
- **Cain Firesong** (tir_cain) · Forsaken (male) · mage trainer at the Gallows' End. Chatty, vain, quick; chose the name because fire was the first spell he remembered; blames Dalaran's pride.
- **Marion Call** (tir_marion) · Forsaken (female) · rogue trainer at the Gallows' End. Smooth, watchful; Sevren's ears; distrusts a courier's clean boots (Astor Hadren, Silverpine).
- **Faruza** (tir_faruza) · Forsaken (female, assumed) · herbalism trainer. Calm, earnest; sells to Johaan and Holland without asking; likes to be near living things.

Never says: what the apothecaries' plague is for.
