## Identity / voice
- Adon · high elf man · proud, wry, formal; talks of the sealed gate as a late ship. Polite to everyone, sad about it with no one.

## Authored, not canon
- That he was abroad when Arthas invaded and came home to find the gate shut; that he called to the far side for weeks; the contents of the trunk (books, clothes, a bottle of Silvermoon wine).
- Community `thalassian_pass` is new.

## Never
