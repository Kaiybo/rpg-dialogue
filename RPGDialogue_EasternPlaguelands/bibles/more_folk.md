# Anthion Harmon  (key: anthion)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Anthion_Harmon (read 2026-11-18 via tools/npc.py): male human ghost outside Stratholme; added 1.10.0; seen with the Extra-Dimensional Ghost Revealer; part of the Dungeon Set 2 upgrade chain ("the Strat 45").
- Quest pages read: Dead Man's Plea, Proof of Life, Anthion's Strange Request, Anthion's Old Friend.

## Their quests
- In Search of Anthion (turn-in): he is found as a ghost by Stratholme's gate.
- Dead Man's Plea (gives): "My beloved wife has been taken prisoner by Rivendare's undead scum. I strongly objected to her joining the Argent Dawn's foolish crusade"; rescue Ysida. Grief and guilt; love before everything.
- Proof of Life (turn-in, from Ysida): "That locket... you found her! I can rest in peace at long last... my time in this world is not long."
- Anthion's Strange Request (gives): Valthalak's medallion divided with the spoils; Theldren took the first piece and beat him; Theldren a Dark Iron gladiator. Materials: Dark Iron Bars, Enchanted Leather, Mooncloth, Cured Rugged Hide.
- Anthion's Old Friend (gives): banner in Theldren's family colours; Falrin Treeshaper in Dire Maul's library "has a particular way of... aggravating people".
