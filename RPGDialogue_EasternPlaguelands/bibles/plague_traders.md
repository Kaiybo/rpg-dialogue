# Plague traders  (keys: augustus, breechlock)

npcs/plague_traders.lua.

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Augustus_the_Touched (insane Forsaken fungus vendor in Terrordale; was staying at the inn when the Scourge came and never left; "he is insane, and so are his prices"; bought and sold everything; Egan lives in his house per bibles/egan.md). His quests (Just a Little Touched, A Fate Worse Than Butchery, Augustus' Receipt Book) were added in 4.0.3 and are not used.
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Quartermaster_Miranda_Breechlock (dwarf, Argent Dawn quartermaster at Light's Hope Chapel; Classic-only reputation turn-ins)

## Their quests
- None used. Augustus reacts to Egan's The Restless Souls.

## Voices
- Augustus: manic, delighted, every sentence a sales pitch; never threatening.
- Breechlock: gruff dwarf ("ye"), counts everything, plain about the war.

## Never
- Augustus's Cataclysm quests or the Argent Crusade; Naxxramas, valor tokens, the Scourge invasion.

## Authored, not canon
- Augustus was upstairs at the inn counting stock when Terrordale fell; Egan pays him "in conversation".
- Breechlock was sent by Ironforge when the Dawn called for hands; a cousin died of the plague near Hearthglen; she writes the tally at the bottom of every page.
