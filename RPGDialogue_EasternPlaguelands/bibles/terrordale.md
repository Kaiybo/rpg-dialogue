# Augustus the Touched  (key: augustus)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Augustus_the_Touched (read 2026-11-17: undead fungus vendor in Terrordale; "insane, and so are his prices"; was staying at the inn when the Scourge came and has been there ever since; sells cave mold, dried king bolete, A Crazy Grab Bag)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Augustus'_Receipt_Book_(quest) (read: fetch his receipt book from his room at the inn in Terrordale; "my prices are INSANE"; afterwards he does business)
- bibles/egan.md (Egan lives in Augustus's house)

## Their quests
- Augustus' Receipt Book (gives/turn-in): he can't open shop without it; the book is who he was. Wants: to be a merchant again. Proud of: his prices, absurdly.

## Identity
Augustus the Touched · Terrordale, Eastern Plaguelands · neutral · undead (free-willed) · fungus vendor
Personality: manic, cheerful, scattered, oddly honourable, sad underneath
Speech: run-on excited sentences, repeats himself for joy, calls everything insane; never stacked fragments
Knows and cares about: his mushrooms, his book, the inn, Egan
Relationships: Egan (lodger in his back room)
Default attitudes: delighted by any customer; wary of priests' prayers; refuses queens and forms
Lore anchors: the plague reaching Terrordale; the Scourge's call
Never says: anything sinister on purpose

## Authored, not canon
- He was a travelling merchant on the road to Stratholme with a cart and a mule (the mule "went north with the Scourge").
- He stayed "for the bill"; the innkeeper walked off with the ghouls.
- He remembers faces at the inn (a tinker with a red scarf, two soldiers, a girl who sang) but not names.
- He heard the Scourge's call and refused it because he "had a shop to run".
- The Undercity once invited him to swear oaths and he declined.
- The Argent folk bought one bolete and never returned.
- The grab bag holds things from empty rooms.

## Threads he could carry
- none.
## Agenda
- Open his shop again; keep his mind the shape it is.
## Secrets
- He knows exactly what happened to the inn; the madness is how he stands it (implied, never stated).
## Reliability
- Honest about prices, unreliable about everything else.
## What they notice
- Forsaken (kin), hunger (low health), herbalists.
## Topics
- background, advice, stratholme

# Eris Havenfire  (key: eris)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Eris_Havenfire (read: human priest ghost on the mountain path between Terrordale and Stratholme; Church of the Holy Light; priest-only quest giver for Benediction; added 1.4.0, removed 4.0.3a)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Balance_of_Light_and_Shadow_(quest) (read: save 50 peasants cut down fleeing Stratholme before 15 are slain; "you must do what I could not"; "our cursed existence shall continue"; reward Splinter of Nordrassil)

## Their quests
- The Balance of Light and Shadow (gives/turn-in, priests only): she failed the fleeing peasants; she and they are cursed until someone saves them. Fears: the curse never lifting. Wants: a priest who holds Light and shadow in balance.

## Identity
Eris Havenfire · Terrordale mountains above Stratholme · neutral · human ghost · priestess
Personality: grave, gentle, self-blaming, honest, tired
Speech: quiet, formal, full sentences; speaks to priests as kin
Knows and cares about: the refugees on the road; the balance of Light and shadow
Relationships: none named
Default attitudes: priests are hers to ask; others are welcome company; paladins remind her the Silver Hand wasn't there
Lore anchors: the plague and the purge of Stratholme
Never says: Molten Core, Majordomo or the Eye of Divinity by name (Forever is set before Molten Core; the staff's parts stay "two eyes" / "a staff made whole")

## Authored, not canon
- She was set to see the refugees safely down the road; she stopped counting the dead at fifty.
- A post by the road where their names "would be".
- Argent Dawn patrols sometimes come up the mountain and some see her.
- She had "the Light without the shadow" and didn't know what to do with grief.

## Threads he could carry
- none.
## Agenda
- Find a priest to break the curse.
## Secrets
- none beyond canon.
## Reliability
- Honest; harsh only on herself.
## What they notice
- Shadowform priests; Forsaken.
## Topics
- background, advice, argent_dawn
