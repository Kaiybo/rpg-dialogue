# Eastern Plaguelands, Darrowshire and Light's Hope  (keys: carlin, pamela, smokey, betina)

## Sources (warcraft.wiki.gg, Classic sections)
- Carlin_Redpath, Villains_of_Darrowshire, Heroes_of_Darrowshire, Marauders_of_Darrowshire, The_Battle_of_Darrowshire: brother of Joseph and Marlene, survivor of Darrowshire, at Light's Hope Chapel; wants the villains' skull and the heroes remembered, and finally the battle relived through Chromie's work.
- Pamela_Redpath, Pamela's_Doll, Auntie_Marlene, Uncle_Carlin: ghost of Joseph's little daughter in ruined Darrowshire; wants her doll mended and her aunt and uncle found.
- Smokey_LaRue, Smokey's_Special_Compound: dwarf at Light's Hope who wants Scourge parts and Argent Dawn work, with a gruff manner.
- Betina_Bigglezink, Dawn's_Gambit: gnome of the Argent Dawn at Light's Hope; alchemical device to purge Scholomance's viewing room.

## Their quests
- Carlin: Villains of Darrowshire, Heroes of Darrowshire, Marauders of Darrowshire, The Battle of Darrowshire.
- Pamela: Pamela's Doll, Auntie Marlene, Uncle Carlin.
- Smokey: Smokey's Special Compound.
- Betina: Dawn's Gambit.

## Never
- Death Knights, the Ebon Blade, anything after Classic at Light's Hope.

## Authored, not canon
- Carlin's guilt at surviving; Pamela's view of the "pale people"; Smokey's pipe; Betina's opinion of Scholomance's architecture.
