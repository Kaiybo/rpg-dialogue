# Eastern Plaguelands  (keys: tyrosus, leonid, tirion, nathanos)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Light's_Hope_Chapel (a Church of the Holy Light chapel in the Eastweald, later fortified; the Argent Dawn's base from 22 ADP, after splitting from the Silver Hand on Alexandros Mograine's death; Classic guards are Argent Guards of several races)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Maxwell_Tyrosus (human paladin, leader of the Argent Dawn after Lord Raymond George's death; Light's Hope; left the Silver Hand when it became the Scarlet Crusade, with Pureheart; insisted on working with other races; reclusive, no audience to outsiders for years by 25 ADP; planned Stratholme with Duke Nicholas Zverenhoff; Classic quests Lord Maxwell Tyrosus, The Argent Hold)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Leonid_Barthalomew_the_Revered (Argent Dawn at Light's Hope; killed in the Scourging, raised mindless, freed by Sylvanas, loyal to the Dawn; revered for his sacrifices; sees undeath as a malady and as a blessing to smite the Scourge; was at Stratholme when Ras Frostwhisper became a lich)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tirion_Fordring (Classic: cottage on the Thondroril River with his horse Mirador; rose from common birth to knight; one of the first Silver Hand paladins; governor of Hearthglen; befriended the orc Eitrigg and defended him at trial; stripped of his powers, excommunicated; wife Karandra told son Taelan he had died; Taelan joined the Scarlet Crusade; honorable, judges by character not race, stern but warm; Classic quests Demon Dogs, Blood Tinged Skies, Carrion Grubbage, Redemption, Of Forgotten Memories, Of Lost Honor, Of Love and Family, Find Myranda; the tasks: plague-free rations, Taelan's hammer, the Symbol of Lost Honor, the painting, Myranda)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Nathanos_Blightcaller (Classic: at the Marris Stead, his childhood home, on the Banshee Queen's orders, recruiting the Horde against plaguebats, the Scourge and the Scarlet Crusade; Champion of the Banshee Queen, the Blightcaller; born Nathanos Marris, the first and only human ranger lord, trained by the high elves; killed by the Scourge, raised, freed by Sylvanas; secretive, scornful of the elves who scorned him; a bully and braggart)
