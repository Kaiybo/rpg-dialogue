## Their quests
- Urs'endris: Strength and Mercy (active/done), The Great Ursera Spirit (active; ends with him).
- Raan: The Adventurer (active), The Great Outdoors (active/done).

## Identity
- Urs'endris · spirit beast · patient, grave; calls the player "child" / "child of the winds"; endurance as strength.
- Brother Zendraas · tailor in grey · dry, wary, vain about his designs, uneasy about his choices. Vendor (windstones).
- Raan Wildwind · adventurer · jovial, easy; road and fire talk.

## Authored, not canon
- Urs'endris: Strength and Mercy read as the druid ending Ur'endra's suffering because she has lost herself (inferred from the summary and the killable mate; the quest text is unread); he won't say how the cubs died; the Nightclaw stopped sending their young; spirits "for the feather and the branch" besides the cat; the wind spirits never said goodbye; head low in bear form.
- Zendraas: the player met cultists who tried to kill them (wiki gossip, reworded); recognising a former acolyte from Lorthuna's sermons; worrying that his robes went with Lorthuna; a man still in grey asking whether leaving is worth it.
- Raan: Aetheen "sends him the restless ones"; the road advice (bandits in the lowlands, grey robes at the temple ruins, Hanaa's post); fortune-vs-home musing.
- Skipped: Skypriest Aanders (a hostile cultist with combat yells only); Swimmers already has zep_swimmers.
