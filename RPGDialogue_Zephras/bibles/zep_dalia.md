## Their quests
- Harvesting Windstones (gives and turn-in): windstones hold the raw, concentrated energy of the winds; the wind elementals vanished years ago and the island's sustaining magic fades; stones are limited but reliable and very valuable; elves are deeply bound to magic; the anchor pylons fade; other things can be harvested from the land (Mining for Dummies, Wild Harvest, Pelt Collecting for Beginners).

## Identity
Dalia the Collector · Thendal Village, Zephras Isle · neutral · Skyborne, female · collector, quest giver

Speech: friendly, practical, counts things; Skyborne: "winds be with you", the fading pylons and windstones
