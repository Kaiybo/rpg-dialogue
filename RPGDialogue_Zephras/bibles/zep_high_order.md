## Authored, not canon
- Elaadrin: "Rohash struck her like a child knocking a cup from a table"; he told Ayessa to her face it was only a truce; Persuade reveal that he sent Belathaan himself, believed Lorthuna could be bargained with, and blames himself (the errand on his behalf is canon; the guilt is ours); Ansirem's letters joking about gnomes.
- Dondallion: he keeps the order's records twice over; went out once, got lost, took up the records; Elaadrin not sleeping since Belathaan's death; the hall suspecting the Windshapers of the missing scholar; the Peacekeepers too stretched to search.
- Iaadaria: the lost Highborne draughts and salves she describes; copying recipes from Kirin Tor libraries; a scar from trying to distil a windstone; changes her mind weekly on old vs new formulas.
- Othesia: teaches at home to save windstones; her opinion of the unnamed Falaath tailor (Zendraas) who designed the cult's robes; braiding hippogryph feathers.
- Gelia: everything (sits on a step near the hall; cousin a Windshaper, neighbor High Order; "on the side of bread"; lamps burning all night in the hall).
- Dockmaster: nods to the western dockmaster every morning; ships from the last days "nobody's seen since"; mainlanders going quiet at the dock edge.
