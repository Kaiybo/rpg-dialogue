# RPG Dialogue: Zephras Isle (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.18.0 (2026-11-19)
- Seena Skybreaker has a word for warriors leaving Zephras.

## 1.17.0 (2026-11-19)
- Warlocks: NPCs react to the demon at your side, your Demon Armor, soul shards, soulstones and Fel Taint. Where warlockry is banned, warlocks speak in hints and people sense rather than name it; some NPCs openly dislike warlocks. Warlock trainers and quest-givers rewritten and deepened.

## 1.16.0 (2026-11-18)
- More named NPCs the earlier passes missed now have conversations, and thin ones have more to say.
- Major NPCs answer Admire, Taunt and Intimidate in their own voice, and turn you down in their own words when they won't talk.

## 1.15.0 (2026-11-18)
- Thin NPCs deepened with more to say, and NPCs that were missed added.
- Journal: the note about each person is one short sentence (12 words or fewer) and shows on the People page only, not in the diary.

## 1.14.0 (2026-11-18)
- NPCs notice what you have done: quests for their neighbours, notable kills, your deaths, rank and reputation.
- Writing pass: stance replies end on something concrete, fewer echoes and stock phrases.
- Druid greetings that assume a night elf or tauren no longer fire for other druid races.

## 1.13.0 (2026-11-18)
- Writing pass: repeated phrases cut and replaced with concrete detail.
- Clarity pass: answers answer the question, names are introduced, quest lines stand alone, fewer contradictions.
- NPCs answer each stance reply before the talk moves on.

## 1.12.0 (2026-11-17)
- Named NPCs answer many more "Ask about" topics (3 to 6 each, more for leaders), mostly the new world topics added in World 1.36.0.
- New Forever-only characters (new files: full game restart): Nineteen islanders: quest givers Ailee Farheart, Windshaper Boro, Elaadrin Evengale, Dondallion Whisperwind, Iaadaria Bitterwind, Quel'ana Quickgale, Seena Skybreaker, Riaani Nightwind, Alvarion Windfield and Urs'endris; the adventurer Raan Wildwind; Blademaster Ren, Tai'ree Farsight, Destin Thriceforged, Othesia Evengale, Gelia Shardsong, the High Order Dockmaster, Melasa Fairmend, Brother Zendraas.
- Origin nods (core 0.81.0): people here now notice the newer backgrounds from 0.70.0.

## 1.11.1 (2026-11-17)
- Writing pass on the characters added in 0.74.0: stock phrases ("fair enough", "truly", "worth more than") rewritten.

## 1.11.0 (2026-11-17)
- New people to talk to (see the main changelog for 0.74.0). New files: restart the game fully (a /reload won't load them).
- Fix: Aetheen's "peace below the clouds" question offered only one stance; it now has a second, opposing one, so the choice is a real choice.

## 1.10.0 (2026-11-17)
- Writing pass: stock phrases ("Fair enough", "I won't pretend", "truly", neat little morals) rewritten in each speaker's own voice, and more grit and humour in the everyday lines.

## 1.9.0 (2026-11-17)
- People here now and then recognise where you come from (your origin) and react to it.

## 1.8.0 (2026-11-17)
- The town's leading people greet you differently when you come back after a fortnight or more, notice how far you've come since they last saw you, and have heard what you did with their neighbours. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.67.0.

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-17)
- Ten new people to talk to, written from the zone's documented story: in Thendal Village, Rorian the Dayseeker, Aetheen of the Gales, the rangers Myriaal Mistwake and Halaan Hawk-Eye, Ventaari Brightwish of the Windshapers and Falorne Fallwind of the High Order; in Valanaar, the Ban'aethal refugee Ealaane Nimbuswalker, "Swimmers" Starfeather and Donaal Downbreeze at the Calmbreeze Inn, and Nyalah Brightfire the cook. Visitors from Dalaran and Mulgore get their own reactions, by race and class.
- High Elder Talaanis Shadowsong now tells the story as it happened (the Shal'nan's abdication, the wards and Lorthuna's beacon, who stood against Rohash), and can talk about Lorthuna, the refugees, the relics of the fallen and what Valanaar makes of mainlanders.
- Peacekeepers, innkeepers, traders and townsfolk mention the skydocks, the anchor pylons, refugees, Luminarian's craft and the cult's grey robes; Zephras townsfolk now have their own lines.
- New files: needs a full game restart, not /reload.

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-17)
