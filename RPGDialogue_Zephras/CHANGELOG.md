# RPG Dialogue: Zephras Isle (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-17)
- Every class this place could plausibly meet now gets a greeting and its own conversation, including this server's Forsaken paladins and dwarf shamans where they'd be welcome. Some people here also notice when you've been thinning out the local trouble.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Replies for the classes and races these people would really react to (warriors, hunters, rogues, mages, warlocks, druids, shamans, paladins; every race of the faction, and the Skyborne), each leading somewhere.

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Every person here writes a first impression in your diary after you first talk (the journal's People page shows it too).

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-15)
- High Elder Talaanis Shadowsong: Skywall → the patrons → nobody knows where; the Windlord → Ansirem Runeweaver and Muln Earthfury; who are the Skyborne → Eldre'narr and Skywall; the orders → their leaders; the Shal'nan; the Council → Peacekeepers and Empyrean Blades; druid, mage and shaman replies. All from brief §10. Topics: background, advice.
- Bible: depth-pass note.

## 1.0.2 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: the High Elder, the Peacekeepers, the innkeepers and the cloth sellers of Zephras Isle now talk in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments, formal and measured the Skyborne way (Talaanis' weary, wry care; the Peacekeepers' dry duty). Same lines, same facts, same conditions.

## 1.0.1 (unreleased)
- The High Elder's account of leaving Skywall is tighter.

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- High Elder Talaanis Shadowsong, and pools for the isle's Peacekeepers, innkeepers and traders. Written only from the Skyborne lore brief (build prompt §10): no invented Skyborne lore. Named NPCs beyond the High Elder wait for capture data.
