## Lore sheet: Burning Steppes (Burning Steppes pack 1.0.0)

Levels 50-58. Everything below is the Classic (pre-Cataclysm) zone, read on warcraft.wiki.gg on 2026-11-15 (Classic-only pages and "Classic only" sections). Cataclysm rebuilt both camps (Morgan herself, Morgan's Militia as an organisation, John J. Keeshan, Colonel Troteman, Eitrigg and Ariok at Flame Crest, Vigil Guards, Flame Crest Bruisers, Gruna, Ragged Jong, Chiselgrip, Flamestar Post, Gorzeeki's Altar of Storms quests); none of it is used.

**Year 1 notes.**
- Nefarian is named only after Message to Maxwell (Maxwell) and never by Helendis or Cyrus; Helendis "won't guess at a name I can't prove", Cyrus refuses to give one.
- Lady Prestor's secret is never stated. After Jail Break! Maxwell only reports that Windsor rode for Stormwind to confront the Highlord and Lady Prestor with his tablets, which is the quest's own completion text, and says he couldn't make sense of half of it. Nothing of The Great Masquerade's end (Windsor's death, Onyxia) is used.
- Ragnaros is spoken of as the thing under the mountain (Helendis, Jalinda, Cyrus); nobody has entered Molten Core.
- Rend Blackhand is alive and calls himself Warchief (Maxwell, Vahgruk). Drakkisath, Voone, Omokk, Wyrmthalak and Halycon are quest targets, before/during/after only.
- Moira: Maxwell repeats only Kharan's Tale (Ironforge's princess taken on the road through the Steppes).

**Assumptions (confirm in game).**
1. Ragged John stands at Flame Crest, as in Classic (the wiki's coordinates and The True Masters (6) notes). If Forever has moved him to Morgan's Vigil, move his `communities` to `morgans_vigil` (nothing else changes).
2. Flame Crest is neutral, with Vahgruk the Horde wind rider master. The town `flame_crest` is written as a neutral camp.
3. Mayara Brightwing as a priest: the wiki infobox lists her class as Priest and her affiliation as the Church of the Holy Light, which may come from her Cataclysm version. Her lines only say the Light keeps her alive and that she learned her prayers as a girl in Stormwind; nothing else is built on it.
4. Mathredis Firestar is an elf of Quel'Thalas; the wiki calls him a blood elf. His lines say "of Quel'Thalas", never "blood elf" or "sin'dorei".
5. No guard pool: the Classic camps had no generic guards (Vigil Guards and Flame Crest Bruisers are Cataclysm). If Forever's capture shows guards, add a `guard_morgans_vigil` pool.
6. Quests that share a title (The True Masters 1-6, A Taste of Flame 1-3) match every quest of that title until capture data gives IDs. Lines keyed to them are written to fit any stage.
7. Gorzeeki Wildeyes and "Thrall's envoy" at Flame Crest: not in Classic Flame Crest (Gorzeeki stood by the Altar of Storms with warlock-only quests; Eitrigg and Ariok came with Cataclysm). Left out.

**Authored, not canon.** Maxwell sits up counting the mountain's lights, says Windsor called a hammer and a prayer the only arguments an orc understands, and that the Light's been quiet in the camp; Helendis sketches the foundations at night and won't say Ragnaros's name on the ridge; Oralius keeps the horn because he didn't like the quiet after the war, and goes quiet after a priest's prayer; Jalinda keeps her mages' singed hats; Mayara says the Count would come himself if the Steppes weren't so far from his tailor; Borgus wets his gryphons' wings every morning; John drinks to Windsor and admits at the bottom of the cask that he could have tried; Maxwort takes the Heart out every night to look at it; Tinkee's eleven tries at the cold coil and Felnok wanting to call the eggscilloscope "the cold thing"; Yuka will shed a tear "once the gold's counted"; Kibler can't sell Opus; Mathredis calls himself the last student of Telmius; Vahgruk calls Rend "a traitor with a big chair"; Cyrus's contempt for the Light.
