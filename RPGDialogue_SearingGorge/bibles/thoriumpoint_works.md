## Their quests
- Burninate: WANTED: Overseer Maltorius (reacts), What the Flux? (gives/turns in), Restoring Fiery Flux Supplies x3 (repeatable). Bitter, vengeful toward a traitor; wants to get to work.
- Scrange: the two board quests (turn-in). Grim humour, practical.

## Identity
- Master Smith Burninate · Thorium Point · neutral (Thorium Brotherhood) · Dark Iron · smith and supplier. Gruff, fierce, proud. Burr; swears by Ragnaros on purpose.
- Taskmaster Scrange · Thorium Point · neutral · Dark Iron · bounty-master. Dry, brisk, cunning.
Relationships: Oilfist (leader), Lolo (the spyglass), Hansel, Maltorius (traitor).
