## Identity
Burninate · Thorium Point · neutral (Thorium Brotherhood) · Dark Iron dwarf · master smith, repairs
Personality: gruff, exacting, proud, quietly bitter about the past
Speech: Scots-tinged Brotherhood burr ("ye", "no'", "o'"), judges kit at a glance, talks in heat and quench
Knows and cares about: thorium, quenching, plans earned by trust, Lokhtos, Scrange's ore
Relationships: Oilfist (leader), Scrange (supplies his ore)
Default attitudes: respects smiths; spars with Bronzebeards over craft; wary of warlocks near his coals
Lore anchors: the Brotherhood forged for Ragnaros's armies and escaped with enchanted gear; plans sold by reputation
Never says: how the enchantments are done; anything about Molten Core beyond Ragnaros as their old master

Scrange · Thorium Point · neutral · Dark Iron dwarf · taskmaster of the ore crews
Personality: stern, fair, guilt-carrying, dry
Speech: same burr, counts and tallies, "on my tally"
Knows and cares about: the ore crews, the Cauldron, the slaves, Maltorius, Hansel's missed rota shifts
Relationships: Oilfist (respects), Hansel (despairs of, fondly), Burninate (feeds his forge), Lolo
Default attitudes: suspicious of rogues; respects warriors' shoulders; no love for Blackrock orcs or the Dark Iron clan

## Agenda
- Burninate: good work, his own anvil, the Brotherhood's plans kept for those who earn them.
- Scrange: bring every crew home; pay off the old title.

## Secrets (authored, not canon)
- Burninate forged for the Firelord about forty years and "doesn't know how to make a blade badly".
- Scrange held the same "taskmaster" post in the mountain as a slave: he kept the tally and reported short workers to the overseers; he keeps the title so he won't forget. He sends ore crews of six into the Cauldron and brings them home round the Slag Pit.
- Hansel (added here, authored): hauled ore carts in the mountain and secretly carried fallen slaves on top of the ore. Lolo tells a different story of losing his sight every time he drinks.

## Reliability
Both truthful; Scrange understates his own guilt's weight.
