# Searing Gorge  (keys: oilfist, hansel, lolo, kalaran)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Thorium_Point (small neutral town in northern Searing Gorge; the Thorium Brotherhood, mostly neutral Dark Iron dwarves; leader Overseer Oilfist; Classic NPCs Master Smith Burninate, Lookout Captain Lolo Longstriker, Hansel Heavyhands, Taskmaster Scrange; Thorium Brotherhood Lookouts guard it)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Thorium_Brotherhood (Dark Iron guild of craftsmen who left the clan; Ragnaros enslaved the Dark Irons after the War of the Three Hammers and put the Brotherhood to mass-producing weapons; Oilfist planned the escape, and their enchanted gear got them out of Blackrock Depths; settled on the cliffs across the gorge to stay near the materials; neutral, trades with anyone who pays, not friendly)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Overseer_Oilfist (male Dark Iron; responsible for all the Brotherhood's activities; the RPG's "takes his job seriously" is non-canon and only colours the voice)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Hansel_Heavyhands (male Dark Iron, huge, almost orcish in build; boisterous, folksy; the Brotherhood's enforcer on Lolo's behalf; Fiery Menace!, Incendosaurs? Whateverosaur is More Like It)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Fiery_Menace! (Oilfist reports lava spiders distressing the local kids; Hansel grumbles at a chore just as he sat down)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lookout_Captain_Lolo_Longstriker (male; blind and often drunk; says drink keeps his senses sharp; WANTED: Overseer Maltorius)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kalaran_Windblade (a black drake in human disguise; tells a false story that the Dark Iron slaughtered his father and asks for vengeance; Divine Retribution, The Flawless Flame, Forging the Shaft, The Flame's Casing, The Torch of Retribution, Squire Maltrake; at the end reveals himself and flies to Blackrock Mountain)

## Their quests
- Oilfist: none used (his quest page lists unlabelled titles and one AQ-era quest; left out).
- Hansel: Fiery Menace!, Incendosaurs? Whateverosaur is More Like It.
- Lolo: WANTED: Overseer Maltorius (The Spiders Have to Go is listed at 15-30, likely later; left out).
- Kalaran: Divine Retribution, The Flawless Flame, Forging the Shaft, The Flame's Casing, The Torch of Retribution.

## Never
- Kalaran's true nature, black dragons, "the Deceiver": never stated. Allowed: the thinness of his story, a skeptical reply, and Lolo's nose (he never smells grief on him). His story is the claim `kalaran_father` (false; exposed by Squire Maltrake). Nothing from his later plots (Varian, Rheastrasza).
- Secrets of the Colossus (AQ war, after Year 1). Molten Core as a place to go: Ragnaros is named as the master they fled, nothing more.
- Lolo's earthen skin (trivia, unexplained).
- Lolo's gossip line verbatim ("the drink keeps me senses sharp").

## Authored, not canon
- Oilfist keeps the forges lit because cold forges once meant punishment; Hansel loves sitting down as the best part of freedom; Lolo tells time by the forge and finds Hansel by his snoring; Kalaran dreams of his father (a lie, inside a lie).

## Voices
- Oilfist: stern overseer, Scots-tinged, weary pride, no patience for old feuds.
- Hansel: folksy grumbler, huge, always just sat down.
- Lolo: jolly blind lookout, proud of senses he may not have.
- Kalaran: brooding, vengeful, vague when pressed.

Persuade/Intimidate/Deceive checks: what a success reveals (authored, not canon, unless marked).
