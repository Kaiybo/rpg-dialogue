# Thorium Point: Scrange and Evonice (keys: scrange, evonice)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg: Taskmaster Scrange (Dark Iron, Thorium Brotherhood, added 1.5.0). Classic turn-in for the Wanted board quests "STOLEN: Smithing Tuyere and Lookout's Spyglass" and "JOB OPPORTUNITY: Culling the Competition" (kill 15 Dark Iron Taskmasters and 15 Slavers; completion text weighs the player's bloody clothes, "15.9 pounds", a record). Later Scrange quests (Twilight Collars etc.) are Cataclysm and left out.
- Evonice Sootsmoker (Dark Iron, female, added 1.5.0). Gossip: the Brotherhood left the Depths "to exploit... er... EXPLORE the world". Her quests are Cataclysm and left out; she is a talker only.
- Dialect matches Oilfist/Hansel in `thoriumpoint.lua` ("tae", "an'", "ye", "dinnae").

## Voices
**Taskmaster Scrange.** Gruff, wry, gallows humour about blood and scales; keeps the purse honestly; carries his past as a slave driver.
**Evonice Sootsmoker.** Cheerful, quick, mercenary; the "exploit/explore" slip is her running joke; dazzled by the sky.

## Authored, not canon
- Scrange drove slaves for the Shadowforge in the Slag Pit and deserted after a feast-day when the weak were marched into the fire for Ragnaros; he keeps the title because Oilfist says it's good to be reminded; freed slaves who reach the Point are fed and given paid work; the Dark Irons steal tools because they think the Brotherhood stole the craft.
- Evonice grew up sweeping soot in Shadowforge City (her name); prospects seams in the gorge for a share; wants to see the sea.

## Never
No Cataclysm Searing Gorge (Master Smith Burninate, Iron Summit, Twilight cult quests), no Moira-as-queen lore.
