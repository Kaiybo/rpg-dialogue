# Tanaris, missed folk  (keys: gaeriyan, winkey)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg zone roster scan; pages read with `tools/npc.py`: Gaeriyan (human ghost on the cliffs north-east of Gadgetzan, seen only while the player is dead; Donova Snowden sends adventurers to him with a Videre Elixir; Meet at the Grave, A Grave Situation; he reveals the secret under the conspicuous gravestone in the Gadgetzan graveyard; his three gossip lines read for tone and rewritten), Winkey (gnome on a small island off Tanaris; "in the game since the beginning but had no dialogue prior to Cataclysm"; her Blingtron grandfather and her Cataclysm line are not used).
- Existing pack: gadgetzan.lua, dunes.lua (Steamwheedle Port); RPGDialogue_Winterspring springs.lua (Donova Snowden); RPGDialogue_UnGoro refuge.lua (Linken).

## Their quests
- Gaeriyan: Meet at the Grave (ends with him), A Grave Situation (from him). Winkey: none in Classic.

## Identity / voice
- Gaeriyan · human ghost, man · gentle, weary, old-fashioned courtesy; wants to be left in peace but answers those who took the trouble to die.
- Winkey · gnome woman · cheerful, chatty, precise even about idleness; percentages and charts.

## Authored, not canon
- Gaeriyan died of thirst before Gadgetzan was built and watched the object being buried; why only the dead see him.
- Winkey as a Gnomeregan valve calibrator on her first holiday, and the beaches she measured. Community `tanaris_coast` is new.
