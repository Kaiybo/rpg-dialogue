# Tanaris  (keys: marin, sprinkle, bilgewhizzle, fizzledowser, pestlezugg, tranrek, fizzgrimble, stoley, krinkle)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gadgetzan (capital of the Steamwheedle Cartel in northern Tanaris; neutral, both factions recognize it; goblin bruisers stop fights; before the Cataclysm, desert all round, water from towers at Waterspring Field kept by the Gadgetzan Water Company and threatened by the Wastewander bandits; Gadgetzan Bruisers; Innkeeper Fizzgrimble)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Marin_Noggenfogger (goblin alchemist and businessman, no title before Cataclysm; money-minded, complains of the heat, excited about his elixir; The Thirsty Goblin (a dew gland from Thistleshrub Valley), In Good Taste (Sprinkle makes it palatable), Delivery for Marin, Noggenfogger Elixir)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sprinkle_Noggenfogger ("Sprinkle" in Classic; female goblin, Marin's friend, not yet married; calls him "Noggy" and guards the name; a secret ingredient from childhood, her sister's cooking, the Hinterlands; Sprinkle's Secret Ingredient, In Good Taste, Delivery for Marin; says Marin owes her)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Chief_Engineer_Bilgewhizzle (chief engineer of the Gadgetzan Water Co.; Wastewander Justice, More Wastewander Justice)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Senior_Surveyor_Fizzledowser (Water Company; finds new sources after the Wastewanders took many; cautious; Gadgetzan Water Survey (dowsing widget at Sandsorrow Watch, insect ambush), Noxious Lair Investigation; believes the threat reaches beyond Tanaris; **in Classic and Forever**)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Alchemist_Pestlezugg (alchemy vendor with an assistant rat, Rose; Insect Part Analysis report for Fizzledowser; March of the Silithid (Zilzibin Drumlore's and Gracina Spiritmight's Un'Goro theory), Bungle in the Jungle (scent gland and Un'Goro soil for a lure))
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tran%27rek (goblin, formerly of Gadgetzan; **Krazek's cousin**; evasive about profits; Tran'rek, Scarab Shells, Thistleshrub Valley, Super Sticky)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Innkeeper_Fizzgrimble (male goblin, the Road Warrior; friendly, chatty)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Stoley (human at Steamwheedle Port; Stoley's Debt, Stoley's Shipment, Deliver to MacKinley; **in Classic and Forever**)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Krinkle_Goodsteel (goblin blacksmithing vendor by the forge on the south side of Gadgetzan; Classic quests Mold Rhymes With... (finish) and Fire Plume Forged (start) in the Scholomance key chain; gossip guesses you aren't a blacksmith but says looks deceive; patch 1.3.0 took away his repairs and 1.5.0 restored them after "a complaint to the local Repairer's Union", used as an in-world joke)

## Their quests
- Marin: The Thirsty Goblin, In Good Taste, Delivery for Marin, Noggenfogger Elixir.
- Sprinkle: In Good Taste, Sprinkle's Secret Ingredient.
- Bilgewhizzle: Wastewander Justice, More Wastewander Justice.
- Fizzledowser: Gadgetzan Water Survey, Noxious Lair Investigation, Insect Part Analysis (turn-in).
- Pestlezugg: Insect Part Analysis, March of the Silithid, Bungle in the Jungle.
- Tran'rek: Tran'rek (from Krazek), Scarab Shells, Thistleshrub Valley. Stoley: Stoley's Debt, Stoley's Shipment, Deliver to MacKinley.

- Krinkle: Mold Rhymes With... (fifteen gold for the mold; the stem lesson free), Fire Plume Forged (start; Fire Plume Ridge in Un'Goro is assumed from the title, kept general).

## Never
- Anything Cataclysm or later: Marin as Baron, his marriage to Sprinkle, the flood and Steamwheedle Port's loss, the Thunderdrome, Legion, Undermine and Gallywix.
- Ahn'Qiraj opening or the silithid war: Fizzledowser and Pestlezugg only fear it's bigger than Tanaris.
- Cage fights at the Road Warrior (the gossip mentions them, but which era isn't clear).
- Gossip verbatim (Bilgewhizzle's "the name's Bilgewhizzle", Tran'rek's "make our profits", Pestlezugg's "in need of some alchemy supplies").

## Voices
- Marin: eager, hot, money-minded inventor.
- Sprinkle: warm cook; "Noggy" is hers.
- Bilgewhizzle: harried water engineer.
- Fizzledowser: cautious surveyor, quietly alarmed.
- Pestlezugg: brisk alchemist; Rose the rat.
- Tran'rek: evasive dealer; Krazek's cousin.
- Fizzgrimble: chatty innkeeper.
- Stoley: nervous debtor.
