# RPG Dialogue: Tanaris (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-15)
- Every person here writes a first impression in your diary after you first talk (the journal's People page shows it too).

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-15)
- Krinkle Goodsteel (Blacksmithing Supplies, Gadgetzan) makes the Scholomance key's mold and never asks what it opens. Written in whole sentences.

## 1.2.1 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: Gadgetzan and Steamwheedle Port now talk in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments. Marin still sells, Tran'rek still dodges and Stoley still frets, each in their own words.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)
- Depth: Marin, Sprinkle, Bilgewhizzle, Fizzledowser, Pestlezugg (and Rose), Tran'rek, Fizzgrimble and Stoley, with their own story and advice.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits bring personal lines (habits, worries, running jokes), and some NPCs get night-time lines. Fizzgrimble, Pestlezugg and Rose, Stoley.

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- Gadgetzan (both factions): Marin Noggenfogger, Sprinkle, Chief Engineer Bilgewhizzle, Senior Surveyor Fizzledowser, Alchemist Pestlezugg, Tran'rek and Innkeeper Fizzgrimble. The Gadgetzan Bruisers.
- Steamwheedle Port: Stoley.
