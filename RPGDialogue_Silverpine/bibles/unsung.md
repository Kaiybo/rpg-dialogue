# The Sepulcher's unsung  (keys: gwyn, boynton, harly)

Shopkeepers with no quests and no gossip in the game. Written from memory of the vanilla world; no wiki page read in this pass.

## Sources
- Vanilla 1.12 Sepulcher roster (memory): Gwyn Farrow (Mushroom Merchant), Andrea Boynton (Clothier), Edwin Harly (General Supplies).
- bibles/silverpine.md for Hadrec, Dalar Dawnweaver, the Deathstalkers, Arugal's worgen, Shadowfang Keep, Pyrewood, Ambermill and Dalaran's barrier.

## Their quests
- None.

## Identity and voices
- Gwyn Farrow: cheerful mushroom seller; caps from the crypts and the logs by Lordamere Lake; grew turnips before she died (authored, not canon).
- Andrea Boynton: fussy clothier; a seamstress of wedding dresses in life (authored, not canon); can't bear bad stitching.
- Edwin Harly: dry, blunt supplier; "a forest that eats travelers"; gives directions (Tirisfal north, Pyrewood/Shadowfang south, Hillsbrad east).

## Never
- Gilneas opened, the worgen's cure, Cataclysm Silverpine (Forsaken Front, Sylvanas's val'kyr).

## What they notice
- Gwyn: the living (orc, tauren, troll) eating her stock. Boynton: tailors, the nearly naked. Harly: wounds, empty purses.
