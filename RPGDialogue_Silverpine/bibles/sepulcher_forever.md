## Their quests
- Diplomatic Incident (Trevan turn-in, from Danitha): Earthen Ring pair missing en route to Tarren Mill; a chance for the Keep's legitimacy; "a damned fine mess, paladin".
- A Curious Pair (Trevan gives, Baldren turn-in): a tauren and "a high elf"; bets his good pinky finger; Baldren "might not care for you paladins" but "your timing is impeccable".
- A Grim Fate (Baldren gives): gnolls burning a large body on Fenris Isle; "brave or stupid"; "snooty elf probably long dead"; paladins love oaths and lost causes.
- Into Fenris Keep / Lumina Windsinger / The Windshaper's Wrath (Lumina): not quel'dorei but shen'dorei, Windshaper, guest of the Earthen Ring with Banon, bound for Arathi's elemental convergences; "Gnolls are the second worst creatures"; "less than charitable things about paladins"; "You and your kind have a friend among the Windshapers".
- The Debt (Lumina): a worgen bit Banon; he turned on Fenris Isle; "We windshapers always pay our debts"; "I swear it upon the sky above".
- A Moon-Kissed Blade (Lumina gives, Trevan turn-in): Old Fire-Eye won't stay dead; materials from Deadmines, Shadowfang Keep, a purified Kor gem (Ulric Frostveil, Zoram'gar).
- An Underrated Talent / The Moonsilver Blade (Trevan): Ott of Tarren Mill, an ordinary smith whose sword amazed the Undercity's smiths; "I knew that Ott had it in him".
- Old Fire-Eye (Lumina): kill him by the Greymane Wall with the Moonsilver Blade.
- A Frightened Request (Tabitha): Edward's weekly trips to the graveyard in the Lordaeron ruins; their son lost when Arthas razed the city; "First my son and now sweet Edward..."; "What I asked of you was not fair".

## Identity
- **Deathguard Baldren** (quest): sour night patrolman; distrusts paladins (red-robed ones burned his patrol mates); grudgingly admits he was wrong about Lumina.
- **Tabitha Heartweaver** (quest): gentle, grieving, loyal to the Banshee Queen; kneels at an empty grave for her son.
- **Trevan Rol** (quest): wry Forsaken paladin of Bandarion Keep posted at the Sepulcher; pinky-finger jokes; champions Ott.
- **Lumina Windsinger** (quest): Skyborne Windshaper, sky-touched, fierce and honest; treats debts as sacred; good with lightning.
- Never says: anything about Jeremy Heartweaver being alive (that is Alliance-side knowledge; Tabitha does not know).

## Agenda
- Trevan: legitimacy for the Keep. Lumina: pay the debt to Banon. Tabitha: learn Edward's fate.

## Secrets
- Tabitha (dramatic irony, canon): her son Jeremy survived and was adopted in Duskwood (Remember That I Love You, Alliance). Nobody in this file knows it.

## Reliability
