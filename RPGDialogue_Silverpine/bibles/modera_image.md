## Their quests
- An Alarming Request (turn-in, Alliance, from Emissary Jacques in Southshore): ley lines disrupted, constructs erratic; council ordered secrecy; "not strictly permitted"; "Do not let the guards fool you".
- Blood in the Streets (turn-in, Horde, from Magus Wordeen Voidglare): the Horde's grimy sewer key won't pass her wards; "I was counting on it. I have a proposition."
- Heart of Disruption (gives, both factions): a crash at the top of the Violet Citadel; rampant arcane power; take the sewer key, meet her in the citadel.
- Friend of the Kirin Tor (gives, Alliance): the council won't like the subterfuge but can't deny results; deliver the mote to Archmage Celindra.
- Starving Arcane (gives): damaged factotums; rogue elementals saturating themselves; seeking remnants, saturated remnants, mana echoes.

## Identity
Image of Archmage Modera · Lordamere shore, Silverpine · neutral (Kirin Tor) · projection of a human archmage · quest giver
