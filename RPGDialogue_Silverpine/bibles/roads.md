# Silverpine, beyond the Sepulcher  (keys: erland, yorick, karos)

## Sources (warcraft.wiki.gg, Classic sections)
- Deathstalker_Erland, Escorting_Erland: a Forsaken deathstalker in Silverpine who needs an escort past the worgen to reach Rane Yorick.
- Rane_Yorick (Classic): deathstalker at a farmhouse in southern Silverpine, watching Pyrewood and the worgen.
- Karos_Razok: Forsaken bat handler at the Sepulcher.

## Their quests
- Erland: Escorting Erland.
- Yorick: Escorting Erland (turn-in); The Deathstalkers' Report.
- Karos: none: bat handler.

## Never
