# Sepulcher guests: Mura Runetotem, Astor Hadren

# Mura Runetotem  (key: sp_mura)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Mura_Runetotem (read: tauren at the Sepulcher; quest content Classic, removed 4.0.3a)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Mura_Runetotem_(quest) (read)

## Their quests
- Mura Runetotem (turn-in; from Tonga Runetotem at the Crossroads, after "Altered Beings"): bring her a Sample Snapjaw Shell. She is trying to help the Forsaken revive dying Silverpine; the shell still glows and pulses with life, and she will study it in hope it helps the forest. Wants: hope; fears: nothing yet.

## Identity
Mura Runetotem · the Sepulcher · Thunder Bluff / Horde · Tauren (female) · druid, guest of the Forsaken
Personality: gentle, patient, stubborn, curious, warm
Speech: tauren cadence, Earth Mother oaths, nature images ("the roots are tired"), calls people "friend" and tauren "child of the plains"
Knows and cares about: Silverpine's sickness, the Barrens' strange growth, druidry
Relationships: Tonga Runetotem (kin at the Crossroads), Renferrel (asks her about mushrooms), Gwyn Farrow, Wallace the Blind, Hadrec (gave her a corner)
Default attitudes: treats the Forsaken as neighbours; warmest to tauren and druids
Lore anchors: Cairne's alliance with Thrall and Sylvanas; Wailing Caverns druids changed; worgen are Arugal's
Never says: anything of the Emerald Nightmare by name, or later events

## Threads he could carry
- none.

## Agenda
- Heal Silverpine slowly and carefully.

## Secrets
- She half-distrusts what the apothecaries do with her answers (authored).

## Reliability
- Honest; her hopes for the forest are hopes, not facts.

## What they notice   (2–3 things)
- tauren kin, druid forms, herbalists

## Topics
- worgen, wailing_caverns, cenarion_circle, background

## Authored, not canon
- A sapling she coaxed out by the north wall; she keeps the shell on her table.
- Some at Thunder Bluff think the Forsaken should be left to die out; Hadrec "gave her a corner".
- Renferrel asks which mushrooms are poisonous; she hears the trees ache, as Wallace says he does.
- Her exact role (druid) is inferred from the quest text; the wiki gives no class.

# Astor Hadren  (key: sp_astor)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Astor_Hadren (read: male Forsaken messenger in a building at the Sepulcher; before Cataclysm secretly carried Syndicate messages between the Sepulcher and Brill; drops Astor's Letter of Introduction for the rogue quest "The Deathstalkers")
- RPGDialogue_Undercity/npcs/mennet.lua (Mennet Carkad's line about Astor's effects)

## Their quests
- The Deathstalkers (Undercity rogue quest, level 13; he is the target): deathstalkers took his letter and impersonated him to learn what his Syndicate employers wanted.

## Identity
Astor Hadren · the Sepulcher · Undercity (secretly Syndicate courier) · Forsaken (male) · messenger
Personality: smooth, evasive, anxious under it, vain about his trade
Speech: chatty deflection, "friend", "there's a good fellow", jokes about his biscuit and socks; never a straight answer
Knows and cares about: the Brill road, who sends what, keeping out of the lake
Relationships: the Syndicate (paymasters, never named by him willingly), the Deathguard (he says they like him), Hadrec and Sevren (official post)
Default attitudes: wary of rogues; flatters the purse-heavy
Lore anchors: the Syndicate as Alterac's old nobles; worgen past Pyrewood
Never says: outright that he works for the Syndicate

## Threads he could carry
- none.

## Agenda
- Keep carrying, keep being paid, keep being ignored.

## Secrets
- The Syndicate letter of introduction (canon); after the quest, he knows someone wore his name.

## Reliability
- Unreliable: everything he says about his employers is evasion.

## What they notice   (2–3 things)
- stealth, rogues, a heavy purse

## Topics
- syndicate, worgen

## Authored, not canon
- He carries the official post too (Hadrec's requisitions, Sevren's reports); the spare socks and the old biscuit.
- He knows Brill wants more Deathguard and the apothecaries want pumpkins (Year 1 Tirisfal quests, his gossip).
