## Identities
- **Alexandre Lefevre** (sp_lefevre) · Leather Armor Merchant · Forsaken (male). Proud, dry, vain about stitching; was a saddler in Lordaeron. Will not work worgen hide ("they were men once"). Cures his hides twice.
- **Nadia Vernon** (sp_vernon) · Bowyer · Forsaken (female). Blunt, dry, grumbles about soft pine and plague-brittle yew; buys Ashenvale staves via the Undercity. Proud the dead archers don't shake.
- **Sebastian Meloche** (sp_meloche) · Armorer · Forsaken (male). Gruff; worgen break shoulders, gnolls shins, the inn's low door helmets. Salvages fallen Deathguard and Dalaran plate; melts Scarlet armor down.
- **Andrew Hilbert** (sp_hilbert) · Trade Supplies · Forsaken (male). Chatty, cheerful; gloomy alive, cheerful dead. The apothecaries buy vials by the crate "for samples"; he doesn't ask.
- **Sarah Goode** (sp_goode) · Stable Master · Forsaken (female). Gentle, steady; animals settle if you don't pretend; her father's farm and a pony whose name she remembers better than her father's face. Hates the worgen howl.

Default attitudes: all serve the Deathguard and keep out of politics. Never say: anything of Cataclysm Silverpine (Gilneas, the Forsaken Front, val'kyr).
