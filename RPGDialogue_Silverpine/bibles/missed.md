# Silverpine, missed folk  (keys: guillaume_sorouy, killian_sanatha, leo_sarn)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg zone roster scan; pages read with `tools/npc.py`: Guillaume_Sorouy (Forsaken, female, Blacksmithing Trainer, the Sepulcher), Killian_Sanatha (Fisherman vendor on the Lordamere Lake dock near the Decrepit Ferry; Fenwick Thatros sometimes stands beside him), Leo_Sarn (Enchanting Supplies, hills north-east of the Greymane Wall).
- Existing pack: sepulcher.lua (Hadrec, worgen, Shadowfang), RPGDialogue_Undercity mennet.lua (the rogue quest Fenwick Thatros).

## Their quests
- None of their own. Killian shares his dock with Fenwick Thatros and knows nothing of why.

## Identity / voice
- Guillaume Sorouy · Forsaken woman · gruff, dry smith; a man's name she is tired of explaining.
- Killian Sanatha · Forsaken man · calm, quiet fisherman; keeps his voice down for the fish.
- Leo Sarn · Forsaken man · precise, wry shopkeeper who likes being hard to find.

## Authored, not canon
- Guillaume was named for her farrier father; silver edging on the Deathguard's blades.
- Killian fished Lordamere in life; he sells to the Sepulcher's living allies.
- Leo kept a curiosity shop in Lordaeron; enchantments on his tent.
- Community `silverpine_wilds` is new for Leo (he stands away from any town).

## Never
- Cataclysm Silverpine (the Scarlet seizure of the dock, Killian moved to the Sepulcher, the Greymane Wall broken, Gilneas' worgen).
