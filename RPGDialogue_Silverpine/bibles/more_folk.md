"Have you too come to purify the waters here and prove your understandin' to Islen?"; he has just defeated the corrupt water spirit and is about to put the bracers and the water on the brazier; "Tiev understan' all too well when somethin' goes wrong"; knows another who came out of the spirit world too quick; "ol' Tiev know a trick or two 'bout how da sapta magic works". Kind, patient, proud of his know-how.
