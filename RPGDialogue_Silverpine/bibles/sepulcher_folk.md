## Their quests
- Astor: none given; he is the mark in The Deathstalkers (Mennet Carkad's rogue chain). Lines react while it's active and after.
- Others: none.

## Voices
- Astor: hurried, evasive, faintly threatening; never names his employer (the Syndicate is only hinted: "a merchant house with interests in the north"). Asks whether the player serves for love or coin.
- Sarah Goode: gentle, patient with animals. Hilbert: dry, honest about his markup. Patrice: cheerful about poisons, contemptuous of the apothecaries. Johan: gruff ex-miner. Nadia: brisk, practical.

## Never
- Astor admitting the Syndicate outright; the New Plague's purpose; anything from the Cataclysm-era Silverpine war.

## Authored, not canon
- Astor lost one letter to a worgen north of the Dead Field; he doesn't remember his life and has never read a letter.
- Sarah Goode keeps spare bats for Karos; a tauren's bear ate a deathguard's boot.
- Hilbert adds road losses to his prices; washes Renferrel's empty vials twice.
- Patrice studied a worgen pelt with a dart in it. Johan dug silver north of the capital for twenty years and died underground; Dalaran's mages carry crates into old workings near Ambermill.
