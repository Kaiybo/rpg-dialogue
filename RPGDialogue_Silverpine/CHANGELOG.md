# RPG Dialogue: Silverpine Forest (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-16)
- New people: Deathstalker Erland, Rane Yorick and Karos Razok.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Replies for the classes and races these people would really react to (warriors, hunters, rogues, mages, warlocks, druids, shamans, paladins; every race of the faction, and the Skyborne), each leading somewhere.

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-15)
- Every person here writes a first impression in your diary after you first talk (the journal's People page shows it too).

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.1.1 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: the Sepulcher now talks in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments, each in their own voice (Hadrec's iron brevity, Renferrel's fussiness, Dalar's dry pride, Allister's smoothness, Bates' crypt-keeper hospitality). Same lines, same facts, same conditions.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits bring personal lines (habits, worries, running jokes), and some NPCs get night-time lines. Bates, Allister, Renferrel.

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- The Sepulcher: High Executor Hadrec, Apothecary Renferrel, Dalar Dawnweaver, Shadow Priest Allister and Innkeeper Bates. The Sepulcher's Deathguards.
