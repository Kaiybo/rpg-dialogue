**Assumptions (confirm in game).**
- Quest titles used with TODO_QUEST: "Ink Supplies", "Rethban Ore", "Underbelly Scales", "A Baying of Gnolls", "Shadow Magic", "Selling Fish", "Kharan's Tale" (new this pass), as the wiki spells them.
- Keeshan respawns in Render's Rock after the escort; his after-rescue lines are written to make sense in the cave too ("Some nights I'm back on that rock").
- Solomon knows no dwarves arrived, not why; only a player who did Kharan's Tale can tell him about Moira.
- Brianna's wounded upstairs, and Solomon's "the guards will show you", are authored scenery: no wounded NPCs exist in Classic Lakeshire.
- The Forever quests listed on NPC pages (Marris: Blackrock Blockade; Feldon: Show of Force; Oslow: Alther's Mill; Solomon: WANTED: Incinerator Gar'im) have no wiki pages yet, so nothing from them is used.

**Year 1 notes (kept out).** Bravo Company and Colonel Troteman (Cataclysm naming; Classic Missing In Action names the 12th Sabre Regiment), Keeshan's wife Jade and his Cataclysm storyline, Watch Captain Parker, Darcy Parker and Libby, Oslow under the boulder, Yowler's sons, Grand Magus Doane and Darkblaze, Solomon's Orcish (a Cataclysm quest), the Scourge attack on Lakeshire (Shadowlands), Verner's death. The Traveler novels' Lakeshire (Greydon Thorne, the Glades) is post-Year 1 fiction and unused.

### World pack additions (ready to paste)

Two topics the Lakeshire NPCs already answer (takes are in solomon.lua, marris.lua and keeshan.lua under these ids; they are inert until the topics exist). Add to `RPGDialogue_World/topics.lua` after `redridge_gnolls`:

```lua
P:Topic { id = "stonewatch_keep", keywords = { "Stonewatch" }, name = "Stonewatch Keep", category = "places", learned = "Magistrate Solomon or Marshal Marris in Lakeshire" }
P:Topic { id = "keeshan", keywords = { "Keeshan" }, name = "Corporal Keeshan", category = "people", learned = "the notice outside the Lakeshire Inn, or Marshal Marris" }
```

Two rumor fixes in `RPGDialogue_World/rumors/redridge.lua` (`redridge_inns`), to match the quest text:

```lua
    { id = "bridge", when = { questBefore = TODO_QUEST("The Everstill Bridge") },
      text = "Oslow's iron from Stormwind never reached him. Gnolls took the whole caravan into the hills. What a gnoll wants with rivets, nobody knows." },
    { id = "tower", when = { minLevel = 20 },
      text = "Lights in the tower in the eastern hills at night. Green ones. The gnolls go quiet when they're lit." },
```

(The current `bridge` rumor says stone and timber; the quest's shipment was iron pikes and rivets. The current `tower` rumor puts the Tower of Ilgalar on the south shore; the State of Lakeshire puts it in the northeast, and the zone map puts it in the east.)
