# Lakeshire's trades and its gryphon master  (keys: ariena, hiett, chapal)

## Sources
- From memory of original WoW (1.x): Ariena Stormfeather, Gryphon Master at Lakeshire; Kimberly Hiett, Fletcher, Lakeshire; Henry Chapal, Gunsmith, Lakeshire. Hiett and Chapal's exact titles and placement unconfirmed; confirm with capture.

## Their quests
- none: flight master and vendors. They react to the gnoll and orc trouble in Redridge (Blackrock orcs, Redridge gnolls) by name only.

## Never
- Anything after Year 1.

## Voices (authored, not canon)
- Ariena: brisk, fond of her gryphons, watches the Blackrock smoke from the sky.
- Hiett: plainspoken fletcher, can't keep up with the watch's demand for arrows.
- Chapal: dry, precise gunsmith, thinks dwarven powder is the best thing to come over the mountains.
