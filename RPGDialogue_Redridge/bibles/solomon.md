## Sources

## Their quests
- Messenger to Stormwind / Westfall / Darkshire: begging letters to everyone. He hates writing them.
- The True Masters: proof that someone directs the orcs. He wants Stormwind's attention, not just its sympathy.
- Wanted: Gath'Ilzogg; Wanted: Lieutenant Fangore: justice by bounty.
