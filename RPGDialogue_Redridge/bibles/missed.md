## Their quests
- None of their own. Daniel and Ashlock teach `everstill_bridge`; Amy and Hiett `redridge_gnolls`; Clyde `blackrock`.

## Identity / voice
- Amy Davenport · human woman · shrewd, friendly; knows what every wagon carried.
- Bridge Worker Daniel · human man · careful mason, few words, checks every course twice; respects Oslow.
- Clyde Ranthal · human man · dry, unhurried; teases Wilma about who has the soft job and worries about her.
- Franklin Hamar · human man · genial, fastidious; judges the common room by its cuffs.
- Gloria Femmel · human woman · brisk, motherly; "dear"; feeding the town is her war.
- Guard Ashlock · human man · steady, brisk, keeps walking; nods to Lindsay at the lantern.
- Guard Hiett · human woman · keen, blunt; an archer made to stand a street post.
