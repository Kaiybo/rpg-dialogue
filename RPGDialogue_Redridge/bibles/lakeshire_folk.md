## Their quests
- Conacher: Solomon's Law.
- Feldon: Assessing the Threat (receives Encroaching Gnolls).
- Parker: Encroaching Gnolls, Visit the Herbalist (receives A Free Lunch).
- Darcy: A Free Lunch (receives Delivering Daffodils).
- Martie: An Unwelcome Guest, Delivering Daffodils (receives Visit the Herbalist).
- Breanna: Redridge Goulash.

## Never
- Anything Cataclysm or later: Darcy and Parker married, Libby, Tower Watch, Watch Captain Parker, Colonel Troteman, Krakauer, the Fourth War, the Scourge attack.
- Franks and Beans (its page describes the later Darcy at her tower).
- How Martie and Alma Jainrose are related.
- Whether Parker returns anyone's feelings. He is shy and keeps his eyes on the road.

## Voices
- Conacher: formal, procedural, dry humour delivered as court language.
- Feldon: anxious arithmetic; counts gnolls, writes reports nobody reads.
- Parker: duty first, flustered about Darcy, "eyes on the road".
- Darcy: bright, chatty, calls people "love"; fond of Parker and a little jealous of Martie.
- Martie: warm, patient, gardener's metaphors; quietly sad that the daffodils go to Darcy.
- Breanna: harried, generous, feeds the garrison and counts the bowls that don't come back.
