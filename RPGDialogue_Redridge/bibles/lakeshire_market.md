- none: five vendors and a waitress. They react to The Everstill Bridge (Karen), Murloc Poachers (Vernon) by name.

## Identity
- Lamar Veisilli · human man · fruit seller. Cheerful, gentle, sentimental about the lost orchards; lets the children steal.
- Karen Taylor · human woman · smithing and mining supplies. Blunt, respects tradesfolk, no-nonsense.
- Kara Adams · human woman · shield crafter. Steady, dutiful, gives shields to the watch on credit.
- Vernon Hale · human man · fishing supplies. Wry, grumbles about murlocs, fond of a tall tale.
- Sherman Femmel · human man · butcher. Gruff, practical; spider meat in the goulash is his and Breanna's secret.
- Gretchen Vogel · human woman · waitress for Chef Breanna. Harried, kind, calls people "love"; carries back the empty bowls.

## Never
- Cataclysm Lakeshire (Lakeshire's Last Stand, ettins, the boulder). Named family for any of them.

## Agenda / Secrets (authored, not canon)
- Lamar's family grew apples east of town before the Blackrock; harvest fair replaced by a curfew.
- Karen's father mined the hills; she sells, doesn't buy, ore.
- Kara apprenticed in Stormwind, learned rim-work from a dwarf; Feldon orders twelve and pays for eight; she fought once.
- Vernon sold tackle since before the bridge fell; the lake-beast tale.
- Sherman threw out a soldier who asked about gnoll meat.
- Gretchen: fewer soldiers each month; Breanna pays in soup when coin is short.

## What they notice
- Lamar: hunger. Karen: smiths and miners. Kara: shield classes; battered kit. Vernon: fishers. Sherman: hunters.
