## Their quests
- Yorus: Yorus Barleybrew (turn-in), The Rethban Gauntlet, The Shieldsmith. Proud of family brew; tests warriors' nerve.
- Lucius: Redridge Rendezvous (turn-in), Alther's Mill. Trains rogues; secretive about Stormwind friends.
- Shawn: Hilary's Necklace. Loyal to his friend; wants to be a hero.
- Hooper, Wental, Alma, Trent: none of their own. Hooper and Trent react to Murloc Poachers, The Lost Tools and The Everstill Bridge; Wental to Dry Times; Alma to An Unwelcome Guest.

## Identity
- Yorus Barleybrew · Lakeshire Inn · dwarf man · questgiver. Jovial, boastful, warm; Khaz Modan burr ("ye", "o'", "yer").
- Lucius · Lakeshire docks · dwarf man · rogue trainer. Sharp, dry, rude, secretive; speaks plainly (no burr; the source gives none).
- Shawn · Lakeshire docks · human boy · child. Eager, polite, run-on sentences.
- Matthew Hooper · Lakeshire docks · human man · fishing trainer. Patient, wry about murlocs.
- Bartender Wental · Lakeshire Inn · human man · food vendor. Cheerful, put-upon about the ale jokes.
- Alma Jainrose · Lakeshire · human woman · herbalism trainer. Gentle, precise, a little stern about technique.
- Bridge Worker Trent · Everstill Bridge · human man · worker. Gruff, loyal to Oslow.
