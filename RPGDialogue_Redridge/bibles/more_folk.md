- Guard Clarke · human woman · steady, rooted; Lakeshire family five generations.
- Guard Pearce · human man · dry, few words; "every man to his post".
- Bridge Worker Alex · human man · quiet; short answers that still join up; defers to Dmitri.
- Bridge Worker Matthew · human man · dour mortar-mixer; watches for rain; pessimist who's usually right.
