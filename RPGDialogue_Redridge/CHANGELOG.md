# RPG Dialogue: Redridge Mountains (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.8.0 (2026-11-15)

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-15)

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Magistrate Solomon knows when you've met Marshal Maxwell at Morgan's Vigil (the True Masters chain runs between them; Burning Steppes pack).

## 1.3.0 (unreleased)
- Depth rewrite: every Lakeshire NPC is now a place to explore (hub, branches two to four deep, "again" lines, their own Ask about takes for Their story and A little advice). All 15 meet the depth standard.
- Magistrate Solomon: the letters (General Jonathan's reply, Stoutmantle's, Ebonlocke's), "something in Stormwind is amiss", Stonewatch Keep, Gath'Ilzogg, Morganth's betrayal, Keeshan's notice, the Court of Lakeshire and the town's history (taken in the First War, rebuilt after the Second). Replies for priests (no chapel, no bandages), paladins, minor nobles, Lordaeron refugees, dwarves (his envoys to Ironforge; after Kharan's Tale, what became of them), Bolvar's deputy, and players who've heard Lady Prestor or met General Jonathan.
- Marris (the 12th Sabre's missing, Tharil'zun's Outrunners, the midnight orbs, Bellygrub's name), Oslow (the sunk toolbox, the hijacked iron, Brother Paxton's Rethban ore), Verner (Argus's bill, whelp scales, Yowler), Keeshan (the 12th Sabre Regiment; a priest can see to his head), Wiley (what his note to Gryan said), Baren, Daniels (and his seamstress wife), Brianna, Conacher, Feldon, Parker, Darcy, Martie and Breanna.
- Voice pass: every line in the pack (all fifteen NPCs and the Lakeshire guards) now speaks in whole sentences, joined the way people talk, instead of stacked fragments. Meanings, facts and line ids unchanged; the choppiness check reports 0 of 473 lines.
- Lore fixes: Keeshan starts Missing In Action himself (Marris's line now comes before it); Oslow's stolen shipment was iron pikes and rivets from Stormwind; Fangore leads the Shadowhides in the eastern hills; Martie doesn't know who the daffodils are for; Breanna's goulash is for you, her cupboard bare from feeding the brigade; Oslow's craft notice now works for engineers and miners too.
