# RPG Dialogue: Thousand Needles (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.3.0 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: Freewind (Longhorn, Rau, Hagar, Thalia, Abeqwa and the guards) and the Mirage Raceway (Pozzik, Fizzle, Kravel) now talk in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments, each in their own voice; what they say is unchanged.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)
- The Mirage Raceway to the depth standard: Pozzik (what makes a car fast, Gazlowe the sponsor, why the gnomes got faster), Fizzle Brassbolts (the flying engine, Martek, the Brassbolts brothers, indurium from Uldaman), Kravel Koalbeard (how he makes a sure thing, what was in the stout; replies for priests, paladins and dwarves).
- Fixed: Razzeric is already the eighteenth pilot; the gnomes are the ones with indurium; Martek is a smith.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits bring personal lines (habits, worries, running jokes), and some NPCs get night-time lines. Abeqwa, Thalia, Rau.

## 1.0.1 (unreleased)
- Cliffwatcher Longhorn knows when you've met his brother Nataka in Desolace.

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- Freewind Post (Horde): Cliffwatcher Longhorn, Rau Cliffrunner, Hagar Lightninghoof, Thalia Amberhide and Innkeeper Abeqwa. Freewind's Braves.
- The Mirage Raceway (neutral): Pozzik, Fizzle Brassbolts and Kravel Koalbeard.
