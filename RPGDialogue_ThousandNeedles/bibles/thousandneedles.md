## Voices
- Longhorn: grim watcher; the Galak are a war, the Grimtotem a worry; misses his brother.
- Rau: scholar-druid; tablets and relics; the land speaks because the Circle taught him to listen.
- Hagar: eager, a little scattered; eggs.
- Thalia: bitter, short; hates that the Grimtotem are tauren.
- Abeqwa: gentle innkeeper; "don't look down".
- Pozzik: fast-talking goblin showman; wagers.
- Fizzle: excitable gnome engineer; MEGA; dodges Gnomeregan.
- Kravel: sly dwarf fixer; "nobody's rigged a thing".
