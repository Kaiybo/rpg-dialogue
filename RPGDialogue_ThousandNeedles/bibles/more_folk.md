# Thousand Needles: more folk (keys: myriam)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg: Myriam Moonsinger (night elf on a hill just south of the boar-skull entrance to Razorfen Downs; Classic: A Host of Evil, kill Razorfen Battleguards, Razorfen Thornweavers and Death's Head Cultists outside the instance. Quest text: the Forsaken do not pass on their illness, but "this diplomat" grants undeath to would-be followers; she suspects he is Scourge; followers "creep from the shadows daily, almost begging him for more power"; on completion she points to the cave that looks like a great boar maw).

## Their quests
- A Host of Evil (TODO_QUEST): before, during and after lines.

## Voice
- **Myriam Moonsinger.** Night elf scholar, watchful and grave, low-voiced; measured, a little formal, Elune and the Sentinels; frightened and steady at once. Will share her hill with the Horde because the Scourge is everyone's enemy.

## Authored, not canon
She came to study the quilboar and stayed to watch the cult; she has not gone inside.

## Never
Amnennar's name (she only knows "the diplomat"); anything after Year 1.

## Added later (keys: elu, wizlo, moktar, montarr, starn, jawn)

### Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg (Classic parts only): Elu <Wind Rider Apprentice> (Freewind Post; Wind Rider: Highperch wyvern eggs). Wizlo Bearingshiner (goblin at Whitereach Post; Hypercapacitor Gizmo, A Dip in the Moonwell, Testing the Tonic). ### Their quests
- Elu: Wind Rider (eggs from Highperch to raise as wind riders).
- Wizlo: Hypercapacitor Gizmo (in a panther's cage), A Dip in the Moonwell (his robot dips at Thalanaar), Testing the Tonic.
- Moktar: The Swarm Grows (silithid at the Rustmaul Dig Site; proof for Belgrom).
- Montarr, Starn, Jawn: none (Horn of the Traitor is Cataclysm; not used).

### Voices
- **Elu.** Eager, anxious, sweeps a great deal; one long nervous sentence at a time.
- **Wizlo Bearingshiner.** Goblin tinker: everything priced, fast, "pal", casual about what the tonic does.
- **Moktar Krin.** Grim, watchful, a soldier who followed orders and is uneasy about the result.
- **Montarr.** Thoughtful, dry; keeps names and where they were last spoken.
- **Starn.** Terse and exact; respects the bow, sells the gun.
- **Jawn Highmesa.** Genial, talkative; born on the mesa and counts the lift's trips.

### Authored, not canon
Elu's mother in Mulgore; Wizlo's robot that cannot turn left; Moktar's private doubt about the raid; Montarr's morning walk and the tale of the quarrelling herd; Starn's painted rock; Jawn's notched posts.

## Added later (keys: laer, synge, riznek, brivelthwerp, tirth)

### Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg (Classic parts only): Laer Stepperunner <Food & Drink> (tauren bread and drink vendor at Whitereach Post). Synge (goblin; before the Cataclysm a gun vendor at the Mirage Raceway). Riznek (goblin; before the Cataclysm a drink vendor at the Mirage Raceway). Brivelthwerp <Ice Cream Vendor> (gnome, neutral; at launch the only vendor of Tigule's Strawberry Ice Cream, at the Mirage Raceway). Magus Tirth (human mage, neutral; before the Cataclysm a chicken vendor at the Mirage Raceway selling the Ancona Chicken; wrote Rituals of Power and sold it over gambling debts to a member of the Scarlet Crusade; "Plucky" Johnson was his student; objective of the mage quests Get the Scoop and Rituals of Power).
- Jinky Twizzlefixxit <Engineering Supplies> is the raceway's Classic engineering vendor and is already in needles_folk.lua (Gagsprocket is Ratchet's, not the raceway's).

### Their quests
- Tirth: Get the Scoop, Rituals of Power (mage only; TODO_QUEST): he no longer has the book, and points to the Scarlet Monastery library.
- Laer, Synge, Riznek, Brivelthwerp: none: vendors, built from role, place and neighbours.

### Voices
- **Laer Stepperunner.** Gentle, curious tauren; feeds everyone, the goblin included; wonders whether Wizlo's machine has feelings (consistent with Wizlo's line about him).
- **Synge.** Goblin gun seller: pushy, cheerful, "pal"; worry is his best salesman; bets on goblin engineering out of loyalty.
- **Riznek.** Goblin drinks seller: slick, amiable; hears everything in the stands and repeats none of it.
- **Brivelthwerp.** Gnome: earnest, fretful, percentages and degrees; keeping ice cream frozen is an engineering problem.
- **Magus Tirth.** Pompous, irritable human scholar; "I wager, I do not bet"; ashamed of the book and will not look at the tally.

### Authored, not canon
Laer's bread left out for the Grimtotem and the seep found by following wasps; Synge's banded barrels and season of lost bets; Riznek's drawer of losing tickets and his shared cold store with Brivelthwerp; the salt-and-ice crate, the forty-one flavours and Zherin's noon ice cream; Tirth conjuring ice and water against his debts, the exploding goblin engine, and the fire elemental behind the gnomish pits.

### Never
Bilgewater Cartel (Synge and Riznek are raceway goblins only); the Speedbarge, the flood, the Ice Cream Empire; Tirth's madness and Tirth's Haunt; what "Plucky" did to earn the spell.
