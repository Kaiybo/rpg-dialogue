## Their quests
- Heather: none: innkeeper only.
- Lewis: A Swift Message, Return to Lewis.
- Thor: Continue to Stormwind (receives A Swift Message).
- Riell: Red Silk Bandanas.
- MacGregor: none: vendor only.

## Never
- Anything Cataclysm or later: the Westfall Brigade, Hope Saldean, the rebuilt Sentinel Tower, the Legion invasions.
- How William and Gina MacGregor are related.

## Voices (all authored; the wiki gives no personalities)
- Heather: tidy, tired, kind; the militia sleep in their boots.
- Lewis: harried quartermaster whose ledger counts robbed wagons.
- Thor: brisk, proud of his gryphons; dry jokes about cargo.
- Riell: watchful, terse; sees everyone from the tower.
- MacGregor: dry craftsman; strings are extra.
