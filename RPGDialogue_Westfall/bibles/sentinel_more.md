## Their quests
- none: all four are vendors. They react to The Killing Fields, Westfall Stew, Patrolling Westfall and the Deadmines deed.

## Never
- Anything Cataclysm or later (Westfall Brigade, the homeless camps, Hope Saldean, the Legion invasions).
- How Gina and William are related: both say "family" and no more.

## Voices (authored, not canon)
- Hewen: wry; reads the hill's health in thread versus dye; spools hidden under turnips; sold chalk line to the masons once and regrets it. Shares what gets through with Gina (honor stance). Former Westfall farmhands get a reply.
- Gina: brisk hard bargainer; charges what the robbed road costs; thinks Christopher undercharges (honor stance on her prices). Dwarves, gnomes and rogues get to haggle and lose.
- Kirk: calm, low-voiced; coyotes and goretusks, vultures that won't settle; the grey horse that carried a rider out of Moonbrook and won't go south (light stance on letting him rest). Hunters get a taming tip.
- Mike Miller: rueful baker using Elwynn flour in the old breadbasket; day-old loaves for walked-off farmers (light stance); sends you to Saldean. Priests and paladins may bless the batch.

## What they notice
- Hewen: tailors, smiths. Gina: cooks, the starving. Kirk: hunters' pets. Miller: hunger.
