## Their quests
- Report to Gryan Stoutmantle (from Elwynn): he takes whoever Elwynn sends.
- The People's Militia (series): clear Defias camps his scouts marked. He measures victories in farmers who sleep safely.
- The Defias Brotherhood (series): the hunt for the Brotherhood's leader, ending in the Deadmines. The steps share one title, so lines don't hinge on them; the Deadmines deed stands in.
- Messenger to Westfall (receives Solomon's plea, sends his answer): he refuses Lakeshire because Westfall would fall; grieves for good men lost.

## Identity
Gryan Stoutmantle <The People's Militia> · Sentinel Hill · Alliance · Human · questgiver
Personality: steady, dutiful, warm with farmers, blunt about the city, carries Lordaeron quietly
Speech: plain soldier's sentences, the occasional grim joke, never self-pitying
Knows and cares about: Westfall's land and families, the militia, the Defias, the Silver Hand's lessons
Relationships: Captain Danuvin and Scout Galiaan (his officers), the Saldeans and Furlbrows (his people), Magistrate Solomon (said no to him), Wiley the Black (useful, tiresome)

Lore anchors: Year 1; Silver Hand-trained; nothing from Cataclysm (no Westfall Brigade, no Vanessa VanCleef)
Never says: what he did at Stratholme; that soldiers are coming from the city
