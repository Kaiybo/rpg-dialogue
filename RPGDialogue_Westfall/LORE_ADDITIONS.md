**Corrections to LORE_NOTES.md (Westfall lore sheet):**
- "The watchers' origin isn't given: never state it" is superseded by Wiley's Note (Classic item): goblins built them for the Defias to frighten superstitious farmers. Saldean still doesn't know; Gryan learns it from the note (reply gated on hearing Wiley's `h_defias`); Galiaan guesses it.
- Gryan: "Westfall's noble house was corrupted" should read "Stormwind's House of Nobles (corrupted by Prestor) gave Westfall no support". Gryan's `s_default` now says "The House of Nobles went rotten".
- Goretusk Liver Pie: the quest page gives Salma as giver (the questing guide lists Verna). Lines treat it as Salma's.
- Theodore's pocket watch was Verna's father's wedding gift (the 1.0 line said his own father's; fixed).
- Quartermaster Lewis now has his quests (A Swift Message, Return to Lewis) in his file.

**Assumptions (confirm in game):**
- Every step of The People's Militia, The Defias Brotherhood and Messenger to Westfall shares one title, and a TODO title resolves to one ID, so `questActive` on those titles may track only one step. Lines that use them say nothing step-specific ("you're on the trail already", "about Solomon's letter").
- Thor's "Continue to Stormwind" reply assumes he still offers that step in Forever.

**Year 1 notes:** Bolvar is Regent ("someone keeps telling the Regent Westfall can wait": Prestor is never named). Queen Tiffin's death in the riots is later-published backstory and is not used. The Juggernaut is not mentioned by name; Gryan and Wiley only talk of "what they're building under Moonbrook". Nothing about the Brigade, Northrend, Vanessa or the homeless.
