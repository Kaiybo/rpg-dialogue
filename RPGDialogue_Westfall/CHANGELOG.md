# RPG Dialogue: Westfall (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.4.0 (unreleased)
- Depth rewrite: all fourteen people of Sentinel Hill and the farms are now a hub with branches two to four nodes deep, "what else?" lines in their own voices, and their own Ask about takes (their story, advice, their work where it fits). Every one meets the depth standard.
- Gryan: the holdouts who still send the city a tithe, Lordaeron (he says he was at Stratholme and nothing more), the House of Nobles, the hunt for the Defias leader (Wiley's debt, Wiley's note on the goblin-built watchers, the Stonemasons' grievance), the militia and its oath, Solomon's plea, and what comes after VanCleef.
- For a priest: Gryan has no chaplain for his dead (or the Defias dead) and hears that masons built the Cathedral; Salma asks a grace over the pot; Verna wants a prayer for Blanchy; Theodore hears that fear isn't a sin; Grayson's crew get a prayer, though he won't leave his lamp; Daphne prays in her orchard; Heather points you at the militia who don't sleep; Saldean wants his fields cleared before they're blessed.
- Replies for other characters: paladins (Gryan's oath, Rall's lesson at Daphne's farm, Saldean, Theodore, Verna), Westfall farmhands (Gryan, Danuvin, Saldean, Theodore), minor nobles (Gryan, Danuvin), warriors (Danuvin's drill, Lewis's ammunition), rogues (Gryan, Galiaan, Riell, Lewis), hunters and druids (Galiaan, Verna, MacGregor), cooks (Salma), Forsaken (Grayson), dwarves (Thor, Daphne), gnomes (Saldean), night elves (Riell).
- Voice pass: every line in the pack (the people, the militia and guard pools, Verna's errand) now speaks in full, joined sentences instead of clipped fragments, in the register of Classic quest text; the choppiness check finds none.
- Corrections from the Classic quest pages: Theodore's pocket watch was a wedding gift from Verna's father; Gryan blames the House of Nobles, not Westfall's own lords; Lewis's swift message asks Osric Strang in Old Town for armor; Salma bakes the goretusk liver pie and the stew is Verna's recipe.
