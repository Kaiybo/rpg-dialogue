- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Stonard (founded by the Old Horde in the Black Morass after the Dark Portal first opened, one of Azeroth's oldest orc settlements; rebuilt by the new Horde; the only Horde outpost nearby; a base for the Temple of Atal'Hakkar; near the Dark Portal; Dreadmaul ogres took Rockard; the swamp largely uncharted, cartographers and scouts sent out (Classic); Classic NPCs Bengor, Fel'zerul, Helgrum the Swift; Stonard Grunts)

- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Helgrum_the_Swift (orc quest giver; Neeka Bloodscar, Report to Helgrum; his companion Neeka)

## Their quests
- Ruag: The Disgraced One, The Missing Orders. Fel'zerul: Pool of Tears, The Atal'ai Exile, Return to Fel'Zerul, The Temple of Atal'Hakkar.
- Helgrum: Neeka Bloodscar, Report to Helgrum. Bengor: The Missing Orders, The Swamp Talker. Karakul: none.

## Never
- The Alliance campaign, Marshtide Watch, the Bloodmire, Helgrum's death (Cataclysm) — and no foreshadowing of any of it.
- Hakkar's return or Zul'Gurub (not in Year 1). Fel'zerul says only that something old stirs.
- What the Swamp Talker is, what the missing orders said, who the disgraced one is.

## Voices
- Ruag: methodical dispatcher; maps and reports.
- Fel'zerul: grim warlock who kept to the Warchief's line.
- Helgrum: restless, fast, fond of Neeka (authored).
- Bengor: stoic old soldier, refuses to call his illness one (authored from his status).
- Karakul: gruff innkeeper.
