## Depth rewrite (Horde towns, authored on sourced quests)
Sources read for this pass (warcraft.wiki.gg, Classic sections): Dispatch_Commander_Ruag, Fel'zerul, Helgrum_the_Swift, Bengor, Innkeeper_Karakul, Stonard, Fallen_Hero_of_the_Horde, Neeka_Bloodscar, The_Disgraced_One, The_Missing_Orders, The_Swamp_Talker, Pool_of_Tears_(Horde), The_Atal'ai_Exile, Return_to_Fel'Zerul, The_Temple_of_Atal'Hakkar_(quest), Neeka_Bloodscar_(Classic), Report_to_Helgrum.
- Ruag: Stonard gives the Horde an advantage in exploration and strikes (gossip). The Disgraced One is given by the Fallen Hero of the Horde near the Blasted Lands and turned in to Ruag: the regiment Thrall's Grasp was destroyed, "the Horde lost some of its best soldiers". The Missing Orders: Ruag insists he relayed the orders; the carriers were ambushed by murlocs, one survived (Bengor), who can hardly speak, cannot walk, is fed through a tube. Ruag is defensive and warns the player about sticking their nose in.
- Bengor: the survivor. The Swamp Talker (a murloc) took the orders as a spear went into his skull; "East... cave". The Fallen Hero: "Nineteen of my men could have been spared."
- Stonard: Old Horde outpost in the Black Morass after the Portal first opened, sister town Rockard now the Dreadmaul ogres' hold; great hall; wayfinders and cartographers map the swamp (Classic).
- Fel'zerul: Thrall sent them to investigate; they found the temple already sunk; green dragons guard it, claiming to protect Azeroth. Atal'ai: dark casters of forbidden magic. A scroll in blood on dried flesh sentenced an Atal'ai priest to exile with the Witherbark in the Hinterlands. Jammal'an the Prophet believes Hakkar's return will grant immortality; Fel'zerul: "foolish trickery to bring about a premature doomsday"; the fetishes must be seized and understood by the Horde.
- Helgrum: Neeka Bloodscar is head scout of Kargath; communication between Stonard and Kargath is often cut (the Badlands lie close to Loch Modan); her last outrunner failed. Report to Helgrum: the Alliance has problems of its own, and some enemies may be shared.
- Fixed in this pass: Helgrum no longer treats Neeka as missing; Bengor no longer speaks fluently or calls the Swamp Talker a story (he is the ambush survivor); Ruag's Disgraced One and Missing Orders lines now match the quests (he is the turn-in, then defensive about the orders).
- Authored, not canon: Ruag's guilt over the papers, the bowl Karakul sets out for late scouts, Helgrum and Neeka's teasing, Bengor's halting speech (written as whole sentences slowed by commas, per CONTENT_GUIDE §2).
