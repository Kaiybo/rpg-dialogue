# Stonard: the tradesfolk (keys: hekkru, thultash, rartar, banalash, thralosh, grimnal, hartash, gharash)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg Stonard page (NPC roster) and NPC pages: Hekkru <Stable Master> (id 10049), Thultash <Food & Drink>, Rartar <Alchemy Supplies>, Banalash <Trade Supplies>, Thralosh <Cloth & Leather Armor Merchant>, Grimnal <Mail & Plate Merchant>, Hartash <Weapon Merchant>, Gharash <Blacksmithing Supplies>. All orc males in Stonard; none has quests or gossip of their own on record.
- Stonard lore from the wiki page: one of the oldest orc settlements, caravans to Stranglethorn twice a year, weaponsmiths who make good blades from traded ore, the cartographers and wayfinders mapping the swamp.

## Voices
**Hekkru (stable master).** Patient and gruff, kinder to beasts than owners. Obsessed with dry straw and foot rot.
**Thultash (food and drink).** Jovial; the town's cook and its ear for gossip. Crocolisk stew, salted stores.
**Rartar (alchemy supplies).** Fussy and careful; keeps the shelf in order, sweeps up after Rogvar and counts his scorch marks.
**Banalash (trade supplies).** Shrewd ledger-keeper with a goblin-taught habit of pricing aloud; buys from Booty Bay via Grom'gol.
**Thralosh (cloth and leather).** Dry; argues daily with Grimnal that plate drowns grunts in the swamp.
**Grimnal (mail and plate).** Proud old soldier; plate saves lives; the dented breastplate over his door.
**Hartash (weapons).** Blunt; tests every edge on his thumb; prefers short blades for the swamp.
**Gharash (blacksmithing supplies).** Quiet, steady smith; fights the damp; works ore with Hartash.

## Authored, not canon
- Hekkru: the stables stand on old-Horde stone footings; lost a worg to foot rot his first season; kept wolves for a Barrens raiding band.
- Thultash: stirred gruel in the internment camps and followed the freed orcs; a hungry winter when the caravans failed and his salted stores since; the cartographers' four maps pinned on Ruag's wall; owes Banalash "forty bowls" of stew for salt.
- Rartar: a firebloom jar spilled into swamp moss closed the hall for a day; keeps volatile stock under his bed; mixed salves in the camps.
- Banalash: once ran supplies for a war band; buys sailors' waxed thread.
- Thralosh: oils leather with crocolisk fat from Thultash's kitchen; a tanner for a lost clan.
- Grimnal: Second War veteran; the Dreadmaul-dented breastplate hung over his door that grunts touch for luck.
- Hartash: sharpened axes for the Warsong before they sailed for Kalimdor.
- Gharash: iron in the hills south under Dreadmaul ground that Ruag won't let miners near.
