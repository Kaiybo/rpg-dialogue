## Their quests
- Biggs, Encroaching Wildlife (gives/ends): the caravan was bound for the eastern coast; Lost Ones killed the guards; he alone survived and swore to protect what remains; beasts close in. Fear, duty.
- Biggs, The Lost Caravan: stunned in the attack, saw a wagon dragged to the Fallow Sanctuary to the north; the chest holds wizards' reagents vital to Nethergarde; he doesn't know what the wizards do, only that it is linked to the Dark Portal and vital to the Alliance.
- Biggs, Driftwood: needs sun-dried driftwood from the east coast to fix the wagon; swamp wood is spongy; "an honorable heart beats within you".
- Biggs, Deliver the Shipment (gives; ends at Lungertz): he packed the most vital supplies "saved from the ogre attacks" and gives road directions; Lungertz thought the caravan lost; Azoran crystals.
- Magtoor, Draenethyst Crystals (gives/ends, neutral): the Lost Ones of the Fallow Sanctuary look as we look but are monsters, driven mad by the journey to Azeroth; they stole the sacred draenethyst; "without those crystals... we too may lose our way".
- Magtoor, Noboru the Cudgel (ends): Noboru and his gang killed any draenei who crossed his path; "a joyous day for the Harborage"; "fortunate to have a friend in you".
- Masat: none (vendor).

## Identity
- Watcher Biggs · human male · Alliance (Stormwind) · footman/caravan watcher. Personality: earnest, weary, dutiful, guilty. Speech: plain Stormwind soldier, "friend", prays to the Light. Never says anything about what the Nethergarde mages actually do.
- Magtoor · draenei (Lost One model in Classic) · neutral · leader of the Harborage. Personality: grave, gentle, patient, haunted. Speech: slow, formal, no contractions. Never says "Broken", "Velen", "Exodar", "naaru" or anything about Draenor's later history (TBC).
- Masat T'andr · draenei · neutral · leatherworker. Personality: quiet, precise, wry. Speech: short careful sentences, "I think".

## Authored, not canon
- Biggs: his guilt as the watcher; names of the dead written inside his shield; a Goldshire teamster who sang; the caravan came past Deadwind; the Night Watch saw them off; Biggs from Stormwind's Old Town; no beasts left to pull the wagon. The quest texts mention both Lost Ones and "ogre attacks"; the lines name only the Lost Ones for the ambush.
- Magtoor: the draenei came "through the gate the orcs made" (vague on purpose; the Classic quest says only "our journey to Azeroth"); Noboru was a smith who sang before the madness; a goblin once tried to buy the crystals; he does not hold the gate against Horde players.
- Masat: forgets names but not the craft; buys green whelp scales from hunters and worries the temple's guardians mind; haggles happily with a goblin regular.
- The Harborage is assumed present in Classic (Noboru's Classic quest names it).
