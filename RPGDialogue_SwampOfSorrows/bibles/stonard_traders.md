## Identity
- **Hekkru** · stable master. Stolid, dry, dutiful; ex-soldier who prefers beasts. Fights damp, leeches, rot; fights Thultash for straw. Relationships: Ruag, Malosh ("going soft"), Thultash.
- **Thultash** · food & drink. Jovial grumbler; supply caravans from Grom'gol past Deadwind Pass; keeps a hidden store. Relationships: Karakul (hall), Tok'Kar (blames him for drunk soldiers), Katar, Dar.
- **Thultazor** · vials and reagents. Fussy, precise; war on the damp (wax, oilcloth, shelves). Relationships: Rartar (rival vial seller), Thultash (ruined crate), Rogvar's students.
- **Hartash** · weapons. Gruff, boastful; oils every edge with crocolisk fat; sells against the Dreadmaul; Rockard. Relationships: Malosh's recruits, Ruag.
- **Grimnal** · mail & plate. Dour, stubborn; "plate keeps you alive". Friendly feud with Thralosh.
- **Thralosh** · cloth & leather. Wry, easygoing; "speed keeps you alive"; crocolisk-oil leather. Friendly feud with Grimnal.
