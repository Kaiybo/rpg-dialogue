## Their quests
- Haromm, Elemental Mastery (gives, shamans 50+): sends shamans to Bath'rah. Reverence, teaching.
- Ogromm, Grokor, Kartosh: none.

## Identity
- Haromm · orc male · shaman trainer. Calm, grave, reverent; "the elements must be asked". Speech: formal, few contractions.
- Ogromm · orc male · hunter trainer. Curt with non-hunters, patient with hunters. Whole short sentences, never fragments.
- Grokor · orc male · pet trainer. Chatty, warm, talks to pets first.
- Kartosh · orc male · warlock trainer. Cold, precise, keeps to the Warchief's terms and watches Greshka.

## What they notice
- Haromm: shamans (totem grip), warlocks (fel smell), tauren. Ogromm: hunters' pets, troll hunters. Grokor: pets, hunters without pets. Kartosh: warlock's imp, Light-users, Forsaken warlocks.

## Authored, not canon
- Haromm: the water "grieves" for the drowned temple; he keeps clear of the temple, where the earth flinches; teaches students to whisper (echoes his later description of teaching shamans to whisper reverently, kept as character only).
- Ogromm: lay all night with a leech on his neck to take a shadow panther.
- Grokor: the cat in Hekkru's back pen (shared with Hekkru's bible).
- Kartosh: the Warchief's terms (serve the Horde, no demon masters its summoner); old Horde warlocks were "masters" in Stonard's halls; he and Greshka watch each other; Fel'zerul keeps the line more strictly than he does (consistent with Greshka's authored quarrel with Fel'zerul in swampofsorrows.md).
