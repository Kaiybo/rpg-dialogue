- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Stonard (NPC list; read: Kartosh <Warlock Trainer>, Ogromm <Hunter Trainer>, Grokor <Pet Trainer>, Rogvar <Alchemy Trainer>, all orc males; Stonard formerly home of the Horde master alchemist)

- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Ogromm (read: non-hunter gossip "Don't waste my time.")

- Pack bible swampofsorrows.md for the town (Ruag, Bengor, Greshka, Fel'zerul, Dar, Tok'Kar, Karakul, Breyk, Malosh).

## Their quests
- None of the four gives quests. Service tier.

## Identity
- **Kartosh** · Stonard · Horde · orc · warlock trainer. Calm, severe, wary, disciplined. Speaks softly, as if overheard; jailer's metaphors (leash, keys, prisoner). Cares about humility, control, the old Horde's damnation; the Dark Portal to the south. Relationships: Greshka (disapproves of her selling to anyone), Fel'zerul (respects; "the Warchief's man"), Karakul (seats them apart). Never: praises Gul'dan; names Outland or anything past the Portal.
- **Ogromm** · hunter trainer. Curt, gruff, proud, patient in the field. "Don't waste my time." Reads tracks the water can't hide (reeds, frogs, birds). Hunts for the town, not sport. Relationships: Grokor (rival who spoils beasts; secretly admits his beasts fight well), Tok'Kar, Dar, Thultash.
- **Grokor** · pet trainer. Warm, patient, gentle; looks at the beast before the owner. Rode wolves as a boy before the internment camps; misses wolves. Swamp beasts: jaguars, sawtooth crocolisks, deathstrike tarantulas. Relationships: Ogromm, Tok'Kar, Dar.
- **Rogvar** · alchemy trainer. Cheerful, earnest, curious, the only orc glad to be in Stonard. Thrall asked him to come (canon gossip). Herbs: Blindweed, Sungrass, Liferoot. Relationships: Bengor (brews him a pain draught), Karakul (the smell), Rartar and Thultazor (vial sellers), Breyk (riders carry flasks north), the Undercity's Royal Apothecaries (letters asking for samples).

## Threads he could carry
- None yet.

## Agenda
- Kartosh: keep young warlocks humble. Ogromm: feed the town. Grokor: see beasts fed first. Rogvar: a wound-rot salve that works.

## Secrets
- Ogromm: admits Grokor's beasts fight well (authored). Kartosh learned from "someone who should never have been allowed to teach" (authored).

## Reliability
All honest; Kartosh withholding about his teacher, Grokor about the camps.

## What they notice   (2–3 things)
- Kartosh: warlock brimstone; shamans at the door. Ogromm: hunter's pet sniffing toward the Pool of Tears; druids. Grokor: muddy pet; warlock demons near the pens. Rogvar: alchemists' green fingers; herbalists' packs.

## Topics
background, advice, _none for each.

## Authored, not canon
Kartosh and Greshka's quiet feud and Karakul seating them apart; Kartosh's buried students; Ogromm and Grokor's rivalry; Grokor's childhood wolves; Rogvar's green flask, his draught for Bengor, his salves in the camps, Apothecary letters by zeppelin, the Rartar/Thultazor vial quarrel.
# Stonard's trainers  (keys: haromm, ogromm, grokor, kartosh)

File: `npcs/stonard_trainers.lua`.

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Earthbreaker_Haromm (read; Classic section only: shaman trainer in Stonard, the only Horde shaman trainer in the Eastern Kingdoms in Classic, started Elemental Mastery; his later fall to dark shamanism is Mists of Pandaria and never hinted at)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Elemental_Mastery_(quest) (read: Classic, shaman level 50+; bring air, fire, earth and water to Bath'rah the Windwatcher, up the river north of Tarren Mill; elementals near the Circles of Binding in Arathi)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Ogromm (read: hunter trainer; non-hunter gossip "Don't waste my time.")
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Grokor (read: pet trainer; gossip "You've come for training in order to pass it on to your pets?", "Ah friend, I only help hunters and their pets." The pet-specialization gossip is Wrath and not used)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kartosh (read: warlock trainer; gossip "You know enough to be dangerous, but you still look like you need training to me", "Your will is not strong enough to manipulate the forces we control")

## Their quests
- Haromm, Elemental Mastery (gives, shamans 50+): sends shamans to Bath'rah. Reverence, teaching.
- Ogromm, Grokor, Kartosh: none.

## Identity
- Haromm · orc male · shaman trainer. Calm, grave, reverent; "the elements must be asked". Speech: formal, few contractions.
- Ogromm · orc male · hunter trainer. Curt with non-hunters, patient with hunters. Whole short sentences, never fragments.
- Grokor · orc male · pet trainer. Chatty, warm, talks to pets first.
- Kartosh · orc male · warlock trainer. Cold, precise, keeps to the Warchief's terms and watches Greshka.

## What they notice
- Haromm: shamans (totem grip), warlocks (fel smell), tauren. Ogromm: hunters' pets, troll hunters. Grokor: pets, hunters without pets. Kartosh: warlock's imp, Light-users, Forsaken warlocks.

## Authored, not canon
- Haromm: the water "grieves" for the drowned temple; he keeps clear of the temple, where the earth flinches; teaches students to whisper (echoes his later description of teaching shamans to whisper reverently, kept as character only).
- Ogromm: lay all night with a leech on his neck to take a shadow panther.
- Grokor: the cat in Hekkru's back pen (shared with Hekkru's bible).
- Kartosh: the Warchief's terms (serve the Horde, no demon masters its summoner); old Horde warlocks were "masters" in Stonard's halls; he and Greshka watch each other; Fel'zerul keeps the line more strictly than he does (consistent with Greshka's authored quarrel with Fel'zerul in swampofsorrows.md).
