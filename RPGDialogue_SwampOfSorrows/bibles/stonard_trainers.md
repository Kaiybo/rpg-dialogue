## Identity
- **Kartosh** · Stonard · Horde · orc · warlock trainer. Calm, severe, wary, disciplined. Speaks softly, as if overheard; jailer's metaphors (leash, keys, prisoner). Cares about humility, control, the old Horde's damnation; the Dark Portal to the south. Relationships: Greshka (disapproves of her selling to anyone), Fel'zerul (respects; "the Warchief's man"), Karakul (seats them apart). Never: praises Gul'dan; names Outland or anything past the Portal.
- **Ogromm** · hunter trainer. Curt, gruff, proud, patient in the field. "Don't waste my time." Reads tracks the water can't hide (reeds, frogs, birds). Hunts for the town, not sport. Relationships: Grokor (rival who spoils beasts; secretly admits his beasts fight well), Tok'Kar, Dar, Thultash.
- **Grokor** · pet trainer. Warm, patient, gentle; looks at the beast before the owner. Rode wolves as a boy before the internment camps; misses wolves. Swamp beasts: jaguars, sawtooth crocolisks, deathstrike tarantulas. Relationships: Ogromm, Tok'Kar, Dar.
- **Rogvar** · alchemy trainer. Cheerful, earnest, curious, the only orc glad to be in Stonard. Thrall asked him to come (canon gossip). Herbs: Blindweed, Sungrass, Liferoot. Relationships: Bengor (brews him a pain draught), Karakul (the smell), Rartar and Thultazor (vial sellers), Breyk (riders carry flasks north), the Undercity's Royal Apothecaries (letters asking for samples).
