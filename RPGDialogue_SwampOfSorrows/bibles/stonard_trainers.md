# Stonard: the trainers (keys: ogromm, grokor, kartosh, rogvar)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg NPC pages: Ogromm <Hunter Trainer> (id 987; non-hunter gossip "Don't waste my time."), Grokor <Pet Trainer>, Kartosh <Warlock Trainer> (id 988; gossip "You know enough to be dangerous, but you still look like you need training..." and "Your will is not strong enough to manipulate the forces we control"; linked on the wiki to Greshka), Rogvar <Alchemy Trainer>, all orc males in Stonard.
- Swamp herbs (blindweed, liferoot) and the Blasted Lands bloodmages as Classic setting.

## Voices
**Ogromm.** Blunt hunter who never stops watching the treeline; respects a clean kill.
**Grokor.** Gentle; talks to the beasts first; trust before tricks.
**Kartosh.** Cold, sharp, proud; speaks of non-warlocks as furniture; "I command the demon, the demon does not command me." Works with Greshka.
**Rogvar.** Excitable, scorched bench, thin eyebrows; the swamp is a garden to him; wary of the Forsaken apothecaries' brews.

## Authored, not canon
- Ogromm: lost a scout to a jaguar at dusk and stands watch every evening; hunted for a Barrens war band.
- Grokor: raised in a war band's kennels; a young troll hunter's tamed crocolisk that Thultash nearly cooked.
- Kartosh: learned the art in the old Horde's last days; goes into the Blasted Lands when he must; he and Greshka decide which young warlocks "will live long enough to matter".
- Rogvar: lost his eyebrows to a fire elixir; learned to brew in the internment camps; refuses blindweed to Forsaken apothecaries.

## Never
No later shaman/portal trainers (Mok, Lorrin Foxfire, Cersei Dusksinger are post-Classic).
