- Lack of Surplus (turn-in from Dar; gives part 2): stationed to warn Stonard of murlocs; no time to relax; sawtooth claws make fine weapons; thanks for "the fresh meat and the good company".
- Threat From the Sea (gives): sends the player to Katar rather than "getting drunk with some of the other louses I share this camp with".
- Threat From the Sea (turn-in, part 3): offers a weapon "for bringing a small smile to Katar's face".
