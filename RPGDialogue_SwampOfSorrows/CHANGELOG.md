# RPG Dialogue: Swamp of Sorrows (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.12.0 (2026-11-16)
- Rogues, mages and warlocks are noticed: named people here greet them as such, with a question to ask and something to argue about. No new files. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.43.0.

## 1.11.0 (2026-11-16)
- Horde shaman get their own conversation with 10 people in Stonard and the swamp.

## 1.10.0 (2026-11-16)
- Horde hunters with a beast out hear their companion named by the people who already talked hunting.
- Innkeeper Karakul now has replies for Horde warriors and hunters.

## 1.9.0 (2026-11-16)
- Druids get their own greeting, question and short conversation with several people here.
- Horde warriors and hunters get their own question and short conversation with several people here.

## 1.8.0 (2026-11-16)
- New people: Galen Goodward and the Fallen Hero of the Horde.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-16)
- Stonard: Dar, Breyk, Greshka (grimoires for warlocks only) and Malosh; Innkeeper Karakul talks about the regulars.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-15)
- Replies for the classes and races these people would really react to (warriors, hunters, rogues, mages, warlocks, druids, shamans, paladins; every race of the faction, and the Skyborne), each leading somewhere.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Every person here writes a first impression in your diary after you first talk (the journal's People page shows it too).

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-15)
- Depth for Stonard: Dispatch Commander Ruag, Fel'zerul, Helgrum the Swift, Bengor and Innkeeper Karakul, built from their Classic quests (the Fallen Hero's chain: The Disgraced One, The Missing Orders, The Swamp Talker; the Pool of Tears and the Atal'ai exile; Neeka Bloodscar's report).
- Ruag on Thrall's Grasp, the orders he insists he sent, Stonard's old Horde past and its lost sister fortress Rockard; Fel'zerul on the sunken temple, the green dragons, the Atal'ai and Jammal'an's prophecy; Helgrum on the road to Kargath and Neeka; Bengor, the ambush's only survivor, on the Swamp Talker and his comrades.
- Replies for who you are: warlocks and troll warlocks (Fel'zerul), orcs (Ruag, Karakul), healers (Bengor), hunters, shamans and druids (Helgrum offers them a race); replies for players who've met Aggro'gosh or recovered the orders.
- Fixes: Bengor spoke fluently and called the Swamp Talker a story, though his quest has him barely able to speak and the Swamp Talker is the murloc who took the orders; Helgrum treated Neeka as missing, though she's Kargath's head scout; Ruag's lines for The Disgraced One and The Missing Orders now match the quests.

## 1.1.1 (unreleased)

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
