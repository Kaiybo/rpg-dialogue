## 1.2.0 (2026-11-15)

- Replies for who you are: warlocks and troll warlocks (Fel'zerul), orcs (Ruag, Karakul), healers (Bengor), hunters, shamans and druids (Helgrum offers them a race); replies for players who've met Aggro'gosh or recovered the orders.
- Fixes: Bengor spoke fluently and called the Swamp Talker a story, though his quest has him barely able to speak and the Swamp Talker is the murloc who took the orders; Helgrum treated Neeka as missing, though she's Kargath's head scout; Ruag's lines for The Disgraced One and The Missing Orders now match the quests.
