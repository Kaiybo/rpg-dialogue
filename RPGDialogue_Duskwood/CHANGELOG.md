# RPG Dialogue: Duskwood (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.25.0 (2026-11-18)
- New NPCs from the Classic-era roster (`npcs/more_folk.lua`). New files: restart the game fully.
- NPCs notice what you have done: quests for their neighbours, notable kills, your deaths, rank and reputation.
- Writing pass: stance replies end on something concrete, fewer echoes and stock phrases.
- Druid greetings that assume a night elf or tauren no longer fire for other druid races.

## 1.24.0 (2026-11-18)
- Writing pass: repeated phrases cut and replaced with concrete detail.
- Clarity pass: answers answer the question, names are introduced, quest lines stand alone, fewer contradictions.
- Some NPCs of the opposite sex flirt with you, and remember how you answered (needs core 0.83.0).
- NPCs who had only a few lines now have full conversations.
- NPCs answer each stance reply before the talk moves on.

## 1.23.0 (2026-11-17)
- Named NPCs answer many more "Ask about" topics (3 to 6 each, more for leaders), mostly the new world topics added in World 1.36.0.
- Prejudices (0.80.0): Commander Althea Ebonlocke (rogues). They let it show, you can push back or win them round, and it fades as they warm to you. No new files: a /reload is enough.
- New people to talk to: Town Crier, Finbus Geargrind, Malissa, Blind Mary, Watcher Dodds.
- New files: restart the game fully (a /reload won't load them).
- Origin nods (core 0.81.0): people here now notice the newer backgrounds from 0.70.0.

## 1.3.0 (unreleased)

- Depth: every Darkshire and Raven Hill NPC is now a conversation to explore, with several subjects that each go two or three steps deep and come back with "Something else". Ello, Althea and Smitts have the most; Trelayne and Clarise a little more than before.
- For a priest: Ello and the untended graves of Raven Hill, Althea's wounded watchers, a blessing for Smitts' regulars, Eva's question (do the dead answer you?), praying with Jitters for the woman whose name he never knew, Sven ("the Light can have the nights"), Abercrombie refusing rites at his wife's grave, Jonathan's "Glory under the Light", Calor's one-line prayer, Sirra on the priest who sent Morgan Ladimore to the graves.
- Others who get their own replies: paladins and warriors (Ello, Calor), warlocks (Eva, Abercrombie), rogues (Jitters), mages (Sirra), druids (Jonathan), gnomes (Viktori), alchemists, cooks and engineers, minor nobles (Ello, Millstipe), Lordaeron refugees (Sirra), Westfall farmhands (Sven).
- Quests: the Morbent Fel chain (Sven's Revenge, Proving Your Worth, Seeking Wisdom and Bishop Farthing, the lightforge bane), Abercrombie's errands (Ogre Thieves, Supplies from Darkshire, Ghost Hair Thread and Blind Mary), The Shadowy Search Continues, Raven Hill, Morgan Ladimore and Mor'Ladim get new lines while they're under way or once they're done.
- Fixes for Classic: Ello doesn't know Abercrombie and can't read his note; Stitches and the Embalmer's heart now follow Translation to Ello and Bride of the Embalmer (The Embalmer's Revenge is a later quest), for the watchers too; Sven's "it's done" follows the quest Morbent Fel, not Sven's Revenge; Daltry's book line follows The Shadowy Search Continues; Sirra hands over the book on Morgan Ladimore rather than having written it.
- "Ask about": everyone has their own story and advice, and takes on Morbent Fel, the Embalmer, the Dark Riders and Morgan Ladimore where they'd have one.
