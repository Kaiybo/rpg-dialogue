# Duskwood, missed folk  (keys: benjamin_carevin, lohgan_eva, watcher_paige)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Duskwood_NPCs (roster scan); Classic-range NPC ids.
- Benjamin_Carevin (read: human child, with his family in Darkshire; relatives Scott, Jonathan and Elaine Carevin).
- Lohgan_Eva (read: Tailoring Supplies, Darkshire; grandson of Madame Eva, brother of Alyssa Eva).
- Watcher_Paige (read: Night Watch, by the central crossroad in Duskwood).
- Existing pack: darkshire_folk.md (Jonathan Carevin's household hunts the dead beyond the lamps; Calor), darkshire_more.md (Elaine keeps lists; Alyssa rolls her eyes at the cards), more_folk.md (Scott sells mushrooms; the other watchers), eva.md, althea.md.

## Their quests
- None. Benjamin teaches `carevins` and `raven_hill`; Lohgan `night_watch`; Paige `night_watch`, `raven_hill`, `worgen`.

## Identity / voice
- Benjamin Carevin · boy · earnest, watchful; listens on the stairs; counts the hunters out and back in.
- Lohgan Eva · human man · wry, patient; the plain one between a seer and a sceptic; sells cloaks on a tally he never expects paid.
- Watcher Paige · human woman · brisk, wary; "torch, horn and sword, in that order".

- Paige alone at the crossing with a horn.
