# Darkshire, more folk  (keys: berrybuck, scott_carevin, watcher_callahan, watcher_brownell, watcher_keefer, watcher_mocarski, watcher_royce, watcher_hartin)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Duskwood_NPCs (roster); Classic presence from Classic-range NPC ids (Berrybuck 271, Scott Carevin 3138, Callahan 2142, Brownell 11040, Keefer 495, Mocarski 827, Royce 999, Hartin 886). Legion (Veiled Hand) sections not used.
- Ambassador_Berrybuck (read: Council of Darkshire, town hall; the Cataclysm quest Ambushed In the Forest and the Legion betrayal not used).
- Scott_Carevin (read: Mushroom Seller, Darkshire). Relation to Elaine/Jonathan never stated (darkshire_more.md rule).
- Watcher_Callahan (read: on the road toward Redridge, guarding Kzixx and his wagon; his quests are Cataclysm, not used). Kzixx is in `darkshire_shops.lua`.
- Watcher_Brownell (read: female; has a crush on Watcher Backus). Watcher_Mocarski (read: guards the southern entrance). Watcher_Keefer, Watcher_Royce, Watcher_Hartin (read: Night Watch in Darkshire; nothing else).
- Existing pack: althea.md (Commander, lamp oil and gratitude), darkshire_folk.md (Millstipe's grievances), watcher_backus.md.

## Their quests
- None in Classic.

## Identity / voice
- Ambassador Berrybuck · human man · pompous but sincere; letters to the House of Nobles that go unanswered.
- Scott Carevin · human man · cheerful mushroom man; "somebody in the family has to do something without a crossbow".
- Watcher Callahan · human man · wry; not sure whether he guards the road or the goblin; doesn't take the goblin's coin (he says).
- Watcher Brownell · human woman · young, earnest; wants the western road; admires Backus and denies anything more.
- Watcher Keefer · human man · gruff old hand; tired; counts no more.
- Watcher Mocarski · human man · nervous at the south gate, holds it anyway; nervousness as one run-on sentence.
- Watcher Royce · human man · the Watch's joker; gallows puns.
- Watcher Hartin · human man · devout; prays on rounds.

## Authored, not canon
- Berrybuck was a Stormwind clerk once; a drawer full of letters thanking Darkshire for its patience.
- Scott picks near the graveyards by morning.
- Brownell's father's farm went dark; Mocarski three months on the gate; Keefer since the Watch was formed; Hartin was meant for the Church.

## Never
- Veiled Hand, Legion, Cataclysm Darkshire, Nightmare Incursions (Season of Discovery).

## Deepening pass and added NPCs  (new keys: watcher_paige, watcher_jan, watcher_petras, watcher_bukouris, watcher_keller, watcher_fraizer, watcher_wollpert, avette_fellwood, lohgan_eva, bliztik)

### Identity / voice
- Watcher Paige · woman · alone at the crossroads, keeps a tally of who takes which road.
- Watcher Jan · man · fills and counts the Watch's lamps; oil from Stormwind or from Kzixx.
- Watcher Petras · man · ex-woodcutter; reads the mud at the town's edge each morning.
- Watcher Bukouris · woman · brisk; sees to the Watch's arms; Dodds's pitchfork.
- Watcher Keller · man · stiff, by the book; town hall steps; wrote down the nine standing orders.
- Watcher Fraizer · man · chatty; patrols by the Scarlet Raven and hears its talk.
- Watcher Wollpert · man · grim; the cemetery road toward Tranquil Gardens; Stitches came up it.
- Avette Fellwood · woman · calm bowyer; sells to the Carevins and the Watch.

- Existing eight: added a second branch each (letters, the round, lamps, the orchard road) and replies for priests, paladins, hunters, engineers, and quest states.

## Second added-NPC pass  (new keys: benjamin_carevin, whit_wantmal, cog_glitzspinner)

### Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg: Benjamin_Carevin (a child, with the Carevin family in Darkshire), Whit_Wantmal (his house is next to the gryphon master, Felicia Maline; nothing else), Cog_Glitzspinner (gnome in a house off the path to the flight master, talking with Finbus Geargrind and Herble Baubbletump; friend of Viktori Prism'Antras, promised to help build a device to look at the stars). Legion (Veiled Hand) lines not used.
- Existing pack: darkshire_folk.lua (Jonathan, Calor, Viktori's star-glass: "Cog drew it and I'm only the hands"), scott_carevin above, darkshire_trades.lua (Felicia Maline), darkshire_shops.lua (Herble "on the hill").

### Their quests
- none: a child, a householder and an inventor. Cog has one reply for players who have heard Viktori on the star-glass (`viktori.d_default`).

### Identity / voice
- Benjamin Carevin · boy · bold, restless; questions in bursts; calls the household by first names (his relation to each is never stated, as with Scott).
- Whit Wantmal · man · wistful, bookish; writes verses about the town; cannot bring himself to leave.
- Cog Glitzspinner · gnome · enthusiastic, exacting; tolerances, focal lengths; fond of Viktori.

### Authored, not canon
- Benjamin's notebook of monsters under the bed, counting to a thousand while the hunters are out, the spare crossbow, the prayer Watcher Hartin taught him.
- Whit's verses, his cousin in Stormwind, the gryphons as his warning bell, that he asked to carry a lamp and Althea told him to keep writing.
- Cog in Ironforge when Gnomeregan fell, the worm gear of a hundred and forty-four teeth, the argument with Finbus about tube or lens first, Herble's rigged door, lamp-brackets for the Commander.

### Never
- Veiled Hand, Legion, Cataclysm Darkshire, Worgen NPCs of Raven Hill (Oliver Harris, Sister Elsington: later than Classic).
