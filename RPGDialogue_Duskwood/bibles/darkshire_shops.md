## Their quests
- none: six vendors, a trainer and a townsman.

## Identity
- Mabel Solaj · Scarlet Raven · human woman · general goods. Warm, motherly ("dear"); candles and lamp oil sell best.
- Gunder Thornbush · Scarlet Raven · human man · tradesman. Dry, stubborn, mourns the town's crafts.
- Morg Gnarltree · Darkshire forge · human man · armorer. Gruff, protective; outfits the Watch on credit.
- Matt Johnson · Darkshire · human man · mining trainer. Weary, cautious; nobody mines Duskwood any more.
- Danielle Zipstitch · above Eva's · human woman · tailoring supplies. Chatty; hears Eva's clients through the floor.
- Sheri Zipstitch · above Eva's · human woman · tailoring supplies. Anxious, careful; keeps the accounts.
- Hogan Ference · Town Hall · human man · citizen. Bitter, persistent petitioner; wants swords, not letters.
- Kzixx · Darkened Bank road · goblin man · rare goods. Smooth salesman ("pal"), neutral, sells to anyone.

## Never
- Legion-era Darkshire (the Veiled Hand), anything after Year 1. How the Gnarltrees or Zipstitches are related. Who raises the Raven Hill dead (Hogan only repeats rumour).

## Agenda / Secrets (authored, not canon)
- Mabel's late husband is buried in Darkshire; she ran a shop when it was Brightwood.
- Gunder's family traded in Brightwood for three generations; lost crafters (a tanner, a weaver, a cooper, unnamed).
- Morg sends armour to the Watch unpaid.
- Matt lost fellow miners when the worgen took the Roland's Doom mine.
- Danielle repeats Eva's clients' worries only to Sheri and the cat; Sheri charges others a little extra to cover the Watch's discount; something scratches Sheri's shutter.
- Hogan farmed east of town; the commander turned him away from the Watch for his knees.
- Kzixx is Booty Bay born; ran from a worgen and from a customer.

## What they notice
- Mabel: hunger. Gunder: crafters. Morg: battered kit; cloth wearers. Matt: miners. Danielle and Sheri: tailors. Kzixx: goblins.
