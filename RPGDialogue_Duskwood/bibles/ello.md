## Their quests
- Messenger to Darkshire: Solomon's plea arrives, from one ignored town to another. He refuses, and is sorry, and would do it again.
- Note to the Mayor: a letter from a stranger in an ancient hand. He can't read it.
- Translate Abercrombie's Note / Translation to Ello: Sirra reads it; Stitches comes down the road. Directing from the Town Hall.
- Bride of the Embalmer: a nursery tale turns out to be a grave with a heart in it.

Lore anchors: Year 1. Never use the Veiled Hand (Legion)
