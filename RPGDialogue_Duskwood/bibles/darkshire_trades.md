# Darkshire's flight master and weaponsmith  (keys: felicia_maline, gavin)

## Sources
- From memory of original WoW (1.x): Felicia Maline, Gryphon Master at Darkshire; Gavin Gnarltree, Weaponsmith in Darkshire (kin of Clarise Gnarltree, relation unstated). Confirm with capture.

## Their quests
- none: flight master and vendor. They react to the Worgen, the undead of Raven Hill and the Night Watch by name only.

## Voices (authored, not canon)
- Felicia: steady, unafraid of the dark, her gryphons hate the wood.
- Gavin: grim, practical; silver-edged talk is rumor, steel is what he sells.
