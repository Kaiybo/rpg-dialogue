## Their quests
- Elaine: Raven Hill (the lights; it was Jitters), The Hermit (she worries about an old customer; it was Abercrombie), Deliveries to Sven (the Carevins feed those who fight). After Translation to Ello she knows what the hermit was.
- Sorigal: Vital Supplies (turn-in, from Andromath), Supplies for Nethergarde (the powerstones run).
- Hann: none of his own; he sells the moonshine for Daniels's Dry Times.
- Alyssa: none; she is the reason for Eva's The Legend of Stalvan, which she isn't told about.
- Herble: none; he sells the bronze tube for Viktori's Look To The Stars.
- Farrin, Steven Black: none: a frightened lodger and a stable master.

## Identity
- Elaine Carevin · Darkshire · human woman · questgiver. Steady, devout, practical; keeps lists. Speech: plain, warm, a little stern about faith ("walk under the Light"). Calls Jonathan by name; never says how they're related.
- Watchmaster Sorigal · Darkshire · human man · soldier (caravan master). Brisk, dutiful, dry; ledgers and roads. Answers to the caravan and Lungertz, not the Watch.
- Barkeep Hann · Scarlet Raven Tavern · human man · drink vendor. Shrewd, cheerful, conspiratorial about the moonshine. Won't say where it comes from.
- Alyssa Eva · Darkshire · human woman · reagent vendor. Warm, wry, fond of her grandmother; trusts locks over cards.
- Herble Baubbletump · Darkshire · gnome man · engineering and mining vendor. Chatty, proud, digressive; an exile from Gnomeregan.
- Farrin Daris · Scarlet Raven Tavern · human man · citizen. Anxious, grieving, bitter at Stormwind; won't go out.
- Steven Black · Darkshire · human man · stable master. Calm, gentle; watches the animals.

## Agenda / Secrets (authored, not canon)
- Elaine: keeps a list of everyone the Carevins worry about; the hermit's betrayal shakes her trust in her own kindness.
- Sorigal: marks a safe delivery "in red, which I save for miracles"; hires guards because the Watch can't spare any.
- Hann: calls his moonshine a public service; doesn't begrudge priests a cup.
- Alyssa: Eva won't read for her ("family's too close to see properly").
- Herble: keeps his old workshop key on a string; has told Viktori six times a tube can't see through fog.
- Farrin: wonders whether his father is still in his grave; was offered an escort and refused.
- Steven: sits up talking to frightened horses; watches them for the worgen.

## Reliability
All honest. Herble is opinionated; Farrin's fear makes everything sound worse than it is.
