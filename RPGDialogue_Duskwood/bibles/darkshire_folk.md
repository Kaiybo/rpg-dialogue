- Calor: Worgen in the Woods (four parts, all the same name; lines speak to the whole chain).
- Sirra: The Weathered Grave, Morgan Ladimore, Translate Abercrombie's Note, Wait for Sirra to Finish, Translation to Ello.
- Viktori: Look To The Stars (the bronze tube; later steps not described).
- Grual: Dusky Crab Cakes, Seasoned Wolf Kabobs, Jitters' Growling Gut.
- Millstipe: Crime and Punishment.
- Trelayne: none: innkeeper only.
- Clarise: none: trainer only.

## Voices
- Jonathan: clipped, suspicious, controlled. Questions back. A shapechanger in his town gets a warning first.
- Calor: zealous, impatient, "Master Carevin". Left the Watch out of frustration, not contempt.
- Sirra: fussy, digressive, delighted by scripts; makes people wait. Authored: loves the dull town ledgers.
- Viktori: eager rationalist, wants a measurable reason; talks about lenses and fog. Kind to druids, unconvinced by them.
- Grual: cheerful, proud, evasive about ingredients (the crab cakes). Never says "spider".
- Millstipe: indignant civic grievance, dry. Hates Stormwind's nobles, not Stormwind's people.
- Trelayne (authored): practical, quietly kind; shutters closed, poker by the bed.
- Clarise: wry, tired, generous with the Watch.
