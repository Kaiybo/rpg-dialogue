## Their quests
- The Shadowy Figure: the cards point to a frightened man, not a monster.
- Supplies from Darkshire, Ghost Hair Thread, Return the Comb, Deliver the Thread: she spins the thread that holds Stitches together, and knows what thread like that can do.

- The Legend of Stalvan: her granddaughter, and the end of the trail.
