# RPG Dialogue: Riverglades (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.0.0 (unreleased)
- New pack for a new Forever zone, Alliance and neutral first. Farholde Keep: Knight Commander Demar Brant, Ashe Amberhall, Sergeant Riftan, Sister Rowland, Cady Lloydriel, Wheatley, Gretchen Mayberry, Alphonse Dumas; the sentries and the sick soldiers in the chapel. Turner's Logging Camp: Hurley and Halligan Turner. Wheeler's Grange: Benjy Wheeler. Powderfuse Port: Melbin Powderfuse, Innkeeper Zizplink, Gray Dawnbright, Jazzle Cheapshot, Fimbo Greasemitz, Argo Brinewhistle, Gritta Chumwater.
- Written to the depth standard from the start (hubs, branches, replies for particular characters).
- Everyone answers "Their story" and "A little advice"; Demar and Gray on Fern Crimsonleaf, and Ashe on the Twilight's Hammer and her father, lead into those branches.
- Horde (Rog'mar, Bolder'ok) not written yet.
