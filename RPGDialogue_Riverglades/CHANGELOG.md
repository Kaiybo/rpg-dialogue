# RPG Dialogue: Riverglades (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.3.0 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: everyone at Farholde, the logging camp, Wheeler's Grange and Powderfuse Port now talks in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments, in their own voices; nothing they say has changed.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)
- For a priest: bless Demar Brant's riders at dawn. Sister Rowland asks a priest in Shadowform to step out of it near the sick.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- The lost of Farholde: Gray's "I spoke to Demar about Fern" branch and its two endings; Gray and Demar remember which one you chose.

## 1.0.0 (unreleased)
- New pack for a new Forever zone, Alliance and neutral first. Farholde Keep: Knight Commander Demar Brant, Ashe Amberhall, Sergeant Riftan, Sister Rowland, Cady Lloydriel, Wheatley, Gretchen Mayberry, Alphonse Dumas; the sentries and the sick soldiers in the chapel. Turner's Logging Camp: Hurley and Halligan Turner. Wheeler's Grange: Benjy Wheeler. Powderfuse Port: Melbin Powderfuse, Innkeeper Zizplink, Gray Dawnbright, Jazzle Cheapshot, Fimbo Greasemitz, Argo Brinewhistle, Gritta Chumwater.
- Written to the depth standard from the start (hubs, branches, replies for particular characters).
- Everyone answers "Their story" and "A little advice"; Demar and Gray on Fern Crimsonleaf, and Ashe on the Twilight's Hammer and her father, lead into those branches.
- Horde (Rog'mar, Bolder'ok) not written yet.
