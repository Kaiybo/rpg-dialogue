# RPG Dialogue: Riverglades (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.17.0 (2026-11-18)
- NPCs notice what you have done: quests for their neighbours, notable kills, your deaths, rank and reputation.
- Writing pass: stance replies end on something concrete, fewer echoes and stock phrases.
- Druid greetings that assume a night elf or tauren no longer fire for other druid races.

## 1.16.0 (2026-11-18)
- Writing pass: repeated phrases cut and replaced with concrete detail.
- Clarity pass: answers answer the question, names are introduced, quest lines stand alone, fewer contradictions.
- NPCs answer each stance reply before the talk moves on.

## 1.15.0 (2026-11-17)
- Named NPCs answer many more "Ask about" topics (3 to 6 each, more for leaders), mostly the new world topics added in World 1.36.0.

## 1.14.0 (2026-11-17)
- Goblins talk like goblins: a voice pass on this pack's goblin speakers (see the main changelog for 0.76.0).

## 1.13.1 (2026-11-17)
- Writing pass on the characters added in 0.74.0: stock phrases ("fair enough", "truly", "worth more than") rewritten.

## 1.13.0 (2026-11-17)
- New people to talk to (see the main changelog for 0.74.0). New files: restart the game fully (a /reload won't load them).

## 1.12.0 (2026-11-17)
- Writing pass: stock phrases ("Fair enough", "I won't pretend", "truly", neat little morals) rewritten in each speaker's own voice, and more grit and humour in the everyday lines.

## 1.11.0 (2026-11-17)
- The town's leading people greet you differently when you come back after a fortnight or more, notice how far you've come since they last saw you, and have heard what you did with their neighbours. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.67.0.

## 1.10.1 (2026-11-17)
- Krom'rosh's first menu had one reply too many, so one was hidden; the Bolder'ok ogres are now asked about from "Why is the Horde in the Riverglades?".

## 1.10.0 (2026-11-17)
- The Horde side of the Riverglades: at Rog'mar, Krom'rosh (Eye of Mugg), Neza Bloodsnout (Poison Tips), Tan'jani (Fetch 'n' Fletch) and Kreza Darkthorn (the Krol'dok key); at Bolder'ok, Clugfist and Jom-jom, ogres who walked out on the Twilight's Hammer. Rog'mar's grunts and Bolder'ok's ogres talk too, and both towns keep track of how they regard you.
- Out in the glades: Marshal Ryan, dying in the Twilight's Shroud; Sleebo Fizzlespout and his marsh-gas engine; Jezap Jinglesprocket, Gadgetzan's fight promoter.
- Demar Brant follows Marshal Ryan's story from his log to Abigail in Stormwind, and Sergeant Riftan asks after the scouts caged in the Shroud.
- New files: needs a full game restart (not /reload).

## 1.9.0 (2026-11-17)
- The class conversations here are rewritten and deepened: dwarf shamans are greeted at Farholde and on the frontier, Wheatley, Mayberry, Zizplink and Gritta have a new topic each, and Demar, Riftan and Benjy notice when you've been fighting the cult and the reapers.

## 1.8.0 (2026-11-17)
- Every person here now has something to say to every class: warriors, paladins, hunters (and their pets), rogues, priests, mages, warlocks and druids, plus shamans from the goblins and Gray. Each leads to a question and something to argue about.
- Knight Commander Demar Brant's warm greeting now shows to a traveller he thinks well of (it had been tucked inside another line).

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-15)
- Replies for the classes and races these people would really react to (warriors, hunters, rogues, mages, warlocks, druids, shamans, paladins; every race of the faction, and the Skyborne), each leading somewhere.

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-15)
- Every person here writes a first impression in your diary after you first talk (the journal's People page shows it too).

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.3.0 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: everyone at Farholde, the logging camp, Wheeler's Grange and Powderfuse Port now talks in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments, in their own voices; nothing they say has changed.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)
- For a priest: bless Demar Brant's riders at dawn. Sister Rowland asks a priest in Shadowform to step out of it near the sick.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)

## 1.0.0 (unreleased)

Farholde Keep: Knight Commander Demar Brant, Ashe Amberhall, Sergeant Riftan, Sister Rowland, Cady Lloydriel, Wheatley, Gretchen Mayberry, Alphonse Dumas; the sentries and the sick soldiers in the chapel. Turner's Logging Camp: Hurley and Halligan Turner. Wheeler's Grange: Benjy Wheeler. Powderfuse Port: Melbin Powderfuse, Innkeeper Zizplink, Gray Dawnbright, Jazzle Cheapshot, Fimbo Greasemitz, Argo Brinewhistle, Gritta Chumwater.
