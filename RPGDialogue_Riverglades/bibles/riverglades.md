## Characters

### Demar Brant, Knight Commander (major)
- Adjectives: proud, embattled, blunt, loyal to his dead, contemptuous of Stormwind.
- Speech: clipped command voice; "the Brotherhood", "my riders"; uses the dead's names, never their ranks.
- Knows and cares: twenty years holding the Riverglades; the siege; Howard, Isolde, Roderick, Rosalind, Fern; the Twilight's Hammer in Sunnyglade; Krol'dok.
- Relationships: Ashe (trusted advisor, younger; he signed Isolde's memorial, she signed Alden's); Riftan (the oldest hand); Cady (a ranger he relies on).
- Attitude: warm to anyone who fights for the Riverglades; cold to Stormwind and its nobles; Horde are invaders here; Twilight cultists are vermin. Notices: class (what can you do for us), standing at Farholde, Stormwind colours (a noble's origin, a Stormwind tabard).
- Agenda: men, steel, and someone to take the war to Krol'dok. Wants the player to carry his grievance to Stormwind, or to stay.
- Secret: he wrote Isolde's tribute himself and won't talk about her.
- Reliability: biased on Stormwind (claim `stormwind_abandoned_riverglades`, partly); honest about the war; certain Fern is dead.
- Topics: brotherhood_horse, siege_of_farholde, sunnyglade, twilights_hammer, krol_dok, fern_crimsonleaf, stormwind.
- Threads: none written; proposed "the lost of Farholde" (see HANDOFF).

### Ashe Amberhall (major)
- Adjectives: brilliant, voluble, grieving, loyal, prickly.
- Speech: talks fast, then catches herself ("I'm babbling"); Dalaran precision; calls her father "my father" and "Alden" in the same breath.
- Knows: magic, the Twilight's curios, her father's teaching, the expedition west and the black dragon, the keep's history (she tells it to visitors).
- Disagrees with Demar on Stormwind: she thinks Stormwind's reasoning was sound and the fight is theirs anyway.
- Notices: mages and arcane talent (Skyborne High Order, gnomes, Dalaran), the Light, cursed things.
- Agenda: understand what the Twilight's Hammer is doing with its relics; keep Demar from burning out; keep her father's work alive.
- Secret: she survived the expedition and doesn't know why.
- Reliability: honest; occasionally wrong about people (she thinks Riftan is tiring).
- Topics: siege_of_farholde, twilights_hammer, sunnyglade (the mage tower).

### Sergeant Riftan (quest)
- Weary veteran, "that old dog", out at the front by Twilight's Shroud. Short sentences. Respects Ashe and complains about her. Notices: kit, nerve. Thinks the cultists' trinkets are worse than their knives.

### Sister Rowland (quest)
- Patient chapel priest; worn, practical, kind; does not know what the illness is and won't guess. Notices: healers, the Light, disease on the player, Shadowform (no healing in that shape, so not near the sick).

### Cady Lloydriel (quest)
- High elf ranger, Brotherhood; quiet, dry, watchful; keeps an eye on the cradle and on Aedamas and Luna. Never says whose child. Notices: hunters and pets, elves and Skyborne (curious, careful, no claimed kinship), druids in animal form (Aedamas has opinions).

### Wheatley, stable master (service)
- Horses above everything; keeps the Brotherhood's mounts; Brienne Stormhart was stable master before him (memorial). Notices: mounts, paladins' chargers, druid travel form.

### Gretchen Mayberry, Gryphon Master (service)
- Brisk, proud of her birds. Notices: things that fly (Skyborne Walk on Air), fear of heights.

### Alphonse Dumas, smith (quest)
- Hot embers, few words, pleased by a puzzle; the Krol'dok moulding is the most interesting thing he's had on his bench in years. Notices: broken kit, smiths, plate.

### Hurley and Halligan Turner (quest)
- Hurley: logger, harried, fond of Curly, worrier ("gray in a year"). Halligan: runs the camp, gruff, protective, "that damn kid". The Horde raids their woodpiles. Notices: axes, Horde colours, the player having saved Curly.

### Benjy Wheeler (quest)
- Outside the farmhouse at Wheeler's Grange; harvest reapers in the fields. Plain-spoken, stubborn, small. Kept modest.

### Melbin Powderfuse (major for the port)
- Master Goblin Engineer; owns (he says) and built (he says) all of Powderfuse Port; turns every conversation to what the player can do for him. Notices: engineers, coin, anyone leaning on his walls. Claim `melbin_built_port` (open); the port's other goblins tease him.

### Innkeeper Zizplink (service)
- Cheerful goblin innkeeper; calls everyone an explorer; the sea air; her grocery list. Passes on the port's rumors.

### Gray Dawnbright (quest)
- Brotherhood man drinking at the port; surly, sour, funny when cornered; misses home; married to Tessa (named only). Insists Fern Crimsonleaf isn't dead (claim `fern_lives`, his belief).

### Jazzle Cheapshot (quest)
- Arena owner with no fights yet; thinks the factions are wasting good violence in the fields. Notices: fighters, gladiator types, PvP titles.

### Fimbo Greasemitz (quest)
- Cafe on the water; bored of boar and fish; a seafood stew dream; murloc eyes cooked quick. Notices: cooks, appetite.

### Argo Brinewhistle (quest)
- Drunk sailor with a sea monster problem and a hiccup; wants its head for his stern; "drinks are on me". Notices: sailors, drinkers.

### Gritta Chumwater, fisherman (service)
- Sells chum, lures, fish; blunt; the best fishing is off the river mouths. Notices: fishing skill.
