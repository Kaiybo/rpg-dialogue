# RPG Dialogue: Dustwallow Marsh (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.11.0 (2026-11-16)
- New people: Tabetha, "Swamp Eye" Jarl, Ogron, Private Hendel, Overlord Mok'Morokk and Draz'Zilb.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.10.0 (2026-11-16)
- Theramore: Baldruc, Morgan Stern and Doctor Gustaf VanHowzen (Triage); Janene and Tesoran on Kul Tiras and Daelin's men.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.9.0 (2026-11-15)
- Replies for the classes and races these people would really react to (warriors, hunters, rogues, mages, warlocks, druids, shamans, paladins; every race of the faction, and the Skyborne), each leading somewhere.

## 1.8.0 (2026-11-15)
- Every person here writes a first impression in your diary after you first talk (the journal's People page shows it too).

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Depth for Brackenwall Village: Krog and Nazeer Bloodpike, built from the Shady Rest investigation (hoofprints, Paval Reethe's badge, the black shield and Mosarn, Ogron and Reethe's death) and Nazeer's Marg Nighteye chain (the lost report, the severed head, the muckshell and the noble's pendant, Zor Lonetree).
- Replies for who you are: tauren (Krog), rogues (Nazeer); replies after Reethe is silenced and after Marg speaks, and for players who've heard Nazeer or met Kin'weelay.
- New quest lines for Suspicious Hoofprints, Lieutenant Paval Reethe, The Black Shield, Return to Krog, Marg Speaks and Theramore Spies.

## 1.4.0 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: Jaina, Tervosh, Vimes, Tesoran, Janene, Krog, Nazeer and Theramore's guards now talk in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments, each in their own voice; what they say is unchanged.

## 1.3.0 (unreleased)
- For a priest: ask Jaina whether the Light still answers her; sit with James Hyal for Captain Vimes. Jaina notices Shadowform.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)
- Depth: Jaina (the peace, Lordaeron and the crossing, the city, replies for mages, Lordaeron refugees, Skyborne and anyone who's met Thrall), Tervosh, Vimes (the Hyals, the taverns, orcish, reading the tracks), Tesoran and Janene; everyone answers Their story and A little advice.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits bring personal lines (habits, worries, running jokes), and some NPCs get night-time lines. Janene, Tesoran, Tervosh, Nazeer.

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- Theramore (Alliance): Lady Jaina Proudmoore, Archmage Tervosh, Captain Garran Vimes, Adjutant Tesoran and Innkeeper Janene. The Theramore Guard.
- Brackenwall Village (Horde): Krog and Nazeer Bloodpike.
