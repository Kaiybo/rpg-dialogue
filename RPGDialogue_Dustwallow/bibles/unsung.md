# Theramore: the unsung (0.39.0; keys: charity_mipsy, hans_weston, piter_verance)

## Sources
- Classic NPC roster of Theramore Isle as remembered (no quests, no gossip on record): Charity Mipsy <General Supplies>, Hans Weston <Armorer & Shieldsmith>, Piter Verance <Weaponsmith>. Wiki pages not re-read this pass; Piter Verance was left out in 0.37.0 as unsure, so he stays on the unsure list.

## Their quests
- none: service NPCs; every conversation keeps BUSINESS.

## Identity
- Charity Mipsy: cheerful, motherly shopkeeper ("dear"); supplies by ship from Menethil; likes Jaina's peace.
- Hans Weston: gruff armorer who hates rust; makes the guard's shields for Captain Vimes.
- Piter Verance: easy-going weaponsmith; shares a forge fire with Hans; won't sell to the Horde; worried about the Shady Rest.

## Authored, not canon
Charity arriving on the first ships; Hans from Kul Tiras; Piter trained in Lordaeron; the shared forge fire; the guard going west in fours. Jaina's grumbling Kul Tiran sailors are kept vague (no Daelin's fate spelled out).

## Never
Theramore's fall and anything after Year 1; who burned the Shady Rest.

## What they notice
Charity: hunger at dusk, resurrection sickness. Hans: battered kit, shield classes. Piter: rogues, dwarves.
