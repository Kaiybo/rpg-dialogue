## Their quests
- Stinky: Stinky's Escape (both factions). Balos: The Deserters (finishes part 1, starts part 2). Groy: Akiris by the Bundle (part 2 ends with him). MacVince, Michael, Zulrg: none.

## Never
- The 2.3 Dustwallow additions (Smiling Jim's ending, Tabetha's new quests, the Mysterious Merchant), the Attack on Theramore Isle, anything later.
- Who Groy's client is; what akiris reed is used for beyond "folk who know what to do with it".

## Voices (authored, not canon)
- Stinky: nervous and cheerful, talks too fast with raptors around; the nickname given by the North Point soldiers; the best patches are "always a little further on". Order stance on clearing the raptors.
- Balos: bitter, tired Kul Tiran ex-sailor; blames the empty towers and half rations; hints Reethe talked of a burned inn and a black shield (Dustwallow thread, no answer given). Order stance on desertion.
- Groy: impatient, gruff dock trader; "mostly legal"; won't name his client; grudging respect for Bloads. Order stance on the Guard.
- MacVince: warm, proud breeder; Elwynn bloodlines; dries hooves and salts leeches. Order stance on Stormwind's rule.
- Michael: quiet stable master who likes animals more than owners; burns marsh herbs against flies; the crocolisk that ate a bucket. Light stance on how to treat a companion.
- Zulrg: Stonemaul ogre who speaks of himself by name in whole, simple sentences; prefers the big stick; wastes nothing since the black dragons took the old village. Order stance on thinking versus smashing.

## What they notice
- Stinky: weary, pets. Balos: soldiers, paladins. Groy: purse, rogues. MacVince: mounts, humans. Michael: pets. Zulrg: warriors, tauren.

### Never

### Voices (authored, not canon)
- Byron: town-beat guard, ex-farmhand from near Andorhal; walks Jim home. Order stance on waiting for proof.
- Evencane: stern drillmaster; recruits drill in the shallows. Order stance on drill versus instinct.
- Karman: grave, kind; eleven who keep the oath; a corner of the drill yard. Light stance on why Lordaeron fell.
- Narett: fussy Dalaran-trained alchemist; "regrettable afternoons" of self-testing. Order stance on rules versus experiment.
- Jasperbloom: gentle gatherer who goes out alone; every third plant. Light stance on draining the marsh.
- Lillian: brisk barkeep; believes the quiet tables; never charges Jim. Order stance on reporting a deserter.
- Balai: sly Darkspear among ogres; won't sleep facing Witch Hill. Light stance on the spirits.
- Krak: jolly ogre who names himself; tests armor with a club; "wear the hat". Order stance on armor versus fists.
- Zanara: blunt orc soldier with an old wound; likes the ogres, not their Overlord. Honor stance on the bow.

### What they notice
- Byron: priests, paladins, night elves. Evencane: warriors, rogues and hunters. Karman: paladins, priests, warlocks. Narett: alchemists, herbalists, mages. Jasperbloom: herbalists, the weary. Lillian: dwarves, rogues. Balai: shamans, priests, trolls. Krak: plate and mail wearers, orcs. Zanara: hunters, melee fighters.
