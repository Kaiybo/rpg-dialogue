## Their quests
- Stinky: Stinky's Escape (both factions). Balos: The Deserters (finishes part 1, starts part 2). Groy: Akiris by the Bundle (part 2 ends with him). MacVince, Michael, Zulrg: none.

## Voices (authored, not canon)
- Stinky: nervous and cheerful, talks too fast with raptors around; the nickname given by the North Point soldiers; the best patches are "always a little further on". Order stance on clearing the raptors.
- Balos: bitter, tired Kul Tiran ex-sailor; blames the empty towers and half rations; hints Reethe talked of a burned inn and a black shield (Dustwallow thread, no answer given). Order stance on desertion.
- Groy: impatient, gruff dock trader; "mostly legal"; won't name his client; grudging respect for Bloads. Order stance on the Guard.
- MacVince: warm, proud breeder; Elwynn bloodlines; dries hooves and salts leeches. Order stance on Stormwind's rule.
- Michael: quiet stable master who likes animals more than owners; burns marsh herbs against flies; the crocolisk that ate a bucket. Light stance on how to treat a companion.
- Zulrg: Stonemaul ogre who speaks of himself by name in whole, simple sentences; prefers the big stick; wastes nothing since the black dragons took the old village. Order stance on thinking versus smashing.

## What they notice
- Stinky: weary, pets. Balos: soldiers, paladins. Groy: purse, rogues. MacVince: mounts, humans. Michael: pets. Zulrg: warriors, tauren.
