# Foothold Citadel and the workshops (keys: samaul, caz, torq)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Commander_Samaul (human, Foothold Citadel, commands the Theramore Guard; Classic: The Missing Diplomat (13) turn-in and (14): knows Hendel, Elling Trias sent you, duty to protect these lands, Hendel stationed at the first tower outside Theramore)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Caz_Twosprocket (gnome engineering trainer, also one of Theramore's best smiths; The Black Shield (2) turn-in, (3): strap burned off, made for a large person, likely a tauren; gossip: siege engines nearly complete, bored; "You're not an engineer, are you?")
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Torq_Ironblast (dwarf gunsmith in Foothold Citadel; Warcraft III: Reforged names him commanding a base in Stonetalon attacked by Grom Hellscream's Warsong)
- Private Hendel, Captain Vimes, Tervosh, Hans Weston and Piter Verance are in `marsh.lua`, `theramore.lua`, `unsung.lua`.

## Their quests
- Samaul: The Missing Diplomat (chain title, TODO_QUEST, as Jaina's file uses it). Caz: The Black Shield (TODO_QUEST, as Vimes's file uses it). Torq: none.

## Voices
- **Commander Samaul**: stern, fair, procedural; hates being kept in the dark; quietly against more graves.
- **Caz Twosprocket**: chatty, restless gnome; bored talk; rivalry with the human smiths.
- **Torq Ironblast**: gruff dwarf burr; the Warsong drums still in his ears.

## Authored, not canon
- Samaul: Hendel asked for Sentry Point himself; Samaul signs the deserter reports Vimes takes; "the marsh has killed more soldiers than the rest put together".
- Caz: came over to fix the harbor crane four years ago and it's still broken; Hans measures his work; the shield's black paint was added later over older paint (consistent with Vimes's framing suspicion); Tervosh calls the engines "an invitation".
- Torq: his Third War post in Stonetalon (from Reforged's naming, kept vague: "holding a post when the Warsong came through").
