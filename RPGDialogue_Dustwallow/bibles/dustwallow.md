## Their quests
- Jaina: The Missing Diplomat (one line, for any step). Tervosh: The Missing Diplomat, Shadowshard Fragments.
- Vimes: Inspecting the Ruins, Suspicious Hoofprints, Lieutenant Paval Reethe, The Black Shield. Tesoran: Lieutenant Paval Reethe, Daelin's Men.
- Krog: Warchief's Command: Dustwallow Marsh!, Flight to Brackenwall, Inspecting the Ruins, The Grimtotem Plot, Justice Dispensed.
- Nazeer: The Severed Head, The Troll Witchdoctor, Report to Zor. Janene: none.

## Never
- Theramore's fall, the mana bomb, anyone's death in it, and any hint of it (Janene is simply devoted; Tervosh simply busy).
- The Missing Diplomat's end (Hendel, the Defias, who's behind them): Jaina says only that someone works from the shadows. Lady Prestor stays secret (Stormwind's rule).
- Who burned the Shady Rest: only Krog's "The Grimtotem Plot" line names the Grimtotem, as the quest title does; Vimes has hoofprints and a hunch.
- The Varian–Thrall summit, Hyjal raid, Kirin Tor leadership, the comics.
- Gossip verbatim (Nazeer's "blood is still shed", Tervosh's "must be brief", Krog's "violated his wishes").

## Voices
- Jaina: earnest, warm, tired of war; won't discuss her father for long.
- Tervosh: brisk archmage; garden; independent of committees.
- Vimes: direct investigator; doesn't believe the easy answer.
- Tesoran: records-keeper; dry.
- Janene: steady, homely innkeeper.
- Krog: blunt; the Warchief's word.
- Nazeer: suspicious spymaster.
