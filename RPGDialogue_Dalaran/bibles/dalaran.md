## Year 1 discipline
- Dalaran here is the city in the Alterac Mountains, rebuilding after Archimonde broke it in the Third War. The Kirin Tor lowered its dome early, while the city is still under repair. Nothing about Northrend, the Violet Hold, a floating city, Rhonin or anyone leading the Kirin Tor, Jaina's later history, the Sunreavers or the Silver Covenant, the Nexus War, or any later expansion.
- Who leads the Kirin Tor now is never said. "The council" is all anyone names.
- The Forever city uses the later city's buildings (Violet Citadel, Purple Parlor, Underbelly, Magical Menagerie). Characters name only the places the Forever pages name.
- Skyborne facts come only from BRIEF.md §10 and the Forever pages below.

## Voice
- **Modera:** gracious, tired, a little dry; protective of the city first and of the council's pride a distant second. Whole, warm sentences with a sting at the end.
- **Celindra:** clipped impatience in full sentences ("Yes? Be quick, please"), every answer measured against the ward she isn't watching. Grief for Antonidas under it, shown only once she trusts you.
- **Alvareaux:** apologetic quartermaster; ledgers and seasons; gentle jokes about wizards and logistics.
- **Laurain:** cheerful greeter who runs on one breath, says the official line and then the honest one, laughs nervously.
- **Denaaris:** courteous Skyborne, "Wind's greetings"; homesick, shy about Stormwind.
- **Belanaa:** quiet, grateful, careful about her people's crafts with outsiders.
- **Alfina:** night elf calm; distrusts concentrated magic; dry about the Kirin Tor's promises.
- **Brammold:** dwarf trader's burr ("ye", "sellin'"), likes old things and an honest price.
- **Lizi:** quick gnome, fixes things, two cats underfoot that aren't hers.
- **Pollero:** steady, a little wistful, walks his street every day.
- **Rexxie:** goblin patter ("pal", "sweetheart", dropped g's), bluffs as a "fellow Dalaranian", calls theft rescue.
- **Pools:** guards say "everything's under control" and don't quite believe it; citizens are coming home to rebuilt streets full of cats; workers set stone under floating blocks; apprentices are flustered and kind.

## Assumed
- The city's subzones are reported as "Violet Citadel", "Sky Enclave", "Path of Renewal", "Dalaran Charter", "Eclipse with Shears", "Magical Menagerie" (names from the wiki), inside the zone "Alterac Mountains" or a zone "Dalaran". Pool lines are gated on either.
- Archmage Modera in the city is a different NPC from her image in Silverpine (the image is not written here).
- The dungeon (Rexxie) may not report a zone the pack loads in; her file is harmless if it doesn't.
- Alliance players can't speak to most of the city until Friend of the Kirin Tor, so most lines assume a welcomed visitor; Horde reactions are written only for NPCs the wiki marks friendly to both factions (Laurain, Celindra, Alvareaux, Alfina, Rexxie) and for Modera, who lets the Horde in.

## Authored, not canon
- Apprentices hurt on the upper stairs when the parlor broke; they rest in the lower halls.
- Modera would defy the council again; she sees the dome as "where we hid while the world needed us".
- What answered from the parlor "wore the shape of an archmage, and none could say which one" (from the boss's name only).
- Celindra checks the Purple Parlor every night; a hundred small wards over broken rooftops.
- Alvareaux: cloth and metal go to scaffolds and wards first.
- Laurain has kept her post only since the dome came down; she is one rung below archmagi.
- Denaaris: the Kirin Tor asked that the portal stay for the High Order only; a Stormwind crier calls the Skyborne friends of the Kirin Tor (echoes the Stormwind pack); he is homesick.
- Belanaa: the cloth becomes cloaks against Alterac's cold.
- Alfina: came because the Skyborne asked for a teacher and the Cenarion Circle should know what they learn; the garden's blossoms are "Felwood's green".
- Brammold buys from magi clearing cellars and families; keeps humming items with the Kirin Tor's leave.
- Lizi: gnomes long studied in Dalaran; more came after Gnomeregan.
- Pollero: the Path of Renewal was laid after the fall; he walks it daily.
- Pools: the watch's "under control" line; a citizen's lost magus brother; masons and floating blocks; an apprentice whose room sits on a ley line.
