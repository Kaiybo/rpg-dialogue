# The Tempered Tomes scholars  (keys: dal_kaela, dal_latanja, dal_ysabel)

File: npcs/tempered_tomes.lua. Forever-only NPCs (patch 1.60.1): three Skyborne of the High Order, standing together in Tempered Tomes, a bookshop in Dalaran. The wiki gives name, race, affiliation and place only; their reason for being here follows Elaadrin Evengale's call for explorers.

## Their quests

## Identity
- **Kae'la Brightsong** · eager, chatty, kind. Reads everything, on the list or not; uses reading to keep from fearing for home. Asks the player whether they love Azeroth. Wants to share knowledge with the Windshapers.
- **La'tanja Brightgale** · brisk, sharp, dutiful, wary. Keeps the High Order's list (wards anchoring stone, anchors that need no spirit, the elemental planes). Polite to the Kirin Tor and watches them; defensive about the Shen'dar Highlands.
- **Ysa'bel Brightwind** · calm, gentle, loyal. Studies the ancestors (Eldre'Thalas, the Shal'nan, the Highborne); keeps visiting Ranath Nightstride; wears her grandmother's heirloom brooch.
- Speech: "Winds be with you"; whole, warm sentences; no Azerothian slang. Never state Skyborne facts outside BRIEF §10 and the Skyborne page.
- Never says: anything linking the missing patrons to Thunderaan or the Wind Hollows; any later-expansion lore.

## Threads he could carry
- Ysa'bel and Ranath: Ysa'bel's `ranath3` node opens only after the player heard `dal_ranath.s2_default`.
- Each names the other two; Kae'la and Ysa'bel both mention Ranath.

## Agenda
- La'tanja: find a way to anchor the isles; not trade one set of unexplained patrons for another.
- Kae'la: read, and share.
- Ysa'bel: bring home something better than "rebels who ran".

## Secrets
Authored, not canon:
- Kae'la is from Valanaar and summered in Siroccas with an aunt she can't reach; packed shawls and no books.
- La'tanja was a ward-keeper's apprentice in Valanaar and watched the pylon by her house go dark; a footnote names a book nobody in Dalaran can find.
- Ysa'bel's heirloom is her grandmother's brooch; she has visited Ranath three times.
- The Kirin Tor assign a magus who wants to know what they read.

## Reliability
La'tanja is one-sided about the Windshapers (honest but biased). Kae'la and Ysa'bel honest.

## What they notice
- Kae'la: Skyborne (books from home), mages (her upside-down diagram).
- La'tanja: Skyborne (the pylons), mages (ward anchors).
- Ysa'bel: night elves (kin long parted), Skyborne (heirlooms).
