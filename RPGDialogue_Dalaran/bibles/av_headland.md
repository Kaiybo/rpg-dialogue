## Their quests
- Haggerdin, The Sovereign Imperative (turn-in): Ironforge calls taking the valley "a sovereign and territorial imperative"; "Let's get to work!"
- Haggerdin, Proving Grounds (gives): "These Frostwolf savages just won't give up!"; Stormpike Banner in the Icewing Caverns southwest of Dun Baldar; harpies; insignia grows with rank; "the Eye of Command".
- Haggerdin, Rise and Be Recognized / Honored Amongst the Guard / Earned Reverence / Legendary Heroes / The Eye of Command: insignia upgrades by reputation.
- Laggrond, In Defense of Frostwolf (turn-in): "The favored clan of your Warchief is under attack from the nefarious Stormpike Guard"; "one must first be tested".
- Laggrond, Proving Grounds (gives): Frostwolf banner (Wildpaw Cavern).
- Laggrond, Rise and Be Recognized (gives): "a soldier of Frostwolf but also... a friend"; "Die with honor!"
