## Their quests
- none: no quests on the wiki.

## Identity
- **Brock**: says he's the oldest and in charge upstairs; wants to join the Kirin Tor; remembers smoke and being carried. Eager, proud.
- **Dallas**: keeps the rabbits; knows their titles and tempers; thinks everyone should be important to somebody. Earnest, gentle.
- **Josie**: tells bedtime stories by the fire; dreams of the skycutter and the island in the sky; badgers the lady at the landing (Laurain). Cheerful.
- **Korben**: paces and counts the boards (three creak); listens for the lamps to flicker (the Citadel's loose magic). Anxious, careful.
- **Liam**: Luna the wolf is his best friend; wants to be a hunter. Cheerful, loyal.
- **Lilly**: newest and smallest, came from Stormwind by cart; misses the ducks and the cathedral bells; Countess Bunidict has "chosen" her. Shy.
- **Cody** (Baker): feeds the children first and sells the rest for flour. Warm, jovial.
- **Paige** (Chef): runs the kitchen; prices set, "haggle with a goblin"; cats Nyx and Roger. Brisk, warm.
- Children speak in run-on, whole sentences; no fragments. Never graphic about how they were orphaned.

## Threads he could carry
- Josie and the skycutter connect to Arcanist Laurain (sky_enclave.lua); Korben's flickering lamps echo Laurain's; Korben reacts to the Shade of the Archmage being killed.

## Agenda
- The children want to be someone (archmage, hunter, story-teller, guard). Paige and Cody want the larder full.

## Secrets
Authored, not canon:
- The children named the rabbits together over three suppers; Count Ash Bunnington is grey "like ash", which made Lilly cry, so now it's "because he's grey".
- The Count visits Luna downstairs and sits on her paws; Liam taught Luna "you don't eat family".
- Liam found Luna as a crying pup in the snow by the city wall and carried her home in his coat.
- Lilly came from Stormwind because there weren't enough beds there; Josie rings a spoon on a pot for her at dinner.
- Paige came from Stromgarde after the wars; the Kirin Tor pay for the roof, not the larder.
- Cody's Mulgore Spice Bread recipe came from a far-travelled trader.
- The orphanage's children are orphans of the city's fall (implied, not stated outright).

## Reliability
Children are honest and often wrong (Brock's "the island will fall on us").

## What they notice
- Brock: mages. Dallas: pets near the rabbits. Josie: Skyborne (Walk on Air). Korben: warriors and paladins. Liam: pets, shapeshifting. Lilly: humans from Stormwind. Paige: cooks.
