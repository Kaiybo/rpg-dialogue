# More of the Alterac Valley front (keys: dal_stonehewer, dal_durgen, dal_teeka, dal_thanthaldis)

Wiki pages read 2026-11-18 (warcraft.wiki.gg, via tools/npc.py): Prospector Stonehewer, Sergeant Durgen Stormpike, Corporal Teeka Bloodsnarl, Thanthaldis Snowgleam; quest pages The Battle of Alterac, Hero of the Stormpike, Speak with Prospector Stonehewer, Capture a Mine (Alliance/Horde), Towers and Bunkers (Alliance/Horde), Alterac Valley Graveyards, The Graveyards of Alterac. The quests send players back "in the Alterac Mountains", so they sit with Haggerdin and Laggrond in this pack (see av_headland.md).

## Sources
- **Prospector Stonehewer** (female dwarf, Explorers' League, added 1.5.0): The Battle of Alterac (kill Drek'Thar in Frostwolf Keep; "The Explorers' League believes there are artifacts in Alterac Valley, but the Frostwolf orcs won't let us explore"; completion: "breathing room we need to explore"), Hero of the Stormpike ("these recently plun... er, recovered treasures").
- **Sergeant Durgen Stormpike** (male dwarf, added 1.5.0): Capture a Mine (supplies cached in Irondeep and Coldtooth; kill the leader), Towers and Bunkers (pull the banner; white banner; destroyed if held), Alterac Valley Graveyards ("prized territories... fight savagely for these sacred grounds").
- **Corporal Teeka Bloodsnarl** (female orc, Frostwolf): Capture a Mine, Towers and Bunkers ("Are you waiting for others to claim your glory?"), The Graveyards of Alterac ("spirits of our enemies will wander more lost and aimless"). Later Kosh'harg appearance not used.
- **Thanthaldis Snowgleam** (male night elf, Stormpike Supply Officer, added 1.5.0): reputation-gated rewards vendor. Bronze Celebration and Legion notes not used.

## Their quests
- Stonehewer: wants the fighting over so the League can dig; frank about "recovered" plunder.
- Durgen: practical orders; proud of the Guard; dislikes waste.
- Teeka: impatient for glory; proud of the clan; enjoys the enemy's lost spirits.
- Thanthaldis: none: vendor.

## Voices
- **Stonehewer**: dwarven burr, eager scholar-prospector; carvings, thorium, shovels.
- **Durgen**: dwarven burr, gruff sergeant; "soldier"; won't discuss his name.
- **Teeka**: plain, blunt orc; Durotan, Gul'dan's exile, the Warchief's blood; "Lok'tar".
- **Thanthaldis**: measured night elf, dry humour about dwarves; "Ishnu-alah" once.

## Authored, not canon
- Carvings on the Irondeep and Coldtooth walls that no dwarf can read (kept vague: "titan-work, maybe").
- Durgen: "half the clan's called Stormpike"; troggs left to fight orcs.
- Teeka: peons digging in captured mines.
- Thanthaldis: sent by Tyrande to learn the Alliance; the Stormpike wanted someone who counts without ale.

## What they notice
- Stonehewer: dwarves, exalted Stormpike. Durgen/Teeka: soldiers, PvP rank. Thanthaldis: night elves, exalted Stormpike.
