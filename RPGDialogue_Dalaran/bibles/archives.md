## Identity
- **Delv Fortuz** · Antonidas' Antiquities · Kirin Tor · human · archivist. Precise, dry, reverent, a little mournful. Full, careful sentences; "Grand Magus". Knows the collection and the man. Friendly rival of Helm (weekly supper argument); disapproves of Brammold selling old things. Never names who leads the council.
- **Helm Anvilhand** · Archival Exhibit · Kirin Tor · dwarf · archivist. Jovial, hands-on, talkative. Dwarf burr ("ye", "aye", "{g:lad|lass}", dropped g's), lighter than Brammold's. Lets visitors touch exhibits ("a child who held a seal remembers it"). Enough magic to read a ward and light a lamp.
- **Ranath Nightstride** · Linguists Lounge · Kirin Tor · Highborne shade (night elf, undead). Calm, wry, courtly, proud, with old guilt. Long, measured sentences. Reads the old tongue for the Kirin Tor. Four cats (Bub, Merry, Mister Tibbs, Pippin) sleep against him. Keeps his own story to himself.
- **Spellweaver Thaldris** · Talismanic Textiles · night elf · transmogrifier (in-world: weaves glamours so gear looks as the owner likes and still works as it did). Fussy, wry, vain about craft, kind underneath. Momo the cat sleeps on the best silk.
- **Seasoned Adventurer** · Cantrips & Crows · human · adventurer. Dry, weathered, practical. Waiting on a late party for the sewers; light touch.

## Threads he could carry
- Ranath and Ysa'bel Brightwind (tempered_tomes): she keeps asking about Eldre'Thalas and he won't answer; his line `dal_ranath.s2_default` (key) unlocks a reply in her conversation.
- Delv and Helm cross-reference each other; Helm echoes Opportunistic Education (the gold-painted Founding of Dalaran).

## Agenda
- Delv: keep the Grand Magus remembered for his stand, not his failure.
- Helm: put history in people's hands; keep the Perenolde seal out of the Syndicate's.
- Ranath: read in peace and not be judged for being Highborne.

## Secrets
Authored, not canon:
- Delv's collection is Antonidas's belongings dug from his tower's rubble; a cold-water teapot; a locked cabinet (thought to hold Antonidas's last notes) whose key Celindra keeps; she visits and never opens it; Delv keeps a note from Antonidas in his coat; Antonidas's primer with "Patience before power" in the margin.
- Delv was a Citadel clerk before the fall.
- Helm came from Ironforge to look after what the Explorers' League digs up; a smooth man (implied Syndicate) has twice asked to buy the Perenolde seal, which Helm now carries.
- Ranath has been dead "longer than this city has stood" and won't say how he died; he has told Ysa'bel nothing because he doubts she'll like the truth.
- Thaldris came to Dalaran because night elf elders frown on the arcane; his mother writes every season; "from Ashenvale's edges".
- The Seasoned Adventurer has heard shuffling (Atrexis's dead) through the sewer grate.

## Reliability
All honest. Delv is biased in Antonidas's favour; Helm's Alterac account is a dwarf's view.

## What they notice

- Helm: dwarves; gnomes.
- Ranath: night elves (old exile); mages.
- Thaldris: night elves; warriors' mismatched gear.
- Seasoned Adventurer: green adventurers.
