# RPG Dialogue: Arathi Highlands (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.8.0 (2026-11-15)
- Replies for the classes and races these people would really react to (warriors, hunters, rogues, mages, warlocks, druids, shamans, paladins; every race of the faction, and the Skyborne), each leading somewhere.

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-15)
- Every person here writes a first impression in your diary after you first talk (the journal's People page shows it too).

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Depth for Hammerfall: Drum Fel, Zengu and Innkeeper Adegwa, built from their Classic quests (The Hammer May Fall, Call to Arms, The Real Threat, Trollbane and the sigil chain to Trol'kalar).
- Drum Fel on the camp that was, the orcs who live there now, the Witherbark and Boulderfist, the ogres in Stromgarde and what Hammerfall will become; Zengu on Ignaeus Trollbane, the sigils, Tor'gan and where the sword goes; Adegwa on the grunts' songs about Doomhammer.
- Replies for who you are: orcs, Darkspear trolls, hunters, tauren; a reply for players who've helped Korin Fel (Drum doesn't learn her secret) or met Nimboya.

## 1.3.1 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: Refuge Pointe and Hammerfall now talk in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments. Nials is still brief and Drum Fel still driven, each in a sentence or two rather than a string of orders.

## 1.3.0 (unreleased)
- For a priest, while Stromgarde Badges is open: bless the badges before they go home to the families.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)
- Depth: Captain Nials (the keep, the Syndicate, Hammerfall, a reply for Lordaeron refugees).

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits bring personal lines (habits, worries, running jokes), and some NPCs get night-time lines. Adegwa.

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- Refuge Pointe (Alliance): Captain Nials. Stromgarde's defenders.
- Hammerfall (Horde): Drum Fel, Zengu (sent by Vol'jin for Trol'kalar) and Innkeeper Adegwa. Hammerfall's guardians.
