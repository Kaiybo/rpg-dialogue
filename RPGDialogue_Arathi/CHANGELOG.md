# RPG Dialogue: Arathi Highlands (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.25.0 (2026-11-17)
- New Forever-only characters (new files: full game restart): Muggol Breezebeard.

## 1.24.0 (2026-11-17)
- Shakes O'Breen and Captain Steelgut at Faldir's Cove have much more to say about the Raiders, the wreck and each other.

## 1.23.1 (2026-11-17)
- Small fixes; missing characters added to the file list where needed.

## 1.23.0 (2026-11-17)
- New people to talk to: Captain Steelgut, Foggy MacKreel, Shakes O'Breen, Zaruk. New files: restart the game fully (a /reload won't load them).
- Writing pass: stock phrases ("Fair enough", "I won't pretend", "truly", neat little morals) rewritten in each speaker's own voice, and more grit and humour in the everyday lines.

## 1.22.0 (2026-11-17)
- The town's leading people greet you differently when you come back after a fortnight or more, notice how far you've come since they last saw you, and have heard what you did with their neighbours. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.67.0.

## 1.21.0 (2026-11-17)
- Some people here notice who you travel with: a greeting about your companions, a reply to bring them up, and a conversation with stances to take. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.59.0.

## 1.20.0 (2026-11-17)
- Every class this place could plausibly meet now gets a greeting and its own conversation, including this server's Forsaken paladins and dwarf shamans where they'd be welcome. Some people here also notice when you've been thinning out the local trouble.

## 1.19.0 (2026-11-17)
- Druid replies for every named person here who had none: a greeting, a question and something to argue about. Night elf and tauren druids are both spoken to where it fits.

## 1.18.0 (2026-11-17)

## 1.8.0 (2026-11-15)

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-15)

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Depth for Hammerfall: Drum Fel, Zengu and Innkeeper Adegwa, built from their Classic quests (The Hammer May Fall, Call to Arms, The Real Threat, Trollbane and the sigil chain to Trol'kalar).
- Drum Fel on the camp that was, the orcs who live there now, the Witherbark and Boulderfist, the ogres in Stromgarde and what Hammerfall will become; Zengu on Ignaeus Trollbane, the sigils, Tor'gan and where the sword goes; Adegwa on the grunts' songs about Doomhammer.
- Replies for who you are: orcs, Darkspear trolls, hunters, tauren; a reply for players who've helped Korin Fel (Drum doesn't learn her secret) or met Nimboya.
