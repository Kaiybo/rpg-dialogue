# Arathi folk (keys: ar_oslight, ar_dwire, ar_victor, ar_narj, ar_tharlidun, ar_keena)

Wiki pages read 2026-11-17 (warcraft.wiki.gg): Refuge Pointe, Hammerfall, Field Marshal Oslight, Deathmaster Dwire, Doctor Gregory Victor, Triage (Horde), Narj Deepslice, Tharlidun, Keena.

## Sources
- **Field Marshal Oslight** (male human, Refuge Pointe; added 1.7.0): starts/ends "The Battle for Arathi Basin!", "Control Four Bases", "Control Five Bases" (PvP). Gossip: the League of Arathor has sworn to retake Arathi for the humans of Stromgarde and the Alliance, "a battle hard fought, but a battle worth fighting".
- **Deathmaster Dwire** (female Forsaken, Hammerfall; added 1.7.0): "The Battle for Arathi Basin!", "Take Four Bases", "Take Five Bases". Gossip: the Alliance must not expand their power base in Arathi; "even their miniscule Refuge Pointe may one day grow to threaten us."
- **Doctor Gregory Victor** (male Forsaken, Trauma Surgeon, Hammerfall): ends "Horde Trauma", starts/ends "Triage" (Horde; save 15 patients before 6 die; criticals first). Gossip: "head trauma surgeon of Horde Trauma". Completion: "Doctor <name>, welcome to Horde Trauma." `TODO_QUEST("Triage")` shares its title with the Alliance version (Theramore).
- **Narj Deepslice** (male dwarf, meat vendor, Refuge Pointe).
- **Tharlidun** (male orc, stable master, Hammerfall).
- **Keena** (female orc, trade goods, Hammerfall).

## Voices
- **Oslight**: straight-backed Stromgarde officer, formal, "soldier"; bitter about the Alliance's neglect only in private.
- **Dwire**: cold, clipped-but-whole sentences, contemptuous of the living; anger she calls useful.
- **Victor**: brisk, dry, exacting surgeon; "doctor" and "patient".
- **Narj**: loud dwarf butcher. **Tharlidun**: few words, wolves. **Keena**: hurried trader, no haggling.

## Authored, not canon
- Oslight: a Stromgarde soldier all his life; officers urging him to burn what they can't hold; rumour that the Defilers' Deathmaster was an Arathi woman.
- Dwire: was Arathi once and knew the Basin's farms (deliberately vague); the old farm wall by the stables; her tactic of letting the League take the stables.
- Victor: a Lordaeron surgeon and lecturer before the plague; Forsaken flesh doesn't mend on its own unless stitched.
- Narj: the League pays him late; soldiers eat on credit; raptor tastes like a boot in vinegar.
- Tharlidun: wolf bites as lessons; keeps his wolves where the internment camp's cages stood.
- Keena: goods come by ship to Undercity and wagon through the Foothills; the Forsaken buy salt by the barrel.
