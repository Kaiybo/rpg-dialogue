## Their quests
- Genavie Callow: To Steal From Thieves (Horde; heads of Kenata, Fardel and Marcel Dabyrie).
- Valorcall is the Horde's target for the Sigil of Arathor and the caretakers stand over Trol'kalar; none of that is spoken, since only Alliance characters would be on speaking terms with them.
- Jorell is the target of the Alliance's plague investigation; not spoken.

## Identity / voice
- Androd Fadran · human man · plain, steady supplier; not a soldier and says so.
- Apothecary Jorell · Forsaken man · cold, absorbed; livestock as "honest subjects"; reports to Apothecary Lydon.
- Caretaker Alaric · human man · eldest keeper, grave and reverent.
- Caretaker Nevlin · human man · the watcher; suspicious, blunt, loyal to the other two.
- Caretaker Weston · human man · youngest; gentle, glad of a living voice.
- Deckhand Moishe · goblin man · grumbling, counts his lost wages; street talk.
- First Mate Nilzlix · goblin man · brisk, keeps the ledger and the crew; blames the chartmaker "officially".
- Deneb Walker · human man · dry, tired; last shop in Stromgarde; sells the field-surgery manuals.
- Lieutenant Valorcall · human man, paladin · brisk and proud, speaks from the saddle.
- Genavie Callow · Forsaken woman · bitter fallen gentry; wants heads, not her belongings.

## Authored, not canon
- Androd followed the militia; raptor hide takes dye well.
- Jorell was a physician in life; orcs of Hammerfall keep the Alliance looking away.
- The caretakers' division of labour (Alaric prays, Weston sweeps, Nevlin watches); Weston saw Thoras ride out as a boy; Nevlin was a soldier. Thoras's death is left as "an assassin's hand, the truth not known to me".
- Moishe's finder's fee; Nilzlix's ledger view of the wreck.
- Deneb is Stromgarde-born; his runner from Refuge Pointe; the dead surgeon.
- Valorcall knows every horse by name; keeps the Silver Hand's vows.
- Genavie's family "an old house with a pew near the front".
