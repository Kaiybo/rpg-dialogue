# More Arathi folk (keys: ar_lolo, ar_draxlegauge, ar_maclear, ar_mortis, ar_hawke, ar_twing, ar_slagg, ar_uttnar)

Wiki pages read 2026-11-18 (warcraft.wiki.gg, via tools/npc.py): Lolo the Lookout, Doctor Draxlegauge, Sergeant Maclear, Deathstalker Mortis, Samuel Hawke, Rutherford Twing, Slagg, Uttnar; quest pages Land Ho!, Sunken Treasure (1)-(3), Arathi Basin Resources! (level 60), Cut Arathor Supply Lines.

## Sources
- **Lolo the Lookout** (male ogre, Blackwater Raiders, Faldir's Cove; in the game since alpha): starts Land Ho! Speaks of himself in the third person ("Lolo sees a tiny ...! Always on the lookout, Lolo is!"). Friend of Goutgut in Booty Bay (trivia).
- **Doctor Draxlegauge** (male gnome, Blackwater Raiders, Faldir's Cove): gives Sunken Treasure (the Goggles of Gem Hunting, 10 Elven Gems) and sends the gems to O'Breen. Quest text: "a little gnomish ingenuity goes a long way"; "I'd swim down there myself but... I have important scientific business... on the safe, dry land"; after weeks with pirates, "the last thing that's good for our health is to be caught hanging on to their treasure".
- **Sergeant Maclear** (female human, League of Arathor, Refuge Pointe; added 1.7.0): Arathi Basin Resources! "A battle fought with poor gear or on an empty stomach is lost before it starts"; metals, weapons, food, lumber; sends crates to the quartermasters.
- **Deathstalker Mortis** (male Forsaken, Defilers, Hammerfall; added 1.7.0): Cut Arathor Supply Lines. "The League of Arathor is sparing no expense in their move to retake Arathi... the best means to stop them is to take their supplies."
- **Samuel Hawke** (male human, League of Arathor Supply Officer, Refuge Pointe; added 1.7.0): Arathi Basin reward vendor; Arathor care packages.
- **Rutherford Twing** (male Forsaken, Defilers Supply Officer, Hammerfall; added 1.7.0): the Horde counterpart; Defiler's care packages.
- **Slagg** (male orc, Superior Butcher, Hammerfall): cooking trainer; teaches Barbecued Buzzard Wings.
- **Uttnar** (male orc, Butcher, Hammerfall): meat vendor.

## Their quests
- Lolo, Land Ho! (gives): proud of his eyes; afraid they'll be stuck far longer than the captain promised; mourns the two lost ships.
- Draxlegauge, Sunken Treasure (gives, turn-in): proud of his invention; scared of deep water; wary of being caught holding pirate gold.
- Maclear, Arathi Basin Resources! (gives, turn-in): wars are won by supplies; proud of quiet work.
- Mortis, Cut Arathor Supply Lines (gives, turn-in): starve the League rather than kill it; resents Stromgarde's growth.
- Hawke, Twing, Slagg, Uttnar: none: service NPCs; built from role, place and neighbours.

## Voices
- **Lolo**: always third person, simple, cheerful, whole sentences; proud of being a lookout; counts gulls badly.
- **Draxlegauge**: quick, anxious gnome engineer; long excited sentences; "um, well"; theories and arithmetic.
- **Maclear**: brisk Stromgarde sergeant, "soldier"; crates, manifests, empty stomachs.
- **Mortis**: cold, dry Forsaken; contempt for honour talk and for Stromgarde's kings.
- **Hawke**: steady quartermaster; fair but strict about earning kit.
- **Twing**: Forsaken clerk, prim and dry; loves his ledger.
- **Slagg**: proud orc cook; food as strength; the internment camps' scraps remembered.
- **Uttnar**: few words, blunt, "Buy."

## Authored, not canon
- Lolo saw the naga the night the ships sank and wasn't believed; he gave Steelgut a blanket.
- Draxlegauge drew a diving bell and won't build it; he left Gnomeregan before its fall for a Booty Bay post.
- Maclear: the Basin as the "first mile" home to Stromgarde Keep; Stromgarde earns Alliance help "one crate at a time".
- Mortis: killed by men who sang about honour; old grudge against Stromgarde for leaving the Alliance.
- Twing: was a Lordaeron quartermaster's clerk; the orc who claimed a mark twice.
- Slagg: cooks properly because of the camps. Uttnar: raptors strayed from the Witherbark.

## What they notice
- Lolo: small races, strangers. Draxlegauge: engineers, battered kit. Maclear/Mortis: soldiers, PvP rank. Hawke/Twing: reputation, PvP rank. Slagg: cooks, hunger. Uttnar: hunger.

_none for each.

## Added later: missed folk (Faldir's Cove, Refuge Pointe, Hammerfall)
Keys: ar_nilzlix, ar_karwn, ar_fadran, ar_strongbrew, ar_graud, ar_muuta, ar_junha, ar_tunkk (all in npcs/more_folk.lua).

Sources (warcraft.wiki.gg): First_Mate_Nilzlix (goblin, Blackwater Raiders, Faldir's Cove; quest Deep Sea Salvage), Hammon_Karwn (human, Superior Tradesman, Refuge Pointe), Androd_Fadran (human, Leatherworking Supplies, Refuge Pointe), Drovnar_Strongbrew (dwarf, Alchemy Supplies, Refuge Pointe), Graud (orc, General Goods), Mu'uta (orc, Bowyer), Jun'ha (orc female, Tailoring Supplies), Tunkk (orc, Leatherworking Supplies), all Hammerfall. The Hammerfall four's "Hammerfall Down" and "Resupplying Our Suppliers" are later quests and are not used.

Their quests
- Nilzlix, Deep Sea Salvage (gives, turn-in): the charts and logs of the Maiden's Folly and the Spirit of Silverpine; fears Fleet Master Seahorn's plank more than the naga; the ships struck "some sort of temple in the reef". Lines before, during, after and later.
- The seven vendors: none: built from role, place and neighbours.

Voices
- **Nilzlix**: harried goblin bookkeeper; "pal", "friend", "whoa, whoa"; everything is a ledger line; decent underneath (logs Lolo's sighting properly, shields Steelgut).
- **Karwn**: genial, a little vain human tradesman; proud of simply being there.
- **Fadran**: quiet human; hides, salt and patience.
- **Strongbrew**: hearty dwarf with a burr; wishes he sold ale; fond of the camp.
- **Graud**: old orc campaigner; water first, glory second.
- **Mu'uta**: patient, proud orc bowyer; tests every bow at dawn.
- **Jun'ha**: sharp, warm orc woman; keeps the fort's banners whole.
- **Tunkk**: loud, cheerful, thrifty orc; wastes nothing.

Authored, not canon
- Nilzlix: the lockboxes in the captains' cabins; no figure in the ledger's column for the dead; he lists Steelgut as a passenger.
- Karwn painted his own title on the sign; robbed twice on the road and paid the Syndicate once.
- Fadran goes out for hides only with two of the Captain's men; a wife who writes to him.
- Strongbrew: brewers in the family; a brother in Ironforge and a keg that never arrives.
- Graud carried stores on Thrall's march against the camps and at the landing in Durotar.
- Mu'uta learned bowmaking in the camps from an old hunter; unstrings one bow in three.
- Jun'ha cuts every Hammerfall banner; spider silk fetched by grunts.
- Tunkk's vats behind the wall, his scrap barrel for grunts without coin; the tauren who made needles from bones.

What they notice
- Nilzlix: goblins, gnomes, rogues, druids and shamans. Karwn: dwarves, tailors, engineers. Fadran: skinners, hunters, pets. Strongbrew: dwarves, alchemists, herbalists. Graud: weariness, trolls, tauren. Mu'uta: hunters, pets, trolls. Jun'ha: cloth wearers, torn kit, tailors. Tunkk: skinners, hunters, pets.
