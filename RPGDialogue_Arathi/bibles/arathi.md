## Depth rewrite (Horde towns, authored on sourced quests)
Sources read for this pass (warcraft.wiki.gg, Classic sections): Drum_Fel, Zengu, Innkeeper_Adegwa, Hammerfall, The_Hammer_May_Fall, Call_to_Arms_(1)/(2)/(3), The_Real_Threat_(Classic), Trollbane_(quest), Sigil_of_Strom_(Classic), The_Broken_Sigil, Sigil_of_Arathor_(Classic), Sigil_of_Trollbane_(quest), Trol'kalar_(Classic), Trol'kalar_(2).
- Drum Fel: Tallow in Tarren Mill sends players up (either hoping they die at the ogre mound or thinking them strong). Witherbark trolls and Boulderfist ogres work together to rid Hammerfall of the Horde; the trolls turned their backs on the Horde, the ogres would use orcs for food and bedding. Boulderfist lords hold the ruins of Stromgarde, "a glaring symbol of human frailty". Hammerfall will thrive once they're dealt with. Married to Korin Fel, who reads auguries and hid Or'Kalar's presence from him (The Real Threat); Or'Kalar hates Drum for defeating him years ago.
- Hammerfall (wiki history): after the Third War the ruins were reoccupied as an unofficial refuge for discontents who felt they had no place in the new world.
- Zengu: Trol'kalar, "troll slayer" in the ancient tongue, wielded by Lord Ignaeus of Strom, called Trollbane, against the forest trolls in the days of Arathor. Sigils shared among Stromgarde's survivors: one taken by the Syndicate, the Sigil of Thoradin split into eleven pieces by Prince Galen and rebound by Tor'gan, Hammerfall's troll shaman; one carried by Lieutenant Valorcall of the cavalry; the last held by Galen in the chapel. The sword goes to Nimboya at Grom'gol, for the war against Zul'Gurub's trolls.
- Authored, not canon: the grunts' songs about Doomhammer, Adegwa humming, Zengu's patience lesson, Hammerfall's hoped-for fields and herds.

## 0.37.0: Hammerfall and Refuge Pointe people
Sources (warcraft.wiki.gg, Classic sections): Hammerfall, Refuge_Pointe, Gor'mul, Tor'gan, Korin_Fel, Urda, Skuerto, Apprentice_Kryten, Cedrik_Prose, Vikki_Lonsav, Hammerfall_(Classic), Raising_Spirits_(2)/(3), Guile_of_the_Raptor_(2)/(3), Foul_Magics, The_Real_Threat_(Classic), Malin's_Request, Worth_Its_Weight_in_Gold_(Classic), Wand_over_Fist_(Classic), Trelane's_Defenses_(Classic), An_Apprentice's_Enchantment, Attack_on_the_Tower_(Classic).
- Gor'mul (orc): captive at the camp when Thrall and Orgrim broke it; saw Doomhammer killed by a lance from behind; left behind as the others escaped, wandered alone for years, returned when the Horde resettled; despairing; throws Tor'gan's raptor talon amulet in the mud (Raising Spirits); the fleshstalker-heart orb convinces him "for the time being" (Guile of the Raptor). Quest titles: Hammerfall, Raising Spirits, Guile of the Raptor.
- Tor'gan (troll shaman): believes the shaman's power will return Gor'mul to himself; Foul Magics (demonic summoning traces and a drained bloodstone amulet at Northfold Manor; Syndicate's dealings with dark magic); rebinds the Sigil of Thoradin (already in Zengu).
- Korin Fel (orc, Drum's wife, auguries): The Real Threat; Or'Kalar, Mug'thol's pawn in Stromgarde, hates Drum for defeating him years ago; she hid the signs so Drum wouldn't rush into the trap.
- Urda (orc, wind rider master). Cedrik Prose (human, gryphon master). Vikki Lonsav (human, general goods in Classic; innkeeper only from Cataclysm).
- Skuerto (dwarf) and Apprentice Kryten (human) are at Refuge Pointe at Archmage Malin's request to recover the artifacts of Malin's master Archmage Trelane, who defended the Tower of Arathor when Stromgarde fell. Skuerto: rude when busy, sensitive about his scouting; Wand over Fist (Kor'gresh in Boulderfist Hall, east of Thandol Span), Trelane's Defenses (Boulderfist shaman's agate), Attack on the Tower (phylactery, orb, ember agate). Kryten: apologizes for Skuerto; Worth Its Weight in Gold (Witherbark tusks, witch doctor pouches, shadow hunter knife), An Apprentice's Enchantment.

Never (this pass): Field Marshal Oslight, Sergeant Maclear, Deathmaster Dwire, Radulf Leder, the Defilers and League of Arathor (Arathi Basin, 1.7); Commander Amaren, Quae and Kinelory, Zaruk's Twilight's Hammer past, Skuerto's Ironforge eviction (Home Sweet Gnome) and Shakes O'Breen (Cataclysm). Tor'gan's "once-cursed blood" line not used.

Authored, not canon: Gor'mul's view of Drum and of Thrall, the camp-born advice; Tor'gan coming to Hammerfall because Drum needed a shaman, his raptor lesson; Korin's porridge and crows; Urda's lost rider and wide routes; Skuerto scouting troggs as a lad, his ale wager; Kryten's notes on the shadow hunter knife, Malin's tea; Cedrik carrying the badges south; Vikki's tallies and that she sold goods in Stromgarde before the fall.
