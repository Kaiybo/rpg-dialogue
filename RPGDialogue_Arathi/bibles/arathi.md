# Arathi Highlands  (keys: nials, drumfel, zengu, adegwa)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Refuge_Pointe (Stromgarde outpost of tents, no mailbox; after the keep fell in the Third War the surviving army stayed to retake it; Captain Nials leads and refused to spare soldiers for Thandol Span)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Captain_Nials (human, **female**; Classic gossip: one of the few remaining patches of Stromgarde territory, "we must take what is ours", duty forbids leaving the Highlands; Stromgarde Badges (Syndicate assassins took the badges of fallen soldiers, for their families), Plea To The Alliance, Northfold Manor)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Hammerfall (one of the Alliance's largest internment camps after the Second War; destroyed by Thrall and Orgrim Doomhammer to free the orcs; Orgrim fatally wounded by a lance; the Horde reoccupied the ruins and named it for him; Boulderfist and Witherbark attacks; Hammerfall Guardians)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Drum_Fel (orc warrior who rules Hammerfall, married to Korin Fel; sends champions against the Witherbark, the Boulderfist and Stromgarde; believes Hammerfall will thrive once its enemies are dealt with; The Hammer May Fall, Call to Arms)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Zengu (Darkspear hunter at Hammerfall; Vol'jin sent him for Trol'kalar to use against the Gurubashi; Trollbane, the Sigil quests, Trol'kalar; in Classic and Forever)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Innkeeper_Adegwa (male tauren innkeeper of Hammerfall)

## Their quests
- Nials: Stromgarde Badges, Plea To The Alliance, Northfold Manor.
- Drum Fel: The Hammer May Fall, Call to Arms. Zengu: Trollbane, Sigil of Strom, The Broken Sigil, Sigil of Thoradin, Sigil of Arathor, Sigil of Trollbane, Trol'kalar. Adegwa: none.

## Never
- Arathi Basin, the League of Arathor and the Defilers (patch 1.7, after Year 1's start; and battlegrounds aren't dialogue).
- Zengu's death after he gets the sword (it happens; he never foresees it). Hakkar, Zul'Gurub's return.
- Nials's later Red Dawn and the War Within, Drum Fel's later fates, Korin Fel and Or'Kalar (not sourced for Classic).
- Gossip verbatim (Nials's three lines, Adegwa's "wind on the plains").

## Voices
- Nials: firm, duty-bound captain of a kingdom in tents.
- Drum Fel: driven; Hammerfall will thrive; Doomhammer's name.
- Zengu: patient hunter, enjoys the irony of the sword.
- Adegwa: warm tauren innkeeper among orcs.
