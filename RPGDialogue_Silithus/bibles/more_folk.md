## Their quests
- Torrig: Report to Marshal Bluewall. Bloodfist: Report to General Kirika. Ralo'shan: none.

## Never
- Everything on silithus.md's Never list (the gates opening, the war effort, the gong, anything inside Ahn'Qiraj, C'Thun by name).
- Ralo'shan's later fate (the Sword of Sargeras, the Wound) and any "new sundering" line: her original gossip is paraphrased without it.
- What silithyst is for: nobody here knows.

## Voices (authored, not canon)
- Torrig: dwarf burr ("ye", "aye"); gallows humour on purpose, so recruits carry water. Order stance on fighting over silithyst.
- Bloodfist: blunt orc scout; the hives hum at night; kills the Twilight's Hammer when they cross his path. Honor stance on ambushing Alliance gatherers.
- Ralo'shan: grave, tired, remote; quotes her own lot plainly; the young one who fell before the Wall (Valstann, unnamed). Light stance on standing against the stirring forces versus surviving them.

## What they notice
- Torrig: dwarves, Alliance warriors. Bloodfist: orcs, Horde warriors. Ralo'shan: night elves, priests.
