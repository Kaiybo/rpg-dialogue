## Their quests
- Bluewall: Scouring the Desert (Alliance silithyst); the Horde got a head start.
- Blackanvil: Field Duty papers signed.
- Janela: Arms for the Field, Armor Kits for the Field.
- Kirika: Scouring the Desert (Horde); a race against the dwarves.

## Identity
- Bluewall: gruff dwarf marshal, impatient about the late start.
- Blackanvil: jovial dwarf captain, calls battles "skirmishes".
- Janela: brisk dwarf paladin lieutenant and supply officer.
- Kirika: blunt orc general, no small talk.

## Authored, not canon
- Half the Brigade's supplies sank off Tanaris, half "borrowed" in Gadgetzan; Blackanvil's clan from eastern Dun Morogh and his father who thinks he's somewhere snowy; Blackanvil writing letters to the families of the dead.
- Janela doubting whether the Silver Hand belongs in the Plaguelands instead.
- Kirika's grunts wanting to raid the dwarves' camp; her "alive when better orcs weren't" rise.
