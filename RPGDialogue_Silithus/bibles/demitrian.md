# Highlord Demitrian  (npcs/demitrian.lua; key demitrian)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Highlord_Demitrian (human, Twilight's Hammer / Prince Thunderaan, Ravaged Twilight Camp, Crystal Vale; added 1.3.0)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Examine_the_Vessel, https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Thunderaan_the_Windseeker_(quest) ("I have told you all that I know..."; "BEHOLD HE WHO IS OLDER THAN EXISTENCE")

## Their quests
- Examine the Vessel (ends), Thunderaan the Windseeker (both binding halves, 10 Enchanted Elementium Bars, Essence of the Firelord), Rise, Thunderfury! (ends).

## Identity
Human, devout to the Windseeker, grave and then suddenly ecstatic; calls the player "mortal"; frank about having worn the Hammer's robes.

## Never
- Anything about Cataclysm-era Twilight's Hammer, Cho'gall's later plans, or Legion-era Demitrian.

## Authored, not canon
- He joined the Hammer to "hear the sky speak", was left alone when the camp was ravaged, and no longer counts himself one of them.
- His gratitude even though the player kills Thunderaan to make the blade.
