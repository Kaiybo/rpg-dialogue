# Silithus  (keys: maralith, proudhorn, larksbane, vish_kozus, calandrath, beetix, bor_wildmane, huum_wildmane, aurel_goldleaf, baristolth, layo, ortell, rutgar, frankal; pool: guard_cenarion_hold)

Levels 55-60. The Classic zone as rebuilt in patch 1.8: Cenarion Hold in the middle of the desert, Valor's Rest at the top of the Un'Goro path, the Twilight's Hammer camps in the west, Bronzebeard's Encampment in the south, three silithid hives, and the Scarab Wall sealing Ahn'Qiraj. Everything below was read on warcraft.wiki.gg on 2026-11-15, Classic sections and Classic-only quest pages only. Nothing from Cataclysm (Ortell in Hyjal, the 4.1 rework) or Legion (Sargeras' sword, the Wound, Magni) is used.

**Forever and the gates.** The WoW: Forever page (read 2026-11-15) lists no Ruins or Temple of Ahn'Qiraj among Forever's raids (only Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia's Lair; Blackwing Lair isn't listed either), and its roadmap names no Ahn'Qiraj event. No NPC page read had a Forever section. So the pack is written for **sealed gates**: Ahn'Qiraj stands shut behind the Scarab Wall, the Circle watches and gathers, and nobody speaks of the war effort, the gong, the gates opening or anything inside. The 1.9 war-effort NPCs (Windcaller Kaldon, Warden Haro, Keyl Swiftclaw, Dirk Thunderwood, Janela Stouthammer) and the 1.12 Silithyst NPCs (Rifleman Torrig, Scout Bloodfist, Marshal Bluewall, General Kirika) are left out. Baristolth is in, but only as a Watcher keeping a very old promise; the Scepter chain is touched only through its first step (What Tomorrow Brings), never Blackwing Lair or the gates.

## Their quests
- Mar'alith: Dearest Natalia (gives and ends), Into The Maw of Madness (gives and ends), A Terrible Purpose (ends; letter afterwards). Reveals: a commander bound to his post while his wife is lost; he noticed her strangeness and let it pass; duty over grief ("whether she is alive or dead, I must do what is right"); he gives the order he can't carry out himself and turns away; horror at a name he has never heard; grateful, formal, changes his plans when the evidence says so.
- Proudhorn: Securing the Supply Lines, Stepping Up Security (gives and ends), Taking Back Silithus (ends). Reveals: a quartermaster's mind in a druid's robe; certain of victory if the supplies hold; worms worry her more than bugs; praises in measured, proper words.
- Larksbane: The Twilight Mystery (gives and ends), The Deserter (gives), Unraveling the Mystery (ends). Reveals: a geologist who hears the crystals hum louder each day; patient with puzzles, knows when to ask someone else; sees the prism's trick before anyone and doesn't like what it says.
- Vish Kozus: Wanted - Deathclasp, Terror of the Sands (ends), Boots for the Guard (1.9 logistics; noted only for the mounted division). Reveals: a captain who wants proof, not stories ("no pincer, no reward"); thinks in routes and patrols; worried for his commander and blunt about everything else.
- Calandrath: Desert Recipe (gives), Sharing the Knowledge, Kitchen Assistance (gives and ends). Reveals: homesick for Auberdine; makes do in a hard place; fusses over travellers; amused to learn the cult can cook.
- Beetix: Deadly Desert Venom, Noggle's Last Hope (gives and ends); Noggle's Lost Satchel (Noggle gives). Reveals: family first, then business; brisk under pressure; owes you "big time" and means it, in goblin terms.
- Bor Wildmane: Secret Communication, Encrypted Twilight Texts (gives and ends), the Abyssal Council crest, signet and scepter quests. Reveals: cheerful, practical, the younger brother; can't read the cult's gibberish and doesn't need to; keeps score of your victories.
- Huum Wildmane: Twilight Geolords, Vyral the Vile (gives and ends), Aurel Goldleaf, Goldleaf's Discovery (gives). Reveals: the spiritual elder brother; hears the land's grief; strikes at heads, not hands; hands the bigger plan to Aurel.
- Aurel Goldleaf: Aurel Goldleaf (ends), Dukes of the Council, Lords of the Council (Revered), Twilight Ring of Lordship, Medallion of Station. Reveals: a craftsman of disguises; patient, exact; believes a well-made lie can beat an elemental lord.
- Baristolth: What Tomorrow Brings (gives and ends); the later Scepter steps exist but aren't used. Reveals: a Watcher bound by the bronze a thousand years ago, waiting for a sign; speaks of the hive and the second war as though he can see them; distrustful of mortals on his master's behalf.
- Layo Starstrike: Wasteland (ends), The Spirits of Southwind, Hive in the Tower (gives and ends), Umber, Archivist (gives). Reveals: young for a night elf, careful, reverent of the dead; believes in freeing souls whatever their banner; hopes a journal in a shell can open a window on the past; fond of Umber despite himself.
- Ortell: The Deserter (ends), The Twilight Lexicon, A Terrible Purpose (gives), True Believers, Still Believing. Reveals: frightened and proud of his cleverness; hunted; hates the cult that hunts him; muddles his own code; wants them gone so he can stop hiding.
- Rutgar: Glyph Chasing, Unraveling the Mystery (gives); tells the first half of Natalia's story. Reveals: blunt, impatient, protective of the dig; exasperated with Brann; angry at Ironforge for ignoring them; certain they're onto something huge.
- Frankal: Breaking the Code; tells the second half. Reveals: grumbling storyteller, proud of their languages, watches the skies; describes Natalia's shadow magic in terms a priest will recognise.

## Identity and voices
- Race voice (0.80.0): kaldorei (Mar'alith, Vish Kozus, Calandrath, Layo, Aurel, Kania, Leoren, Mishta, Cloud) lean on Elune, the moon, long memory and Malfurion's balance; tauren (Proudhorn, the Wildmanes, Runk, Khur, Vargus) on the Earth Mother, herds and plains; dwarves keep the burr.
- **Commander Mar'alith** (night elf, Cenarion Circle, commands the Hold). Grave, formal, tired, distracted mid-sentence, then all duty again. Speaks in long, careful sentences; uses "Natalia" softly and "the cult" coldly. Knows: the hives, the cult camps, the Hold's soldiers, Natalia's last weeks. Relationships: Natalia (his wife), Vish Kozus (trusts him), Larksbane and the Wildmanes (his eyes on the cult), Layo (the Circle's man at Valor's Rest), the dwarves (grateful, doubtful). Attitudes: Alliance and Horde both welcome if they keep the peace; respects druids and soldiers; priests stir something painful (Natalia was a priestess). Notices: priests (and Shadowform), druids, night elves. Agenda: hold the line; find Natalia; win. Never: C'Thun before Into The Maw of Madness; the gates opening; Ossirian; anything about what lies inside Ahn'Qiraj.
- **Windcaller Proudhorn** (tauren druid). Measured, confident, proper, a quartermaster's precision; "provided" and "assuming" are her favourite hedges. Notices: tauren, druids, a ragged-looking kit. Never: the 1.9 war effort.
- **Geologist Larksbane** (dwarf, the Circle's geologist). Warm, curious, practical; a light burr ("ye", "aye", dropping the odd g); listens to stone. Notices: dwarves, miners, mages and priests who read. Never: the Prophecy of C'Thun's text (The Calling needs the gates).
- **Vish Kozus** (night elf, Captain of the Guard). Dry, blunt, sardonic, protective of the commander; never explains the blindfold. Notices: rogues and stealth, weapons drawn, Horde and Alliance in the same courtyard.
- **Calandrath** (night elf, innkeeper). Fussing, kind, homesick, a cook's chatter; "you poor thing". Notices: sand-covered travellers (low durability), leatherworkers, night elves from Darkshore.
- **Beetix Ficklespragg** (goblin alchemist). Fast, practical, frets for Noggle while pricing everything. Notices: alchemists, poisoned players, goblin-friendly Horde.
- **Bor Wildmane** (tauren druid, younger brother). Cheerful, plain-spoken, "crazies", "big brother". Notices: tauren, druids, cultist trappings.
- **Huum Wildmane** (tauren druid, elder brother). Slow, solemn, spiritual; speaks of the land and the spirits. Notices: tauren, shamans and druids, priests.
- **Aurel Goldleaf** (night elf craftsman). Precise, quietly proud, a little conspiratorial; treats disguise as a craft. Notices: crafters, rogues (deception).
- **Baristolth of the Shifting Sands** (night elf, Watcher of the bronze). Grave, oracular, patient, slightly outside time; "mortal", "the Brood", "the master". Notices: night elves, druids, anyone who has walked the Caverns of Time. Never: Blackwing Lair, the Scepter's pieces, the gong, the gates.
- **Layo Starstrike** (night elf druid, Valor's Rest). Gentle, earnest, careful; speaks of the dead with respect and of Umber with fondness. Notices: priests and druids (spirits), night elves, Horde visitors.
- **Hermit Ortell** (human, ex-Twilight's Hammer). Jumpy, suspicious, then garrulous and pleased with himself; mixes up his own instructions. Notices: priests (the cult hates the Light), humans, anyone wearing cult trappings. Never: Hyjal, the Guardians, anything Cataclysm.
- **Rutgar Glyphshaper** (dwarf, Bronzebeard's Encampment). Gruff, impatient, funny without trying; "Are ye daft?". Notices: dwarves, Explorers' League types, Alliance.
- **Frankal Stonebridge** (dwarf). Grumbling, storytelling, sky-watching; proud of the camp's languages. Notices: priests (Natalia's spells), mages, the monkey's opinion of you.
- **Cenarion Hold Infantry** (pool). Night elf sentinels of the Circle; firm about neutral ground.

## Agendas, claims, reliability
- Mar'alith: honest; he tells himself Natalia may yet be saved, which the player may know is false.
- Rutgar and Frankal: honest, comic, exact about the attack; they know nothing of C'Thun.
- Ortell: honest about the cult; less honest about why he joined (authored, below).
- Baristolth: tells the truth sparingly; withholds almost everything.
- Proudhorn: certain of victory "provided" the supplies hold; her optimism is a claim, not a fact.

## Never (the whole pack)
- The gates opening, the Scarab Gong, the Scepter's fragments, Blackwing Lair, the war effort, Ossirian, the Twin Emperors, anything inside Ahn'Qiraj.
- C'Thun named before Into The Maw of Madness is done (Mar'alith's own completion line is the first time anyone at the Hold says it).
- Cataclysm and later: Sargeras' sword, the Wound, Magni, Ortell as an elementalist in Hyjal, Twilight Highlands, Deathwing's cult.
- Vish Kozus called a demon hunter.

## Authored, not canon
- Mar'alith keeps Natalia's field notes and hasn't been able to read past the first weeks.
- Larksbane presses her ear to crystal samples at night and counts the hum.
- Vish counts the patrols coming back, not going out.
- Calandrath keeps a jar of Darkshore sand-free soil for a moonberry cutting that won't take.
- Huum and Bor argue about whether the land or the bugs are the bigger problem.
- Aurel once fooled her own brother with a forged Sentinel's badge (a craftsman's joke).
- Layo tends the Valor's Rest graves and doesn't know all their names.
- Ortell joined the Hammer because they promised the world's end would make sense of his bad luck; he left when he saw what they did to a man who doubted.
- Rutgar writes to Ironforge every week; Frankal reads the replies aloud in a pompous voice.

Persuade/Intimidate/Deceive checks: what a success reveals (authored, not canon).
