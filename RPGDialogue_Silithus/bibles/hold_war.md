## Their quests
- Kaldon: Field Duty (report to your faction's camp, get papers signed); Allegiance to Cenarion Circle (Combat/Tactical/Logistics badges); Cenarion Battlegear (to Vargus). Keeps the two armies apart.
- Dirk Thunderwood: The Perfect Poison (Venoxis's and Kurinnaxx's venom sacs for Ravenholdt).
- Zannok: none (vendor).

## Identity
- Kaldon: formal night elf windcaller, quartermaster mind; dry about his "lists".
- Dirk: friendly, sly Ravenholdt dwarf; defends poison as a weapon.
- Zannok: dry, weary night elf at Valor's Rest.

## Authored, not canon
- Kaldon's view that the armies will have to fight together if Ahn'Qiraj wakes.
- Dirk's line that he took the job to escape Alterac's cold; two apprentices who licked their blades.
- Zannok's druid critics of hive-hunting.
