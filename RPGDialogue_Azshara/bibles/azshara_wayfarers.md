# Azshara wayfarers  (npcs/azshara_wayfarers.lua; keys joy_arnareth, rayan_dawnrisen, rageroar)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Joy_Ar%27nareth and https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Rayan_Dawnrisen (high elves at the top of Xylem's Tower, Bear's Head; Classic only)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gatekeeper_Rageroar (before Cataclysm a Timbermaw Hold furbolg in Azshara; killing him cost Timbermaw reputation)

## Their quests
- none: the wiki gives them no quests or gossip.

## Identity
- Joy: proud, sharp, eager quel'dorei scholar; studies the ancestral Highborne ruins.
- Rayan: calm, precise; copies inscriptions so something survives.
- Rageroar: furbolg third-person voice; gruff gatekeeper; reputation-gated greetings.

## Authored, not canon
- Joy and Rayan's reason for being there (studying near Xylem, using his library); Joy wants to go deeper into Eldarath, Rayan wants to go home.
- Some Timbermaw want the Azshara gate sealed with stone; Rageroar thinks a closed gate is a weak gate.
