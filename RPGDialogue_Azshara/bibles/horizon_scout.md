# The Horizon Scout survivors  (npcs/horizon_scout.lua; keys shandril, roland_geardabbler)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Second_Mate_Shandril (human, on a spit of land watching the wreck, waiting for the crew to escape)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Roland_Geardabbler (gnome keeping the trapped crew alive with a water-breathing aura inside the wreck)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Horizon_Scout (shipwreck off the Ruined Reaches; azsharite chunk on the forward stairwell; naga likely sank it), Lindros (mage passenger, business unknown), Patrick Mills (paladin) and Alexandra Blazen (priest) came aboard together.
- Captain Vanessa Beltis (npcs/eldarath.lua), A Crew Under Fire.

## Their quests
- none of their own (Classic gossip-only); they react to Beltis's A Crew Under Fire.

## Identity
- Shandril: steady, anxious second mate; follows orders and hates it.
- Roland: nervous, cheerful gnome who talks to keep his concentration; won't abandon the crew.

## Authored, not canon

- Suspicion of Lindros (never came to dinner, chained trunk); the passengers paid well and wouldn't say why.
- Four crew trapped, sleeping in shifts; the ship's cat Biscuit; Roland's ward flickers when he sneezes.
