## Their quests
- Loh'atu: A Land Filled with Hatred (gives/ends), Spiritual Unrest (gives/ends). Reveals: an oath to the Earthmother kept on night elf ground, a guest who keeps the peace by keeping promises, pity for the dead Highborne and dread of arcane hunger; diplomacy as a daily labour.
- Jarrodenus: none: the hippogryph master; carried by his roost and the routes.
- Ogtinc: Cenarion Aid (ends), Of Coursers We Know, The Ichor of Undeath, Blood of Morphaz (gives); The Hunter's Charm (ends), Courser Antlers, Wavethrashing, The Green Drake. Reveals: a furbolg who left his people for the Circle; reverent of the coursers and grieved to kill them; a healer's patience and a hunter's eye; the poison is part of the cure.
- Peter Galen: Dragonscale Leatherworking (gives/ends). Reveals: a craftsman who works from the scales of a deadly prey, gallows humour about dragon hunters.
- Xylem: Magic Dust, The Siren's Coral, Destroy Morphaz (mages); Encoded Fragments, The Azure Key (rogues, for Lord Ravenholdt); Warlord Krellian, Fragmented Magic (mages); Delivery to Archmage Xylem (ends), Xylem's Payment to Jediga (gives). Reveals: arrogant, impatient, delighted by results; a grudge against a drake that ate his apprentice (and his shard, which he mentions in the same breath); considers violence a kind of persuasion; collects Highborne knowledge; keeps bargains to the letter.
- Sanath: Magecraft (ends), Meeting with the Master. Reveals: a gatekeeper stripped of his toll, loyal without question, bored and spiteful.
- Ag'tor: Betrayed (ends), Betrayed (2), Betrayed (3) (ends), Betrayed (4) (gives). Reveals: a loyal officer cleaning up his commander's mistake; grief for a dozen dead warriors; contempt for those who ran; stubborn hope.
- Jediga: Stealing Knowledge, Delivery to Andron Gant, Delivery to Magatha, Delivery to Jes'rimon, Delivery to Archmage Xylem, and the payments back. Reveals: exiled for saying no to a powerful orc; makes profit of exile; trusts nobody, Xylem least; wants home.
- Kroum: none: the wind rider master.
- Hydraxis: Poisoned Water, Stormers and Rumblers, Eye of the Emberseer (gives/ends). Reveals: pride in purity, war with fire, air and earth; mortals as useful allies to be tested; a duke's patience and a soldier's contempt for cowardice.
- Loramus: The Demon Hunter (ends), Loramus, Breaking the Ward, The Name of the Beast, Azsharite, The Formation of Felbane. Reveals: sees demons nobody else sees; contempt for mortal blindness and foolhardiness, and grudging admiration of it; cold knowledge of Razelikh; works through others because he cannot leave the hunt.
- Arkkoroc: The Name of the Beast (ends), (2) and (3) (gives). Reveals: a god-proud sea giant who speaks of himself as "the one", grieving his children, scornful of the "false god" Hetaera; pays fairly.
- Kim'jael: Kim'jael Indeed!, Kim'jael's "Missing" Equipment. Reveals: a goblin double-crossed and indignant about it, cheerful about other people's danger, quick to renegotiate, still hunting the elves' treasure on his own account.
- Beltis: A Crew Under Fire (repeatable). Reveals: a captain who lost her ship and blames herself for the dead; steadiness under attack; passengers with business she won't explain.
- Xiggs: Arcane Runes (ends), Return to Tymor (gives); An Easy Pickup, Signal for Pickup (in Ironforge). Reveals: a cheerful dwarf pilot doing a friend a favour; the naga aren't his problem; likes his job.

## Identity and voices
- **Loh'atu** (tauren, neutral, a guest at Talrendis Point). Patient, careful, warm, dryly humorous about his welcome; "the Earthmother", "the land"; asks people to keep the peace. Knows: the satyr of Haldarr, the Highborne spirits at the Shadowsong Shrine, the queen's story as the elves tell it, the naga at Eldarath, the mage to the north, the furbolgs' fall, the blue dragonkin. Relationships: the Sentinels (hosts who watch him), Ogtinc (a kindred servant of the land), Thunder Bluff (authored: he writes to the elders). Attitudes: night elves deserve courtesy on their own ground; Horde visitors must keep their tempers here; priests and druids are allies against restless spirits and corruption. Notices: tauren, night elves, the player's faction. Never: Alliance-Horde violence at the camp; Cataclysm.
- **Jarrodenus** (night elf, hippogryph master). Formal, brief, fond of his hippogryphs, wary of the cursed coast. Never: "Commander", the war with Valormok.
- **Ogtinc** (furbolg hunter, Cenarion Circle). Slow, grave, gentle, speaks of "the divine path" and "spirits"; few contractions. Knows: the coursers, the wavethrashers, Felwood's sickness, Morphaz's blood, the furbolg tribes' corruption. Attitudes: priests and druids welcome; hunters tested; anyone who kills for sport, not. Notices: priests, druids, hunters (and pets), furbolg-killers (Timbermaw reputation). Never: which tribe he left (wiki only speculates); Blackmaw Hold's people by name.
- **Peter Galen** (human, Stormwind, dragonscale leatherworker). Dry, practical, morbid jokes; "condolences". Notices: leatherworkers, dragonscale on the player.
- **Archmage Xylem** (human archmage). Grand, impatient, amused, arrogant; calls the player by name or "my friend" when pleased, "dear" class word when not; speaks of apprentices as a resource; delighted by competence. Knows: Highborne lore, Morphaz, the Spitelash sirens, Krellian, the elves' dust, Ravenholdt. Relationships: Sanath (useful), Jediga (a trader), Lord Ravenholdt (a correspondent), Azuregos (authored: an irritable neighbour). Attitudes: mages and rogues earn interest; priests "pray where I calculate"; night elves "squandered" the Highborne's legacy; Skyborne of the High Order are fascinating. Never: the Legion, a grimoire, madness, the Focusing Iris, demon masters. Only dramatic irony: he is eccentric, nothing more.
- **Sanath Lim-yo** (elf of Quel'Thalas, Xylem's servant). Sneering, bored, petty, loyal; insults clothes and faces. Notices: the player's clothes (nearlyNaked), elves, mages. Never: his master's plans.
- **Ag'tor Bloodfist** (orc, Valormok). Gruff, honest, loyal to Belgrom despite himself; grief and stubborn hope; respects anyone who comes. Never: the rear gate, Chawg, Cataclysm.
- **Jediga** (orc, Valormok). Sharp, sardonic, mercenary, proud; wants out; fierce about her refusal. Never: crude jokes about Belgrom's appetites; she states it plainly and moves on.
- **Kroum** (orc, wind rider master). Plain, gruff, short. Never: Bilgewater, Hellscream's Hellions.
- **Duke Hydraxis** (water elemental, Duke of the Waterlords). Formal, cold, grand, impatient with mortal smallness, honest about using them; "the pure waters", "the lesser elements", "Neptulon". Notices: shamans, mages who conjure water, Skyborne (air-touched), the Firelord's enemies (Thorium Brotherhood? no: Blackrock deeds). Never: Molten Core entered, Ragnaros banished, the Firelands, the Earthen Ring alliance (Legion).
- **Loramus Thalipedes** (night elf demon hunter). Cold, scornful, precise, weary; "mortal", "{race}"; sees what others can't and says so. Knows: Razelikh, Kazzak's lieutenants, the triad, true names, the fel. Relationships: his felhounds; Arkkoroc (useful); Galvan (an old acquaintance). Attitudes: priests' Light is a lamp in a dark room; warlocks are fools who think they hold the leash (his own hounds aside); night elves look away from him. Never: Illidan as his master, Outland, Illidari, his later death, the Blasted Lands' later events.
- **Lord Arkkoroc** (sea giant). Grand, slow, wrathful, grieving; speaks of himself as "the one" and "Arkkoroc"; calls Hetaera false. Never: the Heart of Arkkoroc, Cataclysm.
- **Kim'jael** (goblin, Booty Bay). Fast, indignant, mercenary, cheerful; "Kim'jael indeed!"; "employee". Notices: purses, engineers, elves. Never: his real name (unknown).
- **Captain Vanessa Beltis** (human, the Horizon Scout). Steady, sea-salted, grieving, wry; "ahoy". Notices: priests and healers, sailors, the wounded. Never: the passengers' unfinished business beyond hints.
- **Pilot Xiggs Fuselighter** (dwarf, Ironforge pilot). Cheerful, brisk, "lad/lass", proud of his flying machine. Never: Cataclysm, anything but the rubbings run.
- **Sentinels at Talrendis Point** (pool). Watchful, terse but whole-sentenced, protective of the bridge; "the cursed coast".

## Agendas, claims, reliability
- Xylem: honest about what he wants, silent about why (a scholar's silence, nothing darker).
- Jediga: says Xylem can't be trusted (mistaken; he pays in full, and she admits it).
- Kim'jael: says he'll "figure out" the rune; he means to sell it (Intimidate reveals).
- Loh'atu: reliable, says when his knowledge is thin.
- Loramus: reliable and cruel; Arkkoroc: reliable, self-important.

## Never (the whole pack)
- Anything Cataclysm or later: Bilgewater goblins, Gallywix, the rocketway, the Rear Gate, the "Commander" Jarrodenus, Blackmaw Hold's village, Xylem's madness and the Legion, Sanath's Hallow's End errands, Loramus's death and the Illidari, the Earthen Ring's alliance with Hydraxis, Molten Core entered or Ragnaros defeated, Kazzak or the Dark Portal opening.
- Crude language about Rimtori or Jediga.

## Authored, not canon
- Loh'atu writes to the elders of Thunder Bluff about what he finds; the Sentinels read the letters, and he lets them. Jarrodenus won't fly the hippogryphs over the bay at night. Ogtinc names every courser he has had to kill. Peter Galen keeps a list of the dragon hunters who never came back for their armour. Xylem's apprentice in Morphaz's belly was his best and his most annoying; he regrets the shard aloud and the apprentice quietly. Xylem calls Azuregos an irritable neighbour. Sanath counts the days since anyone paid him. Ag'tor has the twelve dead warriors' names cut into the post by his tent. Jediga keeps a list of what she'll drink first in Orgrimmar. Hydraxis compares mortals to rain: brief, but they wear down stone. Loramus feeds his hounds on what he hunts. Arkkoroc counts his sons each tide. Kim'jael means to sell the rune back to whoever pays most. Beltis lights a lamp each night for her drowned crew. Xiggs's helipad is "the finest bit of flat rock in Azshara".

## 1.0.0 checks
Persuade/Intimidate/Deceive: what a success reveals (authored, not canon, unless marked).
- Xylem (Persuade, `tablet_why`): the Highborne wrote down how they bound the arcane into objects; most is madness, but enough is sense to let a mage forge magic as a smith forges steel, the craft Morphaz's shard gave back to him (Destroy Morphaz canon: "forge the arcane").
- Hydraxis (Persuade, `why_mortals`): water cannot go where fire is strongest and stay itself; the Waterlords need mortals for the places they cannot follow, and Neptulon expects results.
- Loramus (Persuade, `price`): he paid for his sight and will not name the price, only that it can't be given back and that he would pay it again.
- Ogtinc (Persuade, `tribe`): his own people went angry and sick, and he left because he would not hunt his brothers; the Circle gave him a way to fight the sickness instead.
- Loh'atu (Persuade, `trust`): he writes to Thunder Bluff and lets the Sentinels read the letters, which is why they let him stay.
- Kim'jael (Intimidate, `rune_truth`): he'll sell the rune to whoever pays most, Rimtori's people included.
