# Barrens: the coast south of Ratchet (keys: islen, mahren)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg (Classic-only sections): Islen Waterseer (powerful tauren shaman by a fishing hut on the coast; strong bond with the spirits of water; old friend of Greta Mosshoof; Call of Water steps: sends shaman to Brine in the southern Barrens, then to the tainted shrine on the Silverpine coast west of the Sepulcher, gives the water totem; Water Sapta; Seeking Spiritual Aid from Felwood, she asks the water spirits whether the cause is just). Mahren Skyseer (camp beside Islen; Jorn Skyseer's sister; "Mahren Skyseer" from Jorn for hunters; Isha Awak, the Deep Doom, lord of those waters, feared by the humans of the coast; "He lurks in your eyes").
- Consistent with Greta Mosshoof (`RPGDialogue_Felwood`), Jorn Skyseer (Crossroads), Xanis Flameweaver (`RPGDialogue_ThunderBluff/npcs/bluff_callers.lua`).

## Voices
**Islen Waterseer.** Gentle, slow, grandmotherly ("little one"); water as rebirth and the danger of pollution.
**Mahren Skyseer.** Quiet, reverent toward prey; the hunt as respect.

## Authored, not canon
- Islen and Xanis sat by the same fire as children; she followed the rivers from Mulgore to the sea; Northwatch patrols walk past her hut.
- Mahren and Jorn were raised by one hunter; Jorn never swims.
