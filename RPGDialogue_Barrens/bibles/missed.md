# The Barrens, missed folk  (keys: gahroot, kelsuwa, sanuye, brine, takar, vrang, lantigah, jorb, pilot_wizzlecrank, gruk_barrens, karu, okla, trok, gerogg_hammertoe, digger_flameforge, heggin_stonewhisker, malgin_barleybrew)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Northern_Barrens_NPCs and Southern_Barrens_NPCs (roster scan); `tools/npc.py` for each page. The wiki shows Cataclysm places (Camp Una'fe, Vendetta Point, Forward Command); everyone here is written in their Classic place and title.
- Gahroot (butcher), Kelsuwa (stable master), Sanuye_Runetotem (leather armor merchant): all Camp Taurajo in Classic.
- Brine (orc shaman on a hill in the southern Barrens; Call of Water; "a sliver of hope").
- Takar_the_Seer (human at a small camp east of Camp Taurajo; friendly to the Alliance only; Devourer of Souls, Heartswood).
- Vrang_Wildgore (weaponsmith & armorcrafter, camp north-west of Dreadmist Peak; injured leg; lion hides for a hermit tanner on the ridge over the Dry Hills; traps in the valley).
- Lanti'gah (at Regthar Deathgate's bunker; "Halfway there..." during Counterattack!), Jorb (Lizzarik's ogre bodyguard; fights with his fists; Gadgetzan-tied), Pilot_Wizzlecrank (steps out of Wizzlecrank's Shredder at the end of The Escape; Tinkers' Union).
- Gruk (orc boy, brings Guard Taruc bread, making a spear), Karu, Okla, Trok (Kranal Fiss's children; their in-game chatter: plainstrider roast, pigs, rocks for a necklace, bees and fire).
- Captain_Gerogg_Hammertoe (upper floor of Bael'dun Keep), Digger_Flameforge (tent at the dig), Heggin_Stonewhisker (chief engineer; crane, flying machine tower), Malgin_Barleybrew (morale officer behind the bar; sells drink). Bael'dun dwarves are friendly to the Alliance.
- Existing pack: taurajo.md (Byula, Jorn Skyseer), regthar.md, lizzarik.md, north_crossroads_more.md (Kranal Fiss), south_coast_seers.md (Islen Waterseer), south_taurajo_roads.md (Gann Stonespire, Prospector Khazgorm).

## Identity / voice
- Gahroot · tauren man · calm, thorough; thanks each animal; nothing wasted.
- Kelsuwa · tauren man · gentle; calms beasts by not hurrying.
- Sanuye Runetotem · tauren man · thoughtful, dry; a Runetotem who never heard the Dream.
- Brine · orc woman · still, searching; asks why you came; water as the quiet power.
- Takar the Seer · human man · serene, odd; has heard the conversation already; won't describe the future.
- Vrang Wildgore · orc man · proud, sore about asking for help; keeps his word.
- Lanti'gah · troll man · sharp-tongued, grumbling camp-keeper; hates the pigs; would let beaten Kolkar run.
- Jorb · ogre man · slow, placid; third person, simple whole sentences; "little boss".
- Pilot Wizzlecrank · goblin man · jumpy, fast; everything is a ledger line.
- Gruk · orc boy · eager, polite ("sir"). Karu · orc girl · bossy, caring. Okla · orc girl · dreamy. Trok · orc boy · frantic, sheepish.
- Captain Gerogg Hammertoe · dwarf man · stern, weary, half sorry for the tauren. Digger Flameforge · dwarf man · keen, single-minded. Heggin Stonewhisker · dwarf man · harried, proud. Malgin Barleybrew · dwarf man · jolly, shrewd listener.

## Authored, not canon
- Lanti'gah is written as a troll to match regthar.lua (the wiki infobox says orc peon).
- Gahroot's words before the knife; the bones going to Byula's pot; hides to Sanuye.
- Kelsuwa's raptor in the third pen, waiting on a troll gone to the Razorfen.
- Sanuye and Arch Druid Hamuul's counsel; smoking hides for many days.
- Brine respected by the Bristlebacks; her view of fire and Kranal Fiss.
- Takar's reason for leaving Stormwind (the city too loud to his sight); Gakin's unanswered letters; tauren braves watching his fire.
- Vrang's wound from a lioness; what his traps catch. The hermit tanner's nature is left unsaid, as in the quest.
- Jorb coming up from Tanaris (from his Gadgetzan tie); throwing a centaur.
- Wizzlecrank's six years, scrip pay and rebuilt valves.
- Okla keeping sharp rocks for Gruk is canon; the necklace for Karu is ours. Trok's torch at the nest is read from his "bees didn't like fire" line.
- Gerogg's thirty years in the guard; Heggin's sand in the bearings; Malgin's keg of Dun Morogh stout; Digger's bell-ringing layer.
