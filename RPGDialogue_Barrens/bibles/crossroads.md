# The Crossroads  (keys: thork, sergra, mankrik, tonga, helbrim, darsok, zargh, boorand)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Crossroads (roster; run mainly by orcs with tauren, trolls and some Forsaken; Thork leads its defence; quilboar and centaur threats; Mankrik and Darsok were there before the Cataclysm)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Thork (orc warrior defending the Crossroads for the Warchief; hospitable but firm, mind your business, loyalty to the Horde; Journey to the Crossroads, Disrupt the Attacks, The Disruption Ends, Supplies for the Crossroads, Report to Kadrak, Betrayal from Within)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sergra_Darkthorn (orc woman in charge of the Crossroads; champions women in the Horde, warns men not to disrespect her; Kargal Battlescar dislikes her for it; Plainstrider Menace, The Zhevra, Prowlers of the Barrens, Echeyakee, Jorn Skyseer, The Angry Scytheclaws, Crossroads Conscription)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Mankrik (he and his wife Olgra fought quilboar until separated and overwhelmed; a tauren druid found him wounded on the Gold Road; watches the horizon daily; Thork refused help because rage clouds his judgment; Consumed by Hatred, Lost in Battle)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tonga_Runetotem (tauren of the Runetotem clan, knows the Barrens better than anyone; sent from Thunder Bluff to find a strange power, traced it to the Wailing Caverns; Moonglade's help against poisonings near Dreadmist Peak; a salve for sick gazelles; The Barrens Oases, The Forgotten Pools, The Stagnant Oasis, Altered Beings, Hamuul Runetotem, Mura Runetotem, Gathering the Cure)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Wailing_Caverns (Naralex and his disciples meant to restore the Barrens through the Emerald Dream; the Nightmare overwhelmed him; the waters, beasts and most followers (the Druids of the Fang) were corrupted)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Apothecary_Helbrim (Forsaken of the Royal Apothecary Society; research-minded; Wharfmaster Dizzywig, Fungal Spores, Apothecary Zamah)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Darsok_Swiftdagger (orc rogue, boastful and bloodthirsty; friend of Witch Doctor Jin'Zil; Harpy Raiders, Harpy Lieutenants, Serena Bloodfeather, Letter to Jin'Zil)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Zargh (orc butcher, friendly; warns travellers to stock up; friend of Innkeeper Gryshka; lost a finger in an accident; Meats to Orgrimmar, Return to the Crossroads (a possible wedding))
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Innkeeper_Boorand_Plainswind (tauren innkeeper, hospitable)

- Sergra: Plainstrider Menace, The Zhevra, Prowlers of the Barrens, Echeyakee, Jorn Skyseer.
- Mankrik: Consumed by Hatred, Lost in Battle.
- Tonga: The Barrens Oases, The Stagnant Oasis, Hamuul Runetotem, Gathering the Cure.
- Helbrim: Fungal Spores, Apothecary Zamah, Wharfmaster Dizzywig.
- Darsok: Harpy Raiders, Harpy Lieutenants, Serena Bloodfeather, Letter to Jin'Zil.
- Zargh: Meats to Orgrimmar, Return to the Crossroads.
- Boorand: none: innkeeper only.

## Voices
- Thork: firm host; the road, the walls, the Warchief.
- Sergra: fierce, proud leader; blunt about orcs who resent a woman in charge.
- Mankrik: grief turned to fury; watches the horizon; softens only about Olgra.
- Tonga: measured, worried druid; "always start with the water".
- Helbrim: clinical Forsaken apothecary, dry and a little unsettling.
- Darsok: swaggering assassin, jokes with an edge.
- Zargh: jolly butcher with a missing finger and a crush on Gryshka.
- Boorand: warm tauren host; the "where is Mankrik's wife" question everyone asks.
