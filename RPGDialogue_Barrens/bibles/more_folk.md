# More of the Barrens  (keys: kelm, halija, kalyimah, larhka, menara, ruga, kirge, wizzlecrank, grub, duhng, tarban, tinkerwiz, traugh, krulmoo)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kelm_Hargunth (Warsong Supply Officer, added 1.5.0; quests Outrider Basic/Standard/Advanced Care Package). Placed by the road to Warsong Gulch in the northern Barrens; the wiki's Mor'shan Base Camp is the later location.
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Halija_Whitestrider (tauren clothier, Crossroads), https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kalyimah_Stormcloud (troll, bags & sacks, Crossroads; wiki gives male), https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Larhka (orc beverage merchant, Crossroads)
- Crossroads neighbours in crossroads.lua and north_crossroads_folk.lua (Thork, Sergra, Mankrik, Boorand, Moorane, Wrahk).

## Their quests
- Kelm: Outrider Basic Care Package, Outrider Standard Care Package, Outrider Advanced Care Package (Warsong Gulch marks of honor).
- The others: none (vendors).

## Never
- Cataclysm Barrens: the Northern/Southern split, Mor'shan Base Camp, Legion invasions, Camp Taurajo's fall.

## Voices (authored, not canon)
- Kelm: gruff, proud Warsong; deeds not words; Grom killed Mannoroth and the clan holds Ashenvale (honor stance on why the land is ours). Rep-gated wares.
- Halija: gentle tauren clothier far from Mulgore; trusts the orcs because Cairne does (order stance).
- Kalyimah: chatty troll, knows the road news; points to Thork, Sergra and Mankrik (honor stance on his prices).
- Larhka: jovial drink seller; oases going bad from the Wailing Caverns (learn); friendly with Boorand (order stance).

## What they notice
- Kelm: orcs, Warsong standing. Halija: tauren. Kalyimah: trolls. Larhka: tauren.

## Added later (missed NPCs)
Sources: warcraft.wiki.gg pages for Menara Voidrender (human warlock tutor, Ratchet; history with Tabetha), Ruga Ragetotem (Camp Taurajo), Kirge Sternhorn (Camp Taurajo, pre-Cataclysm), Wizzlecrank's Shredder (Sludge Fen), Grub, Duhng and Tarban Hearthgrain (the hut east of the Crossroads; Tarban is kin to Moorane), Tinkerwiz (Ratchet), Traugh (Crossroads forge), Krulmoo Fullmoon (Camp Taurajo, alive in Year 1).

### Their quests
- Menara: In Search of Menara Voidrender, Components for the Enchanted Gold Bloodrobe, The Completed Robe (warlock robe; components named only as "kept by difficult people").
- Ruga: Trial at the Field of Giants (twitching antennae from the silithid), then Speak with Thun'grim (north_far_watch.lua). He does not know what the silithid are; nothing of Silithus or Ahn'Qiraj.
- Kirge: Journey to the Crossroads (word to Thork that Taurajo holds but the Bristleback are bold).
- Wizzlecrank: Ignition (key from Supervisor Lugwizzle), The Escape (walk the shredder out; plans to Sputtervalve in Ratchet).
- Grub: Dig Rat Stew (dig rats from Bael Modan; teaches the recipe).
- Duhng, Tarban, Tinkerwiz, Traugh, Krulmoo: none: trainers and a baker, built from role, place and neighbours.

### Never
- Cataclysm: Taurajo burned, Krulmoo's ghost, Kirge at Vendetta Point, Kelsuwa at Una'fe, Southern/Northern Barrens split, Legion invasions. No Silithus, Ahn'Qiraj or qiraji from Ruga or Kirge.

### Voices (authored, not canon)
- Menara: cool, exacting, cultured human; left Stormwind's cellar for a window in Ratchet (order stance); teaches both sides (light stance).
- Ruga: stern, measured tauren; courage without anger (honor stance); small dangers ignored (order stance).
- Kirge: plain watchman; warns the Crossroads without hope of help (honor stance); mate and young son in camp.
- Wizzlecrank: frantic goblin whisper; cut around a tauren camp and was fined (authored; light stance); selling the plans (honor stance).
- Grub: hearty, stubborn; learned to love rat in the camps (honor stance).
- Duhng: terse; won't cook kodo beside Tarban (honor stance); teaching grunts to eat (order stance).
- Tarban: gentle, unhurried; first loaf for the Earth Mother (light stance); the baking stone and the centaur (honor stance).
- Tinkerwiz: brisk, smug goblin; one small bang on day one (order stance); neutral invoice (light stance).
- Traugh: gruff field smith; reads the war from backplates, Thork asked him to keep quiet (order stance).
- Krulmoo: patient; tells each hide what it will become (honor stance); kodo only from the graveyard (light stance).

### What they notice

Duhng: hunters, fishers. Tarban: tauren, the young. Tinkerwiz: miners, mages. Traugh: broken gear, miners. Krulmoo: skinners, hunters.

## Added later (second pass of missed NPCs)
Sources: warcraft.wiki.gg pages for Hraq, Kil'hala, Tari'qa (Crossroads), Kelsuwa, Yonada, Sanuye Runetotem, Dranh, Mahani, Gahroot (Camp Taurajo), Zikkel, Ranik, Vexspindle, Zizzek, Gagsprocket, Vazario Linkgrease, Acolyte Fenrick, Acolyte Magaz, Acolyte Wytula (Ratchet), Waldor and Kalldan Felmoon (cave above the Wailing Caverns), Feegly the Exiled (Bael Modan). All pre-Cataclysm placements.

### Their quests
- Feegly the Exiled: The Tear of the Moons (the gem from General Twinbraid's strongbox in Bael'dun Keep); the lead-in quest Feegly the Exiled gets a greeting. He dug it up, the dwarves took it and drove him out. The later claim that he was once a dwarf is not used.
- Acolyte Magaz: Shard of an Infernal; Acolyte Wytula: Shard of a Felhound. Both are parts of Strahad's Components for the Enchanted Gold Bloodrobe (warlocks only). Where the demons are found is left to Strahad, since the zone is unconfirmed.
- Vazario Linkgrease: Membership Card Renewal (one greeting).
- Kil'hala: the wiki lists Laughing Sisters and Nether-lace Garment; not written (era unconfirmed).
- Everyone else: none: vendors, trainers, a banker, a stable master and an acolyte, built from role, place and neighbours.

### Never
- Cataclysm and later: Taurajo burned (Yonada and Dranh as spirits, Kelsuwa and Gahroot at Una'fe, Sanuye and Mahani at Vendetta Point), the Legion invasions (Zikkel and Zizzek rescued, Gagsprocket's dynamite and his fondness for Liv Rizzlefix), Ranik's move to the smithy, Feegly as a transformed dwarf.

### Voices (authored, not canon)
- Hraq: shrewd, unbothered; one cart in four is lost and the price says so (light stance); shouts at Traugh by day and shares his fire by night.
- Kil'hala: particular Darkspear tailor; lined Sergra's cloak in red (authored); finery in a town under threat (order stance).
- Tari'qa: watchful, dry; reads the war from what is bought; grunts buying road supplies quietly (order stance).
- Kelsuwa: gentle, slow; listens to the beasts; kept a whipped lion from its hunter (authored; order stance).
- Yonada: warm, practical; sends raptor leather north; whether to share the caravans' losses (light stance); the oasis bark dyes wrong.
- Sanuye Runetotem: thoughtful; knows each hide's hunter; lets orcs think the rite is a craft secret (honor stance); Tonga is of his tribe, no closer kinship claimed.
- Dranh: quiet, exact; clean knife and a moment of quiet, or learn elsewhere (honor stance).
- Mahani: cheerful weaver among hide workers; teaching the tribe's patterns to orcs (light stance).
- Gahroot: hearty; the old and the young eat first (light stance); no kodo on his block.
- Zikkel: fussy, nervous second banker; wants customers searched (order stance); a boat under the pier.
- Ranik: glib, ambitious; rents a corner from Jazzik and eyes a better spot (honor stance).
- Vexspindle: smooth, vain; hates the name Plate-n-Chain; the sign under his bed (honor stance).
- Zizzek: the goblin who has enough; quit the docks for a chair by the tavern (order stance).
- Gagsprocket: jumpy, friendly; the red shelf; Venture Company powder on consignment (order stance); lost part of an ear to a short fuse (authored).
- Vazario Linkgrease: loud, proud, half deaf; an apprentice lost two fingers (authored; light stance); cards from Nixx Sprocketspring in Gadgetzan.
- Acolyte Fenrick: earnest, anxious young human; kin letters from Northwatch (authored; order stance).
- Acolyte Magaz: fierce, guarded; studies infernals to turn the weapon (light stance); left Gan'rul's teaching (authored).
- Acolyte Wytula: dry, cold; felhounds against the Scourge and the Scarlet Crusade (light stance); a schoolmistress in life (authored).
- Waldor: calm, curious; works deviate scale while Ebru calls it the skin of a sickness (light stance).
- Kalldan Felmoon: reserved, exacting night elf; came south after Naralex's followers (authored); whether Naralex was wrong to try (order stance). Sells to both factions.
- Feegly: sly, wheedling trogg in the third person, but in whole joined sentences; finder keeps (order stance).
