- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kelm_Hargunth (Warsong Supply Officer, added 1.5.0; quests Outrider Basic/Standard/Advanced Care Package). Placed by the road to Warsong Gulch in the northern Barrens; the wiki's Mor'shan Base Camp is the later location.
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Halija_Whitestrider (tauren clothier, Crossroads), https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kalyimah_Stormcloud (troll, bags & sacks, Crossroads; wiki gives male), https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Larhka (orc beverage merchant, Crossroads)
- Crossroads neighbours in crossroads.lua and north_crossroads_folk.lua (Thork, Sergra, Mankrik, Boorand, Moorane, Wrahk).

## Their quests
- Kelm: Outrider Basic Care Package, Outrider Standard Care Package, Outrider Advanced Care Package (Warsong Gulch marks of honor).
- The others: none (vendors).

## Never
- Cataclysm Barrens: the Northern/Southern split, Mor'shan Base Camp, Legion invasions, Camp Taurajo's fall.

## Voices (authored, not canon)
- Kelm: gruff, proud Warsong; deeds not words; Grom killed Mannoroth and the clan holds Ashenvale (honor stance on why the land is ours). Rep-gated wares.
- Halija: gentle tauren clothier far from Mulgore; trusts the orcs because Cairne does (order stance).
- Kalyimah: chatty troll, knows the road news; points to Thork, Sergra and Mankrik (honor stance on his prices).
- Larhka: jovial drink seller; oases going bad from the Wailing Caverns (learn); friendly with Boorand (order stance).

## What they notice
- Kelm: orcs, Warsong standing. Halija: tauren. Kalyimah: trolls. Larhka: tauren.
