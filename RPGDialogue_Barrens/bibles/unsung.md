# The Barrens' unsung  (keys: devrak, barg, hulamahi, uthrok, omusa, bragok, wenna, ironzar)

People with no quests and little or no gossip in the game: flight masters and shopkeepers. Written from memory of the vanilla world; no wiki page read in this pass.

## Sources
- Vanilla 1.12 rosters (memory): Devrak (Wind Rider Master), Barg (General Supplies), Hula'mahi (Reagents and Herbs), Uthrok (Bowyer & Gunsmith) at the Crossroads; Omusa Thunderhorn (Wind Rider Master) at Camp Taurajo; Bragok (Flight Master), Wenna Silkbeard (Tailoring Supplies), Ironzar (Weaponsmith) in Ratchet.
- bibles/crossroads.md, taurajo.md for Thork, Sergra, Tonga and the poisoned oases, the Wailing Caverns, Razorfen Kraul, Bael Modan; Ratchet's Gazlowe and the Southsea pirates.

## Their quests
- None.

## Identity and voices
- Devrak: gruff orc wind rider master; respect over fear with the beasts; flies high over the Dreadmist harpies.
- Barg: gruff supplier; "water, and then more water"; sells more since the oases fouled.
- Hula'mahi: cheerful, shrewd troll; throws out herbs from bad water (authored, not canon). Gender assumed female (unsure).
- Uthrok: blunt bowyer; bow versus gun; goblin guns from Ratchet, dwarven ones at Bael Modan.
- Omusa Thunderhorn: calm tauren; the wind riders have private names (authored, not canon); quilboar of Razorfen Kraul and the Bael Modan dwarves.
- Bragok: gruff orc flight master in a goblin town; the goblins pay on time; the boat to Booty Bay.
- Wenna Silkbeard: warm; dyes from the Booty Bay boats; sells to both sides in neutral Ratchet. Race not stated (unsure).
- Ironzar: smooth goblin; stock from Booty Bay, Gadgetzan and the Merchant Coast; Southsea Freebooters are bad for business.

## Never
- Cataclysm Barrens (the split, Northwatch's assault, Taurajo burned).
- Promises of discounts.

## What they notice
- Flight masters: wounds, a hunter's pet. Barg: empty purses. Hula'mahi: herbalists, shaman (ankhs). Uthrok: hunters. Wenna: tailors. Ironzar: heavy purses.
