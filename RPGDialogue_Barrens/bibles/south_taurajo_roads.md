# Barrens: the roads around Camp Taurajo (keys: logmar, dogran, gann)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg (Classic-only sections): Grunt Logmar (Camp Taurajo; gives Dogran's Captivity: Dogran held in a quilboar camp to the north, two huts, heavily guarded; no manpower to spare). Grunt Dogran (promoted to the Crossroads, then Camp Taurajo; led grunts to a quilboar camp and was badly hurt; Ken'zigla's Draught from the Malaka'jin; loses use of leg and arm; Love's Gift: gives Zankaja's pendant to the warlock, chooses to die in battle, asks that she not be told). News of Dogran (2): the healing draught from the Malaka'jin trolls; a first messenger killed by goblins. Gann Stonespire (wanders the roads near Bael Modan; the hills were his tribe's home and provider; diplomacy failed; Gann's Reclamation, Khazgorm's journal; Revenge of Gann 1-2: nitroglycerin, wood pulp, sodium nitrate, destroy the flying machine, "champion of the lost tribe").
- Consistent with Gazrog (`gazrog.lua`: the Bristleback took Dogran near Agama'gor) and Zankaja/Gan'rul (`RPGDialogue_Orgrimmar/npcs/warlock_errand.lua`).

## Voices
**Grunt Logmar (orc).** Tired, guilty, honest; "counting the steps back".
**Grunt Dogran (orc).** Proud, wry in pain; thinks of Zankaja first.
**Gann Stonespire (tauren).** Grave, solitary; done with open hands.

## Authored, not canon
- Logmar was promoted with Dogran and followed him; wanted to wait for more grunts. Dogran counted wrong and owns the call; the quilboar missed the pendant under his armour.
- Gann: his tribe buried their dead in the Bael Modan hills, scattered by centaur, the rest joined Cairne; the prospector laughed and offered to sell him a map of his own land; once a husband.
- Pendant bought by Zankaja in the Drag (see the Orgrimmar bible).

## Never
No Cataclysm Taurajo (its burning is later); no Southern Barrens.
