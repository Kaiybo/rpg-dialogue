# The Barrens, Forever-only people  (keys: gurak, pallwick, sulhasa)

NPCs added in World of Warcraft: Forever (patch 1.60.1). The wiki has no quest text for their quests; everything beyond the sources is **authored, not canon**.

## Their quests
- Gur'ak, Chol'aruk the Ravener (gives/turn-in): bring the head of the Razormane flesh-eater of Thorn Hill. Reveals old hatred of the quilboar, vengeance.
- Sulhasa, Field to Clear (gives/turn-in): quest text not on the wiki; from the title, the rewards (Taurajo Headband, Beastculler's Knife) and her perch, she wants the beasts in the field below culled. Reveals embarrassment, practicality.
- Pallwick: none.

## Identity and voices
- Gur'ak: grim old Crossroads veteran; Thork and Sergra named; "the pig that needs killing". Notices warriors, tauren, Razormane kills.
- Pallwick: dry Forsaken bonesetter; orcs watch his hands; fascinated by tauren bones. Notices wrecked armor, Forsaken.
- Sulhasa: warm, rueful tauren of Camp Taurajo, treed by beasts. Notices tauren, druids in bear/cat form.

## Invented details (authored, not canon)
- Gur'ak: at the Crossroads since the first stakes; fought in Thrall's war; was in the internment camps (won't talk about it); a peon lost on the Thorn Hill road; spikes the head by the gate.
- Pallwick: set bones in life; chose "Boneset" himself; sent by the Dark Lady to show the Forsaken are useful; his patients include a quilboar-chewed grunt.
- Sulhasa: from Camp Taurajo; came to judge the field as kodo grazing; the beasts hunt in packs in the long grass (species deliberately not named: unknown).
