## Identity
Kadrak · Mor'shan Rampart, northern Barrens · Horde · Orc (Warsong) · warrior
Personality: fierce, proud, practical about losses
Speech: clipped soldier talk, 'Blood and thunder'
Knows and cares about: Ashenvale, timber, Grom Hellscream, night elves
Relationships: Grom (revered), Thork
Default attitudes: admires orcs and warriors; respects Sentinels as foes
Lore anchors: Grom killed Mannoroth in Ashenvale and broke the blood curse
Never says: anything after Year 1 (no Burning Crusade or Cataclysm events)

## Threads they could carry

## Agenda
Push the Horde into Ashenvale.

## Secrets
None.
- Counts every life a log costs (authored, not canon).
- Night elves: hostile. They hate every orc for Grom killing Cenarius; the Warchief's envoys came back with arrows, so the Horde would still live in tents. Believes, without proof, that the elves pay the Kolkar (claim `kadrak_elves_pay_kolkar`, false; Kuray'bin exposes it).

## Reliability
Biased toward the Horde's claim to Ashenvale.
