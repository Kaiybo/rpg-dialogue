# Barrens: Ratchet, more (keys: drohn, bigglefuzz, wrenix)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg (Classic-only sections): Brewmaster Drohn (orc outside his house in Ratchet; mentor taught Stormstout by Chen Stormstout, who also taught Trogg Ale; Chen's Empty Keg 1-2: savannah lion tusks, plainstrider kidneys, thunder lizard horn; Chen travelled with Rexxar). Crane Operator Bigglefuzz (goblin; Trouble at the Docks: overloaded the crane with the Booty Bay shipment, Mad Magglish fled into the Wailing Caverns with a 99-year-old port meant for Gazlowe; "Huge I say!"). Wrenix the Wretched (goblin with his Gizmotronic Apparatus; Wrenix of Ratchet from Zando'zan in Orgrimmar ("the last five that crazy troll sent me ended up as shark food"); Plundering the Plunderers: the Southsea treasure in the hold of the Tide Razor, pirates' thief den on the second deck, E.C.A.C. and Thieves' Tools from the apparatus; Forever: Parts and Pieces, not used).

## Voices
**Brewmaster Drohn (orc).** Jovial, nostalgic; "never brew angry".
**Crane Operator Bigglefuzz (goblin).** Fast, harried, "I say", costs and accounts, scared of Gazlowe.
**Wrenix the Wretched (goblin).** Smooth showman, shameless about dead thieves; "pal", "on account".

## Authored, not canon
- Drohn fetched water for a brewmaster in the camps, met Chen once as an apprentice in Durotar ("never brew angry"); the port is a favour-seeking gift from Booty Bay; Drohn wants Byula's cactus-ale recipe; trogg brew saved his mentor in a Badlands cave.
- Bigglefuzz worked cranes in Undermine as a kid; the last operator who touched the wrong lever was "found in Tanaris".
- Wrenix: "the Wretched" is a family title; a cousin is Wrenix the Wonderful in Gadgetzan; the apparatus is his bed; the E.C.A.C.'s letters forgotten.
