# Camp Taurajo  (keys: jorn, mangletooth, tatternack, byula)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Camp_Taurajo (tauren outpost in the southern Barrens; inn, vendors, trainers, flight; Jorn Skyseer leads; Mangletooth the quilboar; **the camp remains in Classic and Forever**, destroyed only in Cataclysm)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Jorn_Skyseer (tauren hunter; guide and mentor to young hunters; the Barrens teem with life for those who know where to look; Classic quests: Ishamuhale, Lakota'mani, Owatanka, Washte Pawne and the hunter chain)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Mangletooth (Razormane quilboar held captive awaiting execution; hateful, scheming, snorts; claims the Razormane are stronger than the Bristlebacks; Tribes at War, Blood Shards of Agamaggan (trades shards for the boar god's blessings), Betrayal from Within (against Nak, Kuz and Lok Orcbane, who abandoned him); Agamaggan fell in the War of the Ancients, thorns grew from his blood, his skull lies to the south)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tatternack_Steelforge (orc sent by Thrall to study weapons and armor and meet travelers at Taurajo; curious, open-minded about other cultures, even quilboar and centaur; Weapons of Choice)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Innkeeper_Byula (male tauren innkeeper; in Classic, warm and welcoming)

## Their quests
- Jorn: Ishamuhale, Lakota'mani, Owatanka, Washte Pawne.
- Mangletooth: Tribes at War, Blood Shards of Agamaggan, Betrayal from Within.
- Tatternack: Weapons of Choice. Byula: none.

## Never
- The burning of Taurajo, Byula's grief, Mangletooth's escape, Vendetta Point (all Cataclysm). No hint of any of it.
- The beasts' details beyond what each name and quest implies (authored: "older and fiercer", "moving hill").
- Tatternack's gossip verbatim ("even the most pathetic of cultures").

## Voices
- Jorn: patient hunter-teacher; stillness.
- Mangletooth: snorting, scheming captive; third person.
- Tatternack: curious smith-scholar.
- Byula: warm tauren innkeeper; An'she and Mu'sha, herd imagery, parting blessings.
