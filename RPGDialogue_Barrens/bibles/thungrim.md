# Thun'grim Firegaze  (key: thungrim)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Thun%27grim_Firegaze : orc hermit and renowned blacksmith atop a mountain just south of Thorn Hill, east of the Crossroads; old and near-blind ("eyes have long dried out from staring into the forge's flame"); forged weapons and armor for young Horde warriors.
- Quests (original WoW): Thun'grim Firegaze (from Uzzek), Forged Steel, Speak with Thun'grim (from Ruga), Brutal Armor, Brutal Hauberk, and the brutal pieces sent to Ula'elek, Orm Stonehoof and Velora Nitely.
- 0.93.0: was earlier written as a fire shaman (Call of Fire); that was wrong and is retired. Call of Fire belongs to Kranal Fiss/Telf Joolam.

## Their quests
- Thun'grim Firegaze (ends; from Uzzek after Path of Defense), Speak with Thun'grim (ends; from Ruga after Trial at the Field of Giants), Brutal Armor and the brutal set.

## Identity
Thun'grim Firegaze · forge on a mountain south of Thorn Hill · Horde · Orc · warrior questgiver, smith
Personality: stern, patient, wry, ashamed of the old Horde's fel-forged blades
Speech: slow, by ear and touch; steel "sings" or "thuds"
Relationships: Uzzek and Ruga (send him warriors), Ula'elek, Orm Stonehoof, Velora Nitely (brutal set), the Burning Blade (contempt)
