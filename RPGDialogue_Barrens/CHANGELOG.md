# RPG Dialogue: The Barrens (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.2.1 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: everyone at the Crossroads and Camp Taurajo now talks in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments, each in their own voice (Thork's plain duty, Sergra's pride, Mankrik's grief, Tonga's patience, Helbrim's dry chill, Darsok's swagger, Zargh's chatter, Mangletooth's snorting third person). Same lines, same facts, same conditions.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits now bring personal lines (habits, worries, opinions of the neighbours), night-time lines, and a question or two about the person themselves, not only their quests.
- Byula, Tatternack, Mangletooth, Boorand, Zargh, Thork.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Camp Taurajo: Jorn Skyseer, Mangletooth in his cage, Tatternack Steelforge, Innkeeper Byula.

## 1.0.1 (unreleased)
- Zargh's opening can be heard about elsewhere (Gryshka in Orgrimmar).

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- The Crossroads: Thork and Sergra Darkthorn, Mankrik, Tonga Runetotem and the poisoned oases, Apothecary Helbrim, Darsok Swiftdagger, Zargh the butcher and Innkeeper Boorand Plainswind. The Crossroads' guards.
