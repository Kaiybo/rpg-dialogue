# Sun Rock keepers  (keys: tharm, gereck, hgarth, jeeda)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sun_Rock_Retreat (read 2026-11-17: NPC list)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tharm (read: tauren Wind Rider Master at Sun Rock; level 55 before 3.0.2, so present in Classic)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gereck (read: orc Stable Master)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Hgarth (read: orc enchanting trainer on a cliff above the town reached by a narrow steep path from a nook east-northeast of the largest tent; gossip "Have you come to seek the knowledge of Hgarth?"; title was Artisan Enchanter before 2.3.0)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Jeeda (read: orc female Apprentice Witch Doctor, reagent vendor upstairs in the inn; sells herbs, potions, vanishing powder)
- Classic flight paths from Sun Rock remembered as Thunder Bluff, the Crossroads, Shadowprey Village (not re-checked).

## Their quests
- None. Service NPCs (flight, stable, trainer, vendor).

## Identity
- Tharm · tauren male · Wind Rider Master. Calm, watchful; talks of winds like relatives.
- Gereck · orc male · Stable Master. Gruff with people, fond with beasts; no whips.
- Hgarth · orc male · Enchanting Trainer. Aloof, proud, disciplined; the climb is the test.
- Jeeda · orc female · Apprentice Witch Doctor. Eager, jumpy, wants a real teacher.
Relationships: Maggran, Tammra, Jayka; Kulwia (Hgarth's students buy from her); Jin'Zil at Malaka'jin (Jeeda's hoped-for master).
Never says: anything Cataclysm or later; Jin'Zil's prisoners in detail (Jeeda has only "heard things").

## Agenda
Tharm: keep riders alive. Gereck: keep beasts well. Hgarth: teach only the committed. Jeeda: find a master.

## Reliability
All honest; Jeeda unsure of herself.

## What they notice   (2–3 things)
Tharm: tauren, Skyborne, druids, wounds. Gereck: pets, hunters. Hgarth: enchanters, mages, warlocks. Jeeda: trolls, shaman, herbalists.

## Topics
background, advice, _none.

## Authored, not canon
- Tharm raised riders at Thunder Bluff; flies the long way round the vale after Bloodfury harpies killed a rider; riders avoid the kodo graveyard; won't send carriers to war.
- Gereck keeps an abandoned mountain cougar whose hunter went into Desolace two seasons ago; pays her stall himself; once stabled a troll's giant crab; a raptor took part of his hand; was a wolf handler before the camps.
- Hgarth learned binding from the old Horde's warlocks and refused the rest; a student of his was burned by a badly bound cloak.
- Jeeda grew up in Razor Hill; wrote to the Malaka'jin witch doctor (Jin'Zil) three times and got a frog back; hears tired, angry voices off the Charred Vale at night; a small explosion cost Jayka her eyebrows.
