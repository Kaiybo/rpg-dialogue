## Their quests
- Maggran: Harpies Threaten (the Bloodfuries are the source of the growing number of harpies in Kalimdor and hem in the innocent; "tauren take pride as the protector of these lands"), Bloodfury Bloodline (Bloodfury Ripper, "all but a queen", western hills of the Charred Vale; on completion his anger leaves him and he hopes to focus on the good "as all tauren should"), Arachnophobia (wanted poster south of Windshear Crag; Besseleth and her brood in Sishir Canyon prey on the Webwinder Path at night; her two-foot fang caught him once and he fought her off; he rubs the wound), Calling in the Reserves (a letter to Grish Longrunner at the Great Lift: a reserve warrior will relieve him so he can visit his family; "since the Great War we have been prosperous here in Kalimdor").
- Tammra: Kaya's Alive (Kaya Flathoof, her niece, rescued from the Grimtotem at Grimtotem Post after the attack on Camp Aparaje; Makaba Flathoof is her brother-in-law), Cycle of Rebirth (Gaea seeds by the water at Mirkfallon Lake, north of Sun Rock; "industrial greed and continuous war"; she blesses them with shamanistic power by the Earth Mother's grace and chants), New Life (plant the seeds in mounds in the Charred Vale, to the west; harpies and angry fire elementals; completion: the Venture Co. will pack up, the fire spirits calm, the harpies pushed out by the land, then peace).
- Braelyn: Cenarius' Legacy (kill Sons and Daughters of Cenarius and Cenarion Botanists at Stonetalon Peak; the druids "stifle the flow of magic"; her master won't let her return to Jaedenar until her work is done; channeling here is "a bucket full of holes"; the kaldorei spurn magic), Ordanus (magic shows her Ordanus atop Raynewood; "they spirited him away from the peak"; the peak's ruins were "our ancient temple of Azshara"; she knew him long ago and he held himself above even the Highborne), The Den (the Gatekeeper's Key in a lantern at the Talon Den's entrance; she "extracted" the details from a druid).
- Jin'Zil: Letter to Jin'Zil (from Darsok: the last of the Bloodfeathers, Serena, is dead; Jin'Zil hadn't heard from him in years; Rokhan went with Rexxar to kill her older sister), Jin'Zil's Forest Magic (three caged night elves, "three little cages for three little elves"; Stonetalon sap, twilight whiskers, courser eyes, a fey dragon scale from Stonetalon Peak; on completion the brew kills the prisoners and their wisps fly out).
- Albagorm: Journey to Stonetalon Peak (from Faldreas in Astranaar: "the forest spirits tell me you are brave"; the Talondeep Path, Windshear Crag), Reclaiming the Charred Vale (the vale set afire by "the greedy mechanized army of industrialists"; the Bloodfuries refuse passage to begin regrowth), Reclaiming the Charred Vale (2) (to Falfindel Waywarder on the Thousand Needles/Feralas border, who can call forest spirits).
- Jayka: none: innkeeper only (gossip: places to rest battle-weary bodies in new lands; lay your axe by the fire and share stories).
- Faralia: none in Classic (innkeeper; gossip asks Elune to guide your dreams). A Forever-only turn-in, Stonetalon Supply Run, has no wiki page yet: not used.

## Faction note
Keeper Albagorm and Innkeeper Faralia are **Alliance** (Darnassus) NPCs in Classic, not neutral: a Horde player can't speak to them, so Albagorm's Horde notice (kept) rarely if ever fires. Every Sun Rock and Malaka'jin NPC is Horde: an Alliance character (the user's human priest) can't speak to them at all, so their identity-gated replies are for Horde characters (priest replies would reach a Forsaken or troll priest).

## Voices
- Maggran: hard old tauren warrior, few words, honest about losing his gentleness.
- Tammra: gentle gardener of burned ground.
- Braelyn: bitter, elegant mage with a grudge against druids.
- Jin'Zil: sly, cheerful witch doctor; "ingredients be talking".
- Jayka: warm orc innkeeper.
- Albagorm: serene keeper, "little one", neutral to both sides.
- Faralia: quiet innkeeper on the peak.
