# Stonetalon Mountains  (keys: maggran, tammra, braelyn, jinzil, jayka, albagorm, faralia)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sun_Rock_Retreat (the zone's Horde hub where orcs and tauren repair the damage done by the Venture Company and other threats; Maggran, Braelyn and Tammra were there in Classic)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Maggran_Earthbinder (tauren leader of Sun Rock; says age and battle took his gentleness; Cairne sent him there, where wind and sun ease his pain; escaped Besseleth once; Harpies Threaten, Bloodfury Bloodline, Calling in the Reserves, Arachnophobia)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tammra_Windfield (tauren at Sun Rock; Kaya's Alive, Cycle of Rebirth, New Life)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Braelyn_Firehand (blood elf Shadow Council mage working for the Horde town; wants magic restored by killing the Talon Den druids; hates Keeper Ordanus, whom she says she knew long ago and calls arrogant; Cenarius' Legacy, Ordanus, The Den)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Keeper_Ordanus (keeper in Raynewood Retreat, Ashenvale, atop the great tree; Braelyn sends Horde adventurers to kill him)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Witch_Doctor_Jin%27Zil (Darkspear witch doctor in a cave at Malaka'jin among prisoner cages; friend of Darsok and Rokhan; Letter to Jin'Zil, Jin'Zil's Forest Magic)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Innkeeper_Jayka (orc innkeeper at Sun Rock; warm, stories of battle)

- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Keeper_Albagorm (keeper of the grove at Stonetalon Peak, neutral; Journey to Stonetalon Peak, Reclaiming the Charred Vale)

## Their quests
- Maggran: Harpies Threaten, Bloodfury Bloodline, Arachnophobia, Calling in the Reserves.
- Tammra: Kaya's Alive, Cycle of Rebirth, New Life.
- Braelyn: Cenarius' Legacy, Ordanus.
- Jin'Zil: Letter to Jin'Zil, Jin'Zil's Forest Magic.
- Albagorm: Journey to Stonetalon Peak, Reclaiming the Charred Vale.
- Jayka, Faralia: none: innkeepers only.

## Never
- Anything Cataclysm or later: the Grimtotem attack on Sun Rock, Baine retaking Thunder Bluff, Albagorm's disappearance, Braelyn's death in Legion.
- RPG-only claims about Braelyn (her "mean spirit" toward night elves) beyond her quests.
- Braelyn's past with Ordanus beyond "knew him long ago".

## Voices
- Maggran: hard old tauren warrior, few words, honest about losing his gentleness.
- Tammra: gentle gardener of burned ground.
- Braelyn: bitter, elegant mage with a grudge against druids.
- Jin'Zil: sly, cheerful witch doctor; "ingredients be talking".
- Jayka: warm orc innkeeper.
- Albagorm: serene keeper, "little one", neutral to both sides.
- Faralia: quiet innkeeper on the peak.
