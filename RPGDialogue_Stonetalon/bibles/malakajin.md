## Voices
- **Xen'Zilla** (male Darkspear hunter): easy, sly, warm to guests; troll speech ("mon", "be"), whole sentences. Keeps an eye on the northern trees.
- **Ken'zigla** (male Darkspear brewer): dry, careful, measures twice; every healing is a loan paid back in sleep.
