# Stonetalon's roadside traders, Forever only  (keys: tuuli, xaphod)

NPCs added in World of Warcraft: Forever (patch 1.60.1). Only race, title, affiliation and wares are sourced; personalities are **authored, not canon**.

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tuu%27li (read: jungle troll, "Magnificent Mystic", Darkspear, mana user; sells Empty Vials and Blue/Red Ribboned Wrapping Paper; camp with Xaphod on Stonetalon's southern edge)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Xaphod_Bizznox (read: goblin, Booty Bay affiliation, friendly to both factions; sells Apprentice's Mining Pack, Blacksmith Hammer, Mining Pick and one limited Cat Carrier (White Kitten))
- CONTENT_GUIDE §2 goblin standard; bibles/stonetalon.md for the Venture Company in Stonetalon.

## Their quests
- None.

## Identity and voices
- Tuu'li: jolly Darkspear showman, a little real voodoo and a lot of patter; vague "fortunes" that always come true; sells paper because travellers have someone waiting. Notices real casters, full purses.
- Xaphod: fast, pushy Steamwheedle goblin; "pal"; sells where the competition is a harpy; despises the Venture Company as bad for honest goblins. Notices miners, full purses.

## Invented details (authored, not canon)
- They met broke in Booty Bay and became partners; trust each other "with life, never with money".
- The kitten came in a crate of copper ore off a Booty Bay boat; Tuu'li claims she has a mystic aura.
- Tuu'li waves his hands at harpies and they mostly leave.

## Never
- Kezan, Bilgewater, anything after Year 1. "Time is money" once (Xaphod).
