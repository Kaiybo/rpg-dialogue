## Their quests
- None. All four are vendors (Borand also repairs).

## Identity
- Grawnal · orc male · General Goods · Horde. Gruff, practical old quartermaster; counts crates. Speech: plain, a sergeant's grumble.
- Kulwia · tauren female · Trade Supplies · Horde. Patient, warm, craft-minded; asks what you make.
- Krond · orc male · Butcher · Horde. Loud, jovial, judges people by appetite.
- Borand · orc male · Bowyer · Horde. Dry, steady; an orc who chose the bow.
Relationships: each other; Maggran, Tammra, Jayka (Sun Rock); Hgarth (Kulwia sells his students' rods/formulas); Jeeda.
Default attitudes: Horde-only (an Alliance player can't speak to them). Respect tauren; wary of the Venture Company and the Grimtotem.
Never says: anything Cataclysm or later (the Grimtotem attack on Sun Rock, Krom'gar Fortress, Cliffwalker Post).

## Threads he could carry
- Venture Company damage to Stonetalon (springs, timber).

## Agenda
Keep Sun Rock supplied; Kulwia wants crafters to thrive; Borand wants mountain ash.

## Secrets
None of weight.

## Reliability
Honest traders; Krond exaggerates.

## What they notice   (2–3 things)
- Grawnal: race, purse, worn gear. Kulwia: professions, tauren. Krond: tauren appetites, pets, the hungry. Borand: hunters/warriors, trolls, worn gear.

## Topics
background, advice, _none.

## Authored, not canon
- Supplies come up from the Barrens by tauren kodo caravans past the Grimtotem, who robbed two caravans "since the thaw" (water taken, bread left); Maggran wants escorts, others want talks.
- Springs near the Charred Vale taste of ash; water sells best.
- Grawnal was a warband quartermaster sent by Orgrimmar; refuses credit to anyone.
- Kulwia traded between Thunder Bluff and the Crossroads; buys thread from Thunder Bluff twice a season; dyers used the vale's ash for grey dye; elders' doubts about enchanting.
- Krond: tried harpy once ("feathers and spite"); bad knees; says a bad death sours meat; quail from Mirkfallon Lake; a tauren called his mutton cruel.
- Borand: became a bowyer after three grunts died to harpies they couldn't reach; bows of mountain ash; Venture logging pushes the good stands higher; no smith at Sun Rock (he repairs).
