# Stonetalon, missed folk  (keys: darn_talongrip, gatekeeper_kordurus, saern_priderunner)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg zone roster scan; pages read with `tools/npc.py`: Darn_Talongrip (orc; "formerly located at Malaka'jin", moved to Webwinder Path in Cataclysm; relatives: a deceased mate; his listed quests are Cataclysm-era), Gatekeeper_Kordurus (keeper of the grove guarding the entrance to the Talon Den), Sunwalker_Saern (= Saern Priderunner; a paladin trainer only from Cataclysm; before that an untitled tauren on Hunter Rise, Thunder Bluff, who "taught Plainsrunning" in the beta and looked like a warrior).
- Existing pack: malakajin.lua (Xen'Zilla and Blood Feeders, Jeeda, Ken'zigla), stonetalon.lua (Maggran Earthbinder, Keeper Albagorm, Venture Company), peak_and_crag.lua; RPGDialogue_ThunderBluff hunter_rise.lua (Thunderhorns, Ragetotems).

## Their quests
- None of their own in Classic. Darn reacts to Arachnophobia (the Sun Rock wanted poster for Besseleth, turned in to Maggran Earthbinder).

## Identity / voice
- Darn Talongrip · orc man · grim, blunt widower; honour and patience learned the hard way.
- Gatekeeper Kordurus · keeper of the grove, male · stern, ancient, formal; watches hands, not faces.
- Saern Priderunner · tauren woman · proud, steady plains runner; Earth Mother and wind.

## Authored, not canon
- Darn came to scout for the Warchief with his mate; she died in Besseleth's web (Besseleth as her killer comes from his Cataclysm quest text, used here as background only); his failed lone attack.
- Kordurus' view of the Venture Company; "a son of the forest lord's line" (keepers are sons of Cenarius).
- Saern's family as runners between the tribes (from her name and the beta's Plainsrunning); she still runs at dawn.

## Placement note
- Saern stands in Thunder Bluff, not Stonetalon. She is in this pack's file only because the roster scan filed her here; her community is `thunder_bluff` and she should move to RPGDialogue_ThunderBluff when someone next touches that pack.
