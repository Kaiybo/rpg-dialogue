- Piznik · goblin male · Venture Co. miner · neutral. Jumpy, bitter, fast-talking; goblin standard (prices, "pal", docked wages).
Relationships: Keeper Albagorm, Faralia, Teloren, Gaxim, Kaela (Peak); Ziz Fizziks, Gerenzo (Piznik).

## Agenda

## Reliability
