# Stonetalon: more folk (keys: braug, dennika)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg: Braug Dimspirit (tauren shaman just southeast of the Talondeep Path at Windshear Crag; Classic: the four Test of Lore quests in Dorn Plainstalker's Test of Faith chain. Test of Lore (1) from Dorn; (2) fetch the Legacy of the Aspects from the bottom of Dor'danil Barrow Den in southern Ashenvale, "protected by the dead druids and their slayers", study it; (3) answer his question; (4) seek Parqual Fintallas in the Undercity, "a great historian for the humans before the Plague"). His completion text: the world grows colder as goblins ravage the land and night elves point fingers at the younger races; Forsaken and orcs are not the only ones to blame.
- Denni'ka <Butcher> (jungle troll, Malaka'jin; roster only). Xen'Zilla's spiders-steal-the-hunt thread from bibles/malakajin.md.

- **Braug Dimspirit.** Grave, dry, unhurried; blame for the world's pain spread evenly; questions over answers.
- **Denni'ka.** Cheerful, sly Darkspear; "mon", the odd "dat/dem", whole sentences; jokes about spider meat.
