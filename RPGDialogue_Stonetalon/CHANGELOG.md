# RPG Dialogue: Stonetalon Mountains (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.1.1 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: Sun Rock Retreat, Jin'Zil's cave and Stonetalon Peak now talk in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments, each in their own voice (Maggran's hard few words, Tammra's gentleness, Braelyn's bitter elegance, Jin'Zil's sly troll cheer, Jayka's warmth, Albagorm's calm). Same lines, same facts, same conditions.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits now bring personal lines (habits, worries, opinions of the neighbours), night-time lines, and a question or two about the person themselves, not only their quests.
- Faralia, Jayka, Albagorm, Jin'Zil, Tammra.

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- Sun Rock Retreat: Maggran Earthbinder, Tammra Windfield, Braelyn Firehand, Innkeeper Jayka; Witch Doctor Jin'Zil at Malaka'jin. Stonetalon Peak: Keeper Albagorm and Innkeeper Faralia.
