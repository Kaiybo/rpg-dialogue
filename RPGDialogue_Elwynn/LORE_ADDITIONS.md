**Assumptions (confirm in game):**
- The Stolen Tome is in Forever (it was Classic until 3.3.0); the lines only fire on its quest state, plus one ungated Drusilla branch about the book.
- Forever's McBride quests A Scribbled Letter, The Big Picture and Follow That Kobold!, Dughan's Shhh! We're Hunting Kobolds, Book Return and Rare Books, and Remy's A Man About a Murloc, An Enticing Offer and Baited for Success have no wiki pages yet: not referenced.
- Remen Marcot has no trainer title on the wiki (Crowe is the warlock trainer); his file keeps `title = "Warlock Trainer"` and a "Train me." reply. Check in game and drop both if he only gives Gakin's Summons.
- Classic Farley's wares (the wiki lists retail cheeses; the Classic list may differ).
- Quest title "Seek out SI: 7" (with a space): Keryn's lines now use it. `RPGDialogue_Stormwind/npcs/city.lua` still says `"Seek out SI:7"`, which can't match after punctuation is dropped. Fix there.

**Year 1 notes:** Farley's brother Frandis is pre-Year-1 backstory published later (*Before the Storm*, *Tides of War*). Farley says only what he'd know in Year 1: his brother's caravan went to Lordaeron in the plague year and never came back. Nothing about the Forsaken, Theramore, Anduin's summons or the Arcantina. Dughan's *Stormrage* dream and the *Dark Riders* comic aren't used. Medivh's coma at Northshire is pre-Year-1 history Neals tells from the library.
