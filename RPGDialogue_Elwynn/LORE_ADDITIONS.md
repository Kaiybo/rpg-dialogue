**Assumptions (confirm in game):**
- Forever uses the Classic versions of these quests: Princess at the Brackwell patch with her entourage, Goldtooth inside the Fargodeep Mine, Dead-tooth Jack as a warrior quest, the Theocritus chain, Deliver Thomas' Report, Shipment to Stormwind.
- `questDone "The Symbol of Life"` stands for "Henze was revived", as in the Stormwind pack (Gazin, Rall). The quest actually turned in at Henze is "The Tome of Divinity" (human 7), whose title several steps share.
- `TODO_QUEST("Dead-Tooth Jack")` (here and in World towns.lua) differs in case from the wiki's "Dead-tooth Jack"; titles are matched case-insensitively, so it's left alone.
- Erma, as a Year 1 stable master, keeps hunters' beasts; Michelle Belle teaches First Aid (her file title stays "Physician").
- The tent is the leatherworking camp fixture (The Great Outdoors says so; the Camping 101: Leatherworking page doesn't exist yet).
- Jason Mathers stands on Lee Brown's pier in Forever (Lee calls him "Jason down the end").
- Ma is used as Ma Stonefield's name only; whose mother she is stays unsaid.

**Year 1 notes / not used:** A Visit With Maybell (Cataclysm); the Maclure-Stonefield marriage and the Lunarfall barn (Draenor); Hero's Call: Westfall!, Marshal Patterson, Goss the Swift, James Clark, Bounty on Murlocs, Cloth and Leather Armor (Cataclysm); Sara's Stormwind Army commission (from a Cataclysm quest); Theocritus's Kirin Tor past and Karlain (the Dark Riders comic), the Gadgetzan Times interview, Cote Metcalf (Legion); Sammuel's Legion, Dragonflight and Midnight roles and Season of Discovery's Relics of the Light; the Powers of the Void "stolen tome"; the Lands of Conflict RPG's Westbrook and Azora material. Where Stormwind's army is "abroad" is never stated.

**Removed as invented life stories (ids kept, text replaced):** Sammuel's farm-boy-from-Eastvale origin (p_cart, a_never); Michelle Belle's father in the Second War (w_father); Helene's mother and grandmother (l_mother); Krighton's apprenticeship during the rebuilding (t_rebuild); Raelen's career before the camp (never shipped). **Corrected to canon:** Bernice no longer apologises to Billy (he did take it); Ma Stonefield's Brackwells are gone, not haughty; Helene names Eagan as her husband; Tharynn's "corner of an inn" is now his wagon; Haggard took Jack for Dughan; Pestle's Escape line no longer says the lovers "got away".
