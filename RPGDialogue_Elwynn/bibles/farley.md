## Identity
Innkeeper Fredrik Farley · Lion's Pride Inn, Goldshire · Alliance · Human · innkeeper (rumor hub)
Personality: warm, chatty, proud, gossipy, protective of his inn
Speech: generous, exclamatory, little jokes; calls for Barkeep Dobbins; family pride
Knows and cares about: his inn (run by Farleys since the first King Llane's day), his guests, the town's news
Relationships: Falkhaan Isenstrider (best friend); Barkeep Dobbins (staff); William Pestle and Remen Marcot (regulars); Remy (talks too much, even for Farley); Verina (wife) and Anna (daughter); Frandis (brother, a caravan guard believed dead of the plague in Lordaeron; see the 1.10.0 note for the Year 1 limits)
Default attitudes: every paying guest welcome; warlocks' imps unwelcome in the beds
Lore anchors: Lion's Pride Inn; Stalvan's stay
Never says: promises of discounts or free drinks (the addon can't give them)

Sources read: the wiki pages Innkeeper Farley, Lion's Pride Inn, Rest and Relaxation, The Legend of Stalvan (4) and (5), The Legend of Stalvan (book), Frandis Farley, Verina, Goldshire, Lost Necklace.
- Canon used: Fredrik Farley; wife Verina (in love since their early twenties), daughter Anna; brother Frandis, a caravan guard for Stormwind, better with a sword than Fredrik, died of the plague on his last caravan to Lordaeron; Fredrik has big hands and breaks up brawls; he and his wife lend a sympathetic ear; Stalvan stayed a few nights; years back, on a stormy night, a messenger fled screaming at midnight in his bedclothes, leaving letters in a chest upstairs, one of them from Stalvan; Goldshire fell in the First War and was rebuilt after the Second; the inn sells cheeses (Dalaran Sharp, Alterac Swiss, Stormwind Brie) and Ice Cold Milk; Tomas cooks at the inn; Auntie Bernice blames Billy Maclure for her necklace.
- **Year 1 discipline:** Frandis's death is pre-Year-1 backstory told in *Before the Storm* (published later). Farley believes his brother died of the plague; nothing about the Forsaken, Theramore or the later meeting with Anduin is ever said or hinted.
- Branches: rumors (and who knows more), the inn, his family and Frandis (a prayer for priests and paladins; a Lordaeron refugee's answer when he asks whether it was quick), food, trouble and the messenger, Falkhaan, and the cellar for warlocks.
- Authored, not canon: Verina keeping the books and lighting a candle each winter; "like my father and his before him"; having slept in the messenger's room once.
