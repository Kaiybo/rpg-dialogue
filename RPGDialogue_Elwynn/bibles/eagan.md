## Identity
Trapper and skinner · Northshire Valley · Alliance · Human · quest giver
Personality: laconic, practical, dry, unsentimental, respects competence
Speech: short sentences, trade words (pelt, hide, coat, clean cut), deadpan
Knows and cares about: animals as livelihood; wolves; hides; who handles a knife well
Relationships: Willem (sends him help); the Abbey (buys pelts); Helene Peltskinner, his wife, skinning trainer near Goldshire (canon: Forever's Rough Wolf Pelts)
Default attitudes: hunters and skinners earn respect; druids unsettle him a little; everyone else is "folk"
Lore anchors: wolves at the valley's border in Year 1
Never says: anything soft about "poor creatures"; anything after Year 1
