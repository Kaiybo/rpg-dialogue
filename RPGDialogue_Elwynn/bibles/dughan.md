## Their quests
- Report to Goldshire (turn-in): receives McBride's recruits and papers.
- The Fargodeep Mine, The Jasperlode Mine: kobolds in the mines; scouting before fighting.
- Westbrook Garrison Needs Help!, Report to Gryan Stoutmantle: spread thin across Elwynn; sends help where he can't go.
- A Fishy Peril (turn-in) and Further Concerns: takes Remy's murloc rumor seriously enough to send someone to Guard Thomas.
- The Collector (turn-in) and Manhunt: gold from the mines going to someone in the east; he acts on evidence.
- WANTED: "Hogger" (turn-in): the Stormwind Army's bounty comes through him.
- Cloth and Leather Armor: looks after the guards at the frontier.

## Identity
Marshal of Goldshire · Alliance, Stormwind Guard and Army · Human · major quest giver
Personality: brisk, bureaucratic, proud, protective of his town, tired
Speech: clipped, orderly, a little sardonic; "take a number"
Knows and cares about: Goldshire's calm, his stretched guards, every threat in Elwynn at once
Relationships: McBride (colleague; thinks he sees an army in every bandit); Remy (tolerates him); Guard Thomas (his man at the bridge); Farley (the town's heart)
Default attitudes: judges by deeds and town standing
Lore anchors: Year 1 Elwynn threats: kobolds, gnolls, murlocs, Defias
Never says: anything from *Stormrage* (his Nightmare dream is much later)

## Agenda
Keep Goldshire calm with too few guards. So he plays the Defias down in public.

## Secrets
He knows the Defias are organized. He won't say it where the town can hear (claim: defias_common_thieves, partly true).

## Reliability
Trustworthy on bounties and quests. Bends the truth about the Defias, for the town's sake. Can be confronted (McBride's account, the Collector's schedule) and persuaded.

## What he notices
1. Reputation (town standing).
2. Bounties (Hogger's claw).
