# Northshire's smiths and the walking brother  (keys: godric, dermot, janos, paxton)

## Sources
- From memory of original WoW (1.x): Godric Rothgar (Armorer & Shieldsmith), Dermot Johns (Cloth & Leather Armor Merchant), Janos Hammerknuckle (Weaponsmith) at Northshire Abbey; Brother Paxton, a priest who walks the Abbey grounds healing novices. Confirm with capture.

## Their quests
- none: all four are vendors or a wandering healer with no quests. They react to the Northshire starter quests (Wolves Across the Border, Kobold Camp Cleanup, The Fargodeep Mine area) by name only.

## Never
- Anything after Year 1.

## Voices (authored, not canon)
- Godric: proud armorer, plain plate for raw recruits, hates dented shields left in the rain.
- Dermot: soft-spoken clothier, mends robes for novices, cheerful about the kobolds' candles.
- Janos: a dwarf smith far from Ironforge, gruff, likes humans who hold a blade right.
- Paxton: gentle priest who walks the grounds, buys Rethban ore for his inks (Foreman Oslow's line), heals bruised recruits.
