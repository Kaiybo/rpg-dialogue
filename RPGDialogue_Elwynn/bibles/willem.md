# Deputy Willem  (key: willem)

## Sources

## Their quests
- A Threat Within (gives; turn-in at McBride): the first face every recruit meets. He sends them up to the Marshal.
- Eagan Peltskinner, Milly Osworth (breadcrumbs): knows everyone in the valley and points recruits where they're needed.
- Brotherhood of Thieves: the Defias in the vineyards are *his* problem. Keeps a count of the bandanas.
- Bounty on Garrick Padfoot: a named thief camped nearby; personal irritation that he's so bold.

## Identity
Deputy to Marshal McBride · outside Northshire Abbey · Alliance, Stormwind Army · Human · quest giver
Personality: earnest, quick, chatty, proud of his post, fond of the valley
Speech: friendly, brisk, practical jokes at the Defias' expense; tells you where to go next
Knows and cares about: who's new, who's where, the thieves, Milly's vineyard
Relationships: McBride (his commander; respects him, worries he takes too much on); Milly (protective); Eagan (neighbor)
Default attitudes: newcomers get a welcome and a warning; rogues get a joke with an edge; non-humans get curiosity
Lore anchors: Year 1; Defias in the valley; Garrick Padfoot
Never says: anything cynical about the Marshal; anything after Year 1

## Threads he could carry
- Defias grievance: hears both sides, decides he's "a deputy, not a judge".

## Agenda
Keep the valley's roads safe; get the Marshal good recruits.

## Secrets
None worth the name. He tallies bandanas because McBride didn't believe his first count.

## Reliability
Trustworthy on the valley's people and places. Unsure on the Defias' motives and says so.

## What he notices
1. Newcomers (level 1, first meeting).
2. Thieves (rogues, stealth).
3. Strangers far from home.

## Topics
defias, kobolds

## Depth pass (Elwynn 1.10.0)
Sources read: the wiki pages Willem, A Threat Within, Eagan Peltskinner (quest), Milly Osworth (quest), Brotherhood of Thieves, Bounty on Garrick Padfoot, Garrick Padfoot.
- Canon used: he'd normally be out on the beat as a Stormwind guard, deputized because so many guards fight in other lands (gossip); more wolves in the valley lately; Eagan around the side of the Abbey; Milly is his friend, with her wagon by the stable, and says his duties keep him stuck at the Abbey; the Defias were "seen across the river to the east" and he doesn't know what they're up to; Garrick Padfoot, a cutthroat with a gang of thugs, shack east of the vineyards across the bridge, plaguing farmers and merchants for weeks.
- Branches: the thieves (Garrick; why anyone joins), the valley (Milly; Eagan, whose pelts go to his wife Helene's orders), his beat (a city guard, deputized), and replies for Westfall farmhands and players from far away (dwarf, gnome, night elf, Skyborne). Abbey-novice and veteran lines.
- Authored, not canon: "a shirt and a stick"; his fear of knowing faces under the bandanas.
