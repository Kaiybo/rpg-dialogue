## Voices (authored, not canon)
- Pa: proud, stubborn, gentle about his vines and red to the ears about Stonefields; never sees what's under his nose. Former Westfall farmhands get a kin notice. Stances: family loyalty against "a grudge nobody remembers" (order); Maybell choosing for herself (order).
- Joshua: easygoing seller who smooths over Pa; has covered for Maybell; thinks Tommy Joe decent but won't say it at home (order stance). Dwarves get the ale he hides from Pa.

## What they notice
- Pa: someone sneaking in his rows; a farmer's hands. Joshua: drunkenness.
