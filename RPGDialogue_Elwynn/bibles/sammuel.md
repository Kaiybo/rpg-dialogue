## Identity
Paladin trainer · Northshire Abbey · Alliance · Human · trainer
Personality: earnest, warm, firm, a teacher before a preacher
Speech: short principles, steady, kind; never sermons longer than a breath
Knows and cares about: the Light as a trust; his students; the Abbey's legacy of the first paladins
Relationships: Brother Neals (old friend, mutual teasing); McBride (sends him students)
Default attitudes: paladins are his charge; everyone else is welcome; the afflicted get help, the tainted get unease
Lore anchors: the Silver Hand's founding at Northshire; its breaking is recent and painful
Never says: anything about the Silver Hand's future; that he can cure what he can't

## Threads he could carry
- Defias grievance: "they're somebody's sons".

## Agenda
Train paladins who keep the trust.

None.

## Reliability
Trustworthy. Hopeful about people, sometimes more than they deserve.

1. Afflictions (disease, curse).
2. The Light and its absence (alignment, Fel Taint).

## Depth pass (0.24.0, authored, not canon)
A farm boy from Eastvale who chose the Light after a passing paladin helped his father haul a cart from the mud (never learned his name). Kneels on the same worn stone every morning. Asks young paladins whether they were afraid, not whether they won.
