## Notes per person (authored, not canon)
- Tomas: warm, proud of his kitchen; wolf, then boar, then eggs; Westfall folk at the back door (priests and the kind get a light stance there). Former abbey novices get a reply.
- Kira: cheerful, a seller's patter; keeps off the south-west road (Hogger); guards come running when she shouts. Former city guards get a reply.
- Bo: eager; plays kobolds and guards with Mark and Joshua; watches Wilhelm's paladins through the fence; wants to be a soldier, and his ma says no (order stance).
- Matt: grumbling, hopeful fisher; Lee Brown says his knot's wrong; afraid of Stone Cairn Lake after the lost guards. Fishers get a reply.

## What they notice
- Tomas: hunger, cooks. Kira: hunger, the resurrection-sick. Bo: druid shapes, battered armor. Matt: fishers.
