# Eastvale stables  (keys: katie_hunter, randal_hunter)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Katie_Hunter (Horse Breeder, Eastvale Logging Camp stables; sells horse mounts; only humans can buy by default, others must be exalted with Stormwind; her gossip: years of quality breeding and care; she won't sell to those who haven't earned Stormwind's trust)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Randal_Hunter (Riding Trainer, beside Katie; teaches novices to ride horses; the two are relatives, relation not stated)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Riding (Classic: riding was bought at level 40; each race had its own riding skill; another race's needed exalted reputation)

## Their quests
- none: in Classic neither gives a quest (Katie's horse exchanges are from later patches and removed).

## Identity
- Katie: brisk, proud of her horses, warm to anyone who's kind to them. Notices: mounts, race (sells only to humans unless trusted).
- Randal: patient, dry, safety first. Notices: class (paladins' and warlocks' own mounts at 40), mounts.

## Lore anchors
- Year 1 / Classic: riding at level 40, race-specific. Never prices (they changed over time). Never say how Katie and Randal are related.
- Paladins' warhorse and warlocks' felsteed come at level 40 (class spells); only hinted at.
