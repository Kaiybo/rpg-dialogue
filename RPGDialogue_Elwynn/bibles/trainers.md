# Elwynn's class trainers  (keys: anetta, khelden, jorik, drusilla, lyria, josetta, zaldimar, keryn, remen)

One bible for nine small parts. Each trainer warms to their own class and is short with everyone else, as their in-game greetings are. Their class letters and level-10 quests set the "during" lines. None of them gives rewards or discounts.

## Their quests
- Northshire class letters (Hallowed, Glyphic, Encrypted, Tainted): the first lesson. Healing the Wounded (Anetta).
- Goldshire level-10 quests: A Warrior's Training (Lyria), Garments of the Light and Desperate Prayer (Josetta), Speak with Jennea and Report to Jennea (Zaldimar), Seek out SI:7 (Keryn), Gakin's Summons (Remen). Each sends the student onward; lines never say exactly where unless the source does.

## Priestess Anetta (key: anetta)
- Source: https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Priestess_Anetta (Northshire Abbey, Library Wing ground floor; Brotherhood of Northshire, Church of the Holy Light; heals wounded trainees; teaches novice priests; Healing the Wounded, Hallowed Letter, In Favor of the Light; reminds non-priests not to turn from the Light)
- Voice: kind, brisk, overworked infirmary keeper. Notices: wounds; Shadowform (not in the infirmary).

## Khelden Bremen (key: khelden)
- Source: https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Khelden_Bremen (abbey second floor; teaches the fundamentals; Glyphic Letter; to mages, destiny and knowledge and power whatever the gods think; to others, magic is dangerous untrained)
- Voice: proud, dry, fussy about fundamentals. Sends level-10 mages to Wefhellt. Notices: arcane (singed robes).

## Jorik Kerridan (key: jorik)
- Source: https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Jorik_Kerridan (behind Northshire Abbey; Encrypted Letter; sends non-rogues to their own trainers; warned a recruit that the Defias want spies in Stormwind)
- Voice: wry, watchful, practical. Notices: stealth (badly done).

## Drusilla La Salle (key: drusilla)
- Source: https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Drusilla_La_Salle (abbey graveyard, beside Dane Winslow; Tainted Letter; stern, cautionary: the arcane corrupts the weak, patience and prudence with ambition; curt with non-warlocks)
- Voice: cold, exact, darkly amused. Sends level-10 warlocks to Marcot. Notices: imps, fel taint. Authored: the Brothers "pray about" her.

## Lyria Du Lac (key: lyria)
- Source: https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lyria_Du_Lac (Goldshire, behind the blacksmith; A Warrior's Training at 10; proud of Stormwind's strength; stern and dismissive with non-warriors)
- Voice: drill sergeant. Neighbour of Brother Wilhelm (same spot, per both pages). Notices: kit.

## Priestess Josetta (key: josetta)
- Source: https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Priestess_Josetta (Lion's Pride Inn, upstairs central room with Michelle Belle; wishes she'd had the courage to travel when young; In Favor of the Light, Garments of the Light, Desperate Prayer, Returning Home)

## Zaldimar Wefhellt (key: zaldimar)

## Depth pass (Elwynn 1.10.0): Khelden, Jorik, Drusilla, Lyria, Zaldimar, Keryn, Remen
Sources read: their wiki pages, plus Glyphic, Encrypted and Tainted Letter (the letters' text), The Stolen Tome, Powers of the Void, Dane Winslow, A Warrior's Training, Harry Burlguard, Speak with Jennea, Report to Jennea, Seek out SI: 7, Gakin's Summons (Voidwalker), Mathias Shaw.
- Khelden: the Glyphic Letter's four rules (powerful, corrupting, addicting, draws the Nether); the library's protected wing and the stolen books; sends level-10 mages to Wefhellt, then Jennea. Priest/paladin reply on whether the Light is magic.
- Jorik: the Encrypted Letter (the trade has never been better; war is honest, a souring peace is when spies are wanted); who's hiring (SI:7, the Defias wanting spies in Stormwind; "you are your own person", from his Encrypted Letter quest); behind the stables; a purse lesson for non-rogues.
- Drusilla: The Stolen Tome (Powers of the Void, kept in the protected wing where a warlock loitering would be noticed; a novice ran off to the Defias with it; it's in their tent in the vineyard; she couldn't steal it herself); the Tainted Letter (the Light's followers frighten the curious; not everything from outside wants to rule you; with enough power it follows); "the arcane only corrupts the weak"; Dane Winslow beside her unmakes minions for warlocks. Priest/paladin branch.
- Lyria: A Warrior's Training (Harry Burlguard, an old veteran and a good teacher, at the Pig and Whistle in Old Town; the Bartleby chain that follows is tavern fighting); Stormwind as the pillar of humanity (her gossip), with Lordaeron fallen; a Westfall farmhand reply; a Lordaeron refugee line.
- Zaldimar: Speak with Jennea (a task for the safety of Stormwind and Elwynn), Report to Jennea (the secret war; a first window into the invisible world); Morganth as the worst a mage can become (Theocritus at Azora watches him); a priest's answer about the Light.
- Keryn (female, per the Lion's Pride Inn page): Seek out SI: 7 (she calls the student "fish"; Shaw may mistake them for the cheese delivery). **Fix:** her quest lines used `TODO_QUEST("Seek out SI:7")`, but the quest is titled "Seek out SI: 7" (with a space), and titles are matched after dropping punctuation, so the old spelling could never match. Stormwind's `city.lua` still has the old spelling (not in this pack's scope).
- Remen: Gakin's Summons (Remen spent the day looking for the student; the Slaughtered Lamb's basement, keep it quiet; Gakin thinks little of Remen); his coven affiliation (Slaughtered Lamb); Crowe and Darkheart in the same cellar. The wiki gives Remen no trainer title (Crowe is the warlock trainer); his file keeps the old title, so his lines say he teaches when Crowe's busy.
- Authored, not canon (all seven): Khelden preferring the Defias have the Void book over La Salle; Jorik's horses; Drusilla having read three chapters; the yard's noon quarrel; Zaldimar reading through the singing; Keryn's dreadful romance; Remen's "law of nature".
