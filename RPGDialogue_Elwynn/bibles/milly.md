## Their quests
- Milly's Harvest: the Defias have taken her harvest crates in the vineyard. Worried, angry, practical; it's her living.
- Grape Manifest: sends the manifest to Brother Neals. She trades with the Abbey and knows Neals counts every barrel.

## Identity
Vintner · Northshire Vineyards · Alliance · Human · quest giver
Personality: worried, stubborn, warm to helpers, sharp-tongued about thieves
Speech: plain, quick, homely images (sticky hands, bread, crates); says what she thinks
Knows and cares about: her vineyard (her mother's before her), the harvest, the Defias in her rows
Relationships: Brother Neals (customer, fussy); Willem (protects the valley); the Defias (enemies)
Default attitudes: farmhands are kin; everyone else is judged by whether they help
Lore anchors: Defias occupying Northshire's vineyards in Year 1
Never says: anything sympathetic about the Defias who took her harvest

## Threads he could carry
- Defias grievance: the farmer's side. They took her living; she doesn't care why.

## Agenda
Get the harvest back and keep the vineyard.

## Secrets
None.

## Reliability
Trustworthy on what the Defias did to her. Biased about why: she won't hear their side.

## What she notices
1. Drink (wine on your breath).
2. Farmhands (Westfall farmhand origin).
