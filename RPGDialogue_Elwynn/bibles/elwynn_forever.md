- Stolen Enchanting Supplies (gives/turn-in): sent by Theocritus for enchanting supplies from "the trading post"; ambushed by gnolls; dropped the package; 5 pieces in gnoll camps around Stone Cairn Lake; fears weeks of cleaning up after polymorph experiments. Completion: relief.

# Josephine Carson  (key: josephine_carson)
## Their quests
- Taming the Beast (human 1-3) and Training the Beast (human): titles only. Written as "three beasts, each harder than the last", a rod, then keeping one. All three share one title: `TODO_QUEST("Taming the Beast")` may only match one of them (confirm with capture).
## Identity
Goldshire · human woman · Hunter Trainer. Sharp, proud, practical. Wiki line to non-hunters kept: "you don't look like you could fully draw a longbow, nor load a musket. I teach real hunters."
## Invented
Grew up at the forest's edge shooting rabbits; two scars from taming; "patience, eyes, aim".

# Nordun Steadysight  (key: nordun)
Goldshire · dwarf man (Ironforge) · Traveling Ranged Weapon Salesman (bows, guns, arrows, shot, quiver, shot pouch). Jovial salesman with a dwarf burr.
## Invented
Raised in a gunsmith's shop; brothers in Ironforge; turned away buyers with red scarves (Defias).

# Kelsey Fargo  (key: kelsey_fargo)
## Their quests
- Mining Consultant (turn-in), The Big Picture (gives): titles only. Lines stay vague ("a bigger job ... about the whole ridge"); The Big Picture is written as surveying the mine. **Uncertain.**
## Identity
Echo Ridge Mine entrance · human woman · Junior Mining Trainer. Wiki gossip used: "Blast these kobolds. I can't get any work done..."
## Invented
Miner's daughter from the hills above Goldshire; kobold hit her knee with a pick; copper and tin in the ridge; also reacts to the Classic quest Investigate Echo Ridge.

# Nancy Songflower  (key: nancy_songflower)
Northshire Valley · human woman · Junior Herbalism Trainer. Cheerful, kind.
## Invented
Born in Goldshire, trained under a Stormwind herbalist; peacebloom, silverleaf, earthroot; "leave the root in".

# Riley Peltskinner  (key: riley_peltskinner)
Northshire Valley · human woman · Junior Skinning Trainer. Relatives on the wiki: Eagan and Hannah Peltskinner, **relation unstated**, so she never names it ("we're Peltskinners, the both of us").
## Invented
Respect for the beast; Eagan "does the worrying"; reacts to Wolves Across the Border.

# Tordrin Sternblade  (key: tordrin)
## Their quests
- A Scribbled Letter (turn-in; taken to be the hunter's class letter, sender not named), Follow That Kobold! (turn-in), Shhh! We're Hunting Kobolds (gives): titles only; written as a stealth lesson (follow a kobold home without being seen). **Uncertain.**
## Identity
Northshire Valley · dwarf man (Ironforge) · Hunter Trainer. Wiki gossip used: "Wot kin I do fer ye?" Heavy burr ("ye", "doesnae", dropped g's).
## Invented
Ironforge sent hunters at Stormwind's/the Abbey's request because humans were new to hunting with beasts; points hunters 10+ to Josephine Carson in Goldshire.

# Ormin Pelford  (key: ormin)
## Their quests
- Downstream (gives/turn-in): hauling a supply shipment from the trading post, wagon tipped, supplies in the river; Waterlogged Axe, Saw, Toolbox; murlocs and wolves; "You look a bit damp, <name>. Sorry about that."
## Identity
Near Eastvale Logging Camp · human man. Anxious, apologetic, honest.
## Invented
Hauls for the logging camp; horse shied at a murloc at a high ford; family in Goldshire; fears being called "Ormin the Swimmer"; red bandanas watching the road.
