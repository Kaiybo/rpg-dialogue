## Notes per person (all authored, from their trade)
- Dobbins: Farley's barkeep, cheerful fetcher. Echoes Farley's "never watered a drink".
- Brog Hamfist: everything but swords. Race not stated: never mentioned.
- Toddrick: proud butcher; would have made sausages of Princess.
- Tharynn Bouden: crafters are the loyal customers.
- Andrew Krighton: shields; gets on with Argus.
- Corina and Kurran Steele: same surname; how they're related isn't known, so Kurran says "family business, in a way".
- Erma: animals before people.
- Michelle Belle: bandages save lives; works beside Josetta.
- Maximillian Crowe, Cylina Darkheart: the cellar's lessons and its demon trainer (and her imp).
- Lee Brown, Helene Peltskinner, Adele Fielder: the Crystal Lake house. Helene and Eagan Peltskinner share a name; a relation isn't stated, and she teases about it rather than claiming one.
