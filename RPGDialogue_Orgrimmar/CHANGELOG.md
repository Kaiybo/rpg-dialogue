# RPG Dialogue: Orgrimmar (pack changelog)

The pack's own version is `version` in `pack.lua`; bump both together.

## 1.12.0 (2026-11-17)
- Warriors and hunters are noticed: about 35 new warrior and hunter conversations; people greet a warrior or hunter as one, with a question to ask and something to argue about, and hunters with a beast at their side hear it named. No new files. Needs RPG Dialogue 0.50.0.

## 1.11.0 (2026-11-16)
- Horde rogues, mages and warlocks get their own conversation with 19 people in Orgrimmar, Thrall and Vol'jin included.

## 1.10.0 (2026-11-16)
- Horde shaman get their own conversation with 16 people across Orgrimmar, from Gryshka's inn to the trainers.

## 1.9.0 (2026-11-16)
- Druids are noticed: nine people, Thrall and Vol'jin among them (tauren druids) greet a druid as one and have something to ask and argue about.

## 1.8.0 (2026-11-16)
- New people with no quests, who had nothing to say until now: Karus, Auctioneer Thathung, Doras, Urtrun Clanbringer, Overlord Runthak, Ogunaro Wolfrunner, Lumak, Makaru, Saru Steelfury, Nogg, Xon'cha.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-16)
- New people: Grezz Ragefist, Ormak Grimshot, Kardris Dreamseeker and Zevrost.
- New files: exit the game fully and restart.

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-15)
- Replies for the classes and races these people would really react to (warriors, hunters, rogues, mages, warlocks, druids, shamans, paladins; every race of the faction, and the Skyborne), each leading somewhere.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-15)
- Every person here writes a first impression in your diary after you first talk (the journal's People page shows it too).

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-15)
- Persuade, Intimidate and Deceive checks on the major people here, each with its own success and failure; lines and replies that depend on how warmly they regard you (disposition); a lead-in for "What do you know?".

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-15)
- Alignment now comes only from statements, never questions, and every reply that shifts alignment has an opposing one beside it. Taking one stance with someone closes the others for good.

## 1.2.1 (unreleased)
- Voice pass: Grommash Hold, the Cleft of Shadow and the Broken Tusk now talk in whole sentences instead of clipped fragments, each in their own voice (Thrall calm and honourable, Vol'jin's dropped verbs, Neeru's silk, Gryshka flustered about Zargh, Gamon's gentle resignation). Same lines, same facts, same conditions.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)
- Depth pass: repeat visits bring personal lines (habits, worries, running jokes), and some NPCs get night-time lines. Gryshka after Meats to Orgrimmar, Gamon.

## 1.1.1 (unreleased)
- Thrall knows Gar'Thok at Razor Hill.

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-14)
- Vol'jin, chief of the Darkspear, at the Warchief's side. He knows if you've met Nimboya in Stranglethorn or Master Gadrin in Sen'jin.
- Thrall answers about the Burning Blade and the Echo Isles.

## 1.0.0 (first release, unreleased)
- Thrall, Warchief of the Horde; Neeru Fireblade in the Cleft of Shadow; Innkeeper Gryshka at the Broken Tusk (with news of a butcher at the Crossroads); and Gamon, who has made his peace with being attacked. Orgrimmar's grunts are in the World pack.
