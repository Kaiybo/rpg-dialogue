# Orgrimmar: quiet errands (keys: craven_drok, keldran, therzok, jesrimon)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg (Classic-only sections): Craven Drok (wandering, Cleft of Shadow; starts Alliance Relations), Keldran (north of the Valley of Spirits; Alliance Relations chain; sends Takata Steelblade and Maurin Bonesplitter to Ghost Walker Post in Desolace to clear the Burning Blade, without clear official permission from Thrall, believing the Warchief weary of the war), Therzok (Cleft of Shadow; Shattered Hand recruiter; "Therzok" from Kaplak, "The Shattered Hand": kill the troll Tazan who trades with pirates near Ratchet, locked satchel), Jes'rimon (troll on the top floor of Yelmak's shop in the Drag; Shattered Hand fence; Bone-Bladed Weapons for white ravasaur claws from Un'Goro; Delivery to Jes'rimon from Jediga, Tablet of Jin'yael; gossip about Un'Goro's floating stones and raptors).
- Kept consistent with Takata and Maurin (`RPGDialogue_Desolace`), Kaplak (`RPGDialogue_Durotar/npcs/unsung.lua`), Jediga (`RPGDialogue_Azshara/npcs/valormok.lua`), Shenthul's Cleft.

## Voices
**Craven Drok (orc).** Low, confiding, always pacing; talks as if sharing something he's been told to share. Gently pro-Thrall, pro-peace.
**Keldran (orc).** Measured, scholarly, every word chosen; "Putanni" as a greeting (from his quest text). Works around the Warchief, sure he serves him.
**Therzok (orc).** Hungry for the old Horde's glory without the demons; scornful of Thrall's caution but careful to say he obeys. Calls people "recruit".
**Jes'rimon (troll).** Troll speech ("be", "me", "mon"), sly and friendly, speaks of himself by name; frank about fencing, flirtatious about Jediga.

## Authored, not canon
- Craven Drok: eleven years in an internment camp south of Durnholde, paces since; the name "Craven" after a grandfather who "ran" from a battle with the draenei to warn a village.
- Keldran: learned human letters as a shaman's scribe in the camps; studies wards and seals.
- Therzok: carried a Shattered Hand blade at the Dark Portal; the Hand wants eyes in Ratchet and the Barrens.
- Jes'rimon: came from the Echo Isles with Vol'jin's people; Jediga sends him relics from night-elf ruins.

## Never
Nothing after Year 1: no Garrosh, no Shattered Hand under later leaders, no Cataclysm Cleft.
