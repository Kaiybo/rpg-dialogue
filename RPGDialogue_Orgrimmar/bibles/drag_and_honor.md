# Orgrimmar: the Mighty Pen and the Omosh forge (keys: zilzibin, orokk)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg: Zilzibin Drumlore (troll scholar in the Mighty Pen, upper Drag, with Nerog; "his friends call him Zil"; expert on alien creatures; Rise of the Silithid from Fizzledowser, Zukk'ash Report from Hadoken Swiftstrider, Calm Before the Storm from Pestlezugg; complains the Horde ignores any threat but the Alliance and that Thrall is beset by conflicting advice).
- Orokk Omosh (Valley of Honor; last of a line of orcish smiths, no offspring; Barbaric Battlements, On Iron Pauldrons, Trampled Under Foot, Horns of Frenzy, Joys of Omosh; "Orokk is not crying. Soot is in eye."; the Omosh Dance of Joy).
- Consistent with Hadoken (Feralas) and Pestlezugg, Fizzledowser (Tanaris), Saru Steelfury (male, Orgrimmar trainers).

## Voices
**Zilzibin Drumlore (troll).** Quick, worried scholar; light troll touches ("somethin'", dropped g) without heavy dialect. Would work with Alliance scholars against the silithid.
**Orokk Omosh (orc).** Third person ("Orokk"), whole simple sentences, big-hearted, proud of twenty generations.

## Authored, not canon
- Zilzibin: left the Echo Isles to study; keeps his desk in Nerog's shop for buying his ink.
- Orokk: the first Omosh shod the Frostwolf chieftains' wolves in Draenor; his father made axes for the Second War; Orokk mended the guards' locks in an internment camp and kept spare keys for the breakout; claws on bracers as an Omosh custom.
