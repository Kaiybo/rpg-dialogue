# Orgrimmar  (keys: thrall, voljin, neeru, gryshka, gamon)

## Sources
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Thrall (Warchief, rules from Orgrimmar; honorable, cunning, compassionate; named Warchief by the dying Orgrim Doomhammer, who gave him the hammer and black plate; keeps the peace with Jaina and Theramore against incidents; dealt with the Burning Blade within the Horde; Grom's sacrifice)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Vol%27jin (Darkspear chieftain, Thrall's trusted adviser in Orgrimmar in Classic; wise, witty, cunning; rough dialect with dropped verbs; slow to take offense, understands elves find trolls hard to trust; pledged to the Horde after Thrall saved the Darkspear from the murlocs; his father Sen'jin died fighting murlocs; Zalazane, his childhood friend, enslaved Darkspear with dark magic and drove them from the Echo Isles; Gadrin left to lead the effort to retake them; Yenniku his youngest son)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Neeru_Fireblade (orc warlock in the Cleft of Shadow; secretly leads the Burning Blade inside Orgrimmar while posing as Thrall's loyal servant; arrogant, sees Thrall as a fool, treats the Searing Blade as expendable; Thrall knows and uses adventurers against him; Hidden Enemies, Burning Shadows, Ak'Zeloth, Slaying the Beast)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gryshka (orc innkeeper of the Broken Tusk in the Valley of Strength; fond of meat; welcomes warriors' stories; Zargh sends her choice cuts)
- https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gamon (tauren warrior drunk in the Broken Tusk, Tazan's assistant; attackable, and players attack him constantly)

## Voices
- Thrall: calm, honourable, warm to orcs and allies; firm about betrayal.
- Vol'jin: wry, dropped verbs, "mon" sparingly; loyal to Thrall, wary of the Blade, carries Yenniku quietly.
- Neeru: silky, ironic, "useful friends".
- Gryshka: hearty innkeeper, flustered about Zargh.
- Gamon: resigned, gentle, keeps getting knocked down.
