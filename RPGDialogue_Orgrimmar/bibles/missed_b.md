## Identity / voice
- Morgum · orc man · gruff fitter; leather is for not being hit.
- Ollanus · orc man · soft-spoken for an orc; sells to shamans and trolls; laughed at by the steel sellers.
- Urtharo · orc man · loud gate-side weapon seller; likes selling a young orc a first real blade.
- Tamar · orc man · steady shelf-keeper; salt, thread and dye.
- Whuut · young orc man · hurried, eager apprentice; "Master Yelmak"; wants to brew for war bands.
- Zeal'aya · Darkspear woman · easy, sly; plants have spirits; misses the Echo Isles.
- Sarlek · orc man · loud and stubborn; drags strangers into his argument with Tamaro.
- Tamaro · orc man · dry and calm; lets Sarlek shout.
- Tosamina · orc woman · warm and firm; former fighter, now minds the orphans with the Matron.
- Okothos Ironrager · orc man · stern master armorsmith; armor brings the warrior home.
- Ox · tauren man · slow, gentle; speaks of ore as a gift of the land; the craft above the banners.
- Shayis Steelfury · orc woman · brisk, talks between hammer blows; rivals Kelgruk the weaponsmith.
- Snarl · young orc man · surly bellows-hand with the better eye.
- Ug'thok · young orc man · big, boastful, axe-mad; rival and friend to Snarl.
- Tumi · orc woman · confident seller; the Steelfury who can talk to customers.
- Shoma · orc man · grave; weapons are what stand between the orcs and chains.
- Zendo'jian · Darkspear man · laughing; throwing weapons; teases Shoma.
- Shankys · orc woman · dry, unhurried.
- Tharl Stonebleeder · orc man · gruff, secretly soft over his animals.

## Authored, not canon
- Morgum's hides come from Barrens kodo and thunder lizard through Karolek's tanners.
- Ollanus is teased by Morgum and Soran; the Darkspear are his best customers.
- Urtharo's story of the shop's name (a human knight's axe broken on an orc shield); his bad knees.
- Tamar: salt and thread sell most; black dye for scouts.
- Whuut: singed eyebrow from fire oil; Yelmak's goblin-in-Ratchet story; too young to fight in the crossing; tear-or-crush swiftthistle argument with Kor'geld.
- Zeal'aya and Jandi carry pots out into the sun each morning.
- Sarlek and Tamaro: old soldiers who shared an internment camp and the voyage, later hauled stone for the walls; their argument is Valley of Honor against the Drag. Their room is above the scholars' shop (the pack's Mighty Pen).
- Tosamina was a fighter; orphans go on to the Valley of Trials or to apprenticeships; blankets from Thunder Bluff.
- Okothos tests plate by having Snarl and Ug'thok hit him; he sends finished armorsmiths on to Ox.
- Ox saw his first forging when the orcs came to Mulgore; he trades letters with McGavan.
- Shayis is Saru's kin (degree left unsaid); the raider's shoulder plates; "rivets".
- Snarl's hundred-nails test (reset at sixty-one); Ug'thok's eleven hatchets and his great-axe drawing in the ash.
- Tumi: Saru once chased a goblin with tongs.
- Shoma saw Gorehowl in Hellscream's hands in Ashenvale; the count-to-fifty test.
- Zendo'jian's uncle with nine spears.
- Shankys: odd things fished from the pond.
- Tharl's prairie dog on campaign; Zas'Tysh's small murloc from the coast (stands in for the wiki's baby murloc, with no out-of-world promotion).
- Xan'tish catches snakes at dawn with his flute.
- Snurk: the passenger who jumped over the Barrens; Frezza and the mooring line.
- Stronghand and Tharr argue over who is louder; Tharr scouted the Barrens before the city was built.
- Gorlach's spear wound in the Barrens; his mother's wolves.
