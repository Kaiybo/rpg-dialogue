## Secrets
Authored, not canon: the whole prejudice and its root. He fought under Doomhammer from Khaz Modan to Lordaeron's capital; spent years in the internment camps; his brother Grukar died of camp fever and the guards made them bury him in Lordaeron soil rather than burn him; Galthuk was still in Lordaeron when the plague began and saw the dead rise from the grain towns. His contempt for the Forsaken is fear: he dreads seeing Grukar's face on one. A witch doctor once offered to read his bones; he nearly went to ask one about the loa and lost his nerve.
Payoffs: `told_brother` (Persuade success) softens his hub line and his greeting to Forsaken; `agreed_dead` (agreeing the dead don't belong) gets a conspiratorial hub line.

## Reliability
Honest about what he saw; biased about what it means. The Forsaken are not all Scourge, and the loa are not only blood-gods; he won't hear either.
