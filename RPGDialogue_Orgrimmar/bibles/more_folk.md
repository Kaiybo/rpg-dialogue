## Voices (authored, not canon)
- Sagorne: slow tauren calm, elements as plains weather; Cairne sent him to teach orcs (order stance on the tribes sharing); shaman ask, not command (light stance).
- Sian'tsu: wry Darkspear, laughs at serious students; repays Thrall's debt by teaching (order stance).
- Zamja: brisk troll cook, chops while talking, mocks orc seasoning (order stance on food as fuel vs feast); misses Echo Isles crab.
- Thuwd: gruff, waste nothing from the hunt (honor stance); hides go to Karolek's leatherworkers; the sick mottled boars and Burning Blade rumours.
- Handor: dry, critiques your boots; argues with Thuwd and the stitchers like family (order stance on light armor vs "real warriors").
- Tor'phan: smooth salesman for Magar, friendly rivalry with Handor (honor stance).
- Kor'jus: calm, pale, sun-shy; defends the Cleft's neighbours (light stance on the warlocks); Forsaken are his best customers.
- Muragus: dry; sells to warlocks without asking (order stance); wood from Ashenvale via the Warsong.
- Shan'ti: warm fruit seller fighting the orc meat diet (order stance); goblin boats from Ratchet.
- Zarg: gruff Stone Guard; High Warlord weapons for deeds (honor stance on why you fight).
- Stonehide: gentle tauren; officers serve those below (order stance).

## What they notice
- Sagorne: shaman, tauren, orcs. Sian'tsu: shaman, trolls. Zamja: cooks, orcs. Thuwd: skinners, beast pets. Handor: rogues, druids. Tor'phan: mages. Kor'jus: Forsaken. Muragus: warlocks. Shan'ti: tauren. Zarg: warriors. Stonehide: tauren.
