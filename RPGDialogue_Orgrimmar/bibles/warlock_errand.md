# Orgrimmar: the succubus errand (keys: ganrul, cazul, zankaja)

## Sources
- warcraft.wiki.gg (Classic-only sections): Gan'rul Bloodeye (warlock trainer, Cleft of Shadow; Creature of the Void, The Binding (voidwalker, succubus), Devourer of Souls, Love's Gift, Seeking Strahad; Classic & Forever: Wish You Were Here and The Binding (incubus)); Cazul (blind, ancient orc warlock in the Cleft; gossip "Few have the ambition to seek true power..." and "crippled by age and blinded by things I would rather not discuss"; Blind Cazul); Zankaja (young orc on the bluffs west of the wind rider tower; mate Grunt Dogran promoted to the Crossroads; News of Dogran).
- Chain: Devourer of Souls -> Cazul -> Blind Cazul -> Zankaja -> News of Dogran -> Gazrog (Barrens) -> Logmar -> Dogran's Captivity -> Dogran gives his pendant (Love's Gift) and asks that Zankaja not be told -> Gan'rul cleans the pendant, The Binding, and says he will deliver the news to Zankaja.
- Grunt Dogran and Grunt Logmar are in `RPGDialogue_Barrens/npcs/south_taurajo_roads.lua`.

## Voices
**Gan'rul Bloodeye (orc).** Theatrical teacher, "my star pupil", warm about power, cold about its cost; warns against pride.
**Cazul (orc).** Blind, slow, grim, faces walls; speaks of power as a debt someone always pays.
**Zankaja (orc).** Young, anxious, warm; asks every traveller for news; honest about her own sadness.

## Authored, not canon
- Gan'rul: learned from the Shadow Council's leavings and hid it in the camps; tells Zankaja that Dogran died fighting the quilboar (quest text only says he would deliver "the news").
- Cazul: blinded in Draenor by "something that looked back"; studied under lesser Shadow Council masters.
- Zankaja: the pendant was her gift to Dogran, bought in the Drag; Dogran wore his new pauldron to supper; she watches the Crossroads wind riders from the bluffs. A warlock who finished Love's Gift can tell her the truth or keep the lie (honor stance).

## Never
No later-expansion warlock lore (no Black Harvest, no Dreadscar Rift).
