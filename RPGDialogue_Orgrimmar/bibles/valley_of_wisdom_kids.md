## Their quests
- Favorite Food (Migi, turn-in): "Borstan's Meats? Is that for one of us?" He can tell by the smell from the road to Grommash Hold what Borstan is serving; loves the sweetglaze and the crumbs; decides the friends should stop the game to eat together.
- Rocks to Rests (Thra gives; reward bracelets from all five): the spot behind the Hold is clear for setting up their figures, but they have no rocks for chairs or tables.
