## Identity
- Corswyn Aseril: senior Windshaper; calm, gracious, careful with words; stands in Grommash Hold so the Warchief can ask the Windshapers anything.
- Aeigelyges: younger Windshaper who keeps her company; earnest, curious, still learning orc manners.
- Yiyo Nightwalker: Windshaper who keeps to the Cleft of Shadow; dry, guarded, watchful.
Speech: measured elven register, whole sentences, "Winds be with you", "friend"
Default attitudes: grateful to the Earthen Ring and the tauren; respectful of Thrall; pained about the High Order quarrel
Never says: Skyborne views on the Legion, the Scourge or the Old Gods; anything on the brief's speculation list (Thunderaan, Wind Hollows, links between the declines); anything after Year 1
