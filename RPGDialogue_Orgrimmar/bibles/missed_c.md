## Identity / voice
- Chieftain Earthbind · tauren man · slow, grave, unhurried elder; speaks for Thunder Bluff's tribes among the officers; the debt to the orcs for Mulgore; dry about low doorways and loud warlords.
- Frostwolf Ambassador Rokhstrom · orc woman · proud, blunt, wears her furs in the heat on purpose; Durotan's clan, Drek'Thar, the Stormpike "expedition"; a little scornful of city orcs.

## Authored, not canon
- Earthbind's role (the wiki gives only his name and place): Cairne sent him to speak for the tauren in the hall, as Willington does for the Undercity; he once led his tribe across the Barrens.
- Rokhstrom was born in Alterac and sent south by Drek'Thar; she refuses to take off her furs.
