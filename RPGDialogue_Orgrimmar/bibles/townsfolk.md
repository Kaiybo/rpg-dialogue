# Orgrimmar townsfolk  (keys: karus, thathung, doras, urtrun, runthak, ogunaro, lumak, makaru, saru, nogg, xoncha)

The city's service people: no quests, little or no gossip in the game. Built from role, place and neighbours (CONTENT_GUIDE §1, "almost nothing to go on").

## Sources
- In-game roles and locations (vanilla 1.12, from memory): Karus <Banker> and Auctioneer Thathung in the Valley of Strength; Doras <Wind Rider Master> on the tower there; Urtrun Clanbringer <Guild Master>; Overlord Runthak, an officer who walks the Valley of Strength; Ogunaro Wolfrunner <Kennel Master>, Lumak <Fishing Trainer>, Makaru <Mining Trainer>, Saru Steelfury <Blacksmithing Trainer>, Nogg <Engineering Trainer, goblin> in the Valley of Honor; Xon'cha <Stable Master, troll>.
- Game rules used: orcs buy wolves at 40, other Horde races need Exalted with Orgrimmar; one vault reachable from every bank; faction auction house vs. the goblins' neutral houses; flight paths learned by visiting a roost; zeppelin towers outside the gates (Tirisfal, Grom'gol); engineering specialisation (goblin or gnome).
- Lore: Orgrimmar named for Orgrim Doomhammer, Durotar for Durotan; the internment camps (Durnholde); the Frostwolves never drank the blood; Darkspear brought to Durotar by Thrall; Burning Blade at Skull Rock and in Ragefire Chasm under the Cleft of Shadow.

## Their quests
- none: all eleven are service NPCs without quests. Runthak's in-game yells (later raid announcements) are not used.

## Identity and voices
- Karus (banker, orc): dry, exact, honest; sees deposits as proof of how far the orcs have come.
- Thathung (auctioneer): brisk, a parade-ground voice explaining the deposit for the hundredth time.
- Doras (wind rider master, orc): gruff, loves the beasts, distrusts zeppelins.
- Urtrun Clanbringer (guild master): proud, ceremonial; guilds are the new clans, chosen rather than forced.
- Overlord Runthak (officer, orc): gruff, loyal to Thrall, gives directions like orders.
- Ogunaro Wolfrunner (kennel master, orc): warm; wolves are a bond, not a product.
- Lumak (fishing trainer, orc): calm, patient; fishing as the first peace after the camps.
- Makaru (mining trainer): gruff, practical; listen to the rock.
- Saru Steelfury (blacksmithing trainer): fierce craft pride; a smith must be willing to carry the weapon.
- Nogg (engineering trainer, goblin): eager salesman; explosions as a cost of doing business.
- Xon'cha (stable master, troll): calm, gentle Darkspear speech ("be", dropped g's, "mon" once); talks to the animals.

## Authored, not canon
- Karus keeps the chain struck from his ankle at Durnholde in his own vault; the rogue who tried to rob the bank.
- Lumak caught enough fish to feed eleven freed orcs in three days, and has fished since.
- Saru learned from an old smith who kept the craft in his head through the camps.
- Nogg has blown up his shop twice.
- Xon'cha's crab story.
- Runthak saw Doomhammer once, after a camp was freed.
- Runthak's prejudice (0.80.0): distrusts the Forsaken; two of his grunts from the Durnholde raid later walked with the Scourge and didn't know him.

## Never
- Anything after Year 1 (Garrosh, Cataclysm Orgrimmar, Runthak's later announcements).
- Pronouns for Makaru and Saru beyond what the impressions need (gender unconfirmed).
- Promises of discounts or free wolves.

## What they notice
- Doras: mounts on his tower. Runthak: warlocks, Forsaken. Lumak, Makaru, Saru, Nogg: skill in their own profession; Saru also battered armour. Xon'cha: a hunter's named pet.

## Humans (human patch)
Authored, not canon. Each NPC has a "What do you make of humans?" branch (`hu_view` then `hu_stance`), with Forsaken ("you were one of them"), orc or troll variants.
- Urtrun Clanbringer: the camps split the clans until young orcs forgot their grandfathers' banners; every clan he registers is a name the humans couldn't take.
