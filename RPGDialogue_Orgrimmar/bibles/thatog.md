## Their quests
- Meal Appeal (gives): Migi runs off to play all day and skips the lunch Thatog tells him to pick up at Borstan's; comes home starving. "These kids think their games are more important than their own bodies." Duties keep Thatog at work; can't watch every meal. Worried, loving, a little exasperated.
- Favorite Food (Borstan to Migi): Borstan crumbles sweetglazed hog belly bits on Migi's lunch; Migi loves it and the children start stopping their game to eat together.
- Rocks to Rests (Thra): the player carries boulders so the children have seats behind Grommash Hold.
