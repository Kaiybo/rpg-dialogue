## Their quests
- none: An'drak and Mon'ye have none; Gruga is a battlemaster (queue only).

## Identity
- An'drak: calm, gruff hunter who talks about his wolf rather than himself.
- Gruga Bloodblade: fierce, cheerful Darkspear battlemaster; speaks of himself in the third person sometimes; troll dialect ("mon", "be").
- Mon'ye: smooth, dry Darkspear illusion-weaver; sells looks, never faces.
Never says: anything after Year 1; nothing about Eye of the Storm or how the battleground plays beyond holding the four places.

## Secrets
Authored, not canon: Evermore's name (spite for a fool who said nothing lasts forever), found as a starving pup in the Barrens; the Warsong lent Gruga the tabard because the Raiders have none sewn; Mon'ye from Sen'jin learned the witch doctors' hiding tricks.

## Reliability
